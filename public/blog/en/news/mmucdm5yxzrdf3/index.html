2nd Special Prosecutor, ‘Allegations of Favoritism in Investigation of Kim Geon-hee’ Travel Ban on Lee Chang-soo

Recently, the 2nd Comprehensive Special Prosecutor's Team has imposed a travel ban on former Seoul Central District Prosecutor Lee Chang-soo. This action is closely related to the allegations of 'favoritism in the investigation' concerning Mrs. Kim Geon-hee, with suspicions raised that former Prosecutor Lee bypassed the investigation of Mrs. Kim and communicated directly with the presidential office. The special prosecutor's team requested a travel ban for former Prosecutor Lee and former Deputy Chief Prosecutor Cho Sang-won, which the Ministry of Justice accepted.

This case holds significance beyond a mere investigation. It raises questions about the independence and political neutrality of the prosecution and is expected to have political repercussions and social ramifications in the future. In particular, this incident will serve as an opportunity to reassess the power dynamics between the political sphere and the prosecution.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This issue stems from criticisms that the prosecution did not adequately conduct an investigation regarding the allegations of Mrs. Kim Geon-hee receiving luxury bags. Former Prosecutor Lee reported the investigation of Mrs. Kim only 10 hours after it began, raising suspicions about the power structure and political pressure within the prosecution. Subsequently, the prosecution dismissed all allegations related to Mrs. Kim as unfounded, but this decision has become more controversial in light of the special prosecutor's search and seizure.
