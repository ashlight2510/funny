Original article at a glance Title: ‘Pioneer of Biotechnology’ Dr. Han Moon-hee Passes Away Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun Dr. Han Moon-hee, a scientist who laid the foundation for biotechnology technology, industry, and policy in Korea, passed away on the 30th, as announced by the Center for Scientific and Technological Contributors on the 31st. The center stated, “Dr. Han Moon-hee was a biotechnologist who led the development of technology and industrial advancement in the field of biotechnology in Korea through the development of isomerase enzyme processes and the localization of raw materials for anti-tuberculosis drugs.” The late Dr. Han graduated from the College of Education and the Graduate School of Seoul National University and later moved to the United States. Dr. Han Moon-hee, Who Laid the Foundation of Biotechnology, His Legacy and Significance

Dr. Han Moon-hee, who contributed to the advancement of biotechnology in Korea, has passed away at the age of 91. He is recognized as a key figure who laid the groundwork for Korean biotechnology through the development of isomerase enzyme processes and the localization of raw materials for anti-tuberculosis drugs, significantly influencing the establishment of government biotechnology policies. His research led to technological development and industrial advancement in Korean biotechnology, and his role as the first director of the Genetic Engineering Center is also highlighted.

This news prompts a reflection on the history and present of the biotechnology field. Dr. Han's achievements will remain an important milestone that contributed to shaping Korea's biotechnology industry and policies, going beyond mere research outcomes.

Key Summary

  • Dr. Han Moon-hee was a scientist who contributed to the advancement of Korean biotechnology, particularly through the development of isomerase enzyme processes and the localization of raw materials for anti-tuberculosis drugs.
  • He was directly involved in establishing government biotechnology policies through the development plans for life sciences and biotechnology technologies.
  • He contributed to the establishment of the Genetic Engineering Center and various bio-related associations.
  • He participated in planning various national research and development projects and received several awards, including the National Medal of Merit.
  • His passing is considered a significant loss for the Korean biotechnology community.
