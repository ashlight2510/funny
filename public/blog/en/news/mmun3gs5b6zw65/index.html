Judo's Kim Min-jong and Heo Mi-mi secure Asian Games participation rights

The judo national team representatives for the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games have been determined. Kim Min-jong in the men's heavyweight category and Heo Mi-mi in the women's 57kg category secured their spots for the Asian Games by winning their respective categories at the second national team selection held on the 17th at the Chungcheongnam-do Boryeong Comprehensive Gymnasium. Kim Min-jong defeated Lee Seung-yeop in the final by golden score, while Heo Mi-mi overcame Shin Yu-na with an ippon victory, both rising to the top of their weight classes. As a result, both athletes will also participate in the Asian Judo Championships.

This achievement will be an important opportunity to elevate the status of Korean judo beyond just individual victories. Notably, both Kim Min-jong and Heo Mi-mi are Olympic medalists, and their success is expected to serve as significant motivation for younger athletes.
