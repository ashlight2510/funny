Gyeongnam Governor Election: Park Wan-soo vs. Kim Kyung-soo Confirmed

The Gyeongnam gubernatorial election in June has been confirmed as a two-horse race between current governor Park Wan-soo and former governor Kim Kyung-soo. Governor Park Wan-soo, affiliated with the People Power Party, received a single nomination, while former governor Kim Kyung-soo of the Democratic Party also secured a single nomination. Additionally, during the Democratic Party's candidate interview for the Busan mayoral position, Rep. Jeon Jae-soo emphasized his achievements while denying allegations of receiving money from the Unification Church. This election will be a significant contest where the past experiences and policies of these two politicians will clash, potentially determining the direction of local politics.
