SK Telecom seeks 71 counties with high elderly population... Establishes 'Customer Experience' organization

SK Telecom announced that it will expand its customer-centered ‘visiting service’ targeting 71 counties nationwide. This initiative is part of its efforts to restore customer trust following last year's SIM card hacking incident, with a particular focus on providing security training and communication counseling in areas with high elderly populations. By establishing a Customer Experience (CX) organization, the company aims to strengthen its system for directly listening to and reflecting customer needs, demonstrating its commitment to enhancing trust through on-site communication with customers.

This measure is interpreted as a strategy to not only improve services but also to resolve customer inconveniences and increase long-term customer loyalty through meaningful communication. SK Telecom has stated that it will listen to customer voices and improve its products and services accordingly.

Key Summary

  • SK Telecom provides security training and communication counseling in 71 counties.
  • Establishment of a Customer Experience (CX) organization to strengthen the reflection of customer needs.
  • Prioritizing visits to areas with high elderly populations to provide tailored services.
  • Plans to shorten the consultation process for long-term customers and assign dedicated consultants.
  • Formation of a customer advisory group comprising various age groups to expand on-site communication.

Key Scenes

  • Restoration of Customer Trust: Efforts to strengthen communication with customers to regain trust following last year's hacking incident.
  • Targeting Elderly Population: Prioritizing visits to areas with high elderly populations to provide tailored services.
  • Role of CX Organization: Directly listening to and reflecting customer voices to improve services.
  • Long-term Customer Management: Activities for appreciation and feedback collection aimed at long-term customers.
  • Diverse Customer Participation: Establishing a structure to gather various opinions through a customer advisory group.

Why Background Matters

SK Telecom's announcement comes in the wake of a large-scale customer exodus due to last year's SIM card hacking incident, highlighting the urgent need to restore customer trust. There is a growing recognition of the importance of establishing a system that can directly listen to and reflect customer needs, particularly emphasizing the necessity for tailored services for the elderly population, who are often digitally marginalized.

Why It Matters

This issue goes beyond mere service improvement at SK Telecom and serves as a wake-up call for the entire telecommunications industry regarding the need for customer-centered management. Listening to and reflecting customer voices is a key factor in enhancing a company's sustainability, and it is becoming increasingly important to consider customer segments that may be marginalized in the digital environment.

Points of Interest

In my view, SK Telecom's approach emphasizes redefining relationships with customers beyond just service provision. By directly listening to and reflecting customer voices, the company is creating opportunities to enhance its credibility and, in the long run, strengthen customer loyalty.

Hidden Variables to Consider

However, it remains to be seen how effectively these efforts will restore customer trust. While listening to customer needs is important, if the process of actually reflecting and improving based on that feedback is not supported, the effects may be short-lived. Additionally, since not all customer needs are the same, specific execution plans for tailored services are necessary.

Current Checkpoints

  • Attention should be paid to how SK Telecom's ‘visiting service’ is actually implemented and the customers' reactions.
  • It is also necessary to check the specific achievements of the Customer Experience (CX) organization and the resulting service improvements.

FAQ

Q1: When does SK Telecom's visiting service start?
A1: It has already begun in Jin-an County, Jeollabuk-do, and is expected to expand nationwide to 71 counties throughout the year.

Q2: What role does the Customer Experience (CX) organization play?
A2: It collects and analyzes customer needs to reflect them in the improvement of products and services.

Q3: What form will tailored services for the elderly population take?
A3: Various tailored services, including security training, communication and AI counseling, and mobile phone repair consultations, are planned to be provided.

Q4: What is the composition of the customer advisory group?
A4: It consists of 100 customers from various age groups and professions, including office workers, homemakers, and university students.

Now, the points you should focus on are how effectively SK Telecom can restore customer trust through this service and how customer voices will be reflected in service improvements.
