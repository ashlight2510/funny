Seoul Mayor Candidate Primary: The Designer Debate Between Park Jumin and Jung Won-oh

Ahead of the preliminary primary for the Seoul mayoral candidate, the two main candidates of the Democratic Party, Park Jumin and Jung Won-oh, engaged in a fierce exchange regarding each other's qualifications. Candidate Park Jumin defined the Mayor of Seoul as a 'designer' and belittled candidate Jung Won-oh's experience as a local government head by labeling him as a 'manager.' In response, candidate Jung Won-oh emphasized the possibility of collaboration with the President, highlighting his strengths, indicating a fierce competition ahead. This primary is likely to lead to significant discussions on how to set the future vision for Seoul, beyond just the qualifications of individual candidates.

In this context, the statements made by the two candidates are not merely political checks but could directly influence the policy direction of the Seoul city government and the lives of its citizens.
