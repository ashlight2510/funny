Kyobo Bookstore Ready to Welcome BTS! A Special Transformation at the Gwanghwamun Branch

Recently, ahead of BTS's Gwanghwamun concert, the Kyobo Bookstore Gwanghwamun branch has completed special preparations. On the day of the concert, the 21st, the branch will operate on shortened hours to avoid congestion and has set up a corner displaying recommended books and products related to BTS. This space will showcase books recommended by BTS members, providing fans with a unique experience. Additionally, there is a space introducing traditional Korean crafts and goods, preparing to welcome international fans.

These moves carry significance beyond mere marketing. Kyobo Bookstore's strategy targeting BTS's fandom 'ARMY' aims to strengthen bonds with fans and provide opportunities to share cultural values, going beyond simply selling products.

Key Summary

  • Kyobo Bookstore Gwanghwamun branch sets up a special corner for the BTS concert.
  • A variety of products including recommended books and traditional Korean crafts are displayed.
  • Shortened hours on the day of the concert to manage congestion.

Key Scenes Include

  • Operation of a BTS member recommended book corner.

  • Posting of BTS-related messages on the exterior wall of Kyobo Life's headquarters.

Reasons to Consider the Background

BTS has an enormous fan base worldwide, and their activities have established them as a cultural phenomenon beyond mere musical achievements. Kyobo Bookstore intends to leverage BTS's popularity to enhance communication with fans and convey their cultural message. This concert is a significant event commemorating BTS's comeback, and Kyobo Bookstore aims to use this opportunity to forge closer relationships with fans.

Why It Matters

Kyobo Bookstore's actions are not merely a commercial strategy. Recognizing the importance of fan culture and striving to provide new cultural experiences can promote broader cultural consumption. Particularly, collaboration with global artists like BTS can bring positive brand recognition to Kyobo Bookstore. Fans seek to feel a connection with their cultural icons beyond just purchasing products.
