Intelligent Dispatch System for Traditional Markets and Nationwide Expansion of 119 Pass for Apartment Complexes... Fire Department "Securing Golden Time of 7 Minutes"

The National Fire Agency is introducing a new system to secure the golden time for fire trucks during fire incidents. The "Intelligent Dispatch System for Traditional Markets" and the nationwide expansion of the "119 Pass" are the main components of this initiative. The system that provides real-time guidance on store locations and optimal entry routes within traditional markets is expected to significantly reduce the arrival time of fire trucks during fires. In particular, the 119 Pass system installed in apartment complexes focuses on facilitating the quick entry of fire trucks and eliminating obstacles to their response.

These changes are part of the "Comprehensive Measures for Securing Golden Time for Fire Trucks in 2026" proposed by the National Fire Agency, aiming for a 69.4% rate of arriving at fire scenes within 7 minutes. This is a critical time for saving lives, and the agency is making various technical and institutional efforts to achieve this goal.

Key Highlights

  • Introduction of an intelligent dispatch system in traditional markets: Real-time guidance on store locations and optimal routes.
  • Nationwide expansion of the 119 Pass system for apartment complexes: Immediate opening of common entrance doors during entry.
  • Elimination of response obstacles: Priority signal system for emergency vehicles and maintenance of fire truck access routes.
  • Golden time goal: Achieving a 69.4% rate of arriving within 7 minutes of fire occurrence.
  • Promotion of a culture of yielding for emergency vehicles: Collaborating with traffic broadcasts to guide emergency evacuations.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The National Fire Agency's measures come at a time when the importance of fire response is increasing. Traditional markets often have structurally narrow and complex environments, making rapid response difficult during fires. To address these issues, the agency aims to shorten response times through technological innovation and ensure citizen safety. Additionally, delays in entry during fires have been a significant issue in apartment complexes. The 119 Pass system is being recognized as an effective solution to these problems.

Why It Matters

Securing the golden time for fire trucks goes beyond merely extinguishing fires. It is a matter of life and death, where rapid response plays a crucial role in minimizing casualties. Moreover, the damage caused by fires leads to not only economic losses but also social anxiety. Therefore, the introduction of such systems is expected to have a positive impact on society as a whole.

Points to Note

In my view, the key to this system's introduction lies in the combination of technology and infrastructure. It is not just about the introduction of equipment or systems, but about how quickly we can actually respond to fires through them. Furthermore, the cooperation of citizens is essential, as a culture of yielding will facilitate smoother responses for fire trucks.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Even with technological improvements, narrow alleyways and illegal parking issues may still pose challenges. These structural problems can hinder fire truck responses, making collaboration with local governments essential. Therefore, community cooperation is necessary alongside the system's introduction.

Current Checkpoints

Information on how the National Fire Agency's new system operates in practice and how citizens can utilize it is needed. In particular, it is important to check how to use the 119 Pass system and the areas where the priority signal system for emergency vehicles is installed.

FAQ

Q: When will the intelligent dispatch system for traditional markets be implemented?
A: A specific implementation schedule has not yet been announced, but the National Fire Agency plans to establish the system as soon as possible.

Q: Will the 119 Pass system be installed in all apartment complexes?
A: The National Fire Agency plans to install the 119 Pass system in over 40% of apartment complexes this year.

Q: How much faster will response times be with this system?
A: The National Fire Agency expects that the arrival time at fire scenes can be reduced to within 7 minutes after the system is implemented.

Q: How can citizens participate in this system?
A: Citizens can participate by promoting a culture of yielding and ensuring that roads are cleared for fire trucks when they are responding.
