Iran Football Association Requests to Host World Cup Matches in Mexico

The Iran Football Association has officially announced its desire to hold the group stage matches of the 2026 North American World Cup in Mexico instead of the United States. This request comes in response to former President Trump's remarks that he could not guarantee the safety of the Iranian team, and Iran has stated that it is in discussions with FIFA. However, FIFA expects all teams to play according to the already announced match schedule, making it unlikely that Iran's request will be accepted.

This incident goes beyond a simple request for a change of venue, reflecting a complex situation intertwined with international politics and sports. Amid concerns about Iran's safety, the possibility of FIFA adjusting its schedule, and the stance of the Mexican government, Iran's position reflects its intention to assert its presence on the global stage of the World Cup.
