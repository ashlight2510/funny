“2030 Female Customers Are the Best”... Reasons Convenience Store Owners Welcome Them

Recently, the convenience store industry has seen female consumers in their 20s and 30s emerging as a major customer base, with their preferred product categories driving sales growth. Notably, there has been significant growth in the dessert, beauty and fashion, and health supplement sectors, prompting convenience stores to adjust their store layouts and product lineups to reflect these trends. This change is not just about increasing sales; it is also significantly impacting the image of convenience stores and consumer experiences.

The convenience store industry is expanding various product categories to cater to the tastes of the MZ generation and female consumers, which is gaining more attention alongside changes in consumer purchasing patterns. In particular, while tobacco sales are declining, the growth of desserts and beauty products has become a new growth driver for convenience stores.

Key Summary

  • Sales of desserts, beauty and fashion, and health supplements are surging in convenience stores, with female consumers in their 20s and 30s making up a significant portion.
  • GS25 and CU are expanding specialized stores and strengthening products that reflect SNS trends to boost sales.
  • While tobacco sales are declining, desserts and health supplements are establishing themselves as new major product categories for convenience stores.

Key Scenes

  • Change in Consumer Trends: The product categories preferred by women in their 20s and 30s are the main drivers of sales growth.
  • Expansion of Specialized Stores: Opening specialized stores for desserts and beauty products enhances consumer experience.
  • Influence of SNS: Developing products that reflect social media trends targets younger consumer demographics.
  • Growth of Health Supplements: Increased interest in health has led to a surge in health supplement sales.
  • Decline in Tobacco Sales: As traditional revenue sources diminish, new product categories are coming to the forefront.

Why Background Matters

The convenience store industry has traditionally relied on tobacco and convenience foods for revenue, but this structure is being shaken by recent changes in consumer preferences. In particular, as the MZ generation and female consumers show increased interest in health and beauty, convenience stores are moving towards strengthening product categories aimed at these demographics. Additionally, the influence of digital platforms like SNS is diversifying the products that consumers prefer.

Why It Matters

This change is having a significant impact on the revenue structure of the convenience store industry and is likely to lead to a greater emphasis on consumer experience in the future. The transformation of convenience stores from mere purchasing spaces to places that offer diverse experiences presents important implications for the entire retail industry. Understanding what consumers want and reflecting that in offerings will be a key factor in determining competitiveness.

Personal Comment

In my view, the most important aspect of convenience stores reflecting changing consumer trends is communication with consumers. It is not just about increasing product offerings; providing the experiences that consumers desire will ultimately lead to increased sales. For example, as specialized dessert stores increase, consumers have more choices, which naturally leads to sales growth. Strengthening relationships with consumers will determine the future success of the convenience store industry.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While the changes in convenience stores are a positive signal, as consumer interest in health and the environment rises, the quality and sustainability of the products offered by convenience stores will also become crucial. For instance, in the case of health supplements, low-quality or poorly informed products could lead to a loss of consumer trust. Therefore, quality control and transparent information provision are essential.

Points to Check Now

In light of recent trends in the convenience store industry, it is important to closely observe what new product categories are emerging and what experiences consumers are seeking. In particular, it is advisable to check how marketing strategies utilizing SNS and product development reflecting consumer feedback are being implemented.
