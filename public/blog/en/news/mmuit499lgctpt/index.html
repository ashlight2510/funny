Korea Exchange Pre-Market, Implementation Delayed Due to Securities Industry Backlash

The Korea Exchange has decided to postpone the opening of the pre-and-after market, originally scheduled for the end of June, to mid-September. This decision was made in consideration of the backlash from the securities industry and the need to secure sufficient testing time to enhance the completeness of the system development. Notably, the operating hours of the pre-market have also been shortened from the initial 7 AM to 8 AM to 7 AM to 7:50 AM. This is also a measure taken in consideration of competition with the alternative exchange Next Trade.

This decision affects various stakeholders, particularly highlighting the backlash from securities firms and labor groups. There are concerns that conducting ETF and short selling transactions in the pre-market could impose additional burdens on securities firms. Labor groups are strongly opposing the rushed implementation by the exchange, indicating that future controversies are likely to continue.

Why Background Matters

This issue arises from the Korea Exchange's plan to introduce the pre-and-after market. However, securities firms opposed it due to insufficient operating hours and system preparation, leading to the postponement of the opening. In particular, the exchange had to coordinate various interests amid competition with Next Trade.
