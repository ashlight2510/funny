Jeonnam Province invests 34.8 billion won in welfare projects for female farmers

Jeollanam-do is investing 34.8 billion won to promote various welfare projects aimed at improving the quality of life for female farmers. This year, the province plans to provide support for cultural life, health check-ups, and childcare services through nine different projects, with particular attention on the 'Happy Voucher for Female Farmers and Fisherwomen' and the 'Farm Assistant' program. These initiatives are designed to help female farmers carry out their agricultural activities more stably.

This support is expected to contribute to enhancing the quality of life for female farmers and strengthening the sustainability of rural communities, going beyond mere financial assistance. With the expansion of the support targets, more female farmers will be able to benefit from these initiatives.

Key Highlights

  • Jeonnam Province invests 34.8 billion won in welfare projects for female farmers
  • 'Happy Voucher for Female Farmers and Fisherwomen' provides 200,000 won support, benefiting 107,000 individuals
  • Expansion of age eligibility for special health check-up support, with an expected 10,271 beneficiaries
  • Operation of the farm assistant program for pregnant female farmers and their spouses
  • Provision of community meal services and childcare during busy farming seasons

Why Background Matters

Female farmers play a crucial role in rural society, yet their welfare and quality of life have often been overlooked. Jeonnam Province recognized this issue and planned these welfare projects to support the health and cultural lives of female farmers. The need for support has become even more pronounced in light of the economic difficulties faced by rural areas following the COVID-19 pandemic.

Why It Matters

This initiative plays a vital role in improving the quality of life for female farmers and enhancing the sustainability of rural communities, going beyond mere financial assistance. By supporting female farmers in continuing their agricultural activities stably, it is also expected to contribute to revitalizing the rural economy. This will have a positive impact on addressing the issue of population decline in rural areas.

Noteworthy Aspects

In my view, a particularly noteworthy point in this initiative is the range of uses for the 'Happy Voucher for Female Farmers and Fisherwomen.' Designed to be used in various sectors such as cinemas, bookstores, and beauty salons, it will provide many opportunities to enrich the cultural lives of female farmers. Additionally, the expansion of age eligibility for health check-ups will provide practical assistance in maintaining the health of middle-aged and older female farmers.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While it may seem that this initiative will benefit all female farmers, there may actually be eligibility criteria or conditions for application. For example, the use of vouchers may be limited, or specific conditions may need to be met to receive support for health check-ups. It is important to understand and prepare for these details accurately.

Points to Check Now

  • Application methods and usage for the Happy Voucher for Female Farmers and Fisherwomen
  • Detailed conditions and application procedures for the special health check-up program
  • Scope of support and eligibility for the farm assistant program

FAQ

Q1: How do I apply for the Happy Voucher for Female Farmers and Fisherwomen?
A1: You can inquire at local agricultural institutions or check the Jeonnam Provincial Office website for application methods.

Q2: What tests are included in the special health check-up program?
A2: The program includes tests in five areas: musculoskeletal, cardiovascular, fractures, lung capacity, and pesticide poisoning.

Q3: How is support provided for the farm assistant program?
A3: It supports labor costs for agricultural assistance within 180 days before and after childbirth, with female farmers eligible for up to 70 days and male farmers for up to 20 days of support.

Q4: Why was the age eligibility expanded?
A4: The age was adjusted to widen the support target, allowing more female farmers to benefit.

This welfare initiative by Jeonnam Province presents an opportunity to provide tangible assistance to female farmers. Be sure to carefully review the details of each program and ensure you do not miss out on the support that suits you best.
