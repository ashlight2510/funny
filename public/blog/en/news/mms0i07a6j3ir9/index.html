Smart Tips to Reduce Electricity Bills When Using Air Conditioning

When summer comes, it's really tough without an air conditioner. No matter how nice the cool breeze is, the worry about electricity bills can make you uneasy. Every summer, I pondered how to use the air conditioner efficiently to reduce electricity costs. So, I’ve compiled some tips that I found useful.

1. Setting the Air Conditioner Temperature

Set the Optimal Temperature

The first thing to do when using an air conditioner is to set the optimal temperature. Generally, 26 to 28 degrees Celsius is considered the most efficient. By setting it this way, the air conditioner doesn't have to work too hard, which can help reduce your electricity bill.

I tried setting it to 25 degrees, but it was too cold, and the air conditioner kept running. So, I adjusted it to 27 degrees, and it felt much cooler while costing less on the electricity bill. You should give it a try!

Use a Fan Together

Sometimes, just turning on the air conditioner isn't enough. In such cases, using a fan can be a good idea. The fan helps to distribute the air conditioner’s breeze better, making you feel cooler even at the same temperature.

Personally, I found that keeping the fan on and raising the air conditioner temperature a bit was still sufficiently cool. I think you should try this method too.

2. Regular Filter Cleaning

Importance of Cleaning the Filter

When the air conditioner's filter gets dirty, air circulation is hindered, causing the air conditioner to work harder. This can significantly increase your electricity bill. That's why I clean the filter every summer before it arrives.

After doing this, I noticed that the air conditioner operated more comfortably, and later, I felt the decrease in my electricity bill. Cleaning the filter is simpler than you might think, so I highly recommend doing it.

3. Changing Air Conditioner Usage Patterns

Use According to Time of Day

Paying attention to the time of day when using the air conditioner can also be beneficial. It's better to run the air conditioner strongly during the hottest part of the day and lower it a bit at night. Especially in the evening, the temperature drops, making it less necessary.

After trying this method, I could really feel the reduction in my electricity bill. Lowering the air conditioner a bit after sunset is also a good tip.

4. Utilizing Windows and Curtains

Proper Opening and Blocking

When using the air conditioner, it's basic to close the windows and draw the curtains. Direct sunlight can raise the indoor temperature. However, if the outside temperature is lower during the evening, opening the windows to let in a cool breeze can be nice.

By doing this, I found that I could enjoy a cool breeze without turning on the air conditioner, saving on electricity costs. I recommend opening the windows in the evening!

I've summarized ways to reduce electricity bills when using air conditioning. There were various methods such as setting the optimal temperature, cleaning the filter, adjusting usage patterns, and utilizing windows and curtains. By using these tips, you can spend a cooler and more economical summer.

If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I think starting with these tips would be great.
