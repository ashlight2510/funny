‘KPop Demon Hunters’ Wins Two Awards at the Academy Awards

‘KPop Demon Hunters’ achieved great success at the 98th Academy Awards by winning the Oscar for Best Animated Feature and Best Original Song. Maggie Kang, the co-director of the film, expressed in her acceptance speech that “the next generation will not have to long for such films” and dedicated the film to Korea and Koreans everywhere. This Netflix original film triumphed over strong competitors such as ‘Zootopia 2’, ‘Arco’, ‘Little Amelie’, and ‘Elio’.

This news reaffirms the rising global recognition of K-pop and the status of Korean content, holding significant meaning in boosting cultural pride. In particular, K-pop's recognition at the Academy Awards symbolically showcases the growth of Korean popular culture.

Key Highlights

  • Awards Received: ‘KPop Demon Hunters’ won the Oscars for Best Animated Feature and Best Original Song.
  • Cultural Message: Co-director Maggie Kang mentioned dedicating the film to Koreans and Korean immigrants.
  • Impact of K-pop: ‘Golden’ is the first K-pop song to win a Grammy, showcasing the musical achievements of K-pop.
  • Connection with Fans: The award ceremony created a K-pop concert atmosphere, strengthening the bond with fans.
  • Message of Growth and Resistance: Co-songwriter Ejae emphasized stories of resistance rather than just success.

Why Background Matters

‘KPop Demon Hunters’ was produced with the support of Netflix, emerging at a time when the popularity of K-pop is spreading globally, increasing interest in Korean content. This film is the first animated work themed around K-pop, making it significant in its own right. Especially in recent years, as Korean films and music have gained international attention and continued to win awards at various ceremonies, this Academy Award win is a continuation of that trend.
