Perfect 3-Night, 4-Day Tokyo Travel Itinerary Guide

March 15, 2026

Perfect 3 Nights 4 Days Travel Itinerary in Tokyo

When planning a trip, especially to a place as diverse as Tokyo, it can be challenging to decide on an itinerary. Questions like "Where should I go?" and "What food should I try?" often come to mind. So in this post, I’ve organized how to make the most of a 3-night, 4-day trip to Tokyo. Let’s embark on this journey together!

Day 1: Tokyo's Symbols, Shinjuku and Shibuya

Starting in Shinjuku

When you arrive in Tokyo, Shinjuku is a must-visit. Shinjuku Station is one of the busiest stations in the world, and starting here allows you to truly feel the essence of Tokyo. What I loved most about Shinjuku was the famous "Kabukicho" area. At night, the vibrant neon signs create a stunning atmosphere. I had dinner with a friend at an izakaya in this area, and the snacks paired with Japanese beer were absolutely delicious!

Shibuya's Scramble Crossing

After Shinjuku, head to Shibuya, where you can't miss the Shibuya Scramble Crossing. The sight of so many people bustling about was truly impressive. Also, be sure to see the famous "Hachiko Statue" in Shibuya. I recommend taking photos here! When I visited, both Japanese tourists and foreigners were taking pictures together, and their smiles were really heartwarming.

Day 2: History and Culture in Asakusa and Ueno

Experiencing Tradition in Asakusa

On the second day, let’s head to Asakusa. Senso-ji Temple is one of the oldest temples in Japan, and the atmosphere there was incredibly peaceful. Don’t forget to take a commemorative photo at the wooden "Kaminarimon" gate before heading to the temple! Also, I recommend trying the street food nearby, especially the "Takoyaki" I had, which was really delicious!

Exploring Ueno Park

Next, let’s move from Asakusa to Ueno. Ueno Park is home to various museums and a zoo, making it a place you can enjoy all day long. In particular, you can see pandas at Ueno Zoo, which are just adorable. I remember leisurely strolling through the park and enjoying some downtime. It was a moment to relax amidst Tokyo's busy life.

Day 3: Modern Attractions, Odaiba and Roppongi

Futuristic Scenery in Odaiba

On the third day, let’s go to Odaiba. You need to cross a bridge that spans Tokyo Bay to get there. Once you arrive in Odaiba, don’t miss the digital art exhibition like "TeamLab Borderless"! When I visited, I had a fantastic experience with colorful lights and sounds blending together. It was my first time at such an exhibition, and it was truly fascinating.

Enjoying Night Views in Roppongi

Finally, head to Roppongi to enjoy the night view of Tokyo Tower. The view of Tokyo from Roppongi Hills is absolutely spectacular. Having a simple dinner with a friend while gazing at the city lights made that moment truly precious.

Day 4: Last Shopping and Cherishing Memories

Shopping in Akihabara

On the last day, let’s enjoy shopping in Akihabara. This place is filled with Japanese electronics and anime-related products. I went with a friend who loves anime, and I picked up a few goodies that I still cherish.

Souvenir Shopping and Heading to the Airport

Finally, let’s stop by the "Marui" department store near Tokyo Station to buy souvenirs. It’s filled with various traditional Japanese items. While buying souvenirs is great, don’t forget to take some time to soak in the last moments of your trip.

After finishing your trip, you'll have so many memories, and you'll find yourself wanting to return to Tokyo. I hope you can use this itinerary to have a fulfilling travel experience. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment!
