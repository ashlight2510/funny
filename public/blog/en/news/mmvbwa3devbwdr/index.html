Sookmyung Women's University Faculty Startup Lab Incube Secures 14.7 Billion Won Series B Investment

Lab Incube, a faculty startup of Sookmyung Women's University, has recently successfully secured a Series B investment of 14.7 billion won. This investment saw the new participation of Metabiomed, a global biodegradable medical materials company, alongside existing investors who also made follow-up investments. Lab Incube plans to utilize these funds to enhance its MOF (metal-organic framework) based porous material platform and expand its business. In particular, the company aims to strengthen collaboration in the global medical device market through a strategic partnership with Metabiomed.

This news is noteworthy not only as an investment announcement but also for its significant impact on Korea's new material industry and startup ecosystem. Lab Incube aims to establish global competitiveness in the chemical and bio sectors through the successful commercialization of MOF materials. This serves as a good example of Korea's research and development capabilities and the growth of its startup ecosystem, indicating potential future developments in related industries.
