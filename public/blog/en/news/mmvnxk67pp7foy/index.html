Jungnang-gu Opens Public Park Golf Course at Night

Jungnang-gu, Seoul has decided to operate the Jungnang Public Park Golf Course at night to meet the high demand from residents. This facility, a 9-hole recreational space established on the banks of the Jungnangcheon River in April 2024, has garnered significant interest from the community, with cumulative visitor numbers exceeding 55,000 since its opening. Particularly, the reservation rate has skyrocketed to 97%, prompting the district to undertake improvements for night use. Now, residents can enjoy the golf course until 9 PM, providing a more convenient environment for recreational sports.

This news signifies more than just the start of night operations. It is expected to support healthy leisure activities for local residents and enhance accessibility to recreational sports. This initiative is part of Jungnang-gu's ongoing efforts to expand sports facilities, which will positively impact the quality of life for residents.
