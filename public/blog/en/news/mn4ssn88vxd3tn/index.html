Iran appoints hardliner ‘Khamenei confidant’ from the Revolutionary Guards as security chief following Larijani

Recently, Iran appointed Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr, a hardliner from the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), as the successor to Ali Larijani, the Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, who died in an Israeli airstrike. Zolghadr is known to be a close confidant of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Khamenei and is expected to have a significant influence on Iran's major policy decisions. His appointment comes amid complex political tensions and security situations within Iran.

This news is not merely about the appointment of an individual. It can be interpreted as a signal of significant changes in Iran's internal affairs and foreign policy. In particular, Zolghadr is known to be one of the most hardline figures since the Iranian Revolution, and his policy direction is expected to have a substantial impact on Iran's security and diplomacy in the future.

This appointment is closely related to Iran's political environment. Iran's security situation has deteriorated due to recent Israeli airstrikes, which has also fueled internal political conflicts.
