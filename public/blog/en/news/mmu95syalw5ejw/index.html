A low and slow prayer for the 'world of killing'... "Stop the horrific war"

Recently, monks from the Jogye Order of Korean Buddhism conducted a five-body prostration in Seoul, urging an end to the war. They expressed deep concern over the horrors of the Middle Eastern war caused by military operations from the United States and Israel, and prayed for those suffering from the pain of war and violence. Particularly, they faced the reality of the increasing casualties among children and civilians, highlighting the brutality of war and the importance of peace.

This event was not just a protest but a part of Buddhist practice filled with deep repentance and wishes. The monks knelt and laid down on the asphalt in prayer, earnestly hoping that the warring parties would lay down their weapons and the mindset of killing. During this process, various citizens participated together, wishing for peace worldwide.

Key Summary

  • Monks from the Jogye Order of Korean Buddhism conducted a five-body prostration in Seoul, urging an end to the Middle Eastern war.
  • They expressed deep concern over the impact of war on children and civilians.
  • The five-body prostration is a Buddhist practice of repentance and wishes, emphasizing the need for peace and awareness of the horrors of war.

Key Scenes

  • Horrors of War: The death toll from the Middle Eastern war has exceeded 3,000, with many being children.
  • Meaning of the Five-Body Prostration: The monks touched the five parts of their bodies to the ground, appealing for an end to the war through deep prayer.
  • Citizen Participation: Various citizens participated together, forming a consensus on the tragedy of war.
  • Responsibility of Warring Parties: The monks urged the warring parties to lay down their weapons and move toward peace.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This five-body prostration was planned to raise awareness of the continuity of the recent wars in the Middle East and the resulting civilian casualties. Military operations by the United States and Israel have intensified amid conflicts with Iran, leading to significant controversy over the resulting civilian deaths worldwide. Particularly, the reality of children becoming victims of war has deeply saddened many.

Why It Matters

This event is an important opportunity to highlight the horrors of war and emphasize the need for peace. Beyond being a simple protest, the gathering of people from various backgrounds wishing for peace reminds us of the importance of social solidarity. It can particularly contribute to raising awareness about the victims of war, especially children. Such actions play a crucial role in conveying the message of peace socially, without forgetting the brutality of war.

Personal Comment

In my view, the significance of this five-body prostration goes beyond merely opposing war; it lies in the effort to deeply understand and empathize with the pain caused by war. The message conveyed by the monks is not just a slogan but a call for deep reflection on the realities faced by the world. This applies not only to the warring parties but to all of us observing the situation.

Hidden Variables to Consider

What was emphasized in this event is not just the cessation of war but the need to eliminate the fundamental causes of war, such as greed and anger. Many focus only on the results of war, but without reflection on its causes, true peace cannot be achieved. Therefore, discussions on the structural issues that lead to war are also necessary.

Points to Check Now

  • The situation of civilian casualties in the Middle East and the need for humanitarian aid.
  • Social discussions on the causes of war and the resulting policy changes.
