High-Risk Driver License Fitness Test Period: Key Issues Explored

March 17, 2026 · 한겨레 · View source

Original article at a glance Title: High-risk driver 'driver's license fitness test' period shortened from 10 months to 5.5 months Source: Hankyoreh The National Police Agency and the Korea Road Traffic Authority announced on the 17th that the 'periodic fitness test' procedure for determining whether high-risk drivers can maintain their driver's licenses will be expedited starting this August. The 'periodic fitness test for driver's licenses' is a post-management system that assesses whether drivers with mental illnesses such as dementia or acquired physical disabilities can retain their licenses. Read the original article

Shortening the Driver's License Fitness Test Period for High-Risk Drivers: Why Is It Important?

According to a recent announcement by the National Police Agency and the Korea Road Traffic Authority, the period for periodic fitness tests for high-risk drivers will be reduced from the previous 10 months to 5.5 months. This measure aims to quickly assess the license retention of drivers with mental illnesses such as dementia or acquired physical disabilities. In particular, the recent increase in traffic accidents involving drivers with dementia has raised the need for system improvements, prompting the National Police Agency to simplify the testing process through legislative amendments.

These changes are expected to contribute not only to the reduction of administrative procedures but also to achieving the important social goal of preventing traffic accidents. In other words, there is an intention to improve the situation where high-risk drivers maintain their licenses for extended periods, thereby ensuring traffic safety.

Key Points

  • The driver's license fitness test period for high-risk drivers is shortened from 10 months to 5.5 months.
  • The notification cycle for periodic fitness test subjects is changed from quarterly to monthly.
  • No additional testing period will be granted if the driver does not respond to the test.
  • Legislative amendments are scheduled to take effect on August 1.
  • The main purpose is to strengthen the management of high-risk drivers to prevent traffic accidents.

Why the Background Matters

This measure is closely related to the recent increase in traffic accidents involving drivers with dementia. When drivers with mental illnesses such as dementia cause accidents, the repercussions are felt by society as a whole. The previous testing procedures were too lengthy, allowing high-risk drivers to maintain their licenses, which raised concerns. Therefore, the legislative amendment can be seen as a step to address this issue.

Why It Matters

This measure is not just about improving administrative efficiency. By ensuring proper management of high-risk drivers, it can contribute to preventing traffic accidents and reducing social costs. Especially as the elderly population increases, leading to a rise in drivers with conditions such as dementia, this system improvement is considered an essential measure for traffic safety.

Explanation for Practitioners and General Readers

Practitioners involved with driver's licenses should prepare for the faster testing procedures that will come into effect with this amendment. Additionally, general readers can view the strengthened management of high-risk drivers positively, as it may better protect their safety and that of their families. However, the actual effectiveness of these changes remains to be seen.

Points to Note

In my view, the actual implementation of this amendment is crucial. There have been past system improvements, but often their effectiveness in the field has been lacking. Therefore, monitoring whether this system will genuinely contribute to preventing traffic accidents is necessary.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One aspect missing from this amendment is the definition and criteria for high-risk drivers. If clear criteria for selecting high-risk drivers are not provided, problems may still arise. Additionally, concerns about personnel shortages or budget issues during the testing process are also noteworthy.

Current Checkpoints

  • How will the definition and selection criteria for high-risk drivers be established?
  • How will the actual testing procedures proceed after the amendment takes effect?
  • How will statistical changes regarding traffic accident prevention manifest?

FAQ

Q: In what cases is the periodic fitness test conducted?
A: It targets drivers with conditions such as dementia, physical disabilities, or mental illnesses, who will be notified by relevant authorities.

Q: When will the shortened testing period take effect?
A: It will be implemented starting August 1, 2023.

Q: What penalties exist for not responding to the test?
A: If a driver does not respond to the test, no additional testing period will be granted, and measures such as license cancellation may be taken.
