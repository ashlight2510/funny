"Food Spots to Check Out Near Gwanghwamun Ahead of BTS Comeback"

March 17, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Food and Drug Administration Inspects 2100 Restaurants in Gwanghwamun Area Ahead of BTS Comeback Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) will conduct pre-inspections of restaurants in downtown Seoul, expecting an influx of tourists for the BTS comeback event. The FDA announced on the 17th that it will carry out hygiene inspections and food poisoning prevention promotions at approximately 2100 restaurants near BTS comeback venues such as Gwanghwamun, Namdaemun, and Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) until the 21st.

Hygiene Inspections of Restaurants in Gwanghwamun Area Ahead of BTS Comeback

The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is set to conduct hygiene inspections at around 2100 restaurants in the Gwanghwamun area ahead of the BTS comeback concert. This measure aims to preemptively address potential issues such as food poisoning and price gouging that may arise due to the expected increase in tourists. The inspections will take place until the 21st, focusing on hygienic handling, compliance with consumption dates, and price labeling.

This action can be seen as the government's proactive response to minimize consumer harm, especially as BTS's concert is expected to attract a large number of tourists to downtown Seoul. In particular, hygiene management supplies will be distributed to restaurants near the event venue, and basic hygiene practices such as handwashing will be promoted to ensure safe food offerings.

  • Inspections will continue until the 21st and will expand to other concert venues afterward.

Reasons to Consider the Background

BTS enjoys immense popularity both domestically and internationally, and their comeback concert will draw many fans to Seoul. While restaurants in the Gwanghwamun area can expect increased revenue, concerns about hygiene issues and price gouging also rise. Considering this situation, the FDA is taking steps to protect consumers through pre-inspections. Similar issues have arisen during past large-scale events, making this preventive measure even more significant.

Why It Matters

This hygiene inspection goes beyond merely checking the sanitary conditions of restaurants. It is a crucial measure to ensure that BTS fans and tourists have a safe and enjoyable experience in Seoul. Additionally, it conveys a message that the government is actively working to protect consumers, increasing the likelihood of similar measures for future large-scale events.

Personal Comment

In reality, this inspection holds more significance than just a hygiene check. It not only ensures that consumers can eat safe food but also encourages restaurants to adhere to hygiene standards. However, it remains to be seen how thoroughly these inspections will be conducted.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The items addressed in this inspection are basic hygiene standards, but checks on service quality or pricing policies at restaurants are not included. Therefore, the possibility of price gouging at some restaurants cannot be entirely ruled out. Additionally, it is questionable whether inspections conducted during a peak tourist season will be effective.

Points to Check Now

  • Inspection Results: It is essential to closely monitor future inspection results and any consumer harm cases.
  • Tourist Experience: It is important to verify the actual experiences of tourists who attended the BTS concert, including service and pricing at restaurants.
