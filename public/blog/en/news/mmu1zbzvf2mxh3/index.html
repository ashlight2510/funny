Geosan-gun Opens Arborist Academy: A New Turning Point in Forest Management

Geosan-gun, Chungcheongbuk-do, has solidified its position as an Arborist Academy by opening the country's first indoor training center for arborist training. This facility, which was built with a project budget of 2 billion won, provides a space for training in advanced rope techniques, safety equipment operation, and tree management methods without being affected by climate. Since 2020, Geosan-gun has been operating an arborist training program and has produced 439 tree management experts to date. These individuals are actively involved in forest management and handling local complaints.

This news signifies more than just the opening of an educational facility. Geosan-gun is presenting a model that considers both local economic development and environmental protection through arborist training, which is being recognized as an important case nationwide.
