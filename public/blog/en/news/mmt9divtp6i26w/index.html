Rubio and Foreign Minister Cho Hyun Discuss Ensuring Security in Hormuz: What Questions Remain?

March 16, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article Overview Title: Rubio tells Foreign Minister Cho Hyun, "Cooperation of multiple countries is important for securing safety in Hormuz" Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun On the 16th, U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio had a phone call with Foreign Minister Cho Hyun, emphasizing that "to secure safety in the Strait of Hormuz in the long term and stabilize the global economy and international oil prices, cooperation among multiple countries is more important than ever, and we should communicate closely for this purpose," according to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. During the call, Rubio discussed the recent situation in the Middle East with Minister Cho. Read the original article

Secretary Rubio emphasizes the importance of securing safety in the Strait of Hormuz during call with Foreign Minister Cho Hyun

Recently, the phone call between U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Foreign Minister Cho Hyun reminded us once again of the need for international cooperation for safety and stability in the Middle East. Secretary Rubio highlighted that securing safety in the Strait of Hormuz is essential for stabilizing the global economy and international oil prices, mentioning the necessity of close cooperation among multiple countries. In response, Minister Cho stated that peace in the Middle East and the free navigation of the Strait of Hormuz are crucial for the security and economy of all countries, including South Korea.

This call was made at the request of the U.S. side, and the two ministers agreed to continue in-depth discussions on the situation in the Middle East and the Korea-U.S. relationship. In particular, Secretary Rubio requested South Korea's interest and support for peace in the Middle East, to which Minister Cho expressed gratitude for U.S. cooperation.

Why Background Matters

This call took place amid rising tensions in the Middle East. The Strait of Hormuz is a critical route for global oil transportation, and instability in this region can have direct impacts on international oil prices and the economy. Additionally, the request made by former President Trump for several countries to send vessels to the Strait of Hormuz reflects this tense situation.

Why It Matters

This call carries significance beyond a mere diplomatic conversation. Securing safety in the Strait of Hormuz can have a substantial impact on the economy and security of several countries, including South Korea. Given South Korea's high dependence on energy from the Middle East, stability in this region is crucial from an economic perspective. Therefore, recognizing the need for such international cooperation will be a key element of South Korea's diplomatic strategy.
