Accommodation and Experience Integrated Payment with Discounts... Full Overhaul of Jeonnam Tourism Platform

Jeollanam-do is significantly revamping its tourism platform by introducing an integrated payment system for accommodation and experience products, as well as expanding various discount benefits. This overhaul is a measure to enhance tourism accessibility in Jeonnam and maximize user convenience, with the new 'JN TOUR' app set to operate from April 1. The app features a one-stop solution for searching tourism information, making reservations, and processing payments, and notably includes a Google login feature for foreign tourists, enhancing global accessibility.

In addition, Jeonnam Province will provide benefits of up to 40,000 won off for payments over 100,000 won through the 'Namdo Accommodation Discount Big Event', with plans for future discount events on experience products as well. These changes are expected to have a positive impact on Jeonnam's tourism industry.

Key Summary

  • Jeonnam Province revamps the tourism platform with the introduction of the 'JN TOUR' app and an integrated payment system.
  • Expanded accommodation discount benefits: up to 40,000 won off based on payment amount.
  • Google login feature added for foreign tourists.
  • Future discount events for experience products planned, with 1+1 events at various tourist sites.
  • Enhanced user convenience to strengthen accessibility to Jeonnam tourism.

Key Scenes

  • Integrated Payment System: Ability to book and pay for accommodation and experience products at once.
  • Discount Benefits: Tiered discounts based on payment amount, with additional discounts for supporter members.
  • User Convenience: Simplified login procedures and fast page transitions.
  • Consolidated Tourism Information: Easy access to information about tourist attractions, dining, and accommodation.
  • Global Accessibility: Added features for foreign tourists.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The background for Jeollanam-do's overhaul of its tourism platform stems from recent changes in the tourism industry. With increasing travel demand following COVID-19, efforts to revitalize local tourism have become necessary. Jeonnam boasts beautiful nature and diverse tourist attractions, but there have been many complaints about inconvenient access to information and booking systems. To address these issues, Jeonnam Province has revamped the app and introduced an integrated payment system.

Why It Matters

This overhaul is expected to have a positive impact on Jeonnam's tourism industry. As tourists find it easier to search for information and make reservations, the number of visitors to Jeonnam is likely to increase. This can contribute to revitalizing the local economy. In particular, discount benefits will play a crucial role in attracting tourists.

FAQ

A1: The discount benefits will take effect from April 1.

A2: Discounts are available for residents outside of Jeonnam and Gwangju, with tiered discounts based on payment amounts.
