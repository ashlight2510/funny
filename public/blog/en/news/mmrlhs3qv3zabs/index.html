The Impact of Vitamin D on Blood Pressure and Recommended Intake

March 15, 2026

The Effect of Vitamin D on Blood Pressure and Required Intake

These days, there are many days when it's hard to get sunlight, right? This makes it easy to become deficient in vitamin D, and many people are curious about how this affects blood pressure. So today, I've summarized the relationship between vitamin D and blood pressure, as well as how much we should be consuming.

What is Vitamin D?

The Role of Vitamin D

Vitamin D plays several important roles in our body. It is essential for maintaining bone health by helping with the absorption of calcium and phosphorus. I have experienced a deficiency in vitamin D, and I felt a dull ache in my bones. This made me realize even more how important vitamin D is.

The Process of Vitamin D Production

Vitamin D can be naturally produced in the skin through sunlight. Therefore, it seems to be particularly deficient in winter. I also have had experiences where my vitamin D levels dropped as I went out less in winter. So nowadays, I consciously try to get some sunlight.

The Relationship Between Vitamin D and Blood Pressure

Vitamin D Deficiency and Hypertension

According to research, a deficiency in vitamin D increases the risk of hypertension. This is because vitamin D plays a crucial role in vascular health. While learning about hypertension, I found it fascinating that vitamin D deficiency can lead to inflammation of the blood vessels.

The Effects of Vitamin D Supplementation

Adequate intake of vitamin D can help lower blood pressure. From my experience taking vitamin D supplements, I felt my blood pressure stabilize a bit. Of course, individual differences exist, so it may not have the same effect on everyone, but I think it's worth trying.

Required Intake of Vitamin D

Recommended Intake

Generally, adults need about 600 to 800 IU of vitamin D per day. However, this can vary depending on individual circumstances, so in my case, I consulted with a doctor to determine the appropriate amount. I initially started with 600 IU but adjusted it as I felt I needed more.

Methods of Vitamin D Intake

There are various ways to consume vitamin D. Getting sunlight is good, and you can naturally intake it through foods like eel, salmon, and egg yolks. Personally, I found that taking supplements alongside food was the most efficient, especially in winter when sunlight is scarce and food alone may not be sufficient.

Conclusion

Vitamin D is an important nutrient that can have a positive impact on blood pressure. Therefore, adequate intake is necessary. If you have any questions or want to know more, feel free to leave a comment! I think starting with this would be great. Have a healthy day!
