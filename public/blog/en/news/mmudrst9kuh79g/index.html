Season of Growth, Blooming Flowers of Learning: Enrollment for Spring Semester 2026 at Wonkwang Digital University Lifelong Education Center

The Lifelong Education Center of Wonkwang Digital University will begin recruiting new students for the 2026 Spring Semester from March 23 to April 26. This spring semester will offer over 30 courses in various fields, including traditional medicine health, yoga meditation, and tea culture, characterized by programs that enhance practicality and professionalism. In particular, the number of high-quality free courses provided through government support projects will be expanded to broaden learning opportunities in the community.

Additionally, courses aligned with the latest trends, such as AI literacy, marketing, and AI utilization for office work, will be offered, providing opportunities to respond to changes in the times. Director Yoo Sook-hee emphasized that learning is an essential element that enriches individual lives and stated that the center will support students' passion to lead to professional achievements.

  • New AI-related courses will be introduced to respond to the latest trends.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This recruitment comes at a time when the importance of lifelong education is growing. Especially after COVID-19, with the activation of non-face-to-face education and online learning, people are seeking various educational opportunities for better self-development and improvement of job skills. Wonkwang Digital University aims to meet this demand by providing practical and professional educational programs.
