7 Must-Visit Popular Hotels in Southeast Asia Travel

When planning a trip, choosing accommodation seems to be the most challenging part. I remember feeling overwhelmed when I said I was going to Southeast Asia. Especially since the place you stay can greatly influence the mood of your trip, it's essential to choose wisely. So this time, I’ve compiled a list of popular hotels in Southeast Asia that you shouldn't miss. Let's find out which hotels are on the list!

1. Bangkok: Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok

Harmony of Tradition and Modernity

If you visit Bangkok, you must check out the Mandarin Oriental. This hotel beautifully blends traditional Thai architecture with modern amenities. I still vividly remember having breakfast in my room while enjoying a stunning view of the Mekong River. The spa service was particularly excellent, perfect for relieving travel fatigue.

Importance of Location

Additionally, this hotel is located in the city center, providing excellent access to major tourist attractions. It's convenient to use buses or the subway, making it easy to get around via public transportation. It seems like the perfect place to fully experience the charm of Bangkok.

2. Bali: Kamasana Resort in Ubud

Healing in Nature

Kamasana Resort, located in the Ubud area of Bali, is a place where you can heal in nature. Nestled in the forest, it is incredibly quiet and peaceful. During my stay, I started each day with a yoga class, which was truly wonderful. Practicing yoga with the sounds of nature was a special experience.

Experience the Charm of Ubud

Moreover, this place is close to the Ubud market and various cultural experiences, allowing you to enjoy Bali's traditional culture as well. If you want to fully enjoy the scenery of Bali, I recommend this place!

3. Da Nang: InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula

Luxurious Sea View

One of the most popular hotels in Da Nang is the InterContinental Danang. This hotel allows you to spend luxurious time overlooking the sea. The sunset viewed from the infinity pool is truly spectacular. I spent the whole day relaxing while listening to the sound of the sea.

Attractive Facilities

Additionally, there are various restaurants and bars where you can enjoy delicious food. There’s a restaurant that specializes in seafood dishes. It's one of the must-visit spots during your trip to Da Nang!

4. Kota Kinabalu: Shangri-La Tanjung Aru Resort

Luxury in Harmony with Nature

Shangri-La Tanjung Aru Resort in Kota Kinabalu offers beautiful views of the sea and mountains. A day spent here felt like a dream. The swimming pool within the resort is stunning, and you can feel the sea right in front of you. Personally, I loved taking evening walks along the beach the most.

Variety of Activities

You can also enjoy a variety of activities here. You can experience marine sports like snorkeling and diving, making it a perfect choice for those who love activities.

Conclusion

The popular hotels in Southeast Asia each have their unique charm. All the hotels I experienced left me with special memories, and I hope this article helps you in choosing your accommodation. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment, and I'll be happy to answer! I hope you have a wonderful time at one of these hotels!
