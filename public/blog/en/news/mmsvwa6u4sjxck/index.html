Rude Moment at the Academy Awards: Lee Jae's Speech Cut Short

Recently, the Korean K-pop song 'Golden' won the Best Original Song award at the 98th Academy Awards, generating significant buzz. However, controversy erupted when the acceptance speech of winner Lee Jae was abruptly cut off. Lee mentioned his childhood experiences of being teased for liking K-pop and aimed to convey a message of resilience rather than mere success. However, before he could finish his speech, music began to play, interrupting his remarks, which has been criticized as a rude attitude from the Academy.

This incident has evolved from a mere awards ceremony episode into an important issue revealing discrimination and unfairness in a cultural context.

This incident is not just a moment from the Academy Awards; it occurred amidst the rising global popularity of K-pop.
