Kim Jong-un replies to Xi Jinping, "I am pleased that the North Korea-China relationship has reached a new level."

Recently, Kim Jong-un, Chairman of the State Affairs Commission of North Korea, expressed a positive stance on the development of North Korea-China relations in a reply sent to Chinese President Xi Jinping. He conveyed his pleasure that the traditional North Korea-China relationship is moving to a new stage and expressed deep gratitude for Xi's congratulatory message. This reflects a commitment to further deepen friendly cooperation between the two countries based on the agreements reached during last year's meetings between the two leaders.

This statement suggests that the relationship between North Korea and China is entering a new phase of cooperation, moving beyond past tensions. Through this, both countries emphasize the importance of mutual support while sharing socialist ideologies. This change raises important questions about how the positions of both countries in the international community may evolve.

Key Summary

  • Kim Jong-un expresses joy to President Xi about the new stage of North Korea-China relations.
  • A commitment to deepen friendly cooperation based on significant agreements from last September's summit.
  • Emphasizes gratitude for Xi's congratulatory message and the unwavering support between the two countries.

Key Highlights

  • Development of North Korea-China relations: Kim Jong-un emphasized that the traditional relationship is moving to a new stage.
  • Cooperation between parties: The two countries are strengthening mutual support centered around socialist ideologies.
  • Communication between leaders: Agreements reached during last year's summit are influencing the current relationship.
  • China's role: President Xi is closely monitoring changes in North Korea and values the development of the bilateral relationship.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The relationship between Kim Jong-un and Xi Jinping has experienced several tensions and conflicts over the years. Particularly, North Korea's nuclear development and international sanctions have complicated the relationship between the two countries. However, since the summit in Beijing last September, both countries have been moving towards strengthening mutual support and cooperation. This change significantly impacts North Korea's diplomatic strategy, with China positioning itself as an important partner supporting North Korea's stability and development.

Why It Matters

This statement is significant as it indicates that the diplomatic relationship between North Korea and China is evolving beyond a mere alliance to a stage of cooperation that considers each other's interests. This could contribute to strengthening both countries' positions in the international community and laying the groundwork for strategic negotiations with the United States. Especially amid North Korea's economic difficulties, China's support is essential, which could also affect neighboring countries, including South Korea and the United States.

Points to Watch

In my view, the most crucial point in Kim Jong-un's statement is the potential for the relationship between the two countries to expand beyond a political alliance into economic cooperation. In reality, North Korea requires economic support, and China can further strengthen its influence through this. Therefore, this change is likely to lead to actual actions rather than remaining a mere diplomatic declaration.

Hidden Variables to Consider

An aspect that may be overlooked in this article is North Korea's internal situation. The North Korean economy remains in a difficult state, and it is uncertain how these diplomatic changes will actually impact the lives of its citizens. Additionally, if China's support leads to the strengthening of North Korea's regime, it could act as a tension factor in the international community.

Current Points to Check

  • Attention should be paid to the strengthening of economic cooperation between North Korea and China.
  • There is a need to analyze the impact of the diplomatic developments of both countries on the international community.
  • It is important to continuously monitor North Korea's internal situation and the international community's reactions.
