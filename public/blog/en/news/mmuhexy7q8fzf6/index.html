‘Han River Belt’ Seongdong-gu Official Price Soars 29%... 14 Times Higher than Dobong-gu

The rapid increase in official apartment prices in Seoul starkly highlights the disparities among autonomous districts. In particular, Seongdong-gu recorded a high increase rate of 29.04%, showing a 14-fold difference compared to Dobong-gu's 2.07% increase. The official prices in the three districts of Gangnam and the Han River Belt area also rose by over 20%, deepening the polarization of the real estate market in Seoul. These changes raise concerns about serious financial burdens for the elderly and low-income groups.

This increase in official prices carries significance beyond mere numbers. It exacerbates the imbalance in the real estate market and can lead to a concentration of tax burdens on specific classes. In particular, owners of high-priced homes are likely to face increased tax burdens, leading to greater tax resistance.

Key Highlights

  • Seongdong-gu's official price increase rate: 29.04%, 14 times that of Dobong-gu
  • Surge in official prices in the Gangnam three districts and Han River Belt area
  • Concerns over increased tax burdens for low-income and elderly groups
  • Potential for increased tax resistance and financial burdens
  • Growing dissatisfaction due to the time lag between official prices and actual home prices

Reasons to Consider the Background

The rise in official prices in Seoul is concentrated in specific areas, particularly Gangnam and the Han River Belt. This trend shows an even steeper increase compared to 2021, resulting in a concentration of market demand in certain regions. The rise in official prices is also linked to changes in government policy, with increased tax burdens on high-priced homes directly impacting low-income and elderly groups.

Why It Matters

This increase in official prices signifies more than just numbers. As polarization in the real estate market deepens, tax burdens on specific classes will increase. This could further exacerbate social inequality and lead to greater tax resistance. In particular, the elderly may face serious impacts on their livelihoods as tax burdens increase in a situation where they have limited fixed income.

Personal Observations

In my view, this increase in official prices is not just a change in real estate values but a significant signal that deepens social inequality. As the tax burden on high-priced home owners increases, the likelihood of them raising rents also grows. This could ultimately lead to a burden being passed on to the homeless and low-income classes. Therefore, this issue appears to require social discussion beyond just a real estate issue.

Hidden Variables to Consider

An important variable not mentioned in the article is the time lag between the criteria for assessing official prices and actual market prices. Currently, the market is experiencing a downward trend in home prices due to loan regulations, but taxes are assessed based on the latter half of last year, which could lead to increased dissatisfaction. This discrepancy may further fuel tax resistance.

Points to Check Now

  • Check the difference between official prices and actual home prices.
  • Pay attention to how the increase in property tax affects rental prices.
  • Observe how the financial burdens on the elderly and low-income groups change.

FAQ

Q: Why did Seongdong-gu's official price increase so rapidly?
A: Areas like Seongdong-gu in the Han River Belt are densely populated with high-priced homes, and concentrated demand has led to a rapid increase in official prices.

Q: What impact does this official price increase have on the elderly?
A: The elderly often have limited fixed income, so an increase in tax burdens can seriously affect their livelihoods.

Q: Why is there a high likelihood of tax resistance?
A: As official prices rise and tax burdens increase, discrepancies with actual home price declines can lead to dissatisfaction.

Q: What is the outlook for the real estate market going forward?
A: With tax resistance and an increase in urgent sales in the Gangnam area alongside supply shortages in outer districts, market uncertainty is expected to grow.
