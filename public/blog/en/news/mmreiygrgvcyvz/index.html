Kimchi Storage Temperature and Methods: Let's Enjoy It Properly

If you love kimchi, you probably know that if it's not stored properly, it can easily change in taste or spoil. There are times when it can be overwhelming to figure out how to store such precious kimchi. So, I've organized information about the temperature and methods for storing kimchi. By protecting kimchi in the right way, you can enjoy it even more deliciously!

Basic Principles of Kimchi Storage

What is the Ideal Temperature?

The storage temperature for kimchi is really important. Personally, I think the best range is between 0 to 5 degrees Celsius. Storing it at this temperature allows fermentation to proceed slowly, minimizing changes in taste. If you're storing it in a regular refrigerator, it's best to place it in the coldest spot. I once stored kimchi in a regular fridge and it tasted strange, which is when I realized this!

Choosing a Kimchi Container

The container you use for storing kimchi also plays a role. It's good to use a kimchi-specific container, and airtight containers are also fine. Since air can cause kimchi to spoil quickly, it's important to minimize air exposure. I prefer storing it in glass containers because I can easily check the condition of the kimchi later. Plus, it seems that glass containers absorb less of the kimchi smell.

Points to Note When Storing Kimchi

Varies by Type of Kimchi

The storage method for kimchi varies depending on the type. For example, aged kimchi is better stored at a lower temperature, while freshly made kimchi should be kept at a slightly warmer temperature to promote fermentation. By storing them differently, I've been able to appreciate the taste of kimchi even more. In particular, my favorite baek-kimchi tastes much better when stored in a slightly warmer place.

Check Regularly

It's also important to check kimchi periodically when storing it. Especially during the fermentation process, the taste can change, so it's good to taste it occasionally and adjust the storage temperature as needed. I tend to open my kimchi container once a week, and each time I discover new flavors. This little effort makes it possible to enjoy kimchi even more deliciously.

Conclusion

Now that I know how to store kimchi properly, I think I'll be able to enjoy it more deliciously in the future. By adhering to the appropriate temperature and methods, I can preserve the taste and nutrition of kimchi for a long time, right? If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! Now would be a great time to start with kimchi storage.
