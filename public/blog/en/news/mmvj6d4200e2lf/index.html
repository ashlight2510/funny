Chungbuk Eumseong-gun, Promoting Local Root Industries

Chungbuk Eumseong-gun is launching a full-scale promotion project to strengthen the roots of local manufacturing industries. Recently, Eumseong-gun was selected for the 2026 Local Industry Customized Job Creation Support Project organized by Chungbuk Province, securing a budget of 1.6 billion won. This budget will be used to create new jobs and support long-term employees through the ‘Coexistence Job Project’. In particular, while Eumseong-gun's manufacturing sector is primarily focused on metal processing, rubber, and plastics, there is a growing trend of young people avoiding small businesses. Consequently, Eumseong-gun is preparing various support measures to alleviate employment instability and improve worker welfare.

These projects aim not only to create jobs but also to establish a sustainable growth model for the local economy and a job ecosystem. An official from Eumseong-gun emphasized that this project will contribute to revitalizing the local economy by creating an environment where workers and businesses can coexist.

Key Summary

  • Eumseong-gun is promoting the ‘Coexistence Job Project’ to foster local manufacturing industries.
  • Selected for the 2026 Local Industry Customized Job Creation Support Project, securing 1.6 billion won.
  • Plans to support the creation of 40 new jobs and 40 long-term employees (7 years or more).
  • Aims to enhance worker welfare and strengthen corporate competitiveness.
  • Addresses the trend of young people avoiding small businesses and employment instability.

Key Highlights

  • Budget Secured: Eumseong-gun to proceed with the project using 1.6 billion won.
  • Job Creation: Support for 40 new hires and 40 long-term employees.
  • Welfare Support: Various welfare programs such as health check-ups and vaccinations provided.
  • Support for Small Businesses: Support targeted at businesses that create at least one new job.
  • Economic Revitalization: Aiming to strengthen the competitiveness of local manufacturing and stabilize jobs.

Why Background Matters

This project stems from serious concerns about the declining competitiveness of root industries, which form the foundation of local manufacturing, and the increasing turnover rate among young workers. Recent employment insurance statistics indicate that the turnover and resignation rates in root industries are higher than the rate of new hires, which acts as a hindrance to the sustainable development of local industries. In this context, Eumseong-gun has prepared various measures to create an environment where businesses and workers can thrive together.

Why It Matters

This project could serve as an important stepping stone for sustainable growth in the local economy, beyond just job creation. If these efforts are successful in Eumseong-gun, where manufacturing is the backbone of the local economy, there is a high likelihood that similar models will be adopted in other regions. This could have a positive impact on the overall local economy.

Noteworthy Points

In my view, the most noteworthy aspect of this project is the proactive approach to worker welfare. It goes beyond merely creating jobs, as it includes various programs aimed at improving the quality of life for workers, which will contribute to strengthening corporate competitiveness in the long run. In fact, as worker satisfaction increases, turnover rates decrease, leading to greater stability for businesses.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One limitation of this project may be the restricted support for small businesses. In Eumseong-gun, where many small businesses employ fewer than 50 people, it remains uncertain whether all companies will be able to receive support. Therefore, detailed discussions on the selection criteria for supported companies and the methods of support will be necessary.

Current Points to Check

  • Project Progress: Attention should be paid to how Eumseong-gun's support will be implemented in practice.
  • Youth Response: It will be important to monitor how young workers respond to this project, especially regarding changes in their turnover rates if job creation occurs.

FAQ

Q: When will this project start?
A: The project is scheduled to start next month.

Q: How will the support be provided?
A: Welfare support programs and retention bonuses will be provided to companies that create new jobs.

Q: Who are the main beneficiaries of this project?
A: The main beneficiaries are newly hired workers and long-term employees.

Q: Are similar projects being conducted in other regions outside Eumseong-gun?
A: Currently, Eumseong-gun is leading the initiative, but if it becomes a successful model, there is a possibility that similar projects will be introduced in other regions.

This project in Eumseong-gun could become a significant milestone for revitalizing the local economy. It is necessary to closely monitor the progress going forward.
