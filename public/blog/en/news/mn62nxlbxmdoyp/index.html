“So Ji-sub and Bae Doona's voices to ensure Hind's voice is not lost”···Narration solidarity in documentary about Palestinian girl

Recently, the documentary <Hind's Voice>, which tells the story of a Palestinian girl, Hind, is being introduced to Korean audiences. This work deals with the survival story of Hind Rajab, who lost her family due to an attack by the Israeli military, and actors So Ji-sub and Bae Doona are participating in the narration, drawing attention. They are voicing the Palestinian issue, which is deeply related to human rights issues that are becoming a topic in Korean society.

The documentary is scheduled to be released in January 2024, and the participation of So Ji-sub and Bae Doona is expected to play an important role in raising awareness about Palestine. In particular, So Ji-sub emphasized the importance of this work with the message, "I add my voice so that Hind's voice does not disappear." Alongside this, soccer player Son Heung-min is also voicing concerns about the hunger issue in Palestine, contributing to raising awareness of Palestinian issues within Korea.
