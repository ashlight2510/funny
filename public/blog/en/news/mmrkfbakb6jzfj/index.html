Best Hotels in Bali: Perfect Stays in a Dream Destination

March 15, 2026

Best Hotels in Bali! Perfect Stay in a Dreamy Resort

One of the biggest concerns before going on a trip is choosing accommodation. This is especially true in beautiful resorts like Bali. I was at a loss about which hotel would be good and what views would be stunning, so I organized the best hotels I experienced while staying in Bali. I hope this helps with your travel plans!

1. Accommodation Surrounded by Nature in Ubud

Terrace Resort Ubud

Ubud is a place where you can fully enjoy the nature of Bali. Personally, the Terrace Resort where I stayed here left the most memorable impression. The tranquility felt in the forest where the resort is located is truly healing. In the morning, you can wake up to the sound of birds and head to the pool for a swim. The breakfast here offers fresh fruits and local dishes, and it was really delicious!

What I loved most about this place was the spa service. You can enjoy a massage while admiring the surrounding scenery, and it feels like all your stress just melts away. I highly recommend experiencing this when you visit Ubud!

2. Luxurious Retreat in Seminyak

The Legian Bali

Seminyak is one of Bali's hot spots, and among them, The Legian boasts a luxurious atmosphere. Its close proximity to the sea makes the sunset on the beach truly spectacular. In particular, the view of the ocean from the pool is a scene that you wouldn't want to miss capturing in a photo.

Here, I enjoyed a cocktail while looking at the sea, which felt like the essence of vacation. However, the prices are a bit on the higher side, so I recommend considering your budget carefully. But for a special occasion, it would be a really great choice!

3. Family Travel Recommendation in Nusa Dua

Grand Hyatt Bali

If you're traveling with family, I recommend the Grand Hyatt Bali. This place has a large pool and various facilities that are perfect for spending time with kids. Not only is there a pool, but the kids' club is well-equipped, allowing children to play safely. Thanks to this, I have a memorable experience where the parents could relax comfortably.

Additionally, the restaurants here offer a variety of foods, and there are menus available for kids as well. It was great to have meals that satisfied the whole family. If you're planning a family trip in Nusa Dua, I think this place would be the best!

4. Optimized Accommodation for Short Trips

Batur Resort

If you're planning a short trip to Bali, I recommend Batur Resort. The location is really good, and it's close to tourist attractions, making it convenient to get around. Especially, being able to see the famous Batur volcano up close is really appealing.

The view of the volcano from the room was impressive. Waking up early to enjoy hiking and then leisurely having brunch at the resort is also a great way to spend your time. Despite the short itinerary, I had a fulfilling experience where I could relax adequately.

To ensure a perfect stay in Bali, it's important to choose a hotel that matches your preferences and travel style. I hope the hotels I recommended will help with your travel plans. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! Let's think about where to go for the next trip!
