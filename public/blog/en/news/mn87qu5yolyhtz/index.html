New intercity bus route connecting Taebaek, Gangwon to Gangnam Express Bus Terminal, Seoul

The city of Taebaek in Gangwon Province has announced that it will establish a new intercity bus route connecting Taebaek and Gangnam, Seoul, starting April 10. This route will operate from the Taebaek Intercity Bus Terminal, passing through the Jeongseon Gohan area, and will run three round trips a day. The fare is set at 35,000 won from Taebaek and 31,400 won from Gohan. The establishment of this route is part of efforts to improve accessibility to the southern metropolitan area and to supplement the limitations of transportation infrastructure in the mining area.

This decision is expected to significantly enhance the mobility of local residents, providing a new alternative for citizens who have experienced inconvenience due to the previous focus on routes centered around the Dongseoul Terminal.

Key points

  • Route opening date: Intercity bus service between Taebaek and Gangnam starts on April 10.
  • Operating route: Taebaek Intercity Bus Terminal → Jeongseon Gohan → Gangnam Express Bus Terminal.
  • Frequency: Three round trips per day.
  • Fare: 35,000 won from Taebaek, 31,400 won from Gohan.
  • Purpose: To supplement transportation infrastructure in mining areas and improve accessibility to the metropolitan area.

Reasons to consider the background

The establishment of this intercity bus route is part of Taebaek's efforts to strengthen transportation connectivity with the metropolitan area. In the past, residents faced inconvenience as they mostly had to go to the Dongseoul Terminal when traveling from Taebaek to Seoul, which acted as a constraint on their mobility. Especially in the mining area of Taebaek, where economic difficulties and a lack of transportation infrastructure exist, this new route is expected to contribute to revitalizing the local economy.

Why is it important?

The establishment of this route is significant not only for providing convenience in mobility for local residents but also for strengthening the economic and social connectivity between Taebaek and Seoul. With improved access to the metropolitan area, opportunities for tourism and business in the Taebaek region may expand, likely having a positive impact on the local economy.

Points to note

In my view, the greatest advantage of this new route is that it allows Taebaek citizens to move more easily to the metropolitan area. For example, Taebaek natives working in Seoul will be able to visit their hometown more quickly and conveniently on weekends. This could further strengthen the bonds within the local community.

Hidden variables to consider

However, it cannot be assumed that this new route will solve all problems. For instance, issues such as traffic congestion between Taebaek and Seoul and the sustainability of route operations still need to be addressed. Additionally, the relatively high fare could pose an economic burden that should be taken into account.

Points to check now

  • Start date: April 10; it is advisable to check for reservations and information before the route begins operation.
  • Fare comparison: It is necessary to compare the fare differences with the existing Dongseoul Terminal route and examine the economic benefits.
  • Bus timetable: It is recommended to check the bus timetable in advance to prepare for planned travel.
