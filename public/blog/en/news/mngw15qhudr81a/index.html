Coupang also displays ‘100 won per 100g’... Implementation of unit price display system for online shopping malls

Price transparency in online shopping malls will be significantly enhanced. The Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy announced that starting from the 7th, it will implement the unit price display system for online shopping malls with annual transaction amounts exceeding 10 trillion won. This system aims to allow consumers to check product prices based on unit standards, making price comparisons easier and encouraging rational consumption. Coupang and Naver Plus Store are the main targets of this system, and they are required to display unit prices for 114 essential items.

The implementation of this system reflects the government's efforts to strengthen consumer protection amid concerns over rising prices. Consumers will now be able to make purchasing decisions based on better information.

Key Summary

  • The Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy introduces the unit price display system for online shopping malls.
  • Coupang and Naver Plus Store are the main targets.
  • Mandatory unit price display for 114 essential items.
  • The goal is to enhance consumer choice and comparison.
  • Consumer protection measures in response to concerns over rising prices.

Key Highlights

  • Introduction of the unit price display system: Consumers can check product prices based on unit standards.
  • Targeted entities: Online shopping malls with annual transaction amounts exceeding 10 trillion won.
  • Mandatory display items: 114 types of essential goods, including processed and fresh foods.
  • Strengthening consumer protection: Increased ease of price comparison encourages rational consumption.
  • Trial period: A six-month trial period before full implementation.

Why Background Matters

This issue arises as the government steps in to protect consumers amid growing concerns over rising prices. By expanding the unit price display system, which was previously only implemented in offline stores, consumers will gain access to more transparent price information. This has become increasingly necessary in light of recent price increases and heightened consumer price sensitivity.

Why It Matters

The introduction of this system helps consumers make better choices. As price comparisons become easier, consumers will be able to make more rational purchasing decisions. Additionally, this could lead to online shopping malls enhancing their price competitiveness. Ultimately, this structure can have a positive impact on both consumers and sellers.

Points to Note

In my view, this system will not only benefit consumers but will also significantly impact sellers. As price comparisons become easier, consumers will be able to find cheaper alternatives more readily. Therefore, sellers will need to be more cautious in setting their prices.

Hidden Variables to Consider

However, this system does not solve all problems. Even with mandatory unit price displays, consumers still need to consider factors beyond price. For instance, quality or shipping costs may add to the total, making it difficult to make a perfect purchasing decision based solely on price comparisons.

Points to Check Now

Consumers will now be able to make better choices through price comparisons on products with mandatory unit price displays. Therefore, it is important to check unit prices when shopping in the future and practice rational consumption based on this information.
