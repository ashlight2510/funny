Southeast Asia Island Travel: The Most Beautiful Beaches and Recommended Accommodations List

When planning a trip, it can often be overwhelming to decide where to go, right? Especially in Southeast Asia, there are so many beautiful islands that it's hard to choose. So, I’ve compiled a list of the most beautiful beaches and recommended accommodations from the islands I’ve visited. I hope this article helps you find a wonderful travel destination!

1. Pattaya, Thailand: Glamorous Beaches and Various Activities

Beautiful Beaches

Pattaya is a world-famous beach destination, especially the 'Jomtien Beach,' which is truly beautiful. Watching the sunset while enjoying delicious seafood by the beach makes you feel really happy. When I visited, the sea was clear, and the beach was clean, which was great.

Recommended Accommodation

I recommend the 'Centara Grand Mirage Beach Resort' in Pattaya. It's great for families, and the pool is large, perfect for playing with kids. When I stayed there, the staff were friendly, and the breakfast was delicious, so I was very satisfied.

2. Bali, Indonesia: Paradise-like Beaches

Beautiful Beaches

Bali is truly a paradise. The sunrise viewed from 'Tegallalang Rice Terrace' in the 'Ubud' area was an unforgettable experience. The harmony of the blue sky and green rice fields was really beautiful. Also, 'Kuta Beach' is a great place for surfing, so if you love water activities, I highly recommend it.

Recommended Accommodation

The 'The Legian Bali' where I stayed was close to the beach, allowing me to enjoy the sea anytime. Moreover, the sea view from the room was absolutely fantastic. The breakfast buffet was diverse, making every morning enjoyable.

3. Cebu, Philippines: Hidden Treasure-like Beaches

Beautiful Beaches

Cebu is truly a hidden treasure. The 'White Beach' on 'Malapascua Island' was particularly impressive. The white sand and transparent sea felt like a dream. When snorkeling and seeing various fish, I lost track of time.

Recommended Accommodation

'Malapascua Residence' is an affordable yet clean accommodation. The staff are well-versed in the local atmosphere and provided a lot of good information. Its proximity to the sea made it attractive, allowing me to enjoy the beach anytime.

Conclusion

Traveling to the islands of Southeast Asia is truly an enchanting experience. With a variety of beaches and accommodations, the options are broad, and above all, the nature there is really beautiful. I hope the beaches and accommodations I introduced are helpful. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! I hope you get to visit one of these destinations soon!
