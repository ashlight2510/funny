UK Rejects Request to Dispatch Ships to the Strait of Hormuz: Stance of Not Being Dragged into War

Recently, the UK has effectively rejected the US request to dispatch ships to the Strait of Hormuz. Prime Minister Keir Starmer stated in a press conference, "We will not be dragged into a larger war," clearly drawing a line regarding military intervention. This decision comes in the context of US President Donald Trump requesting several countries to participate in the defense operations of the Strait of Hormuz, with Germany also expressing opposition to military intervention. Prime Minister Starmer emphasized the need for legal grounds and added that caution is necessary when sending troops to dangerous areas.

This incident is more than just a military decision; it serves as an important case showing how European countries respond to US military pressure. In particular, the positions of the UK and Germany could trigger discussions about relations with NATO, international law, and the legitimacy of military intervention.
