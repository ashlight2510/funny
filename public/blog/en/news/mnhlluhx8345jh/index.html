Recently, an incident in Cheongju, Chungbuk, which began with a simple allegation of embezzlement of 3 drinks, has sparked various controversies as the café owner withdrew the lawsuit. Café owner A filed a lawsuit against part-time worker B, claiming that B took drinks on their way home, but later withdrew the lawsuit. This incident may not end as a mere act of misconduct within the café but could expand into discussions about trust issues between part-time workers and owners, as well as the working environment.

This incident reveals not just a simple embezzlement allegation, but also the rights of part-time workers and the realities of café operations. In particular, the background of the incident intertwines the relationship between the owner and the part-time worker, as well as the operational methods of franchise cafés, giving this incident deeper significance.

Key Summary

  • The café owner in Cheongju sued the part-time worker for embezzlement but later withdrew the lawsuit.
  • Part-time worker B claims that the drinks were to be discarded due to a manufacturing error.

  • The owners expressed their intention to apologize and mentioned issues regarding settlement with B.
