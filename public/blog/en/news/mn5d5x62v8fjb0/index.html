How to Reduce Photo and Video Size on iPhone

March 25, 2026

How to Reduce Photo and Video Size on iPhone

If you're an iPhone user, you've likely experienced the issue of photos and videos taking up space on your device. Solving this storage problem allows you to save more content and improve your device's performance. In this article, we will practically introduce various methods to reduce the size of photos and videos on your iPhone.

Why Should You Reduce Photo and Video Size?

Reducing the size of photos and videos is important for several reasons.

  • Save Storage Space: For example, if your 256GB iPhone has 100GB of photos and videos, reducing the size to 50GB frees up 50GB of space.

  • Improve Device Performance: With more available storage, your device operates more smoothly. It helps prevent slow app launch times due to lack of storage.

  • Easier Backup and Sharing: Reducing size makes cloud backups and file sharing easier. For instance, when backing up photos to iCloud, smaller sizes allow for more files to be stored.

Checkpoint

  • Check your current storage usage and assess the need to reduce size.

How to Reduce Photo Size on iPhone

1. Change Settings to Reduce Size

You can reduce the size of photos and videos by changing the default settings on your iPhone.

  • Use High Efficiency Format: Saving in HEIF (High Efficiency Image Format) and HEVC (High Efficiency Video Codec) formats can significantly reduce size. Go to Settings > Camera > Formats and select "High Efficiency."

  • Set Storage Options: Activating the 'Optimize Storage' option allows your iPhone to automatically store photos in the cloud, leaving only lower-resolution versions on the device. You can enable this option in Settings > Your Name > iCloud > Photos.

2. Edit and Delete Photos

Organizing and editing photos is also a good way to reduce size.

  • Delete Unnecessary Photos: Save space by organizing and deleting duplicates or blurry photos. For example, if you have multiple pictures of the same landscape, keep just one and delete the rest.

  • Use Editing Tools: Resize or apply filters to photos to reduce size. Try adjusting them easily in the iPhone's default Photos app.

Action Tip

  • Developing a habit of regularly organizing photos can help manage storage.

Utilize External Apps

You can use specialized apps to reduce the size of photos and videos.

  • Use Specialized Apps: Apps like 'Compress Photos' allow you to easily compress photos and videos. This app is user-friendly and quickly reduces size.

  • Use Cloud Services: Storing photos and videos in cloud services like Google Photos or Dropbox saves device space. These services also offer automatic backup features for convenience.

Checkpoint

  • Check reviews and ratings of the apps you intend to use to assess their reliability.

How to Reduce Video Size

1. Adjust Video Resolution

You can reduce size by adjusting video resolution.

  • Change Resolution Settings: Go to Settings > Camera > Record Video and change the resolution from 4K to 1080p to save space. 1080p still maintains good quality.

  • Adjust Frame Rate: Changing the frame rate from 60fps to 30fps reduces the size of the video. This setting can be changed in Settings > Camera > Record Video.

2. Edit Videos

Editing videos is another way to reduce size.

  • Remove Unnecessary Parts: Delete unnecessary clips from the video to reduce size. You can easily edit using the iPhone's built-in video editing features.

  • Use Compression Tools: Use apps like 'Video Compressor' to compress videos. This app allows you to compress multiple videos at once.

Action Tip

  • After editing videos, delete the original files to save additional space.

Size Reduction Checklist

  1. Activate High Efficiency Format
  2. Delete duplicates and unnecessary photos
  3. Adjust video resolution
  4. Download and utilize external apps
  5. Use cloud services

Points to Note

There are important considerations when reducing the size of photos and videos.

  • Quality Degradation: Be cautious as reducing size may lead to quality loss. This is especially true for high-resolution photos or videos, which may lose quality after compression.

  • Backup is Essential: Always back up important photos and videos before proceeding. Use cloud services or external storage to keep them safe.

  • Careful App Selection: Only use reliable apps to prevent data loss. Check reviews and ratings before downloading.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. Does reducing photo size on iPhone lower its quality?
    Yes, reducing size can lead to quality degradation. Be especially cautious when compressing high-resolution photos.

  2. Which apps are most effective for compressing photos and videos?
    Apps like 'Compress Photos' and 'Video Compressor' are popular among users. These apps are easy to use and effectively reduce size.

  3. How do I manage files stored in cloud services?
    To manage files in cloud services, download the respective app, log in, and you can organize and delete files. Google Photos and Dropbox offer features that make management easy.

Now you know how to reduce the size of photos and videos on your iPhone. Start by activating the high-efficiency format and deleting unnecessary photos. Even small changes can lead to significant effects.
