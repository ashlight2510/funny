The Voice of Non-Regular Workers for Wage System Improvement: What is the Social Response?

The improvement of the wage system for non-regular workers has emerged as a crucial issue in Korean society today. Non-regular workers face social inequality due to job insecurity and low wages, which ultimately has a negative impact on society as a whole. In this context, it is essential to listen to the voices of non-regular workers and reflect them in society.

The impact of non-regular workers' voices on society is greater than expected. Non-regular workers in the public sector are demanding improvements in the wage system through collective action, and this movement has the potential to bring about positive change. This article will specifically examine the current status of the wage system for non-regular workers, the need for improvement, and the resulting social response.

What are the Problems with the Current Wage System for Non-Regular Workers?

Currently, the wage level of non-regular workers is significantly lower than that of regular workers. For example, in 2022, the average wage of non-regular workers in Korea was only about 60% of that of regular workers. This wage gap is exacerbated by the type of employment, as non-regular workers are generally categorized into contract workers, part-time workers, and daily workers. They lack a stable employment environment, resulting in various rights and protections being absent in addition to low wages.

Checkpoints

  • Check Wage Differences: Compare the average wages of non-regular and regular workers.
  • Analyze Employment Types: Understand the employment types of non-regular workers such as contract, part-time, and daily workers, and investigate the wage and rights differences for each type.

The lack of rights and protections for non-regular workers is one of the main reasons they face discrimination in society. For instance, non-regular workers often receive disadvantages in severance pay, paid leave, and health insurance compared to regular workers. This structural inequality not only lowers the quality of life for non-regular workers but also exacerbates social inequality.

Why is Improving the Wage System for Non-Regular Workers Important?

Why is it important to improve the wage system for non-regular workers? First, it serves as a foundation for addressing social inequality. The low wages of non-regular workers hinder their economic stability, which in turn leads to economic imbalances in society as a whole. Second, securing economic stability improves individuals' quality of life and contributes to the overall development of society.

Action Tips

  • Policy Suggestions: Propose policies to establish fair wage-setting criteria by gathering the opinions of non-regular workers.
  • Continuous Efforts: Operate a continuous monitoring and feedback system to avoid settling for short-term solutions.

What is the Impact of Non-Regular Workers' Voices on Society?

The voices of non-regular workers have a significant impact on society. Their voices can lead to changes in social awareness and remind people of the importance of solidarity. For example, recent demands for wage increases by non-regular workers in the public sector have garnered widespread sympathy and led to social discussions. Such collective actions play a crucial role in enhancing the rights of non-regular workers.

Examples

  • Case of Collective Action: Research cases where non-regular workers in specific regions have protested collectively for wage increases.

The social response does not stop at mere discussions but can lead to actual policy changes. For instance, the government's response to demands for wage increases for non-regular workers can result in policy improvements, contributing to enhancing the quality of life for non-regular workers.

Actionable Checklist for Protecting Non-Regular Workers' Rights

A checklist for reflecting the voices of non-regular workers can provide a systematic approach. Here are actionable steps for protecting the rights of non-regular workers and improving the wage system:

  1. Establish methods for gathering the opinions of non-regular workers.
  2. Develop fair wage-setting criteria.
  3. Propose policies for protecting the rights of non-regular workers.
  4. Build social solidarity and support networks.
  5. Operate continuous monitoring and feedback systems.

Utilizing this checklist can effectively reflect the voices of non-regular workers and develop concrete measures for improving the wage system.
