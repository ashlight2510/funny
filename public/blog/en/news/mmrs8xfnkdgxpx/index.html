Prompt Engineering: How to Make the Most of ChatGPT

Sometimes when using ChatGPT, you might feel frustrated when you don't get the desired answer. Haven't we all experienced that at least once? You might wonder, "What did I do wrong?" So, this time, I've organized some thoughts on prompt engineering. I'll share how to use ChatGPT more effectively, mixed with my own experiences!

What is a Prompt and Why is it Important?

Definition of a Prompt

A prompt is simply a question or instruction given to AI. No matter what information you request, a well-written prompt is essential to get the desired answer. When I first used ChatGPT, I thought, "This should be a good question," and asked vaguely, but that led to irrelevant answers.

Importance of Prompts

The higher the quality of the prompt, the more accurate and useful the AI's responses will be. So, let me share some tips I've learned from experience. Asking specific questions is beneficial. For example, instead of asking, "Recommend a good movie," you could ask, "Is there a recommended action movie released in 2023?" to receive better answers.

Various Prompt Examples

Creating Specific Questions

In fact, it's not easy to write a perfect prompt from the start. That's why it's important to try multiple times. What I've found helpful is to change the way I ask questions.

  • Example 1: "Explain the theme of Alice in Wonderland."
  • Example 2: "Analyze Alice in Wonderland from a modern perspective."

By slightly varying your questions, you'll learn how ChatGPT responds and can obtain better answers.

Assigning Roles

Another tip is to assign a specific role to ChatGPT. For instance, if you say, "You are a historian. Explain the political system of ancient Rome," you'll notice that the responses become more in-depth and professional. Personally, I found this method quite useful!

Modifying and Experimenting with Prompts

Modifying Based on Feedback

After writing a prompt, if you're not satisfied with the AI's response, try modifying it based on feedback. If you feel that "this part is lacking," emphasize that part in your question. Doing this has helped me get better answers.

Experimenting with Various Approaches

Lastly, it's important to experiment with different prompts. There were times when I felt overwhelmed about what to write, but by approaching it in various ways, new ideas emerged.

Conclusion

I've shared how to use ChatGPT more effectively through prompt engineering. To summarize, you can elicit the desired answers by asking specific questions and assigning roles. If you have any questions, please leave them in the comments! This is a good starting point!
