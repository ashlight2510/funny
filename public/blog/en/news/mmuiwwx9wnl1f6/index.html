"President's Key Points on Climate Measures Amid Deteriorating Middle East Situation"

March 17, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: President Yoon “If the situation in the Middle East worsens, even a vehicle restriction system…” Climate Ministry “Reviewing from multiple angles” Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun President Yoon Suk-yeol stated on the 17th, “We need to prepare measures considering the possibility of the situation in the Middle East prolonging, even to the worst-case scenario,” and added, “If necessary, I hope early measures for demand reduction such as a vehicle restriction system or a 10-vehicle system will be established.” President Yoon referred to the additional budget proposal the government is preparing as a ‘war supplementary budget’ and urged the government and the National Assembly for prompt preparation and execution.

What Does President Yoon's Statement on the Worsening Situation in the Middle East Mean?

President Yoon Suk-yeol emphasized the need to prepare early demand reduction measures such as a vehicle restriction system and a 10-vehicle system, considering the possibility of prolonged tensions in the Middle East. This is a measure aimed at reducing energy consumption and protecting vulnerable groups, made in the context of urging quick preparation and execution of the government's additional budget proposal. The Climate Ministry is reviewing these measures from multiple angles, and discussions are ongoing regarding how vehicle restrictions will be applied in both the public and private sectors.

This statement goes beyond merely announcing measures; it reveals the government's intention to protect vulnerable groups in light of the instability in the Middle East and its impact on domestic energy policy. Such measures will be an important indicator of the government's response capability in the face of an energy crisis.

Key Summary

  • President Yoon Suk-yeol directed the preparation of demand reduction measures such as a vehicle restriction system considering the possibility of prolonged tensions in the Middle East.
  • The additional budget proposal is named 'war supplementary budget' and calls for prompt execution.
  • The Climate Ministry is reviewing the implementation and scope of vehicle restrictions.
  • Emphasizing support for vulnerable groups and the underprivileged, preferring direct support over fuel tax cuts.
  • Pointing out the delay in financial reform legislation in the National Assembly.

Why Background Matters

This statement comes amid growing concerns about the impact of political instability in the Middle East on the energy market. Conflicts between Iran and Israel, as well as the volatility of oil prices, could directly affect domestic energy consumption. Against this backdrop, the government appears to be intent on strengthening its policy responses for energy conservation and protection of vulnerable groups.

Why It Matters

This news goes beyond just the government's announcement of measures. The impact of instability in the Middle East on domestic economy and energy policy can affect virtually all citizens. Particularly, as rising energy prices significantly impact the lives of the underprivileged, effective implementation of these measures could positively influence the stability of vulnerable groups' livelihoods.
