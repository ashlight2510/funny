People Power Party Shifts to Primary for Busan Mayoral Candidate

Conflicts within the People Power Party are intensifying over the selection of the Busan mayoral candidate. On the 17th, the Candidate Management Committee became the center of controversy by deciding to select the candidate through a primary election. Chairman Lee Jung-hyun had proposed cutting off the current mayor Park Hyung-jun to nominate lawmaker Joo Jin-woo unilaterally, but ultimately shifted to a primary due to opposition from party members. This change is the result of local lawmakers strongly advocating for the necessity of a primary.

This issue transcends a simple nomination problem and could significantly impact the internal cohesion of the People Power Party and its future election strategies. With the decision to hold a primary, attention is drawn to how various opinions within the party will be reconciled and how the political landscape in Busan will change.
