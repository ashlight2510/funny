“Keyring Lead Levels Exceed Standard by 549 Times” 10 Overseas Purchased Children’s Products Found ‘Unsuitable’

Recently, the Seoul Metropolitan Government conducted safety inspections on children’s stationery sold on overseas online platforms, revealing that numerous products contained harmful substances, including lead and phthalate-based plasticizers, exceeding the standard limits. In particular, lead levels exceeding the standard by 549 times were detected in children’s keyrings, raising serious concerns. Additionally, harmful substances were found in various stationery items such as colored pencils, pencil cases, and recorders, prompting the Seoul government to request a halt to the sale of these products.

This incident can be interpreted as a serious warning regarding the safety of overseas purchases and consumer protection, going beyond mere product safety issues. The safety of children’s products is of utmost importance, and parents are now in a position where they must make more cautious purchasing decisions.
