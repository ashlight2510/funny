Police's Full-scale Response Ahead of BTS Concert: What It Means

The police are preparing thorough safety measures in anticipation of the large crowd expected at BTS's comeback concert on the 21st at Gwanghwamun Square in Seoul. The estimated number of attendees is about 260,000, which would be the largest since the street cheering during the 2002 World Cup. The police are establishing a multi-tiered response system for crowd management and safety, as well as enhancing measures to prevent terrorism and safety accidents.

The police's response to such a large-scale concert is interpreted as an essential measure for ensuring public safety and the smooth conduct of the event, going beyond mere safety management. This incident demonstrates that BTS's popularity has transcended simple fandom to become a social phenomenon.
