President Lee Jae-myung's Remarks on Adjusting Stock Trading Regulations

President Lee Jae-myung recently mentioned regulations related to stock trading during a meeting on stabilizing and normalizing the capital market. He raised questions about the current margin trading method, where money is paid two days after a stock transaction, hinting at the possibility of adjustments in this area. These remarks reflect the government's commitment to enhancing trust and stability in the capital market.

This statement serves as an important opportunity to spark in-depth discussions about the structure and regulations of the capital market, going beyond a mere personal opinion. In particular, President Lee's comments could impact both individual and institutional investors.
