7 Mistakes to Avoid When Storing Kimchi

What concerns do you have when storing kimchi? You want to enjoy it deliciously for a long time, but there are often moments of confusion about how to store it. I also struggled with how to store kimchi after making it for the first time. So, I’ve organized this information. Let me introduce you to 7 mistakes you must avoid to store kimchi deliciously!

1. Ignoring Contact with Air

One of the most common mistakes when storing kimchi is ignoring contact with air. Many people think they can just put kimchi in a container and cover it with a lid. However, if air gets in, the kimchi can spoil quickly. Therefore, you should minimize contact with air by pressing down the kimchi when putting it in the container and ensuring the lid is tightly closed. From my experience, paying attention to this helped the kimchi stay flavorful for a longer time.

2. Storing at Room Temperature

You might often leave kimchi at room temperature, especially during the summer. However, if you leave it at room temperature, fermentation can progress too quickly, altering the taste. The basic rule is to put it in the refrigerator, and if you have a kimchi refrigerator, I recommend using it. Personally, using a kimchi refrigerator keeps the kimchi really fresh for a long time!

3. Storing with Other Foods

You should also avoid storing kimchi with other foods. Especially if it's with strong-smelling foods like fish or meat, the kimchi can absorb those odors. Therefore, it's better to store kimchi separately. I once stored it with fish, and I regretted it when the kimchi went bad. It may seem trivial, but it's really an important aspect.

4. Adding Excessive Seasoning

Adding too much seasoning when storing kimchi is also one of the mistakes. If there’s too much seasoning when making kimchi, fermentation happens quickly, causing the kimchi to become mushy over time. So, it’s better to use seasoning moderately and to keep it to a minimum when storing. From my experience, doing this helped the kimchi maintain a crunchier texture.

5. Storing for Too Long

Finally, you should avoid storing kimchi for too long. Kimchi is a fermented food, and it naturally spoils over time. Therefore, it’s best to eat it while it’s fresh. Personally, I recommend consuming it within 1-2 weeks. I found that doing this made a significant difference in the flavor of the kimchi.

In conclusion, when storing kimchi, you need to be mindful of air contact, room temperature storage, mixing with other foods, excessive seasoning, and storing for too long. If you pay attention to these things, you can truly enjoy delicious kimchi for a long time! If you have any questions, please leave a comment. I think starting with these tips would be great!
