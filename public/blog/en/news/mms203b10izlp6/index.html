How to Effectively Reduce Air Conditioner Electricity Bills in Summer?

When summer comes, it's the season when we can't help but turn on the air conditioner. But the problem is the electricity bill! Many of us have experienced that heart-sinking feeling when we see the monthly electricity bill. So this time, I've compiled ways to effectively reduce air conditioner electricity costs. There are tips to use the air conditioner coolly while saving on electricity!

1. Set the Right Temperature

Recommended Temperature: 26 Degrees!

Everyone knows that the optimal temperature for an air conditioner is 26 degrees, right? Setting the air conditioner to a much lower temperature in summer can significantly increase the electricity bill. Personally, I really enjoyed the cool breeze at 26 degrees. At first, I set it a bit lower, but surprisingly, there wasn't much difference!

Create a Comfortable Environment

Also, when using the air conditioner, it's important to keep the temperature difference between indoors and outdoors not too large. When you come back home after being out, you might be tempted to lower the air conditioner to 20 degrees because of the heat, but this will quickly raise your electricity bill. It's better to slowly adapt at 26 degrees.

2. Use a Fan Together

Circulate Air with a Fan

Using a fan along with the air conditioner is really great. It helps distribute the air conditioner’s breeze evenly and makes you feel cooler. I usually turn on both the air conditioner and the fan, directing the fan towards the ceiling to push the air down. This way, the coolness lasts longer!

Just the Fan at Night!

Especially at night, it’s a good idea to just use the fan while sleeping. Thanks to this method, I saved a lot on unnecessary electricity bills. Of course, if the weather is too hot, you’ll need to turn on the air conditioner, but when it’s cool, just the fan is sufficient.

3. Blocking and Insulation

Keep Windows and Doors Closed

You need to keep windows and doors closed to prevent hot air from entering. Especially during the day when the sunlight is strong, it's good to draw the curtains to block the sunlight. After doing this, I definitely felt that the indoor temperature didn't rise as much.

Use Insulation Materials

If you can invest a little more, I recommend applying insulation materials to windows or doors. This is useful not only in summer but also in winter! The cost is not as high as you might think, so in the long run, it's a really good choice.

4. Regular Cleaning and Maintenance

Cleaning the Filter is Essential

You should regularly clean the air conditioner filter. If the filter is dirty, the airflow is not smooth, which leads to higher electricity consumption. I initially neglected this part, but after cleaning it properly, I experienced a significant reduction in my electricity bill.

Get a Professional Check-Up

Sometimes, if the air conditioner is not functioning properly, the electricity bill can increase. In such cases, it’s good to have a professional check it out. Ignoring small problems can lead to larger costs, so it's wise to get it checked in advance.

Conclusion

There are various ways to reduce air conditioner electricity bills in summer. From setting the right temperature to using fans, blocking and insulating, and regular cleaning! By effectively utilizing these tips, you can significantly reduce the burden of electricity bills. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I think starting with these tips would be great.
