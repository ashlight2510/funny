In particular, they reflect on their identity as “country boys from Korea” and emphasize the message of the new album by using the traditional Korean folk song ‘Arirang’ as background music. The documentary is set to be released on Netflix on the 27th, while BTS plans to release their new album on the 20th and hold a comeback performance on the 21st.
