Collision of fishing boats in Jeonnam Deugnyang Bay···1 dead, 1 injured

A collision accident between two fishing boats occurred in Deugnyangdo, Goheung-gun, Jeonnam. At around 2:43 PM on the 21st, fishing boat A, weighing 1.94 tons, collided with fishing boat B, weighing 2.54 tons, resulting in one person aboard boat A falling into the sea and being rescued, but ultimately succumbing to injuries, while another crew member was injured. The Coast Guard is investigating the circumstances of the accident.
