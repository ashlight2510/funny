“20 years of waiting paid off” “Thank you, Samsung”···Samsung Electronics' shareholders meeting transformed by 200,000 won, shareholders also show ‘bright faces’

Samsung Electronics held its regular shareholders' meeting on the 18th in Suwon. This year's meeting took place in an atmosphere filled with high expectations from shareholders, coinciding with a rise in stock prices. The stock closed at 208,500 won, showing a significant increase from last year's 50,000 won. Shareholders attending the meeting expressed feelings of reward after a long wait, showering the management with messages of encouragement and gratitude.

This meeting was an important occasion for restoring shareholders' trust, and Samsung's management reflected on last year's poor performance while promising to recover competitiveness in the future. However, some shareholders still expressed concerns about global conditions and uncertainties in the semiconductor market.

Why Background Matters

Last year, Samsung Electronics' stock price fell to 50,000 won, leading to increased dissatisfaction among shareholders. In this context, this year's meeting, held alongside the recovery of stock prices, served as an important opportunity to restore shareholders' trust. Given that shareholders have patiently waited for a long time with confidence in Samsung Electronics, the positive atmosphere at this meeting is seen as a reward for their patience.
