This year marks the 70th anniversary of pianist Baek Gun-woo's debut, and he has released a new album titled . Reasons to Consider the Background

Baek Gun-woo was born in Seoul in 1946 and began his musical career at the age of 10. He continued his studies at Juilliard, growing into a world-class performer, and his career has spanned over 70 years. This album holds special significance as it is a product of his deep exploration of Schubert, marking the first Schubert-related album released in 13 years.

He possesses an identity as an artist who deeply explores the meanings embedded in music rather than merely performing it. This background is crucial for understanding what Baek Gun-woo aims to convey through music.
