Why Background Matters

The Japanese military comfort women issue addresses the tragic experiences of women who were forcibly mobilized by the Japanese military during World War II. This issue is seen as a symbol of historical conflict between Japan and Korea, with the voices of the victims not being adequately represented. In recent years, demands for apologies and compensation from the Japanese government have continued, yet the issue remains unresolved. As a result, survivors continue to suffer, and it is viewed as a societal responsibility to ensure their stories are not forgotten.
