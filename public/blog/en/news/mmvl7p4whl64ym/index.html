“Worst Timing” Will Takaiichi Respond to the Deployment to the Hormuz Strait One Day Before Meeting Trump

Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaiichi is contemplating the issue of deploying Self-Defense Forces to the Hormuz Strait one day before her summit with the United States. It is expected that the US will demand Japan to deploy the Self-Defense Forces to the Hormuz Strait during this meeting, and the Japanese government is anticipated to avoid giving a definitive answer. There is a prevailing opinion in Japan that the Self-Defense Forces cannot be dispatched to combat areas, but there are also discussions about sending them for investigation and research purposes. This situation demonstrates the complex dilemma the Japanese government faces in maintaining its relationship with the US while ensuring its own security.

This issue is likely to have a significant impact on Japan's foreign and security policies, particularly revealing Japan's intention to strengthen its alliance with the US. Prime Minister Takaiichi plans to seek economic benefits through cooperation with the US, but the risks associated with military intervention in the context of conflicts with Iran cannot be overlooked.

This issue can be seen as part of Japan's efforts to maintain its military alliance with the US. However, there is strong opposition within Japan to deploying the Self-Defense Forces to combat areas, which raises complex issues related to Japan's pacifist constitution.
