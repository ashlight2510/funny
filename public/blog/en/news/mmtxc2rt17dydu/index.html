‘Spring Festival Opens in Boryeong Muchangpo with Seasonal ‘Jukkumi and Doddari’

The ‘2026 Muchangpo Jukkumi & Doddari Festival’ will be held in Muchangpo, Boryeong City from the 20th of this month to the 5th of next month. This festival is designed to revitalize the local economy and attract tourists by focusing on the representative seafood of spring, jukkumi and doddari, featuring various programs and food experiences. In particular, the ‘Mysterious Sea Road’ experience is expected to be the highlight of the festival, offering visitors a unique experience as the sea road appears due to the tidal changes.

The importance of this festival lies in its potential to contribute to the local economy and tourism, going beyond a simple food festival. Jukkumi and doddari are local specialties, and various experiences and events utilizing them can highlight Boryeong's charm.

Key Summary

  • Festival Dates: The 2026 Muchangpo Jukkumi & Doddari Festival will be held from March 20 to April 5.
  • Main Programs: Mysterious Sea Road experience, children's bare-hand fishing, and food experience booths.
  • Specialties: Jukkumi and doddari are known for their cardiovascular health benefits and flavor, with many tourists expected to visit during their peak season in March and April.
  • Mysterious Sea Road: The approximately 1.5 km sea road that appears due to tidal changes is a major attraction of the festival, often referred to as a modern-day miracle of Moses.

Why Background Matters

This festival is part of efforts to revitalize the local economy of Boryeong City. The tourism industry has been hit hard in recent years due to COVID-19, leading to significant economic impacts. Therefore, a festival utilizing local specialties has become an important method for economic recovery through tourism. Jukkumi and doddari are particularly delicious in spring, and hosting the festival during this time is a strategic move to attract tourists.

Why It Matters

The festival holds significance beyond being a local event. It contributes to increasing the income of local residents and provides new experiences for tourists visiting Boryeong, thereby enhancing the local brand value. Additionally, it can serve as a direct source of income for local farmers and fishermen, becoming an important opportunity for economic independence.

Points to Note

In my view, the most noteworthy aspect of this festival is the ‘Mysterious Sea Road’ experience. It offers not just a tourist attraction but an opportunity to experience local culture and nature. Tourists will likely develop a positive perception of Muchangpo through this unique experience, increasing the chances of return visits.

Hidden Variables to Consider

However, the success of the festival is not limited to the diversity of programs or food. Various factors such as weather, competition with other local events, and tourist safety issues can affect the festival's success. Preparations considering these factors are necessary.

Points to Check Now

  • Check Festival Dates and Programs: It is advisable to check the festival's official website or related information in advance to plan your visit.
  • Benefits of Jukkumi and Doddari: This festival could be an opportunity to raise awareness about jukkumi and doddari and explore ways to incorporate them into a healthy diet.

FAQ

Q: When can I experience the sea road?
A: The sea road experience will be available on March 21, 22, April 1, 2, and 3. Please refer to the official announcements for exact times.

Q: Do I need to make a reservation to participate in the festival?
A: Most programs allow for on-site participation, but some specific experiences may require advance reservations, so it’s best to check in advance.

Q: What booths will have jukkumi and doddari dishes?
A: Various food experience booths will be operating during the festival, offering dishes made with jukkumi and doddari.
