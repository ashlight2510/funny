Hidden Gems in Southeast Asia: Hotel Reviews

March 15, 2026

Hidden Gems and Hotel Reviews in Southeast Asia

When planning a trip, many people struggle with the question, "Where should I go?" Southeast Asia has many famous tourist spots, and finding hidden gems within them is quite enjoyable. So, I've organized a review of the hidden gems I visited and the hotels nearby. Let's embark on this journey together!

Hidden Gem: Luang Prabang, Laos

The Charm of Luang Prabang

Luang Prabang in Laos is a truly quiet and peaceful place that captivates visitors. Here, you can usually stroll along the Mekong River and feel the traditional local life. In particular, hidden spots like the Pak Ou Caves are not well-known to many, but the view from here is simply breathtaking. Personally, the sunset I experienced here is the most memorable.

Recommended Hotel: Monkey Mountain Resort

I highly recommend the Monkey Mountain Resort where I stayed in Luang Prabang. Its quiet location was perfect for relaxation, and the spa services offered by the resort were excellent. After a day of sightseeing, returning to unwind was rejuvenating. The Laotian breakfast I had through room service in the morning was also delicious!

Wonders of Nature: Banaue, Philippines

The Terraces of Banaue

Have you been to the rice terraces in Banaue, Philippines, which are designated as a UNESCO World Heritage site? They are truly spectacular! Located in a mountainous region, you can enjoy beautiful scenery along with cool weather. The rice terraces here symbolize traditional agriculture that has been passed down for hundreds of years. When I visited, I was really moved by seeing the farmers working hard in the fields.

Recommended Hotel: Highlands Resort

I recommend Highlands Resort in Banaue. This hotel is located in a spot where you can see the terraces at a glance, and waking up to that view is breathtaking. Especially, enjoying a beer while watching the stars in the evening was delightful. My stay here was truly a special experience.

Mysterious Island: Lombok, Indonesia

Hidden Beaches of Lombok

Lombok is less known than Bali, making it perfect for those seeking quiet beaches. In particular, Selong Belanak Beach is like a hidden gem. The clean sea and white sands stretch endlessly, making you feel like you’re in paradise. I tried snorkeling here, and the variety and beauty of the underwater creatures were amazing.

Recommended Hotel: Selong Belanak Beach Resort

The Selong Belanak Beach Resort where I stayed in Lombok was conveniently located right by the beach. Having breakfast with a view of the sea made me feel good from the start of the day. The staff at the resort were also friendly, which added to my comfort. I strongly recommend staying here when traveling to Lombok.

Conclusion

I hope this hotel review of hidden gems in Southeast Asia helps you find new and special travel destinations. Everyone has different criteria for travel, but these places were truly captivating. If you have any questions or other recommended spots, feel free to leave a comment! It would be great to check out these places for your next travel destination.
