Asan Foundation Holds the 19th Asan Medical Award Ceremony

The Asan Foundation held the 19th Asan Medical Award ceremony on the 18th. This ceremony honored Professor Lee Ho-young from Seoul National University's College of Pharmacy, Professor Kim Seung-eob from Severance Hospital, Professor Martin Steiniger from Seoul National University, and Professor Lee Joo-myung from Samsung Medical Center, who received awards in their respective fields. They were recognized for their innovative research contributions to the advancement of medical science. The Asan Foundation hopes that this award will encourage researchers in the medical science field and lead to more achievements in the future.

This ceremony served as a reaffirmation of the importance of medical science beyond just the awarding of prizes. In particular, it highlighted how the research of the awardees can contribute to actual patient treatment, emphasizing the need for close collaboration between the medical and research communities.

  • The Asan Foundation promises ongoing support for the advancement of medical science.

Why Background Matters

The Asan Medical Award was established in 2008 to encourage researchers who have made outstanding achievements in the field of medical science. This can be seen as part of efforts to improve the medical research environment in South Korea and enhance international research competitiveness. Particularly in recent years, global pandemics like COVID-19 have further highlighted the importance of medical science, making this award ceremony even more significant.
