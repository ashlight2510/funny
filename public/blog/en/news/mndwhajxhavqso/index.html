Kim Boo-kyum's suitability for Daegu mayor at 49.5% '1st'... higher than the combined support rate of the People's Power candidates

Recent polling results have shown that former Prime Minister Kim Boo-kyum of the Democratic Party has secured 49.5% support in the suitability for Daegu mayor, placing him in first place. This figure is more than 10 percentage points higher than the combined support rate of 36.1% for the six candidates from the People’s Power party, indicating a growing interest in the Daegu mayoral election. Based on this survey result, Kim has officially declared his candidacy for the Daegu mayoral position.

This survey was conducted by TBC, commissioned to Realmeter, targeting 804 adults residing in Daegu from the 28th to the 29th, and it is noteworthy that Kim Boo-kyum's support rate shows a significant lead over the candidates from the People’s Power party. In particular, in hypothetical head-to-head matchups, Kim outperformed all candidates from the People’s Power party outside the margin of error.

Key Highlights

  • Former Prime Minister Kim Boo-kyum records 49.5% in Daegu mayor suitability survey, ranking first.
  • The combined support rate of the six People’s Power candidates is 36.1%.
  • Kim shows an advantage in hypothetical head-to-head matchups against other candidates.
  • Official declaration of candidacy for Daegu mayor.
  • Survey sample consisted of 804 adults residing in Daegu, with a margin of error of ±3.5%.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This survey is noteworthy as the Daegu mayoral position holds significant political symbolism in the upcoming election. Daegu has long been considered a stronghold for conservative parties, but political changes have been detected in recent years. Former Prime Minister Kim Boo-kyum, being a native of Daegu, has strong ties to the local community, which likely contributed to his rising support rate.

Moreover, the low support rates of the People’s Power candidates may create a sense of crisis within the conservative camp in light of Kim's high support rate. Given that Daegu has traditionally been a strong base for conservative parties, Kim's performance could have a significant impact on the future political landscape of Daegu.

Why It Matters

The high support rate for candidate Kim Boo-kyum suggests potential political changes in the Daegu region beyond mere individual political achievements. This could greatly influence political alliances or strategies in future presidential and local elections. Particularly, since Daegu has been a traditional support base for conservative parties, Kim's strong showing may prompt a reevaluation of the Democratic Party's strategic approach.

Hidden Variables to Watch

Given the margin of error and low response rate of this survey, it should not be interpreted too optimistically. The political landscape in Daegu is quite complex, and there is a possibility that the poll results may not align with actual election outcomes. Additionally, the support rates reflected in the survey are only snapshots of a specific moment and can fluctuate over time.
