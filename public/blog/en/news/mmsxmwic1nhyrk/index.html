Year-End Tax Settlement: Credit Card Points Utilization Checklist

March 16, 2026

Year-End Tax Settlement Card Points Utilization Checklist

Why are Card Points Important for Year-End Tax Settlement?

Year-end tax settlement is an important process that many people pay attention to every year. During this time, individuals seek various ways to maximize tax deductions and refunds, and utilizing card points is one of them. Effectively using card points can increase tax refunds and reduce financial burdens.

Card points play a significant role in year-end tax settlements beyond just consumer benefits. Therefore, understanding the importance and utilization methods of card points is essential.

Tax Deduction Benefits of Card Points

Card Points Included in Tax Deductions

Benefits obtained through card points can be included in tax deductions. For example, a certain percentage of annual card usage is eligible for income deductions, so appropriately utilizing card points can increase refund amounts.

Execution Tip: Keep a record of your annual card usage and check your points accumulation history to maximize tax deductions.

Points Usable Like Cash

Additionally, there are cases where card points can be used like cash. In this case, the points used are considered actual consumption and can be included in tax deductions.

Checkpoint: Be sure to keep the usage statement provided by the card company when using points.

Types and Characteristics of Card Points

Card points can be broadly divided into general points and cashback.

  • General Points: Points that can be used at specific merchants, which can be exchanged for various products or services.
  • Cashback: A form where a portion of the card usage amount is refunded in cash, making it advantageous for reducing financial burdens as it can be used immediately.

Moreover, they can be categorized into accumulation cards and usage cards. Accumulation cards earn points upon usage, while usage cards allow points to be used immediately during consumption. Understanding and choosing the characteristics of these cards is important.

Strategies for Accumulating Card Points

Efficient Point Accumulation Strategies

To efficiently accumulate card points, you should check the point accumulation rate when using the card.

  • Check the List of Accumulation Merchants: Some specific merchants offer more points, so it’s good to keep track of frequently used merchants.
  • Caution: Always check the accumulation limits and expiration dates. For example, some cards may have annual accumulation limits or short point validity periods.

How to Use Card Points

Various Point Usage Options

There are various methods for using points.

  • Using Points at Payment: You can pay part of the payment amount with points.
  • Using Points for Product Purchases: Options to use points when purchasing specific products are available.

Checkpoint: Be sure to check the usage deadlines and conditions. Points will expire if not used by the deadline, so planning ahead is crucial.

Including Card Points in Year-End Tax Settlement

Required Documents and Submission Methods

Here’s how to include card points in your year-end tax settlement:

  • Prepare Required Documents: You need to prepare the usage statement and points statement provided by the card company.
  • Caution: Required documents may change due to tax law changes, so it’s advisable to check annually.

Card Points Utilization Checklist

Here’s a checklist for utilizing card points effectively:

  1. Check card usage history
  2. Confirm accumulated and usable points
  3. Prepare documents related to year-end tax settlement
  4. Plan for point usage
  5. Check for changes in tax laws

By systematically preparing with this checklist, you can maximize the use of card points during your year-end tax settlement.

Case Studies of Card Points Utilization

Let’s examine the effectiveness of point utilization through actual cases. For example, Mr. A spent 3 million won on his card annually and accumulated 300,000 won in points. By using these points during the year-end tax settlement, his refund increased from 500,000 won to 700,000 won.

Item Before Point Utilization After Point Utilization
Refund Amount 500,000 won 700,000 won
Card Usage Amount 3 million won 3 million won
Accumulated Points 300,000 won 300,000 won

As shown, effectively utilizing points can significantly increase refund amounts.

Precautions When Utilizing Card Points

Here are some precautions to take when utilizing card points:

  • Point Expiration Dates: Each card company has different point expiration dates, so check in advance and prepare accordingly.
  • Impact of Tax Law Changes: Changes in tax laws may affect the tax deduction benefits of card points.
  • Differences in Policies by Card Company: Each card company has different policies for point accumulation and usage, so it’s important to be well-informed about the card company you use.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How are card points accumulated?
    Card points are automatically accumulated based on the accumulation rate when using the card. It’s advisable to check the terms and conditions of each card company as they vary.

  2. How do I report card points during year-end tax settlement?
    Card points should be reported by submitting the card usage statement and points statement together during the year-end tax settlement. Keep the documents issued by the card company safe.

  3. What should I be cautious about when using card points?
    Always check the usage deadlines and conditions. Additionally, it’s good to know the merchants where points can be used and the methods of usage in advance.

Conclusion: Start Planning to Utilize Card Points Now

By effectively utilizing card points while preparing for your year-end tax settlement, you can increase your refund amount. Starting today, refer to the checklist above to plan your card points utilization. Begin with checking your card usage history and accumulated points!
