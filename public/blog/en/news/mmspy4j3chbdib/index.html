Launch of Intergovernmental Committee for River and Valley Maintenance: What Questions Remain?

March 16, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Launch of the 'River and Valley Maintenance' Intergovernmental Council···“Verification Using Aircraft, Satellites, Drones, and More”

An intergovernmental council has been launched to manage illegal occupancy facilities in rivers and valleys. Under the auspices of the Ministry of Interior and Safety, various ministries including the Ministry of Environment, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs, and the Korea Forest Service are participating in this initiative. They plan to conduct a comprehensive re-investigation of illegal facilities and verify any omitted structures using aerial and drone technology. This measure appears to be part of efforts to recognize the seriousness of illegal occupancy issues and establish an effective management system.

The launch of this council goes beyond simple facility maintenance, demonstrating the government's strong commitment to addressing illegal occupancy issues. Depending on the investigation results, severe penalties and fines will be imposed, and accountability for local governments and responsible officials will be strengthened.

Key Points

  • Launch of Intergovernmental Council: A council has been formed to manage illegal occupancy facilities in rivers and valleys.
  • Comprehensive Re-investigation: Various technologies including aircraft, satellites, and drones will be utilized to thoroughly verify the status of illegal facilities.
  • Penalties and Fines: Legal amendments will be pursued to impose severe penalties and fines for any omissions in the investigation.
  • Central-Local Cooperation: Regional coordinators will be established to continuously monitor the implementation status.
  • President Lee Jae-myung's Strong Administrative Will: Emphasis on the need for audits and inspections in the public sector to resolve illegal occupancy issues.

Background and Context

Illegal occupancy of rivers and valleys has been pointed out as a social problem for a long time. Particularly during the peak summer season, the number of illegal facilities increases, leading to environmental destruction and dissatisfaction among local residents. To address this issue, the government has been conducting surveys on illegal occupancy facilities since last year, and the launch of this council is a continuation of those efforts. With the recognition that stronger measures are needed under President Lee Jae-myung's directive, intergovernmental cooperation has been decided.

Why This Issue is Important

The problem of illegal occupancy in rivers and valleys does not only lead to environmental destruction but also significantly impacts the living environment of local residents. Illegal facilities can damage natural landscapes and cause water pollution. Furthermore, the government's strong measures present an opportunity to build trust with citizens. If illegal occupancy is effectively controlled through this investigation, a foundation will be established to prevent similar crimes in the future.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What are illegal occupancy facilities?
A1: Illegal occupancy facilities are structures installed in rivers or valleys without legal permission, including pavilions, shade structures, and bungalows.

Q2: How will the results of this investigation be utilized?
A2: If illegal facilities are identified in the investigation results, measures such as the removal of those facilities and the imposition of fines will be taken.

Q3: What technologies will be used in the investigation process?
A3: Various spatial information technologies including aerial photographs, satellite images, and drone footage will be used to verify the absence of omitted facilities.

Q4: How will the accountability of local governments be strengthened?
A4: In cases of omissions in the investigation, severe penalties will be imposed on the respective local governments and responsible officials, and regional coordinators will be established to ensure effective implementation management.
