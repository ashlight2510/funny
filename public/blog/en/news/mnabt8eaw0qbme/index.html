Sunday's high of 22 degrees, spring weather... "Be cautious of fine dust during outdoor activities"

As the weather warms up, the daytime temperature on Sunday, the 29th, is expected to reach a high of 22 degrees. However, the Korea Meteorological Administration warns that the fine dust concentration in Seoul and southern Gyeonggi Province will reach 'bad' levels. In particular, due to the dry air and significant temperature differences, health precautions are necessary, and caution regarding fine dust is needed during outdoor activities. Additionally, dense fog is expected in the morning, which could pose traffic safety concerns.

This article highlights the arrival of spring weather while reminding us of the issues of fine dust and air pollution. Such weather changes suggest that we should consider the impacts on health and the environment beyond just rising temperatures.

FAQ

  1. How is fine dust concentration measured?

    • Fine dust concentration is assessed by measuring the tiny particles (PM10, PM2.5, etc.) in the air.

  2. What actions should be taken when fine dust levels are 'bad'?

    • Avoid going outside, wear a mask if necessary, and keep indoor air clean.

  3. What is the relationship between fine dust and health?

    • Fine dust increases the risk of respiratory diseases, allergies, and cardiovascular diseases.
