Mad for Garlic presents a new vision with 'Mad for Family'

MFG KOREA, the operating company of Mad for Garlic, recently declared a new brand mission called 'Mad for Family' at its Yeongdeungpo Times Square branch in Seoul, unveiling its long-term vision through 2026. This declaration is not just a marketing strategy; it embodies Mad for Garlic's commitment to emphasizing familial bonds and strengthening relationships with customers.

This change reflects Mad for Garlic's intention to move beyond being a simple dining brand and pursue a path of forming deep connections with customers while aiming for sustainable growth. It can be interpreted as a strategy to enhance the brand's identity and increase customer loyalty.

Key Summary

  • Mad for Garlic declares 'Mad for Family' as its long-term vision through 2026.
  • Emphasizes familial bonds with customers and aims to strengthen brand identity.
  • Interpreted as a strategy to enhance competitiveness in the dining industry.

Key Scenes

  • Brand Mission: 'Mad for Family' conveys the message of treating customers like family.
  • Long-term Vision: The goal is sustainable growth and improved customer loyalty by 2026.
  • Customer Relationships: Strengthening brand loyalty through deepening relationships with customers.
  • Market Competitiveness: A strategy to differentiate itself amid fierce competition in the dining industry.

Why Background Matters

Mad for Garlic has been exploring various strategies in recent years to keep pace with changes in the dining industry. Particularly, after the COVID-19 pandemic, dining culture has shifted, altering customer demands and expectations. In this context, how the brand forms relationships with customers has become a crucial challenge, and the declaration of 'Mad for Family' appears to be a result of this trend.

Why It Matters

This declaration shows that Mad for Garlic aims to establish itself as a brand that values relationships with customers rather than just being a dining brand. It is expected to play a significant role in providing better experiences for customers and enhancing brand loyalty. Additionally, it suggests the potential for a trend across the dining industry that prioritizes relationships with customers.

My Thoughts

In my view, the brand mission 'Mad for Family' should not just be a simple slogan; it needs to be followed by concrete execution plans on how to actually build relationships with customers. If the brand can strengthen communication with consumers and demonstrate authenticity in the process, customers will naturally feel a sense of loyalty towards the brand.

Hidden Variables to Consider

A key point to note in this declaration is the specific execution plan for the brand mission. Simply emphasizing 'familial bonds' is not enough. It is important to reflect customer feedback and implement tangible changes in service improvement or menu development based on that feedback. Otherwise, this declaration risks remaining an empty slogan.

Points to Check Now

  • It is essential to pay attention to how Mad for Garlic's future marketing strategies and communication with customers evolve.
  • Confirming what specific experiences 'Mad for Family' will actually provide to customers and the concrete execution plans is crucial.
