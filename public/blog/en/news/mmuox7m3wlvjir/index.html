Oh Se-hoon Registers as a Candidate for Seoul Mayor, A New Phase in Seoul Politics

On the 17th, Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon announced at a press conference that he would register as a candidate for the Seoul mayor from the People Power Party. He criticized the lack of will for change from Representative Jang Dong-hyuk and the party leadership, while expressing his determination to start change from Seoul. This announcement reflects the complex situation of internal conflicts and candidate registrations within the party ahead of the Seoul mayoral election, and there is growing interest in the impact of Mayor Oh's decision on future Seoul politics.

This candidate registration holds significance beyond just running for election. Mayor Oh has argued that a change in the party's direction is necessary, and this decision further highlights the internal conflicts within the People Power Party. Coinciding with the announcement of a negotiation proposal on judicial reform by Democratic Party leader Jeong Cheong-rae, political tensions between the ruling and opposition parties are expected to intensify.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This announcement comes as Mayor Oh has continuously raised complaints about the need for a change in the party's direction. He criticized Representative Jang Dong-hyuk for not demonstrating a willingness for party innovation and expressed his intention to become the agent of change himself. Against this backdrop, Mayor Oh's candidate registration further highlights the internal conflicts within the People Power Party and foreshadows a new phase in Seoul politics. In particular, the timing of this announcement coinciding with Jeong Cheong-rae's announcement of the judicial reform proposal is likely to heighten political tensions between the ruling and opposition parties.

Why It Matters

Mayor Oh Se-hoon's candidate registration is not merely the beginning of the Seoul mayoral election. It can be interpreted as an attempt to resolve internal conflicts within the People Power Party, which could significantly influence the future political landscape. The key will be how the demand for a change in direction raised by Mayor Oh resonates within the party and how the confrontation with the Democratic Party unfolds. In particular, the impact of the political choices of Seoul citizens on the future political landscape is expected to be substantial.
