Kim Dong-yeon, Registered as a Candidate for Gyeonggi Province Governor... Acting Authority of the Deputy Governor

Recently, Gyeonggi Province Governor Kim Dong-yeon registered as a preliminary candidate with the National Election Commission, officially entering the race for the Gyeonggi Province governor election. His registration as a preliminary candidate is part of the Democratic Party's preparations for the June 3 local elections, during which the duties of Governor Kim Dong-hyun have been suspended, and Deputy Governor Kim Seong-joong has taken on the acting authority. Governor Kim stated that he decided to register as a preliminary candidate to expand contact with party members while addressing current issues in the province.

This registration as a preliminary candidate is not merely a political move but could be a crucial turning point that will influence the future of Gyeonggi Province. Governor Kim Dong-yeon's participation in the primary is expected to change the competitive landscape within the Democratic Party and spark discussions on various pressing issues that concern the residents of Gyeonggi Province.
