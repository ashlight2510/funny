LGU+, Free SIM Replacement for All Customers... Response to Security Flaw

LG U+ has announced that it will proceed with free SIM (USIM) replacements for all customers. This decision was made after a security flaw was discovered in the International Mobile Subscriber Identity (IMSI) used for subscriber identification. The issue arose because the subscriber identification number was generated by reflecting the customer's phone number. Although there has been no actual data breach, there was a risk of personal information exposure for anyone who knew the phone number. To mitigate this risk, LG U+ has decided to replace the SIM cards for free starting April 13.

This incident has sparked significant controversy in the telecommunications industry, as it highlights a failure to adhere to basic security principles. SK Telecom and KT manage their subscriber identification numbers using random values, differentiating their approach from that of LG U+. LG U+ stated that it became aware of this issue and has been working on improvements since the SIM information leak incident at SK Telecom last year.

Why Background Matters

This issue stems from the design of LG U+'s subscriber identification number. LG U+ appears to be taking measures to address its security issues in light of this market trend.

Protecting customers' personal information is one of the most important responsibilities of a telecommunications company, and neglecting this can severely damage trust. Customers should feel assured that their information is securely protected while using services, and this incident poses a risk to that trust.
