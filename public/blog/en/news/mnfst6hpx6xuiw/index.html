Recently, the tension in the political arena has heightened as Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon faces court over allegations of polling fraud. Former People Power Party lawmaker Kim Young-sun testified in court that Mayor Oh asked political broker Myung Tae-kyun to "become his mentor." This case is further complicated by promises of apartment provision in exchange for commissioned polling related to the 2021 Seoul mayoral by-election. Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident stems from allegations of polling fraud related to the April 7, 2021 Seoul mayoral by-election. At that time, claims were made that Mayor Oh Se-hoon sought polling results through his meeting with Myung Tae-kyun, with former lawmaker Kim Young-sun reportedly facilitating this meeting. Since polling results are a crucial factor that can determine the flow of elections, this case extends beyond personal issues to a broader controversy regarding political trust and ethics.
