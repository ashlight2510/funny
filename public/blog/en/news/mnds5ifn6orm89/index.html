‘Master of Priests’ Monsignor Jeong Hak-won Passes Away

Monsignor Jeong Hak-won has passed away at the age of 99. As an elder of the Korean Catholic Church, he was known as the master of priests and dedicated himself to nurturing future generations. After being ordained a priest in 1951, Monsignor Jeong continued his educational and pastoral activities both domestically and internationally, producing over 700 priests. His funeral home has been set up at the Catholic Diocese of Masan, and the funeral mass is scheduled for the 31st.
