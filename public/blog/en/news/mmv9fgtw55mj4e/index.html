Lisa Su, CEO of AMD, met with Choi Soo-yeon, CEO of Naver, during her first visit to Korea. This meeting was held to sign a memorandum of understanding (MOU) for AI semiconductor cooperation, with AMD aiming for stable procurement of next-generation high-bandwidth memory (HBM). This meeting is interpreted as part of AMD's strategy to actively pursue customer acquisition and counter the Nvidia-centered market structure.

This news goes beyond a simple corporate meeting and represents a significant issue that could greatly impact the future of the Korean IT industry and the competitive landscape of the global semiconductor market.
