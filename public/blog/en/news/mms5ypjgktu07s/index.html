Temperature and Humidity Management Tips for Storing Kimchi

If you love kimchi, you know how important proper storage is. Watching freshly made kimchi ferment deliciously is enjoyable, but if stored incorrectly, it can quickly lose its flavor. So, let's summarize this. Let's talk about the temperature and humidity management tips you need to know when storing kimchi!

The Importance of Storage Temperature

The most important factor when storing kimchi is the temperature. Generally, it's best to store kimchi at a cool temperature between 0°C and 4°C. From my experience, kimchi ferments most deliciously in this temperature range. When storing in the refrigerator, it's best to place it in a spot where the temperature remains consistent, avoiding the door area, as it frequently changes temperature due to opening and closing.

Tips for Maintaining the Right Temperature

Having a thermometer is really useful when storing kimchi. I found that using a thermometer made it easy to determine if the temperature was too high or too low in certain areas. So, I set my refrigerator to 3°C and placed the kimchi in a corner, and it fermented much better. After doing this, I definitely noticed a difference in the taste of the kimchi!

Humidity Management, What Is This?

Humidity cannot be ignored when storing kimchi. The ideal humidity level is between 80% and 90%. If it's too dry, the kimchi spoils quickly, and if it's too humid, mold can develop. Therefore, I recommend sealing the container that holds the kimchi. This way, outside air doesn't enter, helping to maintain the humidity.

Methods for Controlling Humidity

To prevent moisture buildup in the kimchi container, it's good to place a paper towel in the middle. From my experience, this made humidity control much easier. If moisture increases, you can replace the paper towel, and these little tips greatly help in preserving the taste of the kimchi!

Points to Note When Storing Kimchi

There’s another important point to consider when storing kimchi: avoiding mixed storage with other foods. Kimchi absorbs odors well, so storing it with other foods can alter its flavor. Therefore, it's best to create a dedicated space for kimchi. From my experience, doing this allowed me to appreciate the original flavor of the kimchi much better.

Conclusion

By managing the temperature and humidity well when storing kimchi, you can enjoy delicious kimchi for a long time.
