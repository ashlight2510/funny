Lee Jae-myung “Useful and stable reform… Policies should be approached on their own merits”

President Lee Jae-myung's emphasis on the direction of reform and the attitude of the ruling party during a dinner with first-term lawmakers of the Democratic Party is garnering attention. He highlighted the need to embrace the people's aspirations through “useful and stable reform” and stressed that policies should be approached on their own merits. This statement is interpreted in the context of how the ruling party should respond to the recent controversy over prosecutorial reform.

During the dinner, President Lee pointed out that “excessive competition for clarity” could lead to the reorganization of vested interests, conveying a positive message that the Democratic Party is doing well. This reflects a sense of responsibility as the ruling party and an emphasis on the essence of policy.
