Companies Pressing Down Stock Prices ‘Stigmatized’…Financial Services Commission “Low PBR Company List to be Released”

Recently, the Financial Services Commission announced its plan to release a list of undervalued companies. This measure aims to prevent certain listed companies from intentionally neglecting their low stock prices and to enhance market transparency. In particular, companies with a price-to-book ratio (PBR) of less than 1 will be tagged as ‘low PBR’ to help investors easily recognize them. This action is understood as part of efforts to enhance corporate value and address the undervaluation issues in the Korean stock market.

This issue is one of several policies introduced by the financial authorities to stabilize and normalize the capital market, alongside the reorganization of the KOSDAQ market. The release of the low PBR company list is expected to induce companies to engage in efforts such as share buybacks or improvements in governance through a kind of 'stigma effect.'

Key Highlights

  • The Financial Services Commission announced plans to release a list of low PBR companies.
  • Companies with a PBR of less than 1 will be tagged as ‘low PBR’ to enhance investor awareness.
  • The reorganization of the KOSDAQ market into dual leagues and the prohibition of dual listings are also included.
  • Low PBR companies can be removed from the list if they disclose plans to enhance corporate value.
  • Efforts to strengthen the monitoring function of institutional investors through the enhancement of the stewardship code are also being pursued.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This announcement is part of efforts to address the undervaluation issues in the Korean stock market. Low PBR companies are in a situation where their stock prices are significantly lower than their asset values, which hinders their growth potential and leads to distrust among investors. The financial authorities aim to encourage low PBR companies to engage in efforts such as share buybacks or improvements in governance.

Additionally, the reorganization of the KOSDAQ market is a measure to invigorate the growth stock market, aiming to provide clearer information to investors by separating early-stage growth companies from mature companies.

Why It Matters

The release of the low PBR company list is not just about raising stock prices; it is an important measure to enhance the transparency and credibility of the Korean stock market. This provides investors with better opportunities to make informed investment decisions and creates a positive cycle that encourages companies to voluntarily improve.

If low PBR companies can enhance their corporate value through this measure, it could increase the overall market's credibility and attract more foreign investors.

My Perspective

In my view, the release of the low PBR company list is an effective way to induce necessary improvements without undermining corporate autonomy. However, the actual effectiveness of this measure depends on how seriously companies take this issue and their willingness to improve. There remains a question of whether current low PBR companies will indeed formulate concrete plans for share buybacks or governance improvements.

Hidden Variables to Consider

There is no guarantee that this measure will have the same effect on all low PBR companies. It is important not to overlook that market reactions may vary depending on the internal circumstances of the companies or external environments. Additionally, simply tagging a low PBR company does not automatically lead to an increase in corporate value; substantial improvement efforts must be supported.

Points to Check Now

  • When the low PBR company list is released, pay attention to which companies are included.
  • It is also important to examine what specific plans each company has for enhancing corporate value.
  • Check how the league reorganization in the KOSDAQ market will actually proceed and the resulting market reactions.

FAQ

Q1: When will the low PBR company list be released?
A1: The Financial Services Commission plans to release the low PBR company list as early as the second half of this year.

Q2: How is the low PBR tag assigned?
A2: The low PBR tag will be assigned to companies that fall within the bottom 20% of their industry based on PBR, making it easier for investors to recognize them.

Q3: How can a company be removed from the list?
A3: A low PBR company can be removed from the list if it discloses plans to enhance corporate value within a certain period.

Q4: How will the KOSDAQ market's league reorganization be implemented?
A4: The KOSDAQ market will be reorganized by placing large companies in the ‘premium’ league based on market capitalization, while general companies will be placed in the ‘standard’ league.
