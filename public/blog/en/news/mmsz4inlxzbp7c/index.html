Eco-Friendly Ways to Remove Washing Machine Odor

March 16, 2026

Natural Ways to Remove Washing Machine Odors

Unpleasant odors from the washing machine can not only degrade the quality of laundry but also signal poor cleanliness inside the washing machine. Therefore, removing odors using eco-friendly methods positively impacts both health and the environment. In this article, we will introduce the causes of washing machine odors and effective natural methods to eliminate them.

Main Causes of Washing Machine Odors

Mold and bacteria inside the washing machine are the main culprits of odors. Residues or moisture from laundry can decompose inside the washing machine, causing unpleasant smells.

Checkpoint

  • Neglecting the cleaning of the washing machine interior can lead to persistent odors. Regular inspection and cleaning are essential.

Eco-Friendly Odor Removal Methods

1. Removing Odors with Baking Soda

Baking soda acts as a natural deodorizer, and placing it in the washing machine drum and running an empty wash can effectively eliminate odors.

Application Method

  1. Add 1 cup of baking soda to the washing machine drum.
  2. Run an empty wash cycle with hot water.

Caution

  • Using too much can leave residues inside the washing machine, so it's important to use the appropriate amount.

2. Effective Use of Vinegar

Vinegar is effective for killing bacteria and deodorizing. Adding vinegar to the detergent compartment of the washing machine can greatly help in eliminating odors.

Application Method

  1. Add 1 cup of vinegar to the detergent compartment of the washing machine.
  2. Proceed with a regular wash.

Caution

  • Using it too frequently can damage the rubber seals, so the frequency of use should be adjusted.

3. Adding a Refreshing Scent with Lemon Juice

Lemon juice is an effective natural antibacterial agent for removing washing machine odors. Mixing lemon juice with water and adding it to the washing machine leaves a refreshing scent.

Application Method

  1. Mix 1 cup of lemon juice with 2 cups of water.
  2. Add this mixture to the washing machine and wash.

Caution

  • Due to its strong acidity, it should be rinsed thoroughly after use. Excessive acidic substances can damage the interior of the washing machine.

Washing Machine Maintenance Tips

Regular maintenance is key to odor prevention. Keeping the door open after washing allows the interior to dry, preventing the growth of mold and bacteria.

Application Method

  1. Always leave the washing machine door open after washing to allow it to dry.
  2. Periodically clean the washing machine filter to prevent residue buildup.

Checkpoint

  • Be careful to avoid water pooling inside the washing machine. Standing water can cause odors.

Checklist: Eco-Friendly Methods for Removing Washing Machine Odors

  1. Leave the door open after using the washing machine.
  2. Clean the washing machine with baking soda monthly.
  3. Use vinegar or lemon juice periodically.
  4. Clean the washing machine filter.
  5. Only load the appropriate amount of laundry.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. What should I do if my washing machine smells bad?
    Start by cleaning the washing machine with baking soda or vinegar. An empty wash can help eliminate odors.

  2. Can I use baking soda and vinegar together?
    It is not recommended to use both together. They react and produce bubbles, so it's better to use them separately.

  3. Why does my laundry still smell after removing odors?
    If laundry has a smell, it may be due to contaminants on the laundry itself. Check the condition of the laundry before washing.

Removing odors from the washing machine can be resolved with simple maintenance. Try the methods introduced today to keep your washing machine clean. We recommend starting with a cleaning using baking soda.
