Midjourney vs DALL-E: Who is the Ultimate AI Art Generator?

March 16, 2026

Midjourney vs DALL-E: Who is the Ultimate AI Art Generator?

These days, AI art generation tools are advancing rapidly, right? So I tried using several tools and became curious about "which one is better?" Especially, Midjourney and DALL-E are being used by many people. So I summarized the features and pros and cons of these two tools.

Midjourney: Creativity and Style

Unique Artwork

Midjourney is particularly specialized in generating unique artwork. From my experience, this tool creates images that stimulate the imagination. For example, when I input the prompt "a cat dancing in space," it produced a truly inventive image. I think this is the strength of Midjourney.

Community and Feedback

Since Midjourney operates on Discord, it's great that users can share their work and provide feedback to each other. The power of this community greatly aids in creativity. I gained a lot of ideas by looking at other people's works.

DALL-E: Realistic Image Generation

Realistic Style

On the other hand, DALL-E seems to excel in generating realistic images. When I input the prompt "a picnic on the beach," it created an image with a very vivid feel. I felt that DALL-E is more suitable when realistic depiction is desired.

Ease of Use

DALL-E has an intuitive interface, making it easy to use. You can create the desired images simply without complicated settings. This makes it accessible for beginners as well. I remember being able to try it out without any pressure when I used it for the first time.

Price and Accessibility: Which is Better?

Cost Comparison

Midjourney operates on a subscription basis, which can be a bit pricey. However, considering the quality of the images provided, I think it's worth the investment. On the other hand, DALL-E is credit-based, so the cost varies depending on usage. If you want to use only as much as you need, DALL-E might be more advantageous.

Accessibility Issues

Midjourney requires the use of Discord, which can be a bit challenging for first-time users. In contrast, DALL-E is web-based, making it easy for anyone to access. Therefore, if you want to try AI art for the first time, I recommend DALL-E.

Conclusion: My Choice?

In conclusion, which tool is better really depends on personal preference. If you want creative art, Midjourney is great, and if you want realistic images, DALL-E might be better. Personally, I think it would be good to alternate between the two tools depending on the situation.
