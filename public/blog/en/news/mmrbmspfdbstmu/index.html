Tips for Regular Maintenance to Remove Washing Machine Odors

It can be really frustrating when your washing machine emits odors every time you use it, right? Especially when your clothes smell bad after washing, it’s truly disappointing. So, I’ve organized some tips for you. Here’s how to remove odors from your washing machine and some regular maintenance tips!

Cleaning the Inside of the Washing Machine

Frequency of Cleaning

From my personal experience, I believe that washing machines should be cleaned at least once a month. Dust and bacteria accumulate inside the washing machine, causing odors to start. I once neglected cleaning, and it resulted in a really unpleasant smell. Since then, I’ve been trying to remember to keep up with the cleaning schedule.

Cleaning Method

Cleaning the washing machine is really simple. Using a washing machine-specific cleaner or a mixture of vinegar and baking soda is effective. From what I’ve tried, adding one cup of vinegar and two tablespoons of baking soda and running the hottest water cycle helps eliminate a lot of the odor. This process is really important, so I recommend you not to forget it!

Managing Laundry

Pre-Wash Check

Before doing laundry, you should definitely check the condition of your laundry. In particular, if wet clothes remain in the washing machine for a long time, they are likely to smell. So, I’ve made it a habit to take them out immediately after washing and hang them up to dry. This small habit has been a great help in odor removal.

Separating Laundry

Additionally, I think it’s important to separate laundry by type. Especially, it’s better not to wash dirty sportswear or towels with other laundry. Doing this has made the odors less noticeable, and the laundry feels cleaner.

Utilizing Essential Accessories

Washing Machine Deodorizer

Using a washing machine-specific deodorizer is also a good method. The deodorizer I’ve tried neutralizes odors inside the washing machine. Even after washing, a fresh scent remains, which feels nice. Regularly using such deodorizers can help prevent unpleasant odors from the washing machine.

Conclusion

I’ve summarized some regular maintenance tips for removing washing machine odors. Cleaning, managing laundry, and utilizing essential accessories are key. Through this, you can use your washing machine more pleasantly. If you have any questions, please leave them in the comments! I think starting with this would be great.
