How to Resolve Jeonse Deposit Return Issues?

March 21, 2026

How to Solve the Jeonse Deposit Return Problem?

The issue of returning the jeonse deposit is a serious concern for many tenants. If the deposit is not returned on time after the contract ends, tenants may experience financial burdens and threats to their housing stability. To resolve this issue, legal knowledge and practical response methods are necessary. In this article, we will examine the procedures and precautions for resolving the jeonse deposit return problem in detail.

Importance of Returning the Jeonse Deposit: Why Should You Know About It?

The issue of returning the jeonse deposit is important for several reasons.

  • Financial Burden: If the return of the deposit is delayed, tenants may struggle to find another residence or manage living expenses. This can lead to serious financial burdens.

    • Action Tip: If the return of the deposit is delayed, immediately prepare alternatives for other housing.

  • Legal Rights: Tenants have the legal right to demand the return of their deposit. According to the lease agreement, landlords must return the deposit within a specified period, and failure to do so can lead to legal action.

    • Checkpoint: Check the deadline for the return of the deposit specified in the contract and act accordingly.

  • Housing Stability: Resolving the deposit return issue allows tenants to find a new residence more stably, contributing to increased housing stability.

Procedure for Requesting the Return of the Jeonse Deposit: How to Proceed?

The process of requesting the return of the jeonse deposit can be divided into several steps.

Step-by-Step Procedure for Requesting the Return of the Deposit

  1. Draft and Deliver an Official Request to the Landlord: The request must clearly state the demand for the return of the deposit, including the date and signature.

    • Example: State in the request, "Please return the deposit within 30 days from the contract termination date."

  2. Check the Landlord's Response: If the landlord refuses or delays the return, it is essential to record the reasons.

    • Checkpoint: Ensure that the landlord's response is received in writing to use as legal evidence.

  3. Follow Legal Procedures: It is advisable to seek the advice of a legal professional if necessary.

    • Action Tip: Plan specific actions through legal consultation.

Legal Response Methods: What Options Are Available?

  • Mediation Application: You can apply for mediation through the Housing Lease Dispute Mediation Committee. This process is relatively simple and quick.

    • Example: When writing the mediation application, include the landlord's contact information and contract details.

  • Litigation Process: If mediation fails or the landlord does not respond, consider litigation. Litigation should be viewed as a last resort.

    • Checkpoint: Consult with a lawyer thoroughly before proceeding with litigation.

  • Consultation Services: You can receive specific advice through legal consultation. Especially for first-time experiences, it is crucial to seek professional help.

Precautions When Returning the Jeonse Deposit: What Should You Check?

To resolve the jeonse deposit return issue, there are several precautions to take.

  • Check the Contract: It is essential to verify the clauses in the contract. Ensure that the clauses regarding the return of the deposit are clearly stated.

    • Action Tip: Highlight the clauses regarding the return of the deposit in the contract.

  • Secure Evidence: Keep all documents and communication records related to the return of the deposit. These documents can serve as important evidence in legal proceedings.

    • Checkpoint: Organize and store emails, text messages, requests, etc., in one place.

  • Evaluate the Landlord's Credibility: It is important to assess the landlord's reliability in advance. Referring to reviews from others or checking the landlord's past transaction history is also a good method.

    • Example: Asking previous tenants about the landlord's credibility can be useful.

Checklist for Returning the Jeonse Deposit: What Should You Prepare?

The following is a checklist for smoothly proceeding with the return of the jeonse deposit:

  1. Prepare the contract and related documents
  2. Send an official request to the landlord
  3. Record the landlord's response to the request
  4. Consult a lawyer if legal action is necessary
  5. Apply for mediation through the Housing Lease Dispute Mediation Committee

Following this checklist will allow you to resolve the issue more systematically.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How should I request the return of the jeonse deposit?

    • You should draft and deliver an official request to the landlord. The request must specify the reason for the deposit return and the deadline.

  2. What legal actions can I take if the landlord does not return the deposit?

    • You can apply for mediation through the Housing Lease Dispute Mediation Committee or proceed with legal action through litigation.

  3. What documents are needed to resolve the deposit return issue?

    • You will need the lease agreement, communication records related to the deposit, and the request letter. All documents should be kept thoroughly.

The issue of returning the jeonse deposit can be complex, but with a systematic approach, it can be resolved. Start by checking your contract and preparing the necessary documents today.
