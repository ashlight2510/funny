Spring Begins with Plum Blossoms and Cornelian Cherries... Let's Go to the Tulip Festival After the Cherry Blossoms

As spring arrives, various flower festivals are preparing to take place across South Korea. This season, which begins with plum and cornelian cherry blossoms, will soon transition to cherry blossoms and tulips, with each region welcoming tourists through its uniquely charming festivals. The Gwangyang Plum Blossom Festival, Gurye Cornelian Cherry Blossom Festival, and Jinhae Cherry Blossom Festival mark the beginning, followed by the tulip festivals held in Everland and Taean, which will highlight the peak of spring.

These festivals contribute not only to flower viewing but also to the revitalization of the local economy and culture. In particular, plum and cornelian cherry blossoms are flowers that herald the calm arrival of spring, providing comfort to many. We are now ready to enjoy this beauty of nature.

Key Summary

  • The Plum Blossom and Cornelian Cherry Blossom Festivals are representative events that signal the start of spring, held in Gwangyang and Gurye.
  • The Cherry Blossom Festival takes place in Jinhae, Hadong, Yeouido, and attracts millions of tourists.
  • The Tulip Festival is held in Everland and Taean, offering visual enjoyment through a variety of flowers.
  • Each festival revitalizes the local economy and provides visitors with a special experience.

Key Scenes

  • Beginning of Spring: The Plum Blossom and Cornelian Cherry Blossom Festivals are significant events that announce the arrival of spring.
  • Tourism Attraction: The Cherry Blossom Festival is a popular event that attracts millions of tourists each year.
  • Diverse Festivals: Various flower festivals, including the Tulip Festival, are held in multiple regions.
  • Revitalization of Local Economy: Festivals provide opportunities to highlight the value of local agricultural products and culture.
