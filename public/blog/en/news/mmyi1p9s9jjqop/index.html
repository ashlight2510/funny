Iksan City Small Business Card Fee Support: Application Method and Conditions Guide

The Necessity of Small Business Card Fee Support

Small businesses need various support to maintain competitiveness amid economic difficulties. In particular, the burden of fees due to the increase in card payments is a significant strain on small businesses. Iksan City's card fee support system alleviates this burden, helping small businesses attract more customers and sustain their operations.

Checkpoints

  • Card fee support provides practical economic assistance to small businesses.
  • The support can also contribute to the revitalization of the local economy.

What is the Card Fee Support System?

Iksan City's card fee support system is a program that partially supports the fees incurred when small businesses accept card payments. The support amount is up to 1 million won, allowing small businesses to reduce their fee burden and focus on management.

Scope of Support

  • Maximum Support Amount: 1 million won
  • Eligible Recipients: Small businesses

How to Apply for Card Fee Support

The application process is simple, but it is essential to prepare the required documents accurately. The necessary documents are as follows:

  1. Business registration certificate
  2. Card payment statement
  3. Identification
  4. Application form

Applications can be made online or offline, each with its pros and cons. Online applications are convenient, while offline applications allow for direct consultations.

Checklist

  • Use a checklist to ensure that no documents are missing when preparing your paperwork.
  • Adhere to the application deadline to avoid missing out on support funds.

Actual Application Case

Mr. A applied online. Thanks to his preparation of the necessary documents, he received the support funds within two weeks and invested them in marketing, increasing his customer base.

Support Conditions and Eligibility

Eligibility for support is limited to small businesses with annual sales of less than 300 million won, with slight variations depending on the industry. Various sectors, including food service and retail, are included, so it is essential to verify whether your industry qualifies for support.

How to Confirm Eligibility

  • Check the business registration certificate to see if your industry is eligible for support.
  • Accurately calculate annual sales to ensure you meet the eligibility criteria.

How to Utilize Support Funds

To effectively utilize the support funds, it is advisable to invest directly in business improvements. For example, funds can be used for marketing campaigns or store interior renovations. This can strengthen customer attraction strategies.

Cautions

  • Misuse of support funds can lead to problems, so it is crucial to establish a budget management and expenditure plan.
  • Create a usage plan and keep records of expenditures for management purposes.

Successful Utilization Case

Mr. B utilized the support funds to develop a new menu and conducted SNS advertising, resulting in a 30% increase in sales.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How often can I receive card fee support?

    • The support cycle is once a year, with a maximum of 1 million won available.

  2. What documents are required for the application?

    • Required documents include a business registration certificate, card payment statement, and identification, with additional documents potentially needed.

  3. What should I do if I do not receive the support funds?

    • You can contact the relevant department for guidance on filing an objection and reapplying.

Conclusion: Apply Now!

Take the opportunity to reduce your economic burden and enhance your competitiveness through Iksan City's card fee support system. Prepare your business registration certificate and card payment statement now and apply!
