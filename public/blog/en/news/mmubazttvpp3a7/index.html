Naver Webtoon Distributes 4.15 Trillion Won Over 5 Years... Importance of the Creative Ecosystem

Recently, Naver Webtoon announced that the revenue distributed to creators over the past five years has reached 4.15 trillion won. This can be interpreted as an important signal emphasizing the health and sustainability of the webtoon ecosystem. Naver Webtoon is pursuing sustainable growth through a virtuous cycle structure among creators, content, and users, and plans to focus on supporting creators with an investment of over 70 billion won this year.

This announcement encompasses various elements that could influence the future of the webtoon industry, going beyond simple revenue distribution. In particular, the investment direction aimed at strengthening competitiveness in the global market and ensuring the sustainability of the creative ecosystem is garnering attention.

Key Highlights

  • Naver Webtoon has distributed over 4.15 trillion won to creators in the past five years.
  • Plans to invest over 70 billion won this year for creator support.
  • Establishing a virtuous cycle structure among creators, content, and users through a "flywheel" strategy.
  • President Kim Yong-soo emphasized the importance of investment for growth in the global market.
  • Ensuring growth potential through management of illegal distribution sites and utilization of various formats.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This announcement comes amid concerns about the slowdown in growth in the webtoon industry. While the webtoon market has rapidly grown in recent years, there have been criticisms regarding the decline in the discovery of new works. In this context, Naver Webtoon is strategizing to enhance the quality of content through collaboration with creators, thereby increasing user influx. President Kim Yong-soo particularly emphasized the importance of investment for sustainable growth and competitiveness in the global market.

Why It Matters

This announcement presents a direction for resolving structural issues in the webtoon industry, going beyond mere revenue distribution. An increase in revenue returned to creators signifies the establishment of an environment where they can produce better content. This, in turn, will provide users with better content, ultimately enhancing the overall health of the webtoon ecosystem. Therefore, supporting creators is becoming a growth strategy for the company, rather than just a social responsibility.

My Perspective

In my view, Naver Webtoon’s approach is very positive. Collaboration with creators will contribute to enhancing the quality of content in the long term, rather than just focusing on revenue distribution. In particular, strengthening the platform for amateur writers and expanding various content formats will play a crucial role in increasing the diversity of the creative ecosystem. However, it remains to be seen whether these changes will actually take place.

Hidden Variables to Watch

A point to note in this announcement is the external factors such as the management of illegal distribution sites. The growth of the webtoon industry must be based on trust between creators and users. Therefore, if the issue of illegal distribution is not resolved, creators may face difficulties in continuously producing quality content.
