Han Kang's 'We Do Not Part' Wins NBCC Award

Han Kang's novel has won the National Book Critics Circle (NBCC) Award. This marks the first time a work by a Korean author has received this honor, which will further elevate the global status of Korean literature. The novel delicately portrays the aftermath of the Jeju 4.3 Incident and the resulting human suffering through the perspectives of three women, with the English version published through the collaboration of translators Lee Ye-won and Paige Morris.

This news is an important event that confirms the international standing of Korean literature, going beyond mere literary achievement. In particular, the NBCC is regarded as a prestigious literary award in the United States, and winning this award demonstrates that Han Kang's work has left a profound impression on American readers.
