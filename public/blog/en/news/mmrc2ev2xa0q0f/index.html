5 Tips for Saving Air Conditioning Electricity Bills in Summer

When summer arrives, air conditioning is essential! However, on the other hand, the electricity bill can be a concern. Especially these days, it seems like electricity bills have gone up a lot. Is there any way to save even a little during this time? So, I’ve compiled some tips for saving electricity bills while using air conditioning in summer.

1. Set the Right Temperature

Setting the air conditioning temperature too low can significantly increase your electricity bill. Personally, I think a temperature between 24 to 26 degrees Celsius is the most suitable. By doing this, I felt cool while saving a lot on my electricity bill. Especially on very hot days, it’s good to set it to 23 degrees initially, and once it cools down quickly, raise it to 25 degrees.

2. Use a Fan Together

While it would be nice to just use the air conditioner, using a fan along with it not only makes it cooler but also helps save on electricity bills. The fan circulates the air, making it feel cooler even at the same temperature. I found it really effective to combine the air conditioner with a fan. I highly recommend trying this!

3. Clean the Filter

If the air conditioner filter is dirty, its performance decreases, which can lead to higher electricity bills. Therefore, it’s good to clean the filter before summer arrives each year. After I cleaned it once, I felt that the air conditioning breeze was much cooler, and it seemed like my electricity bill decreased as well. Cleaning the filter is simple, so I hope you definitely give it a try.

4. Block Out Sunlight

On sunny days, the sunlight coming through the windows can make it really hot, so blocking it with curtains or blinds is a good method. By doing this, I was able to effectively prevent the indoor temperature from rising. It’s a simple tip, but the effect was really significant.

5. Adjust Usage Time

Rather than keeping the air conditioner on all day, it’s better to turn it on only when needed. For example, I adjusted it to only turn on during the hot hours in the afternoon and turned it off in the morning or evening when the temperature is lower, which significantly reduced my electricity bill. By doing this, I was able to reduce unnecessary usage and use the air conditioner more efficiently.

There are various ways to save on air conditioning electricity bills in summer. It would be good to utilize tips such as setting the right temperature, combining with a fan, cleaning the filter, blocking sunlight, and adjusting usage time. Especially in summer, these small efforts can make a big difference, so I hope you definitely give it a try! If you have any questions, please leave a comment.
