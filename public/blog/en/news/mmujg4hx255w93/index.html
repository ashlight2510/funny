“Sundang Husa” Oh Se-hoon, for now, declares candidacy

Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon responded to internal pressure within the People Power Party by declaring his candidacy for the mayoral election on June 3. In a press conference, he emphasized his sense of responsibility towards the citizens of Seoul and the spirit of ‘sundang husa,’ demanding the formation of an innovative election campaign committee. This can be interpreted as a criticism of the party leadership's uncooperative attitude, as well as a demonstration of his will to lead change from Seoul. Mayor Oh’s declaration of candidacy is an important event that goes beyond mere candidate registration, showcasing the complex dynamics of intra-party politics.

Key Summary

  • Mayor Oh Se-hoon declared his candidacy for the mayor of Seoul in response to internal pressure.
  • He criticized the party leadership while demanding the formation of an innovative election campaign committee.
  • The possibility of independent election campaigns at the Seoul party level has been raised.
  • The declaration of candidacy by Rep. Park Soo-min and the pressure from the candidate management committee were significant factors.
  • Future conflicts and cooperation between Mayor Oh and the party leadership are anticipated.

How intra-party conflicts and cooperation unfold during this process will greatly impact the outcome of the upcoming Seoul mayoral election.
