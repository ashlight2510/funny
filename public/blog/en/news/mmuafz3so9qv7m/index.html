“Beyond memory to action”…Declaration of ‘SAN in AN Safe City’ in Ansan

In Ansan, a citizens' promotion committee has been launched to remember the pain of the Sewol Ferry disaster and aims to prioritize life and safety in urban management. Under the slogan “SAN in AN Safe City,” they announced the start of collective actions to reflect citizens' voices in policies and create a safe society. In particular, they emphasize safety issues for socially vulnerable groups such as people with disabilities and migrants, and plan various forums and discussions to address these issues.

This declaration, marking the 12th anniversary of the Sewol Ferry disaster, embodies the will to achieve substantial change beyond mere remembrance. Citizens pledged to make life and safety the core criteria for urban management and to make the lives of those in danger the starting point for policy.

Key Highlights

  • Citizen Participation: 130 civic organizations are participating in efforts to reflect citizens' voices in policies.
  • Protection of Vulnerable Groups: A commitment to prioritize safety issues for socially vulnerable groups such as people with disabilities and migrants.
  • Practical Actions: Plans to discuss specific policy priorities through various activities such as life safety forums and large discussions.
  • Remembering the Sewol Ferry Disaster: A goal to transform the city into a symbol of life safety beyond the pain of the Sewol Ferry disaster.
  • Policy Connection: Establishing a system to connect citizens' voices with policies and regulations.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This declaration comes 12 years after the Sewol Ferry disaster, as life and safety have emerged as the top priorities in the Ansan area. The disaster revealed serious issues in our society's safety systems, and various safety accidents and human rights issues have continued to arise since then. In this context, the voluntary gathering of citizens to take concrete actions to create a safe city is a significant change.

Why It Matters

This declaration goes beyond simply commemorating past pain; it calls for concrete actions for a safe society in the present and future. The efforts of citizens to demand policies and to incorporate the voices of the socially vulnerable are crucial. This can serve as a model for addressing safety issues not just locally, but throughout South Korea.

Voices of the Citizens

The citizens of Ansan aim to directly address safety issues in their community through this declaration. For example, the issue of safety for people with disabilities should be treated not just as protection but as a fundamental area of ensuring the right to life and human rights. This sends an important message to citizens facing similar issues not only in Ansan but in other regions as well.
