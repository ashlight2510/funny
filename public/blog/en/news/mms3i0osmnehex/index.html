Smart Shopping Cost Savings with Card Points

March 16, 2026

Smartly Reducing Shopping Costs with Card Points

These days, with prices rising, my heart sinks every time I go shopping. The things I want are getting more expensive, and I find myself worrying more with each purchase. Is there a way to alleviate these concerns? So, I've organized how to utilize card points. If you use points wisely, you can significantly reduce your shopping costs!

What are Card Points?

Understanding the Basic Concept

Card points refer to the points accumulated when using credit or debit cards. Each card company operates differently, but generally, a certain percentage of the spending amount is credited as points. For example, if a card offers 1% rewards, spending 1,000,000 won would earn you 10,000 won in points. You can use these accumulated points for discounts on your next shopping trip.

The Appeal of Point Accumulation

From my experience, the biggest advantage of accumulating points is that you can receive discounts without spending a lot of money. When you accumulate points using cards at supermarkets or shopping malls that you frequently visit, you can effectively save money on your next shopping trip. This is the charm of card points.

How to Utilize Points

1. Check Your Frequently Used Cards

The first thing you should do is check what benefits your frequently used cards offer. Since the point accumulation rates and usage locations vary, it's useful to check in advance. I usually choose cards with higher accumulation rates for shopping, so this part is really important.

2. Take Advantage of Point Accumulation Events

Card companies often hold point accumulation events. For example, if you make a payment at specific merchants during a certain period, you can earn additional points. By checking these events in advance and planning your shopping accordingly, you can accumulate more points. I always check the card company's app to ensure I don't miss these events.

3. Tips for Using Points

While accumulating points is important, how you utilize them is even more crucial! Some card companies allow you to use points like cash. For instance, some may calculate 1 point as 1 won, so it's worth checking how you can use your accumulated points like cash. Personally, using points this way has significantly reduced my burden while shopping.

Advantages of Shopping with Points

Cost Reduction Effect

Using card points definitely helps reduce shopping costs. From my own experience, I accumulated about 50,000 won in points per month, and utilizing them significantly decreased unnecessary expenses. When I realized this, I felt that such small changes could make a big difference.

Discounts on Repeat Purchases

Additionally, receiving discounts with points also helps economically when making repeat purchases. When buying necessary items, utilizing points can lessen the burden, so I encourage you to actively use them when purchasing essential goods or products you need frequently. I found that doing this made shopping much more enjoyable.

In conclusion, if you utilize card points well, you can save a lot on shopping costs. I've realized how useful points accumulated simply by using a card can be. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I hope you start using card points from your next shopping trip!
