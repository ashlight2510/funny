‘Kim Eo-jun YouTube’ appearance Jeong Cheong-rae “The 45th article of the Serious Crime Investigation Agency law was edited out at the request of the Blue House”

Jeong Cheong-rae, the leader of the Democratic Party, appeared on Kim Eo-jun's YouTube broadcast and revealed the process of editing out Article 45 of the Serious Crime Investigation Agency law (Serious Crime Law). He explained the background of why the provision was maintained without modification at the request of the Blue House, claiming that this reflects the president's commitment to prosecutorial reform. While emphasizing that he coordinated various opinions within the party during the legislative discussion process, he also mentioned the importance of direct communication with the Blue House. However, following the broadcast appearance, some party members have shown distancing reactions, raising concerns about internal conflicts within the party.

This incident is not merely about the passage of a law but is a significant issue that can gauge political trust and the direction of prosecutorial reform. Jeong's remarks could influence the future discussions on prosecutorial reform, which may have a substantial impact on both the political sphere and the public.

This incident occurred amidst active discussions related to the Serious Crime Law. Jeong Cheong-rae's remarks are at the center of this conflict and illustrate the impact of the relationship with the Blue House on the passage of the law.
