President Lee Jae-myung's Surprise Visit to Jinju Central Market···Strawberry Purchase and Hotteok Eating Show “The People Must Be Healthy”

President Lee Jae-myung made a surprise visit to Jinju Central Market located in Jinju, Gyeongnam Province, on the 25th. This market is a traditional market that opened in 1884, and President Lee has visited this place before during his time as a presidential candidate. On this day, he purchased various local foods and listened to the voices of merchants through direct communication. He particularly emphasized the message that “the people must be healthy,” highlighting his connection with the citizens.

This visit can be interpreted not merely as a show, but as an important opportunity to present the government's policy direction through direct communication with market merchants and citizens. President Lee's remarks and actions reveal the current government's willingness to communicate with the public, while also demonstrating interest in revitalizing the local economy.
