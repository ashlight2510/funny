Warning on Conveyor Belt Accidents and Safety: The Message Left by Tu-an's Death

This article discusses the tragic accident involving Tu-an (23 years old) that recently occurred in Icheon City. Tu-an lost his life due to a caught-in accident while working on a conveyor belt without safety devices, and his mother, Kim Mi-sook, head of the Kim Yong-kyun Foundation, was deeply shocked by the reality that similar incidents continue to occur. Ms. Kim emphasized the seriousness of safety issues once again due to this accident and called for accountability and an investigation into the truth.

Tu-an's accident is not just a personal tragedy but a reminder of the industrial safety issues in Korean society. This incident reveals how the safety of migrant workers is often neglected and suggests the need for social discussions regarding their treatment and rights.

Key Summary

  • Tu-an died in an accident while working on a conveyor belt without safety devices.
  • Representative Kim Mi-sook expressed anger over the similarity of her son's death to the current situation and pointed out the seriousness of safety issues.
  • Tu-an's family and the Gyeonggi Migrant Equality Coalition have filed a lawsuit against the company, demanding accountability.
  • This incident calls for social attention to the safety and rights of migrant workers.

Key Scenes

  • Violation of Safety Regulations: There were no emergency switches or safety devices during the conveyor belt operation.
  • Reality of Migrant Workers: Tu-an worked in Korea to support his family, and his death highlights the dangerous working conditions faced by migrant workers.
  • Demand for Accountability: The family and labor organizations are calling for a thorough investigation and punishment of those responsible for the accident.
  • Social Resonance: This incident is likely to spark discussions about the safety issues of migrant workers in Korean society.

Why Background Matters

Tu-an's accident illustrates that safety issues in industrial settings remain serious. Representative Kim Mi-sook feels despair that, despite fighting for safety for seven years after her son lost his life due to safety issues, there has still been no change. The reality that migrant workers must work in dangerous environments suggests that their rights are not adequately protected.
