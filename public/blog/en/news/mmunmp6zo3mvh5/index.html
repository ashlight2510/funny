A frame that shows photos and videos as '3D holograms' has been introduced

Recently, a new frame called 'Musubi', developed by the American startup Looking Glass Factory, has garnered attention. This product features innovative functionality that allows it to display photos and videos like a 3D hologram without the need for a separate headset or special glasses. Users can enjoy images that appear to float in space from various angles, and it is characterized by the ease of transferring content via USB-C. The price is approximately 220,000 KRW, and it is currently being sold at a discounted price on Kickstarter.

This device goes beyond a simple photo frame, proposing a new way of consuming content. In particular, the ability to appreciate precious moments with family or friends in 3D form will be a significant attraction for users. However, it is also necessary to consider the changes this technology may bring and the resulting social responses.

Key Highlights

  • 3D Hologram Technology: Allows viewing photos and videos in 3D without additional equipment.
  • Ease of Use: Easily transfer content after converting it with software on PC or Mac via USB-C.
  • Storage Capacity: Capable of storing 1,000 photos and 30-second video clips.
  • Price: Approximately 220,000 KRW, available for about 140,000 KRW on Kickstarter.
  • Consumer Experience: Provides a new visual experience with images that appear to float in space.

Reasons to Consider the Background

In recent years, hologram technology has rapidly advanced, presenting possibilities for various fields. Particularly, the need for non-face-to-face communication has increased following the COVID-19 pandemic, leading to heightened interest in virtual reality and augmented reality technologies. In this context, Looking Glass Factory has commercialized hologram technology in a form that consumers can easily access. This has the potential to create new consumption trends beyond mere technological innovation.

Why It Matters

This product has the potential to change the way individuals consume photos and videos in their daily lives. In particular, the transition from 2D to 3D in remembering precious moments with family or friends can provide users with a deeper emotional experience. Furthermore, as this technology becomes commercialized, it is likely that other companies will also pursue similar research and development directions. This represents a significant change that could impact various industries in the future.

If the pace of consumer acceptance does not keep up with the speed of technological advancement, the success of this product in the market may be uncertain.

FAQ

Q: What content formats does Musubi support?
A: It supports photos and short video clips (30 seconds).

Q: How do you transfer content?
A: You can convert it using software provided on PC or Mac and then transfer it via USB-C.

Q: What is the price?
A: It is currently being sold for about 140,000 KRW on Kickstarter, with a regular price of approximately 220,000 KRW.

Q: When will this product be released?
