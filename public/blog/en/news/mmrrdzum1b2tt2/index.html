AI Automation with ChatGPT: Real-Life Use Cases

These days, interest in AI is really heating up, right? At first, I was at a loss about what to do, but once I discovered how AI tools like ChatGPT could help in my daily life, a lot changed. So today, I’ve compiled some use cases of ChatGPT that I’ve experienced. I hope this helps you too!

1. Work Automation: Email Writing

Writing Emails Efficiently

When you work, you often have to send emails, right? Especially when you need to convey complex information or multiple details, it can be quite burdensome. What I tried was using ChatGPT to draft my emails.

For example, when I wanted to send an email to coordinate a meeting schedule, I typed, "Please write an email to coordinate the meeting schedule," and it generated a neat and specific email. With just a few minor edits, I could send it right away. Thanks to this method, my email writing time has significantly decreased. I really recommend it!

2. Content Creation: Blog Writing

Brainstorming Ideas

As a blog owner, I’ve always struggled with content ideas. When I wonder, "What should I write about today?" I ask ChatGPT, and it suggests various topics. For instance, when I said, "Give me blog topic ideas about AI," it listed five topics for me.

I chose one of them and started writing, and it turned out to be much easier than I expected. I think it's a really useful tool for those like me who struggle with writing.

3. Learning Assistant: Review and Summarization

Let's Use AI While Studying

These days, you can also use AI while studying. What I do is ask ChatGPT to summarize textbooks or materials. For example, when I input, "Summarize the history of artificial intelligence," it organizes the key points concisely.

Reviewing based on this summarized content helps me understand better and remember longer. Personally, I found this part really useful. It saves time when studying!

4. Solving Everyday Questions: Information Search

Just Ask Your Questions!

There are many things we’re curious about in everyday life, right? Cooking recipes, travel information, and so on. When I ask ChatGPT about these, I get quick responses. For example, when I asked, "What are some important tips for making pasta?" it easily organized several tips for me.

Using AI for even simple questions has made searching for information much easier. I highly recommend it for those who, like me, have a lot of questions!

Conclusion

Today, I summarized various automation cases using ChatGPT. From writing emails, generating blog content, acting as a learning assistant, to solving everyday questions, it can be utilized in many ways. If you make good use of these tools, your daily life will become much more convenient.

Feel free to leave any questions in the comments! And I think you should try this out first~!
