Actor Lee Jae-ryong, Prosecuted for Drunk Driving Charges···‘Kim Ho-jung Prevention Law’ Also Applied

Actor Lee Jae-ryong has been sent to the prosecution on charges of fleeing after causing a drunk driving accident. The incident occurred on the 6th in Gangnam, Seoul, and Lee is suspected of consuming additional alcohol after the accident to evade a breathalyzer test. The police stated that such behavior falls under the revised Road Traffic Act, known as the ‘Kim Ho-jung Prevention Law.’ Lee has admitted to the drunk driving charges but denies the allegations of failure to report after the accident and obstruction of the breathalyzer test.

This case goes beyond a simple drunk driving incident, reigniting discussions about the legal and social responsibilities of celebrities. In particular, it is expected to raise social awareness regarding drunk driving, alongside the background of the implementation of the 'Kim Ho-jung Prevention Law.'

Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident is closely related to the revised Road Traffic Act, or the ‘Kim Ho-jung Prevention Law,’ which was enacted last June. This law prohibits consuming additional alcohol to evade a breathalyzer test after a drunk driving accident. Lee's case serves as an important example of how this law is applied, reminding us of the seriousness of drunk driving.
