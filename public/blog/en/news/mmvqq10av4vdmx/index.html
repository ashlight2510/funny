Lisa Su, AMD CEO, Meeting with Chief AI Advisor Ha Jung-woo: Discussing the Future of Korea's AI Ecosystem

Lisa Su, CEO of AMD, is set to visit Korea to meet with Chief AI Advisor Ha Jung-woo to discuss collaboration opportunities in the domestic AI ecosystem and semiconductor industry. During this meeting, the Korean government's AI three-pillar strategy will be introduced, and opinions will be exchanged regarding collaboration on high-performance computing and AI data centers between AMD and Korean companies. This meeting is expected to explore opportunities for AMD to participate more actively in Korea's AI market, contributing to securing Korea's global AI competitiveness.

This meeting is not just a simple corporate encounter but could serve as an opportunity to elevate Korea's AI ecosystem to the next level. Notably, this is Lisa Su's first visit to Korea, and her direct exploration of collaboration possibilities with Korean AI companies adds significant meaning to the event.

  • This is Su's first visit to Korea, indicating interest in the Korean AI market.

Why Background Matters

This meeting is significant as it underscores the Korean government's commitment to making the AI industry a national strategy. Korea aims to enhance its global competitiveness through the advancement of AI technology, making collaboration with various companies essential. AMD, the second-largest player in the AI accelerator market after NVIDIA, has the opportunity to foster mutual benefits by collaborating with Korea's AI ecosystem.

AI technology is now driving changes across the economy, going beyond mere technological innovation. If such collaboration occurs, it will provide opportunities for Korean companies to further develop.
