NVIDIA's New AI Chip Announcement and Samsung's Role

Recently, NVIDIA unveiled its inference-specific AI chip 'Grok 3 LPU' and a new CPU, revealing that Samsung Electronics is responsible for the production of this chip. CEO Jensen Huang expressed gratitude towards Samsung in this announcement, showcasing aspirations to lead the era of AI agents. This marks a new strategy for NVIDIA and is expected to positively impact Samsung's foundry business.

This announcement is seen as a significant turning point that goes beyond the emergence of new technology, potentially changing the landscape of the AI and semiconductor industries. NVIDIA's goal is to enhance the real-time response speed of AI agents, thereby expanding the scope of AI applications. Meanwhile, Samsung Electronics is seizing the opportunity to overcome sluggishness in its foundry business through AI chip production.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This announcement can be viewed as part of NVIDIA's strategy to gain a foothold in the AI chip market. In recent years, with advancements in AI technology, many companies have begun developing their own AI chips. Samsung Electronics is strengthening its foundry business in line with NVIDIA's strategy.
