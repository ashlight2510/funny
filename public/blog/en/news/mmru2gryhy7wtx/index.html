Hotel Comparison in Bangkok, Thailand: A Perfect Guide by Price Range

When planning a trip, choosing a hotel is always a concern. Bangkok, in particular, has a variety of accommodations, making it overwhelming to decide what to choose. So this time, I've organized hotels in Bangkok by price range. Based on the places I've personally visited and reviews, I'll let you know which hotels are suitable for different travel styles!

1. Budget Accommodation: A Backpacker's Paradise

Hostels Around Khao San Road

The most famous budget accommodation in Bangkok is the hostels around Khao San Road. Prices range from 10,000 to 20,000 KRW per night. Personally, I stayed at 'Bodega Bangkok', and the atmosphere was fantastic. It was easy to communicate with other travelers, and there’s a bar, making it perfect for enjoying the evening.

These hostels are usually close to tourist attractions, making travel convenient. However, they can be noisy, so if you prefer quiet, you should be cautious. When I visited, my roommates weren't too loud, which was nice.

2. Mid-Range Accommodation: For Travelers Seeking Comfort

Asiatique Hotel & Resort

If you're looking for mid-range accommodation, I recommend hotels around 'Asiatique The Riverfront'. Prices are around 40,000 to 80,000 KRW per night, with a good location and clean facilities. Especially, many hotels have swimming pools, which is great for escaping the heat.

The 'Chatrium Hotel Riverside' where I stayed boasted a truly wonderful view! Located by the river, it was lovely to enjoy a beer while looking at the river in the evening. The service was excellent for the price, and the breakfast was delicious, so I was very satisfied.

3. Luxury Accommodation: A Choice for Special Occasions

Louvre Hotel

If you're visiting Bangkok for a special day or anniversary, consider a luxury hotel. Places like 'Mandarin Oriental Bangkok' or 'The Siam' cost over 200,000 KRW per night, but they are worth it. I had an unforgettable experience at 'Siam Kempinski Hotel' once.

The service here was truly top-notch. The rooms were spacious, and the staff was incredibly friendly, taking care of everything I needed. The pool was large and quiet, making it a perfect place to relax. This kind of experience feels like something you buy with money.

Conclusion

Bangkok's hotels offer a wide variety of options depending on your budget. From budget accommodations to luxury hotels, it's important to find a place that suits your style. I hope my experiences help you. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! And if you're planning a trip to Bangkok, it would be good to check out these accommodations first!
