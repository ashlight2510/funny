5 Tips to Lower Your Air Conditioning Electric Bill in Summer

When summer arrives, there are many days when it's hard to live without air conditioning, right? However, using the air conditioner too much can really rack up the electric bill. But we can't just turn it off and suffer through the heat... So, I've compiled some tips that I've personally tried to lower the electric bill from air conditioning in summer. If you refer to these, you can enjoy a cooler summer without the financial burden!

1. Clean the Air Conditioner Filter

Importance of Filter Cleaning

Air conditioner filters can get dirty more easily than you think. When dust accumulates, air circulation is hindered, causing the air conditioner to work harder and increasing your electric bill. Therefore, it's important to clean it regularly.

Personal Experience

After cleaning the filter myself, I really felt like the cold air was coming out better. By cleaning it about once a month, I noticed a reduction in air conditioner usage and a definite savings on my electric bill. Cleaning the filter is easy and simple, so I highly recommend you try it!

2. Set the Right Temperature

Avoid Setting Too Low

Setting the air conditioner to between 24 and 26 degrees Celsius allows you to maintain a comfortable environment while saving on your electric bill. If you set it too low, the air conditioner has to run continuously, leading to higher bills.

Personal Experience

At first, I had it set to 22 degrees, but it was too cold and I was worried about the electric bill. So, I adjusted it to 25 degrees, and surprisingly, it felt cool enough. Setting a proper temperature really makes a difference!

3. Use a Fan Together

Effect of a Fan

Using a fan along with the air conditioner in summer makes it feel much cooler. The fan circulates the air, enhancing the efficiency of the air conditioner.

Actual Experience

When I turned on both the air conditioner and the fan, it felt cooler. Even with a slightly higher air conditioner temperature, the fan circulated the air well, helping to save on the electric bill. I really recommend this combination!

4. Block Out Sunlight

Covering Windows

Windows that let in direct sunlight reduce the efficiency of the air conditioner. By closing curtains or lowering blinds, you can lower the indoor temperature.

My Tip

Since I installed blinds, the indoor temperature has definitely dropped. Especially around noon when the sunlight is strong, I noticed that blocking the windows during that time allows the air conditioner to run less. It's a small change, but it has a big effect!

5. Get Regular Inspections

Professional Touch

As air conditioners age, they are prone to breakdowns, and their efficiency decreases, leading to higher electric bills. It's important to get regular inspections.

My Experience

Once, after getting my air conditioner inspected, they refilled the refrigerant, and it started blowing really cold air again. Regular inspections might seem bothersome, but in the long run, it's a way to save on your electric bill.

I've summarized ways to lower your air conditioning electric bill in summer, and I hope it helps! By using the air conditioner efficiently, you can maintain coolness while reducing the burden of electric bills. If you have any questions, leave a comment! It would be good to start with these tips.
