Semiconductor Stocks Rise Again After the Shock of War

Recently, semiconductor stocks have shown a rapid recovery, attracting the attention of investors. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix have seen their stock prices recover to 200,000 won and 1,000,000 won, respectively, signaling positive trends even amidst the unstable market conditions following the outbreak of war. Additionally, the stock price of Micron, a major US semiconductor company, is also on the rise, contributing to a positive outlook for the semiconductor industry as a whole.

This change is not just a simple increase in stock prices; it has sparked expectations for the overall recovery of the semiconductor industry and its future prospects. In this article, we will explore the reasons behind this recovery, the stakeholders affected, and the points we should pay attention to.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The recovery of semiconductor stocks has emerged amidst a generally shaken stock market following the outbreak of the US-Iran war. Initially, semiconductor companies saw significant declines in stock prices due to uncertainty, but over time, positive forecasts regarding semiconductor demand have resurfaced. Notably, the rise in Micron's stock price indicates that demand for memory semiconductors remains strong, positively impacting Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix as well.

Why It Matters

The semiconductor industry plays a crucial role in the modern economy. Its recovery goes beyond mere stock price rebounds; it can contribute to global supply chain stability and technological advancement. Furthermore, as the stock prices of semiconductor-related companies rise, there is a higher likelihood of increased investment and job creation in these firms, which can have a positive impact on the overall economy.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the current recovery of semiconductor stocks can be interpreted not just as a rise in stock prices but as a signal of broader economic recovery. The fact that semiconductor demand remains strong even in uncertain global economic conditions can provide a sense of stability for investors. However, it remains uncertain whether this recovery will continue, and there is still a risk of decline due to external variables.

Hidden Variables to Watch

While the recovery of the semiconductor industry is being viewed positively, there are still variables such as global supply chain instability and rising raw material prices. Additionally, the impact of geopolitical risks, such as war, on the semiconductor market cannot be overlooked. Therefore, it is important to focus not just on short-term stock price increases but to carefully observe long-term market trends.
