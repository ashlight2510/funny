Essential Methods for Odor Removal Through Washing Machine Cleaning

Removing Odors Through Washing Machine Cleaning: Essential Methods You Should Know

Strange smells coming from the washing machine can be really bothersome, right? Even after washing clothes cleanly, there are times when I have to wash them again due to the smell from the machine. I thought I was the only one, but it turns out my friends have the same concerns. So, I’ve organized this information. Let me share how to remove odors through washing machine cleaning.

Cleaning the Inside of the Washing Machine

Cleaning the Filter and Drum

First, it’s important to clean the inside of the washing machine. Dust and foreign substances accumulated in the filter and drum are the main culprits of the odor. I make it a point to clean the washing machine filter at least once a month. It’s quite simple; just detach the filter and rinse it under running water. At first, it felt like a hassle, but now it’s become a habit. Mixing water and vinegar and running the drum also effectively removes stubborn stains.

Using Washing Machine-Specific Detergent

Using a detergent specifically designed for washing machine cleaning is also a good method. From my experience, it was much more effective than regular detergent. When I add the detergent and run a hot water wash cycle, the smell completely disappears. While doing this, I realized that washing machines need to be maintained just like people do. If you neglect it, it will eventually come back to haunt you!

Cleaning the Outside

Checking the Rubber Gasket

You also need to check the rubber gasket of the washing machine. If water accumulates in the rubber gasket and mold starts to grow, that’s when the smell begins. I make sure to wipe the rubber gasket thoroughly to prevent any moisture from remaining. It’s easy to do with a wet wipe, so this part is manageable. After cleaning, I noticed a significant reduction in odor.

Removing Dust and Foreign Substances

You should also keep the area around the washing machine clean. If dust accumulates behind or underneath the washing machine, mold can easily develop. Therefore, it’s good to clean it periodically. Personally, I like to clean on weekends to start fresh.

Washing Machine Usage Habits

Leaving the Door Open After Washing

You should develop the habit of leaving the door open after washing. This allows air to circulate inside, reducing the chances of mold growth. At first, I found it bothersome to leave the door open, but after sticking to it, I noticed less odor. It seems that small habits can lead to big changes.

Washing the Right Amount

Finally, the amount of laundry is also important. If you overload the machine, it won’t wash properly and the smell will worsen. I try to stick to the appropriate amount when doing laundry. This part is not as easy as it sounds, but I’m getting used to it with consistent effort.

Conclusion

I’ve realized that cleaning the washing machine is simpler than expected yet very important. I’ve organized methods to remove odors through cleaning, and I think it’s a good idea to start with these. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment!
