Three Vietnamese Arrested for Manufacturing Drugs from Smuggled Raw Materials in Residential Areas

Recently, three Vietnamese nationals were arrested in Incheon for allegedly smuggling raw materials to produce drugs and manufacturing and distributing drugs in residential areas. They are illegal immigrants and reportedly smuggled 5.4 kg of raw materials from Vietnam between January and December 2025, producing drugs in a secretly installed manufacturing facility. This incident marks the first case of uncovering the entire process of smuggling raw materials, manufacturing, and distributing drugs in the country, highlighting a new method employed by drug crime organizations.

This incident goes beyond mere criminal activity, indicating serious implications for the safety and health of our society. Drug manufacturing in residential areas poses direct risks to nearby residents, and the distribution of drugs can have negative effects across society. Therefore, this case serves as a reminder of the need for public safety and crime prevention beyond just a criminal incident.

Key Summary

  • Three Vietnamese nationals were arrested for smuggling raw materials and manufacturing and distributing drugs in residential areas.
  • They smuggled 5.4 kg of raw materials to produce drugs worth approximately 880 million won.
  • This incident is the first case to uncover the entire process of smuggling, manufacturing, and distributing raw materials.
  • The drug manufacturing facility was located in a residential area, raising concerns about potential human casualties.
  • Incheon Airport Customs is paying attention to the new methods of drug crime organizations.
