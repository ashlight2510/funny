Common Mistakes When Choosing Engine Oil for Your Car and How to Avoid Them

Car engine oil directly affects the performance and lifespan of your vehicle. Choosing the right engine oil can contribute to better fuel efficiency and lower maintenance costs. However, many drivers often make mistakes when selecting engine oil. In this article, we will explore how to avoid these mistakes and choose the right engine oil.

Importance and Role of Engine Oil

Engine oil is essential for protecting the engine and maintaining its performance. It reduces friction and heat generated inside the engine, removes contaminants, and lubricates engine components. Choosing the appropriate viscosity and type of oil can extend engine life and maintain efficient fuel economy.

  • Checkpoint: Always check the oil specifications stated in your vehicle's manual. For example, certain vehicles may recommend synthetic oil with a viscosity of 5W-30 for high-temperature performance. Ignoring viscosity and type and using a different oil can lead to engine damage.

Five Common Mistakes When Choosing Engine Oil

1. Ignoring Manufacturer Recommended Oil

Using oil that is compatible with your vehicle's design is crucial. Ignoring the oil specifications and types recommended by the manufacturer can lead to decreased engine performance.

  • Action Tip: It is essential to check the vehicle manual and use the recommended oil. Even similar oils can have performance differences, so arbitrarily using a different oil can be risky.

2. Ignoring Oil Change Intervals

Contaminated oil can cause engine damage. Regular inspections and changes are necessary, and the change interval should be adjusted based on driving conditions.

  • Example: If you frequently drive in congested urban areas, it is advisable to set the oil change interval to 5,000 km. Follow the intervals specified in the manual but adjust based on driving conditions.

3. Using Low-Quality Oil

Low-quality oil can lead to engine wear. It is important to check the brand and certifications and not just consider the price.

  • Checkpoint: Avoid using inexpensive oils that are not certified. Choose oil from brands that guarantee quality.

4. Mixing Different Oils

Mixing oils should be avoided due to compatibility issues with oil components. It is best to use oil of the same brand and type.

  • Action Tip: When changing oil, it is best to use only one type of oil. Mixing can lead to performance degradation and negatively impact the engine.

5. Neglecting Oil Filter Changes

To block contaminants and protect the engine, the oil filter should also be changed. Always change the filter during an oil change, and pay attention to the quality of the filter.

  • Example: Not changing the filter during an oil change can degrade the oil's performance and potentially damage the engine.

How to Choose the Right Engine Oil

To select the right engine oil, you need to understand the basic information. It is necessary to understand the differences between viscosity, synthetic oil, and mineral oil, and to check the manufacturer's recommended oil.

  • Action Tip: Use the vehicle manual and online resources to select the appropriate oil. Consider the driving environment as well. The performance of the required oil may differ between urban driving and highway driving.

Engine Oil Selection Checklist

Use the following checklist when selecting car engine oil:

  1. Check the manufacturer's recommended oil
  2. Verify the oil viscosity
  3. Plan the oil change interval
  4. Confirm whether to change the oil filter
  5. Choose oil suitable for the driving environment

This checklist can help reduce mistakes and take necessary actions to protect the engine.

Engine Oil Comparison Table

Oil Type Advantages Disadvantages
Synthetic Oil High performance, long change intervals Expensive
Mineral Oil Affordable Short change intervals, lower performance
Semi-Synthetic Moderate price, decent performance Not as high performance as synthetic oil

This table will help you compare the advantages and disadvantages of each oil type and choose the optimal oil for your vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How often should I change my engine oil?

    • It is generally recommended to change it every 5,000 to 10,000 km. Adjustments may be needed based on driving conditions.

  2. What is the difference between synthetic and mineral oil?

    • Synthetic oil is artificially manufactured and offers superior performance, while mineral oil is extracted from natural sources. Synthetic oil provides higher performance.

  3. Should I always change the filter when changing oil?

    • Yes, changing the oil filter along with the oil helps protect the engine. The quality of the filter is also important, so pay attention to it.

Conclusion

Based on the information mentioned in this article, take the first steps to reduce mistakes when selecting car engine oil and protect your engine. Start by checking your vehicle manual and selecting the recommended oil.
