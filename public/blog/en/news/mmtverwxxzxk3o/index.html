Jeong Cheong-rae Eliminates Possibility of Investigation Intervention with Amendments to Prosecution Reform Bill

On the 17th, Jeong Cheong-rae, the leader of the Democratic Party, announced at an emergency press conference that several toxic clauses in the prosecution reform bill, which establishes the Serious Crimes Investigation Agency and the Public Prosecution Agency, have been deleted. He stated that this amendment reflects the public's concerns and emphasized the complete separation of investigation and prosecution. Additionally, President Lee Jae-myung expressed his commitment to pushing for reforms and mentioned that the agreement could be amended multiple times if necessary. These changes are expected to play a crucial role in calming the controversy over prosecution reform within the ruling party and completing the legislative process for the Democratic Party.

This news could serve as an important milestone in redefining the direction of prosecution reform and the political position of the Democratic Party. Jeong's announcement goes beyond merely amending the bill; it will also influence future political cooperation and conflicts.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This announcement comes amid recent controversies regarding the retreat of prosecution reform legislation within the ruling party. After submitting the bill to establish the Serious Crimes Investigation Agency and the Public Prosecution Agency, the Democratic Party underwent a process of amending the bill through internal opinion gathering and party-government consultations. Jeong emphasized that these amendments reflect public concerns and aimed to block the possibility of prosecutor intervention in investigations.

Why It Matters

Prosecution reform is a highly sensitive topic in Korean politics. The separation of investigative and prosecutorial powers presents an opportunity to secure political neutrality and fairness. Additionally, it could strengthen the Democratic Party's political position. Furthermore, the amendment of the reform bill is expected to contribute to resolving internal conflicts within the ruling party.
