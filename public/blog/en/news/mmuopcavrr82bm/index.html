Recently, the conflict between the Democratic Party and the government surrounding prosecution reform has been temporarily resolved; however, the trust between the party and the Blue House has been significantly damaged in the process. President Lee Jae-myung pointed out the lack of management in the discussion process regarding the establishment of the Serious Crimes Investigation Agency and the Public Prosecution Service, expressing concern over the reasons for the escalation of the conflict and the resulting loss of trust. As differences emerged between Representative Jeong Cheong-rae and hardline members within the party, President Lee attempted to intervene directly to resolve the issue, emphasizing the lack of internal coordination within the party.

This incident is noteworthy not only as a simple policy discussion issue but also for the serious damage it has inflicted on the trust relationship between the party and the Blue House. This conflict could have significant implications for the Democratic Party's political standing and the government's policy implementation in the future.
