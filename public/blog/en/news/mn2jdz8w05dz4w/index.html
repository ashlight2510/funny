Gyeonggi Provincial Council, First in the Nation to Promote 'Support for Families of Foreign Workers Who Died from Industrial Accidents'

The Gyeonggi Provincial Council has announced a legislative notice for an ordinance aimed at supporting the families of foreign workers who have died in industrial accidents. This ordinance encompasses comprehensive support from entry procedures to funerals and repatriation of remains, addressing various difficulties faced by the families of foreign workers who have died due to industrial accidents. Lawmaker Yoo Ho-jun emphasized that the establishment of this ordinance reflects the need for humanitarian support in Gyeonggi Province, where many foreign workers reside.

The ordinance is scheduled to be reviewed at the provincial council's temporary session next month and is expected to play a significant role in resolving the language barriers and complex administrative procedures faced by the families of foreign workers.

Key Summary

  • Gyeonggi Provincial Council announces legislative notice for 'Support Ordinance for Families of Foreign Workers Who Died from Industrial Accidents.'
  • Includes policies to support family members' entry, funerals, and repatriation of remains.
  • Establishes a rapid response system for incidents involving the deaths of foreign workers.
  • The ordinance is set to be reviewed at the provincial council's temporary session next month.

Key Scenes

  • Family Support: Assistance with entry and funeral procedures for families.
  • Rapid Response System: Establishment of a cooperative system with relevant agencies.
  • Investigation and Situation Assessment: Includes surveys on the current status of family support.
  • Language Barrier Resolution: Provision of information through interpretation and counseling support.

Why Background Matters

This ordinance reflects the reality that foreign workers in Gyeonggi Province are employed across various industries. Particularly, as incidents of death due to industrial accidents continue to occur, many families reside overseas, highlighting the difficulties they face. In a situation where families do not receive adequate support due to language and administrative complexities, the Gyeonggi Provincial Council sought to establish a legal basis to address this issue.

Why It Matters

This ordinance represents an important first step not only in providing legal support but also in ensuring the rights and safety of foreign workers. Foreign workers are vital members of our society, and creating a safe working environment for them is a collective responsibility. Additionally, alleviating the psychological pain and practical difficulties faced by families deepens the meaning of social solidarity.

Important Point

In my view, the true value of this ordinance lies in its potential to contribute to a change in perception towards foreign workers beyond mere support. If this ordinance is passed, it will foster an environment where foreign workers and their families are respected and protected within Korean society. Ultimately, this can lead to enhanced safety and welfare for our entire society.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The limitation of this ordinance lies in the feasibility of actual support implementation. Even if the ordinance is passed, without securing the necessary budget and personnel, practical support may be difficult to achieve. Therefore, systematic budget allocation and personnel management for family support are essential.

Current Points to Check

  • Attention should be paid to how the ordinance will be reviewed in the provincial council's temporary session next month.
  • It is also necessary to examine plans for securing budget and personnel to enhance the feasibility of the ordinance.

A: The ordinance is scheduled to be reviewed in the provincial council's temporary session next month and will be implemented immediately if passed.

Q: How will family support be provided?
A: Support will include assistance with entry procedures, funeral arrangements, repatriation of remains, and will also encompass interpretation and counseling services.

Q: What are the statistics on deaths of foreign workers?
A: Specific statistics regarding deaths of foreign workers in Gyeonggi Province will be investigated according to the ordinance.

This ordinance presents an opportunity for substantial change for foreign workers and their families. It is important to closely monitor the progress moving forward.
