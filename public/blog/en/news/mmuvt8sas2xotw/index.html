Judo's Kim Min-jong and Heo Mi-mi secure Asian Games qualification

Ahead of the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games, Kim Min-jong and Heo Mi-mi each won their respective categories in the judo national team selection tournament, securing their spots for the competition. Kim Min-jong defeated Lee Seung-yeop in the men's +100kg category, while Heo Mi-mi triumphed over Shin Yu-na in the women's 57kg category. Both athletes have already made their mark in international competitions, shining a light on the future of Korean judo.

This selection tournament has established itself as a significant event that greatly influences not only the athletes' careers but also the nation's prestige. Given that both athletes have already achieved success on the international stage, expectations are high for their performance at the Asian Games.

Key Summary

  • Kim Min-jong and Heo Mi-mi selected as representatives for the 2026 Asian Games judo team.
  • Kim Min-jong won in the men's +100kg category, and Heo Mi-mi won in the women's 57kg category.

Key Highlights

  • Kim Min-jong: Men's +100kg representative, World Ranking No. 2.
  • Heo Mi-mi: Women's 57kg representative, World Ranking No. 10.
  • Both athletes secured spots for the Asian Games and the Asian Judo Championships.
  • Kim Min-jong won in overtime, while Heo Mi-mi secured victory with a decisive point.
  • The Korea Judo Association confirmed the Asian Games roster based on these results.
