Why Background Matters

The film reflects various crises faced by humanity in modern society—climate change, ecosystem destruction, etc. In particular, with the emergence of the microorganism ‘Astro Phage’ threatening Earth, humanity’s future has become uncertain, leading Grace to participate in a transnational project. This is not just a simple SF story but offers a new perspective by metaphorically relating our societal issues to a cosmic scale.

Why It Matters

This film breaks away from traditional formulas of the SF genre, emphasizing the importance of solitude and solidarity in space. It provides an opportunity for the audience to reflect on human nature and the value of friendship without requiring scientific knowledge. In highlighting the need for communication and cooperation in modern society, this film plays an important role in conveying a social message beyond mere entertainment.
