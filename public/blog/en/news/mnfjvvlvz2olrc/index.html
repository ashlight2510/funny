From Cherry Blossoms to Baby Bears... Bear Tree Park's 'Spring Flower Festival' Opens

Bear Tree Park will host a spring flower festival from the 4th to June 7th. This festival includes various experiential programs, performances, and ecological education, providing an opportunity to enjoy the changing floral displays alongside cherry blossoms. In particular, it is expected to positively impact the local economy through collaboration with local businesses.

This festival goes beyond mere flower viewing, offering educational and experiential programs for families, enhancing understanding of the ecosystem. These diverse programs add to the festival's appeal and serve as an important element in promoting coexistence with the local community.

Key Summary

  • Bear Tree Park to hold a spring flower festival from the 4th to June 7th.
  • Various experiential programs, performances, and ecological education programs will be offered.
  • Collaboration with local businesses to promote local economic revitalization.
  • Family-friendly content such as baby bear ecological classrooms and special tours.
  • Cherry blossoms and various spring flowers will bloom sequentially to welcome visitors.

Key Highlights

  • Experiential Programs: Various experiences such as making cherry blossom soap and spring flower keychains.
  • Local Coexistence: Collaboration with the Sejong Rural Complex Support Center and participation of local businesses.
  • Educational Value: Opportunities for ecological education through the baby bear ecological classroom.
  • Family-Friendly Performances: Special performances on Children's Day to attract family audiences.
  • Special Tours: Unique experiences through the Chairman's special tour.

Why Background Matters

The spring flower festival is an annual event focused on highlighting the beauty of nature and the importance of ecological education. Especially in recent years, as the connection with nature becomes increasingly important, such festivals play a role in strengthening ties with the local community beyond mere tourism.

Moreover, collaboration with local businesses is a vital way to revitalize the local economy, with many visitors expected to frequent local shops during the festival period. This will also have a positive impact on local residents.

Why It Matters

This festival emphasizes the importance of both local economy and ecological education, going beyond simple flower viewing. As environmental protection and local coexistence emerge as significant social issues, such festivals provide opportunities that can positively impact both local residents and visitors.

Additionally, educational programs like the baby bear ecological classroom can enhance children's understanding of nature and ecosystems, playing a crucial role in raising environmental awareness in the next generation.

Points to Note

In my view, the most noteworthy aspect of this festival is the 'baby bear ecological classroom.' This program offers participants a chance to observe baby bears while receiving education about ecology, providing an important experience that can instill curiosity and love for nature in children.

Points to Check Now

  • Program Reservations: Advance reservations through Naver Ticket are required, so it’s important to check and book ahead.

FAQ

Q1: What flowers will bloom during the festival?
A1: Various cherry blossoms, including king cherry blossoms, weeping cherry blossoms, and double cherry blossoms, along with Korean quince, azaleas, and roses, will bloom sequentially during the festival.

Q2: How do I reserve programs?
A2: Participation in programs requires advance reservations through Naver Ticket.

Q3: When is the baby bear ecological classroom held?
A3: The baby bear ecological classroom will operate every weekend from the 23rd of next month until the end of June.

Q4: How can I participate in the special tour?
A4: The Chairman's special tour is a paid program that requires advance reservation.
