In this article, we will summarize the background and context of the incident, as well as the key points we should pay attention to.

Article Summary

Background and Context

This incident did not simply arise from a conflict between a tenant and an owner. In recent years, issues of workplace violence and harassment have emerged as significant social issues, prompting many companies to make efforts to ensure the safety of their employees. However, such problems still occur seriously in some workplaces, and this incident can be viewed as one of them. Notably, A's justification for the assault starkly reveals the imbalance of power dynamics in the workplace.

Why This Issue is Important

This incident serves as a reminder of the seriousness of violence that can occur in the workplace, going beyond a mere assault case. Many people tend to take workplace abuse of power or violence lightly, but it can pose a direct threat to individual lives and safety. In particular, the extreme outcome of C's loss of sight underscores how serious the issue of workplace violence is. Through this incident, we feel the necessity for regulations and education related to the prevention of workplace violence.
