Smart Ways to Reduce Summer Air Conditioner Electricity Bills

March 15, 2026

Smart Ways to Reduce Air Conditioning Electricity Bills in Summer

As summer approaches, the use of air conditioning increases, leading to worries about electricity bills. I have also struggled with this issue every summer, pondering the question, "How can I reduce my electricity bill?" So, I have compiled various methods. If you want to lower your air conditioning electricity bills this summer, I recommend referring to these tips!

1. Clean the Air Conditioner Filter

Importance of Filter Cleaning

When the air conditioner's filter gets dirty, the airflow becomes poor, causing the machine to work harder. This results in higher electricity bills. Keeping the filter clean improves the air conditioner's efficiency, which can help reduce your electricity costs.

Personal Experience

After cleaning the filter, I found it really effective. The air conditioner cooled down quickly after cleaning. Cleaning the filter is simpler than you might think, so it's good to do it regularly. I recommend cleaning it once a month.

2. Set the Appropriate Temperature

What Temperature Should the Air Conditioner Be?

Setting the air conditioner's temperature too low can significantly increase your electricity bill. Typically, setting it between 26 to 28 degrees Celsius helps save energy. If the air conditioner is too cold, it can also be unhealthy for your body.

My Experience

I tried setting it to 26 degrees, and it was cooler than I expected. It was a slightly hotter day, but I remember that my electricity bill was definitely lower than when I set it to 24 degrees. So, I hope you maintain an appropriate temperature and avoid setting it too low!

3. Use with a Fan

Air Conditioner + Fan Combination

Running only the air conditioner can be expensive, so using a fan together is a good method. When you turn on the fan, the cool air spreads evenly throughout the room, which can reduce the time you need to use the air conditioner.

Results from Practice

When I used the air conditioner while turning on the fan, the usage time decreased significantly. Thanks to the fan, I could set the air conditioner to a slightly higher temperature and still feel cool. I really recommend this combination!

4. Turn It Off When Not in Use

Habit of Turning Off the Air Conditioner

You must turn off the air conditioner when it's not in use. If you're not home or while sleeping, turning it off and only using it when necessary can greatly reduce your electricity bill.

Change in My Behavior

At first, it was annoying to turn off the air conditioner, but I quickly adapted because it cools down quickly when I turn it back on. By reducing unnecessary waste, I noticed a significant decrease in my electricity bill.

Conclusion

Worries about electricity bills while using air conditioning in summer are common for everyone. I hope you can utilize the methods introduced above. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I hope you have a cool and enjoyable summer by using your air conditioner smartly this summer!
