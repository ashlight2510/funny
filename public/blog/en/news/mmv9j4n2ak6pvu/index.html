Netanyahu's Message to the Iranian People and US Concerns

Recently, on the occasion of Iran's New Year holiday, Nowruz, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu urged the Iranian people to come out and enjoy the festivities. However, at the same time, it has been reported that US diplomats were warned that if anti-government protests occur in Iran, the protesters would be "massacred." In the context of the assassination of Iran's security chief Ali Larijani, Netanyahu's comments are interpreted as an attempt to incite anti-government protests within Iran.

This news reflects the political tensions in the Middle East and the instability within Iran, providing significant implications for the safety of the Iranian people and the potential for international intervention.
