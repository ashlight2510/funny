Fun Savings: Earn Interest While Exercising and Playing Games

Recently, banks have introduced unique savings products that go beyond simple financial offerings, encouraging customer participation. Hana Bank operates the 'Challenge 365 Savings', which offers interest rates based on the number of steps taken, and Woori Bank has launched products like 'Woori Bingo Savings' that incorporate game elements. These changes are interpreted as strategies to attract customers amid intense competition in the savings and deposit market. In particular, various attempts are being made to capture the interest of the younger generation, given the high demand for safe asset management.

Unlike traditional savings products, these new offerings add fun elements that allow customers to participate directly, enhancing the appeal of financial products. However, it should not be overlooked that, in the current low-interest-rate environment, the actual benefits for customers are limited.

  • Recently, banks have launched savings products that combine games and exercise.
  • Hana Bank's 'Challenge 365 Savings' offers interest rates based on step counts, while Woori Bank's 'Woori Bingo Savings' provides preferential rates through games.

  • Fun elements that encourage customer participation are playing a crucial role even in a low-interest-rate environment.

Key Scenes

  • Increase in Participatory Savings Products: Various savings products that encourage customer participation are being launched.
  • Targeting Young Customers: Banks aim to expand their customer base through products aimed at the younger generation.
  • Harmony of Interest and Fun: While base interest rates are low, the structure is designed to allow customers to increase their rates through participation.
  • Interests of Consumers and Banks: Consumers seek fun and interest, while banks aim for customer acquisition and increased app usage.
  • Limits of Safety and Profit: While savings products are safe, the actual profits are limited, which is a factor consumers need to consider.

Why Background Matters

The savings and deposit market has attracted customer interest in recent years due to rising interest rates. Following the Bank of Korea's rate hike in 2022, demand for savings and deposits surged, but now, with lower interest rates, it is challenging to capture customer attention. As a result, banks are exploring various ways to encourage customer participation, leading to the emergence of unique products that combine games and exercise, moving away from monotonous traditional savings products.

Why It Matters

These changes go beyond merely diversifying financial products; they are innovatively transforming how customers engage with finance. Particularly, encouraging the younger generation to take an interest in financial products is directly linked to the future growth potential of banks. These savings products offer customers both fun and interest, while providing banks with opportunities to enhance customer loyalty.

Personal Comment

In my view, these savings products can serve as an important means of strengthening communication with customers, transcending the boundaries of simple financial products. Especially for the younger generation, accessing financial products through fun activities like games and exercise can increase interest in finance and, in the long run, foster a greater interest in financial management. However, the key question will be how much real benefit these products can actually provide to customers.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While unique savings products may appear attractive to consumers, it is easy to overlook the small monthly contribution limits and the limited actual interest rates. Additionally, there is a need to analyze how these products will affect customers' financial habits. In the long term, it is important to guide customers to develop a deeper understanding of financial products beyond merely seeking fun.

Points to Check Now

It is important to compare the interest rates and conditions of current financial products and choose a savings product that suits you. In particular, while savings products that include game or exercise elements can add fun, it is necessary to closely examine how much actual benefit they provide.

FAQ

Q: How do I sign up for unique savings products?
A: You can sign up through each bank's mobile app or website. Be sure to check the conditions and interest rates upon signing up.

Q: What are the conditions for preferential interest rates?
A: Generally, you need to complete specific missions or achieve a set number of steps to receive preferential interest rates.

Q: Are these products safe?
A: Generally, savings and deposits guarantee the principal, but low interest rates may limit actual profits.

Q: Do these products actually provide benefits?
A: While they add fun elements, considering the interest rates and monthly contribution limits, they may have limitations as a primary financial management tool.
