The Basics are Essential, Plus 'One More Role'... Coach Hong Myung-bo Wants 'Multi-Position' Players

Coach Hong Myung-bo is emphasizing the importance of 'multi-position' players for the national football team ahead of the World Cup. In a situation where the midfield resources are inevitably reduced due to injuries, the likelihood of including players who can perform in various positions in the final roster has increased. During the announcement of the A-match roster, Coach Hong expressed his intention to prioritize players who can take on various roles beyond their primary positions, stating, "The final roster has not yet been determined."

This decision is a strategy to prepare for injuries and unexpected variables while preparing for the 2026 North and Central America World Cup, where players' versatility is becoming a decisive factor for selection. In particular, with the reduction of midfield resources due to injuries, the competition between attacking midfielders Hong Hyun-seok and Lee Dong-kyung is representative, and Coach Hong is evaluating the players' ability to perform various roles through this competition.

Key Highlights

  • The reduction of midfield resources due to injuries is increasing the need for 'multi-position' players.
  • Coach Hong Myung-bo has stated that he will prioritize selecting players who can perform in various positions.
  • The A-match roster consists of 27 players, and the final roster will be adjusted based on the condition of injured players.
  • The competition between Hong Hyun-seok and Lee Dong-kyung is serving as an important evaluation criterion for the ability to perform roles beyond their primary positions.
  • Players' versatility is expected to have a direct impact on the outcomes in the World Cup qualifiers.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This issue has arisen ahead of the 2026 North and Central America World Cup. The World Cup is the pinnacle stage for players and an opportunity to elevate the country's status. However, with several key players' participation becoming uncertain due to injuries, Coach Hong has placed greater emphasis on players' versatility. This decision will significantly impact not only the evaluation of players' skills but also the overall strategy and tactics of the team.

Why It Matters

This news provides insights into the overall operation and strategy of the national football team, going beyond just player selection. When there are gaps due to injuries, how to overcome them is a critical challenge for all sports teams. Moreover, success on the grand stage of the World Cup is directly linked to the country's image, making players' multi-position abilities an essential factor.

Players' Diverse Roles

In my view, Coach Hong Myung-bo's approach is a positive change that offers players more opportunities and encourages them to perform various roles. For example, Hong Hyun-seok is an attacking midfielder but can also play as a central midfielder and winger, allowing for flexible responses to tactical changes in the team. This will be a significant help in tactical adjustments during actual matches.

Hidden Variables to Watch

However, this multi-position selection strategy may not always yield positive results. When players with specialization in specific positions are needed, versatile players may not fully execute those roles, which is a variable that cannot be overlooked. Therefore, it is necessary to have criteria for evaluating players' specialization in addition to their ability to perform roles.

Current Points to Check

Currently, the multi-position abilities of players that Coach Hong Myung-bo is focusing on will significantly impact the outcomes in the World Cup. Therefore, it is important to closely observe how these players are utilized in upcoming A-matches. In particular, monitoring the recovery status of injured players and the resulting changes in the final roster is essential.
