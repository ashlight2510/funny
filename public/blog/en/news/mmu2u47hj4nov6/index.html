Pilot of a private airline stabbed to death, suspect is a former colleague

A tragic incident occurred in Busan where a pilot of a private airline was stabbed to death. The victim, Mr. O, was found in the hallway of an apartment, and the suspect, Mr. K, is identified as a former co-pilot who had worked at the same airline. It appears that Mr. K, who was excluded from flying due to mental health issues, committed the crime out of resentment. This incident raises serious concerns about safety and mental health management in the aviation industry.

This case is not just a simple crime; it serves as a reminder of the importance of mental health management for pilots. To prevent the recurrence of tragic incidents from the past, a system for early detection and proper management of mental health issues is necessary.
