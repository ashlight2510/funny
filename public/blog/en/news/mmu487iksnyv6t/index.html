"9 Required Documents for Support Projects? Now Cut in Half, What Questions Remain?"

March 17, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Government Support Document Requirements Reduced to Half for SMEs, Startups, and Small Businesses Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun The application documents for government support programs for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), startups, and small businesses will be reduced by about half, making it easier for them to utilize these services. An integrated platform will also be piloted in May to provide support policy services with just one phone call or one login. The Ministry of SMEs and Startups announced this at a Cabinet meeting presided over by the President in Sejong City on the 17th, outlining the establishment of a ‘one-stop support system for SMEs, startups, and small businesses’... Reasons and Significance of Reducing Support Program Document Requirements by Half

The application process for government support programs for SMEs and small businesses is set to be significantly simplified. Starting in May, the Ministry of SMEs and Startups plans to reduce the average number of documents required for support programs from 9 to 4.4 and provide one-stop services through an integrated platform. This change will alleviate the administrative burden on businesses and make it easier for them to access support programs. It is expected that these changes will contribute to revitalizing the SME and startup ecosystem.

This announcement is part of efforts to enhance the efficiency of SME support policies, including reducing application documents and providing AI-assisted drafts of business plans. Now, businesses will have a pathway to receive the necessary support without going through complicated procedures.

Key Summary

  • The average number of application documents for support programs will be reduced from 9 to 4.4.
  • Businesses can receive drafts of business plans using AI.
  • An integrated support platform will be piloted starting in May.
  • 17 government ministries will collaborate to improve the system.
  • The consultation phone number will be unified to ‘1357’ for better accessibility.

Key Highlights

  • Document Simplification: Automatic collection of administrative documents and elimination of unnecessary paperwork.
  • AI Utilization: Saving time by providing drafts of business plans.
  • Integrated Platform: Access to support program information and applications with a single login.
  • Ministry Collaboration: 17 ministries working together for policy improvement.
  • Unified Consultation: Providing consultation services through a single phone number.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The government has made various efforts to reduce the administrative burdens faced by SMEs and small businesses. Especially after COVID-19, the need for economic recovery and revitalization of the startup ecosystem has made these policies even more critical. Previously, many businesses were unable to participate in support programs due to complicated document submissions, which ultimately had a negative impact on the overall economy.

Why It Matters

This policy change is expected to provide tangible benefits to SMEs and small businesses. With lower administrative barriers, more companies will be able to participate in support programs, which is likely to lead to job creation and economic growth. In particular, support utilizing AI technology will offer businesses the opportunity to enhance their competitiveness in line with the latest trends.

Noteworthy Points

In my view, the provision of AI-assisted drafts of business plans is the most intriguing aspect. Many businesses struggle with writing business plans due to a lack of expertise, and assistance from AI could significantly alleviate this burden. This could be a major advantage, especially for startups or small enterprises.

Hidden Variables to Consider

However, this policy does not solve all problems. Simplifying documents does not automatically improve the quality of support programs. The actual effectiveness of the support programs in providing substantial help to businesses is more important. Additionally, the accuracy and reliability of AI should not be overlooked.

Points to Check Now

  • AI-Based Business Plan Writing: It’s good to familiarize yourself with how to utilize the drafts provided by AI.
  • Integrated Platform Usage: Check in advance how the new platform will operate and what features it will have.
  • Consultation Phone Number: Since inquiries about support programs can be made through the unified phone number, make sure to note it down.

FAQ

Q1: What benefits come from reducing the application documents for support programs?
A1: The reduction in administrative burden allows businesses to participate more easily in support programs, which can lead to various benefits such as financial support or technical assistance.

Q2: How reliable are the business plan drafts provided by AI?
A2: AI provides drafts based on data, so the basic information is reliable, but modifications tailored to the unique circumstances of the business are necessary.

Q3: When will the integrated platform be available?
A3: The integrated platform is scheduled to be piloted starting in May.
