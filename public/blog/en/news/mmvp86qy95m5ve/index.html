Samsung Electronics Union Votes 93% in Favor of Dispute Action... Total Strike Realized in May

The Samsung Electronics union has secured legal dispute rights with a 93.1% approval rate in a recent dispute action vote. This vote was conducted with participation from 73.5% of over 89,000 members from three unions: the Samsung Electronics branch of the inter-company labor union, the National Samsung Electronics Labor Union, and the Samsung Electronics Union Together. The union is demanding transparency in the performance bonus calculation criteria, the abolition of the performance bonus cap, and a 7% wage increase, planning a total strike from May 21 to June 7. If the strike materializes, it will be the second in Samsung Electronics' history and is expected to have a serious impact on the semiconductor sector.

This news is not just a union decision but an event that could significantly affect the future of Samsung Electronics and the South Korean economy. The union's demands are not merely about wage increases but call for overall transparency related to the company's performance bonus system. Such demands could have implications that extend beyond Samsung Electronics, affecting large corporations and the labor market in South Korea as a whole.

The Samsung Electronics union's dispute action vote follows the breakdown of wage negotiations that began in December last year. In this context, the conflict between the union and Samsung Electronics is intensifying.
