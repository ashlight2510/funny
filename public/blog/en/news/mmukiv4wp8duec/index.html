Coupang Raises the Standard Price for Free Shipping... A Trick to Compensate for Customer Compensation Losses?

Recently, Coupang announced that it will raise the standard price for free rocket delivery. As a result, general members will only receive free shipping benefits if the amount after applying coupons and instant discounts exceeds 19,800 won. This policy change is analyzed as a strategy to improve profitability and increase the number of paid 'Wow Membership' subscribers amid Coupang's low operating profit margin. However, consumers view this measure negatively, with some criticizing Coupang for passing losses onto customers.

This situation is not just an internal strategy of Coupang but could have a significant impact on the entire e-commerce market. Consumers can no longer easily enjoy the benefits of free shipping, which could weaken Coupang's competitiveness.

Key Summary

  • Coupang raises the standard price for free rocket delivery to 19,800 won.
  • The change is based on the amount after discounts, while paid membership members retain existing benefits.
  • Interpreted as a measure to improve profitability and expand paid membership due to low operating profit margins.
  • Consumer backlash and criticism of the company passing losses onto consumers.

Key Scenes

  • Change in Free Shipping Standards: Strengthening free shipping conditions based on the amount after discounts.
  • Retention of Paid Member Benefits: Wow Membership subscribers can continue to enjoy existing benefits.
  • Need for Profitability Improvement: Operating profit margin remains in the 1% range, interpreted as a measure to strengthen profitability.
  • Consumer Backlash: Many consumers show negative reactions, with growing dissatisfaction over price increases.
  • Concerns Over Market Competitiveness: There is a possibility of negative impacts on Coupang's competitiveness.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Coupang has achieved rapid growth in recent years but faces profitability issues with an operating profit margin in the 1% range. In this situation, Coupang has chosen to raise the standard price for free shipping, placing an additional burden on consumers as a way to compensate for losses. Alongside this, there is an intention to secure a stable revenue source by increasing the number of paid membership subscribers. However, these changes are provoking consumer backlash.

Why It Matters

This policy change is not just a simple price adjustment; it reveals the structural problems Coupang is facing. Consumers have become accustomed to Coupang's past free shipping policy, and this change has resulted in a betrayal of their expectations. Additionally, this could serve as a catalyst for other e-commerce platforms to implement similar measures. Particularly, if consumer trust is lost, it could negatively impact Coupang's long-term growth.

Points of Attention

In my view, the most important aspect of this measure is the trust relationship with consumers. The fact that Coupang is damaging the image of free shipping it built in the past could be fatal to future customer loyalty. Moreover, if consumers begin to consider alternatives outside of Coupang, it could significantly impact Coupang's market share.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While the article emphasizes that Coupang's policy change negatively affects consumers, it could also impact Coupang's selling partners. Claims that sellers have been inflating prices to provide free shipping to consumers could create tension in relationships with sellers.

Points to Check Now

It is crucial to closely observe how the change in Coupang's free shipping standards actually affects consumer purchasing patterns. This will help predict Coupang's future strategies and market responses.

FAQ

Q: What changes occur due to Coupang's free shipping standard change?
A: The conditions for free shipping are strengthened based on the amount after discounts, meaning consumers will need to order a higher amount to receive free shipping benefits.

Q: What benefits can Wow Membership members continue to enjoy?
A: Wow Membership members can continue to receive free shipping on products without a minimum order amount limit.

Q: What impact does this policy change have on consumers?
A: Consumers will need to purchase more products to receive free shipping benefits, which could lead to additional financial burdens.

Q: What other measures could Coupang take to improve profitability?
A: Coupang may need to expand paid memberships across various product categories or strengthen marketing strategies to enhance price competitiveness.

This policy change by Coupang could serve as an opportunity to rethink trust with consumers. It is essential to closely monitor the current situation and check future consumer reactions.
