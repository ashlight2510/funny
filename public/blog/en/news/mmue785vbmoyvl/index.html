Final Draft of the Public Prosecution and Serious Crimes Agency Law: Weakening of Prosecutors' Authority and Its Implications

Recently, the final draft of the Public Prosecution and Serious Crimes Investigation Agency law announced by the Democratic Party has been modified to weaken the authority of prosecutors. This bill abolishes the supervisory authority of public prosecutors over special judicial police officers (special agents) and limits the scope of prosecutors' duties by law, blocking the possibility of expanding their authority through enforcement decrees. As a result, approximately 20,000 special agents will be exempt from judicial control by prosecutors. This change is interpreted as clarifying the direction of prosecutorial reform and making it difficult to restore prosecutorial authority in the event of a change in government.

The main changes in this bill include not only the reduction of prosecutors' authority but also the expansion of the investigation scope of the Serious Crimes Agency. The crime of legal distortion has been added to the list of crimes that the Serious Crimes Agency can investigate, and now includes not only public officials from the existing Public Prosecution and police but also court-affiliated officials. This is expected to strengthen legal accountability and contribute to the readjustment of cooperative relationships among investigative agencies.

Key Highlights

  • Abolition of public prosecutors' supervisory authority and limitation of duties by law.
  • Approximately 20,000 special agents will be exempt from prosecutors' control.
  • Addition of legal distortion crime to the investigation scope of the Serious Crimes Agency, expanding the targets of investigation.
  • Reduction of prosecutors' authority in warrant requests and execution.
  • Maintenance of priority investigation rights for the Serious Crimes Agency.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This bill reflects the expansion of prosecutorial investigative powers under the Yoon Suk-yeol administration and is promoted as part of the prosecutorial reform led by the Democratic Party. Amid ongoing criticism that the powers of prosecutors have been excessively strengthened in the past, there has been a growing call for legal mechanisms to distribute power and enhance accountability. In this process, while the investigative powers and scope of the Serious Crimes Agency have expanded, the authority of the Public Prosecution has been weakened.

Why Is It Important?

This bill could have a significant impact on the future authority and role of prosecutors. While the reduction of prosecutors' authority may enhance independence and fairness in the investigative process, there are concerns about inefficiencies due to a lack of cooperation among investigative agencies. In particular, the introduction of the crime of legal distortion has a positive aspect of strengthening legal accountability, but there is also a risk of inconsistencies in the application of the law.

Important Points in My Opinion

In my view, the most significant meaning of this bill is that it clarifies the direction of prosecutorial reform. In particular, there is an effort to maintain political neutrality by limiting the authority of prosecutors. However, it remains to be seen how these changes will actually operate in the field. If the cooperative relationships among investigative agencies weaken, investigations into serious crimes may become opaque.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While the content of the bill focuses on the reduction of prosecutors' authority, the expansion of the Serious Crimes Agency's investigative scope and the introduction of the crime of legal distortion should not be overlooked. This will be a factor that prompts a reevaluation of the roles and responsibilities of investigative agencies in the future. Additionally, the maintenance of priority investigation rights for the Serious Crimes Agency should be noted for its impact on relationships with other investigative agencies.

Points to Check Now

  • The actual impact of the expansion of the Serious Crimes Agency's investigative scope on investigations.
  • Case studies of the application of the crime of legal distortion and the resulting changes in legal accountability.
  • The impact of the reduction of prosecutors' authority on cooperation among investigative agencies.
