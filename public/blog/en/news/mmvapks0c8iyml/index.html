Kim Geon-hee, Request to Amend Indictment to Add Aiding Charges in Deutsche Motors Stock Manipulation Case

Recently, the Special Prosecutor's Office has filed a request to amend the indictment to include aiding charges against Kim Geon-hee in the appeal trial concerning the Deutsche Motors stock manipulation case. This raises new legal issues regarding Kim, who was acquitted in the first trial, and is likely to have a significant impact on the outcome of the upcoming trial. This case is not merely a stock manipulation issue but is drawing considerable attention due to its political context.

The Special Prosecutor's Office has charged Kim with being a co-perpetrator of a violation of the Capital Markets Act, but by adding aiding charges, they aim to lead the court's judgment in a new direction. This amendment to the indictment carries the potential to work in Kim's favor when compared to similar past cases.
