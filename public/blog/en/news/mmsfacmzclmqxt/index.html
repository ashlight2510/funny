Managing Cholesterol with Supplements: Effective Tips

These days, many people are worried about their cholesterol levels, right? I used to be concerned about my high cholesterol too, but after trying various methods, I've seen some improvements. So, I’ve compiled the supplements I’ve tried and their effects. If you have similar concerns, this article will be helpful!

1. Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Why Omega-3?

Omega-3 is known to be really good for cardiovascular health. In particular, the components EPA and DHA are effective in reducing triglycerides in the blood and increasing HDL (good cholesterol). I’ve been consistently taking omega-3 supplements, and I’ve noticed my blood pressure stabilizing and my mood improving.

How to Take It?

Omega-3 is often available in fish oil form, and it’s recommended to take about 1,000mg a day. However, if you don’t eat fish often, there are many capsule forms available, so keep that in mind. Initially, I was hesitant because I disliked the fishy smell, but these days I think it’s better than taking tasteless supplements!

2. Lecithin

Effects of Lecithin

Lecithin is a component that helps regulate cholesterol. It plays an important role in breaking down cholesterol in the liver. Since I started taking lecithin, I’ve felt that my digestion has improved significantly.

How to Take It?

Lecithin is mainly sold in a form extracted from plants, and about 1,200mg a day is appropriate. Personally, I recommend taking it in powder form. Mixing it into smoothies or yogurt makes it tasty and nutritious.

3. Dietary Fiber

Why is it Important?

Dietary fiber is an important component that affects not only gut health but also cholesterol levels. In particular, soluble dietary fiber is said to absorb cholesterol and help eliminate it. Therefore, it’s good to eat foods like oatmeal or oats frequently.

How to Utilize It?

Making a bowl of oatmeal in the morning and adding fruits or nuts is really delicious. This is one of my daily breakfasts, and it keeps me full while also being effective for cholesterol management.

Conclusion

Managing cholesterol isn’t something that can be solved solely with supplements; consistent management is necessary. I think it would be good to refer to what I’ve tried. Don’t forget to maintain healthy eating habits along with supplements! If you have any questions, please leave a comment. I think starting with this would be great!
