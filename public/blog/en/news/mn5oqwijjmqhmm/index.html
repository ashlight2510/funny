After the arrest of the group leader, 'insults' have also quieted down... Will the statue of the girl finally escape the barricade bars?

Recently, it has been reported that the police barricades installed around the statue of the girl of peace are undergoing the process of being dismantled. This statue, which symbolizes the victims of the Japanese military comfort women, has required protective measures since 2020 due to insulting protests from far-right groups. However, with the recent arrest of the leader of the far-right group, the protests have quieted down, leading to increased calls for the removal of the barricades. Jeonguiyeon has officially requested the removal of the barricades and conducted an inspection of the statue's condition.

This change could strengthen safety around the statue while also providing an opportunity to reestablish historical awareness regarding the comfort women victims. However, it remains uncertain whether the removal of the barricades will be a true solution.

Key Summary

  • Police barricades around the statue of the girl of peace are in the process of being dismantled.
  • With the arrest of the far-right group leader, the insulting protests have decreased, and Jeonguiyeon is demanding the removal of the barricades.
  • Kim Seok-kyung, the creator of the statue, inspected its condition and emphasized the need to reestablish historical awareness.

Key Scenes

  • Jeonguiyeon's request for the removal of the barricades is a response to the decrease in protests from the far-right group.
  • The police are reviewing whether to maintain the barricades in consideration of the enforcement of the comfort women victims law.
  • The creator of the statue mentioned the need for legal punishment for the actions of the far-right group.
  • The importance of safety around the statue and historical awareness is highlighted simultaneously.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The statue has been a symbol of the issue of Japanese military comfort women, and since its installation, there have been ongoing protests and insults from far-right groups. These protests have been considered acts that tarnish the dignity of the comfort women victims, prompting the police to install barricades in response. However, with the recent legal punishment of the far-right group's leader, the protests have decreased, leading Jeonguiyeon to request the removal of the barricades.

Why It Matters

This news is more than just about the removal of barricades. The issue of comfort women victims is directly linked to the historical wounds of Korean society, and the conflicts surrounding it are still ongoing. If safety around the statue can be secured, it could lead to greater respect for the victims and an improvement in historical awareness.

Points to Watch

In my view, the removal of the barricades around the statue is not just a change of space. It will serve as an important test of how society accepts historical truths and respects the voices of the victims. With the decrease in protests from the far-right group, it remains to be seen whether this change can be sustained and what kind of social consensus can be achieved through it.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Even if the barricades around the statue are removed, the far-right group's opposition has not completely disappeared. They still exist, and the possibility of protests resuming cannot be ruled out. Therefore, it will be necessary to continuously monitor the situation after the removal and implement additional protective measures if needed.

Points to Check Now

  • Progress on the removal of barricades around the statue
  • Reactions and future plans of the far-right group
  • Social reactions following the enforcement of the comfort women victims law
