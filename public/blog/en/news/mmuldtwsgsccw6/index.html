This situation is likely to have significant implications for the diplomatic relations between the two countries.

This article contains various interpretations of how Trump's request to postpone his visit to China impacts the complex dynamics of U.S.-China relations. As tensions with Iran escalate, the U.S. is applying military pressure on China, and China is contemplating how to leverage this situation.
