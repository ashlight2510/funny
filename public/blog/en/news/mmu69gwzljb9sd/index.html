2nd Special Investigation ‘Yangpyeong Expressway Suspicion’ Former Minister of Land, Infrastructure and Transport Won Hee-ryong Banned from Leaving the Country

Recently, former Minister of Land, Infrastructure and Transport Won Hee-ryong has been banned from leaving the country in relation to the suspicion of preferential treatment regarding the Yangpyeong Expressway. This action was taken at the request of the Kwon Chang-young special investigation team, and the key issue in this case is the fact that the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport changed the endpoint of the Yangpyeong Expressway in May 2023. The changed endpoint is adjacent to land owned by Kim Geon-hee, raising suspicions of preferential treatment. In response to the growing controversy, former Minister Won canceled the construction plan for the road in July 2023.

This case reflects a complex situation that goes beyond the departure issue of a single politician, intertwining the government’s policy decision-making process and political interests. The special investigation team plans to investigate whether former Minister Won was involved based on facts revealed in previous investigations.

  • Former Minister Won Hee-ryong is banned from leaving the country due to the Yangpyeong Expressway preferential treatment suspicion.
  • The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport changed the endpoint of the road, raising concerns about its connection to land owned by Kim Geon-hee.
  • The special investigation team is currently investigating former Minister Won's involvement.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident began with the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport changing the endpoint of the Yangpyeong Expressway after the Yoon Suk-yeol administration took office. This endpoint change raised suspicions of preferential treatment due to its proximity to land owned by Kim Geon-hee. The special investigation team has banned former Minister Won from leaving the country to address these suspicions. Although a previous special investigation team led by Min Jung-ki already looked into this case, former Minister Won's involvement was not clarified. Therefore, the results of this special investigation are expected to have a significant impact on the future political landscape.
