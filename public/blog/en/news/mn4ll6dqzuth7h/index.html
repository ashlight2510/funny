“How long will we only sell the traces of colonialism... We must awaken the ‘history of resistance’ for Mokpo to thrive”

In a recent article reported by Kyunghyang Shinmun, labor activist Son Young-deuk argued that the history and spirit of resistance in Mokpo, Jeollanam-do, must be revived. He emphasized that Mokpo should move beyond simply using the traces of colonialism as tourist resources and properly inform people about its history of resistance, thereby opening a new future based on this history. He aimed to convey the message that through the history of resistance, local residents can feel pride and that the community can be revitalized.

Son's remarks are not limited to Mokpo but point to broader issues in our society. He criticizes the political monopoly of the Democratic Party and the indifference towards local issues, representing the voices of marginalized people. His arguments could serve as an important starting point for discussions that can lead to change in the local community.
