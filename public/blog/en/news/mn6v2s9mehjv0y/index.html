Gidong Car New Subscriber Recharge Amount 10% Payback···Seoul City’s Efforts to Revitalize Public Transportation

The Seoul city government has announced various measures to revitalize public transportation in the era of high oil prices. In particular, it is introducing a climate companion card system that offers a 10% payback on the recharge amount for new subscribers, encouraging the use of public transportation. Along with this, the city plans to extend the concentrated dispatch of subways and buses during rush hours and provide incentives to autonomous districts that have worked to revitalize public transportation. These measures aim to reduce the burden of living expenses due to rising oil prices and respond to the increasing demand for public transportation.

This plan goes beyond simple fare discounts. The Seoul city government is supporting citizens to use public transportation more conveniently, reflecting the trend of increasing public transportation users. This is expected to encourage public transportation use while alleviating traffic congestion in the city.

Key Summary

  • The Seoul city government announced a public transportation revitalization plan in response to high oil prices.

Why Background Matters

This plan reflects the increasing demand for public transportation due to the rising burden of living expenses from recent oil price hikes. The Seoul city government analyzed changes in the number of public transportation users before and after the price fluctuations and prepared support measures accordingly. The core of this policy is to ensure that citizens can use transportation more conveniently as public transportation usage increases.

Why It Matters

This policy is not just about reducing public transportation fares; it can also enhance citizens' mobility convenience and alleviate traffic congestion. Revitalizing public transportation positively impacts environmental issues and will play an important role in enhancing the sustainability of the city in the long term.

FAQ
