Bithumb's Customer Verification Negligence Leads to 36.8 Billion Won Fine and 6-Month Business Suspension

Recently, the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) imposed a fine of 36.8 billion won on the virtual asset exchange Bithumb for violating the Specific Financial Transaction Information Act, along with a decision for a 6-month partial business suspension. This was the result of Bithumb violating several legal obligations, including customer verification duties, with approximately 6.65 million violations detected. In particular, Bithumb inadequately verified customer identities and neglected trading restriction obligations, leading to serious issues.

This incident will serve as a reminder of the importance of regulatory compliance and customer protection in virtual asset exchanges. The imposition of the fine is expected to enhance the credibility of the virtual asset market and contribute to preventing similar incidents in the future.

Key Summary

  • Bithumb fined 36.8 billion won and subjected to a 6-month business suspension for customer verification negligence.
  • FIU detected approximately 6.65 million legal violations.
  • Violations of customer verification duties and trading restriction obligations were the main causes.
  • The fine is the largest ever imposed on a virtual asset exchange.
  • Bithumb has expressed its intention to improve and resolve the issues.

Key Highlights

  • Legal Violations: Violations of customer verification duties, trading restriction obligations, and data retention obligations.
  • Fine Amount: A record-high fine of 36.8 billion won imposed.
  • Business Suspension: Restrictions on external virtual asset deposits and withdrawals for new customers.
  • Market Credibility: Emphasis on the need for legal compliance by virtual asset exchanges.
  • Stakeholder Impact: Strengthening investor protection and market stability.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident began with an on-site inspection conducted by the FIU in March-April of last year. At that time, Bithumb was found to have violated several legal obligations, such as failing to verify customer identities or using unreliable methods for verification. This incident occurred in the context of the rapid growth of the virtual asset market, highlighting the need for regulation and demonstrating the government's intent to strengthen regulatory measures.

Why It Matters

Virtual asset exchanges have a legal obligation to safely protect customers' assets and prevent money laundering. Bithumb's incident illustrates the significant consequences of neglecting these responsibilities. This will serve as a wake-up call for other exchanges, reiterating the importance of regulatory compliance.
