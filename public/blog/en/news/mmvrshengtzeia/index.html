‘Collusion between religion and politics’ investigation team summons spouse of Rep. Jeon Jae-soo over ‘Unification Church bribery allegations’

Recently, the joint investigation team has summoned the spouse of Rep. Jeon Jae-soo of the Democratic Party of Korea to investigate allegations of bribery involving the Unification Church. Rep. Jeon is accused of receiving a watch worth 10 million won and 20 million won in cash from the Unification Church in 2018, and the investigation team views this case as a violation of the Political Fund Act. There are also suggestions that the Unification Church attempted to lobby for long-desired projects like the Korea-Japan undersea tunnel. This situation is a significant issue that could greatly impact political credibility.
