Easy Home Tips to Eliminate Washing Machine Odor

March 17, 2026

Easy Tips to Remove Washing Machine Odor at Home

Odors coming from the washing machine can negatively affect laundry and reduce the machine's efficiency. These smells can arise from various causes, and if left unattended, they can lead to health and hygiene issues. Fortunately, there are simple methods to effectively eliminate washing machine odors. In this article, we will introduce various tips for removing washing machine smells.

Cleaning the washing machine means more than just eliminating odors. Regular maintenance and cleaning can extend the life of the washing machine and improve the quality of laundry. Now, let’s take a closer look at the causes of washing machine odors and how to remove them.

What Are the Main Causes of Washing Machine Odors?

The main causes of odors from washing machines are as follows:

  • Laundry Residue: Detergent or fabric softener left behind after washing can decompose inside the machine, causing unpleasant smells.

    • Checkpoint: Make sure there are no leftover items in the washing machine after washing.

  • Stagnant Water: Water that remains inside the washing machine can lead to mold growth, which is a source of odor.

    • Tip: Check the drainage to ensure water does not remain after washing.

  • Filters and Drainage: Dirty filters and drainage can be the main culprits of odors.

    • Example: If foreign substances accumulate in the filter, water may not drain properly, leading to odors.

Understanding these causes is crucial for effective solutions, so it is important to accurately comprehend why washing machine odors occur.

What Are the Basic Cleaning Methods to Remove Washing Machine Odors?

To eliminate washing machine odors, regular cleaning is essential. Here are the basic cleaning methods:

  • Regular Washing Machine Cleaning: Clean at least once a month.

    • Tip: Mark your cleaning schedule on the calendar.

  • Reduce Detergent and Fabric Softener Usage: Use the appropriate amount to minimize residue.

    • Checkpoint: Follow the recommended amounts listed in the user manual.

  • Physical Cleaning: Wipe the inside and outside of the washing machine with a damp cloth.

    • Example: Pay special attention to cleaning the washing machine door and rubber sealing areas.

Always turn off the washing machine before cleaning to ensure safety.

What Are the Tips for Odor Removal Using Natural Ingredients?

Using natural ingredients can safely eliminate washing machine odors.

Effective Combination of Vinegar and Baking Soda

The combination of vinegar and baking soda is effective for odor removal.

  • Why It’s Important: These two ingredients are natural and safely eliminate odors.
  • How to Apply: Add 1 cup of vinegar and 1/2 cup of baking soda to the washing machine drum and run a hot water cycle.
  • Caution: Excessive use can damage the inside of the washing machine, so maintain the appropriate amounts.

Powerful Combination of Lemon and Salt

Lemon and salt form an effective combination for odor removal.

  • Effective Combination: The acidity of lemon and the cleaning power of salt provide strong odor removal effects.
  • Application Method: Cut a lemon in half, sprinkle salt on it, and place it inside the washing machine before washing.
  • Caution: Excessive use of acidic substances can damage metal parts, so be careful.

How to Clean Washing Machine Filters and Drainage?

The filters and drainage of the washing machine can be major sources of odors.

  • Filter Cleaning Frequency: Clean the filter at least every 3 months.

    • Checkpoint: Ensure the filter is properly reassembled after cleaning.

  • Drainage Inspection: Regularly check to ensure no foreign substances accumulate.

    • Example: If foreign substances accumulate in the drainage, it can cause operational issues with the washing machine.

  • Cleaning Method: Remove the filter and rinse it under running water, and clear any debris from the drainage.

Be cautious when reinstalling the filter after cleaning, as improper installation can lead to leaks.

What Are the Usage Tips to Prevent Washing Machine Odors?

To prevent washing machine odors, it is advisable to follow these usage tips:

  • Open the Door After Washing: Leave the door open after washing to allow the interior to dry.
  • Regular Use: Prolonged periods of non-use can lead to odors.
  • Washing Machine Placement: Install in a well-ventilated area to prevent moisture buildup.

Checklist

  • Set washing machine cleaning schedule
  • Prepare natural ingredients
  • Inspect filters and drainage

Using this checklist for regular washing machine maintenance can help prevent odors.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How often should I clean my washing machine to remove odors?

    • It is recommended to clean at least once a month.

  2. What is the ratio for using vinegar and baking soda?

    • Generally, 1 cup of vinegar and 1/2 cup of baking soda is appropriate.

  3. What should I do if the washing machine odor is severe?

    • In severe cases, it is advisable to consider professional washing machine cleaning services.

Removing washing machine odors can be easily resolved through regular maintenance and cleaning. Start cleaning your washing machine today and try using natural ingredients. As a first step, it is recommended to check the filters and drainage.
