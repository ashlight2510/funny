People Power Party's Gwanak-gu Chairperson Accused of 'Nomination Bribery'

Recently, the chairperson of the People Power Party's Gwanak-gu branch has come under police investigation for allegedly providing money ahead of the local elections. Chairperson Lee Seong-sim ran as a preliminary candidate for Gwanak District Mayor during the 2022 local elections but is accused of providing money to former chairperson Kang Min-gu after losing in the party's primary. The police have secured recordings and documents as evidence related to the case, while Lee denies the allegations. The investigation is ongoing under the Political Fund Act and the Anti-Corruption Act.

This incident reveals not just an individual corruption issue but also structural problems related to the nomination process in the political arena. Allegations of monetary transactions surrounding nominations can undermine political trust and diminish voters' motivation to participate in politics.
