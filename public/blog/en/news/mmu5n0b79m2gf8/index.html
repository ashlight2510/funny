Hyundai Motor Group participates in the International Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Expo... "Accelerating the Hydrogen Society"

Hyundai Motor Group participated in the 'International Hydrogen & Fuel Cell Expo 2026' held in Tokyo, Japan, showcasing hydrogen technology and sharing its hydrogen mobility vision. This expo serves as a platform to introduce the latest technologies and trends in the hydrogen and fuel cell-related industries, with Hyundai demonstrating various hydrogen technologies centered around the hydrogen electric vehicle 'The All-New Nexo'. In particular, the company is exploring the potential for the development of the hydrogen ecosystem through an automatic charging robot for hydrogen electric vehicles and a packaged hydrogen charging station.

At this expo, Hyundai Motor Group is showcasing various use cases for hydrogen-based mobility and expressing its commitment to strengthening global cooperation in the hydrogen ecosystem. This movement is expected to be a significant turning point for the activation of the hydrogen economy.

Key Summary

  • Hyundai Motor Group participates in the 'International Hydrogen & Fuel Cell Expo 2026' in Tokyo.
  • Demonstration of the hydrogen electric vehicle 'The All-New Nexo' and the automatic charging robot for hydrogen electric vehicles.
  • Introduction of hydrogen value chain technology and various hydrogen application cases.
  • Discussion of global cooperation as a co-chair of the Hydrogen Council.
  • Plans to launch hydrogen vehicles in the Japanese market.

Key Scenes

  • Hydrogen Electric Vehicle Technology: Hyundai emphasizes the market competitiveness of hydrogen vehicles by demonstrating the performance and charging technology of The All-New Nexo.
  • Automatic Charging Robot: Demonstration of an AI-based automatic charging robot for hydrogen electric vehicles showcases technological advancements.
  • Packaged Hydrogen Charging Station: A modular charging station proposal for expanding hydrogen charging infrastructure.
  • Global Cooperation: Aiming to build an international hydrogen ecosystem through collaboration with the Hydrogen Council.
  • Sustainable Vision: Plans to introduce eco-friendly facilities like hydrogen burners to promote sustainable energy transition.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The hydrogen economy has garnered significant attention in recent years, and the international community recognizes the importance of hydrogen technology in achieving carbon neutrality goals. Hyundai Motor Group is continuously investing to build a hydrogen mobility ecosystem and secure technological advantages in line with this trend. This expo serves as an important opportunity for Hyundai to solidify its position in the global hydrogen market.

Hyundai Motor Group's participation in this expo and its technology demonstrations are expected to contribute to accelerating the development of the hydrogen economy. If Hyundai's technologies are successfully commercialized, it will significantly impact not only the growth of the company but also the resolution of environmental issues.

Q: What is the purpose of Hyundai's hydrogen burner?
