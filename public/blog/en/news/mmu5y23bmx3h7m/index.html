The boomerang of 'Yoon Seok-yeol marketing'... Are the basic local government heads of the National Power Party trapped in the 'impeachment shadow'?

Recently, it has been reported that the basic local government heads in Gyeonggi Province are thoroughly erasing President Yoon Seok-yeol's name and focusing on administration centered around people's livelihoods and achievements as they prepare for the elections. Analysis suggests that the achievements of the National Power Party in the 2022 local elections are now having a negative impact due to their association with the Yoon Seok-yeol government. On the other hand, candidates from the Democratic Party are strengthening their election strategies through 'Lee Jae-myung marketing' based on the support for the Lee Jae-myung government. As the political environment rapidly changes, attention is focused on the future actions of the basic local government heads.

This issue goes beyond mere changes in election strategies; it illustrates how political trust and voters' expectations for regional development are formed. Basic local government heads are contemplating what message to convey to voters regarding their past political associations, which is likely to have a significant impact on the future political landscape.

Why We Need to Look at the Background

This issue illustrates how President Yoon Seok-yeol's political influence has affected basic local government heads since the National Power Party secured 22 out of 31 cities and counties in Gyeonggi Province during the 2022 local elections. However, following the unprecedented events of martial law declaration and impeachment, the political landscape has changed dramatically, prompting basic local government heads to thoroughly erase their association with President Yoon. They aim to gain voters' trust through administration focused on people's livelihoods and achievements, contrasting with the Democratic Party's Lee Jae-myung marketing.

Why It Matters

This matter is not just about changes in election strategies; it shows how political trust and voters' expectations for regional development are formed. Basic local government heads are contemplating what message to convey to voters regarding their past political associations, which is likely to have a significant impact on the future political landscape. Voters are demanding specific alternatives for candidates' achievements and regional development, which can influence the fundamental direction of politics.
