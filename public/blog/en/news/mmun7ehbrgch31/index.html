What Was the Problem with the Exchange's Lifting and Re-designation of Management Stocks?

Recently, there was a commotion when SCM Life Science, a KOSDAQ-listed company, was mistakenly lifted from management stock designation by the Korea Exchange, only to be re-designated the next day. This incident resulted from a misjudgment of the criteria for lifting the management stock designation, causing stock prices to fluctuate sharply and creating confusion among investors. Such a situation is not merely a simple mistake but a significant event that could impact market credibility.

This incident does not end as a mere error. It can act as a factor undermining the stability and credibility of the stock market, necessitating follow-up measures. The Korea Exchange has stated it will establish preventive measures through internal audits, but whether these measures will be effective remains questionable.

Key Summary

  • The Korea Exchange mistakenly decided to lift the management stock designation for SCM Life Science and re-designated it the next day.
  • The reason for the misjudgment of the lifting criteria was a misunderstanding regarding the decrease in continuing business losses before corporate tax deductions.
  • The stock price surged by 28.05% after the lifting but turned to a downward trend after re-designation.

Key Scenes

  • Error in the Lifting Process: A mistake caused by the exchange's misjudgment of the lifting criteria.
  • Sharp Fluctuations in Stock Prices: The rise in stock prices following the lifting news and the decline after re-designation.
  • Market Credibility Issues: The impact of the exchange's error on the market.
  • Need for Follow-up Measures: Mention of internal audits and the use of AI technology.
  • Investor Confusion: Potential harm to general investors due to sharp fluctuations.

Why Background Matters

This incident stemmed from a judgment based on the audit report submitted by SCM Life Science. The exchange decided to lift the management stock designation considering the decrease in continuing business losses before corporate tax deductions, but made a mistake in the process. This reveals issues with the exchange's management system or internal procedures, and if similar situations recur, it could further undermine market trust.

Why Is This Important?

This incident is not just a simple mistake but a matter directly related to the credibility of the Korea Exchange. Investors rely on the exchange's judgments to make investment decisions; thus, such errors can negatively impact the overall market. Furthermore, the designation of management stocks significantly affects a company's ability to raise funds, leading to serious consequences for both companies and investors.
