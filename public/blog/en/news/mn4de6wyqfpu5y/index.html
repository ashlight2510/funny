How to Get Maximum Discounts on Coupang: Tips and Tricks

March 24, 2026

How to Get the Maximum Discounts on Coupang and Tips

Coupang is an online shopping platform loved by many consumers for its wide range of products and fast delivery service. However, to enjoy the maximum discount benefits, a few strategies are necessary. In this article, we will introduce ways to get discounts on Coupang and useful tips.

Understanding and Utilizing Coupang's Discount Policies

Understanding Coupang's discount policies can help you shop more effectively. Coupang offers various discount benefits such as coupons, reward points, and promotions. It is important to utilize these appropriately.

Check Discount Conditions and Validity Period

Discount coupons may only apply to specific categories of products and have limited usage periods. For example, certain coupons can only be used for purchases over 50,000 won. Therefore, it is crucial to carefully review the conditions of the coupon before making a purchase.

Execution Tip: Note the coupon validity period and conditions in advance, and refer to them when selecting products to purchase.

Utilizing Coupang Coupons and Reward Points

How to Receive Coupons

Coupons can be found in emails, app notifications, and on the Coupang homepage. Many coupons require a minimum purchase amount to be used.

Checkpoint: Always check the minimum purchase amount when using a coupon.

Reward Points Utilization Strategy

Reward points are accumulated based on a certain percentage of the purchase amount and can be used for the next purchase. It is advisable to keep track of how much reward points accumulate for frequently purchased items.

Example: Organize products you purchase regularly each month and prioritize buying items that accumulate reward points.

Comparing Discount Coupons and Reward Points

Discount Method Advantages Disadvantages
Coupons Instant discount Limited validity period
Reward Points Accumulable Complex usage conditions

If you want an instant discount, coupons are advantageous, while utilizing reward points is better for long-term shopping plans.

Utilizing Time Sales and Events

Coupang regularly holds time sales, and taking advantage of these can allow you to purchase desired products at lower prices. Set up notifications to ensure you don't miss out on time sale news.

The Importance of Quick Purchases

Popular products can sell out quickly, so prompt purchasing is necessary. For example, a time sale item with a 30% discount may be sold out within minutes. It is advisable to bookmark desired products in advance.

Execution Tip: Organize your wishlist before the time sale starts.

Saving on Shipping Costs

Conditions for Free Shipping

To receive free shipping on Coupang, the general condition is to purchase over 30,000 won. You can also receive free shipping benefits when using the regular delivery service.

Checkpoint: It is economical to purchase necessary items in one go to benefit from free shipping.

Comparing Shipping Costs and Discounts

Condition Free Shipping Paid Shipping
Minimum Purchase Amount Over 30,000 won 2,500 won
Regular Delivery Available Not available

If shipping costs are incurred, additional expenses will arise, so it is advisable to meet the free shipping conditions.

Checklist: Getting Maximum Discounts on Coupang

Here is a checklist for getting maximum discounts on Coupang:

  1. Check coupons and reward points.
  2. Check the schedule for time sales and events.
  3. Confirm free shipping conditions.
  4. Review product ratings and compare prices.
  5. Verify discount application before payment.

Use this checklist to ensure you don't miss anything while shopping.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How can I receive Coupang coupons?
    Coupang coupons can be found in emails, app notifications, and on the Coupang homepage. Check regularly to avoid missing out.

  2. How long can I use reward points?
    Reward points generally need to be used within one year, and the expiration date may vary according to Coupang's policies.

  3. How do I set up notifications for time sales?
    In the Coupang app, activate time sale notifications in the 'Notification Settings' menu to receive news about time sales for desired products.

To enjoy maximum discount benefits on Coupang, try utilizing the methods introduced above. We recommend starting your shopping today using the checklist!
