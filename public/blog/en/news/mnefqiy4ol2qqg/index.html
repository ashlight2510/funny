In an era where even truth is recommended, journalists tracking the truth behind algorithms… the play

The play explores the complex relationship between data and algorithms that we face today. This work unravels the processes of sophisticated opinion manipulation and truth tracking in a thriller format, questioning the impact of algorithms on our lives. In particular, it sharply reveals how truth is distorted in a society accustomed to convenience.

This play is performed at the Sejong Center and visually conveys to the audience how algorithms shape our choices and beliefs. Under the direction of Artistic Director Lee Jun-woo, the process of journalists tracking the truth contains an important message that reflects the complexities of modern society rather than just a simple story.

Key Summary

  • The play deals with the story of journalists tracking the truth in an algorithm-dominated society.
  • The work unfolds the issues of opinion manipulation and data surveillance in a thriller format, delivering realistic anxieties to the audience.
  • Director Lee Jun-woo aims to make the audience feel the experience of being exposed to media.

Key Scenes

  • Opinion Manipulation: Explores how information manipulated by algorithms is delivered to us.
  • Distortion of Truth: Reveals the difficulties and realities faced by journalists in the process of tracking the truth.
  • Visual Expression: The stage design using transparent screens and glass materials emphasizes the structure of control.
  • Social Anxiety: Warns how easily the information we trust and choose can be manipulated.
  • Quick Scene Transitions: The rapid transitions composed of over 60 scenes reflect the era of information overload.

Reasons to Consider the Background

In recent years, the advancement of digital media and algorithms has deeply penetrated our daily lives. In particular, social media and portal sites provide information through recommendation systems based on individual choices. In this environment, we find ourselves in a situation where it is difficult to judge the authenticity of information. The play explores how algorithms shape our thoughts and actions based on this historical context.

Why It Matters

We currently live in a flood of information. While algorithms make our choices convenient, they also carry the risk of distorting the truths we believe in. This play addresses these complex issues and provides the audience with an opportunity to reflect on their own beliefs and choices. Raising awareness of the impact of algorithms on our lives is very important.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the reason this play is particularly noteworthy is that it does not merely address the issue of algorithms but vividly shows how they operate in our daily lives. The audience will reconsider the authenticity of the information they consume through the events on stage. Since algorithms can provide convenience while simultaneously limiting our choices, conveys a profound message that goes beyond mere entertainment.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The themes addressed in the play are very profound, but one point that the audience may overlook is the convenience of algorithms and the control structures behind them. Many people take algorithmic recommendations for granted and may overlook the social anxieties that arise from them. This play serves to awaken such awareness.
