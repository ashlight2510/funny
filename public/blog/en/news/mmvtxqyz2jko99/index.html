Notification for March 19: AI Talent Development and Civil Society Call for Proposals

On March 19, Hankuk University of Foreign Studies and LG CNS signed an agreement to foster talent in the field of artificial intelligence (AI) and strengthen industry-academia cooperation. This agreement plans to promote various educational programs, including human exchanges related to the AI-centered university project. Additionally, the Korea Federation of Small and Medium Enterprises is supporting the overseas expansion of small and medium enterprises by organizing a group pavilion at a consumer goods exhibition held in Las Vegas, USA. The Ministry of Unification has started a call for proposals for civil society organizations for peace and unification education, while the Ministry of Personnel Management distributed a casebook on public service culture innovation, highlighting a day of significant collaboration between the government and the private sector.

This news is not just a simple announcement but an important indicator of the future of AI, international trade, and social education. In particular, as the link between education and industry strengthens, attention should be paid to how talent development and corporate competitiveness will change in the future.
