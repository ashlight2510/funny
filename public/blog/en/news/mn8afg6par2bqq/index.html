Ulsan Voucher Taxi Sees Surge in Users After Lowering Age Limit

As a result of lowering the age limit for the 'Senior Voucher Taxi' introduced by Ulsan City from 85 to 80 years old, the number of new registrants has surged by 380% within a month, leading to an 81% increase in users. This change is part of a policy aimed at improving mobility convenience for the elderly, with new registrations reaching 2,702 compared to January, indicating a significant rise in overall users. Ulsan City plans to continue efforts to maintain service quality by increasing the number of vehicles.

This news reflects more than just a change in numbers; it serves as an important case study on how mobility convenience can be improved in an aging society. It particularly highlights the urgent need for support in elderly mobility and suggests the potential for similar policies to spread to other regions in the future.

Key Summary

  • Ulsan City lowered the age limit for the senior voucher taxi to 80 years and above, resulting in a surge of 2,702 new registrants and 1,684 users.
  • Total new registrations increased by 380% compared to the previous month, and usage frequency rose by 81%.
  • Plans to expand support to include disabled individuals, pregnant women, and infants by 2025.
  • Users can utilize the service up to four times a month at a low base fare of 1,000 won.

Key Highlights

  • Increase in Users: The number of new registrants surged, significantly enhancing mobility convenience for the elderly.
  • Policy Effectiveness: The relaxation of age criteria is contributing to the practical alleviation of mobility inconveniences.
  • Maintaining Service Quality: Ulsan City plans to focus on improving service quality by increasing the number of vehicles.
  • Future Expansion: There are plans to expand support by 2025, raising expectations for the sustainability of the policy.

Why Background Matters

This change arises from Ulsan City's recognition of the need for mobility support for the elderly as it enters an aging society. Since 2022, Ulsan City has gradually expanded support starting with the disabled voucher taxi. Lowering the age limit for the senior voucher taxi is a policy decision aimed at easing the mobility challenges faced by the elderly.

Why It Matters

Support for elderly mobility goes beyond mere convenience; it directly impacts their social participation and quality of life. In a society with an increasing elderly population, such policies are essential and open the door for similar initiatives in other regions. This can have a positive impact not only on the elderly but also on their families and society as a whole.

FAQ

Q: How can I use the voucher taxi?
A: You can register by submitting relevant documents such as your resident registration certificate and ID at the Ulsan Transportation Vulnerable Mobility Support Center.

Q: What are the usage fees?
A: The base fare is 1,000 won, which is about 22% of the regular taxi fare, with a maximum fare of 4,500 won.

Q: What direction will future policy changes take?
A: Ulsan City plans to expand support to include pregnant women, infants, and elderly individuals over 85 by 2025.
