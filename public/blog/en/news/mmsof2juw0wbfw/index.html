Samcheok Chogok Yonggul Candle Rock Trail Extension Project Transforms into a Safe and Attractive Tourist Destination

Samcheok City in Gangwon Province has announced a new project to extend the passageway of the Chogok Yonggul Candle Rock Trail. The core of this project is the installation of pedestrian bridges in areas restricted due to rockfall risks, with a total investment of 8.5 billion won to create a 80m suspension bridge and a 48m deck bridge. This will provide a safe environment for tourists to enjoy the coastal scenery, with a goal of completion by the end of this year.

Article Summary

Samcheok City is promoting a project to create new pedestrian and deck bridges to extend the passageway of the Chogok Yonggul Candle Rock Trail.

Key Points

  • Enhanced Safety: The installation of pedestrian bridges to bypass rockfall risk areas significantly improves passage safety.
  • Expansion of Tourism Infrastructure: The creation of an 80m suspension bridge and a 48m deck bridge allows for closer appreciation of the coastal scenery.
  • Economic Impact: The establishment of a safe tourism environment is expected to attract more tourists and revitalize the local economy.
  • Completion Schedule: Construction is underway with a target completion by the end of this year, and prompt completion is important.
  • Stakeholder Commitment: Samcheok City aims to enhance the utilization of marine scenic resources to develop into a national tourist destination.

Why This Issue is Important

This project is expected to have a positive impact on the local economy beyond simply enhancing the safety of the tourist site. A safe tourism environment will lead to an increase in visitors, which can translate into local business growth and job creation. Additionally, Samcheok shows a commitment to establishing itself as a tourist destination by leveraging the natural scenery of the East Coast, indicating significant potential for future development.
