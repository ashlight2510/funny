US Counterterrorism Director Resigns... "Iran Was Not an Imminent Threat" Public Backlash

Recently, Joseph Kent, the director of the US National Counterterrorism Center (NCTC), resigned, causing a significant stir with his letter. He claimed that Iran was not an imminent threat to the US and withdrew his support for the ongoing war against Iran. His statements, which directly deny the core justification of the war, "imminent threat," are inflicting serious damage on the administration's rationale for war. Kent emphasized that the war against Iran was initiated under pressure from Israeli and US lobbying, clearly stating that he could not support a war that provides no benefit to the American people.

This issue goes beyond a simple personal resignation and could have a substantial impact on the credibility of the US government's foreign and military policies. In particular, Kent's denial of the war's legitimacy as a senior counterterrorism advisor in the Trump administration raises questions about how public opinion on future US military interventions may change.
