2026 KBO League Average Salary Hits All-Time High... The Top Spot Goes to Doosan's Yang Eui-ji with 4.2 Billion Won

The average salary in the 2026 KBO League has reached an all-time high, once again proving the economic growth of Korean professional baseball. According to the KBO, the average salary of 529 players in the league is 175.36 million won, which is a 9.1% increase from last year. Notably, Doosan Bears catcher Yang Eui-ji has recorded a personal best salary of 4.2 billion won, making him the highest-paid player in the league. This result stems from the FA contract Yang signed with Doosan in 2022, with the salary structure adjusted considering the team's salary cap.

These changes are in line with the overall growth trend of the KBO League. The KBO League, which opened the era of 12 million spectators last year, shows through this increase in average salary that the value of players is rising.

Key Summary

  • KBO League 2026 season average salary is 175.36 million won, an all-time high.
  • Yang Eui-ji of Doosan sets a personal best salary record at 4.2 billion won.
  • Average salary increased by 9.1%, reflecting the rising value of players.
  • SSG ranks first in both total salary and average salary.
  • Other teams like LG and Doosan also see an increase in average salary.

The KBO League has seen a surge in attendance over the past few years, achieving significant success. In Yang Eui-ji's case, the salary structure was adjusted through his FA contract, creating an environment where players can demand higher amounts in contracts with their teams.
