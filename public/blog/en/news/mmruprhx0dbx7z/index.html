Japan Hotel Review: A Perfect Accommodation Selection Guide in Tokyo

When planning a trip to Tokyo, one of the biggest concerns is choosing the right accommodation. It can be overwhelming to figure out what to look for and where to stay. So, I've organized some tips for selecting accommodation in Tokyo! Based on my multiple visits, I’ll share useful information with you.

1. Location Matters

Tokyo's Transportation System

Tokyo has an excellent transportation system, making it easy to get almost anywhere using the subway or trains. However, the location of your accommodation is really important. When I first chose my accommodation, I thought it would be best to stay somewhere convenient for transportation, and that turned out to be a huge help. For example, staying in central areas like Shinjuku or Shibuya makes shopping and sightseeing much easier.

Recommended Areas

Personally, I recommend Shinjuku and Asakusa. Shinjuku is lively even at night, with many restaurants and bars, which I enjoyed. Asakusa offers a traditional Japanese atmosphere, adding a unique charm to the trip. Choosing these areas will allow you to experience the allure of Tokyo even more.

2. Price Range and Accommodation Type

Choosing According to Your Budget

Accommodation prices vary widely. You can choose from hostels to luxury hotels. It’s good to select based on your budget; I often used mid-range business hotels. The rooms are small but clean, and they have all the necessary facilities, making it convenient.

Personal Experience

Once, I stayed at a luxury hotel, and the service was excellent. The breakfast was great, and room service was satisfying. However, I don’t think it’s necessary to stay at luxury hotels every time. It’s best to choose appropriately based on the purpose of your trip.

3. Check for Amenities

Important Facilities to Look For

When choosing accommodation, I recommend checking for the amenities you need. Free Wi-Fi, laundry services, and whether breakfast is included are important factors. Especially for those like me who run a blog while traveling, Wi-Fi is essential!

My Accommodation Experience

One hotel I stayed at had delicious breakfast, which made my mornings enjoyable. Also, they offered laundry services, allowing me to wash my clothes during the trip, which was convenient. These little things enhance the quality of your travel.

4. Refer to Reviews

Utilizing Review Sites

Before booking accommodation, it’s important to refer to reviews from other travelers. The sites I frequently use are TripAdvisor and Booking.com. Reading actual user reviews helps me learn about aspects I might not have considered.

Importance of Reviews

Once, I booked a place after reading reviews, but there were many comments about poor soundproofing, so I switched to another accommodation. Thanks to that, I was able to enjoy a comfortable stay. By referring to reviews, you can make choices without regrets.

Conclusion

Choosing accommodation in Tokyo is a significant part of your trip. By carefully considering location, price, amenities, and reviews, you can find the perfect place to stay. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I hope this information helps. Good luck with your next travel preparations!
