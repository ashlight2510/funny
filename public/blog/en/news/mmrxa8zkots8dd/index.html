Essential Maintenance Tips to Solve Washing Machine Odor Problems

The smell coming from the washing machine is really unpleasant, right? Especially when that smell lingers on freshly washed clothes, it’s truly unfortunate. I once struggled with this issue too, and I felt lost on how to resolve it. So, I’ve compiled some tips to help solve the washing machine odor problem. After reading this article, you’ll be able to tackle the odor issue easily!

Cleaning the Inside of the Washing Machine

Keep the Drum Clean

One of the main culprits of washing machine odor is the residue that builds up inside the machine. One of the most effective methods I’ve tried is cleaning the drum. You just need to run a cycle using a washing machine cleaner, and it’s simpler than you might think.

After cleaning, I noticed a significant reduction in the smell coming from the drum. Even if you don’t use a specialized washing machine cleaner, adding vinegar or baking soda and running a cycle will completely eliminate the odor. This part is really important! It would be good to develop a habit of cleaning regularly.

Checking the Drying Condition After Washing

Things You Must Check

It’s also important to check the drying condition after washing, as neglecting this can lead to odors returning. If laundry is left damp or if items are left in the machine, bacteria can easily breed. Therefore, it’s best to immediately remove the laundry and let it dry after washing.

I once missed this step and ended up with mold on my washed clothes. I really regretted it at that time. So nowadays, I make sure to take the laundry out right after it’s done and dry it properly. This small habit greatly helps in preventing washing machine odor issues!

Checking the Filter and Drain

A Commonly Overlooked Area

Another area that is often overlooked is the washing machine filter and drain. If dust or residue builds up in the filter, it can cause odors, so it’s good to check it regularly. Cleaning this is not difficult; you just need to remove the filter and rinse it with water.

At first, I didn’t pay attention to the filter, and I struggled with odors coming from the washing machine. So now, I make sure not to miss checking the filter. Regularly doing this will help keep the washing machine cleaner.

Washing Machine Usage Tips

Wash in Small Loads

Finally, when using the washing machine, washing in small loads is also a good tip. If you wash too much at once, water can accumulate, preventing the washing machine from functioning properly. So, I recommend washing in appropriate amounts.

Doing this, I felt that my laundry was getting as clean as I wanted. Plus, the occurrence of odors from the washing machine has decreased.

Conclusion

The washing machine odor problem can be sufficiently resolved with regular maintenance and small habits. I hope you can manage your washing machine by referring to the tips introduced today! If you have any questions, please leave a comment. Starting with these tips will make it much easier to solve washing machine issues!
