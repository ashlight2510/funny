Essential Checklist for Traveling to Japan: Must-See Attractions and Tips

Have you ever felt overwhelmed about what to pack when preparing for a trip? Especially when going to a captivating country like Japan, you want to make sure you don't miss anything. Based on my experiences from traveling to Japan, I've organized a checklist of things you must consider and some tips. I'll also introduce some attractions that are easy to overlook!

Must-See Attractions

1. Tempozan Ferris Wheel in Osaka

When thinking of traveling to Japan, Tokyo or Kyoto often comes to mind first, but Osaka is also incredibly charming. You absolutely have to visit the Tempozan Ferris Wheel! When I went, I was truly impressed by the view of the sea and the city all at once. If you go up during sunset, you can experience a unique atmosphere. I think it's good to know this in advance.

2. LEGOLAND in Nagoya

In Nagoya, there's LEGOLAND, which is fun for both kids and adults. To be honest, I didn't have high expectations when I first went. However, there were so many exhibitions and attractions that I lost track of time having fun. If you have a friend who loves LEGO, it will be even more enjoyable to go together!

Tips to Check Beyond Attractions

3. Utilize Transportation Passes

Japan has an excellent public transportation system, so if you make good use of transportation passes, your trip will be much more convenient. For example, the JR Pass can be used nationwide, making it essential for those who travel a lot by train. When I bought a pass for my journey, I ended up saving more money than I expected. It's a good idea to check this out in advance!

4. Make Restaurant Reservations

Japan has an abundance of delicious food, right? Especially popular sushi and ramen places often require reservations. The sushi restaurant I visited wouldn't let anyone in without a reservation. So, if you book online in advance, you'll have a much smoother experience. Don't forget to check this!

Conclusion

Traveling to Japan is truly an enchanting experience. Just by keeping track of the attractions that are easy to miss and some useful tips, your trip can be much more enjoyable. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! Starting with this will make your trip to Japan even more delightful.
