Kim Jong-in “Oh Se-hoon Likely to Challenge for Party Leadership Instead of Running for Mayor”

Recently, Kim Jong-in, former chair of the emergency committee of the People Power Party, mentioned that Oh Se-hoon, the Mayor of Seoul, is likely to shift his focus from running in the local elections to challenging for the party leadership. Kim assessed that it would be meaningless for Oh to run for mayor given the current situation of the party, and he revealed in discussions with Oh that the mayor is considering a challenge for the party leadership. This statement has garnered more attention as it coincides with the third round of candidate nominations for the mayor of Seoul scheduled for the 17th.

This news can be interpreted as an important signal that goes beyond Oh Se-hoon's candidacy, providing insights into the internal power structure of the People Power Party and its future political direction. Kim Jong-in's comments offer significant clues regarding internal conflicts within the party and the changes in the political landscape following the presidential election.
