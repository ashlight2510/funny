Spring Island Outing… The Home of Seaweed ‘Jeopdo’

Recently, EBS 1TV's program highlighted Jeopdo, the center of seaweed production, along with the beautiful nature of the Jodo Islands. The Jodo archipelago consists of 154 islands, among which Jindo is the third largest island in Korea. Particularly, Jeopdo is actively engaged in seaweed production due to its rich marine resources, and here, Mr. and Mrs. Kim Ho-rim have been cultivating seaweed for over 20 years. They sell fresh seaweed through auctions every morning, and as Korean seaweed gains global popularity, they are leading increasingly busy lives.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The Jodo archipelago is located in the southwestern part of the Korean Peninsula, where the marine ecosystem is well-preserved. In particular, Jeopdo has an optimal environment for seaweed production due to its abundance of marine plants. Thanks to this natural environment, seaweed farming has developed in Jeopdo, directly affecting the livelihoods of local residents. Recently, as Korean seaweed gains popularity overseas, the production of seaweed in Jeopdo is receiving more attention.
