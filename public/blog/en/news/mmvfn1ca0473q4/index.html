‘World Sparrow Day’ Do You Know··· Ulsan City Operates Birdwatching Bus

Ulsan City announced that it will operate a ‘Sparrow Birdwatching Bus’ from the 18th to the 25th to commemorate ‘World Sparrow Day’ on the 20th. This event is designed to raise awareness about the declining sparrow population globally and to convey the importance of sparrow ecology and protection to Ulsan citizens. The birdwatching tour, which is free of charge, offers an opportunity to observe sparrow ecology under the guidance of a natural environment interpreter and to experience feeding the birds directly.

This news goes beyond mere bird observation; it serves as a reminder of the importance of environmental protection and ecosystems. Understanding the ecology of sparrows, which are rarely seen in urban areas, will provide an opportunity to feel the necessity of nature conservation.

Key Summary

  • Ulsan City operates a birdwatching bus for ‘World Sparrow Day’.
  • Raises awareness about sparrow ecology and the need for protection through free birdwatching tours.
  • Provides ecological observation and feeding experience with a natural environment interpreter.
  • Departs from Taehwa River National Garden, operating twice a day.
  • Offers an opportunity for citizens and students to develop interest in nature.

Key Scenes

  • Sparrow Population Decline Issue: An event to raise awareness about the global decline of sparrows.
  • Free Birdwatching Opportunity: Provides an educational chance for citizens to experience and learn about nature.
  • Accompanied by Ecological Interpreters: Enhances understanding of ecosystems through expert explanations.
  • Participant Limit: Limited to 12 people per session for more focused observation.
  • Safety Considerations: Requires personal injury insurance to create a safe participation environment.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This event is significant as it highlights Ulsan City's efforts to address the serious issues of sparrow habitat loss and ecosystem destruction. World Sparrow Day focuses on raising awareness of these problems and encouraging citizens to take an interest in nature. The disappearance of resident birds like sparrows due to urbanization and environmental degradation is recognized as a serious environmental issue.

Why It Matters

This event provides an important opportunity to raise awareness about environmental protection beyond simple birdwatching activities. It is particularly effective in educating children and teenagers about the importance of natural ecosystems. The community's active participation in nature conservation can serve as a cornerstone for sustainable development.

FAQ

Q: Is the birdwatching tour free?
A: Yes, the birdwatching tour is free of charge.

Q: How many participants are allowed?
A: Participation is limited to 12 people per session.

Q: What is required to participate?
A: Personal injury insurance is required.

Q: When will the birdwatching tours take place?
A: Tours will be conducted from the 18th to the 25th, twice a day (at 9:30 AM and 1:30 PM).
