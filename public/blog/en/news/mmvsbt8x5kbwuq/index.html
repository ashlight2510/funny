Venezuela has achieved a historic first victory in the 2026 World Baseball Classic (WBC). This tournament garnered additional attention due to Venezuela's political situation, as it occurred shortly after the Venezuelan president and his wife were arrested during a U.S. military operation. This backdrop heightened the tension of the match.
