A New Turning Point for K-Pop: ‘KDEHUN’ Wins Oscars

Recently, the Korean animated film (hereafter ‘KDEHUN’) received significant attention by winning two awards at the 98th Academy Awards: Best Animated Feature and Best Original Song. This film, which combines Korean shamanism and K-pop idols, has surpassed 500 million global views, enhancing the status of K-pop. Director Maggie Kang has become the first Korean-Canadian to win in this category, marking an event that reaffirms the global influence of Korean culture.

This award goes beyond the mere success of a film; it symbolizes the globalization of Korean culture and the influence of K-pop. Composer Lee Jae expressed his emotions, stating, “K-pop, which was once mocked in my childhood, is now loved by everyone.” This change opens up possibilities for Korean popular culture to be recognized globally and create more opportunities.

Key Highlights

  • Globalization of K-Pop and Korean Culture: The success of ‘KDEHUN’ shows that K-pop has established itself as a cultural icon beyond just a music genre.
  • Historic Achievement by a Korean Director: Director Maggie Kang has elevated the pride of Koreans by becoming the first to win the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature.
  • Changing Status of K-Pop: It has been confirmed that K-pop is recognized as a music genre worldwide, transcending past boundaries.
  • Importance of Cultural Solidarity: Filmmakers emphasized the power of music and storytelling to connect people across cultures and borders.
  • Message to Next-Generation Creators: Director Kang conveyed an encouraging message to young artists to share their own stories.

Why Background Matters

K-pop has rapidly grown globally over the past few years. With the emergence of world-renowned groups like BTS and BLACKPINK, K-pop has transcended being just a music genre to become a global cultural phenomenon. In this context, has gained attention as a new attempt to combine K-pop with traditional Korean culture. Notably, the animated genre can target a diverse audience, including children and teenagers, paving the way for new markets for K-pop.

Why It Matters

This award is a case where K-pop has been recognized not just as a music genre but as a cultural asset. This signifies that Korean cultural content can compete globally, providing a foundation for more creators to produce works utilizing Korean cultural elements. Furthermore, it can serve as an opportunity for various cultures worldwide to converge.

My Perspective

In my view, this award is not just a reason to celebrate. It can be interpreted as an opportunity to break down various cultural barriers we face in the process of Korean culture being recognized globally. Particularly, the potential for the younger generation to find their identity through K-pop and share it with the world adds deeper significance.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While this article addresses the success of K-pop from a positive perspective, there are indeed issues stemming from the excessive commercialization of the K-pop industry. For instance, the overwhelming schedules and mental pressures faced by idols remain unresolved challenges. Additionally, we must consider the risk that the globalization of K-pop could overshadow the diversity of Korean music.

Current Points to Check

  • Global Expansion of K-Pop: It is essential to pay attention to how K-pop artists can create more opportunities overseas in the future.
  • Fusion of Diverse Cultures: Discussions are needed on how other Korean cultural content, beyond K-pop, can establish itself in the global market.
