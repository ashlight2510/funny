Daejeon to Introduce '3-Module Articulated Bus'... Construction of Infrastructure Begins

The city of Daejeon plans to introduce a new public transportation option, the 3-module articulated bus, starting in the second half of this year. This vehicle, which consists of three connected modules, will be the first of its kind to operate in South Korea. To facilitate this, Daejeon is investing 1.16 billion won in infrastructure construction by September, which includes the establishment of new bus stops and dedicated lanes. This project is expected to bring significant changes to Daejeon's public transportation system.

The introduction of the 3-module articulated bus became possible after Daejeon passed the special exemption review through the mobility regulatory sandbox. The city aims to enhance punctuality and increase the capacity of public transportation with this new mode of transport.

Key Points

  • Daejeon plans to operate the 3-module articulated bus starting in the second half of this year.
  • Introduction of the country's first trolley-free 3-module vehicle.
  • Investment of 1.16 billion won in infrastructure construction, including new bus stops and dedicated lanes.
  • The pilot operation route will be 6.5 km long, with buses running at intervals of 15 to 25 minutes.
  • Improvement of public transportation infrastructure to provide a convenient environment for vulnerable road users.

Background and Context

Daejeon has been exploring various transportation options to enhance the efficiency of public transport and alleviate traffic congestion. In particular, the city has faced challenges due to an inadequate transfer system between urban rail and buses, causing inconvenience for public transport users. In this context, the 3-module articulated bus is gaining attention as a public transport solution with large-scale transport capacity. The government's mobility regulatory sandbox system has also played a crucial role in enabling the introduction of innovative transportation modes.

Why This Issue Matters

The introduction of the 3-module articulated bus is expected to have a positive impact on Daejeon's overall public transportation system, going beyond merely adding a new mode of transport. It can help alleviate the inconveniences faced by public transport users and contribute to solving traffic congestion issues. Additionally, this project could serve as a model for similar transportation solutions in other regions.
