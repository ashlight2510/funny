Truck and Tank Truck Accident on Suncheon-Wanju Expressway: Key Points to Note

On the morning of March 18, 2026, a 2.5-ton cargo truck crashed into a tank truck in the Cheonma Tunnel on the downhill side of the Suncheon-Wanju Expressway in Gurye, Jeollanam-do. As a result of the accident, a 31-year-old man who was in the passenger seat of the truck died, and the 54-year-old driver was injured and transported to the hospital. Following the accident, the area experienced temporary congestion, but traffic has since resumed. The police are currently investigating the exact circumstances of the accident.

This incident carries more significance than just a simple traffic accident. It brings various road safety issues back into focus. In particular, accidents on major roads like highways can affect many people, highlighting the need for a reassessment of traffic safety policies.
