‘Foreign Investment’ Inducing Return Exchange Rate Stabilization Law Passes Finance Committee Meeting… Likely to be Processed in the General Assembly on the 19th

The recently passed ‘Exchange Rate Stabilization 3 Laws’ in the National Assembly are measures aimed at inducing the return of overseas stock investors, known as ‘foreign investors,’ to the domestic market. This bill includes provisions that allow for up to 100% exemption on capital gains tax if individuals sell their overseas stocks and manage the funds in the domestic market for a certain period. This is intended to alleviate the rise in exchange rates caused by imbalances in the foreign exchange market and is expected to be finalized in the general assembly on the 19th.

The key point of this bill is that individual investors who sell overseas stocks held before December 23 of last year can receive up to 100% exemption on capital gains tax if they deposit those funds into a domestic market return account (RIA) and manage them for one year. Additionally, tax benefits for individual investors who purchase foreign exchange hedge products are also included, which is expected to have a positive impact on the foreign exchange market.

Key Summary

  • The ‘Exchange Rate Stabilization 3 Laws’ passed the Finance Committee meeting and are scheduled for processing in the general assembly.
  • Up to 100% exemption on capital gains tax when returning to the domestic market after selling overseas stocks.
  • New tax benefits for purchasing foreign exchange hedge products, with expectations for foreign exchange market stabilization.
  • Increased non-deductible income ratio to reduce corporate tax burden.
  • Inducing the return of foreign investors with the goal of stabilizing exchange rates.

Why Is It Important?

This bill goes beyond simple tax benefits and is expected to have a significant impact on the domestic economy and foreign exchange market. In particular, promoting the inflow of overseas assets is a crucial task in the current situation where exchange rates are continuously rising. The return of foreign investors could invigorate the domestic capital market and contribute to exchange rate stabilization, making this bill likely to serve as a positive signal for the overall economy.

Reasons to Consider the Background

In recent years, foreign investors have sparked a frenzy of investment in overseas stocks, leading to capital outflows from the domestic capital market. As the imbalance in the foreign exchange market has worsened and exchange rates have risen, the government has prepared legislation to induce the return of foreign investors. After previous tax benefits ended, many investors turned their attention to overseas stocks, necessitating efforts to reverse this trend.

Personal Comment

In my view, this bill will serve as an important opportunity to rekindle the interest of foreign investors in the domestic market, going beyond merely providing tax benefits. In particular, the goal of stabilizing exchange rates can have a positive impact on the national economy, beyond the individual benefits for investors. However, it remains to be seen how effective this bill will be in practice. Even if the bill passes, whether foreign investors will actually return depends on various factors.

Hidden Variables to Consider

If there is a lack of understanding of the details of the bill, investors may not know what benefits they can actually receive. Additionally, analysis is needed on how many investors will actually be able to purchase foreign exchange hedge products and how much this will increase the supply of dollars in the foreign exchange market. If the implementation of the bill proceeds without follow-up measures, the expected effects may be minimal.

Points to Check Now

  • The likelihood of this bill being finalized in the general assembly on the 19th.
  • The schedule for the government to announce detailed requirements for tax support after the bill passes.
  • The reactions of foreign investors and the actual influx of investments.

FAQ

Q: When can I start receiving benefits if this bill passes?
A: The bill is expected to be implemented immediately upon passage, with specific implementation dates to be announced later.

Q: How much capital gains tax exemption can I receive after selling overseas stocks?
A: You can receive a 100% exemption on capital gains tax for amounts up to 50 million won based on the sale amount.

Q: How will tax benefits for foreign exchange hedge products be applied?
A: You can deduct 5% of the purchase amount of foreign exchange hedge products from your capital gains tax amount, which will apply for one year only.

This bill could be a crucial turning point in inducing the return of foreign investors. In particular, it has a high potential to positively influence exchange rate stabilization, so it is important to closely monitor related follow-up measures and market reactions.
