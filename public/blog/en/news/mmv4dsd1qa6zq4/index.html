Is the Comprehensive Real Estate Tax for One Bailey 19 Million Won This Year?…It Decreases by ‘This Much’ When Deductions Are Applied

Recently, due to the sharp rise in apartment prices in Seoul, the burden of the comprehensive real estate tax (commonly known as the property tax) has significantly increased. However, it has been revealed that utilizing various deductions can reduce the actual tax burden by nearly half. For example, in the case of an 84㎡ apartment in One Bailey, Seocho-gu, Seoul, the expected comprehensive real estate tax is 19.08 million won, but if owned jointly by a couple, it can be reduced to 10.26 million won. Applying deductions for seniors and long-term ownership can further decrease the tax amount, making it essential for homeowners to be aware of this information.

This news goes beyond simple tax calculations and highlights an important issue that can have a substantial financial impact on homeowners. Especially for seniors and long-term owners, understanding related policies is crucial as they receive more benefits.
