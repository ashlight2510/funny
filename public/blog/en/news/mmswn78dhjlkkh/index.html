Tensions between the United States and China are escalating once again. As U.S. President Donald Trump pressures China to participate in military operations in the Strait of Hormuz, the Chinese Foreign Ministry has responded that all countries must cease military actions. In this context, there are predictions that the US-China summit will conclude without significant achievements. This issue reveals complex interests in international politics beyond mere military tensions.

This incident illustrates the complicated situation intertwined with U.S. military pressure and China's backlash. President Trump has requested multinational participation from various countries and even mentioned the possibility of postponing the summit to elicit cooperation from China. On the other hand, China has avoided an immediate response to the U.S. request while demanding a cessation of military actions. This situation is likely to further deteriorate trust between the two nations and act as a destabilizing factor in the international community.
