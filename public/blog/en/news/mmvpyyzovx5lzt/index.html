Violence in Parks: The Seriousness of Conflicts Among Residents

A recent incident in Gwangju highlights the conflict issues in our society once again. A man in his 50s shot steel marbles at a resident playing noisily in a park, injuring him. This incident cannot simply be dismissed as a case of violence; it carries various social contexts that warrant our attention.

The protagonist of this incident, A, felt anger towards B, who was making a commotion in the park, and shot steel marbles with a slingshot. As a result, B suffered a wound on his forehead, and A was urgently arrested by the police. The police plan to apply for an arrest warrant, determining that A's crime is serious and poses a risk of reoffending. This incident illustrates how conflicts in public spaces can escalate beyond mere emotional outbursts.
