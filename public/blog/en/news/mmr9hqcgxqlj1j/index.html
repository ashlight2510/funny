5 Essential Tips for Saving on Air Conditioner Electricity Bills

When summer comes, it's hard to live without an air conditioner, right? But looking at the electricity bill every month can be painful. I think many people struggle with whether to turn on or off the air conditioner. Today, I've compiled some tips for saving on air conditioner electricity bills that I've personally tried. I hope this article can help reduce your electricity bill burden a little!

1. Set to an Appropriate Temperature

The Importance of Setting the Right Temperature

If you set the air conditioner to a temperature that's too low, your electricity bill will just keep piling up. Personally, I found that a temperature between 24 to 26 degrees Celsius is the best. When I set the air conditioner to this temperature, I feel cool, and the electricity bill is lower. I initially started at 22 degrees, but it was too cold, so I ended up raising the temperature.

The Importance of Adjusting the Set Temperature

When you first turn on the air conditioner, setting it to a low temperature too quickly seems to put a strain on the machine. So, I started at 26 degrees and gradually lowered it. Doing this makes me feel like the lifespan of the air conditioner is increasing.

2. Use with a Fan

Utilizing the Air Conditioner Fan

Using a fan along with the air conditioner is much more effective than just turning on the air conditioner alone. When I turn on the fan, it feels like the cool air from the air conditioner spreads throughout the room. So, even if I raise the air conditioner temperature a bit, it still feels cool enough.

Personal Experience

After using the fan together, I definitely felt a reduction in my electricity bill. In the middle of summer, I worry about the electricity bill when I only use the air conditioner, but using a fan allowed me to feel cool while saving on costs.

3. Regular Filter Cleaning

The Importance of Cleaning the Filter

You need to clean the air conditioner filter regularly. If dust accumulates, air circulation doesn't work well, and the air conditioner consumes more power. So, I try to clean it at least once a month.

After Cleaning

Since I started cleaning the filter, the coolness of the air conditioner has definitely changed. In the past, I felt the heat continuously, but now I can feel the coolness coming in after cleaning the filter. It saves on electricity bills and solves the heat issue, so it's a win-win.

4. Turn Off Air Conditioners in Unused Rooms

Turn Off Air Conditioners in Unused Rooms

If you keep the air conditioners on in all rooms during summer, the electricity bill can be enormous. So, I tend to turn off the air conditioners in rooms that are not in use. This way, I can save a lot on electricity bills.

My Experience

When the whole family goes out, I only turn on the living room air conditioner and keep the others off. As a result, the electricity bill decreased, and since I spent more time alone, it felt cooler.

Conclusion

With these few tips, you can save on air conditioner electricity bills. Setting the appropriate temperature, using a fan, cleaning the filter, and managing room usage! It would be great to start with these. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I hope you have a cool summer!
