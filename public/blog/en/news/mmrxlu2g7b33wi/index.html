Top 5 Recommended Resorts in Southeast Asia

March 16, 2026

Southeast Asia's Best Resorts: 5 Recommendations from Travelers

When planning a trip, one of the most challenging aspects is choosing accommodation. Especially with resorts, which symbolize relaxation, it can be overwhelming to find the right one. So, I've compiled a list of the best resorts in Southeast Asia recommended by travelers. I hope this information helps you with your next trip!

1. Nusa Dua Resort in Bali

Why is it recommended?

Nusa Dua is like a hidden gem in Bali. This area is home to luxury resorts, making it a perfect place to relax. The sea is truly beautiful, and there are plenty of pools, making it a great spot to play all day. Personally, I recommend not to miss the sunrise on the beach. It was truly an unforgettable experience!

My thoughts

Swimming here while listening to the sound of the waves on the beach was incredibly healing. Also, the traditional Balinese food served at the resort's restaurant is delicious, so be sure to try it!

2. Phuket Paradise Resort in Thailand

Location and Facilities

Phuket Paradise Resort is conveniently located near the beach. It's especially popular among families. There are well-equipped play areas for children, and the spa services are excellent, making it perfect for relieving travel fatigue.

Personal Tip

I highly recommend enjoying a cocktail while watching the sunset here! The atmosphere is truly wonderful. A friend of mine proposed here and successfully created a romantic moment.

3. Langkawi Resort in Malaysia

Harmony with Nature

Langkawi boasts beautiful natural scenery, and the resorts are harmoniously integrated with nature. Especially the beachfront resorts have excellent sea views. I spent time here reading a book while gazing at the emerald sea, and it was truly relaxing and happy.

Experience

There are a variety of water sports available here. I tried snorkeling and jet skiing, and it was so exciting! The tropical fish I saw underwater were so beautiful that I lost track of time.

4. Da Nang Resort in Vietnam

Culture and Cuisine

Da Nang Resort beautifully combines modern facilities with traditional Vietnamese elements. Plus, there are so many delicious foods that I was really happy. I had Vietnamese pho at the resort's restaurant in the evening, and it was genuinely tasty!

Why I love it

Especially, the night view of Da Nang is truly beautiful. The lights of the bridge viewed from the resort were so pretty. It will remain a memorable scene from my trip.

Conclusion

Southeast Asian resorts each have their unique charm. Choosing a place that suits your taste will make for a truly memorable trip. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! And I hope you use this list to plan your next trip.
