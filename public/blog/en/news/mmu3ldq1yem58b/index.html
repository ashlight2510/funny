Hyundai launches Casper Electric Lounge... the top trim that realizes 'my own space'

Hyundai's recently announced 'Casper Electric Lounge' is an urban compact electric SUV, emphasizing luxurious interior and exterior designs as the top trim. This model is based on the concept of 'my own space', offering a sophisticated interior design and various conveniences, unlike the existing outdoor-themed cross model. Notably, it features natural leather seats and Kevlar cone speakers, adding to its luxury while also ensuring practicality.

The vehicle is equipped with a 49kWh battery, allowing for a driving range of 295 km on a single charge, and the charging speed has been improved, enabling a charge from 10% to 80% in about 30 minutes. Hyundai aims to meet the diverse preferences of customers with this model.

Key Highlights

  • Top Trim: The Casper Electric Lounge is launched as the top trim with luxurious interior and exterior design and various convenience features.
  • Use of Premium Materials: It utilizes premium materials such as natural leather seats and Kevlar cone speakers to create a sophisticated interior space.
  • Driving Range: With a 49kWh battery, it can drive 295 km on a single charge.
  • Fast Charging Time: It takes about 30 minutes to charge from 10% to 80%.
  • Versatile Utility: Enhanced practicality with a standard roof rack.
