Broadcast Media Communication Review Committee elects Kim Woo-seok as a permanent member amid 'gagging review controversy'

The Broadcast Media Communication Review Committee (BMCRC) recently elected Kim Woo-seok as a permanent member. Kim has been criticized for his political bias in reviews, particularly for leading the imposition of fines related to MBC's report on 'Biden-Nalimyeon', and his election has sparked strong backlash from the media and unions. They argue that Kim's appointment undermines the independence and fairness of the review body and have called for his resignation.

This issue raises serious concerns about the independence and credibility of the Korean media, going beyond the mere election of an individual. The BMCRC's decision is expected to have significant implications for future broadcasting reviews, which are directly linked to the public's right to know.
