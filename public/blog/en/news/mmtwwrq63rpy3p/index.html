Police Arrests 20-Year-Old Man Who Threatened to Blow Up U.S. Airport Ahead of BTS Concert

Recently, a 20-year-old Korean man made a bomb threat against the Detroit airport in the United States. The Cyber Investigation Division of the Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency announced that they arrested the man for making public threats and sent him to the prosecution without detention. It is reported that the man posted the threat due to dissatisfaction with the airport last March, and the police conducted the investigation through international cooperation. They have particularly warned of a stronger response to threats related to large-scale performances or events.

This incident raises awareness not only about simple threats but also about safety issues related to large-scale events. With a performance by popular artists like BTS approaching, it has caused significant concern among fans and stakeholders.
