Haman Nakwha Play, Recruiting Spectators by Reservation

Gyeongsangnam-do's Haman County has announced that it will recruit all spectators for the public event 'Haman Nakwha Play' to be held on May 24 through a reservation system. This year marks the 33rd edition of the event, which will feature various programs to experience the charm of traditional culture, with a total limit of 5,800 spectators. Reservations will be conducted in two phases, and those who reserve will be provided with temporary parking and shuttle bus services.

This event will be an important opportunity to inherit traditional culture as an intangible heritage of Gyeongsangnam-do and to promote the revitalization of the local community.

Key Summary

  • Haman Nakwha Play, held on May 24
  • All spectators operated by reservation system, recruiting a total of 5,800
  • Reservers will receive temporary parking and shuttle bus services
  • The ignition time for Nakwha is scheduled for 7 PM
  • Special recruitment linked to the hometown love donation system is also underway

Key Scenes

  • Reservation System: Limiting the number of spectators to create a safe and orderly viewing environment.
  • Temporary Parking and Shuttle Bus: Providing transportation for the convenience of spectators.
  • Inheritance of Traditional Culture: Emphasizing the importance of local culture through the Haman Nakwha Play as an intangible heritage of Gyeongsangnam-do.
  • Link with Hometown Love Donation System: Encouraging participation in the event while contributing to the local community.
  • Prevention of Scalping: Safe entry management through a QR code system.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The Haman Nakwha Play is a traditional folk event that began during the 16th century under King Seonjo of the Joseon Dynasty and is held annually on Buddha's Birthday. This event symbolizes the well-being and good harvest of local residents and plays a crucial role in preserving and disseminating traditional culture. In recent years, many cultural events have been canceled or scaled down due to COVID-19, and this event is expected to contribute to the revitalization of the local economy and culture.

Why It Matters

This event holds significance beyond just a festival. It provides an opportunity for local residents and tourists to participate in a cultural event, revitalizing the local economy and inheriting traditional culture. Notably, the reservation system allows for the regulation of spectator numbers, creating a safe viewing environment. This could serve as a good model for other cultural events in the future.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One aspect not covered in the article is the weather and safety issues on the day of the event. As it is an outdoor event, the viewing experience can vary significantly depending on the weather. If it rains or there are strong winds, the event may face disruptions, so measures for this need to be in place. Additionally, it is important to check whether alternatives are available for on-site visitors who do not have reservations.

Points to Check Now

  • Reservation schedule and method: Confirm reservation schedules for Haman residents and the general public.
  • Location of temporary parking and shuttle bus operation times: Check in advance for convenience on the day of the event.
  • QR code issuance and usage: Understand the system to prevent scalping.

FAQ

Q: How do I make a reservation?
A: Haman residents can make reservations through their local office, while the general public can reserve through YES24.

Q: Where is the temporary parking?
A: The location of the temporary parking will be provided to those who have made reservations.

Q: What happens if the weather is bad on the day of the event?
A: The status of the event will be announced separately based on the weather.

Q: How do I use the QR code?
A: On the day of the event, entry tickets will be provided after verifying identity through the QR code. QR codes sent to others will not be accepted.
