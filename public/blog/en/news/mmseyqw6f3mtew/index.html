Odor from Washing Machine: Causes and Solutions Explained

March 16, 2026

Summary of Causes and Solutions for Odors from Washing Machines

When using a washing machine, it would be nice if the laundry always ended with a pleasant feeling, but sometimes the unpleasant odors from the machine can be quite bothersome, right? You might even feel reluctant to do laundry while thinking, “What is this?” So today, I’ve summarized the causes and solutions for such odors.

Causes of Odors from Washing Machines

1. Mold Inside the Washing Machine

From my experience, moisture can accumulate inside the washing machine, leading to mold growth. This is especially true if you leave the door closed after finishing a wash, which can cause even more unpleasant smells. Therefore, it’s a good idea to leave the door open after washing. By doing this, I found that the next time I used the washing machine, it smelled much fresher.

2. Residue from Detergent and Fabric Softener

Using too much detergent or not rinsing it properly can cause residue to build up inside the washing machine, leading to odors. I once added too much detergent, and after a while, the washing machine started to emit a strange smell. This made me realize how important it is to use the right amount of detergent.

Solutions for Odors

1. Regular Cleaning of the Washing Machine

I’ve tried it, and regularly cleaning the washing machine is really effective. Using a detergent specifically for washing machines can help remove residues and mold inside. Cleaning it about once a month is advisable. After doing this, the odors significantly decreased.

2. Leave the Door Open After Washing

As mentioned earlier, it’s crucial to leave the door open after washing. This helps prevent moisture buildup and reduces the occurrence of mold. I also practiced this, and the unpleasant smells from the washing machine disappeared.

3. Use the Right Amount of Detergent and Fabric Softener

Using the right amount of detergent is also an important point. I have experienced my washing machine emitting odors because I used too much detergent. So now, I add detergent little by little and make good use of the rinse function. At first, I was worried, but the laundry came out clean.

Conclusion

Odors from washing machines can arise from surprisingly simple causes. Regular cleaning, adjusting detergent usage, and leaving the door open after washing can greatly improve the situation. I hope you try these tips the next time you do laundry! If you have any questions, please leave a comment.
