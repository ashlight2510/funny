‘When will THAAD return to Korea?’ In response to the question… US Deputy Defense Secretary “I don’t know” avoids a clear answer

Recently, the US Deputy Secretary of Defense expressed an uncertain stance regarding the redeployment of the THAAD (Terminal High Altitude Area Defense) system stationed in Korea. During a hearing of the House Foreign Affairs Committee, Deputy Secretary Duffy stated, “I have no knowledge about the timeline for the redeployment of THAAD,” failing to provide specific information regarding its return to Korea. This statement comes amid reports that the THAAD system stationed in South Korea is being moved to the Middle East, raising concerns about the future of the US-South Korea alliance.

Such remarks could significantly impact Korea's defense system and the security environment in East Asia, casting doubt on the trust in military cooperation between the US and Korea. Although President Lee Jae-myung expressed opposition to the removal of THAAD, it also revealed that Korea's position regarding the US decision remains weak.

Key Points

  • The US Deputy Secretary of Defense did not provide specific information regarding the redeployment of THAAD in Korea.
  • There are reports that THAAD is being moved to the Middle East, which could affect Korea's defense system.
  • President Lee Jae-myung expressed opposition to the removal of THAAD, but in reality, Korea's position is limited.
  • The redeployment of THAAD could send negative signals regarding the trust in the US-South Korea alliance and East Asian security.
  • Political reactions and public opinion in Korea are expected to play a significant role in discussions about the THAAD issue.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The THAAD issue has already become a symbol of the complex diplomatic conflicts between Korea and the US. Since the decision to deploy THAAD during the Trump administration, Korea has paid a heavy price, experiencing economic retaliation from China. Reports of THAAD being relocated due to military needs in the Middle East further exacerbate anxieties about Korea's security situation. In this context, the uncertain statements from the US Department of Defense amplify concerns about the future of the US-South Korea alliance and Korea's defense capabilities.

Why It Matters

This issue is not merely about the movement of military assets. It is directly related to Korea's security and could significantly impact US foreign policy and the military balance in the East Asian region. Especially in a situation where the military threat from North Korea still exists, the redeployment of THAAD plays a crucial role in Korea's defense system. Therefore, the uncertain attitude of the US Department of Defense could negatively affect Korea's diplomatic standing.

My Thoughts

What stands out in this issue is the response of the Korean government. Despite expressing opposition to the US decision, it is time to seek ways to exert real influence. If the US ignores Korea's concerns, it could lead to serious fractures in the US-South Korea alliance in the long term. Thus, the Korean government needs to make stronger diplomatic efforts.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One aspect that cannot be overlooked in this incident is the internal political reaction in Korea. The THAAD issue is not just a military matter but also a center of political controversy. It is essential to closely observe how changes in public opinion and political pressure will affect the THAAD redeployment issue.

Points to Check Now

  • It is important to pay attention to how the Korean government's subsequent responses and diplomatic efforts will unfold.
  • Observing how discussions regarding THAAD are progressing within the US, especially any changes in positions within the Democratic Party, is crucial.
  • The response from North Korea and the corresponding military countermeasures from Korea are also key elements to monitor.
