Kim Geon-hee's side says "The necklace is a gift, not a quid pro quo" ... First trial of the 'maekwanmaejik' suspicion

Recently, the first trial of the 'maekwanmaejik' case, in which Kim Geon-hee was indicted for violating the Act on the Aggravated Punishment of Specific Crimes, was held. While Kim acknowledged receiving money and valuables from businessmen and religious figures, she claimed there was no quid pro quo. The controversy centers around whether the valuables, totaling 300 million won, including jewelry and bags that Kim received, were provided as a reward for favors. In this trial, Kim specifically regarded the necklace she received as a congratulatory gift and denied any quid pro quo relationship.

This case is not just about the receipt of valuables; it encompasses various social issues such as abuse of power and the ethics of soliciting favors, drawing significant attention. The court is demanding clarity regarding the quid pro quo relationship and is contemplating how Kim's claims will be interpreted legally.
