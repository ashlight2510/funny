President Yoon Warns of Real Estate Loan Fraud: Criminal Penalties Expected

President Lee Jae-myung issued a strong warning regarding the practice of purchasing real estate using loans for individual businesses. He cited recent media reports indicating an increase in cases where individuals are buying real estate through business loans to evade government household loan regulations, stating that such actions could be subject to criminal penalties for fraud. He particularly emphasized that if individuals deceive financial institutions to obtain loans as business funds, the Financial Supervisory Service and the National Tax Service will conduct a joint comprehensive investigation to recover the loan amounts.

This statement serves to warn about the legal risks associated with real estate speculation and aims to inform the public about the potential losses arising from such speculation. Through the message “Don’t get hurt by using tricks,” President Lee expressed the government's commitment to ensuring the stability of the real estate market.

Background and Context of the Issue

This warning arises from the recent instability in the real estate market and the tightening of household loan regulations. The government has been taking various measures to curb household loans, leading some individual business owners to attempt to evade regulations by deceiving lenders to purchase real estate. This phenomenon could exacerbate the speculative issues in the real estate market.

It will likely lead to stricter loan screening criteria by financial institutions and serve as a reminder to investors about the risks of real estate speculation.
