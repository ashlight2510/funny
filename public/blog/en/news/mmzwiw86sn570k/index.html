BTS ‘Arirang’ sells 3.98 million copies on the first day… breaks its own record

BTS's fifth studio album ‘Arirang’ recorded sales of 3.98 million copies on its first day, breaking the group's own record. This marks their comeback after 3 years and 9 months and easily surpasses the first-week sales of their previous album ‘Map of the Soul: 7’. The album topped the iTunes ‘Top Albums’ chart in 88 countries worldwide, and the title track ‘Swim’ also reached number one on the ‘Top Songs’ chart in 90 countries, once again proving BTS's global popularity.

This album was produced under the overall supervision of Bang Si-hyuk and addresses the identity of BTS and universal emotions. In particular, ‘Swim’ conveys a message of perseverance in the face of life's challenges, resonating with fans. A comeback live performance is scheduled for that afternoon at Gwanghwamun in Seoul, which will be broadcast live worldwide via Netflix.
