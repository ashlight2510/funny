NVIDIA Resumes H200 Production, Benefiting from Chinese Customer Orders

Recently, NVIDIA's CEO Jensen Huang announced the resumption of H200 AI chip production, drawing attention once again to the complex semiconductor export situation between the US and China. The H200 is an essential chip for running large-scale AI models in data centers, and the resumption of supply to the Chinese market could mark a significant turning point in NVIDIA's business strategy. This announcement signifies that the export routes, which had been blocked for a while due to US-China tensions, are now reopening.

This news is more than just a production resumption; it encompasses elements that could significantly impact global semiconductor market trends and the advancement of AI technology. Notably, the fact that NVIDIA has resumed production of the H200 and received orders from Chinese customers serves as an important indicator of how economic relations between the two countries are evolving.

Key Highlights

  • NVIDIA has resumed production of the H200 AI chip and started receiving orders from Chinese customers.
  • The H200 is based on the previous generation Hopper architecture and is a high-performance chip for data centers.
  • As US-China semiconductor export regulations ease, NVIDIA's supply chain is being reactivated.
  • The scale of H200 sales in China has not yet been mentioned.
  • Changes in US government semiconductor export policies are influencing NVIDIA's strategy.

Why Background Matters

The resumption of H200 production occurred amid the complex semiconductor export regulatory environment between the US and China. The US government has been restricting exports of advanced AI semiconductors to China, but the situation changed last December when the Trump administration shifted to a policy of limited export allowances. However, the approval for H200's export to China was delayed, leading to a temporary halt in production. Against this backdrop, NVIDIA's resumption of H200 production could signal a thaw in economic tensions between the US and China.

Why It Matters

The resumption of H200 production is not merely a corporate decision; it reflects significant events in the economic relationship between the US and China and the trends in the global semiconductor market. NVIDIA is demonstrating a willingness to maintain its leadership in the AI semiconductor market by not abandoning the Chinese market. This shift could also impact other semiconductor companies and bring about substantial changes in the competitive landscape of the market.
