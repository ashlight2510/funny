Concerns Over U.S. Attack on Harg Island Oil Facilities Deepening Energy Crisis

Recently, U.S. President Donald Trump mentioned the possibility of a military attack on Iran's oil facilities on Harg Island, raising concerns about the global energy market. Harg Island is a major oil export base for Iran, and there are analyses suggesting that a U.S. attack could trigger retaliation from Iran. If Iran targets oil facilities in the Gulf region, it could have serious implications for global oil supply.

In this context, major analysts in Europe and the U.S. have warned that an attack on Harg Island could reduce oil supply by 1.5 to 2 million barrels per day. There are also analyses suggesting that the U.S. is turning a blind eye to Iran's ongoing oil exports. Countries heavily reliant on Iranian oil, such as China, are likely to be directly affected by this situation.

Key Points

  • Concerns about the impact of President Donald Trump’s remarks on Harg Island on the global energy market.
  • Increased likelihood of Iranian retaliation, potentially targeting oil facilities in the Gulf region.
  • Expected reduction of 1.5 to 2 million barrels of oil supply per day, raising concerns over rising oil prices.
  • Ongoing Iranian oil exports, with analyses suggesting U.S. tacit approval.
  • Harg Island accounts for 90% of Iran's total crude oil exports, making it a critical base.

Why Background Matters

Harg Island is located just 24 km from the Iranian mainland and serves as a key hub for Iran's oil exports. As tensions between the U.S. and Iran have escalated, President Trump has hinted at attacks targeting Iranian military facilities. Iran is likely to retaliate against other Gulf countries, particularly Saudi Arabia's oil facilities, which could further exacerbate security instability in the Middle East.

Why It Matters

This situation is not just about the potential for military conflict; it could significantly impact the global energy market. If oil supply decreases, prices will likely surge, negatively affecting the global economy. Countries dependent on Iranian oil will face even more severe challenges. If Iran's economy suffers, it could worsen political instability in the Middle East.

Points to Note

Concerns about the possibility of Iranian retaliation extend beyond military aspects. Economically, Iran continues to export crude oil despite U.S. sanctions. If Iran's economy is impacted, it will further increase instability in the global energy market.
