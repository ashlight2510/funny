How to Systematically Organize Development Ideas with Obsidian

How to Systematically Organize Development Ideas with Obsidian

When developing, there are moments when ideas suddenly come to mind, right? However, if you don't organize them well, it's easy to forget, and it can be hard to recall them later. So, this time, I organized how to systematically arrange ideas using Obsidian.

What is Obsidian?

I Found It Unfamiliar at First

Obsidian is a tool for managing knowledge by linking notes. At first, I thought it was just a simple note-taking app, but later I realized that I could connect various ideas as if creating a tree of knowledge. Personally, I chose Obsidian because of this interconnectedness. When organizing code or development ideas, I can visually show interconnected thoughts rather than just listing text.

Creating My Own Space

When you first use Obsidian, you start with a blank space, right? At this point, it's good to think in advance about "what topics I will take notes on." For example, if you organize notes on a specific project or technology stack, it will be easier to refer back later. After doing this, I found that I could easily look up what I needed whenever I wanted.

Creating Idea Notes

Record in Detail

When organizing ideas, it's important to write down details rather than just simple keywords. The method I tried was to make each idea a title and organize explanations, use cases, and required technologies related to it. Doing this really helped when I needed to elaborate on the ideas later.

Utilizing Tags and Links

One of Obsidian's powerful features is tags and links. When I write down an idea, if I connect it with related ideas or notes, I can easily find them later. For example, if I tag contents like 'React' and 'Node.js' under the topic of 'Web Development', I can easily access related information. It's really useful!

Visualizing Ideas

Using Mind Maps

Obsidian is also great for visually organizing information. When I was organizing my ideas, I tried using the mind map plugin, and it was really helpful. It allows me to neatly organize complexly intertwined ideas at a glance. This kind of visualization greatly aids in understanding the flow of ideas.

Regular Review

After organizing ideas, it's also important to periodically review the content. I revisit my notes every month, making necessary edits and adding newly emerged ideas. Doing this makes me feel that my ideas continue to evolve and do not dull over time.

Conclusion

We learned how to organize development ideas through Obsidian. Using this tool allows ideas to remain clear and organized systematically. Even if starting is difficult, I encourage you to give it a try! If you have any questions, please leave a comment.
