Prompt Engineering: How to Maximize the Potential of ChatGPT

Using AI requires not only a basic understanding of how to use it but also careful consideration of how to ask questions to achieve the desired results in conversations with AI. Especially with models like ChatGPT, the results can vary significantly based on the given prompt, making prompt engineering truly important. So, I’ve organized my thoughts on this.

What is a Prompt?

Basic Concept of a Prompt

A prompt is an input phrase that requests a specific task from the AI. For example, a simple question like "Describe a cat" can serve as a prompt. While you can start this simply, more specific and clear prompts are necessary to obtain the desired information.

My Experience

When I first interacted with AI, I only asked very basic questions. As a result, I was often confused when I didn’t get the answers I wanted. So, I tried asking, "Explain the ecology and behavior of cats. Also, tell me about the relationship between cats and humans," and I was able to get much more informative responses. This made me realize how important it is to set prompts well.

How to Optimize Prompts

Providing Specificity and Context

When writing prompts, it’s important to be specific in your requests. Instead of simply saying, "Recommend a good movie," try saying, "Recommend an action movie that has been released in the last five years." This way, the AI is more likely to understand better and provide the desired answer.

From My Experience

Once, I was trying to choose a movie with a friend, but we didn’t know what genres each other liked, which made it difficult. So, I modified my prompt to say, "I like thriller movies. Recommend a movie that offers a similar thrill," and I received really good suggestions. Adding context like this seems to be important.

Utilizing Various Types of Prompts

Questions, Commands, and Situational Settings

Prompts are not just in the form of questions. You can use commands like "Tell me the weather for next week," or set a situation by asking, "If I were an alien, how would I explore Earth?" By utilizing these various forms, the AI's responses can become much more diverse.

My Thoughts

Once, I tried the prompt, "If I were a famous author, what topics would my book cover?" and it suggested a variety of topics. Thanks to this creative approach, I was able to gain new ideas and organize my thoughts.

Conclusion

Prompt engineering is key to utilizing AI effectively. I’ve learned that asking specific and contextual questions is important to obtain the desired information. By trying out various types of prompts, conversations with AI can become much more beneficial, so I highly recommend giving it a try. If you have any questions, please leave a comment!
