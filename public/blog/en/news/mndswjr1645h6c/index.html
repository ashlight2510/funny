Middle East War Causes 840 Trillion Won Loss in Market Capitalization Over a Month... 'Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix' Account for 44% of the Decrease

The impact of the recent war in the Middle East on the South Korean stock market has been immense, with the total market capitalization of domestic listed companies evaporating by 840 trillion won. Notably, 372 trillion won was lost from just Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, accounting for 44% of the overall decrease. This war is negatively affecting corporate performance forecasts alongside rising commodity prices, further increasing uncertainty about the future direction of the KOSPI index.

This situation is not merely a numerical issue. Given the complex interplay of its impact across the economy and future outlook, it is crucial to understand and prepare for the current economic flow through this article.
