President Yoon Emphasizes Communication via YouTube to First-Term Lawmakers

President Lee Jae-myung recently highlighted the importance of one-person media platforms like YouTube during a dinner with first-term lawmakers from the Democratic Party, urging politicians to strengthen their communication with the public. He emphasized that as the public's desire for direct democracy is growing, efforts should be made to reflect this in communication. Additionally, he reiterated the significance of policies, mentioning issues related to real estate.

These remarks provide important implications for the changing modes of political communication and the Democratic Party's future strategies. Given that platforms like YouTube are playing an increasingly vital role in conveying political messages, attention should be paid to how politicians' responses will evolve.
