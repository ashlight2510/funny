Seoul Subway Line 9 Control Center Malfunction... Express Train Suspension

On the morning of the 17th, a malfunction occurred in the signal equipment at the control center of Seoul Subway Line 9, leading to the suspension of express train operations. As a result, express trains from Gaehwa Station to Chungang Veterans Hospital Station are being operated as regular trains, and monitoring of trains remains challenging. This malfunction has caused inconvenience for many passengers, and the Seoul Metro Line 9 management has stated that they are doing their best to identify the cause and take action.

While this incident appears to be a simple technical issue, it raises concerns about the operational stability of the Seoul subway.
