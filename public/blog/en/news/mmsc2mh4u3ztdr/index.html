How to Save 1 Million Won Annually with Card Benefits

Are you making the most of your card benefits? You might be thinking, “There are so many card benefits, but I’m not sure what to use or how.” So, I’ve organized how to save 1 million won annually with card benefits. From now on, I’ll share the tips I’ve experienced!

The Importance of Choosing the Right Card

Which Card Should You Choose?

To maximize card benefits, it’s crucial to first choose the right card. When I first got a card, I simply chose one based on good points accumulation or cashback offers. However, I later realized that selecting a card that aligns with my frequent spending areas provides much greater benefits.

For example, I tend to dine out a lot, so I chose a card with dining discounts, which ended up saving me a significant amount annually. Analyzing my spending patterns and selecting the right card accordingly is essential.

Types of Card Benefits

Utilizing Various Benefits

The benefits offered by cards are incredibly diverse. There are rewards points, cashback, discounts, and more, and if you utilize these well, you can save a lot of money. The card I primarily use has a slightly higher annual fee, but if I exceed a certain amount each month, I automatically receive cashback.

By doing this, I found that cashback accumulated little by little each month, giving me a substantial amount at the end of the year. In my case, I saved over 1 million won thanks to this card. It would be great for you to compare various card benefits and choose the ones that fit your situation.

Events and Promotions

Don’t Miss Out on Promotions

Card companies frequently hold events or promotions. Missing out on these can be quite disappointing. I remember when I traveled with a certain card, there was a discount event at a specific hotel. Using that card reduced my accommodation costs by half.

To take advantage of such opportunities, it’s important to frequently check the card company’s website or app. Sometimes they also send promotion information via email, so keeping an eye on that can help you enjoy more benefits.

Managing Your Card

Keeping Track of Card Usage

Finally, managing your card usage is also important. When I first started using my card, I only checked my spending records. However, later on, I realized where I was spending a lot of money, which allowed me to consume more efficiently.

In particular, the card company’s app categorizes usage records, and doing this helped me significantly reduce unnecessary spending. This is not unrelated to saving 1 million won annually!

In conclusion, to effectively utilize card benefits, it’s crucial to choose a card that suits you, not miss out on various benefits and promotions, and manage your usage records well. By doing this, you too can easily save 1 million won annually. If you have any questions, please leave a comment!
