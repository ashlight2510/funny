‘Exploring the Perspective of AI’ Trevor Paglen Wins LG Guggenheim Award

Recently, LG selected American media artist Trevor Paglen as the recipient of the ‘LG Guggenheim Award’. This award is given to artists who present innovative art using technology, and Paglen is known for visually exploring the power structures and surveillance systems of AI and digital technology. His works reveal the biases and discriminations of AI while criticizing the neutrality of technology.

Paglen's award is not just an artistic achievement; it prompts reflection on how AI and technology influence our identities and culture in modern society. His work explores the ethical responsibilities of technology and its social context through audience-participatory projects, raising questions about the multifaceted meanings of technology in contemporary society.
