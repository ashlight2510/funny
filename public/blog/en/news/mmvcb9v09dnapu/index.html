Korea Southern Power Brings Warmth to Military Units

Recently, Korea Southern Power held a sponsorship event for soldiers in military units. The event was themed 'Spring Warmth, Sprouting Hope in Military Units' and included a total of 1 million won worth of items, including 80 sets of hamburgers and other snacks, to encourage the soldiers. Notably, all items were purchased from nearby traditional markets using Onnuri gift certificates, considering the local economy. Korea Southern Power emphasized its commitment to social responsibility and coexistence with the local community through this event.

This event is expected to serve as an important opportunity to strengthen the connection between military units and the local community, going beyond simple sponsorship. Such efforts by Korea Southern Power reflect a commitment to corporate social responsibility and could have a positive impact on other public enterprises and private companies in the future.
