‘The Familiar Faces of the ‘National Actor’... Special Exhibition of Ahn Seong-ki at the Jeonju International Film Festival

The news of a special exhibition dedicated to the late Ahn Seong-ki at the Jeonju International Film Festival will serve as an important opportunity to reflect on the history and flow of Korean cinema, beyond just a film festival program. Under the title 'Meeting the Slightly Unfamiliar Ahn Seong-ki', various works of his will be screened, shedding light on his challenging and artistic aspects hidden behind the familiar face to the public. This special exhibition is a chance to revisit his acting legacy and reaffirm his footsteps that broadened the scope of Korean cinema.

This special exhibition will comprehensively showcase Ahn Seong-ki's acting capabilities through seven works spanning various genres and themes from the 1980s to the 2010s. In particular, he contributed to the independent film ecosystem by participating in low-budget art films. This exhibition will re-examine the legacy he left behind and provide audiences with a new perspective.
