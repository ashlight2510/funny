Cultural Tourism Interpreters Promote the Charms of Gyeongnam Every Week on Radio

Gyeongnam Province has launched a new radio program in collaboration with tbn Gyeongnam Traffic Broadcasting to promote local tourism resources. The program airs every Thursday at 5:35 PM, featuring cultural tourism interpreters who directly present the diverse tourist attractions, food, festivals, and historical stories of Gyeongnam. The first broadcast will focus on 'Goseong Sangjokam,' with plans to cover various topics in future episodes.

This program is expected to contribute to enhancing the tourism value of Gyeongnam and provide beneficial information to both local residents and tourists. At a time when the tourism industry needs recovery, it is gaining attention as an effective way to promote local resources.

Why Background Matters

This program was initiated in the context of the significant impact of COVID-19 on Gyeongnam's tourism industry. With a decrease in the number of tourists, the local economy has also contracted, necessitating various efforts for recovery. Cultural tourism interpreters, who are experts well-versed in the region's history and culture, provide an opportunity to enhance the quality of tourism and effectively promote local resources through their explanations.
