Police Apply for Arrest Warrant for Representative of Group that Insulted 'Comfort Women' Victims

Recently, the police applied for an arrest warrant for Kim Byeong-heon, the representative of 'National Action for the Abolition of the Comfort Women Law', on charges of insulting victims of the Japanese military's comfort women system. Kim was accused of displaying a banner that belittled comfort women victims during a protest in front of the statue of a girl in Seoul last December. He faces charges including violations of the Information and Communications Network Act, defamation of the dead, and violations of the Assembly and Demonstration Act. This incident has once again highlighted the issue of defamation against comfort women victims and has sparked social controversy.
