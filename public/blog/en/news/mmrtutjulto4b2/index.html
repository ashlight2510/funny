Causes of Washing Machine Odor and Simple Removal Methods

Sometimes, when using a washing machine, you might be surprised by an unpleasant smell. You might wonder, "What could this be?" It's truly an unpleasant experience, and you may be concerned about how to eliminate it. So today, I've summarized the causes of washing machine odors and simple removal methods.

Causes of Washing Machine Odor

1. Drainage Issues

One of the most common causes is drainage issues. If water accumulates inside the washing machine, bacteria can easily breed. This is especially true for front-loading washing machines, where water often remains. I remember a time when I used a washing machine, and there was still some water left after washing, which caused a strong odor. Therefore, it's important to frequently check the drainage status.

2. Residual Laundry Detergent

After washing, residues of detergent or fabric softener can remain inside the washing machine. If this builds up, it can lead to odors. Once, after cleaning my washing machine for the first time in a while, I noticed a strange smell coming from where the detergent had accumulated. So, I developed a habit of checking the interior after removing the laundry.

Methods to Remove Washing Machine Odor

1. Using Vinegar and Baking Soda

Vinegar and baking soda are really effective for eliminating washing machine odors. I tried it myself: just add one cup of vinegar and half a cup of baking soda to the washing machine drum, and run it on the hottest water setting. This significantly reduced the odor. This method is simple yet effective, so I highly recommend trying it!

2. Regular Washing Machine Cleaning

It's also important to clean the washing machine regularly. Using the washing machine cleaning mode about once a month or cleaning it with the vinegar and baking soda method mentioned above is beneficial. After doing this, I noticed less odor and felt that my laundry was cleaner.

Washing Machine Maintenance Tips

1. Leave the Door Open After Washing

Leaving the washing machine door open after washing is a good practice. This allows the interior to dry properly and reduces the chances of bacteria breeding. I didn't know this at first and kept the door closed, which made the odor worse. So, I got into the habit of leaving the door open, and it improved significantly.

2. Use Washing Machine-Specific Detergent

Finally, using detergent specifically designed for washing machines is also a good idea. It seems to manage the interior of the washing machine better than regular detergent. After using washing machine-specific detergent, I found that there was less odor and my laundry came out softer, which was satisfying.

Conclusion

We explored the causes of washing machine odors and removal methods. By paying attention to drainage issues and residual laundry, and cleaning regularly, you can prevent odors. If you have any questions, please leave them in the comments! I think starting with these tips would be great!
