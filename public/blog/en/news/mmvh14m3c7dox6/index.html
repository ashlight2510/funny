There Was a Reason for High School Uniform Prices: FTC Penalizes Bid Collusion in Gwangju

Recently, school uniform sellers in the Gwangju area have faced sanctions from the Fair Trade Commission (FTC) for colluding in school uniform purchase bids. On the 18th, the FTC imposed corrective orders and a total fine of 321 million won on 27 school uniform sellers. From 2021 to 2023, these sellers created favorable conditions for specific companies through prior agreements in 260 bids, resulting in abnormally high school uniform prices.

This incident reveals a structural problem that imposes a real economic burden on parents and students, going beyond a simple pricing issue. Complaints about high school uniform prices have existed for a long time, and this sanction can be seen as a first step in identifying and resolving the fundamental causes of the problem.

Key Summary

  • School uniform sellers in Gwangju colluded in school uniform purchase bids, leading to FTC sanctions.
  • Corrective orders and a fine of 321 million won were imposed on 27 companies.
  • The collusion resulted in abnormally high school uniform prices, with the total contract amount reaching approximately 10.5 billion won.
  • Bid collusion for school uniforms has been detected multiple times in the past, and the FTC stated it would investigate swiftly and take strict action.

Key Scenes

  • Scope of Collusion: 27 companies reached prior agreements in 260 bids.
  • Economic Impact: Collusion led to increased school uniform prices, placing a significant burden on parents.
  • Legal Action: FTC imposed corrective orders and fines.
  • Historical Context: 47 cases of school uniform collusion detected since 2010.
  • Political Reaction: President Lee Jae-myung directly addressed the issue of school uniform prices.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The issue of school uniform prices is not merely a local problem. Nationwide, the situation has become serious enough for parents and students to describe school uniform prices as "back-breaking." Such problems stem from unfair trading practices like collusion among uniform companies, creating a structure that artificially inflates prices. The FTC is actively working to resolve these issues, and this sanction can be seen as part of that effort.

This incident demonstrates that the issue of school uniform prices cannot simply be dismissed as consumer complaints. The relationship between schools and companies, and the resulting economic burden, directly impacts students and parents. Furthermore, the FTC's actions will play a crucial role in preventing similar incidents in the future.
