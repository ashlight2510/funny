Jeonnam Province to Innovate Fisheries Structure: Reasons to Read Now

March 16, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Jeonnam Province to Innovate Fisheries Structure···Investing 51.6 Billion Won in Vessel Reduction and Disaster Insurance

Jeollanam-do is embarking on a large-scale investment to improve the structure and competitiveness of the fishing industry. This policy aims to respond to the changing fishing environment due to climate change and the decline of marine resources, with a total investment of 51.6 billion won across 16 projects. Key components include vessel reduction, easing fishing regulations, and strengthening safety measures, with a focus on enhancing the management stability and safety of fishermen.

These changes reflect a commitment to address practical issues in the fishing industry, going beyond mere policy measures. In particular, the distribution of various equipment and the expansion of disaster insurance support to address the aging population and labor shortages in fishing villages are becoming key elements.

Summary of the Article

Jeollanam-do is investing 51.6 billion won in various policies such as vessel reduction, easing fishing regulations, and strengthening safety measures to enhance the competitiveness of the fishing industry.

Key Points

  • Jeollanam-do invests 51.6 billion won in modernization policies for the fishing industry
  • Reducing excess vessels through coastal fishing vessel reduction
  • Easing fishing regulations to reduce management burdens
  • Strengthening safety nets for fishermen and expanding disaster insurance support
  • Distributing equipment to address labor shortages due to aging

Background and Context

This policy was initiated in response to the chronic issues of excess vessels and declining marine resources in the fishing industry. Jeollanam-do has been developing various policies to enhance the safety and management stability of fishermen, and this investment of 51.6 billion won is a continuation of those efforts. The aging problem in fishing villages has led to labor shortages, creating a widespread recognition of the need for concrete measures to address this issue.

Why This Issue is Important

Fishing plays a crucial role in the local economy, but it is currently facing challenges due to climate change and resource depletion. In this context, Jeollanam-do's policy aims not only to improve the structure of the fishing industry but also to enhance the quality of life for local residents and create a sustainable fishing environment. Additionally, the expansion of disaster insurance that guarantees the safety of fishermen is significant as it directly relates to their livelihoods, contributing to the establishment of a social safety net.

What to Check Now

It is essential to closely monitor how vessel reduction and the expansion of disaster insurance support will be implemented and the effects of this policy on fishermen. In particular, attention should be paid to how much the management stability and safety of fishermen improve after the policy is implemented.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is vessel reduction?
A1: Vessel reduction is a policy aimed at solving the problem of excess vessels by reducing the number of fishing boats. The goal is to recover marine resources and enhance the competitiveness of the fishing industry.

Q2: What does easing fishing regulations include?
A2: Easing fishing regulations aims to reduce the management burden on fishermen through system improvements that fit the field. Specific details will be announced in the future.

Q3: How will the expansion of disaster insurance support be implemented?
A3: The disaster insurance support will be expanded from existing fishing vessels of 3 tons or more to all fishing vessels, allowing more fishermen to be protected.

Q4: What changes are expected after the policy is implemented?
A4: After the policy is implemented, improvements in the safety and management stability of fishermen are expected, along with an increase in the vitality of fishing villages.
