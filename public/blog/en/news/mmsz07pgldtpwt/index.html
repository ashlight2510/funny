After the awards ceremony, a congratulatory stage featuring K-pop performances, pansori, and samulnori was held, providing an opportunity to showcase the charm of Korean traditional culture to the world.

This event holds significance beyond just winning an award. The share of Korean cultural content in the global market is increasing, demonstrating how traditional and modern Korean cultures can merge.
