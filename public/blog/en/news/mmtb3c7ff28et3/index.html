Reasons to Consider the Background

This U.S.-China high-level meeting took place at a time when trade wars and technological competition between the two countries were intensifying. The U.S. and China have significant grievances regarding each other's economic policies and technological advancements, and these tensions are negatively affecting the global economy. Therefore, this meeting is regarded as an important opportunity to resolve conflicts and seek paths for cooperation.

Why It Matters

Economic cooperation between the U.S. and China extends beyond the issues of the two countries, significantly impacting the stability of the global economy and politics. Particularly, since the economies of both nations constitute a major pillar of the world economy, smoother cooperation could reduce uncertainty in global markets and promote economic growth. Conversely, if the talks fail, conflicts such as trade wars could escalate again.
