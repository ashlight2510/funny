Dormitory for Agricultural Workers Completed in Yeongam, Jeollanam-do···Over 40 Cambodian Nationals Expected to Move In

A dormitory for foreign seasonal agricultural workers has been completed in Yeongam-gun, Jeollanam-do. This dormitory was constructed with a budget of 5.5 billion won after being selected for a public project by the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs in 2022, and over 40 Cambodian nationals are expected to move in for the first time in early April. The dormitory is two stories high and has 23 rooms, each equipped with individual bathrooms and showers. Yeongam-gun expects that this dormitory will help alleviate the labor shortage in rural areas and improve the human rights issues of foreign workers.

The construction of this dormitory is the result of Jeollanam-do's continuous requests, and currently, there are a total of 10 agricultural workers' dormitories operating in the Jeollanam-do region. Acting Governor Hwang Gi-yeon emphasized that this dormitory is a welfare model that encompasses the housing, safety, and living convenience of foreign workers, going beyond mere accommodation.

Why Background Matters

The construction of this dormitory is the result of Jeollanam-do's ongoing efforts, initiated by the need to improve the living conditions of foreign workers. The labor shortage in rural areas has become severe, increasing reliance on foreign workers, which has led to frequent discussions about their living conditions and human rights. Jeollanam-do proposed the construction of agricultural workers' dormitories to be reflected in government policy, resulting in the completion of this dormitory.

Why It Matters

This dormitory is expected to play a significant role in improving the human rights and welfare of foreign workers, going beyond just being a living space. It will not only help alleviate labor shortages in rural areas but also support foreign workers in working in a more stable environment, contributing to an improved quality of life. This is likely to enhance agricultural productivity, positively impacting the local economy.

A1: Residents are selected from seasonal workers of Cambodian nationality who have signed labor contracts with the Wolchulsan Agricultural Cooperative.
