Last year's visitors spent 80,000 won per person at 'National Parks'... Total 3.5 trillion

In 2025, visitors to South Korea's 23 national parks spent a total of 3.5 trillion won. This corresponds to approximately 82,000 won per person, with Bukhansan recording the highest number of visitors at 7.53 million and the highest spending at 623.5 billion won. Interestingly, the 15 national parks located in areas with declining populations accounted for about 47% of total spending, playing a significant role in the local economy. The Korea National Park Service announced plans to expand programs that encourage visitors to stay longer based on these spending patterns.

This article not only illustrates the spending patterns of national park visitors but also provides important insights related to the economic revitalization of areas with declining populations. It is essential to note that national parks are not merely tourist attractions but have a substantial impact on the local economy.

Key Highlights

  • Total visitor spending in national parks in 2025: 3.5 trillion won, approximately 82,000 won per person.
  • Bukhansan recorded the highest number of visitors (7.53 million) and spending (623.5 billion won).
  • Spending in areas with declining populations (including 15 national parks) accounted for 47% of total spending.
  • Spending increases with the length of stay: average 83,000 won for a one-day visit, 356,000 won for a 3-night, 4-day visit.
  • The Korea National Park Service plans to expand stay-oriented programs.
