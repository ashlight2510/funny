Oh Se-hoon registers as a candidate for Seoul mayor... “The heart of a frontline commander”

On the 17th, Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon registered as a candidate for the Seoul mayor from the People Power Party and held a press conference. He emphasized his sense of responsibility towards the citizens of Seoul and demanded innovation and change within the party. In particular, he criticized the lack of will for change from Representative Jang Dong-hyuk and expressed his intention to start change from Seoul. Mayor Oh stated that he would enter the election with “the heart of a frontline commander,” pledging to fulfill the responsibilities entrusted to him.

This candidate registration is interpreted as a move to strengthen Mayor Oh's political position ahead of the Seoul mayoral election. The necessity of the innovative election committee he proposed reflects the need for internal conflict and change within the party, which is expected to have a significant impact on future elections.

Why Background Matters

This issue arises as Mayor Oh Se-hoon seeks to solidify his political position ahead of the Seoul mayoral election. He has previously served as the mayor of Seoul, and this candidate registration is interpreted as an intention to present a new vision based on past achievements. In particular, in a situation where internal conflicts within the party are intensifying, Mayor Oh's remarks can also be interpreted as an effort to solidify internal unity.
