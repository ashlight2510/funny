How to Use Claude: A New World of Creation with AI

Recently, as interest in AI has been rising, I found myself pondering how I could utilize it. In particular, I wanted to explore the assistance of AI in the creative field, and I came across a tool called Claude. So, through this article, I have organized how to use Claude along with my impressions.

What is Claude?

A New Companion for AI

Claude is an AI-based creative tool that helps in writing various forms of text. For example, it can generate blog posts, stories, articles, and more. When I first used it, I wondered, "Can I really write what I want?" But after trying it out, I was amazed by its possibilities.

Since I mainly write blog posts, Claude has been a great help in organizing my ideas and even structuring my writing. I found it to be more intuitive than I expected, making it accessible for first-time users.

Using Claude: Basic Approach

Starting with Simple Commands

To use Claude, you just need to input simple commands. For instance, if you request, "Write an article on this topic," the AI generates text that fits the topic. Initially, I was concerned, "Will my desired style or tone be reflected?"

However, after a few attempts, I found that if I slightly modified my requests to match my desired style, the AI could create text accordingly. For example, if I said, "Write a blog post in a friendly tone," I received much more natural-sounding text.

Using Claude: Expanding Creativity

A Treasure Trove of Creative Ideas

One of the best aspects of using Claude is that it acts like a treasure trove of ideas. Sometimes, when writing, I hit a wall or feel overwhelmed about what to write. In those moments, if I ask Claude, "Is it okay to write on this topic?" it suggests various ideas.

When I recently asked Claude about a topic I was trying, it provided ideas from multiple angles. This greatly assisted me in writing and broadened my creative horizons. Personally, I found this aspect to be incredibly useful.

Limitations and Cautions of Claude

Avoiding Over-Reliance

While Claude is a really useful tool, it's important not to rely on it completely. The content generated by AI is not always perfect. Sometimes, the context may be awkward or include unnecessary information. Therefore, I always make sure to add my thoughts and make revisions based on the text created by AI.

By doing this, my style shines through better, and I can complete more personalized writing. It's crucial to receive help from AI while ensuring that I don't lose my voice.

Conclusion

Claude can be an excellent tool that serves as a new companion in creation. It has greatly helped in expanding ideas and alleviating the overwhelm of writing. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I plan to continue using Claude, and I hope this article has been helpful!
