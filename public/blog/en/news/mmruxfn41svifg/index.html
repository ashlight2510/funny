Everything About Project Management Using Notion

Have you ever wondered which tool to use for project management? I used to think about that a lot. After trying various tools, I finally settled on Notion, and today I’ve organized how to utilize Notion based on my experience.

What is Notion?

The All-in-One Tool, Notion

Notion is an all-in-one workspace that can be used for various purposes such as note-taking, schedule management, and project management. When I first started using it, I was overwhelmed by the many features and didn’t know where to begin, but once I learned the basic usage, I found it incredibly useful. Particularly, the real-time updates when working with team members is a significant advantage.

My Experience

Personally, I liked Notion's intuitive interface. There are various templates available, making it easy to modify them to fit the project, and I appreciated being able to customize the pages as I wanted. This allowed me to keep project management visually organized and neat.

Creating a Project Management Template

How to Create a Template

Notion provides basic templates, but creating your own is also really easy. You just need to create a new page and select the 'Template' option. Here, you can freely add the project's goals, schedule, to-do lists, and more.

This Part Was Important

The most important thing I considered when creating my template was 'prioritization.' Organizing tasks by priority allows you to see at a glance which tasks are most urgent. Since I implemented this, the speed of project progress has definitely increased.

Collaboration with Team Members

Advantages of Real-Time Collaboration

One of the biggest advantages of Notion is the ability to collaborate with team members in real-time. It’s really convenient to write down tasks and exchange feedback. For example, when a team member comments on a document I wrote, I can check it immediately and make quick corrections.

Insights from Collaboration

At first, we were using different tools, but after integrating into Notion, I felt that our teamwork improved. Since we can easily check each other's progress, communication among team members has become smoother, and maintaining project consistency has become much easier.

Utilizing Databases

Managing at a Glance with Databases

Notion allows you to systematically manage projects using its database feature. You can add necessary information to each item and easily extract only the required data through filters. For example, filtering by deadlines when managing schedules is really useful.

The Effects I Felt

Thanks to this feature, I could grasp the overall project at a glance. Previously, I managed projects using Excel, but switching to Notion made it much more intuitive. Now, checking Notion daily has become a part of my routine.

Conclusion

Today, I talked about project management using Notion. I felt that utilizing various features is genuinely beneficial. I hope you also use this tool to manage your projects more efficiently. If you have any questions, please leave a comment!
