Daejeon Factory Fire, Rescue Operations Begin

A fire at an automobile parts manufacturing factory in Daejeon on the 20th has left 14 employees unaccounted for. The fire broke out at 1:17 PM, and the fire department delayed rescue and search operations due to concerns about structural collapse after initial suppression, beginning serious search efforts around 10:30 PM, approximately 9 hours after the fire started. It is presumed that the missing individuals were mainly in the break room on the second floor.

This incident is not just a simple fire but a serious situation related to potential loss of life, raising questions about the safety management systems of the Daejeon community and businesses.
