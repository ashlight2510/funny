President Yoon Reviews War Supplementary Budget and Direct Support for Citizens

Recently, President Yoon Jae-myung directed the preparation of a 'war supplementary budget' in anticipation of the prolonged conflict between the U.S., Iran, and Israel. He also emphasized the need to consider implementing a vehicle restriction system for energy conservation and direct support measures for citizens. This action stems from concerns that instability in the Middle East will significantly impact the South Korean economy.

This announcement signifies the government's proactive response to ensure the stability of citizens' lives and protect livelihoods, going beyond mere financial support. Notably, the government has clarified its policy direction by prioritizing support for economically vulnerable regions. The government's swift response to external shocks like war is expected to play a crucial role in enhancing public trust.

Background and Context of the Issue

This issue arose as the possibility of a prolonged war in the Middle East has increased. With escalating conflicts between Iran and Israel, concerns about rising energy prices and supply disruptions are growing. South Korea imports a significant amount of energy through the Strait of Hormuz, making these external factors likely to have a direct impact on the economy. Therefore, the government needs to swiftly devise countermeasures.
