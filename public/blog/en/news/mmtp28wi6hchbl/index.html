Examining the Selective Investigation of the Special Prosecutor

Recently, it has come to light that Kwon Chang-young's second comprehensive special investigation has returned some of the cases received from the police back to them, raising criticisms about the investigation methods of the special prosecutor. In particular, the exclusion of the allegations regarding former President Yoon Suk-yeol's secret campaign operations from the investigation has raised questions about whether the special prosecutor truly had the will to clarify the suspicions. This has led to criticisms that the comprehensive special investigation is selectively investigating cases, raising concerns that it may be avoiding investigations based on the severity of the cases.

Such a situation can have serious implications beyond mere legal procedures, potentially affecting the overall trust in society. If the special prosecutor's selective handling of cases becomes a norm, it could foster doubts about fair investigations and ultimately undermine the justice of the law.

Key Summary

  • Kwon Chang-young's second comprehensive special investigation has returned some cases received from the police back to them.
  • The exclusion of the allegations regarding former President Yoon Suk-yeol's secret campaign operations has drawn criticism.
  • There are claims that the special prosecutor is selectively investigating cases.
  • The lack of investigation requests for insurrection-related cases is also a point of controversy.
  • There are no regulations in the special prosecutor law regarding the re-transfer of cases, allowing for selective choices by the special prosecutor.

Key Scenes

  • Selective Investigation by the Special Prosecutor: A tendency to selectively investigate specific cases has emerged.
  • Declining Trust: Growing doubts about fair investigations.
  • Legal Vacuum: Unclear regulations in the special prosecutor law allow for arbitrary interpretations.
  • Social Fallout: There is a risk of undermining trust in legal justice.
  • Pushback from Stakeholders: Anticipated backlash from victims and civic groups.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident arose during the investigation process of the comprehensive special prosecutor aimed at clarifying the remaining suspicions from last year's three major special prosecutors' investigations. There are still unresolved cases that the three major special prosecutors did not investigate, and the selective handling of these cases by the comprehensive special prosecutor is seen as a significant issue. In particular, the return of cases related to former President Yoon Suk-yeol to the police cannot be ruled out as being influenced by political interests.

Why It Matters

This incident goes beyond a simple procedural issue, leading to questions of social trust necessary to uphold legal justice and fairness. If the special prosecutor selectively handles cases, it could result in absolving certain individuals or groups, threatening the foundations of democracy and the rule of law. Therefore, this matter is likely to be an important issue for many.

Points to Watch

In my view, the most noteworthy aspect of this incident is the unclear regulations of the special prosecutor law. This creates room for the special prosecutor to selectively handle cases, ultimately undermining trust in legal fairness. Additionally, given the expected backlash from victims and civic groups, it is essential to listen to their voices.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One aspect that may be overlooked in this incident is the criteria used by the special prosecutor to judge the cases under investigation. If avoiding investigations based on the severity of the cases or the likelihood of guilt becomes a norm, it could hinder the realization of legal justice. Furthermore, the absence of investigation requests for insurrection-related cases suggests the possibility of political influences at play.

Current Points to Check

  • Progress of the Special Prosecutor's Investigation: It is essential to pay attention to which cases the special prosecutor will investigate in the future.
  • Need for Legal Regulations: As calls for amendments to the special prosecutor law are expected to grow, discussions on this matter are also crucial.
  • Reactions from Civic Groups: Monitoring the responses of civic groups and victims regarding this incident and understanding their demands is important.
