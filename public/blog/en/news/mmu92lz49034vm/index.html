Common Mistakes in Choosing Engine Oil and the Right Approach

Engine oil is a key factor that directly affects the performance and lifespan of a vehicle. However, many drivers make mistakes when selecting engine oil, and we will explore how to avoid these mistakes. Choosing the wrong oil can lead to engine damage or performance degradation, so careful selection is necessary.

This article will provide insights into the importance of engine oil, common mistakes, the correct selection methods, a checklist, and finally, a comparison table of different types of engine oils to aid understanding.

Importance of Engine Oil: Why Should You Be Careful?

Engine oil reduces friction within the engine, dissipates heat, and prevents corrosion. If these functions do not operate correctly, engine performance can decline, leading to serious failures.

  • Checkpoint: Always refer to the vehicle manual to check the manufacturer's recommended specifications. For example, certain vehicles require synthetic oil, and using mineral oil can cause engine damage.

Common Mistakes When Choosing Engine Oil

1. Viscosity Selection Error: Choosing According to the Season is Important

Viscosity indicates the flow characteristics of engine oil and significantly impacts engine operation. Choosing the wrong viscosity can increase engine load and worsen fuel efficiency.

  • Action Tip: In summer, choose oil that performs well at high temperatures, and in winter, select oil that flows well at low temperatures. For example, using 20W-50 oil in summer may perform well at high temperatures, but its flow may decrease in winter.

2. Ignoring Oil Change Intervals: Regular Checks are Essential

Oil deteriorates over time, which can lead to engine damage. It is important to change the oil regularly according to the manufacturer's recommended intervals.

  • Checkpoint: Generally, change the oil every 5,000 to 10,000 km, but if you frequently drive on congested roads, set a shorter change interval. Regular checks and changes are the key to keeping the engine healthy.

3. Using Low-Cost Oil: Quality Affects Performance

Low-cost oil may be of inferior quality, which can negatively impact engine performance.

  • Action Tip: Check for quality certification marks to choose reliable products. Using low-cost oil can result in increased engine noise or reduced fuel efficiency.

How to Choose the Right Engine Oil

Choosing the right engine oil plays a crucial role in extending vehicle performance and lifespan. Refer to the vehicle manual to select the appropriate oil.

  • Checkpoint: Understand the characteristics of various types of oils, such as synthetic oil, mineral oil, and semi-synthetic oil. For instance, synthetic oil offers excellent performance at high temperatures and longer change intervals but is more expensive.

Engine Oil Selection Checklist

Use the following checklist when selecting engine oil:

  1. Check the vehicle manual
  2. Choose viscosity and type
  3. Set change intervals
  4. Verify quality certification
  5. Check oil level

This checklist can help reduce mistakes in selecting engine oil and maintain vehicle performance and lifespan.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How often should engine oil be changed?

    • It varies according to the manufacturer's recommendations, but generally, it is advisable to change it every 5,000 to 10,000 km.

  2. What is the difference between synthetic oil and mineral oil?

    • Synthetic oil provides higher performance, while mineral oil is cheaper but may have lower performance.

  3. How do I choose the viscosity of engine oil?

    • It is important to refer to the vehicle manual to select the appropriate viscosity and adjust according to the climate.

  4. What are the risks of using low-cost oil?

    • Low-cost oil may be of inferior quality, leading to engine performance degradation and increased noise.

In conclusion, engine oil is a significant factor that greatly affects vehicle performance and lifespan. To choose the right oil, check the vehicle manual and carefully consider viscosity and type. Check your vehicle manual now and select the appropriate oil!
