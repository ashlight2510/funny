Men's U-20 Football Team Begins First Training Camp Under Coach Kim Jung-soo

On March 18, 2026, the men's U-20 football team announced that it will conduct its first training camp under the guidance of newly appointed coach Kim Jung-soo. The training will take place from March 23 to 31 at the Jeonnam Beolgyo Ecological Park, with 34 players participating to assess tactical understanding and player combinations. Coach Kim was appointed through a public recruitment process for age-specific national team coaches, and his experience and vision are expected to significantly impact player development.

This training camp is an important opportunity in preparation for the AFC U-20 Asian Cup qualifiers scheduled for August, and Coach Kim expressed his ambition to build a competitive team. Through this training, players will be tested on the skills necessary for international competition and will lay the groundwork for their future growth into the senior national team.
