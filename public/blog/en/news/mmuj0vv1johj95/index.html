Seoul's Joint Housing Public Price Rises 18.67% - Impact of Soaring House Prices in Gangnam and the Han River Belt

Recently, it has been reported that the average public price of joint housing in Seoul has increased by 18.67% compared to last year. This is the highest increase since 2021, primarily attributed to the rise in transaction prices of high-priced housing. Notably, the price increases are particularly pronounced in the three districts of Gangnam, Seocho, and Songpa, as well as areas adjacent to the Han River, significantly impacting the real estate market nationwide, with about 170,000 additional homes subject to comprehensive real estate tax.

These changes are likely to lead not only to an increase in public prices but also to a rise in property taxes and pressure for increased listings. Therefore, this news will serve as an important clue in predicting the future trends of the real estate market.

Key Summary

  • Average public price of joint housing in Seoul rises 18.67%, the highest since 2021
  • Significant price increases observed in the three districts of Gangnam and areas adjacent to the Han River
  • Approximately 170,000 additional homes subject to comprehensive real estate tax, indicating an increase in property taxes
  • Public price realization rate frozen at 69%, reflecting last year's market price increase
  • Experts predict an increase in tax-efficient sales

Key Scenes

  • Rise centered on high-priced housing in Seoul: The increase in the three districts of Gangnam is the highest at 24.7%.
  • Increase in homes subject to comprehensive real estate tax: The number of homes with public prices exceeding 1.2 billion won has risen to 487,362.
  • Increase in property taxes: Tax burdens for owners of high-priced homes are expected to surge.
  • Pressure for increased listings: There is a possibility of increased sales volumes for tax-saving purposes.
  • Existence of price decline regions nationwide: Public prices have decreased in eight local governments, including Incheon and Daejeon.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The recent rise in public prices is closely related to last year's real estate market trends. The surge in transactions of high-priced homes has led to an overall increase in public prices. According to the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, it is noteworthy that the public price realization rate has been frozen at 69% for four consecutive years, failing to reflect actual market price increases.

In this context, particularly in the Gangnam and Han River belt areas, where high-priced homes are concentrated, the magnitude of price increases has been more pronounced. This could result in increased tax burdens for owners of high-priced homes.

Why It Matters

The rise in public prices is not just a numerical change but an important signal predicting the future direction of the real estate market. As property taxes increase, there is a higher likelihood that multi-homeowners and high-priced property owners will decide to sell for tax-saving purposes. This could ultimately lead to an increase in market listings, resulting in a temporary decline in house prices.

Points to Watch

In reality, owners of high-priced homes are likely to consider selling to reduce their tax burdens. In this case, while the increase in listings could stabilize the market, it could also induce price declines. Therefore, real estate investors and actual buyers should closely monitor these changes.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While the article emphasizes the rise in high-priced homes, there are actually regions experiencing price declines. Public prices have decreased in areas such as Incheon, Daejeon, and Daegu, which could affect local economies or housing demand. Such regional differences are also important factors to consider.

Points to Check Now

  • Public Price Viewing: Available for viewing from the 18th to the 6th of next month at the relevant city or district office and through the real estate public price notifier.
  • Predictions on Property Tax Changes: It is important to analyze trends in property tax changes by region and housing type.

FAQ

1. What impact does the rise in public prices have on house prices? The rise in public prices leads to an increase in property taxes, which may prompt multi-homeowners to decide to sell. This can result in an increase in listings, potentially leading to a decline in house prices.

2. How has the number of homes subject to comprehensive real estate tax changed? The number of homes subject to comprehensive real estate tax has increased from 317,998 last year to 487,362.

3. How can I view public prices? Public prices can be viewed from the 18th to the 6th of next month at the real estate public price notifier and at the relevant city or district office where the property is located.

4. What is the outlook for the real estate market? Experts predict an increase in listings, which could lead to a decline in house prices.

Considering these factors comprehensively, the current real estate market situation encompasses complex trends beyond simple price changes. Therefore, it is necessary to closely observe future changes.
