Today's Personnel: Personnel Changes in the Ministry of Personnel Management and Major Institutions

Recently, there have been personnel changes in the Ministry of Personnel Management and several major institutions. This personnel change seems to be part of an effort to enhance the efficiency of public servants and strengthen the expertise of each institution. In particular, there have been transfers at the department head level in the Ministry of Personnel Management, as well as personnel movements in Samjin Pharmaceutical, the Jogye Order, and Buddhist Broadcasting. These changes are expected to have a significant impact on the direction and operation of each institution.

This personnel change is not just a simple position change but could serve as an important opportunity to redefine the strategies and visions of each institution.
