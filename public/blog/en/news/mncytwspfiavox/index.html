Digital Times CEO Park Hak-yong Reappointed: Its Significance and Impact

Digital Times has recently reappointed CEO Park Hak-yong through its regular shareholders' meeting and board of directors. Park has been serving as the CEO and publisher of Digital Times since 2018 and is currently also the president of the Korea Online Newspaper Association. This reappointment is expected to have a significant impact on the future direction and strategy of Digital Times.

This news raises important questions about the role Digital Times will play in the changing landscape of digital media and the news industry, going beyond the mere reappointment of an individual.
