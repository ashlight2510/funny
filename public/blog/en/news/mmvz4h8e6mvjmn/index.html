Mother and Son Found Dead in Gunsan... Rent and Electricity Bills Overdue

Recently, a tragic incident occurred in Gunsan, Jeollabuk-do, where a mother in her 70s and her son in his 30s were found dead. They had overdue rent and electricity bills, and the police are investigating the possibility that financial difficulties may have been the cause. The discovery of the incident was made when the landlord visited the apartment after losing contact with the tenants, and so far, the possibility of homicide appears low. This incident carries significance beyond a simple tragedy.

This case reveals the complex context intertwined with our society's welfare system and economic hardships. It illustrates how families that are not affluent struggle to survive while also reminding us of the need for a social safety net.

This incident is not limited to the tragic deaths of two individuals. Amidst a recent economic downturn and an increase in isolated lives, it highlights the insufficient social support for these individuals. Mr. A, who was not classified as a recipient of basic living assistance, was living on a monthly basic pension of 350,000 won, which is grossly inadequate for basic sustenance.

Moreover, the overdue electricity and water bills, combined with their isolation from the outside world, suggest that they were living without any support network. This isolation can lead to mental and emotional issues, creating a vicious cycle that leads back to economic hardship.
