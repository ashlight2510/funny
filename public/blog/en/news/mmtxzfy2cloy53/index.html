Is Lee Jin-sook, the candidate for Daegu mayor, a match for Go Seong-guk? Kim Jong-in says, "People will laugh."

Recently, the possibility of Lee Jin-sook, former chair of the Korea Communications Commission, being nominated as a candidate for Daegu mayor has sparked heated controversy in the political arena. Kim Jong-in, former chairman of the People Power Party's emergency response committee, strongly criticized the nomination, asserting that it does not represent true innovation and that the current political situation should not overlook what the citizens of Daegu want. He specifically raised concerns about Lee Jin-sook's connections with the far-right YouTuber Go Seong-guk, questioning whether such a figure running for Daegu mayor can truly be considered innovative.

This issue extends beyond a mere candidate nomination; it raises significant questions about the identity and future direction of the People Power Party. In particular, in a conservative stronghold like Daegu, discussions are needed on how a new generation of politicians can establish themselves amidst the ongoing influence of past figures.
