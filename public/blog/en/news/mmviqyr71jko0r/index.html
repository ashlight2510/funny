Deputy Prime Minister Baek Kyung-hoon Emphasizes the 'Golden Time' for AI Innovation in Korea

Recently, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Science and ICT Baek Kyung-hoon presented his vision for Korea's artificial intelligence (AI) industry. He mentioned that global AI companies like Anthropic and Google DeepMind should also emerge in Korea, emphasizing that the next 2-3 years will be a critical period for achieving this goal. His remarks came during a meeting aimed at securing independent AI and promoting AI services for the public, highlighting the need for cooperation between the government and businesses.

The Deputy Prime Minister stressed the importance of AI technology for national security and the economy, expressing his commitment to support domestic AI companies in gaining global competitiveness. He particularly forecasted that this year and next year will be the peak of the AI boom, adding that government policy support is essential.

Key Summary

  • Deputy Prime Minister Baek Kyung-hoon emphasized that Korean AI companies must achieve global competitiveness.
  • He mentioned that the next 2-3 years will be the 'Golden Time' for the AI industry.
  • He highlighted the need for cooperation between the government and businesses to secure independent AI and promote AI services for the public.
  • He pointed out the importance of AI technology for national security and the economy.
  • The government plans to introduce an equity participation investment model for domestic AI companies.

Key Points

  • Global Competitiveness of AI Companies: The assertion that global AI companies like Anthropic are needed in Korea.
  • Golden Time: The next 2-3 years are emphasized as a critical point for the AI industry.
  • National Security: The importance of AI technology as a strategic asset for the nation.
  • Government Support Policies: Plans to introduce an equity participation investment model beyond simple project support.
  • Peak of AI Boom: This year and next year are predicted to be the time when AI technology accelerates.

Why Context Matters

These remarks stem from the urgency for Korea to secure independent AI technology and strengthen its position in the global market amid fierce global AI competition. In particular, the emphasis on autonomous control of AI technology due to recent situations in the Middle East highlights the government's search for various policies to expand the AI ecosystem.

Why It Matters

This news is significant as it underscores the need for Korea's AI industry to consider the impact on national security and the economy beyond mere technology development. There are growing concerns that if AI technology does not achieve global competitiveness, it could negatively affect Korea's economy and security. Therefore, cooperation between the government and businesses is essential to lay the foundation for healthy growth in the AI industry.

FAQ

Q: What does securing independent AI mean?
A: Securing independent AI refers to building a self-sufficient and stable technological ecosystem through AI technologies developed domestically, without relying on external technologies.

Q: What is the government's equity participation investment model?
A: This model involves the government directly investing in promising startups, sharing joint responsibility for the growth and success of the companies, thereby supporting their funding and growth.

Q: What is the AI boom?
A: The AI boom refers to a period when artificial intelligence technology rapidly develops and becomes commercialized, expected to bring significant changes across industries.

Q: How can the global competitiveness of Korean AI companies be enhanced?
