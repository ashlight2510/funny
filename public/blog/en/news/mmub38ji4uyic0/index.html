Eco-friendly Marathon 'Muhae Run' will be held again this year

This year, the eco-friendly marathon 'Muhae Run' will take place. Scheduled for April 5th at Seoul World Cup Park, this marathon is known as a zero-waste event that minimizes the use of disposable products and reduces carbon emissions. The organizers plan to continue the success of last year's event, which resulted in only one piece of trash being produced, accounting for just 0.01% compared to a typical marathon. The aim is to spread a sustainable running culture and to commemorate Arbor Day by reflecting on the value of resources.

Such eco-friendly marathons are establishing themselves as platforms that go beyond mere athletic competitions, raising awareness about environmental protection and the importance of sustainable living. Participants in the event will use reusable timing chips and cups, and will be provided with vegan food, engaging in various environmentally conscious ways.

Key Summary

  • Event Overview: 2026 Muhae Run, to be held on April 5th at Seoul World Cup Park.
  • Eco-friendly Elements: Use of reusable and recyclable items, provision of vegan food.
  • Achievements: Last year's event produced only one piece of trash, accounting for 0.01% compared to typical marathons.
  • Registration: 1,000 participants to be recruited, applications can be made through an online platform.

Key Highlights
