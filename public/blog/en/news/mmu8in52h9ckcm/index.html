Eunpyeong-gu, Support for Insurance Premiums for Disabled Electric Mobility Devices

Eunpyeong-gu in Seoul has announced support for insurance for electric mobility devices to facilitate the mobility of disabled individuals and the elderly. This insurance is targeted at residents who are registered disabled and elderly individuals over 65 years old living in Eunpyeong-gu, using electric wheelchairs or electric scooters, and it covers damages to third parties resulting from accidents. The coverage amount is up to 50 million won, and enrollment is automatic without a separate application process, with a personal contribution of 200,000 won.

This policy is implemented to reduce the anxiety that disabled individuals and the elderly may experience while using electric mobility devices and to help them move more freely. Kim Mi-kyung, the head of Eunpyeong-gu, mentioned that this insurance support will promote the participation of disabled individuals in daily life.

Key Summary

  • Eunpyeong-gu announces support for insurance for electric mobility devices for disabled individuals and the elderly.
  • The insurance covers damages to third parties resulting from accidents while using electric wheelchairs and electric scooters.
  • Coverage is up to 50 million won, with a personal contribution of 200,000 won.
  • Enrollment is automatic without a separate application process.

Key Points

  • Target Group: Registered disabled individuals and elderly individuals over 65 living in Eunpyeong-gu.
  • Coverage Scope: Damages to third parties caused during the use of electric mobility devices.
  • Coverage Amount: Up to 50 million won.
  • Enrollment Process: Automatic enrollment without a separate application process.
  • Purpose: To ensure the mobility freedom of disabled individuals and alleviate anxiety about accidents.

Why Background Matters

This insurance support is part of a policy aimed at enhancing the mobility of disabled individuals and the elderly, responding to the social demand for consideration and support for vulnerable groups. Electric mobility devices are crucial means of transportation for disabled individuals and the elderly, but many have faced situations where they were not compensated for damages in the event of an accident. Eunpyeong-gu is actively addressing this issue.

Why It Matters

Ensuring that disabled individuals and the elderly can safely use electric mobility devices contributes to their mobility rights and quality of life. The freedom to move impacts not only convenience but also social participation and independence, making this insurance support significant in itself. Particularly in South Korea, which is entering an aging society, such policies will become increasingly important.

Personal Comment

I believe that this insurance support will positively impact social awareness beyond mere economic assistance. As understanding and consideration for disabled individuals and the elderly spread, an environment will be created where they can participate more actively in society. Of course, it will be necessary to evaluate how many people will actually benefit from this policy, but I think it is a positive first step.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Although the personal contribution for this insurance is set at 200,000 won, it may still be a burden for some low-income disabled individuals or the elderly. Additionally, it is important to note that the coverage is limited to third-party damages, excluding personal injury or automobile accidents. Therefore, it is necessary to recognize that this insurance does not cover all situations.

Points to Check Now

  • For inquiries related to the insurance, you can contact the dedicated consultation line for electric mobility device insurance (02-2038-0828, ARS 1) or the Eunpyeong-gu Office's Welfare for the Disabled Department (02-351-7313), so feel free to reach out if needed.
  • It is also advisable to check if there are safety training or seminars available to prevent accidents while using electric mobility devices.
