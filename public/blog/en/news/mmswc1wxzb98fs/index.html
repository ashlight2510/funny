The Reality of the Hormuz Strait and Iran's Nuclear Weapon Card: Week 3 of War

Currently, the war between Iran and the US-Israel alliance has entered its third week, and Iran continues to show its will to resist amidst the fighting. Iran's strategy to gain an advantageous position in negotiations with the US through the blockade of the Hormuz Strait and its stockpile of highly enriched uranium is having a significant impact on the international community. Despite military losses due to the war, the Iranian regime remains resilient, and while the US hints at an early end to the conflict, the situation is intricately complicated.

Despite military strikes against Iran, an exit from the war remains elusive, and Iran's possession of highly enriched uranium implies the potential for nuclear weapon development. The Iranian Foreign Minister has suggested a prolonged conflict and expressed a determination to resist despite US pressure. Iran's strategy goes beyond mere military resistance; it focuses on leveraging international oil prices and economic pressure to exert pressure on the US.

Key Points

  • Iran's Will to Resist: Iran shows a willingness to resist despite attacks from the US and Israel, suggesting a prolonged conflict.
  • Blockade of the Hormuz Strait: Iran's blockade of the Hormuz Strait affects international oil prices, which could severely impact the global economy.
  • Threat of Highly Enriched Uranium: Iran's possession of highly enriched uranium increases the possibility of nuclear weapon development, conflicting with US military objectives.
  • US Military Achievements and Limitations: The US and Israel have inflicted significant damage on Iran's military capabilities, but the collapse of the Iranian regime has not occurred.
  • Unpredictability of the War: The Iranian regime is likely to engage in unpredictable actions, complicating the dynamics of the conflict further.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This war was triggered by Iran's military provocations and US military interventions. The Iranian regime has been strengthening its military capabilities in response to US economic sanctions and military pressure, deepening the conflict with the US. The Hormuz Strait is a crucial route for global oil transportation, and Iran's blockade strategy significantly impacts the international economy. Iran's will to resist and the blockade of the Hormuz Strait have direct implications for the global oil market, potentially causing serious repercussions for the global economy. Additionally, the possibility of Iran developing nuclear weapons could destabilize security in the Middle East. The fact that US military intervention has not been successful may encourage ongoing resistance from the Iranian regime, negatively affecting diplomatic efforts by the international community.
