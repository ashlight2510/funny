Fire at Yongin Cheoin-gu Logistics Warehouse, Level 1 Response Issued

At 5:36 PM on the 29th, a fire broke out at a logistics warehouse in Cheoin-gu, Yongin, Gyeonggi Province. The warehouse is a single-story building made of reinforced concrete, with a total floor area of over 5,300 square meters. Immediately after the fire broke out, the fire authorities issued a Level 1 response and began firefighting operations with over 100 firefighters and more than 30 pieces of equipment. As of now, the specific extent of the damage and the cause of the fire have not been confirmed, and the police and fire authorities plan to conduct an investigation after the fire is extinguished.

This fire incident is not just a simple accident; it could significantly impact various stakeholders. The logistics warehouse plays a crucial role in the logistics industry, and if the fire occurred in a large-scale logistics center, it could lead to economic losses and disruptions in the logistics supply chain.
