Kakao's Jeong Shin-ah, Aspires to Achieve Practical Results with AI Following Re-election

Kakao recently announced the reappointment of CEO Jeong Shin-ah at its regular shareholders' meeting. CEO Jeong expressed her determination to achieve practical results through the restructuring of the business centered around artificial intelligence (AI), and she acknowledged the heavy responsibility she feels regarding shareholder dissatisfaction over the poor stock performance. Kakao surpassed 8 trillion won in annual consolidated revenue last year, achieving financial success, but its stock price has still fallen over 70% from its peak. In light of this situation, CEO Jeong pledged to do her utmost to meet shareholders' expectations.

This issue highlights the importance of Kakao's management strategy and the restoration of shareholder trust. CEO Jeong promised to prioritize shareholder value without selling treasury stocks while restructuring the business around AI and KakaoTalk. However, as shareholder dissatisfaction remains, the recovery of Kakao's stock price will be closely watched.

Key Summary

  • CEO Jeong Shin-ah emphasizes restructuring the business around AI following her reappointment.
  • Expresses heavy responsibility regarding shareholder dissatisfaction over poor stock performance.
  • Last year, Kakao surpassed 8 trillion won in annual consolidated revenue, but the stock price has fallen over 70% from its peak.
  • Promises to prioritize shareholder value without selling treasury stocks.
  • Plans to increase total dividends by 10% compared to the previous year and to buy back shares.

Key Highlights

  • Restructuring Around AI: CEO Jeong expressed her commitment to restructuring the business with a focus on KakaoTalk and AI.
  • Need to Restore Shareholder Trust: Dissatisfaction among shareholders is growing due to poor stock performance, and she feels responsible for this.
  • Financial Performance: Last year, Kakao recorded an annual consolidated revenue of 8 trillion won, achieving the highest operating profit in history.
  • Urgency of Stock Recovery: The stock price continues to decline, necessitating efforts to restore shareholder trust.
  • Dividend Expansion: Kakao announced plans to expand distributable profits and buy back shares.

Why Background Matters

CEO Jeong's reappointment is closely related to Kakao's management strategy. Kakao experienced rapid growth during the pandemic, but its stock price has recently seen a significant decline. Consequently, Kakao is making various efforts to restore shareholder value alongside business restructuring. In particular, advancements in AI technology will play a crucial role in enhancing Kakao's future growth potential.

Why It Matters

Kakao is one of the leaders in the South Korean IT industry, and its performance and stock fluctuations significantly impact many investors. CEO Jeong's reappointment and the AI-centered business strategy indicate Kakao's future direction, which could influence the entire IT industry beyond just the company's performance. Restoring shareholder trust will be a critical factor in enhancing Kakao's sustainability.

My Interpretation

The restructuring of the business around AI, emphasized by CEO Jeong, is expected to play a significant role in enhancing Kakao's future growth potential. However, as long as the stock price remains poor, shareholder dissatisfaction is unlikely to dissipate easily. In this regard, Kakao needs to strengthen communication with shareholders. Additionally, practical measures for stock recovery are necessary, and a long-term vision should be presented rather than focusing solely on short-term results.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While CEO Jeong promised to prioritize shareholder value without selling treasury stocks, this may only focus on short-term stock recovery. A concrete plan is needed to achieve practical results alongside a long-term growth strategy. Furthermore, questions remain about how effective Kakao's AI business will be in delivering actual results.

Points to Check Now

  • It is essential to pay attention to what actual results Kakao's AI business restructuring yields.
  • We should observe whether communication with shareholders improves and if specific measures for stock recovery are implemented.
  • It is necessary to verify how positively Kakao's dividend expansion and share buyback plans impact shareholders.
