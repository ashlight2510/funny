Board of Audit and Inspection conducts regular personnel appointments at the director level after two years... Exclusion of 'Yoo Byung-ho's close aides'

Recently, the Board of Audit and Inspection conducted regular personnel appointments at the director level for the first time in two years, further highlighting the conflict between the Yoon Suk-yeol administration and the Lee Jae-myung administration. In this appointment, Kim Suk-dong, the audit management officer known to be a close aide of Commissioner Yoo Byung-ho, was excluded, and individuals who criticized the reform of the Board under the Lee Jae-myung administration were assigned to key positions. As a result, concerns about internal politics within the Board and the future direction of audit operations are growing.

This appointment was conducted with the aim of organizational renewal and citizen-centered audit activities, but it is noteworthy that political interests are intertwined in the background. While the Board appears to be addressing past issues of inadequate audits, controversies surrounding retaliatory actions during the appointment process still remain.
