Gwanak Youth Cultural Space ‘Sillim-dong Three Room’ Receives Highest Grade

The youth cultural space ‘Sillim-dong Three Room’ in Gwanak-gu, Seoul has received the highest grade, S grade, in the Seoul Youth Center performance evaluation. This marks the third achievement following 2022 and 2023, and Sillim-dong Three Room provides various cultural spaces and programs for youth, particularly helping those experiencing emotional and social isolation. This space has the largest number of youth members in Seoul (over 52,000) and operates various programs that encourage active participation from young people.

This achievement further highlights the importance of youth policies in Gwanak-gu, which has a high youth population ratio. Sillim-dong Three Room has established itself as a space where youth can stay and grow, playing a crucial role in realizing Gwanak-gu's vision as a youth-friendly city.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Sillim-dong Three Room opened in 2019 as Seoul's first youth center, designed to provide various cultural spaces considering the challenges youth face living in one-room apartments or study rooms. In recent years, the social isolation of youth has intensified, highlighting the need for such spaces. Gwanak-gu has a vision of becoming a youth-friendly city and has been implementing various policies centered around Sillim-dong Three Room to realize this vision.

Why Is It Important?

The achievement of the S grade is not just a result of evaluation but serves as a reaffirmation of the importance of spaces and programs that youth need. As youth cultural spaces become more active, the quality of life for young people improves, and opportunities to overcome social isolation are provided. Furthermore, this achievement can positively influence other districts to strengthen their youth policies.
