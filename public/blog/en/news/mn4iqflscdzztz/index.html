Trump Announces 15-Point Agreement with Iran… Raises Possibility of Joint Management of Hormuz

U.S. President Donald Trump has stated that he has reached an agreement on 15 points in negotiations with Iran, claiming that the U.S. will take possession of Iran's highly enriched uranium. However, Iran has denied the existence of such negotiations, creating a situation where the claims of both sides are conflicting. President Trump also mentioned the possibility of joint management of the Strait of Hormuz, expressing a willingness to improve relations with Iran. However, the Iranian response has been negative, and uncertainty regarding the progress of negotiations remains.

This news could significantly impact the geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, particularly concerning Iran's nuclear development issues and U.S. diplomatic strategies. There is growing interest in how the conflict with Iran will unfold and whether joint management of the Strait of Hormuz will actually take place.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This negotiation with Iran can be seen as part of the Trump administration's shift in foreign policy. After withdrawing from the Iran nuclear agreement (JCPOA), the U.S. has intensified sanctions against Iran. However, recent signs of de-escalation could be interpreted as efforts to enhance stability in the Middle East. The Strait of Hormuz is a major route for global oil transport, and stability in this region directly affects the global economy.

Why It Matters

This negotiation represents a significant opportunity to redefine U.S.-Iran relations. Iran's nuclear development issue raises concerns in the international community, increasing the need for dialogue to resolve it. Additionally, the proposal for joint management of the Strait of Hormuz could positively impact regional security and the economy.
