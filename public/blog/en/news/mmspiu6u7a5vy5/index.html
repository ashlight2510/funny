Integration of Citizens and NGOs in Gwangju, Jeonnam: Key Issues for the First Teacher Labor Union

March 16, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Citizens and Social Organizations Begin Integration into ‘Jeonnam Gwangju’... First Name Change of Teacher Labor Union

As the administrative integration of Gwangju and Jeonnam is scheduled for July 1, local labor unions and citizens' social organizations are taking their first steps toward integration. The Gwangju Teacher Labor Union recently changed its name to ‘Jeonnam Gwangju Integrated Special City Teacher Labor Union’ through a delegate meeting, opening the door for teachers in the Jeonnam region to join. This change is aimed at the integration of the education offices in Gwangju and Jeonnam, and citizen and social organizations are also discussing integration operational plans.

This movement towards integration goes beyond a mere name change, implying the potential for greater change through cooperation and solidarity within the community. In particular, the process of labor unions and civic organizations joining forces is expected to have a direct impact on local residents.

Key Points

  • Gwangju Teacher Labor Union changes its name to ‘Jeonnam Gwangju Integrated Special City Teacher Labor Union’.
  • Teachers in the Jeonnam region can also participate as union members.
  • Negotiation targets will be unified due to the integration of education offices.
  • Citizen and social organizations are discussing integration operational plans.
  • Joint activities for the launch of the integrated special city have begun.

Background and Context

The administrative integration of Gwangju and Jeonnam is considered an important milestone for regional development and decentralization. Over the past few years, cooperation between the two regions has gradually strengthened, leading citizens and labor unions to recognize the necessity of integration. In particular, integration in the education sector has a direct impact on teachers' working conditions and educational policies, making the integration of teacher labor unions symbolically significant.

Why Is This Issue Important?

This integration is not merely an administrative change but could serve as a crucial opportunity to reshape the political and social structures of the local community. The process of citizens and labor unions joining forces will provide a foundation for better education and social services for local residents. Ultimately, this will lead to an improvement in the quality of life for residents.
