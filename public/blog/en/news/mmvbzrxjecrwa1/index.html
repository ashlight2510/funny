President Yoon's Constitutional Amendment Request: Key Issues Examined by Justice Minister Jeong Seong-ho

March 18, 2026 · 한겨레 · View source

Original Article Overview Title: President Yoon's 'Constitutional Amendment Request' and Minister Jeong Seong-ho's "The Ministry of Justice Will Also Begin Legal Review" Source: Hankyoreh Minister of Justice Jeong Seong-ho announced that the Ministry of Justice will begin a legal review of the constitutional amendment tasks related to President Yoon Suk-yeol's directive to consider a phased constitutional amendment. On the 17th, Minister Jeong stated through Facebook, "President Yoon mentioned the government's review of constitutional amendments." He emphasized that if the 1987 constitutional amendment laid the foundation for democracy, a new amendment is needed to reflect the values of the current era of AI technological revolution. He expressed his intention to incorporate issues that resonate with the public, such as including the spirit of May 18 in the constitutional preamble, tightening the requirements for martial law, and expanding local autonomy.

This discussion on constitutional amendments raises fundamental questions about how to implement democracy and national sovereignty in our society, going beyond mere legal procedures.

Overview of President Yoon's Constitutional Amendment Request and the Ministry of Justice's Legal Review

Recently, President Yoon Suk-yeol expressed his intention to consider a phased constitutional amendment, prompting Minister Jeong Seong-ho to announce the start of legal review work on this matter. Minister Jeong emphasized that while the 1987 constitutional amendment laid the foundation for democracy, a new amendment is necessary to encapsulate the values of the AI technological revolution era. He stated his commitment to reflecting issues that resonate with the public, such as including the spirit of May 18 in the constitutional preamble, tightening martial law requirements, and expanding local autonomy.

These discussions on constitutional amendments pose fundamental questions about how to realize democracy and national sovereignty in our society.

Key Summary

  • President Yoon Suk-yeol mentioned the need for a phased constitutional amendment, leading to Minister Jeong Seong-ho's announcement of the start of legal review.
  • Minister Jeong emphasized the intention to reflect public consensus issues in the constitution.
  • The amendment is highlighted as an essential task for the development of democracy and the acceptance of values in the AI era.

Key Points

  • Need for Amendment: The necessity of constitutional revision in line with changes since the 1987 amendment.
  • Role of the Ministry of Justice: The Ministry of Justice will support the constitutional amendment tasks through legal review.
  • Public Consensus: Reflection of issues that resonate with the public, such as the spirit of May 18 and local autonomy.
  • Values of the AI Era: The argument for constitutional revision to align with technological advancements.
  • Phased Approach: The sequential constitutional amendment proposal suggested by President Yoon.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This discussion on constitutional amendments began with President Yoon's mention in a Cabinet meeting. He proposed, "Let’s amend the constitution step by step, starting with easy issues that can be agreed upon by the public," emphasizing a systematic approach at the government level. Minister Jeong's remarks can be seen as a practical response to the President's intention, indicating the Ministry of Justice's willingness to actively participate in the constitutional amendment discussions.

Why It Matters

This constitutional amendment discussion could serve as an important opportunity for deepening democracy and realizing national sovereignty, going beyond mere legal procedures. Particularly, contemplating how the constitution should evolve in response to rapid technological advancements like AI is a crucial factor in determining the future of our society. Moreover, if the amendment is realized, it could mark a significant turning point in achieving political stability and social consensus.

Personal Observations

In my view, the most important aspect of this constitutional amendment discussion is how well it can reflect the voices of the people. The "public consensus" emphasized by Minister Jeong should not merely be the result of opinion polls but should be formed through various social agreements and discussions. If this process is overlooked, the amendment could become a seed for further political conflict.

Hidden Variables to Consider

In the constitutional amendment process, communication with the public is essential, not just legal reviews. If there are no platforms or forums for direct public participation while the Ministry of Justice conducts its legal review, the purpose of the amendment could be undermined. Additionally, even if an amendment occurs, it is necessary to consider how its content will actually bring about changes in the lives of the people.

Points to Check Now

  • What specific direction will the Ministry of Justice's legal review take?
  • If an amendment occurs, what impact will its content have on the lives of the people?

FAQ

Q: What changes will occur if a constitutional amendment is made?
A: If a constitutional amendment is made, the contents specified in the constitution will be changed, directly impacting political and social structures. For example, if local autonomy is expanded, the authority of regional governance may be strengthened.

Q: How will public opinions be reflected in the amendment process?
A: Public opinions can be gathered through public hearings, opinion polls, and online platforms during the amendment process. However, the transparency and fairness of these processes are crucial.

Q: What does the Ministry of Justice's legal review include?
A: The Ministry of Justice's legal review involves analyzing the legal basis, procedures, and whether the contents of the proposed amendments comply with the constitution and laws.

Now, we must closely observe how the constitutional amendment discussions will unfold and how well the voices of the people will be reflected in that process.
