Decades-old Cedar Forest Cut Down… Environmental Groups Oppose Wansan Chilbong Park Development Project

The Wansan Chilbong Hanbitmaru Park development project being pursued by Jeonju City in Jeonbuk is facing strong opposition from environmental groups. The Jeonbuk Environmental Movement Union claims that this project is destroying the urban ecological axis, with decades-old cedar and oak trees being felled and serious damage occurring. They warn that the forest, which serves as a resting space for citizens and an ecological asset, is being ruined beyond recovery, and they are demanding a halt to the construction and a complete reassessment of the project.

This issue goes beyond a simple development controversy; it poses serious questions about the urban ecosystem and the quality of life for citizens. Jeonju City plans to invest 20.6 billion won in the park, but the controversy is escalating due to the environmental destruction occurring in the process.

Key Summary

  • The Jeonbuk Environmental Movement Union claims that the Wansan Chilbong Hanbitmaru Park development project is destroying the urban ecological axis.
  • Decades-old cedar and oak trees are being felled, and environmental damage is ongoing, including the cutting of mountain slopes.
  • Jeonju City plans to push forward with this project starting in 2024, emphasizing its connection to tourism, but environmental groups point out that this violates ecological conservation principles.
  • The Environmental Movement Union demands a reassessment of road expansions and the installation of artificial structures.

Key Scenes

  • Opposition from environmental groups: Concerns over the destruction of the urban ecological axis and the degradation of citizens' living spaces.
  • Ecological inconsistency of the project: Conflicts with urban planning.
  • Installation of artificial structures: Concerns over excessive development of the natural environment.
  • Controversy over the necessity of road expansion: Overlap with existing parking facilities.
  • Lack of citizen feedback: Development that does not consider environmental conservation and citizen opinions.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Jeonju City has designated Wansan Chilbong as a key urban ecological axis according to the 2035 Urban Master Plan and the 2030 Park and Green Space Master Plan. However, the park development project is proceeding in a direction contrary to these plans, which environmental groups see as a serious contradiction. In particular, civil engineering projects like road expansions are emerging as major issues for violating ecological conservation principles.

Why It Matters

This issue transcends mere environmental conservation; it directly affects the quality of life for citizens. When urban development proceeds while ignoring citizen opinions and destroying the environment, the natural and ecological assets that citizens should enjoy are ultimately harmed. Furthermore, such issues can raise awareness of similar development projects in other regions.

My Thoughts

What is noteworthy in this matter is that the voices of environmental groups are not merely opposing opinions but reflect a positive intention to protect the future assets of citizens. Additionally, Jeonju City needs to actively gather citizen opinions and push the project in a direction that respects ecological values.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The article claims that Jeonju City is conducting road-related projects separately, but it is essential to recognize that these two projects are interconnected. The impact of road expansion on park development is significant, making it unrealistic to consider them separately.

Current Points to Check

  • Changes in Jeonju City's official stance: The city's response to the opposition from environmental groups and future plans.
  • Citizen feedback procedures: How the process of gathering citizen opinions for the park development project will be conducted.
  • Examples of similar development projects: How similar cases have been handled in other regions.

FAQ

Q: What specific demands are being made by environmental groups?
A: They are demanding a complete reassessment of the Wansan Chilbong road expansion project, reevaluation of artificial structures, formation of an urban park committee, and a shift in ecological conservation policies.

Q: Why is Jeonju City pursuing this project?
A: Jeonju City aims to create a resting place for citizens and a hub for healing tourism through this project.

Q: What changes can be expected once this project is completed?
A: Facilities such as observation decks, artificial waterfalls, and gardens are planned to provide new resting spaces for citizens.

Q: What practical impacts might concerns about environmental destruction have?
A: Forest destruction can disrupt the balance of ecosystems and limit wildlife habitats and citizens' opportunities for natural experiences.

In this situation, readers should focus on the fact that the park development project being pursued by Jeonju City must move in a direction that considers both citizens and the environment, rather than being a simple development initiative.
