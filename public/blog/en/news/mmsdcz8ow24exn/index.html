Smart Strategies to Save on Air Conditioning Bills in Summer

As summer approaches, air conditioning becomes essential. However, seeing the electricity bill rise along with the cool breeze can be a headache. So, I’ve compiled a few smart strategies to save on air conditioning costs during the summer. Thanks to these methods, I’ve been able to alleviate some of the burden.

1. Set the Right Temperature

The most important thing when using an air conditioner is the temperature setting. If you set it too low, the electricity bill skyrockets. Personally, I found it best to set it between 26 to 28 degrees Celsius. By doing this, I was able to stay cool while keeping the electricity bill lower. Initially, I wanted to set it to 24 degrees, but after realizing how much that impacts the bill, I reconsidered.

My Experience

Setting it to 26 degrees and using a fan along with it made it feel cooler. The air circulation made the indoor temperature feel more uniform. Trying this method reduced my electricity bill and allowed me to stay more comfortably.

2. Regularly Clean the Filters

Cleaning the air conditioner filters is something that can easily be overlooked. If the filters are dirty, the airflow gets worse, causing the air conditioner to work harder. That’s why I try to remember to clean the filters every month. After cleaning the filters, I noticed that the cool air came out much better.

How to Clean the Filters

  1. Turn off the air conditioner.
  2. Carefully remove the filters.
  3. Rinse lightly with water and dry.
  4. Reattach the filters.

With such a simple task, the efficiency of the air conditioner increases significantly, which naturally reduces the electricity bill, and I really appreciated that.

3. Adjust Air Conditioning Usage Times

In summer, the days are long, and we tend to use the air conditioner a lot during the day, which greatly affects the electricity bill. Therefore, I use the strategy of closing the curtains between 2 PM and 4 PM when the sunlight is the strongest to maintain the indoor temperature instead of using the air conditioner.

Effective Time Management

By doing this, I found that the frequency of using the air conditioner in the afternoon decreased, which allowed me to save on electricity costs. Plus, I can turn the air conditioner back on in the evening to stay cool, making it more convenient and pleasant.

4. Utilize Smart Devices

Using smart devices is also a good method. Nowadays, there are many smart plugs that can connect to air conditioners, right? With these, you can remotely control the air conditioner, allowing you to turn it off or on before you leave.

My Smart Tip

After trying this, I found that if I turn on the air conditioner in advance according to my outing schedule, I can come home to a cool house. Additionally, since I can automatically turn off the air conditioner, unnecessary electricity waste is reduced, and I feel a sense of accomplishment.

I’ve summarized smart strategies to save on air conditioning bills during the summer. By setting the right temperature, regularly cleaning the filters, adjusting usage times, and utilizing smart devices, I think we can spend the summer a bit more wisely. If you have any questions, please leave them in the comments. I recommend starting with these tips!
