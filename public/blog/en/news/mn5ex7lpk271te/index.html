Daegu is in trouble... Kim Boo-kyum outperformed all candidates from the People Power Party in the head-to-head match

Recent polling conducted in Daegu shows that Kim Boo-kyum, former Prime Minister from the Democratic Party, has gained an advantage in hypothetical matchups against candidates from the People Power Party for the mayoral race. This survey was commissioned by Yeongnam Ilbo and conducted by Realmeter, revealing that Kim Boo-kyum led by double digits against all eight candidates, including those who were cut off from the nomination process. This result could significantly impact the political landscape in Daegu.

This outcome carries more significance than just a simple poll. While Daegu has traditionally been known as a stronghold for conservative parties, the fact that Kim Boo-kyum has shown superiority against the People Power Party candidates in this survey suggests a potential shift in the upcoming mayoral election.

  • Kim Boo-kyum has shown superiority in hypothetical matchups against the Daegu mayoral candidates.
  • According to the survey, Kim Boo-kyum led by double digits against all eight candidates, including the cut-off Lee Jin-sook and lawmaker Joo Ho-young.
  • This survey is interpreted as having significant implications for the political landscape in Daegu.

Key Highlights

  • Kim Boo-kyum received over 47% support in Daegu.

  • The rise in support for the Democratic Party in Daegu presents new possibilities for future elections.
  • The low support for the People Power Party candidates hints at a crisis for the conservative party.

Why Background Matters

This poll was conducted amid a rising trend of support for the Democratic Party in Daegu. Traditionally known as a stronghold for the conservative People Power Party, recent political changes and social issues seem to be favoring the Democratic Party. Notably, Kim Boo-kyum's local roots likely contribute positively to his appeal among residents.

Why It Matters

The results of this survey are interpreted as a significant signal that could change the political landscape for the Daegu mayoral election. If the Democratic Party continues to gain support in Daegu, it could herald major changes in future local and national elections. Particularly, an increase in support for the Democratic Party in a conservative stronghold like Daegu could influence the national political landscape as well.
