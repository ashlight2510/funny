US Pressures China on Potential Postponement of Visit, China Says "Countries Must Halt Military Actions"

As tensions rise between the US and China, President Donald Trump has linked China's dispatch of warships to the potential postponement of the US-China summit, putting the bilateral relationship back on the test. China has urged a halt to military actions while avoiding a direct response to US military pressure. This situation warrants attention as it goes beyond mere diplomatic conflict and has significant implications for regional and global economies.

The Chinese Foreign Ministry warned that military tensions in the Hormuz Strait undermine global stability and called for a halt to military actions by all countries. Such statements are interpreted as drawing a line against the US's troop deployment request. Furthermore, the trade conflict between the US and China is entering a new phase, suggesting that the impact of this issue on the international community will grow.

Key Summary

  • President Trump links China's dispatch of warships to the potential postponement of the summit.
  • China calls for a halt to military actions while avoiding a direct response.
  • The US-China trade conflict emerges as a new issue.
  • The summit could serve as an opportunity to emphasize the importance of the Hormuz Strait.
  • Experts predict that compromise or tangible results are unlikely.

Key Scenes

  • US Military Pressure: President Trump hints at the possibility of postponing the summit while demanding China dispatch warships.
  • China's Response: China urges a halt to military actions and does not express immediate intentions to dispatch warships.
  • Deepening Trade Conflict: The initiation of new tariff investigations by the US highlights trade issues.
  • Importance of the Summit: This meeting could provide an opportunity to discuss the direction of the US-Iran conflict and the reopening of the Hormuz Strait.
  • Expert Predictions: Despite a positive atmosphere surrounding high-level talks, analysts believe that substantial outcomes are unlikely.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This issue arises amid escalating trade conflicts between the US and China. Particularly following the US Supreme Court's ruling on "reciprocal tariffs" and the illegality of fentanyl tariffs, the US is investigating China's manufacturing sector's overproduction and forced labor issues through Section 301 of trade law. Against this backdrop, President Trump is complicating relations by requesting China to dispatch warships.

Why It Matters

This situation could have serious implications for global economics and geopolitical stability, going beyond mere diplomatic tensions. China is the largest importer of Iranian oil, and if the US-Iran conflict prolongs, the impact on the Chinese economy will be significant. Therefore, this US-China summit is seen as an important opportunity for regional and global stability, not just a platform for dialogue.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the most crucial aspect of this situation is that dialogue between the two countries must continue. While it is uncertain how US military pressure will provoke a reaction from China, as long as a platform for dialogue exists, there is a possibility to avoid escalating conflicts. Particularly, the issue of Iran, deeply intertwined with China's economic interests, will act as an important variable in negotiations between the two countries.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Many may expect immediate results from the upcoming US-China summit, but in reality, the deep-rooted conflicts between the two nations are not issues that can be resolved quickly. Additionally, as experts have noted, despite the positive atmosphere surrounding high-level talks, it is essential to remember that substantial compromises or outcomes are unlikely.

Points to Check Now

  • Progress of the US-China Summit: Attention should be paid to how the relationship between the two countries changes based on future meeting schedules and outcomes.
  • Progress of Trade Conflicts: It is important to observe how the US's new tariff investigations proceed and what China's responses will be.
