Young artists who want to be 'scary kids'... A spring exhibition march with new senses and new forms

Recently, new works by young artists in their 20s to 40s are gaining attention in various exhibition spaces in Seoul. They aim to communicate with the audience through new forms of expression that break away from existing artistic formats and senses. In particular, exhibitions held in diverse locations such as Bukchon, Insadong, and Sewoon Shopping Center are filled with works that capture the intimate emotions and memories of young artists. These exhibitions not only reveal the individuality and creativity of the artists but also provide the audience with a new visual experience.

These exhibitions serve as a platform to explore the complex emotions and memories of modern society, going beyond simple artistic attempts. Artists address personal and social themes, seeking to evoke deep empathy from the audience. The new senses and forms presented by young artists are bringing a fresh breeze to the art world.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Recently, there has been an increase in artists who are breaking away from traditional formats to try new approaches in the art world. This trend is also influencing how audiences appreciate art, making it a medium for communication rather than just a subject for consumption.
