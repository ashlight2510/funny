Content Creation Using ChatGPT: Prompt Examples and Tips for Utilization

Have you ever felt lost about what to write when you need to create content? I've had that feeling many times. That's why these days, I often use ChatGPT to generate content, and it's really useful. So, I've organized my experiences. Let's explore how to make content creation with ChatGPT more effective!

Creating Suitable Prompts for ChatGPT

What is a Prompt?

A prompt is simply a question or request you give to ChatGPT. The outcome can vary greatly depending on what you ask. When I first started creating prompts, I only threw out 'simple questions,' but I didn't get very good results. However, when I began to ask more specific and clear questions, I started to receive better answers.

Examples of Good Prompts

For instance, if you ask, "Recommend travel destinations for summer," you'll get a very basic response. But if you ask, "Recommend family-friendly travel destinations for summer, along with activities that kids would enjoy," you'll receive much more useful information. This is really important.

From my experience, the more you specify the prompt, the better information you get. So, I felt it was good to clearly think about the desired outcome at the beginning.

Various Ways to Utilize ChatGPT

Generating Blog Post Ideas

Since I run a blog, I often run out of writing ideas. In those moments, I asked ChatGPT, "Suggest 5 blog post ideas about popular trends," and a flood of unique ideas came pouring in. Using it this way has significantly reduced the pressure of writing.

What was important here was that while I had many ideas, I was struggling with how to develop them, but thanks to ChatGPT, I was able to find new directions.

Content Summarization and Editing

Additionally, it's really useful for summarizing or editing long texts. For example, when I asked it to "summarize this content in 3 sentences," it provided a concise and well-organized summary of the key points. This helped make it easier for readers to digest the information.

In my experience, the longer the content, the more the reader's attention wanes. Therefore, the summarization feature seems essential.

Tips for Using ChatGPT

Continuous Feedback

When using ChatGPT, it's important to provide feedback. By asking follow-up questions like, "I wish this answer were a bit more specific," or requesting, "Please write more deeply in this direction," you can achieve better results. At first, it might be unclear, but after trying several times, I noticed improvements.

Personally, I find this feedback process really enjoyable, and it feels like my content is evolving.

Conclusion

Utilizing ChatGPT for content creation is truly useful and fun. There are various ways to use it, so I recommend trying it whenever you feel lost, just like I do. If you have any questions, leave them in the comments! And let's discover our own good prompts and create our unique content!
