BTS Again… Ranked No. 1 for 8 Consecutive Years as the Most Preferred Korean Artist by Foreigners

Recently, it was reported that BTS has secured the top spot for 8 consecutive years in the category of the most preferred Korean artist by foreigners. This survey was part of the '2026 Overseas Korean Wave Survey' conducted by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Korea International Cultural Exchange Agency, targeting 27,400 people across 30 countries. The results indicate that BTS has solidified its status as a representative K-pop artist of Korea and that negative perceptions of Korea are shifting towards a positive view of K-culture.

These results hold significance beyond mere rankings. BTS's popularity is generating positive responses towards Korean cultural content and contributing to the enhancement of Korea's cultural status. Notably, BLACKPINK's ranking at No. 2 also suggests the ongoing growth potential of the Korean Wave.

Key Summary

  • BTS ranks No. 1 in the foreign preference survey for 8 consecutive years.
  • Negative images of Korea are decreasing, while positive perceptions of K-culture are increasing.
  • BLACKPINK ranks No. 2 for 7 consecutive years, reaffirming the global popularity of K-pop.
  • Favorability towards the Korean Wave is rising in various fields such as Korean food, music, and dramas.
  • The main reasons for negative perceptions are excessive commercialization and issues related to North Korea.

Key Highlights

  • BTS is the most preferred Korean artist for 8 consecutive years among foreigners.

  • Favorability towards Korean Wave content is rising in both Southeast Asia and Western countries.
  • The causes of negative perceptions are divided into commercialism and political factors.

Why Background Matters

This survey was conducted to understand the overseas responses to Korean cultural content. The consecutive top rankings of BTS and BLACKPINK serve as indicators of the globalization of K-pop and the spread of the Korean Wave, showing that past negative images are gradually fading. Notably, historical events like the Korean War are no longer prominent, indicating that Korean culture is moving away from political factors.

Why It Matters

The results of this survey shed light on the overall status of Korean culture beyond just BTS's popularity. The positive global reception of Korean cultural content can have a beneficial impact on Korea's economy and cultural industry. In particular, the rising favorability in various fields such as K-pop, dramas, and food will contribute to strengthening the global competitiveness of Korean brands.

BTS's Influence

BTS is not just a music group; they play a crucial role in promoting Korean culture worldwide. Their success demonstrates the potential for Korean cultural content to be embraced globally. Their music and messages resonate with many, contributing to a positive image of Korea.

Hidden Variables to Consider

It is important not to overlook that negative perceptions of Korea still exist according to the survey results. Concerns about excessive commercialization and issues related to North Korea continue to influence many people's views. These factors could pose obstacles to the growth of K-culture, highlighting the need for ongoing efforts to improve the image.

Points to Check Now

  • Changes in global recognition of other K-pop artists besides BTS and BLACKPINK.
  • Trends in favorability changes across various fields of K-culture.
  • Consumer responses to the commercial approach of Korean cultural content.
