Daejeon Factory Fire, One Missing Person Found Dead

On the 20th, a fire at an auto parts manufacturing plant in Daedeok-gu, Daejeon, left 14 people unaccounted for, and one of them has been found dead. Nine hours after the fire broke out, rescue teams were deployed to begin search operations; however, the search is complicated by concerns over the building's structural integrity. So far, 55 people have been injured, and the search for the remaining 13 missing persons is ongoing.

This incident goes beyond a simple fire accident, highlighting the need for corporate safety management alongside human casualties. Questions have arisen regarding the operating conditions of the factory where the fire occurred.
