“What about investor losses?”… Exchange’s mistake in reversing management designation causes ‘stock price volatility’

Recently, the Korea Exchange caused confusion as it reversed the management designation of SCM Life Science just one day after its announcement, leading to significant fluctuations in stock prices. The exchange mistakenly judged that the company met the criteria for removal during the analysis of its audit report, resulting in a 28.05% increase in stock prices immediately after the market opened. However, once the news of the re-designation as a management stock was announced, the prices fell again. This incident has raised concerns about potential investor losses due to the exchange's judgment error, and the exchange has promised to enhance its disclosure system using AI technology along with an internal audit.

This incident is not just a simple mistake; it could seriously impact investor trust and market stability. In particular, such mismanagement in a highly volatile stock market can lead to real losses for investors. Consequently, there are increasing calls for a review of the Korea Exchange's internal systems and disclosure procedures.

Key Points

  • Reversal of management designation: The Korea Exchange re-designated SCM Life Science as a management stock just one day after its removal.
  • Stock price volatility: After the removal announcement, the stock price surged by 28.05%, but fell by 5.73% after re-designation.
  • Exchange's responsibility: Plans announced for internal audits and enhancements to the disclosure system using AI technology.
  • Investor trust issues: Concerns about declining investor trust due to repeated mistakes.
  • Need for legal and institutional responses: A review of the criteria and procedures for management designation is necessary.

Why Background Matters

This incident stemmed from an error in analyzing SCM Life Science's audit report, where the exchange incorrectly assessed the ongoing business losses before tax. According to the criteria for management designation, companies that incur ongoing business losses should be designated as management stocks, and the failure to apply this criterion correctly is the crux of the issue. Such mistakes can act as threats to the credibility of the stock market, going beyond mere management issues.

Why It’s Important

This incident has revealed not only the volatility of stock prices but also the vulnerabilities in the credibility of the Korea Exchange and its disclosure system. Investors may suffer direct losses due to such errors, which will significantly impact their future investment decisions. This is particularly critical in high-risk sectors like the biotech industry, where such mistakes can be even more detrimental.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Additionally, it should not be overlooked that this issue is not limited to SCM Life Science but could occur in other listed companies as well.

In particular, information about how the internal audit results and AI disclosure system enhancements will be implemented could significantly influence future investment strategies.
