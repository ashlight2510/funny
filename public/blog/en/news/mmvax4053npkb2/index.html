SK Ecoplante Implements Artificial Intelligence Deep Tech Open Innovation Program

Recently, SK Ecoplante has decided to implement an open innovation program in the field of artificial intelligence (AI) deep tech. This program is based on a collaboration system between industry, academia, and research, aiming for innovation and commercialization of AI technologies. Through a memorandum of understanding signed at Hoseo University in Asan, Chungnam, SK Ecoplante plans to strengthen its competitiveness in the AI field by collaborating with various institutions such as KAIST and Chungcheongnam-do.

This program is an open innovation model specialized in AI deep tech technology, focusing on four main activities: development of innovative technologies, participation in government research and development, commercialization of technologies, and cultivation of specialized personnel. SK Ecoplante aims to leap forward as a provider of AI infrastructure solutions through this initiative.

Key Highlights

  • SK Ecoplante is implementing the AI Deep Tech Open Innovation Program.
  • Collaboration with Hoseo University, KAIST, and others to establish a collaboration system between industry, academia, and research.
  • Promotion of four main activities: development of innovative technologies, participation in government research and development, commercialization of technologies, and cultivation of specialized personnel.
  • Strategic investment and strengthening of research and development for a leap as a provider of AI infrastructure solutions.
  • Active participation in discovering innovative technologies through technology competitions for startups.

Why Background Matters

As the importance of AI technology has been highlighted recently, companies are strengthening their investments in the AI field to enhance competitiveness. SK Ecoplante is establishing a collaboration system between industry, academia, and research to secure competitiveness in the AI deep tech field and is embarking on innovative technology development. This will serve as a stepping stone to solidify its position as an AI solution provider, going beyond mere technology development.

Why It Matters

This program is an important milestone that demonstrates SK Ecoplante's commitment to strengthening its competitiveness in the AI field. The process of developing and commercializing innovative technologies through a collaboration system between industry, academia, and research can ultimately have a positive impact on the economy as a whole. In particular, AI technology has high applicability across various industries, and the success of this program will influence the AI strategies of other companies in the future.

FAQ

Q1: What is the goal of this program?
A1: The program aims for innovation and commercialization of AI deep tech technologies, promoting the development of innovative technologies and cultivation of specialized personnel through a collaboration system between industry, academia, and research.

Q2: Which institutions are participating?
A2: A total of 12 institutions and companies are participating, including SK Ecoplante, Hoseo University, KAIST, Chungcheongnam-do, Seoul Creative Economy Innovation Center, Korea Electronics Technology Institute, and SK Securities.
