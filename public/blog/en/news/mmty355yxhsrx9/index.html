President Lee Proposes Inclusion of the Spirit of May 18 in the Constitution

On the 17th, President Lee Jae-myung proposed including the spirit of May 18 in the preamble of the constitution during a Cabinet meeting. He emphasized that strengthening local autonomy and martial law requirements would also gain public agreement, and the government should work towards progress on these issues. This statement is related to National Assembly Speaker Woo Won-sik's proposal to conduct a constitutional referendum simultaneously with the June 3 local elections, and a request for the formation of a special committee for constitutional amendment in the National Assembly followed.

President Lee's remarks reflect not just political rhetoric but also the significance of the spirit of May 18 in Korean society and the demand to incorporate it into the constitution. This could be an important first step in garnering political consensus and public agreement.

Key Highlights

  • President Lee Jae-myung proposes inclusion of the spirit of May 18 in the preamble of the constitution.
  • Emphasis on public agreement regarding strengthening local autonomy and martial law requirements.
  • National Assembly Speaker Woo Won-sik proposes simultaneous conduct of the June 3 local elections and constitutional referendum.
  • Request for the formation of a special committee for constitutional amendment in the National Assembly.
  • Highlighting the necessity of political consensus and public agreement.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This statement comes at a time when the historical significance of the May 18 Democratic Movement is being re-evaluated in Korean society. The call to include the spirit of May 18 in the constitution has been continuously raised by both ruling and opposition parties, and President Lee has indicated that the government will officially review this matter. Additionally, strengthening local autonomy and martial law requirements have emerged as significant topics in recent political discussions. These issues will play a crucial role in reflecting changes in the government and political circles as well as the demands of the public.

Why It Matters

This issue is not just about constitutional amendment; it can play a crucial role in redefining perceptions of democracy and human rights in Korean society. Including the spirit of May 18 in the constitution will contribute to establishing the values of democracy and historical truth. This is also an important process of leaving historical lessons for future generations, and if political consensus is reached, it can further enhance public trust.

Points to Note

One particularly noteworthy aspect of President Lee's remarks is his suggestion of "equity." He expressed the opinion that the process of including the spirit of May 18 in the constitution should also address the Busan Uprising. This indicates a balanced approach to historical events and holds the potential to alleviate political conflicts.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One point that may be overlooked in this statement is the political interests involved in the process of drafting the constitutional amendment. It should be kept in mind that if consensus is not reached between the ruling and opposition parties, it will be difficult for the amendment to pass. Furthermore, if the process of gathering public opinion is not sufficiently conducted, there may be significant backlash against the amendment.

Current Points to Check

  • Progress of discussions between ruling and opposition parties regarding the inclusion of the spirit of May 18 in the constitution.
  • Public opinion on strengthening local autonomy and martial law requirements.
  • Activities following the formation of the National Assembly's special committee for constitutional amendment.
