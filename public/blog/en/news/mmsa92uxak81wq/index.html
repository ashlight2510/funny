The Best Methods and Products for Removing Washing Machine Odors

The smell coming from the washing machine is really unpleasant, right? You might have experienced having to wash your clothes again due to the musty smell even after doing laundry. I've had that experience many times, so I thought about how to effectively remove odors from the washing machine. I've organized this information for you. Here are the methods and products to eliminate washing machine odors!

Causes of Washing Machine Odors

Mold and Bacteria Inside the Washing Machine

The main culprits of the odors from the washing machine are mold and bacteria. Especially if you close the door after washing, the moisture inside remains, leading to mold growth. Therefore, it's important to regularly check the inside of the washing machine. From my experience using a washing machine, I found that simply keeping the door open periodically significantly reduces the smell.

Residue from Detergent

Another cause is the residue left by the detergent. If too much detergent remains during washing, it can accumulate inside the washing machine and cause odors. I have experienced that reducing the amount of detergent I use made a significant difference. It’s best to use just the right amount when doing laundry.

Methods for Removing Washing Machine Odors

1. Using Vinegar and Baking Soda

One of the most effective methods for removing washing machine odors is using vinegar and baking soda. Pour 1 cup of vinegar and 1/2 cup of baking soda into the washing machine drum, then run a hot water cycle. You will notice a significant reduction in odors inside the washing machine. Personally, I really liked this method!

2. Using Washing Machine-Specific Detergent

Using a detergent specifically designed for washing machines is also a good way to eliminate odors. Compared to regular detergents, washing machine-specific products more effectively remove residues inside the machine. Among the products I have tried, the A brand washing machine detergent was particularly effective. The smell disappeared, and the washing machine felt cleaner.

3. Establishing a Regular Cleaning Habit

The best way to prevent washing machine odors is to develop a regular cleaning habit. It's good to clean the washing machine at least once a month. During this time, using the vinegar and baking soda mentioned above will be even more effective. Maintaining a clean state will reduce your worries about odors!

Conclusion

I realized that removing washing machine odors is simpler than I thought. With just vinegar, baking soda, washing machine-specific detergent, and a regular cleaning habit, you can eliminate the unpleasant smells from your washing machine. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I think you should start with this!
