Canadian billionaire buys 26.9% stake in The Economist

Recently, it was reported that Canadian real estate finance billionaire Stephen Smith has decided to acquire a 26.9% stake in The Economist Group, a leading British current affairs weekly. This transaction will be conducted through Smith and his family business acquiring shares from the Rothschild family, and the purchase amount has not been disclosed. The Economist, founded in 1843, has continuously maintained editorial independence, raising concerns about how this transaction might affect that tradition.

This deal is expected to bring significant changes to The Economist's ownership structure, marking the largest shift since Pearson sold its stake in 2015. Smith has stated that he will not influence The Economist's strategy and operational policies, but reactions from readers and the media industry are being closely watched.
