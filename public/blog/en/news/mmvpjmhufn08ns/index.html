Death on display by Hirst... Unveiling Asia's first large-scale solo exhibition

Damien Hirst's large-scale solo exhibition ‘There is no truth, but everything is possible’ opens at the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Seoul. This exhibition marks Hirst's first large-scale solo show in Asia, providing an opportunity to directly appreciate his representative works. The exhibition features over 50 pieces created over 35 years, arranged chronologically, and is expected to leave a strong impression on visitors through various installations and paintings that reveal Hirst's unique perspective on death and life.

This exhibition carries a significance beyond a mere art display. Hirst's works explore the essence of human existence centered around the theme of death, breaking down the boundaries of contemporary art. Through his works, visitors will gain a new perspective on death and experience how art can explore the boundaries between life and death.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Damien Hirst is one of the most controversial artists in contemporary art. His works often revolve around the themes of death and life, reflecting his long-standing interest in death since he was 16. Even before the exhibition opened, various opinions about Hirst's works emerged, showcasing how his commercial success intersects with artistic experimentation. Song Su-jeong, the exhibition curator at the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, emphasized the importance of experiencing Hirst's works firsthand.

Why It Matters

This exhibition provides an opportunity to deeply explore the theme of death through Hirst's works. In modern society, death is often a topic that is avoided, but Hirst's works confront it head-on. Hirst's Perspective

Hirst explores the boundaries between science and religion through art. His works go beyond merely providing visual shock, posing profound questions to the audience. For example, a piece that recreates a pharmacy illustrates how humans choose medications with belief to avoid death. This prompts deep reflection on life and death for the audience.

Hidden Variables to Consider

As the exhibition encompasses Hirst's early works to major pieces, visitors should pay attention to understanding his artistic evolution. Additionally, the commercial success of Hirst's works and the critical perspectives on them should also be considered. These diverse viewpoints will aid in a richer understanding of Hirst's art.
