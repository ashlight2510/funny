Seoul City to underground Tancheon Road... Traffic control on Bongeun Bridge and Tancheon Road from the 7th

Seoul City has decided to create waterfront spaces along the Tancheon and Han River through the undergrounding of Tancheon Road. This project is scheduled to be completed by 2030, and traffic will be controlled in the section from Bongeun Bridge to Tancheon Road starting from the 7th. The city stated that this construction aims to enhance pedestrian accessibility and strengthen recreational spaces. However, some inconvenience is expected for citizens during the construction period, so alternative routes are recommended.

This news is significant as it indicates that Seoul City is pursuing urban development and environmental improvement simultaneously. In particular, this undergrounding project is expected to alleviate traffic congestion in Seoul and provide better recreational spaces for citizens. However, discussions are needed on how these changes will actually impact citizens.
