Hyengseong-gun to Significantly Expand Pregnancy and Childbirth Support: Childbirth Incentives and Congenital Anomaly Testing Support

Gangwon Hyengseong-gun is set to significantly expand support for pregnancy and childbirth starting in April. The key changes include an increase in childbirth incentives and support for congenital anomaly testing for high-risk pregnant women. The existing 'Childbirth Incentive Support Ordinance' will be revised to the 'Happy Childbirth Support Ordinance,' providing more support for families residing in Hyengseong-gun for at least six months prior to the infant's birth. For the first child, the support amount will increase from 200,000 won to 1,000,000 won, for the second child from 1,000,000 won to 2,000,000 won, and for the third child and beyond, support will increase from 1,200,000 won to 3,000,000 won annually. Additionally, congenital anomaly testing costs for pregnant women over 35 will be supported up to 500,000 won.

These policy changes are part of efforts to increase the birth rate in Hyengseong-gun and reduce economic burdens.

Key Summary

  • Hyengseong-gun to expand childbirth incentives and congenital anomaly testing support starting in April.
  • Childbirth incentives: 1,000,000 won for the first child, 2,000,000 won for the second child, and up to 3,000,000 won annually for the third child and beyond.
  • Congenital anomaly testing support for pregnant women over 35 up to 500,000 won.
  • Revision of the ordinance to 'Happy Childbirth Support Ordinance' targeting residents who have lived in the area for six months.

Key Highlights

  • Increase in Childbirth Incentives: Adjusted to 1,000,000 won for the first child and 2,000,000 won for the second child.
  • Congenital Anomaly Testing Support: Up to 500,000 won for pregnant women over 35.

  • Economic Burden Relief: Efforts to reduce economic burdens during pregnancy and childbirth.

Why Background Matters

The expansion of childbirth support in Hyengseong-gun can be seen as a community effort to address the low birth rate issue. Amid a nationwide trend of declining birth rates in South Korea, local governments are increasingly attempting to maintain regional populations through childbirth incentive policies. Hyengseong-gun has developed more practical support measures in line with this trend.

Why It Matters

This policy is significant not only for its financial support but also for creating an environment where local residents feel it is good to have and raise children. In particular, support for congenital anomaly testing for high-risk pregnant women can be a crucial factor in alleviating anxiety during pregnancy. Ultimately, this will contribute to increasing the birth rate in the community and stabilizing the population structure.

FAQ

Q1: When will the childbirth incentives be disbursed? A1: Childbirth incentives will be provided to families who have been registered as residents for at least six months prior to the infant's birth.

Q2: Under what conditions can the congenital anomaly testing support be received? A2: Pregnant women over 35 who are registered as residents in Hyengseong-gun can receive support up to 500,000 won, including consultation and testing fees on the day of the examination.

Q3: How can I apply for the support funds? A3: Information on how to apply for the support funds can be obtained through the Hyengseong-gun Health Center, and detailed procedures can be inquired at the local health center.
