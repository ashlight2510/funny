Yuhan-Kimberly Holds 'Newlywed Tree Planting' Event in Wildfire-Affected Areas

Recently, Yuhan-Kimberly hosted the 'Our Green Land, Green Green 2026 Newlywed Tree Planting' event in Andong, Gyeongbuk. This event is a citizen participation program that has been ongoing since 1985, where prospective and newlywed couples plant trees to create healthy forests. This year marks the 42nd iteration of the program, which is particularly meaningful as it takes place in a wildfire-affected area.

This event carries significance beyond just planting trees. Yuhan-Kimberly pursues both environmental protection and community recovery while providing newlywed couples with an opportunity to awaken their responsibilities for future generations. Such activities contribute to strengthening ties with local residents, going beyond corporate social responsibility.
