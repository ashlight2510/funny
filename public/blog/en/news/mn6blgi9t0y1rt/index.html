Status of Real Estate Ownership by 50 Members of Parliament in Seoul's Gangnam 3 Districts

According to recent data released by the National Assembly's Public Officials Ethics Committee, out of a total of 287 members of the National Assembly, 50 own real estate such as houses or mixed-use buildings in Seoul's Gangnam 3 districts. Notably, seven members own three or more properties, with many of them belonging to the People Power Party. This fact provides a new perspective on discussions related to real estate policy and raises social interest in how members accumulate their wealth.

This issue goes beyond simply reporting on the real estate holdings of members of parliament; it raises questions about the asset accumulation of politicians and the imbalance in real estate policies. The real estate market in the Gangnam area is particularly concentrated with high-priced houses and commercial properties, making it impossible to ignore the potential influence of the interests of members who own real estate in this region on policy decisions.
