Lotte Chilsung Expands Products Using 100% Recycled Plastic

Lotte Chilsung Beverage has announced the launch of three new PET bottle products made from 100% recycled plastic. These products, 'Pepsi Zero Sugar Lime', 'Isis', and 'Saero', are scheduled to be produced and sold sequentially starting this month. Since last year, Lotte Chilsung has been showcasing eco-friendly packaging technology, starting with 'Chilsung Cider', and this year plans to expand its application to a variety of products from beverages to soju. This is expected to reduce annual plastic usage by approximately 4,200 tons.

This announcement signifies an important step for the company towards sustainable management, not just an expansion of its product lineup. Products made from 100% recycled plastic raw materials contribute to solving environmental issues while providing consumers with opportunities for eco-friendly choices.
