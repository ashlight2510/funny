The statement by the prosecutor of Ssangbangwool, “Lee Jae-myung must be the main culprit,” must be investigated in the National Assembly

Recently, it was revealed that Prosecutor Park Sang-yong, who investigated the Ssangbangwool Group's North Korean remittance case, stated in a conversation with former Vice Governor Lee Hwa-young that “Lee Jae-myung must confess to being the main culprit for bail to be possible.” The Democratic Party of Korea claims that this indicates the prosecution has set up a fabricated scenario to pin the blame on Lee Jae-myung, and this incident has become a central issue in the National Assembly investigation into the political prosecution allegations of the Yoon Seok-yeol administration. This situation is seen as a significant example of how the abuse of prosecutorial power and political pressure occur.

This incident raises serious questions not only about individual accountability but also about the relationship between political power and the prosecution, as well as the transparency of legal procedures. In particular, the suspicion that the prosecution attempted to negotiate sentencing targeting a specific politician could severely undermine trust in the rule of law.

  • The Democratic Party of Korea considers this a fabrication by the prosecution and strongly protests.
  • This incident has emerged as a major issue in the National Assembly investigation, discussing the abuse of prosecutorial power and political pressure.

Key Scenes

Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident is related to the Ssangbangwool Group's North Korean remittance case. The prosecution is identifying Representative Lee Jae-myung as the main culprit, and claims have been made that the statements from the former vice governor's side were made under prosecutorial pressure. The Democratic Party sees this situation as a serious political intervention by the prosecution and urges that it must be clarified in the National Assembly investigation.

Why is it Important?

This incident is not merely a criminal case but a significant example revealing the relationship between political power and the prosecution. If it is true that the prosecution attempted to negotiate sentencing targeting a specific individual for political purposes, this poses a serious problem that undermines the foundations of the rule of law and democracy. Therefore, uncovering the truth of this incident will have a significant impact on future discussions about prosecutorial power and political neutrality.
