Hidden Hotels in Bangkok: 7 Local Recommendations

Bangkok is truly an enchanting city. It's filled with amazing food, culture, and the warmth of its people, but choosing a place to stay is always a dilemma. While famous hotels are great, staying at hidden gems recommended by locals can provide a more special experience. So, I’ve compiled a list of local-recommended hotels based on my stay in Bangkok.

1. Thai Boutique Hotel

Cozy Atmosphere and Friendly Service

Thai Boutique Hotel is a place where you can escape the noise of Bangkok and enjoy a cozy atmosphere. When I visited, the staff welcomed me very warmly. They greeted me with a warm tea during check-in.

Moreover, the hotel is decorated in a traditional Thai design, making it feel like you’ve traveled back in time. Personally, I really enjoyed spending leisurely time by the pool.

2. Kamta Hotel

Excellent Facilities for the Price

Kamta Hotel is truly a great value for money. The rooms are spacious and clean, and above all, the prices are reasonable. If you want to save on your budget while traveling in Bangkok, I highly recommend it!

Additionally, there are many local restaurants near the hotel, making it easy to find delicious Thai food for dinner. The Pad Thai I had when I went out for dinner was absolutely fantastic.

3. The Lifestyle Hotel

Unique Interior and Convenient Location

The Lifestyle Hotel is famous for its unique interior. Each room has a different theme, so the experience at the accommodation is never boring. The room I stayed in had a vintage style, which was perfect for taking photos.

The location was also really great. It was close to the BTS station, making it easy to move around to various tourist attractions in Bangkok. With such a convenient location and such a wonderful interior, I was truly satisfied.

4. Siam Casa

A Place to Experience Local Atmosphere

Lastly, I want to introduce Siam Casa. This place is a popular accommodation among locals, allowing you to feel the real essence of Bangkok. There are traditional markets nearby, so I could go out in the morning and enjoy a Thai breakfast.

Especially, the breakfast here was really delicious. Fresh fruits and Thai rice noodles were available, making it feel like a part of daily life. If you want to truly taste Bangkok, this place is a must-visit!

Conclusion

I’ve introduced some hidden hotels in Bangkok, and staying at these local-recommended accommodations can provide a different charm to your travels. There are various price ranges and unique characteristics, making the selection process enjoyable. If you’re planning a trip to Bangkok, consider these accommodations as well.
