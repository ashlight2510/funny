"Do Not Desecrate the Victims" 249 Civic Groups Condemn the Neglect of Remains at Muan Airport and Urge a Full Reinvestigation

Recently, 249 civic organizations strongly condemned the issue of remains being neglected in relation to the Muan Airport Jeju Air passenger plane disaster and called for a full reinvestigation. They questioned the government's inadequate recovery process and demanded measures to protect the dignity of the victims. In particular, given that remains discovered by bereaved families are still being neglected at the site, they conveyed a strong message: "Do not desecrate the victims."

This incident is not merely a reflection on a past tragedy but a current issue of human rights and dignity. Civic groups criticized the government's announcement that the recovery was complete, while remains continued to be discovered, emphasizing the psychological distress experienced by bereaved families. They are urging the government to take genuine responsible actions to uphold the dignity of the deceased.

Key Points

  • 249 civic groups condemn the neglect of remains in the Muan Airport disaster and demand a full reinvestigation.
  • They call for accountability regarding the government's inadequate recovery process.
  • Remains discovered by bereaved families are currently being neglected.
  • They argue that recovering remains is an essential process to protect the dignity of the deceased.
  • There are demands for an official apology from the government and measures to prevent recurrence.

Background and Context

The Muan Airport Jeju Air passenger plane disaster occurred on December 29, 2022. The incident resulted in significant loss of life, and the government subsequently announced that the recovery was complete. However, as time passed, bereaved families began discovering remains at the site, contradicting the government's announcement. Civic groups argue that this inadequate recovery process undermines the dignity of the deceased and are calling for a thorough investigation.

Why This Issue Is Important

This incident is not just about the recovery of an accident. It is a significant case that illustrates how the state treats bereaved families and victims. The issue of neglected remains is a necessary procedure to uphold the dignity of the deceased and is also a test of the government's credibility. The voices of civic groups are not merely protests but essential demands for social justice and human rights.
