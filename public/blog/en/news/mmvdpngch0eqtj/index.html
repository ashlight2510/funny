Song Eon-seok Protests "Biased National Investigation Special Committee" to Chairperson Woo

Recently, Song Eon-seok, the floor leader of the People Power Party, strongly opposed the formation of a special committee for a national investigation related to the allegations of fabricated indictments by the Yoon Suk-yeol government’s prosecution. He claimed that this investigation is biased and constitutes an abuse of legislative power, warning that it could serve as pressure regarding crimes related to President Lee Jae-myung. He added that he would point out any issues that arise during the operation of this special committee in the future to ensure a proper investigation.

This incident is further escalating political tensions, and there is growing interest in how the conflict between the National Assembly and the government will unfold. Reactions from various stakeholders and the direction of future discussions are being closely monitored.
