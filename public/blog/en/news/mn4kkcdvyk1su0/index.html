Monitor Purchase Checklist: 7 Essential Factors You Might Overlook

Monitors play a crucial role in the work environment, and making the right choice can significantly impact productivity and eye health. This article introduces 7 factors that you must consider when purchasing a monitor.

1. Resolution Choice: What is the Optimal Resolution for Your Needs?

Resolution determines the clarity and detail of the screen. The higher the resolution, the more pixels are displayed, providing a sharper image.

  • Application Examples:

    • Gaming: Recommended 1080p (Full HD) or higher
    • Design Work: 4K resolution is ideal
    • General Office Work: 1080p is sufficient

  • Checkpoint:

    • Consider the performance of your graphics card. Using a 4K monitor with a graphics card that does not support 4K resolution may lead to performance degradation.

2. Screen Size and Ratio: What is the Suitable Choice for Your Workspace?

Screen size and ratio directly affect your workspace and viewing experience.

  • Application Examples:

    • General Office Work and Multimedia Consumption: 27-inch 16:9 screen
    • Multitasking: 34-inch 21:9 ultrawide monitor

  • Checkpoint:

    • A screen that is too large can cause visual fatigue. Consider your workspace to ensure you can view the screen from an appropriate distance.

3. Understanding Panel Types: What are the Differences Between IPS, TN, and VA?

The type of panel in a monitor makes a significant difference in screen quality and response time.

IPS vs. TN vs. VA Panels

  • Application Examples:

    • IPS Panel: Excellent color reproduction and viewing angles, suitable for design work
    • TN Panel: Fast response time, advantageous for gaming
    • VA Panel: Provides deep colors with high contrast ratios

  • Checkpoint:

    • Consider the balance between price and performance for each panel type. IPS panels are valuable for tasks where color accuracy and viewing angles are important.

4. Refresh Rate and Response Time: Essential Elements for a Smooth Screen

Refresh rate and response time affect the smoothness of the screen during gaming and video viewing.

  • Application Examples:

    • General Use: Minimum refresh rate of 60Hz, response time of 5ms or less
    • For Gaming: Recommended refresh rate of 144Hz or higher

  • Checkpoint:

    • You may need a graphics card that supports a high refresh rate. Check the specifications of your graphics card.

5. Additional Features and Connection Ports: Consider Compatibility and Convenience

The various connection ports of a monitor enhance compatibility and user convenience.

HDMI, DisplayPort, USB-C, etc.

  • Application Examples:

    • Monitors with USB-C Ports: Good compatibility with the latest devices
    • Monitors with Built-in Speakers: Usable without additional equipment

  • Checkpoint:

    • Always check compatibility with your devices. Also, verify the version of each port to maximize performance.

6. Price Range and Brand Comparison: Trustworthiness of Quality and Service

The brand and price of a monitor significantly impact its quality and after-sales service.

  • Application Examples:

    • Comparing Brands and Models within Your Budget: Well-known brands guarantee quality but may be expensive; smaller brands can also offer excellent performance.

  • Checkpoint:

    • Be cautious with low-cost products as they may compromise quality. Referring to user reviews is also a good way to verify quality.

7. Monitor Purchase Checklist: Considerations You Might Overlook

  • Check resolution: Confirm the resolution suitable for your needs
  • Decide on screen size and ratio
  • Understand panel types: Compare IPS, TN, VA
  • Check refresh rate and response time
  • Verify necessary connection ports
  • Compare brands and prices
  • Refer to user reviews

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How should I choose a monitor resolution?

    • It is important to select an appropriate resolution based on your usage. For example, 1080p or higher for gaming, and 4K for design work.

  2. What is the difference between IPS and TN panels?

    • IPS panels have excellent color reproduction and wide viewing angles, while TN panels are advantageous for gaming due to their fast response times.

  3. What is the most important factor when purchasing a monitor?

    • Factors such as resolution, panel type, and refresh rate are important depending on personal usage. It is necessary to set criteria that suit your needs.

Now, based on this checklist, consider choosing your monitor. First, clarify your usage purpose and decide on the appropriate resolution and panel type.
