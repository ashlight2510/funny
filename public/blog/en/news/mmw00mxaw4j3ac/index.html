Kim Boo-kyum's Candidacy for Daegu Mayor Becomes Clearer... Will a Match with Lee Jin-sook Be Realized?

News has emerged that former Prime Minister Kim Boo-kyum is considering running for Daegu mayor in the upcoming June 3 local elections. He is expected to announce his intention to run within this month, and there are growing voices in the Daegu region anticipating his candidacy. In particular, the competitive landscape with former Korea Communications Commission Chairperson Lee Jin-sook from the People Power Party is drawing attention. Kim is likely to make his decision considering the administrative issue of changing his address to Daegu and the region's strong conservative characteristics.

This candidacy issue is expected to have a significant impact on the political landscape in the Daegu region. If Kim runs, he will solidify his position as the only candidate within the ruling party, and the competition with former Chairperson Lee will complicate the rivalry between the Democratic Party and the People Power Party.
