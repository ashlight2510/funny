Why is it important for someone to bring world peace, not allowing nuclear Iran?

March 20, 2026 · 한겨레 · View source

Original at a glance Title: [Breaking News] Takaiichi: “Iran's nuclear program is unacceptable... The only person who can bring world peace is Donald” Source: Hankyoreh On the 19th (local time), Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaiichi stated during a summit with U.S. President Donald Trump in Washington, D.C., that “Iran's development of nuclear weapons is absolutely unacceptable, and Japan has been taking action for this.” Prime Minister Takaiichi emphasized that Japan has criticized Iran's military actions and the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, and expressed a negative stance on the request for the dispatch of Japan's Self-Defense Forces. He highlighted the relationship with former President Trump and requested cooperation to strengthen the alliance with the United States.

This statement reflects the diplomatic tensions between Japan and the United States and the complex situation regarding Iran, as Prime Minister Takaiichi's expression of support for former President Trump further clarifies Japan's diplomatic stance.

Key Summary

  • Prime Minister Takaiichi criticizes Iran's nuclear development and supports Trump.
  • Expresses reluctance regarding the request for the dispatch of Self-Defense Forces.
  • Emphasizes strengthening the alliance with the U.S. through the relationship with Trump.
  • Highlights the importance of the Iran issue in Japan's foreign policy.

Key Scenes

  • Criticism of Iran's Nuclear Development: Prime Minister Takaiichi argues that Iran's development of nuclear weapons is unacceptable.
  • Challenges of Dispatching Self-Defense Forces: The Japanese government judges that the dispatch of Self-Defense Forces does not comply with domestic law.
  • Support for Trump: Prime Minister Takaiichi expresses support for former President Trump's diplomatic role.
  • Strengthening the U.S.-Japan Alliance: Emphasizes that the relationship with the U.S. holds a significant position in Japan's foreign policy.
  • Security in the Middle East and Indo-Pacific: Prime Minister Takaiichi mentions not only the Iran issue but also the security environment in the Indo-Pacific.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The issue of Iran's nuclear development has raised concerns in the international community for a long time. As tensions have escalated recently due to attacks from the U.S. and Israel, Japan feels the need to clarify its stance. Prime Minister Takaiichi conveys a message that Japan is actively responding to the Iran issue, reflecting the perception that cooperation with the U.S. is essential in Japan's foreign policy.

Why It Matters

This news illustrates that Japan's foreign policy heavily relies on its relationship with the United States. In particular, Japan's stance on the Iran issue could significantly impact security in the Middle East. Additionally, Prime Minister Takaiichi's remarks are expected to have a considerable influence on diplomatic discussions within Japan.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Prime Minister Takaiichi's negative stance on the request for the dispatch of Self-Defense Forces is a crucial element in discussions about Japan's military autonomy. Japan has a pacifist constitution, which imposes many restrictions on military intervention, so decisions regarding the dispatch of Self-Defense Forces must be made very cautiously. If tensions with Iran escalate, Japan's diplomatic position could become even more complicated.

Current Points to Check

It is essential to pay attention to how the conflict with Iran unfolds and what decision the Japanese government will make regarding the dispatch of Self-Defense Forces. Public opinion and political discussions within Japan will also be significant variables.
