Proposal for Free Public Transport During Commuting Hours: What Questions Remain?

March 29, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Cho Kuk “Proposal for Temporary Free Public Transport for Commuters… Government Measures Should Shift Focus from Private Cars” Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun Cho Kuk, the leader of the Justice Party, proposed on the 29th to include “temporary free public transport support for commuters” in the government’s supplementary budget plan due to the Middle East war. On this day, he stated on Facebook, “There is a need to significantly expand direct support for the majority of citizens who use public transport.” He added, “The government must utilize all available means, including fuel tax reductions and price controls, to respond to the energy crisis.” Read the original article

The Significance and Outlook of Cho Kuk's Proposal for Free Public Transport

Recently, Cho Kuk, the leader of the Justice Party, proposed including temporary free public transport for commuters in the government’s supplementary budget plan due to the repercussions of the Middle East war. He argued that this should reduce the transportation cost burden on citizens and encourage private car users to switch to public transport. He emphasized the need to provide equal benefits to all citizens using public transport and highlighted the necessity of nationwide solidarity to overcome the crisis.

This proposal can be interpreted as a call for a comprehensive restructuring of public transport policy, going beyond mere transportation cost support. Cho cited Germany as an example, asserting that South Korea also needs a long-term public transport policy.

Key Points

  • Proposal for Free Public Transport: Cho Kuk suggested reducing the transportation cost burden on citizens through temporary free public transport for commuters.
  • Encouraging Private Car Users: He proposed a direction to encourage private car users to switch to public transport.
  • Emphasis on Nationwide Solidarity: He stressed the need for nationwide solidarity to overcome the crisis and advocated for equal benefits.
  • Need for Long-term Public Transport Policy: He underscored the necessity of a medium- to long-term public transport policy beyond short-term support.
  • Citing Germany’s Example: He presented the German 9-euro ticket as an example of increased public transport usage and reduced inflation rates.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Cho Kuk's proposal stems from criticism that the government's response to the energy crisis and rising prices due to the recent Middle East war is centered around private cars. There is a growing perception that public transport users are relatively marginalized as government measures focus on private car users. This proposal arises in a context where voices are increasing for comprehensive support for public transport, citing examples like Germany's 9-euro ticket.

Why It Matters

This proposal carries a message to redefine the direction of public transport policy beyond simple transportation cost support. If free public transport is realized, it could significantly reduce the transportation cost burden on citizens and lead to increased usage of public transport infrastructure. This could ultimately have a positive impact on addressing environmental issues and the energy crisis.

Points to Note

In my view, Cho Kuk's proposal is an important step demanding innovation in public transport policy. The comprehensive approach stands out in that it aims to benefit not only public transport users but also private car users. However, if this policy is implemented, securing a budget and the readiness of public transport infrastructure will be crucial.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One aspect that may be overlooked in this proposal is that while free public transport may have short-term effects, long-term improvements in the quality and service of public transport must also occur. Simply offering free transport may not be sufficient to increase usage, and additional efforts will be needed to enhance user satisfaction.

Current Points to Check

  • Political Reactions: It is important to pay attention to how the political sphere responds to and discusses Cho Kuk's proposal.
  • Budget Review: We need to check whether support for free public transport will actually be included in the supplementary budget plan.
  • Stakeholder Opinions: It is crucial to observe the reactions of public transport users, private car users, and the transportation industry.

FAQ

A: It could lead to increased public transport usage and reduced transportation cost burdens, which would also have positive environmental impacts.

Q: What is the likelihood of this proposal actually being implemented?
A: Political consensus and budget allocation are key factors, and it is currently in the discussion stage.

Q: Will private car users also receive benefits?
A: Cho Kuk has stated that he aims to encourage private car users to switch to public transport.

Q: What changes are expected if free public transport is implemented?
A: There will likely be an increase in the usage of public transport infrastructure, along with a need for improvements in the quality of public transport.
