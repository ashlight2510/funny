5 Tips for Saving Electricity Bills When Using Air Conditioning in Summer

When summer comes, it's a time when you can't do without air conditioning. However, if you turn on the air conditioner frequently, you might start worrying about the electricity bill. While thinking, "How can I not turn on the air conditioner when it's this hot?" I wondered if there were ways to reduce the electricity bill. So, I have organized some tips that I found.

1. Clean the Air Conditioner Filter

Cleaning the Filter is Essential!

If the air conditioner filter is dirty, air circulation won't work well, and it will end up consuming more electricity. Therefore, it's good to clean the filter at least once a month.

When I tried it myself, after cleaning the filter, the air from the air conditioner felt much cooler. Nowadays, there are many filters that can be cleaned easily, so it's good to follow along.

2. Set the Temperature Appropriately

The Ideal Temperature is 26 Degrees

You've probably heard that the ideal temperature for an air conditioner is 26 degrees, right? If you set it too low, the electricity bill will rise significantly. Personally, I initially set it to 22 degrees, but on really hot days, 26 degrees was cool enough.

Also, if your air conditioner has a 'power-saving mode', I recommend using it. Setting it to this mode automatically reduces power consumption, which is quite useful.

3. Use a Fan Together

A Fan Helps with Air Circulation

If you only turn on the air conditioner, the airflow can become stagnant, right? So, running a fan alongside it makes you feel cooler. I turned on the air conditioner in the living room and ran a fan in the room, and it felt much cooler while saving on the electricity bill.

Using a fan with the air conditioner helps circulate the air, increasing the efficiency of the air conditioner. By doing this, I found that my electricity bill decreased and the coolness doubled.

4. Utilize Windows and Curtains

Blocking Sunlight is Essential

In the hot summer, leaving windows that let in direct sunlight open makes it harder for the air conditioner to work. So, it's good to close the curtains when you go out.

From my experience, blocking sunlight definitely keeps the indoor temperature from rising too much. This small habit can significantly help reduce the time the air conditioner is used, thus saving on electricity bills.

5. Get Regular Inspections

Seek Professional Help

Finally, it's important to have your air conditioner inspected regularly. Sometimes, having a professional check it can improve the efficiency of the air conditioner. If you get a regular inspection about once a year, you can extend the lifespan of the air conditioner and reduce electricity bills.

By doing this, I found that my electricity bill decreased, and the performance of the air conditioner improved, allowing me to spend the summer comfortably.

Conclusion

Using air conditioning efficiently in summer can save on electricity bills. Remember these five things: cleaning the filter, setting the appropriate temperature, using a fan, managing windows and curtains, and getting regular inspections. By keeping these in mind, you can enjoy a cooler and more economical summer. If you have any questions, leave them in the comments!
