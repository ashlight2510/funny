Secrets Behind K Transformers and Face Masks Ranking First in Global Market Share

Recently, it has been reported that the number of items from Korea that have achieved the highest global export market share has reached 81. In particular, the rise of face masks to the top position for the first time is drawing attention, attributed to the K-beauty trend. According to a report released by the Korea International Trade Association, as of 2024, Korea has maintained its position in the top 10 globally for five consecutive years in terms of the number of products ranked first in global export market share, with memory semiconductors and transformers also achieving the top rank, prompting analysis of the underlying factors.

This growth is not merely a statistic; it serves as an important indicator of Korea's industrial competitiveness and its standing in the global market. The expansion of K-beauty and the demand for power infrastructure are further solidifying the position of Korean products in the global market.

Key Summary

  • The number of Korean products with the highest global export market share is 81.
  • Face masks have risen to the top for the first time due to the K-beauty trend.
  • Memory semiconductors reclaimed the top position, surpassing China for the first time in five years.
  • The demand for transformers has increased due to the expansion of power infrastructure in North America.
  • The competitiveness gap with Japan is narrowing.

Key Highlights

  • Impact of K-beauty: The rise of face masks to the top reflects the global recognition of K-beauty.
  • Demand for Power Infrastructure: Increased demand for transformers positively impacts Korea's power-related industries.
  • Recovery of Memory Semiconductors: Memory semiconductors returning to the top position alleviates market uncertainties.
  • Competition with Japan: The decrease in Japan's export market share strengthens the competitiveness of Korean products.
  • Diverse Top Products: Achieving top positions in chemical products and agricultural and fishery products showcases the results of industrial diversification.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This report illustrates how rapidly Korea is growing in the global market. In particular, K-beauty is gaining immense popularity worldwide, serving as evidence that Korean culture and products are receiving positive responses abroad. In the case of transformers, the increase in demand for power infrastructure in North America has led to a rise in exports of Korean products. This trend indicates that Korea's industrial ecosystem is becoming more robust.

Why It Matters

This data carries significance beyond mere statistics. By achieving first place in the global market, Korean products enhance the country's economic stability and industrial competitiveness. Additionally, global consumers are likely to increase their trust in Korean products, which will lead to a rise in Korea's brand value in the long term.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While Korea's export market share is increasing, it is easy to overlook that the competitiveness gap with Japan is narrowing. Japan has been a major competitor for a long time, and the reduction of this gap is a positive sign for Korea's industrial development, but the possibility of a counterattack from Japan should also be kept in mind.
