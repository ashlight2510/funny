Recently, the conflict between Lee Jeong-hyun, the chairman of the nomination management committee of the People Power Party, and Representative Jang Dong-hyuk has come to the forefront. While Chairman Lee insists on excluding veteran lawmakers from the candidacy for the Daegu mayoral election, Representative Jang has expressed his disagreement by urging for a fair primary. This has intensified conflicts within the party, raising concerns about the possibility of losing to the Democratic Party in the Daegu mayoral election.

This incident goes beyond a simple nomination controversy, revealing issues of generational change and internal cohesion within the People Power Party. Representative Jang has stated his intention to prevent further escalation of conflicts regarding the primaries in Daegu and Chungbuk, while Chairman Lee emphasizes innovative nominations and maintains his stance on excluding veterans. This situation is expected to have significant implications for the party's success or failure in next year's local elections.
