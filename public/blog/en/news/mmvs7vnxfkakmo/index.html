Recently, the assassination of Ali Larijani, the Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council of Iran, by Israel has increased political uncertainty within Iran. Larijani was a key figure who facilitated mediation between hardliners and moderates, keeping the possibility of diplomatic negotiations open. His death is expected to further escalate military tensions in Iran. The Iranian government considers his death a "martyrdom" and has warned of retaliation, highlighting the potential for a prolonged war.

This incident could significantly impact Iran's political landscape and military strategy, potentially leading to major changes in its relationship with the United States. Particularly, given that Larijani was viewed as a pragmatic conservative, his death may reduce Iran's diplomatic flexibility.

Iran's political situation has been complex in recent years. Larijani played a mediating role between the military and moderates, and his death could disrupt this balance. The new Supreme Leader of Iran, Mojtaba Khamenei, is also a military figure, suggesting that military tensions may escalate further.
