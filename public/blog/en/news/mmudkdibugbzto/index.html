Is a trash-free marathon possible? The harmless challenge of 'Muhae Run'

Concerns about waste generated at marathon events are growing. In particular, after the 2026 Seoul Marathon, social media captured scenes of disposable cups, energy gel wrappers, and plastic raincoats piled up, raising awareness of environmental issues. In this context, the 'Muhae Run', taking place on the 5th in Mapo-gu, Seoul, aims for a "trash-free marathon" and explores the possibility of sustainable marathons. This event is hosted by the non-profit organization 'People Who Clean the Earth' and plans to provide participants with new experiences armed with various eco-friendly ideas, just like last year.

This year's Muhae Run aims not only to reduce waste but also to establish a new marathon culture that considers the environment. The remarkable outcome from last year's event showed that only a large guidance banner was left as waste, and positive reactions from participants continued. This trend is also why the event organizers distributed a guide for sustainable marathons for free, hoping that this approach will spread to other events.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Marathon events, with their large number of participants, naturally face ongoing criticism regarding waste generation. In particular, the disposable items and packaging left behind after the event have a serious impact on the environment. To address this issue, People Who Clean the Earth held the Muhae Run for the first time last year, seeking ways to minimize waste. They hope the event will establish a culture that considers the environment beyond mere competition.

Why It Matters

Muhae Run is not just a marathon event. It can raise awareness about environmental issues and serve as a platform for practicing sustainable living. Many people enjoy marathons, but often overlook the environmental impact of these events. Muhae Run highlights this point and provides participants with a new perspective on considering the environment. Furthermore, if such initiatives spread to other events, it could lead to positive changes in the overall marathon culture.

Personal Comment

In my view, the greatest significance of Muhae Run is that it presents the feasibility of practical solutions to environmental issues. It goes beyond merely reducing waste and creates a structure that encourages participants to think about the environment voluntarily. Additionally, the organizers' willingness to reflect participants' opinions and seek change is a positive aspect. However, it remains uncertain whether such initiatives can continue sustainably. Ultimately, a culture of considering the environment must be established among participants for the event organizers to change as well.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The successful operation of Muhae Run relies on active participation from participants and a change in environmental awareness. However, not all participants may embrace this change. For example, some may feel uncomfortable using reusable cups at large-scale events. It is essential not to overlook how these negative reactions could impact event operations.
