How to Eliminate Unpleasant Odors from Your Washing Machine!

March 15, 2026

Unpleasant Smells from the Washing Machine, Here's How to Solve It!

Sometimes, when you open the washing machine, an unpleasant smell can be really embarrassing. You might wonder, "Am I doing laundry correctly?" and worry that your freshly washed clothes might smell bad. So, I've organized some ways to solve this problem!

What Causes the Smell in the Washing Machine?

1. Mold Inside the Washing Machine

In fact, 90% of the smells coming from the washing machine are due to mold. When the washing machine runs in a humid environment, water can accumulate inside, leading to mold growth. Anyone who has experienced this will nod in agreement.

When I first encountered the washing machine smell problem, I was really upset. But it turned out that I wasn't opening the washing machine door often enough, which caused the mold to develop. So now, I've made it a habit to leave the door open after washing. It really works!

How to Remove the Smell from the Washing Machine

2. Using Vinegar and Baking Soda

One of the simplest ways to eliminate unpleasant odors is to use vinegar and baking soda. By adding 1 cup of vinegar and 1 cup of baking soda to the washing machine and running it on the hottest setting, the smell completely disappears.

In my experience, when using this method, make sure the entire washing machine runs. This way, every nook and cranny gets cleaned well. After trying this, I felt really good because the smell from the washing machine decreased significantly.

3. Regular Cleaning

Cleaning the washing machine is also important. You need to clean not only the inside but also the filter and detergent drawer regularly. If you neglect these areas, the smell from the washing machine can become worse.

So, I've decided to clean the filter and detergent drawer once a month. It can be annoying at times, but I feel accomplished after doing it. It seems that such small habits can lead to big changes.

Precautions When Using the Washing Machine

4. Remove Moisture After Washing

Finally, it's important to remove moisture well after washing. After the laundry is done, leave the washing machine door open and let the moisture dry out to help prevent odors.

When I first started doing this, I noticed that the unpleasant smell from the washing machine disappeared. It's such a simple method, but it has a big effect. I often thought, "Why didn't I know something so simple before?"

Conclusion

The unpleasant smells from the washing machine can be solved with simple methods. Just remember to use vinegar and baking soda, clean regularly, and remove moisture well. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! I think it would be great to try this out first.
