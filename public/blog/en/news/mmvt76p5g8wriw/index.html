“Iran was not an imminent threat” US Counterterrorism Center Director resigns… Will it catalyze MAGA division?

Joseph Kent, the head of the US National Counterterrorism Center (NCTC), has resigned, stating that “Iran is not an imminent threat to the US,” thereby shaking the legitimacy of a war against Iran. This is seen as a significant signal revealing the division within the MAGA faction of the Republican Party, drawing attention to its potential impact on US foreign policy and political dynamics. Kent emphasized that a war with Iran would not benefit the American people and could not justify the sacrifices of US soldiers.

This incident goes beyond a mere personal resignation; it reveals internal conflicts within American politics, particularly the deepening divide between factions supporting and opposing the war within the MAGA movement. Such divisions are expected to have a significant impact on future foreign policy decisions.
