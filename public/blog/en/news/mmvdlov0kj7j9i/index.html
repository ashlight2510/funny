A 20-year-old who drank alcohol at a convenience store to avoid being caught for drunk driving has been charged

A 20-year-old has been charged by the police after entering a convenience store to drink alcohol in an attempt to avoid being caught for drunk driving in Gwangju. The incident occurred on the morning of the 15th, and Mr. A, who was charged without detention for obstructing a breathalyzer test, bought and consumed beer after entering the convenience store when police approached him following a citizen's report. However, police confirmed that he had attempted to drive under the influence based on witness statements, and although Mr. A denied the drunk driving, his blood alcohol concentration was ultimately measured at a level that would result in a license suspension.

This incident serves as a reminder of the legal consequences of drinking alcohol to evade a breathalyzer test. Particularly, under the recently passed 'Kim Ho-jung Prevention Law,' individuals who drink alcohol to obstruct a breathalyzer test may face severe penalties.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident occurred amidst increasing legal regulations related to drunk driving. Specifically, the 'Kim Ho-jung Prevention Law' was enacted following an incident involving a famous singer who drank alcohol at a convenience store after driving under the influence. This law aims to strengthen penalties for those who drink alcohol to obstruct breathalyzer tests, with the goal of eradicating drunk driving. This legal backdrop indicates a growing societal awareness of the drunk driving issue.
