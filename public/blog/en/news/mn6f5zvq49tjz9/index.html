Average Wealth of High-ranking Officials Reaches 2.1 Billion Won: Key Points to Consider Amid Rising Housing Prices and Stock Values

March 26, 2026 · 한겨레 · View source

Original at a glance Title: Average assets of high-ranking officials at 2.1 billion won... Increased by 150 million won due to rising house prices and stock prices Source: Hankyoreh Last year, the average assets of high-ranking officials showed a significant increase of over 100 million won due to soaring house prices and stock prices, particularly in the three districts of Gangnam, Seoul. The Government Officials Ethics Committee under the Ministry of Personnel Management reported on the 26th that the average asset increase of high-ranking officials exceeded twice that of the previous year. The report includes asset declarations from 1,903 high-ranking officials, indicating substantial profits from stocks and real estate. Notably, the rise in house prices in the Gangnam area of Seoul is striking, and President Lee Jae-myung's wealth has also increased significantly.

This announcement not only reflects the economic situation of high-ranking officials but also highlights the issue of inequality across society. It is necessary to examine how the increase in the wealth of high-ranking officials relates to the lives of the general public.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The increase in the wealth of high-ranking officials is closely related to the rise in house prices and stock prices. In particular, the real estate market in the Gangnam area of Seoul has shown a sharp increase in recent years, contributing to the rise in the value of assets held by high-ranking officials. Additionally, last year, the stock market recorded its highest growth rate ever, allowing many high-ranking officials to earn significant profits from stock investments. While this economic environment has worked in favor of high-ranking officials, it has created a significant gap when compared to the economic difficulties faced by the general public.

Why It Matters

This announcement is important because the increase in the wealth of high-ranking officials is not just a change in numbers; it is directly linked to the broader issue of inequality in society. While high-ranking officials are increasing their assets, ordinary citizens are facing economic hardships, which can lead to political dissatisfaction and social conflict. The increase in the wealth of high-ranking officials can have significant implications beyond personal wealth, affecting policy decisions and social trust.

Points to Note

In my view, the background of the increase in the wealth of high-ranking officials likely involves not only economic conditions but also significant policy decisions. For example, real estate policies or deregulation in the stock market may have worked in favor of high-ranking officials. These factors should be carefully considered in future policy-making processes.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The reported increase in wealth mentioned in the article is limited to declared amounts, and the actual wealth of high-ranking officials may be greater. Since the publicly assessed value of real estate can be reported lower than actual transaction prices, the true scale of high-ranking officials' wealth could be even larger. This is an important variable, as a lack of transparency in the asset declaration system could exacerbate inequality issues.

Current Points to Check

It is important to closely monitor the trends in the real estate and stock markets. In particular, we should observe how the increase in the wealth of high-ranking officials may influence future policy decisions. Additionally, the impact of this situation on ordinary citizens should also be considered.
