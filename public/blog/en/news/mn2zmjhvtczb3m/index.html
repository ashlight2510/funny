Democratic Party's preliminary election for Seoul mayor begins… Intensifying attacks on candidate Jung Won-oh

As the Democratic Party's preliminary election for Seoul mayor kicks off, the attacks among candidates are intensifying. In particular, with former Seongdong District Mayor Jung Won-oh currently leading in opinion polls, competing candidates such as Rep. Park Joo-min and Rep. Jeon Hyun-hee are ramping up their assaults against him. They are questioning Jung's past actions and pointing out discrepancies with the values and philosophy of the Democratic Party. It will be interesting to see how the negative campaigning unfolds among the candidates during this election process.

This preliminary election is set to determine three candidates for the main election and will be conducted through voting by party members. Candidates are showcasing their respective policies and visions while continuing to criticize their opponents. Jung is countering these attacks while emphasizing his pledges. The dynamics of internal conflicts within the Democratic Party and external public opinion will be crucial points of observation.
