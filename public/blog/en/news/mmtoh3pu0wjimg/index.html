The Significance and Importance of the Broadcast Record from the Last Day of May 18

Recently, a listening record of the emergency broadcast delivered in Gwangju on the last day of the May 18 Democratic Uprising in 1980 has been discovered. This record is contained in a situation report prepared by Gwangju City and the Gwangju District Prosecutor's Office, and it is evaluated as the most accurate reflection of the broadcast content. The protagonist of the broadcast was Park Young-soon, who was a sophomore in college at the time, and her voice urgently informed the citizens of Gwangju as the military's violent suppression began. This document is considered an important resource that solidifies the factuality of the events compared to the testimonies that have been shared until now.

This discovery is expected to deepen the understanding of the May 18 Democratic Uprising and serve as an opportunity to reexamine historical facts. In particular, it demonstrates the intention to include this record in exhibition content to emphasize the truth of the events.

Key Highlights

  • Discovery of a situation report containing the broadcast content from the early morning of May 27, 1980.
  • The protagonist of the broadcast is Park Young-soon, who informed citizens of the military's entry.
  • The records from the two institutions coincide, enhancing the reliability of the broadcast content.
  • The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism plans to include this record in exhibition content.
  • An opportunity to reexamine historical facts and revive citizens' memories.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The May 18 Democratic Uprising is a significant event in modern Korean history, during which violent suppression by the military occurred in Gwangju. Throughout this process, various broadcasts were made as citizens resisted and struggled to convey the truth. However, until now, the exact content of these broadcasts has relied on multiple testimonies, and official records have been lacking. This discovery will serve as an important resource to fill this historical gap.

Why It Matters

This event is not merely about reviving memories of the past. It is essential for understanding how the values of democracy and human rights were formed in the present. Furthermore, how historical facts are recorded and exhibited will have a significant impact on future education and social discussions. If this record is exhibited, it will provide future generations with a deeper understanding of the significance of May 18.
