Japan Hotel Review: Sharing a Special Stay Experience in Tokyo

When traveling, choosing accommodation can be quite a dilemma, right? Which hotel is good, is the price reasonable, is the location convenient, and so on. So this time, I want to share my special stay experience in Tokyo. I’ve organized the hotels I visited. I hope this article helps you!

1. The Heart of Tokyo, the Charm of Shinjuku

Shinjuku Granbell Hotel

I really recommend the Granbell Hotel located in the Shinjuku area! It was very convenient as it is close to Tokyo Metro Shinjuku Station. When I first arrived here, the stylish interior was noticeable right from the lobby. The room was clean and had a modern feel. In particular, my room had a view where I could see the night view of Tokyo all at once, which was really impressive.

What I felt was that it was a stunning view that made me forget the fatigue of the day. Enjoying a beer while looking at the night view in the evening was just perfect. So if I go to Tokyo again, I would love to stay here again!

2. Harmony of Tradition and Modernity, the Charm of Asakusa

Hotel Gracery Asakusa

The Gracery Hotel in Asakusa has a strong traditional Japanese feel. It is close to Sensoji Temple, making it great for sightseeing. The room had traditional Japanese tatami mats, allowing for a comfortable rest. What I felt here was the ability to deeply experience Japanese sentiment.

Especially, waking up on the tatami in the morning and having a cup of coffee was truly fantastic. In contrast to the bustling atmosphere of Asakusa Street, the hotel was quiet and cozy. This harmony was a great attraction for me.

3. A Luxurious Experience, a Night in Roppongi

The Ritz-Carlton Tokyo

The last place I want to introduce is The Ritz-Carlton Tokyo. This hotel provided a truly special experience. Located in Roppongi Hills, it boasts a view of Tokyo Tower and the city of Tokyo all at once. From the moment I checked in, the service from the staff was exceptional.

The reason I stayed at this hotel was to celebrate a special occasion. As soon as I entered the room, I was greeted with a welcome drink and a thoughtfully prepared fruit basket. This small gesture was really touching. In the evening, I dined at the hotel restaurant, and the wonderful atmosphere along with the excellent cuisine created unforgettable memories.

Conclusion

The hotels I experienced during this trip to Tokyo each had their own unique charm. The vibrancy of Shinjuku, the tradition of Asakusa, and the luxury of Roppongi. I think these diverse experiences made my trip special.

If you go to Tokyo next time, I recommend staying at one of these hotels! If you have any questions, leave a comment, and I’ll help as much as I can!
