10 Ways to Save Money with the Best Card Benefits

You know that you can save money by effectively using card benefits, but it can be overwhelming to figure out how to do it. So, I’ve organized this information for you. Based on tips I've personally used, I’ll introduce ways to save money with card benefits!

1. Utilize Point Accumulation

Point Accumulation is Essential!

Have you ever noticed how points accumulate when you use your card? The card I primarily use has a high point accumulation rate, allowing me to receive 1-2% of my monthly spending back as points. I can use these accumulated points for discounts when shopping.

Check Frequently Used Merchants

If there’s a card that offers a lot of points at cafes or supermarkets I frequently visit, it’s better to pay with that card. For example, my favorite supermarket has a partnership with a specific card, so I earn double points when I use that card. Make sure to check these details!

2. Don’t Miss Out on Discount Benefits

Cards with Plenty of Discount Benefits

It’s a good idea to look for cards that offer many discount benefits. Personally, using a card affiliated with my telecom provider has helped me save on my phone bills. It significantly reduces my fixed monthly expenses.

Category-Specific Discounts

Many card companies offer discounts in specific categories. For instance, the card I use gives a 10% discount when dining out on weekends, and since I use it about twice a month, I save a considerable amount. Make sure to take advantage of these offers!

3. Participate in Events and Promotions

Pay Attention to Card Company Events!

By keeping an eye on events or promotions run by card companies, you can receive surprisingly large benefits. For example, I participated in an event that offered additional points based on the amount spent during a specific period, and as a result, I increased my card usage for that month and received bonus points.

Utilize Social Media

Card companies often announce events through social media, so it’s important to stay updated on those news. I often check the latest updates from card companies via social media, and it’s really useful. I share this information with friends so we can all enjoy the benefits!

4. Take Advantage of Overseas Payment Benefits

Choosing the Right Card for Overseas Payments

When making card payments abroad, choosing the right card is crucial. Using a card with low overseas transaction fees can make a significant difference. When I traveled to Europe, I used a card with lower fees and saved quite a bit of money.

Reducing Currency Exchange Fees

Additionally, you should consider currency exchange fees when making overseas payments. Paying with a card often allows you to transact in the local currency, which can help reduce exchange fees. Therefore, it’s important to check and prepare for card benefits in advance.

In conclusion, effectively utilizing card benefits can truly help you save money. From point accumulation to discount benefits, events, and overseas payments, there are various ways to do so. I hope you refer to the tips introduced in this article and give them a try. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment!
