Periodic Maintenance Tips to Prevent Washing Machine Odor

March 16, 2026

Preventing Washing Machine Odor: Periodic Maintenance Tips

The unpleasant smell that you notice every time you use your washing machine can be really annoying, right? When I first encountered the washing machine odor issue, I had no idea why it smelled so bad. So, I’ve organized some tips for you. Let’s explore the periodic maintenance methods to prevent washing machine odors together!

Periodic Maintenance Tips

1. Daily Use: Basic Maintenance

Using the washing machine daily can lead to the buildup of limescale and detergent residue. Therefore, it’s a good idea to always leave the drum door open after washing. This allows for better ventilation, reducing odors. At first, it seemed bothersome, but after trying it, I really noticed a difference. It would be great to develop the habit of opening it right after washing.

2. Weekly: Cleaning the Washing Machine

You should clean your washing machine about once a week. One method I’ve tried is using a detergent specifically designed for washing machines. By adding the detergent and running the machine at the highest temperature, the inside of the washing machine becomes clean. After doing this, the washing machine odor significantly decreased. Additionally, wiping the rubber gasket inside the drum with a wet wipe is also beneficial. This area can accumulate a lot of limescale, so don’t forget to clean it!

3. Monthly: Deep Cleaning

About once a month, you should perform a deeper cleaning of the washing machine’s interior. At this time, using vinegar or baking soda can be very effective. By adding one cup of vinegar and one cup of baking soda and running a hot wash, it’s said that stubborn stains and odors inside the washing machine will disappear. I tried this method, and the smell from the washing machine improved significantly. Plus, this method is environmentally friendly, which I really like.

4. Quarterly: Checking the Filter and Hose

Lastly, it’s important to check the filter and hose quarterly. If foreign substances accumulate in the filter inside the washing machine, it can cause odors. It’s good to take out the filter and wash it thoroughly, and also check for any blockages in the hose. After doing this, I noticed an improvement in the washing machine’s performance and was able to prevent odor issues.

Conclusion

By organizing these methods to prevent washing machine odors, I feel like maintenance will be easier. Regular maintenance can help reduce the unpleasant smells coming from your washing machine, so I hope you give it a try! If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment. I think starting with this would be great!
