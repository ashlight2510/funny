Let's amend the 'Medical Technicians Act' for patients, not just for direct translation

In South Korea, which has entered a super-aged society, the difficulties elderly patients face in accessing medical care are no longer a strange sight. For those who find the journey to the hospital painful, amending the Medical Technicians Act is not just a legal revision but a matter of survival. The current amendment under discussion in the National Assembly stipulates that medical technicians can only perform their duties under the guidance of a physician, but this regulation is detached from reality. The amendment aims to change the expression 'under guidance' to 'according to guidance or prescription/referral' to allow patients to receive medical services more safely and easily.

This change is not merely about adjusting legal authority; it is essential for ensuring the continuity of patient treatment and care. It establishes conditions under which medical technicians can provide services at home according to a physician's prescription, which will be a significant help, especially for elderly patients who have difficulty moving.

Key Summary

  • The amendment to the Medical Technicians Act aims to legally organize the existing collaborative structure rather than expand the scope of medical technicians' duties.
  • The current law restricts medical technicians to performing their duties only under the direct guidance of a physician, which limits the provision of realistic medical services.
  • The amendment changes the term 'guidance' to 'according to guidance or prescription/referral' to make it easier for patients to access necessary medical services.
  • This is particularly important for elderly patients in a super-aged society, as it can be a matter of survival for those who find it difficult to visit hospitals.
  • The amendment includes provisions for documenting tasks performed according to prescriptions, thereby enhancing accountability and transparency.

Key Scenes

Reasons to Consider the Background

South Korea has entered a super-aged society, and the difficulties in accessing medical care have become a social issue. The inconveniences elderly patients face in getting to hospitals are not mere inconveniences; they can directly impact their health and lives. Reflecting this reality, the argument for the need to amend the Medical Technicians Act is being made, and it is seen as an essential measure to improve the quality of life for patients rather than just changing legal authority.

Why is it Important?

This amendment is not simply a change in law. It provides patients with the opportunity to receive medical services more safely and comfortably, and for elderly patients who have difficulty moving, it can be a matter of survival. It will contribute to making the collaborative structure in the medical field more efficient and to building a patient-centered healthcare system.
