Ken Hakuda, Nam June Paik's nephew, expresses hope for a 100th anniversary exhibition in Korea

Ken Hakuda, the nephew of the late Nam June Paik, expressed the significance of introducing Paik's work for the first time in Korea at the recent exhibition ‘Nam June Paik: Rewind/Repeat’ held in Seoul, and he hopes that the 100th anniversary exhibition will take place in Korea. This exhibition marks the first solo exhibition of Nam June Paik in Korea in 25 years, and Hakuda mentioned the conflicts with the Korean art community through the works, suggesting a direction for the future.

The exhibition features a total of 11 works, including Paik's representative pieces and unpublished works, and Hakuda emphasized the importance of shedding light on Paik's artistic legacy in Korea, stating, "If my uncle were here, he would be delighted."

Reasons to Consider the Background

Nam June Paik is recognized globally as a pioneer of video art. His works have had a significant impact not only in Korea but also internationally. However, after his passing, his nephew Hakuda has faced various conflicts with the Korean art community. In particular, copyright issues have limited exhibitions in Korea. This exhibition is seen as an opportunity to overcome these conflicts and shed new light on Paik's legacy.

Why It Matters

This exhibition not only provides an opportunity to see Nam June Paik's works in Korea again but also serves as a chance to solidify his artistic legacy through communication with the Korean art community. Additionally, it is important as it opens up the possibility of an exhibition honoring Paik's 100th anniversary in Korea.

My Thoughts

Hakuda's mention of the "lack of understanding of the structure of the Korean art community" is a very meaningful point. It is necessary for artists and those managing their legacies to enhance mutual understanding through communication and reduce conflicts. Paik's artistic legacy includes not only his works but also the philosophy and messages he left behind.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The conflicts between Hakuda and the Korean art community, as mentioned in his statements, are not merely personal issues. They reflect an important aspect of how the Korean art community is perceived internationally. Therefore, the impact of future exhibitions and communication methods on the development of the Korean art community should also be noted.

FAQ

Q: Why are Nam June Paik's works important?
A: As the founder of video art, Nam June Paik's works introduced new trends in contemporary art. His work breaks down the boundaries between technology and art, exploring various forms of communication.

Q: What works are included in this exhibition?
A: The exhibition features a total of 11 works, including unpublished video installation pieces, as well as early and later works by Nam June Paik.

Q: What are the conflicts Hakuda has with the Korean art community?
A: Hakuda pointed out conflicts regarding copyright issues and artwork management within the Korean art community, highlighting a lack of communication as a problem.

Q: How will Nam June Paik's works be exhibited in the future?
