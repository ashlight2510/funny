US Counterterrorism Director Resigns Over Iran War Opposition, MAGA Camp's Continued Support

Recently, Joe Kent, the director of the US National Counterterrorism Center, resigned in opposition to the Iran war, escalating political tensions in the United States once again. Kent argued that Iran does not pose a threat to the US and that the war was triggered by Israeli lobbying. However, President Trump and his supporters, known as the MAGA camp, continue to show overwhelming support for the Iran war, making it unlikely that Kent's resignation will lead to a division within the MAGA camp.

This incident goes beyond a simple personal resignation and could serve as a significant turning point in gauging the political landscape and public opinion on the war in the US. Kent's resignation has sparked controversy within the MAGA camp regarding the Iran war, which may have substantial implications for future political decisions and foreign policy.
