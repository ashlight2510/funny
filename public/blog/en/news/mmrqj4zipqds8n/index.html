Simple Cleaning Routine to Eliminate Washing Machine Odor

March 15, 2026

Introduction of a Simple Cleaning Routine to Eliminate Washing Machine Odors

That strange smell you notice every time you use the washing machine, it's really unpleasant, right? I totally understand the feeling of not wanting to run the washing machine again because of the smell coming from the washed clothes. So, I've organized this for you. Let me introduce a simple cleaning routine to eliminate washing machine odors!

1. Cleaning the Inside of the Washing Machine

Cleaning the Drum and Filter

First, let's clean the inside of the washing machine. The smell often comes from lint or detergent residue left in the drum. I’ve tried using vinegar to clean the drum. Vinegar is a powerful deodorizer that comes from nature.

  1. Pour 1 cup of vinegar into the washing machine drum and run a hot water wash cycle.
  2. Be sure to clean the filter as well. Dust or lint buildup in the filter can be a source of odor.

After doing this, I found that the drum was clean and the smell was significantly reduced. This part was really important!

2. Cleaning the Detergent Drawer and Rubber Gasket

Managing the Detergent Drawer and Rubber Gasket

If your washing machine smells, there might be an issue with the detergent drawer as well. If detergent hardens or mold develops in the detergent drawer, the smell can become worse. So, it's a good idea to remove the detergent drawer and wash it with water, and don’t forget to clean the rubber gasket area.

  1. Remove the detergent drawer and wash it thoroughly; for the rubber gasket, wiping it with vinegar is a good idea.
  2. The rubber gasket, in particular, doesn’t dry easily, which can lead to mold, so it’s best to leave it open after washing.

Personally, since I clean the detergent drawer often, I worry less about odors. This maintenance really helps!

3. External Cleaning and Ventilation

Cleaning the Outside of the Washing Machine and Ventilating

It’s important to keep the outside of the washing machine clean as well. I’ve developed the habit of cleaning the area around the washing machine every time I use it and ventilating after washing. If the area around the washing machine is dirty, it can easily cause odors.

  1. After running the washing machine, it’s good to leave the door open for ventilation.
  2. Wiping down the dust and moisture around the washing machine will help reduce odors.

By managing the outside like this, the odors from the washing machine have significantly decreased. It’s really pleasant!

Conclusion

I’ve introduced a simple cleaning routine to eliminate washing machine odors. By managing the inside of the washing machine, the detergent drawer, and the outside, you can use it cleanly without worrying about smells. If you have any questions, leave them in the comments! You should definitely try this!
