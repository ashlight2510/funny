The Prelude to the Collapse of U.S. Hegemony: The Iran War

As tensions with Iran escalate, claims are being made that U.S. hegemony is under threat. An article from Kyunghyang Shinmun analyzes this situation in a historical context, pointing out parallels with the decline of British hegemony in the past. In particular, Iran's military retaliation and control over oil transport routes are expected to have serious implications for the international credibility of the U.S. and the dollar hegemony that underpins its economy.

It is important to note that the war with Iran goes beyond mere military conflict; it significantly impacts the U.S.'s diplomatic standing and the global economy. This could signify the beginning of a broader change in the international order and economic system, rather than just a war involving a single nation.

Key Summary

  • The Iran war has a significant impact on U.S. hegemony, showing patterns similar to the decline of British hegemony in the past.
  • Despite U.S. military threats, Iran is strengthening its influence in the Middle East and shaking the global economy through control of the Strait of Hormuz.
  • The U.S. dollar hegemony is under serious threat due to the Iran war, which could have tangible effects on the international economy.

Key Scenes

  • Crisis of U.S. Hegemony: The conflict with Iran is weakening U.S. international credibility and hegemony.
  • Importance of Oil Transport Routes: Control of the Strait of Hormuz has direct implications for the global economy.
  • Crisis of Dollar Hegemony: There is a possibility of reduced reliance on the dollar in oil transactions.
  • Changes in U.S. Foreign Policy: Criticism exists that the Trump administration's foreign policy has weakened the U.S.'s international standing.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The backdrop of the Iran war involves U.S. Middle East policy and Iran's military retaliation. Similar to Britain's Suez Canal crisis, the U.S. attempted to maintain hegemony through military intervention in the Middle East, but Iran's unexpected counterattacks are thwarting U.S. plans. Iran's military actions are increasing anxiety among U.S. allies under its security umbrella, negatively affecting U.S. diplomatic credibility.

Why It Matters

The Iran war is a significant event that could alter the landscape of international economics and politics beyond mere military conflict. In a situation where U.S. hegemony is threatened, other countries are likely to form new alliances or readjust existing foreign policies. This is not just an issue for the U.S., but signifies a global reconfiguration of economics and politics.

Points of Attention

In my view, the outcome of the Iran war will have direct implications for U.S. international credibility and economic foundations, beyond just military victories or defeats. In particular, the weakening of dollar hegemony could undermine global economic stability, posing a problem that all nations may face. Additionally, political instability in the Middle East will have serious repercussions for European and Asian countries as well.

Hidden Variables to Watch

A noteworthy aspect of this conflict is that the Iran war is not merely a military clash but a serious challenge to U.S. hegemony and the international order. Furthermore, it is essential to closely observe how U.S. foreign policy may change and how that will affect the global economy.

Current Checkpoints

  • The progress of the Iran war and U.S. military responses.
  • Control of oil transport routes in the Strait of Hormuz and the resulting economic impacts.
  • Changes in U.S. foreign policy and the international community's reactions.

FAQ
