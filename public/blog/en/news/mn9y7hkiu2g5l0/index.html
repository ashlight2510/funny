Criminal Organization Using Baemin Customer Information Faces Detention Review

Recently, an organization that committed crimes by misusing Baemin customer information is set to undergo a detention review. They engaged in illegal activities such as throwing waste at others' front doors and vandalizing them, and it has been revealed that they obtained customer information through a 40-year-old man who disguised himself as a consultant at an outsourcing company. The police have conducted searches of related companies as part of their investigation into this case.

This incident reveals serious issues related to personal information protection beyond mere criminal acts. Woowa Brothers, the operator of Baemin, has stated that they are in the process of terminating contracts with the outsourcing company involved, but the anxiety of the victims remains.
