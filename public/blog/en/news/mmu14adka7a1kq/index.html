Movie 'The King and the Man' Boosts Tourist Attractions in Yeongwol... Train Travel Package Launched

Recently, the film has surpassed 12 million viewers, gaining immense popularity. As a result, tourist attractions in Yeongwol County, which served as the backdrop for the film, are receiving attention. Yeongwol County and Korail have launched a train travel package that allows visitors to explore these attractions. The package operates every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, departing from Cheongnyangni Station in Seoul, Daejeon Station, and Busan Bujeon Station, and includes a tour of the historical sites related to King Danjong's exile.

This train travel experience goes beyond a simple tourist package; it serves as a case study demonstrating the positive impact of the film's success on the local economy, highlighting both the revitalization of tourist sites and the re-examination of local culture.

Key Summary

  • The success of the film is increasing interest in Yeongwol's tourist attractions.
  • Yeongwol County and Korail have collaborated to launch a train travel package.
  • The package operates every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday and includes major tourist sites.
  • The price is 55,400 won per person, including train fare and admission fees.
  • A special train related to the Yeongwol Danjong Cultural Festival will also be operated.

Key Points

  • Tourist Site Revitalization: The film's popularity is expected to increase visitor numbers to Yeongwol's tourist attractions.
  • Cultural Heritage Spotlight: The historical culture related to King Danjong is being re-examined, potentially strengthening the region's identity.
  • Economic Impact: It provides opportunities that can positively affect the local economy.
  • Diversification of Tourist Products: The launch of the train travel package has created various tourism options.
  • Connection with Cultural Festivals: It can maximize tourist influx by linking with local festivals like the Danjong Cultural Festival.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The film deals with the tragic story of King Danjong, which has brought attention to tourist sites associated with him. Yeongwol is famous as the place of exile for King Danjong, and the historical sites in this area have naturally gained value as tourist destinations due to their connection to the film. In this context, Yeongwol County has developed tourism products to revitalize the local economy.

Why It Matters

This train travel package is an excellent example of how films can influence local economies beyond being mere tourist products. It presents an opportunity to observe the positive effects of cultural content on local tourism. This opens up possibilities for other regions to establish tourism activation models utilizing cultural content.

Important Considerations

It is crucial to note that the train travel package, priced at 55,400 won, only includes basic costs. Additional expenses may arise during the tour, and factors such as crowding at tourist sites and waiting times should also be considered. Moreover, while train services operate regularly, reservations may be difficult during specific seasons or holidays, making advance planning essential.

Key Points to Check Now

  • Yeongwol Danjong Cultural Festival Schedule: This festival will take place from April 24 to 26, and a special train related to it will also be operated, providing useful information for travel planning.
  • Train Travel Package Reservations: As this is a popular tourist product, advance reservations are essential. Check availability and schedules in advance.

FAQ

Q: When does the train travel package operate?
A: It operates every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

Q: What is included in the price?
A: The price includes round-trip train fare, admission fees to tourist sites, and bus transportation.

Q: When will the special train related to the Danjong Cultural Festival operate?
A: The special train will operate on April 25, during the Danjong Cultural Festival.

Q: Where can I find more information about Yeongwol tourist attractions?
A: You can check the official tourism website of Yeongwol County or Korail's official website.

Finally, consider booking the Yeongwol train travel package now. It will be an opportunity to experience the film's backdrop firsthand and deeply appreciate the region's history and culture.
