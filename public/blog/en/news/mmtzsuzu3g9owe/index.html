30s Son Sentenced for Assaulting Mother and Stealing Money: Why It Matters

March 17, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Son assaults mother and steals money... Court sentences him to a long term Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun A 'deviant son' who assaulted his mother and stole money has been sentenced to a long term in prison. The 11th Criminal Division of the Gwangju District Court (Presiding Judge Kim Song-hyun) announced on the 17th that it sentenced A (34) to 8 years in prison for charges including robbery and injury, and ordered 5 years of probation. A was indicted for visiting his mother B's store last September and violently assaulting her while demanding money, stealing valuables worth 900,000 won and two bankbooks. He also withdrew 1 million won from her bank account without permission after overpowering her, and attempted to escape after binding her hands and taking the CCTV equipment to prevent her from reporting. The court sentenced him to 8 years in prison considering his recidivism and brutality.

This incident goes beyond a simple assault case, highlighting the seriousness of violence within families. In particular, violence between family members is perceived as more shocking socially, and the reality of such crimes recurring raises questions about the safety net in our society.

Key Summary

  • A was sentenced to 8 years in prison for assaulting his mother and stealing valuables.

Key Scenes

  • A was indicted for serious crimes including assaulting and robbing his mother.

  • Mother B sustained injuries requiring two weeks of treatment due to the assault.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident reminds us once again how serious the problem of domestic violence is. A has a history of being punished for violent crimes, indicating that his reoffending is not just a random occurrence. Particularly, the fact that the violence was directed at his mother, the closest family member, adds to the social shock. Such incidents illustrate how trust and safety within families can be shattered.

Why It Matters

Domestic violence cases have serious impacts on both victims and perpetrators. The victim, the mother, suffers not only physical injuries but also psychological trauma. Furthermore, socially, violence within families is not just a household issue but an important topic in terms of crime prevention and building social safety nets. This incident should remind us that discussions on prevention and response strategies for domestic violence are necessary.

Personal Comment

In my view, this case prompts us to think beyond mere criminal punishment and consider how society can prevent and support domestic violence. The fact that A's crimes were repetitive indicates that the social support systems surrounding him were not functioning properly. Both the victim, mother B, and the perpetrator, A, likely needed social support. To prevent such incidents from recurring, I believe we need more systematic prevention measures and treatment.

Hidden Variables to Consider

A notable point in this case is that A attempted to escape after stealing CCTV equipment. This can be viewed as a more serious crime, as it indicates planning and execution of the crime rather than a spontaneous act of violence. Additionally, A's prior criminal record underscores the importance of crime prevention. Such cases are not just individual problems but are directly linked to the safety of society as a whole.
