How to Check Tire Pressure and Important Precautions

March 18, 2026

How to Check Tire Pressure and Important Precautions You Must Know

Checking tire pressure is essential for safe driving. Maintaining the proper tire pressure contributes to improved fuel efficiency and extends tire life, so all drivers should regularly check and maintain it. In this article, we will take a detailed look at how to check tire pressure and the precautions to take.

Why is Tire Pressure Important?

Essential Element for Safe Driving

Tire pressure is a fundamental element for safe driving. If the proper tire pressure is not maintained, handling and braking performance can deteriorate, increasing the risk of accidents. For example, driving with low tire pressure can reduce fuel efficiency by more than 10%.

Checkpoints:

  • Check the proper tire pressure in your vehicle manual.
  • Refer to the tire pressure information listed on the inside of the driver's door or the fuel filler cap.

Impact on Economy and Tire Life

Maintaining the proper tire pressure helps save fuel and extends tire life. Conversely, excessive tire pressure can cause the center of the tire to wear out, shortening its lifespan, while insufficient tire pressure can lead to uneven wear, compromising safety.

Execution Tips:

  • Make it a habit to check tire pressure monthly and adjust according to the season.

How to Check Tire Pressure

Required Tools

To check tire pressure, you need a pressure gauge and an air inflator. Pressure gauges are easy to use and can be purchased at most auto accessory stores.

Checking Process

  1. Measure When Tires Are Cold: Measuring immediately after driving can cause the pressure to rise due to heat, so it is best to check after parking the vehicle for at least 3 hours.
  2. Measure Tire Pressure: Compare the measured tire pressure with the proper tire pressure listed in the vehicle manual and adjust if it is too low or too high.

Key Check Points

It is important to check all tires. Be sure to include the spare tire, and adjust the pressure according to driving conditions. For example, if you are carrying a heavy load, it is advisable to slightly increase the tire pressure.

Checklist:

  • Measure when tires are cold
  • Familiarize yourself with how to use the pressure gauge
  • Inflate if necessary

Tire Pressure Maintenance Tips

Regular Check-Up Cycle

It is advisable to check at least once a month. Always check before long trips. For example, if you are planning a long trip of over 1,000 km, be sure to check the tire pressure before departure.

Seasonal Pressure Adjustment

Tire pressure can change with temperature variations between summer and winter, so it should be adjusted according to the season. In winter, lower temperatures can reduce tire pressure, so check monthly and replenish as needed.

Execution Tips:

  • Check tire pressure monthly in winter and replenish if necessary.

Comparison of Problems with Low and High Tire Pressure

Problem Low Tire Pressure High Tire Pressure
Driving Safety Unstable handling, increased braking distance Increased risk of tire damage
Fuel Efficiency Decreased fuel efficiency Possible improvement, but risky
Tire Life Uneven wear Center wear shortens lifespan

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How often should I check tire pressure?

    • It is recommended to check at least once a month and before long trips.

  2. How should I adjust tire pressure in winter?

    • Tire pressure decreases in lower temperatures, so check monthly and replenish as needed.

  3. What should I be careful about when checking tire pressure?

    • Measure when the tires are cold, and it is important to check all tires.

Checking tire pressure is a basic maintenance task for safe driving. Start making it a habit to regularly check and adjust tire pressure as needed. This will be the first step towards safe and economical driving. Check your vehicle manual now and verify the proper tire pressure!
