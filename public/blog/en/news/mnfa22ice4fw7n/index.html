Why Has Iran's New Leader Moztaba Not Appeared in Public?

The controversy surrounding the reasons for Moztaba Khamenei, Iran's new Supreme Leader, not appearing in public is growing. The Iranian ambassador to Russia, Alexei Dedov, recently stated in an interview that Moztaba is in Iran and has not appeared publicly for various reasons. Moztaba is the son of Ayatollah Khamenei, who died in a U.S.-Israeli airstrike in February, raising concerns about his health and appearance.

This issue goes beyond the public activities of a single individual and could significantly impact Iran's political stability and international relations. The question of when and how Moztaba will appear before the public has become a matter of interest both inside and outside Iran.

  • Russia has expressed support for Moztaba, emphasizing its strategic partnership with Iran.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Since being elected as Iran's new Supreme Leader, Moztaba Khamenei has taken on an important political role following in his father's footsteps. However, his absence from public appearances has raised many questions both domestically and internationally. In particular, the impact of the U.S. and Israeli airstrikes on him is drawing attention, as it is closely related to Iran's political situation rather than being merely a personal issue.

The ambassador's remarks come amid deepening relations between Iran and Russia, showcasing Russia's position in supporting Iran's political stability.
