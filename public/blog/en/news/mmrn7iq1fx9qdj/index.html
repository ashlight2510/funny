Essential Discounts to Know When Purchasing Insurance

March 15, 2026

Summary of Discount Benefits You Must Know When Signing Up for Insurance

When signing up for insurance, it can be so complicated that it gives you a headache, right? I felt overwhelmed when I first signed up for insurance too. I kept thinking, "I heard there are many discount benefits, but how do I apply for them?" So, I organized the discount benefits I found out about. If you're considering signing up for insurance, I believe this article will be helpful!

1. Auto Insurance Discount Benefits

1-1. No-Claim Discount

In auto insurance, you can receive discounts based on the no-claim period. The insurance company I signed up with offers a discount of over 10% if you have no claims for more than three years. This was a really big benefit. My friend also went over five years without a claim, and the discount amount was quite significant. Initially, the insurance premium felt burdensome, but after receiving this discount, I felt much more at ease.

1-2. Safe Driving Discount

If you have a good safe driving record, don't miss out on this discount benefit! Vehicles equipped with a dashcam can record driving habits, and if you submit this to the insurance company, you can receive an additional discount. Thanks to my dashcam, I received a few percent more off. Be sure to check this out!

2. Health Insurance Discount Benefits

2-1. Regular Check-Up Discount

When signing up for health insurance, you can receive a discount if you undergo regular check-ups. You might think, "Regular check-ups? That sounds bothersome…" but I found that it was a win-win situation where I could take care of my health and receive a discount. If you submit the results of your regular check-up to the insurance company, the discount is applied immediately, so you definitely should do this.

2-2. Non-Smoker Discount

If you're a non-smoker, make sure to take advantage of this benefit as well. Non-smokers tend to have lower insurance premiums. I'm a non-smoker, and thanks to that, I saved quite a bit. For those who smoke, it might be a good opportunity to consider quitting.

3. Life Insurance Discount Benefits

3-1. Family Enrollment Discount

If your family enrolls in life insurance together, there are discount benefits available. I signed up for insurance with my parents, and the premium dropped significantly due to the family enrollment. I wish I had known about this benefit earlier so I could have signed up sooner; I feel a bit regretful about that.

3-2. Long-Term Enrollment Discount

Life insurance offers significant discounts for long-term enrollment. If you enroll for more than five years, the premium decreases substantially. The insurance company I signed up with had this benefit, and it was important. It seems that insurance should be considered in the long term.

Conclusion

Discount benefits are a really important factor when signing up for insurance. With various options like no-claim discounts, regular check-up discounts, and family enrollment discounts, it's best to take advantage of these when signing up. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! And here's something you should do: compare the benefits of different insurance companies and find the insurance that suits you best!
