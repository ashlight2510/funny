A Miracle After 11 Years, the Opening of ‘Oullim Plaza’ for the Disabled

Finally, ‘Oullim Plaza’ has officially opened its doors in Deungchon-dong, Gangseo-gu, Seoul. This facility is composed of various spaces that can be used by both disabled and non-disabled individuals, and it has come to life after a long wait of 11 years. In particular, the establishment of a dental hospital exclusively for the disabled and various cultural facilities is expected to significantly contribute to the improvement of welfare in the local community.

This opening can be seen as an important milestone in promoting the rights and welfare of disabled individuals, going beyond just the opening of a facility. ‘Oullim Plaza’ serves as a space that helps disabled individuals participate more actively in society, highlighting its necessity at this time.

Key Highlights

  • The Nation's First ‘Barrier-Free’ Facility: Various facilities are provided for both disabled and non-disabled individuals to use together.
  • Dental Hospital Exclusively for the Disabled: This is the second dental hospital for the disabled to open in Seoul, creating an environment for professional treatment.
  • Companion Crew Operation: A service that supports the movement of disabled individuals when using the facility, enhancing accessibility.
  • Expanded Parking Space: Dedicated parking areas for the disabled and general parking spaces are provided, which can also resolve parking issues for local residents.
  • Diversity of Cultural Facilities: Various cultural facilities such as a swimming pool, library, and fitness room are available for all local residents to use.

Why Understanding the Background is Important

The opening of ‘Oullim Plaza’ is the fruit of 11 years of effort. Initially, the project faced difficulties due to opposition from local residents and parents, but the Seoul city government actively reflected residents' opinions, formed a consultation body, and attempted continuous communication. This process was essential for establishing the disabled welfare facility as an integral part of the local community, rather than just a simple facility.

Why It Matters

The improvement of welfare for the disabled is closely related to a change in societal perception, beyond just the opening of facilities. ‘Oullim Plaza’ will play an important role in enhancing mutual understanding and reducing prejudice against disabled individuals by providing a space where both disabled and non-disabled individuals can mingle. This lays the foundation for disabled individuals to participate more actively in society.

Noteworthy Points

Since the announcement of the opening, facilities like the swimming pool and library at ‘Oullim Plaza’ have already garnered significant interest. In particular, the swimming pool has become so popular that pre-registration has closed. This clearly demonstrates the high demand for facilities that disabled individuals need.

It is crucial to reduce the inconveniences that disabled individuals experience when using facilities, and in this regard, ‘Oullim Plaza’ holds great significance. However, it remains to be seen whether all disabled individuals can easily access this facility.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While the dental hospital exclusively for the disabled is a very important facility, there are concerns about whether sufficient personnel and equipment will be secured for its operation. Additionally, the efficiency of the companion crew's operation will also be a point of attention. It will be important to observe how many disabled individuals can actually benefit from these services.

Current Checkpoints

  • Oullim Plaza Program Reservations: Various programs available for disabled individuals require prior reservations.
  • Dental Hospital Appointment Schedule: It is advisable to familiarize yourself with the reservation methods for the dental hospital, which will officially start operations on April 1.

FAQ

  • What facilities are available at Oullim Plaza?

    • Various cultural facilities such as a fitness room, swimming pool, library, and multipurpose auditorium are provided.

  • When will the dental hospital exclusively for the disabled start operating?

    • Official treatment will begin on April 1. Prior reservations are necessary.

  • How can I use the companion crew?

    • When a disabled individual reserves a program, they can receive mobility support.

Now that ‘Oullim Plaza’ has opened, it is important to visit this place. Experience firsthand how a space where disabled and non-disabled individuals can mingle is operated.
