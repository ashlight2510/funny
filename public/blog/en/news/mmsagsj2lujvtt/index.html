Must-Visit Islands in Southeast Asia: Hidden Gem Destinations

March 16, 2026

List of Must-Visit Islands in Southeast Asia: Hidden Gem Destinations

Have you ever wondered, "Where should I go?" while planning a trip? Especially in Southeast Asia, there are so many famous islands that it can be really hard to choose. So this time, I’ve compiled a list of hidden gem islands. While well-known places are great, I believe you can have a special experience on lesser-known islands!

1. Koh Phangan

This island is a coexistence of parties and peace!

Koh Phangan is famous for its 'Full Moon Party.' However, there are many beautiful beaches and tranquil spots as well. Personally, I had a wonderful time sipping beer at a small bar near Haad Rin Beach while watching the sunset over the sea. If you want to enjoy the party, plan your visit around the Full Moon Party, and if you prefer quiet time, it’s best to avoid that period.

2. Koh Rong

The charm of nature untouched

Koh Rong is an island located in Cambodia that is still not heavily visited by many travelers. The charm of this place lies in its clean waters and untouched landscapes. I spent a day at Long Beach, swimming and reading a book, and it was incredibly peaceful. Especially, the experience of seeing fireflies in the sea at night is something I will never forget. If you want to enjoy such quiet moments, I recommend Koh Rong!

3. Komodo Island

Meeting the dragons

Komodo Island, as the name suggests, is home to the Komodo dragons. This place is not just about seeing the dragons; it is also famous for its fantastic snorkeling and diving spots. From my diving experience, I found the marine ecosystem to be incredibly diverse and beautiful. Don’t miss out on exploring the underwater world around the island in addition to the dragon tours!

4. Sinaraja

If you want to feel the local vibe

Located a bit away from Bali, Indonesia, Sinaraja is a place where you can truly feel the charm of the locals. It has a strong sense of tranquility due to the low number of tourists. Just walking around, you can feel the kindness of the villagers. Especially, it was really interesting to eat local food and learn about their culture. Such experiences help you understand the area more deeply.

The islands introduced this time all have their unique charm and special qualities. If you want to step away from famous tourist spots and have new experiences, I think it would be great to visit one of these. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! It would be nice to choose one of these islands as your next travel destination.
