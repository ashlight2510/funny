Micron Announces HBM4 Mass Production Shipment – Strengthening Partnership with NVIDIA

Micron, a U.S. memory semiconductor company, has begun mass production shipments of HBM4, the next-generation high bandwidth memory (HBM), dismissing recent rumors about a potential dropout from its partnership with NVIDIA. At NVIDIA's annual developer conference, GTC 2026, Micron emphasized that HBM4 products offer pin speeds of over 11Gbps and bandwidth exceeding 2.8TB/s, stating that this product will play a crucial role in the advancement of AI technology. Notably, HBM4 boasts a 20% improvement in power efficiency and a 2.3-fold increase in bandwidth compared to its predecessor, HBM3E.

This announcement is interpreted as a signal that the collaboration between Micron and NVIDIA remains strong, and it is expected to contribute to strengthening competitiveness in the memory semiconductor market. Micron plans to solidify its position in the AI and data center markets through the mass production of HBM4.
