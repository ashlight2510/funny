Hyundai Green Food, First in the Food Industry to Obtain 'Nutritional Diagnosis' Patent

Hyundai Green Food has registered a patent for its healthcare service called 'Greeting Nutritional Diagnosis', making it the first in the food industry. This service provides consumers with a personalized nutritional diagnosis report based on their health information input, along with recommendations for suitable meal plans and ingredients. With this, Hyundai Green Food plans to actively expand its healthcare business area.

This news signifies more than just a service launch. Through this patent, Hyundai Green Food has strengthened its competitiveness in health management and the food market, laying the groundwork for providing more personalized services to consumers. This is expected to break down the boundaries between healthcare and the food industry, contributing to the creation of new business models.

Why Background Matters

Hyundai Green Food has been operating the healthcare service 'Greeting Nutritional Diagnosis' since 2022, and this patent acquisition is based on the data and research results accumulated over time. With the rising societal interest in health and wellness, the need for personalized nutritional management has emerged, prompting Hyundai Green Food to develop this service.
