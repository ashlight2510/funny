Seoul Moon: Year-Round Urban Views from April, What Questions Remain?

March 30, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Enjoy the cityscape all year round... 'Seoul Moon' operates year-round from April Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun The tethered gas balloon 'Seoul Moon', which rises and falls over Yeouido, will begin year-round operations from April 1. According to the Seoul Tourism Foundation, 'Seoul Moon' will abolish its regular Monday closure starting in April and will operate daily from noon to 10 PM, regardless of the day of the week. During peak seasons (April to June, September to November), it will be available from 10 AM as before. The reservation system has also been revamped.

Enjoy the cityscape all year round... 'Seoul Moon' operates year-round from April

The 'Seoul Moon', which allows you to enjoy the beauty of the city from above Yeouido, will operate year-round starting April 1. The previous regular Monday closure has been abolished, and it will operate daily from noon to 10 PM, with availability from 10 AM during peak seasons. Additionally, the reservation system has been improved to enhance accessibility for foreign tourists. The Seoul Tourism Foundation emphasizes that these changes will enrich the tourism experience.

This news signifies more than just a change in the operation of a tourist attraction. The year-round operation and expanded reservation channels are expected to provide greater convenience for tourists visiting Seoul and positively impact the city's tourism industry. In particular, the changes to 'Seoul Moon', which has a high proportion of foreign tourists, will serve as an important opportunity to strengthen Seoul's global tourism competitiveness.

Key Summary

  • Change in Operating Hours: 'Seoul Moon' will operate year-round starting April 1, with the previous Monday closure abolished.
  • Improved Reservation System: The number of reservation channels has increased from 7 to 10, enhancing accessibility for foreign tourists.
  • Alternative Programs Available: In case of bad weather, an alternative program called 'Seoul Moon Ground Tour' will be provided.
  • High User Satisfaction: A survey of 1,500 passengers recorded high satisfaction and willingness to recommend.
  • Impact on the Tourism Industry: This could serve to differentiate the tourism experience in Seoul and enhance its global competitiveness.

Why It's Important to Consider the Background

Since its opening in 2024, 'Seoul Moon' has quickly attracted many visitors. The proportion of foreign tourists has reached 40%, indicating that Seoul is establishing itself as an international tourist destination. However, the previous Monday closure caused inconvenience for tourists, prompting the shift to year-round operation. Additionally, the improvement of the reservation system can be seen as an effort to align with global tourism trends.
