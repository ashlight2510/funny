Who are the people who illuminated Incheon Airport, ranked 3rd in the world?

Incheon Airport recorded 74.07 million international passengers and 2.95 million tons of cargo last year, marking its rise to the 3rd largest airport in the world. To commemorate this achievement, Incheon International Airport Corporation held the 'Incheon Airport Awards' and presented the 'Incheon Airport Award' to resident organizations and employees who contributed to airport operations. Notably, the dedication of volunteers like Jang Jong-ye was highlighted, and various companies were recognized for their excellent performance in customer experience and safety management.

This article shines a light on the achievements of Incheon Airport and the efforts of the people behind it, illustrating how the airport has positioned itself in global competition. These achievements carry significance beyond mere numbers and suggest directions for future development.
