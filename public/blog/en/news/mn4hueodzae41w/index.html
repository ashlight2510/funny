Yeongdeok Wind Turbine Fire Accident: What Lies Behind the Truth?

On the 23rd, a tragic accident occurred at a wind turbine in Yeongdeok, North Gyeongsang Province, resulting in the deaths of three subcontracted workers. The representative of the subcontracting company claims that "the workers were not engaged in any work that could cause a fire on the day of the accident," raising questions about the cause of the incident. This event highlights serious issues regarding safety management in the wind power industry and the lives of workers.

This accident serves as a reminder of the risks associated with maintenance work on wind turbines and is likely to spark discussions about future safety management and regulations. In particular, the question of how the safety of subcontracted workers is ensured is something our society must deeply consider.
