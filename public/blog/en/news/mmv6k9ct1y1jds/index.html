Most of the country is rainy... Fine dust in the metropolitan area is 'bad'

According to the Korea Meteorological Administration's forecast, rain is expected nationwide on Wednesday, the 18th, with snow anticipated in the northeastern Gyeonggi Province and Gangwon Province. In particular, the fine dust concentration in the metropolitan area and Chungnam region has reached 'bad' levels, prompting the implementation of emergency reduction measures for fine dust. The amount of rain will vary by region, but it is expected to be 5-10mm in the metropolitan area and Gangwon Province, and 10-30mm in the southern coastal areas of Gyeongnam and Jeju Island. This will reduce visibility and make roads slippery, necessitating caution for traffic safety.

This article does not merely stop at weather forecasting; it also highlights the health issues related to fine dust, traffic safety, and the importance of environmental policies. The fine dust situation in the metropolitan area is particularly noteworthy as it directly affects the daily lives of many citizens.

Fine dust can cause various health problems, including respiratory diseases, allergies, and cardiovascular diseases. It is especially dangerous for children, the elderly, and individuals with underlying health conditions. Therefore, the current fine dust warning and rain forecast are directly related to citizens' health, necessitating immediate attention.
