Shall we look for yellow seasonal ingredients today?

Recently, a new lifestyle called 'seasonal core' has gained attention among the younger generation. This involves enjoying foods that are in season, experiencing the solar terms, and feeling a connection with nature, which has become an increasingly important educational element, especially for children. This article emphasizes various ways to help children physically experience the changes of the seasons and the importance of seasonal education. The core idea is to cultivate a sense of nature through activities with family, going beyond simply finding seasonal ingredients.

This approach helps children directly experience the changes of the seasons and deepen their understanding of their relationship with nature. It emphasizes the importance of utilizing traditional markets or local foods rather than large supermarkets, allowing children to gain vivid information about seasonal foods along with various social experiences.

Key Summary

  • 'Seasonal core' is a lifestyle that enjoys foods in season and experiences the solar terms.
  • It is important for children to develop seasonal sensitivity, which can start with seasonal education at the dining table.
  • Finding seasonal ingredients together with children using traditional markets or local foods is effective.
  • Seasonal education should occur naturally through everyday experiences rather than simple memorization.
  • Utilizing materials like picture books can enrich seasonal education.

Key Scenes

  • Importance of seasonal food: Experiencing nature's changes through seasonal ingredients has high educational value.
  • Family activities: Conversations at the dining table and participation in cooking leave a strong impression on children.
  • Social experiences: Engaging with vendors at traditional markets helps children learn social skills.
  • Approach to seasonal education: It is important to learn about the solar terms in a fun way through activities.
  • Utilizing picture books: Picture books related to the solar terms can enhance children's understanding.

Reasons to Consider the Background

In modern society, children find it difficult to feel the changes of the seasons. With the same fruits and vegetables being sold year-round in supermarkets and increased indoor living, the connection to nature has weakened. The emergence of the concept of 'seasonal core' is part of an effort to help children physically experience the changes of nature and cultivate richer sensitivity.

Why It Matters

Experiencing seasonal foods and the solar terms has a significant impact on children's emotional and social development, going beyond simple education. It plays an important role in helping children form relationships with nature and develop healthy eating habits. Additionally, these experiences strengthen family bonds and contribute to children feeling respect and love for nature.

A Natural Approach is Needed

In my view, seasonal education should not merely involve the transmission of textbook knowledge but should allow children to have direct experiences with nature. For example, going to the market with a child to select ingredients can provide significant meaning beyond just shopping. This approach, which uses nature as a learning environment, will leave a deeper impression on children.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One aspect not covered in this article is that not all families have access to traditional markets or local foods. In urban areas, such experiences may be more limited, and alternative methods or resources should also be considered. Furthermore, seasonal education may not have the same effect on all children. A tailored approach based on each child's personality and environment is necessary.

Points to Check Now

  • Seasonal activities to do with family: Plan activities that you can do together with your child in advance.
  • Create a seasonal food calendar: Make a calendar with your child that lists seasonal foods.
  • Visit the library: Finding and reading related picture books together is also a good method.

FAQ

Q: Why is seasonal food important?
A: Seasonal food is the most delicious and nutritious during its time, and it is an important element connected to the changes in nature.

Q: How can I start seasonal education?
A: You can naturally start by going to the market with your family or cooking foods that align with the solar terms in your daily life.

Q: What are the effects of using picture books for seasonal education?
A: Picture books can introduce the solar terms in a fun way in a familiar format for children, enhancing understanding and sparking interest.

Q: What should I do if it's hard to go to a traditional market?
A: As an alternative, you can consider purchasing seasonal ingredients from local food direct sales or online.
