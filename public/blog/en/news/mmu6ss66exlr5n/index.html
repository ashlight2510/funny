Korea Exchange postpones pre and after market operation by two and a half months due to backlash from the securities industry

The Korea Exchange has postponed the opening schedule of the pre and after market, originally set for June 29, to September 14. This decision reflects the concerns of the securities industry regarding system construction, and the exchange has stated its intention to secure sufficient testing time to enhance system completeness. Additionally, the operating hours of the pre-market will be shortened to 7:00 AM to 7:50 AM as a measure to alleviate the burden on the securities industry.

This decision is not merely a schedule delay but demonstrates the need for close cooperation and adjustment of interests between the Korea Exchange and the securities industry. In particular, if ETF trading and short selling become possible in the pre and after market, it is expected that the liquidity supply burden on securities firms will increase. Such changes must be carefully considered in relation to various factors associated with market volatility, making it a matter that both investors and practitioners should closely monitor.

  • Increased burden on securities firms expected with the possibility of ETF and short selling trading.
  • There is also backlash from labor groups, with a resolution meeting scheduled.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The pre and after market is a system that allows trading of stocks before and after the exchange opens, enhancing market liquidity and providing investors with more options. However, this decision arose from the securities industry's lack of preparation and inadequacies in system construction. In particular, amid competition with NextTrade, the exchange is under pressure to provide more trading possibilities and stability.

Why It Matters

This decision goes beyond a simple schedule change, highlighting the need for cooperation and interest adjustment between the Korea Exchange and securities firms. In particular, the potential for ETF trading in the pre and after market could provide investors with more opportunities but could also impose significant burdens on securities firms. Therefore, the impact of these changes on the market is expected to be substantial, and investors need to prepare accordingly.

Points of Interest

In my view, the most important aspect of this incident is the communication between the exchange and securities firms. If each party's opinions are not adequately reflected in system construction and operation, it could ultimately undermine market credibility. In particular, the backlash from labor groups further highlights the absence of this communication. I believe it is crucial for the exchange to not only build the system but also actively reflect the voices of stakeholders.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One overlooked aspect of this decision is the concern over increased volatility in the pre and after market. While the exchange has announced the introduction of static VI (volatility dampening mechanism), a concrete strategy for managing market reactions and volatility is necessary. Additionally, it should not be overlooked that securities firms will face additional burdens, such as server expansion.
