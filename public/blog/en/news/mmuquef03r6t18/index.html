This provides a clear guideline for content usage and serves as a reminder for readers to be aware of copyright issues.

This notice is particularly significant in the context of the rapid increase in digital content consumption. Many people easily share content through social media, but copyright issues are often overlooked. Therefore, Yonhap News's action can be seen as an important step to raise awareness about copyright and maintain a healthy relationship between content creators and consumers.

In an age overflowing with digital content, copyright issues have become more important than ever.
