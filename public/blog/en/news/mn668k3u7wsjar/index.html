11 Students Charged Without Detention in Dongduk Women's University Occupation Protest: What Are the Key Issues?

March 26, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: ‘Dongduk Women's University Occupation Protest’ 11 Students Indicted Without Detention Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun Students at Dongduk Women's University who staged an occupation protest against the school's discussion of transitioning to coeducation have been referred to trial. The Seoul Northern District Prosecutor's Office announced on the 25th that 11 individuals, including the president of the student council, have been indicted without detention on charges of obstruction of business, joint refusal to vacate, joint confinement, and property damage. Some students claimed that the transition to coeducation was being pushed unilaterally, occupying the main building and the 100th Anniversary Memorial Hall from November 11 to December 3, 2024, resulting in damages estimated at about 4.6 billion won. The university initially filed a lawsuit against the students but later withdrew it.

This incident carries significance beyond a mere protest. The students assert that the significant decision to transition to coeducation is being pursued unilaterally without gathering student opinions, revealing a distrust in the decision-making process of the school.

Key Summary

  • Students opposing the transition to coeducation at Dongduk Women's University staged an occupation protest, resulting in 11 being indicted without detention.
  • The students protested the school's unilateral decision, claiming it caused damages of 4.6 billion won.
  • Although the school withdrew its lawsuit, the police investigation continued, leading to the indictments.
  • The Student Union criticized the lack of student opinion collection, deeming the indictment decision inappropriate.

Key Scenes

  • Student Resistance: Students staged an occupation protest, claiming the school's discussion on transitioning to coeducation was unfair.
  • Legal Procedures: While 11 were indicted without detention, this illustrates the potential legal consequences of student protests.
  • School's Stance: The school claimed damages and pursued legal action but later withdrew the lawsuit.
  • Student Opinion Collection Issues: As pointed out by the Student Union, the failure to adequately reflect student opinions is at the heart of the controversy.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Dongduk Women's University has faced criticism for not sufficiently gathering student opinions during the public discussion process regarding the transition to coeducation. Students argue that such a decision could lead to significant changes in the academic structure, necessitating thorough discussion and deliberation. The school announced the transition to coeducation in December last year, accepting the recommendations of a public discussion committee, but students still feel their opposing views have not been adequately considered.

Why It Matters

This incident exemplifies the need for transparency and fairness in decision-making processes within educational institutions, extending beyond a simple student protest. Students have taken action to assert their rights, which could lead to important discussions about democratic decision-making methods in universities in the future. Additionally, the legal indictment's impact on student protests may serve as a reference for similar situations in other universities.

My Perspective

In my view, the core of this incident serves as a reminder of the importance of student voices. The school must put in more effort to gather student opinions and build trust through this process. It is a legitimate right for students to express their views through protests, and creating an environment where this process can occur smoothly is crucial.

Hidden Variables to Consider

A point that may be overlooked in this incident is that the students' protests are not merely expressions of opposition but also manifestations of their anxiety and distrust. The psychological impact of the school's decisions on students can also serve as an important variable. There needs to be contemplation on how students can address these grievances.

Current Points to Check

It is important to closely observe how discussions regarding the student opinion collection process and decision-making transparency are progressing through this incident. In the event of similar occurrences in the future, it will be necessary to monitor how students respond and how the school reacts.
