Tips to Reduce Dining Out Costs for Saving on Food Expenses

March 16, 2026

Practical Tips for Reducing Dining Out Costs and Saving on Food Expenses

Recently, the cost of dining out has surged, placing a burden on many households' living expenses. While dining out is a convenient way to enjoy delicious food, it inevitably leads to higher spending. Reducing dining out costs is one of the most effective ways to cut food expenses, allowing you to allocate funds to other necessary expenditures. This article introduces various tips and methods for reducing dining out costs, helping you manage your dining expenses more smartly.

Positive Impact of Reducing Dining Out Costs on Living Expenses

Cutting down on dining out has a positive effect on overall living expenses. By reducing the frequency of dining out and developing a habit of cooking at home, you can maintain healthy eating habits while also benefiting financially. For example, if you cook at home instead of dining out twice a week, you can save over 100,000 won per month on average. This saved amount can be used for other essential expenses or savings.

Health Benefits of Cooking at Home

Cooking at home allows you to use fresh ingredients, which can lead to better nutrient intake. When dining out, there is a higher chance of choosing greasy or high-calorie foods, but at home, you can prepare healthier meals.

  • Execution Tip: Plan simple pasta for the weekend, and for weekday dinners, consider salads with grilled chicken breast, easy fried rice, or kimchi stew.

Planning Dining Out: Adjusting Frequency and Budget

Adjusting Dining Out Frequency

Reducing the frequency of dining out is crucial for saving on dining costs. By creating a weekly dining plan, you can control the number of times you eat out and stick to your budget. For example, you could plan to dine out once a week and cook at home for the remaining six days.

  • Checkpoint: Set a weekly dining out frequency and plan your cooking menu accordingly.

Setting a Dining Out Budget

Setting a dining out budget is very important. If you set a monthly dining out budget of 200,000 won and divide it weekly, you would have a budget of about 50,000 won per week.

Tips to Prevent Exceeding the Budget

  • Pre-determine your menu when dining out

  • Avoid expensive drinks or additional orders

  • Execution Tip: Decide on your menu in advance before dining out, and be mindful not to exceed your budget.

Reducing Dining Out Costs by Utilizing Discounts and Promotions

Using Coupons and Discount Apps

Using coupons is a useful way to reduce dining costs. You can easily find coupons through various discount apps. Notable discount apps include ‘Coupang Eats’, ‘Baedal Minjok’, and ‘Yogiyo’.

  • Checkpoint: Always check the expiration date of coupons provided by discount apps.

Finding Restaurant Promotions

Seasonal promotions or discounts on specific days can greatly help in reducing dining costs. For instance, if a certain restaurant offers a 50% discount every Tuesday, taking advantage of this can lead to significant savings.

  • Execution Tip: Research the promotional information of your frequently visited restaurants in advance.

Considering Alternatives to Dining Out: Takeout and Group Dining

Utilizing Takeout and Delivery Services

Takeout and delivery services are another way to reduce dining costs. You can enjoy your favorite foods at a lower price than dining out. Affordable delivery apps include ‘Baedal Minjok’ and ‘Yogiyo’.

  • Checkpoint: Be aware that additional costs may arise with takeout or delivery, so check the final price before ordering.

Group Dining with Friends and Family

Group dining can be economical as costs can be shared. Dining out with friends or family allows you to experience a variety of foods while reducing the financial burden.

  • Execution Tip: When dining out with four people, split the cost evenly and share the menu to try different dishes.

Checklist: Action Items for Saving on Dining Out Costs

  • Reduce dining out frequency
  • Set a budget
  • Check for discount coupons and apps
  • Research takeout and delivery services
  • Plan for group dining

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. What is the first thing I should do to reduce dining costs?
    The first step is to reduce the frequency of dining out and create a weekly dining plan.

  2. Where can I find discount coupons?
    You can easily find coupons on various discount apps and websites.

  3. What should I be careful about when cooking at home instead of dining out?
    It's important to use healthy ingredients and plan your menu in advance to save on food expenses.

By implementing the methods presented in this article, you can effectively reduce dining costs and utilize the saved amount for other essential expenditures. Start by planning your dining out and setting a budget right away.
