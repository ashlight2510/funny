Trump hopes for participation from Germany, Canada, and Australia in Hormuz Coalition

Recently, it has been revealed that U.S. President Donald Trump has requested the participation of several countries, including Germany, Canada, and Australia, in a multinational coalition aimed at ensuring the safety of the Strait of Hormuz. This coalition plans to conduct escort operations for commercial vessels by dispatching warships to the Strait of Hormuz, with the goal of suppressing rising oil prices amid escalating tensions with Iran. However, some countries have shown negative reactions to the Trump administration's request, raising questions about the feasibility of this coalition.

This news suggests extensive implications that go beyond the mere formation of a military alliance, impacting international politics and economics. In particular, the Strait of Hormuz is a major route for global oil transportation, and military tensions in this area could have direct effects on the global energy market.

Why Background Matters

The Strait of Hormuz is a region through which about 20% of the world's oil transportation occurs, and military tensions are increasing as conflicts with Iran deepen. The Trump administration aims to form a multinational coalition to suppress rising oil prices and counter Iran's military threats. However, several countries, including Germany and Australia, have shown negative stances toward the U.S. request, making the coalition's feasibility uncertain.

Why It Matters

This issue illustrates the complex dynamics of international politics beyond merely forming a military coalition. The willingness of various countries to participate may depend not only on military cooperation but also on economic interests and political positions. In particular, the safety of the Strait of Hormuz directly impacts the global energy market, making this matter a focal point for the international community.

Personal Comment

In my view, this attempt to form a coalition reflects the various political pressures faced by the Trump administration. While the U.S. seeks to alleviate military tensions through cooperation with allies amid ongoing conflicts with Iran, the responses from various countries have been lukewarm. Notably, Germany's statement, "This is not our war," reveals European dissatisfaction with U.S. foreign policy.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Although some countries have shown negative reactions to the Trump administration's request, it is difficult to predict how their positions might change if Iran's military threats continue. Additionally, the formation of a military coalition is closely related to economic interests, not just military cooperation.
