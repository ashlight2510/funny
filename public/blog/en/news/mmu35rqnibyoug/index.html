Gyeonggi Content Agency Launches '10th Gyeonggi Hidden Writer' Call for Works to Discover New Writers

The '10th Gyeonggi Hidden Writer' call for works aimed at discovering new writers and revitalizing the local publishing industry in Gyeonggi Province has begun. This call is targeted at residents of Gyeonggi Province who do not have a history of publishing a standalone book, and it will run until April 30. The selected 8 works will be published with a support fund of 5 million won. Over the past 9 years, this program has supported 143 writers and published 71 books.

The significance of this call goes beyond simply discovering new writers; it aims to invigorate the local publishing ecosystem and broaden the base of reading culture. In particular, works published with the support of the Gyeonggi Content Agency will be distributed in major bookstores and online platforms nationwide, and various promotional opportunities such as book talks will also be provided to the writers.

Key Summary

  • Call Overview: '10th Gyeonggi Hidden Writer' call for works, running until April 30.
  • Target Audience: Residents of Gyeonggi Province with no history of standalone book publication.
  • Support Details: 5 million won support and publication for the selected 8 works.
  • Achievements: Supported 143 writers and published 71 books over the past 9 years.
  • Promotional Support: Various events such as book talks after publication.

Why It Matters

This call is significant not only for discovering new writers but also for fostering local culture and expanding the base of reading. Gyeonggi Province, with its large population and diverse culture, will see considerable positive impacts from such support in the community. Additionally, by providing tangible economic support and publishing opportunities to writers, it will contribute to enriching the literary ecosystem.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Since its inception in 2017, the 'Gyeonggi Hidden Writer' call has focused on discovering hidden writers in Gyeonggi Province and bringing their works to the public. Works published in previous calls have received significant attention from readers and critics, and particularly, 'Life is a Library' was selected as a recommended book by former President Moon Jae-in, generating considerable buzz. Such successful cases heighten expectations for this call.

Personal Perspective

In my view, this call will serve as an important stepping stone not just for supporting writers but also for revitalizing the local community and culture. The inclusion of promotional support after publication allows writers to reach a broader audience with their voices. This holds significance beyond merely publishing books and will enhance communication between writers and readers.

Hidden Variables to Watch

One notable aspect of this call is that only writers with no history of standalone book publication can apply. While this aims to provide more opportunities for new writers, it also means that those with prior publishing experience are excluded. This raises concerns about the potential lack of diversity in voices and styles represented from within Gyeonggi Province.

Current Checkpoints

  • Eligibility: Residents of Gyeonggi Province with no history of standalone book publication.
  • Application Deadline: Until April 30.
  • Submission Method: Details available on the official Gyeonggi Content Agency website.

FAQ

Q: What are the requirements to participate in the call?
A: Anyone who is a resident of Gyeonggi Province and has no history of standalone book publication can participate.

Q: What support will selected writers receive?
A: Selected writers will receive a support fund of 5 million won along with publication and promotional support.

Q: How will the submitted works be evaluated?
A: Submitted works will undergo evaluation, and winners will be announced in May.

This call presents an opportunity to bring positive change to the literary ecosystem in Gyeonggi Province. Readers can look forward to the diverse voices of hidden writers through this initiative.
