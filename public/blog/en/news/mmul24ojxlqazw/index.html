Is it the end or evolution… ‘Software as a Service’ shaken by Agentic AI

The impact of recent advancements in artificial intelligence (AI) on the Software as a Service (SaaS) industry is being seriously discussed. In particular, there are analyses suggesting that AI is lowering the entry barriers for software development, putting existing software service models in jeopardy. With the emergence of AI tools like Claude CoWork, an environment is being created where companies can create and use software themselves instead of subscribing to it. This has led to a sharp decline in stock prices in the industry and a series of IPO delays, exacerbating the confusion.

In this situation, experts are voicing opinions that AI will not replace all software. Instead, there are also forecasts that AI could lead to the advancement of the software industry. This article summarizes the core of this issue, its implications, and future directions.

Key Summary

  • The advancement of AI poses a threat to the Software as a Service (SaaS) industry.
  • AI tools like Claude CoWork are lowering entry barriers for software development.
  • The stock prices of software service companies are plummeting, and IPOs are being postponed.
  • Concerns that AI will replace software coexist with forecasts that it will promote advancement.

Key Scenes

  • Software development has become easier due to advancements in AI.
  • The business model of Software as a Service is under threat.
  • There is potential for the evolution of existing software services through AI integration.
  • Experts argue that AI will not replace all software.
  • There are expectations for the advancement of the software service industry.

Why We Need to Look at the Background

The software service industry rapidly grew in the early 2000s with a cloud-based subscription model. Companies preferred this model due to reduced initial cost burdens and ease of maintenance. However, recent advancements in AI technology are challenging this subscription model. In particular, agentic AI tools like Claude CoWork allow users to create software themselves, threatening the business models of SaaS companies. As a result, many companies are experiencing difficulties such as stock price declines and IPO delays.

Why It Matters

This issue goes beyond a mere crisis in software services; it demonstrates the impact of technological advancements on industrial structures. As AI democratizes software development, companies are increasingly required to reassess their existing business models. Additionally, this could significantly impact professionals in the IT industry, as the nature of jobs changes and adaptation to new technologies becomes essential.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the crux of this situation is not 'replacement' but 'collaboration.' AI is not replacing software; rather, a new paradigm where humans and AI work together may emerge. For instance, if companies can use AI tools to enhance efficiency, this could even create new job opportunities. Therefore, we should seize the opportunity to rediscover the value of software services through collaboration with AI.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Many people think that AI will replace all software, but they overlook the fact that personnel for maintenance and verification are still necessary. While AI can write code, it is up to humans to ensure that the code works correctly and to make necessary adjustments. How the roles of AI and humans harmoniously coexist in these areas will be an important challenge moving forward.

    • Many cases show that AI tools contribute to increasing work efficiency, and research is being conducted on how companies utilize them.
