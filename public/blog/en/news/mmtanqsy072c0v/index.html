President Lee Jae-myung's Dinner with First-term Lawmakers: The Need for Stable Reforms

On March 16, President Lee Jae-myung emphasized the need to accommodate the public's demands for reform and to pursue stable and useful reforms during a dinner with first-term lawmakers of the Democratic Party. He stated that as the ruling party, it is essential to act with humility and seriousness to meet the expectations of the people, and that more effort is needed for the success of the government. This dinner served as an opportunity for lawmakers to share their opinions on national issues and local livelihoods, with the president primarily listening.

President Lee particularly mentioned the real estate issue, stressing the importance of stabilizing housing prices. Such remarks can be interpreted as part of efforts to secure public trust in the government's policy direction.
