Civil Society Calls to Reject Deployment to Hormuz

Recently, civil society organizations held a press conference in Seoul to condemn the U.S. attack on Iran and urged the rejection of the demand for deployment to the Strait of Hormuz. They stated, “The invasion of Iran by the U.S. and Israel cannot be justified by any means,” demanding an immediate halt to the war. In contrast, voices from the far-right faction called for government deployment. This confrontation is expected to significantly impact South Korea's foreign policy amid the complex situation in the Middle East.

This issue goes beyond a simple declaration; it reveals the various interests within South Korean society. The conflict between civil society organizations and the far-right faction raises serious discussions about the country's foreign policy and the safety of its citizens.
