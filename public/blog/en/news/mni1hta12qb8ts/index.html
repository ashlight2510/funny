5 Mistakes to Avoid When Using AI Image Generators

AI image generators are powerful tools that simplify creative tasks. Both individuals and businesses can easily create desired images, but beginners often make mistakes that prevent them from achieving the desired results. This article introduces five mistakes to avoid when using AI image generators and provides actionable tips to overcome them.

1. Distorted Results Due to Unclear Prompt Settings

AI generates results based on the input information, so clear and specific prompts are essential.

Action Tip

  • Use specific keywords and phrases when setting prompts. For example, instead of saying "dog," input "a cute miniature pinscher sitting on a blue background" to generate a more accurate image.

Checkpoint

  • Avoid vague expressions and include necessary details. Describing "a river flowing through a green forest with various flowers" is more effective than just saying "nature."

2. Choosing the Wrong Tool for the Purpose

Each AI tool has different characteristics and strengths, so making the wrong choice can lead to inefficient results.

Action Tip

  • Research and compare various AI image generators. Consider the features, intended use, and pricing of each tool to choose the one that best suits your needs.

Checkpoint

  • Understand the differences between free and paid tools, and refer to user reviews. Free tools may have limited features, while paid tools can offer more options and functionalities.

3. Overlooking Copyright Issues

Ignoring the copyright of generated images can lead to legal problems. AI image generators often base their outputs on existing images or works, so checking copyright is essential.

Action Tip

  • Always check the copyright conditions of the images you intend to use. Review the licensing information provided by the image generator, and consider additional licenses if commercial use is required.

Checkpoint

  • Additional licenses may be necessary for commercial use, so be sure to verify in advance. For example, some tools may be free for personal use but charge fees for commercial use.

4. Lack of Feedback Incorporation

Failing to incorporate feedback makes improvement difficult. AI image generators are tools that complement human creativity and imagination, so gathering opinions from others is important.

Action Tip

  • Show the generated images to others and gather their feedback. You can discover and improve the shortcomings of the image through feedback from friends or colleagues.

Checkpoint

  • Don't fear criticism; actively seek out areas for improvement. For instance, feedback like "I wish this color were a bit brighter" can help you modify the image.

5. Deterioration of Output Quality

Low-quality images diminish credibility. It's important to generate high-resolution images and consider post-processing work.

Action Tip

  • Choose high-resolution options when generating images and perform post-processing if necessary. You can enhance the final image using Photoshop or other image editing programs.

Checkpoint

  • Always check the final quality of the image and make adjustments if needed. For example, if the generated image appears blurry, you should increase the resolution.

Checklist: Mistakes to Avoid When Using AI Image Generators

  1. Have you set the prompt clearly?
  2. Did you choose the appropriate tool?
  3. Have you checked copyright issues?
  4. Did you incorporate feedback?
  5. Did you check the quality of the output?

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. I'm using an AI image generator for the first time; which tool do you recommend?

    • For beginners, I recommend Tool A, which is a user-friendly free tool. It's suitable for learning basic functionalities.

  2. How do I check the copyright of generated images?

    • Check the licensing information provided by each image generator or look for copyright conditions on the official website of the tool.

  3. What is the best way to set prompts?

    • It's important to use specific and clear keywords that include details. For example, describe it as "a dog playing on the beach."

By avoiding the mistakes mentioned above when using AI image generators, you can achieve more effective and satisfying results. Start by setting your prompts clearly!
