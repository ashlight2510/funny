Jeonnam Province Supports Rent for Young Non-Homeowners: The Key Insights I Gained

March 16, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Jeonnam Province to Support Rent for Homeless Youths... Up to 2.4 Million Won for One Year

Jeollanam-do has announced the implementation of the 'Housing Cost Support Project for Young Job Seekers' to alleviate the housing cost burden on young people. This project will provide up to 200,000 won per month for a maximum of 12 months to homeless youths aged 18 to 45 who are registered residents in Jeonnam, totaling up to 2.4 million won. Eligibility for application includes youths who have been employed for a certain period or are operating a business in Jeonnam, with income criteria also established. This initiative is designed to help young people settle in the region economically and stably.

Summary of the Article

Jeollanam-do is launching a program to support homeless youths with housing costs of up to 2.4 million won.

Key Points

  • Eligibility: Homeless youths aged 18 to 45 registered as residents in Jeonnam.
  • Support Details: Monthly support of 200,000 won for up to 12 months for those living in jeonse or monthly rent.
  • Application Criteria: Youths who have been employed for at least 3 out of the last 6 months or business owners operating for over 6 months.
  • Income Criteria: Household income must be at or below 150% of the median income.
  • Application Period: Applications can be submitted at local administrative welfare centers from March 17 to 28.

Why This Issue is Important

This support program is a crucial policy to alleviate housing instability among the youth and stimulate the local economy. Creating an environment where young people can settle stably is essential for addressing the issue of population decline. By reducing the housing cost burden on the youth, it will help them establish deeper roots in the community, contributing to the long-term sustainability of the local economy and society.

If you are a homeless youth residing in Jeonnam, it is advisable to thoroughly check the eligibility and procedures for this support project and prepare the necessary documents in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Do I have to reside in Jeonnam to receive the support?
A: Yes, the support is only provided to youths registered as residents in Jeollanam-do.

Q: When will the support funds be disbursed?
A: The funds will be disbursed after an evaluation following the application. The exact disbursement schedule will be provided during the application process.

Q: Are both monthly rent and jeonse supported?
A: Yes, both jeonse and monthly rent are eligible for support. However, there are specific conditions for each, so verification is necessary.

Q: How do I apply?
A: You can apply by visiting the administrative welfare center in your residential area.

In conclusion, youths residing in Jeonnam should not miss the opportunity to reduce their housing cost burden and live stably through this support project.
