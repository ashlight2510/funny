Year-End Tax Refund Tips: Key Items You Might Overlook

March 30, 2026

Tips for Getting Refunds from Year-End Tax Settlement: A Summary of Easily Overlooked Items

Year-end tax settlement is an opportunity for many people to receive refunds each year. However, there are many easily overlooked items, and if you do not prepare properly, you may incur losses. In this article, we will summarize useful information from the basic understanding of year-end tax settlement to easily overlooked deduction items and a preparation checklist.

Basic Understanding of Year-End Tax Settlement

What is Year-End Tax Settlement?

Year-end tax settlement is the process of settling taxes on income earned throughout the year. If the taxes paid exceed the actual income, you may be eligible for a refund. Refunds provide economic benefits regarding taxes, and if utilized well, they can significantly aid household finances.

Major Steps in Year-End Tax Settlement

Year-end tax settlement can be broadly divided into three steps:

  1. Income Confirmation: Confirm your annual income.
  2. Organizing Deduction Items: Organize deductible items and prepare necessary documents.
  3. Deciding on the Reporting Method: Choose a reporting method and file your taxes.

Through this process, you can maximize your refund amount.

Importance of Year-End Tax Settlement

Year-end tax settlement provides opportunities for tax refunds, bringing economic benefits. Paying less tax and receiving refunds can greatly contribute to the financial health of a household. Therefore, it should not be neglected.

Easily Overlooked Deduction Items

Medical Expense Deduction: An Easily Overlooked Item

The medical expense deduction allows you to deduct a certain amount for medical expenses incurred by yourself and your family. Receipt retention is essential. This includes hospital fees, medication costs, etc.

Checkpoint

  • Organize receipts to avoid losing them.
  • Medical expenses must exceed 1 million won annually to qualify for deductions.

Education Expense Deduction: Don’t Miss Out on Children’s Education Expenses

The education expense deduction allows you to deduct expenses related to your children's education. This includes tuition fees, textbook costs, etc., and requires an education expense payment certificate as necessary documentation.

Execution Tips

  • If your child attends two different academies, you must submit receipts from each academy separately.
  • Be careful to avoid duplicate deductions.

Donation Deduction: Donation Receipts Required

The donation deduction allows you to deduct taxes for donations made for public interest purposes. Donation receipts are needed, and the deduction rate may vary depending on the type of donation.

Checkpoint

  • All amounts donated to charitable organizations can be deducted, but there may be restrictions on donations to specific institutions.
  • You must submit donation receipts.

Year-End Tax Settlement Preparation Checklist

When preparing for year-end tax settlement, refer to the following checklist.

  • Prepare income documents
  • Confirm deduction items
  • Organize necessary documents
  • Check reporting deadlines

This checklist will help you prepare thoroughly.

Calculating Year-End Tax Settlement Refund Amount

Calculating the refund amount involves determining the basic tax amount and then applying deduction items to arrive at the final tax amount. The basic tax amount varies based on annual income, and applying deduction items determines the refund amount.

Understanding Through Examples

For example, if your annual income is 50 million won and you apply medical expenses of 1 million won and education expenses of 500,000 won as deductions, the tax amount decreases, increasing the refund amount you can receive.

Execution Tips

  • Learn the calculation method through actual cases and maximize the use of deduction items.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) about Year-End Tax Settlement

  1. What are the most commonly overlooked items in year-end tax settlement?

    • Medical and education expense deductions are often overlooked. It is important to keep receipts well and check the requirements for each deduction item.

  2. What are the tips for maximizing tax refunds?

    • It is advisable to check all possible deduction items and prepare necessary documents in advance. Be especially careful not to miss out on donation and medical expense deductions.

  3. When is the reporting deadline for year-end tax settlement?

    • Generally, you must report between January 15 and February 15 each year. The exact deadline is announced by the tax office each year, so it is necessary to check.

Year-End Tax Settlement and Recent Tax Law Changes

Recent changes in tax law can significantly impact year-end tax settlement. When new tax laws are applied, deduction items may be adjusted, which can also change the refund amount.

Checkpoint

  • Adjustments to deduction items may be necessary according to the changed tax laws.
  • Always check the application period of new tax laws.
