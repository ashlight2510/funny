BTS Returns as a Complete Group After 4 Years… ‘Arirang’ Echoes at Gwanghwamun

On the evening of the 21st, BTS held a performance at Gwanghwamun in Seoul, marking their return as a complete group after about four years. Under the title 'BTS Comeback Live: ARIRANG', they showcased a total of 12 songs, including tracks from their fifth studio album 'Arirang' and global hits. The performance was broadcast live to 190 countries worldwide, and the members expressed their heartfelt emotions about reuniting with fans at such a historic location. Notably, the first song 'Body to Body' sampled the traditional Korean folk music 'Arirang', marking a meaningful start.

This performance signified a new beginning for BTS, as the members returned in a more mature form after overcoming their hiatus. Through the concert, they emphasized communication with fans and heightened expectations for their future activities.

Key Highlights

  • BTS returned as a complete group after 4 years and performed at Gwanghwamun.
  • They showcased a total of 12 songs, including tracks from their fifth studio album 'Arirang' and hit songs.
  • The performance was broadcast live to 190 countries, connecting with global fans.
  • The members shared their thoughts during the hiatus and their future direction.
  • The first song 'Body to Body' sampled 'Arirang', representing a modern reinterpretation of traditional Korean music.

Why the Background Matters

BTS took the stage as a complete group for the first time in about 3 years and 9 months since 'Yet to Come in Busan' held in October 2022. During this period, each member focused on individual activities but made efforts to maintain their connection with fans. This concert served as a celebration of the release of their fifth studio album 'Arirang', which modernly reinterprets traditional Korean music and serves as an important opportunity to introduce it to the world.

Why It’s Important

BTS's comeback goes beyond simply providing an enjoyable time for fans; it plays a crucial role in promoting Korean culture and music worldwide. Particularly, this album titled 'Arirang' embodies their identity and presents a new musical direction through the fusion of traditional Korean music and contemporary sounds. This is expected to have a significant impact on the globalization of K-POP.

My Thoughts

BTS's performance once again proved that they are not just an idol group but have established themselves as cultural icons. The emphasis on the value of traditional Korean music through the sampled song 'Arirang' was particularly impressive. They are likely to continue communicating with fans in various ways and convey social messages through their music.

Hidden Variables to Consider

In this performance, RM could not participate in the choreography due to an ankle injury but focused on singing. This shows that BTS prioritizes individual health and safety. Additionally, as the members mentioned, the efforts of many individuals behind the scenes during the preparation process should not be forgotten, as BTS's success is a result of their teamwork and collaboration.
