Experience Collector Generation Z: How to Communicate with Them

Recently, Professor Song Su-jin of Korea University published ‘The Age of Experience Collectors,’ which provides interesting insights into Generation Z. Born as digital natives, Generation Z values diverse experiences, and it is emphasized that their communication styles and values differ from those of previous generations. In particular, they assess expertise through experience, and their way of life transcends simple consumption, creating a new cultural trend based on respect for themselves and others.

The book describes Generation Z as ‘experience collectors’ and illustrates how they are changing societal standards. This shift is expected to influence not only intergenerational differences but also future social values and communication methods. Therefore, understanding their way of thinking and behavior is essential for responding to future social changes.

Key Summary

  • Generation Z places importance on experience and tends to respect those with expertise.
  • They evaluate professionalism based on different criteria from previous generations and communicate through diverse experiences.
  • Reading among Generation Z has become an important way to express themselves and connect, rather than just a means of learning.

Key Scenes

  • Generation Z is the ‘generation of respect,’ where respecting oneself and others is the default.
  • They value experience-based expertise and have a different perspective from the degrees or certifications of previous generations.
  • Their ways of reading and consuming content differ, allowing them to express themselves and communicate with society.
  • As the cultural influence of Generation Z increases, the methods of communication with them must also change.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Generation Z consists of those born between 1995 and 2012, and their experiences growing up in a digital environment have significantly influenced their way of thinking. They are accustomed to obtaining information and expressing themselves through social media and various digital content. This environment has brought about significant changes in how they communicate with others, widening the gap with previous generations.

Why It Matters

Generation Z will not only be the main consumers in the future but will also lead changes in social values and culture. If the methods of communication with them do not change, businesses or institutions may fail to understand their needs and ultimately lose competitiveness in the market. Therefore, it is essential to understand their values and communication styles.

Points to Note

Generation Z is influencing various fields of society beyond just being consumers. They value individual experiences and are driving social change based on those experiences. Therefore, methods of communication with them should reflect their experiences and values, which will also impact the strategies of businesses or institutions.

Hidden Variables to Consider

What Generation Z values is not just consumption but the experiences and meanings within that process. They do not merely consume products; they place importance on the values and experiences those products embody. Therefore, businesses should respect their experiences and communicate in ways they desire.

Current Checkpoints

  • Consider new approaches for communicating with Generation Z.
  • Understand the content formats and communication styles they prefer and develop strategies that reflect this.

FAQ

Q: What is the most important point for communicating with Generation Z?
A: It is important to respect their experiences and values and apply communication methods that align with them.

Q: What type of content does Generation Z value?
A: They prefer content that allows them to express themselves and connect, rather than just simple information delivery.

Q: What strategies should businesses develop to communicate with Generation Z?
A: Businesses need to develop marketing strategies and communication methods that reflect the experiences of Generation Z. This will help build trust with them.

In conclusion, communicating with Generation Z goes beyond simple marketing strategies; it is essential to understand and respect their experiences and values. It is necessary to start considering communication methods that reflect the values of Generation Z right now.
