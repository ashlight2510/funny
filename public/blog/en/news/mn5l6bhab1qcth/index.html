Lee Jae-yong gifts 'this' to employees staying in the Middle East···“Deep consolation and gratitude”

Recently, Lee Jae-yong, Chairman of Samsung Electronics, delivered encouragement gifts worth 5 million won to employees working in the Middle East. These gifts consist of the latest smartphones and Galaxy Books, aimed at recognizing and comforting the dedication of employees amid the unstable situation in the region. Samsung emphasized the importance of the Middle East, even amid the conflict between Iran and Israel, and stated that it is seeking various business opportunities, including AI and infrastructure investments.

This matter can be seen not just as a simple gift delivery, but as a strategic decision by Samsung to maintain business continuity in the Middle East. Chairman Lee expressed his sincere gratitude for the hard work of employees who are dedicated under difficult conditions, making efforts to boost their morale.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The delivery of these encouragement gifts is a recognition of the difficulties faced by employees working in the Middle East due to the conflict between Iran and Israel. Samsung emphasizes that the Middle East is a crucial point in the global supply chain and has taken measures to retain only essential personnel while withdrawing other employees to ensure business continuity in the region. In this context, Chairman Lee's encouragement holds significant meaning for the employees.
