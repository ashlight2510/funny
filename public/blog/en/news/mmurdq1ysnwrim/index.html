Jo Gil-hyung Resigns as Candidate for Chungbuk Governor and Leaves the People Power Party

Former Chungju Mayor Jo Gil-hyung has decided to resign from his candidacy for Chungbuk Governor and leave the People Power Party. He expressed his disappointment with the party through his Facebook, emphasizing that it is no longer the party he loved. Jo stated that after 13 years as a member of the People Power Party, he has concluded that the party's direction no longer aligns with his own. As a result, the number of candidates for the People Power Party's Chungbuk Governor nomination has decreased to three: Yoon Gap-geun, Yoon Hee-geun, and Kim Soo-min.

This resignation is not merely a personal decision but signals significant changes in the political landscape of Chungbuk. Jo's departure highlights internal conflicts and divisions within the People Power Party, which are likely to have a substantial impact on upcoming elections.
