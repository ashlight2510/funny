Trump: No Need for NATO Help - Why Japan, Australia, and South Korea Matter

March 18, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Trump: "No Need for NATO Help... Same for Japan, Australia, and South Korea" Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun On the 17th (local time), U.S. President Donald Trump claimed that he does not need help from NATO (North Atlantic Treaty Organization) or from South Korea, Japan, and Australia. This statement seems to reflect his anger and disappointment after most allies failed to respond to requests for sending warships to the Strait of Hormuz. President Trump stated on social media, “The U.S. is not receiving support for our military operations against the terrorist regime of Iran from most NATO ‘allies’...” Read the original article

Trump Expresses Disappointment with NATO and Allies

U.S. President Donald Trump recently expressed disappointment with NATO and allies such as South Korea, Japan, and Australia, claiming that he does not need their help. He revealed through social media that most allies have refused to support military operations against Iran, leading to dissatisfaction with the costs of U.S. defense. Trump strongly voiced his grievances about the one-sided nature of the relationship, stating, “We protect them, but they do nothing when we need help.” This statement is likely to reignite debates about U.S. foreign policy and the role of its allies.

Trump's remarks suggest a significant shift in the U.S. military and diplomatic stance, going beyond personal opinion. He has clearly communicated a message of reducing trust and reliance on NATO and Asian allies, prompting attention to how this may impact future U.S. international relations.

Key Summary

  • Trump expresses disappointment over NATO and allies' refusal to support military operations.
  • Voiced grievances about the one-sided nature of U.S. defense costs.
  • Emphasizes that Japan, Australia, and South Korea are no longer necessary.
  • Advocates for an independent U.S. response to Iran's military capabilities.
  • Highlights the potential impact of declining trust in NATO and allies on foreign policy.

Key Scenes

  • One-sided Defense Costs: Complaints about the costs incurred by the U.S. in protecting NATO allies.
  • Refusal of Military Support: Allies' refusal to support military operations against Iran.
  • Declining Trust: Trump's remarks imply an intention to reduce trust and reliance on allies.
  • U.S. Independent Military Capability: Emphasizes U.S. independence in military responses to Iran.
  • Change in Foreign Policy: Potential overall shift in the direction of U.S. foreign policy.

Why Context Matters

President Trump's remarks came amid heightened tensions with Iran, where he urged NATO allies to participate in military operations, but most countries declined. In this context, Trump expressed disappointment with allies, sending a strong message that their support is unnecessary. This indicates a possible shift in U.S. foreign policy toward reducing dependence on allies and emphasizing independent military operations.

Why It Matters

While this statement could be dismissed as a personal opinion, it may signal significant changes in U.S. foreign policy. Particularly, the message "we no longer need help" could be interpreted as a signal to redefine relationships with allies. This could greatly impact the U.S. military role and international relations.

My Perspective

In my view, Trump's remarks carry more weight than mere dissatisfaction. They demonstrate a commitment to reducing reliance on allies, which could lead to significant changes in U.S. foreign policy. Notably, there is a strong possibility that an independent approach to regional threats like Iran will be emphasized.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While Trump's remarks are drawing attention, there are various reasons why allies did not participate in military operations against Iran. Internal political situations, military readiness, and diplomatic stances in the international community likely played complex roles. Focusing solely on Trump's comments without considering this context could overlook the essence of the issue.

Current Points to Check

Following Trump's remarks, it is essential to observe how NATO and Asian allies will respond. Particularly, as military tensions with Iran persist, how allies will react to U.S. requests and how U.S. foreign policy may evolve is crucial to monitor.
