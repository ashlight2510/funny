Banks and Ne·Ka·To Launch 'Credit Loan Switching for Individual Business Owners'

Recently, domestic banks and fintech companies have simultaneously launched credit loan switching services for individual business owners. Various financial institutions, including KB Kookmin Bank and Shinhan Bank, have introduced this service to reduce the financial burden on small businesses, allowing individual business owners to compare loan conditions from 18 financial institutions online and switch to better terms. This expands the scope of loan mobility services, which had previously been limited to personal credit loans or mortgage loans, to include loans for individual business owners, targeting those aged 19 and above with a business registration certificate.

This service is expected to enhance financial accessibility for small business owners by providing them with easier opportunities to compare loan conditions and switch to more favorable loans.

Key Summary

  • Credit Loan Switching Service: KB Kookmin Bank, Shinhan Bank, and fintech companies like Naver Pay, Kakao Pay, and Toss have launched a credit loan switching service for individual business owners.
  • Loan Comparison and Switching: Individual business owners can compare loan conditions from various financial institutions online and switch to loans with better terms.
  • Eligibility and Limits: Individual business owners aged 19 and above with a business registration certificate can apply, with different loan limits set by each bank.
  • Inclusive Finance Practice: The loan mobility service has been expanded to individual business owner loans as part of efforts to reduce the financial burden on small businesses.

Key Highlights

  • Introduction of the individual business owner loan mobility service
  • Expansion of cooperation between financial institutions and fintech companies
  • Improvement of financial accessibility for small business owners
  • Increased ease of loan comparison and user convenience
  • Exclusion of specific products (e.g., loans related to real estate rental)

Why Background Matters

The launch of this service can be seen as part of efforts to alleviate the financial burdens faced by small business owners. Many individual business owners have struggled due to COVID-19, prompting the government and financial institutions to develop various support measures. The loan mobility service has expanded beyond the traditional focus on personal credit loans and mortgage loans to provide benefits to individual business owners.

Why It Matters

This service expands financial choices for individual business owners and provides opportunities to switch to loans with better terms. This could contribute to the economic stability of small businesses and positively impact local economies. Notably, the collaboration with fintech companies has improved the accessibility of financial services.

Personal Interpretation

In my view, the core of this service goes beyond merely providing an opportunity to compare loan conditions. It opens the possibility for small business owners to improve their situations, which were previously hampered by information asymmetry that forced them into unfavorable loan conditions. However, it remains to be seen how many individual business owners will actually benefit from this service.

Hidden Variables to Consider

It is important to note that certain loan products are excluded from this service. In particular, loans related to real estate rental are not included, meaning that business owners in this sector will still miss out on the benefits of loan switching. As a result, some business owners may continue to face challenges in financial accessibility.

A1: It can be accessed on the websites of major banks like KB Kookmin Bank and Shinhan Bank, as well as on fintech platforms like Naver Pay, Kakao Pay, and Toss.

Q2: What are the conditions for switching loans?
A2: Individual business owners aged 19 and above with a business registration certificate can apply, with varying loan limits set by each financial institution.

Q3: Which loan products are excluded?
A3: Some products, such as loans related to real estate rental, are excluded from the loan mobility service.

Q4: How is the effectiveness of the loan switching service evaluated?
A4: The effectiveness of the service may vary depending on the experiences of actual users and the extent of improvement in loan conditions, necessitating ongoing monitoring in the future.
