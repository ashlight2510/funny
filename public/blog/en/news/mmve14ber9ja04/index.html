Sungkyunkwan University's Professor Lee Woong-kyu's research team selected for the Inside Front Cover of the international journal 'Materials Horizons'

Professor Lee Woong-kyu's research team from the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University has developed a new thin-film process technology that can contribute to the performance enhancement of next-generation DRAM memory devices. This research has been evaluated as addressing the capacitor performance degradation issue by precisely controlling the initial interfacial reactions and oxidation behavior in atomic layer deposition (ALD)-based thin-film processes. This achievement has been recognized by being selected for the Inside Front Cover of 'Materials Horizons', a prestigious international journal in the field of materials science, highlighting the importance and originality of the research.

This research can make a significant contribution to enhancing the performance of DRAM memory, which has become a core technology in the semiconductor industry, and is likely to be utilized in various semiconductor thin-film process research in the future.
