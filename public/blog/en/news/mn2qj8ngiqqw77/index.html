Group solo performance 'Discovery of the Archipelago' to be held on the 11th of next month

The group , which pursues experimental music that breaks down the boundaries between traditional Korean music and Western music, will hold a solo performance titled on the 11th of April at Space1 of the Surim Cultural Foundation in Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul. This performance coincides with the release of their first regular album and will feature 13 self-composed songs. The title of the performance metaphorically compares the musical harmony created by performers of various colors to an archipelago, emphasizing close communication with the audience.

This performance is an important opportunity that goes beyond a simple musical experience, presenting a new musical trend through the fusion of traditional Korean music and Western music. Avad is currently planning merchandise related to album production through the funding platform 'Tumblbug', further enhancing communication with fans.
