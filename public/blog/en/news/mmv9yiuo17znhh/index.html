Chabot Mobility Selected as Asia-Pacific High-Growth Company for Three Consecutive Years

Chabot Mobility has been named one of the '2026 Asia-Pacific High-Growth Companies' by the British economic newspaper Financial Times (FT) and the global research institution Statista for three consecutive years. The company boasts an impressive average annual revenue growth rate of 40% and is ranked 237th among 500 companies across 14 Asia-Pacific countries. Notably, it ranks 59th among Korean companies and 15th in the IT and software sector. Chabot Mobility provides new car estimates, financing, insurance, and vehicle management services through its integrated auto commerce model and is internalizing the insurance and finance value chain through its subsidiaries, Chabot Insurance and Chabot Motors.

This news reflects Chabot Mobility's growth trajectory and its strategy to solidify its position in the global market. The company is currently preparing for an IPO and seeking investments for global business expansion, and this growth is garnering significant attention in the industry beyond mere numbers.

  • Strengthening the insurance and finance value chain through subsidiaries Chabot Insurance and Chabot Motors.
  • Currently pursuing additional investments for IPO preparation and global business expansion.

Chabot Mobility's selection is not just a simple achievement. In recent years, the demand for mobility services has surged, leading to growth among related companies. The integrated auto commerce model, in particular, adds convenience by offering various services on a single platform. This change is occurring alongside advancements in the IT and software industries, and Chabot Mobility is effectively leveraging this.
