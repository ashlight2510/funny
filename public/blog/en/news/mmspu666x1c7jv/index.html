Egg Prices Rise to 7,000 Won Amid Epidemic: Key Issues for Chicken and Pork Costs

March 16, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

A Dozen Eggs for 7,000 Won… Prices of Chicken and Pork Have Also Increased Due to Epidemic Outbreaks

Recently, the prices of livestock products such as eggs, chicken, and pork have surged. This phenomenon is due to a decrease in supply caused by the ongoing outbreaks of livestock infectious diseases like highly pathogenic avian influenza (AI) and African swine fever (ASF). In particular, the price of eggs has approached an average of 7,000 won per dozen, marking a 6.7% increase from last year. Alongside this, the prices of chicken and pork have risen by 7.8% and 10.8%, respectively, adding to the burden on the dining tables of ordinary citizens.

This price increase not only affects consumers but also has significant repercussions for those involved in the livestock industry and related sectors. The livestock product price index exceeds the overall consumer price inflation rate, indicating a high potential for broader economic impacts.

Key Points

  • The price of eggs has reached an average of 7,000 won per dozen, a 6.7% increase from last year.
  • Prices of chicken and pork have also increased by 7.8% and 10.8%, respectively.
  • The ongoing outbreaks of livestock infectious diseases have led to a decrease in supply.
  • The livestock product price index exceeds the overall consumer price inflation rate (2%).
  • Price increases are expected to continue in the future.

Background and Context

This price increase is a consequence of the livestock infectious diseases that began last winter, particularly the spread of avian influenza and African swine fever. As a result, many livestock have been culled, leading to a reduction in production. For instance, during this winter season, 9.8 million laying hens were culled, which is double the number from last year. This situation adversely affects not only egg production but also the prices of chicken and pork.

Why This Issue Matters

The rise in livestock product prices does not merely affect consumer prices; it directly impacts the dietary habits of ordinary citizens. Eggs, in particular, are a major source of protein for the public, and when prices rise, they are among the first items to affect low-income households. This can increase the economic burden on households and potentially lead to a contraction in consumption.

Comment from the Article Reader

In my view, this price increase is unlikely to be a temporary phenomenon. If supply disruptions due to the epidemic continue, prices may rise even further. Those working in the livestock industry should take this situation seriously. If measures to address the price increases are not put in place, it could lead to negative outcomes for both consumers and producers.

Often Overlooked Aspects

While the article mentions infectious diseases as the cause of rising livestock prices, additional variables such as climate change and rising feed costs should also be considered. If these factors converge, the price increase could accelerate further. Additionally, if consumers turn to alternative food sources, there may be a decrease in demand for specific items.

What to Check Now

  • Pay close attention to current trends in livestock product price fluctuations.
  • It is advisable to gather information on alternative foods or other protein sources in advance.
  • If the government announces measures to stabilize livestock product prices, it will be important to quickly verify the details.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Will egg prices continue to rise?
A: If the current situation persists, there is a high likelihood that prices will continue to rise. The spread of the epidemic and the recovery of production levels are key variables.

Q: Will prices of other livestock products also rise?
A: Yes, prices of chicken and pork have already increased, and it is expected that the upward trend will continue.

Q: How should consumers respond?
A: It is necessary to consider alternative foods or adjust consumption until prices stabilize.

Q: What is the government's response?
A: The government is preparing measures to stabilize prices, but the effectiveness of their implementation remains to be seen.
