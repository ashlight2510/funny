Jeongseon-gun's 'Silver Etiquette' Program: A Positive Cycle of Welfare and Economy

The 'Silver Etiquette' program implemented by Jeongseon-gun provides hair and beauty and bathing services to seniors aged 70 and older, achieving two goals simultaneously: welfare improvement and local economic revitalization. This year, Jeongseon-gun is investing 1.55 billion won to provide an annual subsidy of 180,000 won to 8,500 seniors, allowing them to freely use services at over 90 affiliated stores in the area. This program is gaining attention not only for its welfare support but also for its contribution to increasing the sales of local small businesses.

Since its inception in 2021, the program has expanded significantly, with a cumulative total of 30,000 beneficiaries and a total support amount reaching 3.8 billion won. Jeongseon-gun has stated that it plans to continue managing the program to enhance its quality and ensure that seniors can use the services without inconvenience.

Key Summary

  • Jeongseon-gun's 'Silver Etiquette' program is a support program for hair and beauty and bathing services for seniors aged 70 and older.
  • It provides services to 8,500 seniors with an annual subsidy of 180,000 won, contributing to increased sales for local small businesses.
  • Since the program's implementation, the cumulative number of beneficiaries has reached 30,000, with a total support amount of 3.8 billion won.
  • Jeongseon-gun plans to enhance service quality through continuous management of affiliated stores and monitoring of usage status.

Why It Matters

This program is significant as it positively impacts the local economy beyond simple welfare support. By receiving hair and beauty services, seniors reduce feelings of social isolation, creating a virtuous cycle that revitalizes local commerce. This provides tangible benefits to local residents and presents a model for simultaneous growth in welfare and the economy.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The 'Silver Etiquette' program was initiated as part of efforts to improve the quality of life for seniors in an aging society. Many seniors struggle with basic hygiene management due to economic reasons, and this program contributes to enhancing their quality of life. Additionally, at a time when local economic revitalization is needed, it positively impacts small businesses.

Points of Attention

The greatest advantage of this program is that seniors can conveniently use services with a voucher card without needing to manage separate usage rights. This approach maximizes user convenience and can encourage more seniors to participate in the program. In fact, Im Myung-im, the president of the Jeongseon-gun Hair and Beauty Association, reported that the increase in senior visits has led to a rise in sales. This serves as a practical example of supporting local commerce while balancing welfare and the economy.

Hidden Variables to Consider

During the implementation of this program, there is a possibility that the quality of service at some affiliated stores may decline. For instance, if the number of service providers becomes insufficient compared to the rapidly increasing demand, seniors may experience inconvenience. Therefore, continuous management and quality checks of affiliated stores by Jeongseon-gun are necessary.

Current Points to Check

  • It is important to closely observe the impact of Jeongseon-gun's 'Silver Etiquette' program on the local economy and the participation rate of seniors.
  • Future surveys on service quality and user satisfaction at affiliated stores should be conducted to identify areas for improvement.

FAQ

Q: How is the subsidy for the 'Silver Etiquette' program used?
A: The subsidy is provided via a voucher card, allowing seniors to freely use hair and beauty and bathing services at affiliated stores in the area.

Q: Who are the beneficiaries of this program?
A: Residents of Jeongseon-gun aged 70 and older are eligible to receive an annual subsidy of 180,000 won.

Q: What types of stores are affiliated?
A: Over 90 bathing and hair and beauty businesses are registered as affiliated stores.

Q: Will the effects of the program be sustained?
A: Jeongseon-gun expects to sustain the effects through management of affiliated stores and monitoring of usage status to enhance service quality.
