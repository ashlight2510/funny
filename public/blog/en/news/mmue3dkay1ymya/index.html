Israel launches large-scale ground operation in Lebanon... concerns over expanding front and prolonged war

The Israeli military has initiated a large-scale ground operation in southern Lebanon, indicating the possibility of a long-term occupation of the Iran-supported militant group Hezbollah. The Israeli Ministry of Defense stated that this operation aims to ensure the safety of residents in northern Israel and dismantle the terrorists' bases. However, there are concerns from Western countries that Israel's large-scale offensive could lead to a humanitarian disaster, alongside the assessment that a new front has been opened in the war with Iran.

This ground operation began amid escalating tensions between Israel and Hezbollah. Israel emphasized that this operation would be similar to the battles in the Gaza Strip and warned that Lebanese residents would not be able to return until the threat from Hezbollah is eliminated. This raises fears that the wounds of the Lebanese civil war may be reopened.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The conflict between Israel and Hezbollah has gradually intensified in recent years. Particularly, Hezbollah, supported by Iran, has carried out retaliatory attacks against Israel, escalating military tensions. Israel has experience occupying Lebanon from 1982 to 2000, and this operation is seen as a more complex issue considering that historical context. A new front has opened as the war with Iran intensifies.

Why It Matters

Israel's military operation is a significant issue that could change the political landscape of the Middle East beyond mere military conflict. As tensions with Iran escalate, it could also affect other countries in the region. In particular, the potential resurgence of war in Lebanon could directly harm many civilians, complicating the international community's humanitarian response.

Points of Attention

In my view, the most significant impact of this operation will be on the civilians in Lebanon. Already, over 1 million Lebanese residents have fled, which corresponds to one-sixth of the total population. Human rights organizations warn that Israel's military operation could lead to war crimes. Therefore, how the international community responds and provides humanitarian aid will be crucial variables.

Hidden Variables to Watch

If Israel's military operation prolongs, it is essential not to overlook how challenging it will be to eliminate armed groups like Hezbollah. Historical cases show that ground operations have not been effective in suppressing self-sustaining armed groups. This could also challenge the combat capabilities of the Israeli military.

Current Checkpoints

It is necessary to continuously monitor the humanitarian situation in Lebanon and the progress of Israel's military operation. In particular, attention should be paid to how the international community responds and how humanitarian aid is provided.
