How to Easily Remove Unpleasant Smells from Your Washing Machine

March 15, 2026

Unpleasant Smells from the Washing Machine: Simple Removal Methods

Have you ever experienced unpleasant smells coming from your washing machine every time you use it? It’s really frustrating, and if that smell gets onto your freshly washed clothes, it becomes an even bigger problem. So today, I’ve compiled some simple methods to eliminate unpleasant odors from your washing machine. With these methods, you can reclaim a clean washing machine!

Cleaning the Inside of the Washing Machine

Essential Items: Vinegar and Baking Soda

The first method you can try to remove odors from your washing machine is using vinegar and baking soda. These two are truly versatile. From my experience, vinegar is effective in eliminating residue and odors inside the washing machine.

  1. Pour 2 cups of vinegar into the washing machine drum.
  2. Add 1/2 cup of baking soda.
  3. Run the longest wash cycle with hot water.

After doing this, the smell significantly decreased. I felt a sense of accomplishment while cleaning the washing machine without even realizing it.

Checking the Washing Machine Drain Filter

Frequently Overlooked Drain Filter Check

While using the washing machine, dust or lint can accumulate in the drain filter, causing odors. It’s important to check this regularly. At first, I didn’t think much of this part, but after cleaning the filter, the smell coming from the washing machine improved significantly.

  1. Open the washing machine drain filter and remove any debris.
  2. Rinse the filter thoroughly under running water.

Doing this will improve the washing machine's performance and reduce odors. It’s a really simple task, so don’t forget to do it!

Leaving the Washing Machine Door Open After Use

The Importance of Moisture Removal

It’s a good idea to leave the washing machine door open after the wash is finished. This allows moisture inside to evaporate quickly, preventing mold and unpleasant odors. When I first learned this, I thought, "Why didn’t I know something so simple?"

If you remember to leave the door open after washing, you can significantly reduce odors from the washing machine.

Creating a Regular Maintenance Routine

Making Cleaning a Habit

Finally, it’s important to establish a routine for regularly maintaining your washing machine. Just having the mindset of "I should clean it regularly" can help prevent odors. Personally, I try to clean it at least once a month.

After creating my own maintenance routine, the unpleasant smells from the washing machine have almost disappeared. I recommend that you create your own routine too!

Today, we learned some simple methods to eliminate unpleasant smells from the washing machine. From vinegar and baking soda to checking the drain filter, leaving the door open, and establishing a regular maintenance routine! With these simple methods, you can maintain a clean washing machine.
