Investment Strategies to Know When Interest Rates Rise

March 17, 2026

Investment Strategies to Know When Deposit Rates Rise

Recently, rising deposit rates have garnered significant attention from many investors. When interest rates rise, both loan interest and deposit interest change, making it essential to establish appropriate investment strategies. This article will start with the background of rising deposit rates and provide various investment strategies and checklists accordingly.

Understanding the Background of Rising Deposit Rates

There are several reasons for rising interest rates, including economic growth, inflation, and central bank interest rate policies. These changes in interest rates affect both loan interest and deposit interest, so investors need to understand them well.

Importance of Rising Interest Rates

When interest rates rise, the overall environment of the financial market changes. For example, borrowers must bear higher interest costs, while the returns from deposits also increase. Therefore, understanding the background of rising interest rates is crucial for formulating investment strategies.

Analyzing Interest Rate Changes

It is important to analyze the causes of rising interest rates. Economic data or announcements from the central bank can help identify the background of interest rate fluctuations. For instance, if inflation has recently increased, there is a high likelihood that the central bank will raise interest rates.

Points to Note

It is essential to distinguish between short-term interest rate fluctuations and long-term trends. Short-term changes may be temporary, but long-term persistent trends can significantly impact investment decisions.

Investment Strategies to Consider When Deposit Rates Rise

When deposit rates rise, various strategies are needed, such as moving to safe assets, changes in the stock market, and managing loans and debts.

1. Moving to Safe Assets

As interest rates rise, the importance of safe assets increases. Therefore, investors should diversify their assets into safer places.

Importance of Safe Assets

When interest rates rise, uncertainty increases, leading to a higher demand for safe assets. These assets have a lower risk of principal loss and provide stable returns.

Application Method

Diversifying into government bonds, gold, and deposit products is effective. For example, gold is useful as an inflation hedge, while government bonds provide stable returns.

Checkpoint

Since the returns on safe assets are relatively low, it is essential to recognize this and adjust the investment ratio. For instance, if the proportion of safe assets becomes excessively high, profitability may decrease.

2. Changes in the Stock Market

Rising interest rates also significantly impact the stock market. Certain industries may become more promising.

Response of the Stock Market

When interest rates rise, the cost of financing for companies increases, leading to greater volatility in the stock market.

Application Method

During periods of rising interest rates, stocks related to finance, healthcare, and energy may be promising. For example, financial stocks can positively benefit from rising interest rates.

Checkpoint

Increased volatility necessitates risk management. It is advisable to diversify investments across various industries to reduce portfolio risk.

3. Managing Loans and Debts

As interest rates rise, the burden of loan interest increases, making management necessary.

Importance of Loan Management

When loan interest rates rise, the repayment burden increases, so it is crucial to prepare in advance.

Application Method

Consider switching to fixed-rate loans. Fixed rates are not affected by rising interest rates, allowing for stable repayments.

Checkpoint

Loan repayment plans should be readjusted. For example, setting a repayment plan before interest rates rise can reduce interest burdens.

Portfolio Adjustment in Response to Rising Deposit Rates

Rebalancing the portfolio in response to interest rate changes is necessary.

Importance of Portfolio Adjustment

If the portfolio is not adjusted, it cannot effectively manage the risks associated with interest rate changes.

Application Method

It is necessary to readjust asset allocation ratios. For instance, increasing the proportion of safe assets or reducing the weight of stocks can be considered.

Checkpoint

Strategies for the timing and method of rebalancing should be established. Monitoring market conditions and adjusting at the appropriate time is crucial.

Checklist: Investment Strategies to Consider When Rates Rise

  1. Analyze current holdings
  2. Check the proportion of safe assets
  3. Review the stock portfolio
  4. Reassess loan conditions
  5. Consider consulting with experts

Examples: Investment Strategy Cases in Response to Rising Rates

Case 1: Moving to Safe Assets

One investor moved 30% of their holdings into gold and government bonds as deposit rates rose. As a result, they were able to achieve stable returns.

Case 2: Stock Portfolio Rebalancing

Another investor rebalanced their portfolio towards healthcare and financial stocks in anticipation of rising interest rates. This allowed them to maintain stable returns despite market volatility.

Case 3: Adjusting Loan Repayment Strategy

One household switched to a fixed-rate loan in preparation for rising rates. This helped them reduce their interest burden and manage their finances more stably.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. What assets should I invest in when deposit rates rise?

    • It is advisable to invest in safe assets such as gold, government bonds, and deposit products. Additionally, considering stocks in promising industries like finance and healthcare during rising interest rates can be beneficial.

  2. What impact does rising interest rates have on the stock market?

    • Rising interest rates increase the cost of financing for companies, leading to greater volatility in the stock market. Consequently, certain industries may become more promising.

  3. What strategies can reduce the burden of loan interest?

    • Switching to fixed-rate loans or readjusting loan repayment plans can effectively reduce interest burdens.

Establishing appropriate investment strategies at the time of rising deposit rates is crucial. Based on the above content, assess your financial situation and take necessary actions. As a first step, it is recommended to analyze your current holdings.
