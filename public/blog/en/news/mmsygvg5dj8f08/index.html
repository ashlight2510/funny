Jeon Han-gil and Ko Seong-guk's 'Jang Dong-hyuk Protection Rally'... Kim Min-soo "Historic Scene"

The recent 'Jang Dong-hyuk Protection Rally' held in Yeouido, Seoul, is a symbolic event that showcases the internal conflicts within the People Power Party and the solidarity of its support base, indicating significant changes in the political landscape. At this event, far-right YouTubers such as Jeon Han-gil and Ko Seong-guk gathered to support Jang Dong-hyuk, the leader of the People Power Party, while criticizing the 'Yoon Again' faction within the party. Kim Min-soo, a senior member of the People Power Party, described the scene as "historic," emphasizing the significance of party members taking to the streets.

This rally is seen as an important case that reveals not only support for a single individual but also the direction of the People Power Party and the conflicts among its internal factions. Particularly, the holding of such a support rally amid threats to the leadership of Jang Dong-hyuk could have a substantial impact on the future of the party.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This rally occurred in the context of growing doubts about Jang Dong-hyuk's leadership following a general meeting demanding 'Yoon Again' within the People Power Party. The support rally by far-right YouTubers defending Jang further highlights the divisions and conflicts within the party. Notably, while Jang has expressed a commitment to not be influenced by the 'Yoon Again' faction, there are still voices within the party calling for adjustments with these factions.

Why It Matters

This rally goes beyond being a mere support event; it serves as a barometer for the political direction of the People Power Party and its internal conflicts. The consolidation of support for Jang Dong-hyuk could enhance the party's unity, but if internal divisions deepen, it could negatively affect the party's future. Given that the outcomes of the upcoming local elections will significantly impact the party's existence, this issue can no longer be overlooked.
