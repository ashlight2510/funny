Spring Island Outing…The Home of Sea Lettuce ‘Jeopdo’

The Jodo Archipelago is a beautiful group of islands located in the southwestern tip of the Korean Peninsula, and its charm is once again being highlighted as spring approaches. In particular, Jeopdo is the center of sea lettuce production, where active auctions take place every morning at the port. EBS's program covers this unique natural environment and agriculture, spotlighting the story of the couple, Kim Ho-rim and his wife, who have been cultivating seaweed for over 20 years. They feel the global popularity of Korean seaweed through their long hours of work at sea.

This article emphasizes the importance of local economy and culture through the natural beauty of the Jodo Archipelago and the sea lettuce production site in Jeopdo. In particular, as sea lettuce has established itself as a representative seafood of Korea, it shows the positive impact it has on the lives of Jeopdo residents.

The natural environment of the Jodo Archipelago has been relatively unknown, but it has recently gained attention with the development of the tourism industry. Against this backdrop, the production of sea lettuce in Jeopdo provides economic stability and pride to local residents.
