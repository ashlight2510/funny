Last year's highest salary among business leaders, not Choi Tae-won or Chung Eui-sun, but this person... excluding retirement pay, 24.8 billion won

In the recently announced salary status of business leaders, Kim Seung-yeon, chairman of Hanwha Group, ranked first in salary excluding retirement pay. He received 24.841 billion won last year, significantly surpassing Choi Tae-won, chairman of SK Group (8.25 billion won), and Chung Eui-sun, chairman of Hyundai Motor Group (17.461 billion won). Additionally, when including retirement pay, Ryu Jin, chairman of Poongsan Group, recorded the highest amount at over 46.6 billion won. This compensation status is considered an important indicator showing the compensation system of corporate executives and the changing landscape of the business world.

This article not only focuses on salary figures but also provides an opportunity to glimpse the overall trends in the business world and the future strategies of companies. In particular, the fact that Chairman Kim significantly raised his compensation is related to Hanwha's push for new businesses, offering important insights into the company's future direction.
