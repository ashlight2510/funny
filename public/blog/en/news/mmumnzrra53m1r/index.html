Oh Se-hoon's Application for Seoul Mayor Nomination: Its Significance and Impact

Recently, Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon has completed his application for the People Power Party's Seoul mayoral nomination. This decision comes ahead of the June 3 local elections, and Mayor Oh had previously delayed his application, demanding the resolution of his relationship with former President Yoon Seok-yeol through the 'Jeol-Yun Resolution' and 'Yoon Again Liquidation.' However, he ultimately registered as a candidate, emphasizing the spirit of 'putting the party first.' This nomination application also includes Rep. Park Soo-min and former Gangdong District Mayor Kim Chung-hwan.

This nomination application is expected to have significant implications for various stakeholders related to the Seoul mayoral election. In particular, Mayor Oh's application can be interpreted as an intention to strengthen his position within the party.

Key Summary

  • Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon has completed his application for the Seoul mayoral nomination for the June 3 local elections.
  • Rep. Park Soo-min and former Gangdong District Mayor Kim Chung-hwan have also applied for the nomination.
