Police Send Two Suspects in Attempted Abduction of Elementary School Student in Seodaemun-gu to Prosecutors

Recently, the suspects involved in the attempted abduction of an elementary school student in Seodaemun-gu, Seoul, have finally been sent to the prosecutors. The incident occurred on August 28 of last year, and two of the three suspects have been handed over to the prosecutors on charges of attempted enticement of a minor. They approached elementary school students by saying, "You're cute, I'll take you home," but the child victims managed to escape. The police acknowledged shortcomings in their initial response, and the transfer to the prosecutors happened only after seven months.

The developments in this case reveal serious issues not only regarding the attempted abduction but also concerning child protection and the police response system. The police failed to respond adequately despite receiving two reports, and additional reports of victimization followed the incident. This situation has heightened anxiety among parents and the local community.

Key Points

  • Two of the three suspects have been sent to the prosecutors on charges of attempted enticement of a minor.
  • The transfer to the prosecutors occurred seven months after the incident.
  • Increased anxiety due to inadequate initial police response and subsequent reports of additional victimization.
  • The suspects claim their actions were "just a prank."
  • The police have determined that applying charges under the Child Welfare Act is difficult.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This incident involved three men in their 20s attempting to abduct elementary school students near a school in Seodaemun-gu last summer. Criticism has been directed at the police for their inadequate initial response, which led to additional reports of victimization. Although the police apprehended the suspects through investigations and digital evidence analysis after the incident, the failure of the police response to adequately reflect the seriousness of the situation remains a concern.

Why Is This Important?

This incident raises serious awareness regarding the police response system related to child protection issues. Parents become worried about their children's safety, and the local community feels a greater sense of unease. Furthermore, it serves as a case highlighting the importance of timely police responses, emphasizing the need for quicker and more effective actions in similar future incidents.
