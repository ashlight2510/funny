Snack, Bread, and Ice Cream Prices Rise in April: What Questions Remain?

March 19, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Following ramen and cooking oil... snacks, bread, and ice cream prices will also drop from April Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun Companies in the confectionery, bakery, and ice cream sectors, including Lotte Wellfood, Binggrae, and Samlip, will reduce prices on some products. The Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs announced on the 19th that confectionery, bakery, and ice cream companies agreed to participate in price reductions starting from April shipments to ease consumer burdens during the third meeting of the special task force (TF) for managing living prices. First, Lotte Wellfood and Haitai Confectionery will reduce prices on an average of seven products, including four types of biscuits and three types of candies...

Following ramen and cooking oil... snacks, bread, and ice cream prices will also drop from April

Recently, news has emerged that food companies will initiate price reductions. Major confectionery and bakery companies such as Lotte Wellfood, Binggrae, and Samlip have decided to lower the prices of some products by an average of 2.9% to 13.4% starting in April. This decision stems from discussions during the government's special task force meeting on living prices and is interpreted as an effort to alleviate consumer burdens. Notably, this price reduction initiative will expand beyond ramen and cooking oil to include various items such as confectionery, bread, and ice cream, with the government planning to continue monitoring to stabilize living prices.

While this movement towards price reductions is welcome news for consumers, the actual impact remains to be seen. Discussions are needed on how positively the price reductions will affect consumers and what strategies companies will adopt as a result.

Key Summary

  • Major confectionery and bakery companies will reduce prices on some products starting in April.
  • Prices of biscuits, candies, and ice cream from companies like Lotte Wellfood and Haitai Confectionery will decrease by an average of 2.9% to 13.4%.
  • The government will establish ongoing price monitoring and improvement measures through the special task force for living prices.

Key Highlights

  • Items with Price Reductions: Various products including biscuits, candies, bakery items, and ice cream are included.
  • Reduction Range: Average reductions range from 2.9% to 13.4%, with particularly large reductions on ice cream.
  • Easing Consumer Burdens: Expected to provide direct economic relief to consumers.
  • Ongoing Government Monitoring: Continuous monitoring will be conducted to stabilize living prices in the future.
  • Inspection of Unfair Trading Practices: Plans to check for unfair trading practices in some items.

Why Background Matters

This decision to reduce prices comes in response to rising inflation, which has increased the burden on consumers. Following the price reductions of ramen and cooking oil, consumer expectations have risen, prompting confectionery and bakery companies to join in the price cuts. The government is inspecting the distribution status of various items to ensure that this measure can provide tangible benefits to consumers.

Why It Matters

This price reduction goes beyond merely benefiting consumers; it can significantly influence corporate pricing policies and consumer behavior. Consumers are likely to purchase more products due to lower prices, which could lead to increased sales for companies. Additionally, if price reductions continue, they could contribute to overall price stabilization, positively impacting the economy.

Personal Comment

In my view, this price reduction is likely to trigger price competition across the industry rather than being a simple consumer benefit. Especially if other companies do not adjust their prices, consumers may shift away, making price reductions a new standard for competition. However, it is also important to consider the potential impact on corporate profitability if price reductions continue.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While price reductions may signal positive news for consumers, there are risks for companies regarding profitability. Additionally, unfair trading practices may still exist for some items, which could undermine the effectiveness of the price reductions.

Key Points to Check Now

  • Pay close attention to the items being reduced in price and the extent of the reductions.
  • Keep an eye on the outcomes of future meetings of the special task force for living prices and consider the possibility of further price adjustments.
