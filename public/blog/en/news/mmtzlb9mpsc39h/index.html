Lotte Department Store Attracts Chinese Tourists through Partnerships with Chinese Platforms

Lotte Department Store has decided to establish official channels on two major Chinese platforms, ‘Godeok Map’ and ‘Dazhong Dianping’. This marks the first case of a domestic department store operating official channels on Chinese platforms, and Lotte Department Store aims to reach approximately 1.7 billion Chinese users and preemptively attract Chinese tourists (Yuker). Godeok Map is the largest map app in China, boasting 1 billion monthly active users, while Dazhong Dianping is a review and lifestyle platform with over 700 million users. Lotte Department Store plans to leverage these platforms to preemptively capture the travel routes of Yuker and strengthen its position as a global shopping hub.

This news is an important example that goes beyond Lotte Department Store's marketing strategy, showing how the Korean retail industry is securing competitiveness in the Chinese market. Notably, as the trend of Chinese tourists visiting Korea is changing, it highlights the need for a shift in marketing strategies.

Key Summary

  • Lotte Department Store establishes official channels on Chinese platforms ‘Godeok Map’ and ‘Dazhong Dianping’.
  • Strategy to attract Yuker, allowing access to 1.7 billion users in China.
  • Godeok Map and Dazhong Dianping have 1 billion and 700 million active users, respectively.
  • Lotte Department Store plans to enhance promotions during peak seasons for Yuker, such as Lunar New Year and National Day.
  • Plans to expand to nationwide stores based on operational results.

Key Scenes

  • The operation of official channels on Chinese platforms by Lotte Department Store is a first in Korea.
  • Utilization of the large user base of Godeok Map and Dazhong Dianping.
  • Strategy to preemptively capture the travel routes of Yuker.
  • Strengthening its position as a global shopping hub.
  • Strategy to attract Yuker through seasonal focused promotions.

Why Background Matters

In recent years, the number of Chinese tourists visiting Korea has sharply increased. However, following the COVID-19 pandemic, the tourism industry has contracted, intensifying competition to attract Yuker. Consequently, Lotte Department Store has sought to analyze Yuker's purchasing patterns and travel trends, exploring partnerships with prominent Chinese platforms. Notably, China's ‘super app’ culture, where users tend to utilize various services within a single app, has made it essential for department stores to leverage this trend.

Why It Matters

Lotte Department Store's decision is part of a strategic approach for the Korean retail industry to strengthen its competitiveness in the Chinese market. Marketing aimed at Yuker goes beyond mere sales increases; it can contribute to enhancing the global recognition of Korean brands. Furthermore, access through these platforms could set a precedent for other companies to adopt similar strategies in the future.

Points to Note

In my view, the official channels established by Lotte Department Store are likely to serve as significant indicators reflecting future trends of Chinese consumers in the Korean market, rather than just attracting Yuker. This channel will be an important testing ground for analyzing what content resonates with Chinese consumers and which promotions are effective.

Hidden Variables to Consider

However, such strategies do not always guarantee success. The Chinese market is rapidly changing, and consumer preferences are continuously evolving. It remains uncertain whether the channels established by Lotte Department Store will actually lead to an increase in Yuker visits, and it should also be considered that utilizing these platforms could potentially leave a negative impression on consumers.

Points to Check Now

  • Changes in Yuker visit numbers and sales after the operation of Lotte Department Store's official channels.
  • Reactions to Lotte Department Store-related content provided on Godeok Map and Dazhong Dianping.
  • Whether other retailers will adopt similar strategies and their outcomes.

FAQ

A1: The official channel on Godeok Map will start on the 18th, and the official channel on Dazhong Dianping will start on the 25th.

Q2: How will promotions to attract Yuker be conducted?
A2: Lotte Department Store plans to conduct promotions through special events like Global Shopping Week.

Q3: If this channel is successful, is there a possibility that other department stores will follow suit?
A3: Yes, if it becomes a successful case, it is highly likely that other retailers will adopt similar strategies.
