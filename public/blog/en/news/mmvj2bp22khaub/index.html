70-year-old mother and 30-year-old son found dead in Gunsan apartment

A tragic incident occurred in an apartment in Gunsan, Jeollabuk-do, where a 70-year-old mother and her 30-year-old son were found dead. On the afternoon of the 18th, the landlord discovered them during a visit to check on the tenants due to unpaid rent. According to police investigations, the bodies were in a state of significant decomposition, and there were no signs of external intrusion. The police currently assess the possibility of homicide as low and plan to investigate the cause of death and any financial difficulties.
