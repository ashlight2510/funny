North Korea convenes Supreme People's Assembly on the 22nd... Kim Yo-jong, Jo Yong-won, and other 15th-term delegates revealed

North Korea is convening the Supreme People's Assembly on the 22nd and has released the list of 15th-term delegates. This meeting is expected to include the election of the Chairman of the State Affairs Commission and the election of key national figures. Notably, with Kim Jong-un's reappointment being highly likely, key figures such as Kim Yo-jong and Jo Yong-won have been named as delegates. This has drawn attention to North Korea's power structure and future policy directions.

This Supreme People's Assembly is an important event that reflects North Korea's political changes and economic situation. The results of the delegate elections and the key issues to be discussed at the meeting are expected to have a direct impact on North Korea's future policy decisions.

The convening of this Supreme People's Assembly is closely related to the political trends in North Korea.
