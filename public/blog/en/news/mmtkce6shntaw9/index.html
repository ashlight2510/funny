Shinsegae to Push for the Construction of the Largest 'AI Data Center' in Korea

Shinsegae Group is collaborating with the American AI company Reflection AI to construct the largest AI data center in Korea. This data center is expected to be built as a 'full-stack AI factory' providing cloud services and customized AI solutions, with Shinsegae planning to leverage AI as a new growth engine. Notably, this project is part of Shinsegae's strategy to integrate AI technology into its retail-centric business, aiming to usher in the era of 'AI Commerce', with the data center's power capacity expected to reach 250MW.

This project is also related to the U.S. government's policy on the dissemination of AI technology, and Shinsegae plans to utilize data and infrastructure at customer touchpoints to provide differentiated AI services. Shinsegae Group Chairman Jeong Yong-jin emphasized that AI will be an essential element across future industries and stated that this collaboration will contribute to the development of the domestic AI ecosystem.

Key Highlights

  • Shinsegae and Reflection AI are collaborating to build the largest AI data center in Korea.
  • The data center is expected to provide cloud services and customized AI solutions.
  • With a power capacity of 250MW, it will exceed existing data centers in Korea.
  • The goal is to lead retail innovation through AI and usher in the era of 'AI Commerce'.
  • The project is linked to the U.S. government's policy on the dissemination of AI technology.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The construction of this AI data center can be seen as an effort to integrate AI into Shinsegae's existing retail business model. Based on its long-standing retail experience, Shinsegae aims to utilize customer data to provide tailored services, making a large-scale data center essential. In particular, the collaboration with Reflection AI focuses on securing a stable GPU supply chain and building innovative business models utilizing AI technology.

Why It Matters

This news is significant not only for Shinsegae's business expansion but also as an important issue related to the development of the domestic AI ecosystem. The integration of AI technology into the retail industry could radically transform customer experiences, potentially impacting other industries as well. Additionally, given that this project is supported by the U.S. government, it holds great significance as an international cooperation model.
