Gyeonggi Provincial Office 24th Floor Restaurant Fire... Employees Evacuated for a Time

At 9:24 AM on the 3rd, a fire broke out in the cafeteria on the 24th floor of the Gyeonggi Provincial Office in Yongtong-gu, Suwon City. This fire led to the evacuation of employees, and the fire department quickly responded, extinguishing the flames by around 9:40 AM. Following the fire alarm, some employees evacuated the building, and the fire department is currently investigating the cause of the fire.

This incident is noteworthy as it may not end as a simple fire, highlighting the need for a review of the safety management system at the Gyeonggi Provincial Office and the safety awareness of employees.
