Gangnam Late Night Autonomous Car, The Beginning of a New Traffic Paradigm

Recently, a pilot service for autonomous vehicles by Kakao Mobility has started in Gangnam, Seoul. This vehicle, based on the Kia EV6, operates during late-night hours to safely transport passengers to their destinations autonomously. Notably, the vehicle emphasizes safety by stopping in front of crosswalks by itself, and it was impressive to see an autonomous driving manager onboard monitoring the situation. This service operates from 10 PM to 5 AM the following day on weekdays and is currently offered for free.

This pilot service is an important example demonstrating the practicality of autonomous driving technology and heralds changes in future traffic systems. However, various elements indicate that it has not yet reached the stage of fully unmanned driving, leading to ongoing concerns about safety.

Key Summary

  • Kakao Mobility has launched an autonomous driving pilot service in Gangnam.
  • EV6-based vehicles safely transport passengers during late-night hours.
  • The vehicle emphasizes safety by adjusting speed and stopping at crosswalks autonomously.
  • An autonomous driving manager is onboard to monitor the situation, and manual driving is switched in some sections.
  • Currently offered as a free service, but waiting times are required.

Key Scenes

  • Technological Advancement: The first instance of autonomous driving technology applied on actual roads.

  • Monitoring System: Ensured safety through the presence of an autonomous driving manager.
  • Free Offering: A free service to attract initial users.

Why Background Matters

This autonomous vehicle pilot service was initiated after Kakao Mobility was selected as a passenger transport operator by the Seoul city government. Autonomous driving technology has the potential to reduce traffic accidents and improve traffic flow, prompting many companies to enter this market. Innovative approaches are particularly needed in large cities like Seoul to address traffic congestion and safety issues.

Why It Matters

This news goes beyond mere technological advancement and could significantly impact future traffic systems and urban life. If autonomous vehicles become a pillar of public transportation, they offer an opportunity to simultaneously solve traffic congestion and environmental issues. Additionally, their potential as a safe means of transportation could alleviate burdens on many drivers.
