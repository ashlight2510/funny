Original article at a glance Title: ‘North Korea Sports Diplomacy’ Former IOC Member Jang Woong Passes Away…Led Joint Entry of Table Tennis Unified Team at Olympics Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun The International Olympic Committee (IOC) announced on the 1st that former IOC member Jang Woong, who was in charge of North Korea's sports diplomacy, passed away on the 29th of last month at the age of 87. He contributed significantly to inter-Korean sports exchanges, leading the participation of the unified team at the 1991 World Table Tennis Championships and the joint entry at the 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics. The IOC expressed deep sorrow upon hearing the news of his passing and plans to lower the flag at the Olympic House in his honor. Among his surviving family members are a son who was a goalkeeper for the North Korean national football team and a daughter who is an international volleyball referee.

Jang Woong's death is expected to have a significant impact beyond just the loss of an individual, affecting sports diplomacy on the Korean Peninsula and inter-Korean relations. His efforts contributed to easing tensions and enhancing mutual understanding through sports exchanges between the North and South. Attention is now focused on how his legacy will continue and the future direction of North Korea's sports diplomacy.
