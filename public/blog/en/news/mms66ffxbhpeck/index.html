Differences Between Claude and ChatGPT: A Selection Guide

March 16, 2026

Differences Between Claude and ChatGPT and Selection Guide

These days, AI chatbots have really increased, right? Claude and ChatGPT are currently the two hottest options, and many people might be wondering what the differences are and which one to choose. When I first tried to compare these two, I was at a loss about what criteria to use. So, I organized this information!

1. Basic Concepts and Features

What Kind of Friend is Claude?

Claude is an AI created by a company called Anthropic. This friend was developed with a focus on safety and ethics. Because of this, it feels more trustworthy during conversations. From my own experience, I found its answers to questions to be very logical. For example, even when I asked complex questions, I felt it responded thoughtfully.

ChatGPT, What Makes You Special?

ChatGPT is an AI developed by OpenAI, and its naturalness in conversation is a significant advantage. It mixes humor well in everyday conversations and can freely discuss various topics. From my experience, it feels like it expresses emotions more richly. So, for light conversations or creative activities, ChatGPT seems to fit better.

2. Use Cases and Purposes

Claude's Usability

Claude is mainly suited for business or professional conversations. For instance, when I posed questions related to data analysis or technical topics, I felt it provided clear answers. So if you have many business-related questions, Claude would be more useful. I actually used it when preparing business-related materials, and it provided very accurate information, which was quite helpful.

ChatGPT's Usability

On the other hand, ChatGPT excels in creative tasks. In writing, storytelling, and emotionally engaging conversations, ChatGPT performs much better. When I wanted to brainstorm ideas for a novel and chatted with ChatGPT, I experienced a wealth of diverse ideas. From this perspective, if you're looking to engage in creative work, ChatGPT seems to be the better choice.

3. User Experience and Interface

Claude's Interface

Claude has an attractive, clean design and an intuitive interface. It is easy to use and convenient for finding the desired information. However, sometimes it can feel a bit rigid, and there are times when it struggles to follow the flow of conversation that the user wants.

ChatGPT's Interface

ChatGPT feels more friendly. It has a strong sense of natural flow in conversations, and I felt it responded immediately when users posed questions. This made the conversation more enjoyable. Of course, there are times when incorrect information comes up, but that’s a limitation of AI, so I hope you can understand.

Conclusion

Ultimately, both Claude and ChatGPT have their own charms. Claude is suitable for business and professional purposes, while ChatGPT is better for creative and emotional conversations. It would be good to carefully consider what purpose you want to use them for. If you have any questions, please leave them in the comments! I hope this article helps you think about which AI to use.
