Emart24 Opens K-Food Lab in Myeongdong: The Allure of the Ramen Archive

Recently, Emart24 opened the ‘K-Food Lab Myeongdong’ in Seoul's Myeongdong. This store is a specialized location where customers can experience K-Food, K-Beauty, and K-Pop in one space, with particular attention on the ramen archive wall located on the second floor. It showcases about 170 different types of ramen, introducing foreign tourists to Korean ramen culture and providing a space where they can cook and enjoy ramen themselves. The store operates 24 hours and is characterized by its enhanced K-Culture experience elements.

Such specialized stores are a strategic approach by Emart24 to maximize the appeal of K-Culture in the Myeongdong area, which is frequented by foreign tourists, and to provide differentiated content alongside the recently launched dessert-specialized stores.

Why Background Matters

Emart24 has been opening specialized stores in recent years to keep pace with changing consumer trends. The Myeongdong area, known as a hub of K-Culture, is frequented by many foreign tourists, and the emergence of such stores is expected to contribute to attracting tourists and increasing sales. The K-Food Lab Myeongdong aims to reflect this trend by serving as a space to experience Korean food culture.

Why It Matters

The K-Food Lab Myeongdong is not just a store selling ramen. It has the potential to become a platform for promoting Korean food culture and K-Culture to the world. It offers foreign tourists the opportunity to experience a variety of Korean flavors and can instill a positive image of Korean food culture.
