Tax Deductions You Might Overlook When Filing: 2023 Essential Checklist

As tax season approaches, it seems many people are filled with worries. "Did I miss any deductions?" or "How much tax do I have to pay this time?" These are common concerns. So, this time, I’ve compiled a list of tax deductions that are easy to overlook when filing in 2023. I’ll also share some useful tips from my own experience!

1. Medical Expense Deduction

Remembering Medical Expenses That Were More Than I Thought

The medical expense deduction is one of the items that is really easy to miss. Last year, I spent quite a bit on hospital bills while taking care of a sick friend, and I ended up forgetting to include this when filing my taxes. Medical expenses include not only your own but also your family’s expenditures, so it’s important to keep track.

As far as I know, the range of medical expenses that can be deducted is quite broad. It includes hospital bills, medication costs, and even transportation costs for treatment. So this year, I made sure to keep all my receipts organized, and I was able to deduct more than I expected. I really recommend checking this part carefully!

2. Education Expense Deduction

Education Expenses for Children Are a Must-Deduct

If you have children, you must not forget the education expense deduction. I once overlooked my child's tutoring fees when filing taxes, and I regretted it immensely. Education expenses include tutoring fees, tuition, and textbook costs, so it’s best to prepare in advance this year.

Especially this year, due to COVID-19, there have been many online classes. These costs can also be included in the education expense deduction, so I want to emphasize checking this carefully once more. I’ll share the documents I prepared; they will be helpful!

3. Charitable Contribution Deduction

Donations Are Good, But Don’t Forget Tax Deductions

If you make a lot of donations, you should not forget about the charitable contribution deduction. In my opinion, donating is good for me, but it also helps a lot when filing taxes. Especially, amounts donated at the end of the year can be deducted in the following year's tax filing, so it’s important to keep the receipts well.

From my experience, donating gives me peace of mind, and at the same time, I can save on taxes, so it feels like a win-win. Therefore, I plan to donate more this year. It would be great to be a smart spender who enjoys donating while also taking advantage of tax deductions!

4. Transportation Expense Deduction

Don’t Forget About Transportation Costs for Commuting!

Did you know that transportation costs incurred during commuting can be deducted? This was something I learned for the first time, and it can be quite useful for those who commute by subway or bus. If you keep your annual transportation receipts, you can claim deductions when filing taxes.

I learned about this thanks to a friend's recommendation. Transportation costs can add up to a significant amount, so this year, I kept track and filed, which resulted in a substantial tax reduction. So, I hope you check the transportation expense deduction!

We’ve looked at various tax deductions that are easy to overlook when filing for 2023. With medical expenses, education expenses, charitable contributions, and transportation costs, it’s best not to miss anything and to check thoroughly this time. If you have any questions or want to know more, feel free to leave a comment!
