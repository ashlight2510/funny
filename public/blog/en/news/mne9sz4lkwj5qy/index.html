Floods from Heavy Rain in Afghanistan and Pakistan Lead to Serious Casualties

Recent heavy rain in Afghanistan and Pakistan has resulted in severe flooding. This disaster has led to the deaths of 45 people and injuries to 74, with the damage primarily concentrated in central and eastern Afghanistan. In particular, in Afghanistan, 1,140 households have been affected as homes and agricultural lands were inundated, and there have been multiple incidents of house collapses in Pakistan. This natural disaster is intertwined with the ongoing armed conflict, further complicating the situation.

The recent heavy rain and flooding are not merely natural disasters but are complex issues intertwined with climate change and political instability, causing greater suffering for the local residents.
