How to Maximize Year-End Tax Refunds for Young Adults

March 16, 2026

How Young People Can Avoid Missing Year-End Tax Refunds

Young people have many opportunities to receive refunds through year-end tax settlements. This is very important as it can help reduce economic burdens. Let's explore how to ensure you don't miss out on refunds through the correct procedures.

Year-end tax settlement is not just a simple tax declaration; it is a process that provides the opportunity to receive refunds based on the expenses you have incurred. Understanding and preparing for this process will be very helpful for young people.

What is Year-End Tax Settlement?

Year-end tax settlement is the process of organizing income and tax amounts over the year to determine the final tax amount. Through this process, you can receive a refund of some of the taxes you have paid. This is particularly important for young people, as they may have low income or various expenses in the early stages of employment.

Refunds are amounts returned to you for taxes you have overpaid through year-end tax settlement. Refunds are calculated through various deductions related to income tax.

Checkpoint

  • Understand why refunds may occur through year-end tax settlement and review your spending history.

Essential Documents Young People Need for Year-End Tax Settlement

Required Document List

To prepare for year-end tax settlement, several essential documents are needed. The main documents include income certificates, expense certificates, and various deduction certificates.

Checklist: Documents to Prepare

  • Wage withholding receipt
  • Medical expense certificate
  • Education expense certificate
  • Donation receipt

Having these documents prepared in advance will make the year-end tax settlement process much smoother.

How to Collect Online Materials

Using the National Tax Service's Home Tax system, you can easily collect the necessary materials. After logging into Home Tax, you can quickly check various deduction items using the 'Year-End Tax Settlement Simplification Service' menu.

Quick Search Tips for Required Materials

  • Utilize the search function of Home Tax to easily find the necessary documents.
  • Organizing the required deduction items in advance will make searching more efficient.

Organizing Income and Expense Records

It is important to systematically organize your annual income and expense records. Even if your income is not high, if your expenses are significant, you may still receive a refund.

Caution: Check for Missing Items

  • When organizing expense items, create a checklist to ensure even small costs are included.
  • For example, transportation and meal costs can also be included for employees.

Calculating Refunds

The basic principle of refund calculation is to subtract tax deductions from annual income to calculate the final tax amount. There are various tax deduction items that young people should pay attention to.

Types of Tax Deductions

There are several types of tax deductions, including basic tax deductions and special tax deductions. It is essential to understand the application conditions and precautions for each deduction.

Application Conditions for Each Deduction

  • Basic tax deduction: Applies to all workers
  • Special tax deduction: Applies only if specific conditions are met

Example of Refund Calculation

For example, let's assume that a young person, Mr. A, has an annual income of 30 million won and has spent 2 million won on medical expenses. Mr. A can calculate his refund by applying the basic tax deduction and the medical expense tax deduction.

Refund = (Income - Tax Deductions) × Tax Rate

By calculating in this way, you can estimate your expected refund in advance.

Execution Tip

  • Try calculating your refund based on your income and expense records.

Year-End Tax Settlement Application Procedure

The year-end tax settlement application must be submitted within a specified deadline and can be easily done through Home Tax.

The application method is simple. Access Home Tax, select the 'Year-End Tax Settlement Report' menu, enter the required information, and submit it. The deadline is usually between late January and early February each year.

Post-Application Confirmation

After applying, it is essential to check the application status. Refund payment schedules usually occur around mid-March, so you will need to wait until then.

How to Check Application Status

  • You can check through the 'Application History Inquiry' menu on Home Tax.
  • If the refund is less than expected or delayed, check immediately.

Common Mistakes and Precautions

There are various common mistakes that occur during year-end tax settlement, such as missing documents or selecting incorrect deduction items.

Tips to Prevent Mistakes

Checklist: Points to Check to Avoid Mistakes

  • Confirm that all submitted documents are prepared
  • Check if deduction items are correctly applied
  • Verify the latest tax law changes from the National Tax Service

By conducting these checks, you can minimize mistakes.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: When will the year-end tax refund be paid?
A1: It is usually paid around mid-March. You can check the application status on Home Tax.

Q2: Do I need to do a year-end tax settlement if I have no income?
A2: If you have no income, you do not need to do a year-end tax settlement, but you may still receive a refund under certain conditions.

Q3: Why is the refund less than expected?
A3: It may be due to deduction items not being properly reflected or an increase in income leading to higher taxes.

Year-end tax settlement is an important opportunity for young people to reduce their economic burdens. To avoid missing out on refunds, it is essential to prepare the necessary documents in advance and follow the application procedures accurately. Start organizing the necessary documents right away!
