“Safety Management as the Top Priority”…Kim Hoe-cheon Takes Office as President of Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power

Kim Hoe-cheon, the new president of Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power (KHNP), held his inauguration ceremony at the Gyeongju headquarters on the 18th, expressing his commitment to prioritize nuclear safety management. He emphasized the uniqueness of the nuclear industry, stating that radiation management and ensuring safety are essential elements for the company's existence. Kim also outlined plans to enhance KHNP's competitiveness through the construction of new nuclear power plants, the establishment of spent nuclear fuel management systems, and the strengthening of decommissioning technologies.

This inauguration is expected to serve as a significant turning point, presenting important directions related to nuclear safety. Kim demonstrated his readiness to lead KHNP into the future based on his past experiences.

Key Summary

  • President Kim Hoe-cheon prioritizes safety management as the foremost value.
  • Emphasizes the uniqueness of the nuclear industry and the importance of radiation management and safety assurance.
  • Plans for new nuclear power plant construction and spent nuclear fuel management system establishment.
  • Chooses the Gori Nuclear Power Headquarters as his first site management, emphasizing safe operations.

Key Scenes

  • Priority on Safety Management: Commitment to prioritize nuclear safety.
  • New Nuclear Power Construction: Promotion of large nuclear power plants and SMR construction according to the 11th Basic Plan for Power Supply and Demand.
  • Responsible Management System: Establishment of management systems for spent nuclear fuel and radioactive waste.
  • Strengthening Global Market Competitiveness: Export of KHNP's integrated management model and development of SMR technology.
  • Building Trust: Aiming to become a KHNP trusted by the public.

Why Background Matters

President Kim Hoe-cheon's inauguration comes at a time when nuclear safety issues have emerged as a social concern. Over the past few years, growing worries about nuclear accidents have led to a widespread recognition that the safety of nuclear power is directly linked to the company's survival. Therefore, Kim's declaration to prioritize safety management is seen as a timely and necessary measure.

Why It Matters

Nuclear safety is an issue that goes beyond corporate concerns and is directly related to the lives of citizens. The safety management emphasized by Kim is expected to enhance the reliability of nuclear operations and positively influence future nuclear policies and the industry as a whole. Additionally, plans for new nuclear power construction are crucial factors that can contribute to stable power supply.
