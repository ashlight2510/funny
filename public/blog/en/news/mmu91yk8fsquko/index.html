Guro-gu, Gocheok Open Library Selected for 'Mikkumso National Expansion Project'

The news that Guro-gu has been selected for the 'Mikkumso National Expansion Project' through the Gocheok Open Library holds significance beyond just a library initiative. This project aims to distribute creative programs for children and teenagers nationwide, allowing the Gocheok Open Library to provide various reading and creative experiences. In particular, hands-on programs where children create things themselves will greatly help in fostering their creativity by combining reading and making.

These programs offer children opportunities to express their imagination, going beyond mere reading. Guro-gu aims to enhance children's reading culture and strengthen the educational foundation of the local community through this initiative.

Key Summary

  • Gocheok Open Library in Guro-gu has been selected for the 'Mikkumso National Expansion Project.'
  • The project distributes library-based creative programs for children and teenagers.
  • Selected libraries receive various support for program operation.
  • The first program will be 'Creating Props from Fairy Tales,' including hands-on creative experiences for children.
  • The library plans to continue creative reading activities even after the project ends.

Key Highlights

  • Project Selection: Gocheok Open Library has been granted the authority to operate the 'Mikkumso' program.
  • Support Details: Use of signage and logos, workshops for enhancing librarian capabilities, dispatch of professional instructors, etc.
  • Program Operation: Conducting hands-on programs where children read fairy tales and create props.
  • Sustainability: Plans to utilize equipment for creative reading activities even after the project ends.
  • Community Contribution: Efforts to enhance children's reading culture and creativity.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The 'Mikkumso' project is a program organized by the National Library for Children and Young Adults, selecting 17 libraries annually since 2021. It was initiated to provide children with opportunities to express themselves through reading and creation. Gocheok Open Library was selected because it can effectively combine reading programs and creative activities tailored to the needs of the local community.

Why It Matters

This project is significant not merely for increasing library programs but for providing children with the joy of reading and fostering creative thinking simultaneously. Especially for children living in the digital age, experiences with books and creation can serve as vital educational resources. This will further strengthen the educational foundation of the local community and contribute to creating a platform for children to grow through diverse experiences.

Points to Note

In my view, the particularly important aspect of this project is the 'hands-on program.' The sense of achievement and confidence that children gain from the process of creating, beyond just reading, will significantly impact their emotional and social development. Additionally, these programs can positively benefit more children through collaboration with local children's centers.

Hidden Variables to Consider

For this project to proceed successfully, ongoing interest and participation are necessary. It is crucial that the programs offered by the library do not remain one-off events but develop sustainably through connections with the local community. Furthermore, diversifying the programs to allow participation from children of various ages and backgrounds should also be considered.

Current Checkpoints

  • Participation Method: Check how to participate in the program through collaboration with local children's centers.
  • Follow-up Programs: It is advisable to gather information in advance about the creative reading activities that will continue after the project ends.
  • Local Library News: Pay close attention to the upcoming program schedules and details at Gocheok Open Library.

FAQ

A: The Mikkumso program is a library-based creative program for children and teenagers, offering various experiential activities that combine reading and creation.
