ATM Fee Waiver Targeting Repeated Withdrawals 600 Times a Day... Supreme Court "Recognizes Fraud"

Recently, the Supreme Court delivered a guilty verdict to a group that repeatedly withdrew cash more than 600 times a day to exploit ATM fee waivers. They committed fraud by withdrawing small amounts abnormally and receiving a portion of the fees incurred in the process, taking advantage of Kakao Bank's fee waiver benefits. The court ruled that their actions constituted fraud and imposed fines.

This case reveals vulnerabilities in the financial system beyond simple financial fraud. The ability to repeatedly withdraw through ATMs raises the need for discussions to prevent similar crimes in the future.
