Harmony of Tradition and Modernity in Japan: Must-Visit Places in Tokyo

Are you wondering where to go when planning a trip to Tokyo? Tokyo, where tradition and modernity harmoniously blend, is a city full of charm in itself. So, in this article, I've organized must-visit places for you.

Shinjuku - The Center of Modernity

Enjoy the Glamour of the Night

Shinjuku is one of the busiest areas in Tokyo. Especially in the evening, the streets filled with neon signs are truly spectacular. When I visited Shinjuku, the izakaya food I had under the streetlights was really delicious. There are various restaurants lined up, making it fun to decide what to eat.

Healing at Shinjuku Gyoen

When you go to Shinjuku, be sure to visit Shinjuku Gyoen as well. It's a place where you can enjoy nature in the heart of the city. Especially in spring, the cherry blossoms are in full bloom, creating a stunning view. Personally, I enjoyed having a simple picnic here. It's a space where you can take a breather away from the noise of the city.

Asakusa - The Charm of Tradition

Experience History at Senso-ji

Asakusa is a place where you can feel the traditional charm of Tokyo. There is a famous temple called Senso-ji, where you can deeply experience Japan's history and culture. When I visited Senso-ji, there were many tourists gathered, and their reverent demeanor was impressive. Don't miss out on enjoying the various snacks sold at the entrance!

Exploring Nakamise Street

Nakamise Street, located in front of Senso-ji, is a place where you can buy traditional snacks and souvenirs. I bought souvenirs here for my friends later, and everyone loved them. It's also fun to taste various snacks, so be sure to stop by.

Shibuya - The Street of Youth

Shibuya Scramble Crossing

Shibuya is famous as a street for young people. Shibuya Scramble Crossing is bustling with people, and just experiencing that atmosphere is an exciting experience. When I first went there, I was amazed by how people disappeared and reappeared all at once. Don't miss out on enjoying such a unique experience.

With the Hachiko Statue

When you go to Shibuya, you must visit the Hachiko statue as well. This statue, famous as a symbol of loyalty, is a great spot for taking photos. Many people take selfies in front of it. I remember standing there, recalling Hachiko's story and getting lost in thought for a moment.

Conclusion

Tokyo is a city where tradition and modernity harmoniously blend. By visiting Shinjuku, Asakusa, and Shibuya, you will truly experience a variety of charms. If you have any questions, leave them in the comments! And it would be great if you could visit at least one of these places first. I hope you prepare well for your trip and have a wonderful time!
