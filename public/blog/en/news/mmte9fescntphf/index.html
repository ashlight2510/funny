Greece Approves Purchase of Drone Defense Network Worth 5.1 Trillion Won

The Greek parliament has approved the purchase of a drone defense network worth 3 billion euros, or approximately 5.1 trillion won. This defense network, known as the "Achilles Shield," plans to acquire necessary missiles in cooperation with Israel. This decision is part of Greece's strategy to spend a total of 28 billion euros on military modernization by 2036, which also includes the approval of performance upgrades for F-16 fighter jets.

This news goes beyond mere military expansion; it is intertwined with Greece's diplomatic and security strategy, as well as the military tensions in Eastern Europe. Therefore, this issue is not just a military investment but could significantly impact regional security and international relations.

Key Summary

  • Greek parliament approves purchase of a 3 billion euro drone defense network.

  • Approval of F-16 fighter jet performance upgrades is also part of the military modernization plan.
  • A total expenditure of 28 billion euros is planned by 2036.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Greece has been actively investing in military modernization and strengthening its defense capabilities in recent years. Particularly, as tensions with neighboring countries, such as conflicts with Turkey, have intensified, there has been a pressing need to enhance military readiness. In this context, the purchase of the drone defense network is seen as an important measure to increase Greece's military autonomy.

Why It Matters

The purchase of this drone defense network is not just about strengthening military capabilities; it is deeply connected to Greece's foreign policy. In particular, cooperation with Israel is linked to military trends in the Middle East, which could provide an opportunity to strengthen Greece's position in international politics.

Points to Watch

Greece is already facing economic difficulties, and large-scale military expansion funded by taxpayer money may provoke public backlash. Additionally, there are questions about how effectively the drone defense network can be operated.
