President Yoon requests the National Assembly to “review the war supplementary budget as quickly as possible”

Recently, President Yoon Suk-yeol has requested the National Assembly to expedite the review of the supplementary budget related to the war. This request stems from concerns that the lives of vulnerable groups and ordinary citizens will become even more difficult as military tensions escalate between the U.S. and Iran. President Yoon emphasized that the government will do its utmost to protect the lives of the people by turning the crisis into an opportunity and urged the National Assembly to conduct a swift budget review.

This request highlights the disparity where certain companies are profiting during the war, while the majority of citizens are facing greater hardships. President Yoon mentioned that income support policies will be essential and that the supplementary budget should be allocated more extensively to local areas.
