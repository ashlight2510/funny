Increase in Lawmakers' Wealth, Where is the Economic Recession?

According to recently released data, the average wealth of 287 members of the 22nd National Assembly is approximately 2.88 billion won. Interestingly, despite the economic difficulties last year, 9 out of 10 lawmakers reported an increase in their wealth. Notably, lawmakers Ahn Cheol-soo and Park Deok-heum reported wealth exceeding 50 billion won, remaining as high-income reporters. These results are based on property declaration records released by the National Assembly's Public Officials Ethics Committee.

These statistics are not just mere numbers. The background of wealth increase involves various interests and political contexts, which hold significant meaning for the public. In particular, the sight of politicians increasing their wealth amid an economic recession can serve as a source of public discontent.

Key Summary

  • Average wealth of 22nd National Assembly members: approximately 2.88 billion won.
  • 9 out of 10 lawmakers have increased their wealth, with 88.5% reporting an increase compared to previous declarations.
  • Lawmakers reporting wealth over 50 billion won: Ahn Cheol-soo and Park Deok-heum.
  • Scale of wealth increase: 62.2% reported an increase of between 100 million and 500 million won.
  • Multiple property owners: Many lawmakers, regardless of party affiliation, own multiple properties in the metropolitan area.

Why is it Important?

This announcement carries more significance than mere statistics. The increase in politicians' wealth, even in the face of an economic recession, can lead to trust issues with the public. Ordinary citizens may feel discontent when contrasting their economic hardships with the wealth increase of politicians, which could lead to political instability. Particularly, the issue of multiple property ownership remains sensitive as it is directly related to housing issues.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The property declarations were made during a period when the economic recession intensified last year. Nevertheless, the increase in lawmakers' wealth suggests that various political and economic factors are at play. It appears that the influence of the real estate market was significant, indicating that politicians have favorable conditions for asset management. The issue of multiple property ownership is particularly sensitive and can provoke public dissatisfaction regarding housing matters.
