Tax Deductions You Might Overlook When Filing Your Tax Return

March 15, 2026

Summary of Easily Overlooked Tax Deductions When Filing Taxes

Every time I file my taxes, I feel overwhelmed about what and how to report. Especially with tax deductions, missing them means losing out on money, which makes it even more stressful. So, I've compiled a list of tax deductions that are easy to overlook when filing taxes. Knowing this will make the filing process much smoother!

1. Medical Expense Tax Deduction

Medical Expense Deduction, Easy to Overlook

The medical expense tax deduction is one of the most useful items I've personally utilized. I tend to visit the hospital frequently each year, so I kept track of my medical expenses and reported them, resulting in a significant refund.

What Medical Expenses Are Included

Medical expenses include not only hospital treatment costs and medication but also dental treatment costs and glasses purchases. It’s easy to overlook this, but by keeping good records of my hospital visits, I was able to receive more deductions than I expected. So, this is definitely something to keep track of!

2. Education Expense Tax Deduction

Child Education Expenses, Don't Miss Out

Families with children should definitely check for education expense tax deductions. Since it includes all educational expenses from kindergarten to university, I found it to be really helpful based on my experience.

What Items Are Included

Various items such as university tuition, academy fees, and textbook costs are eligible for tax deductions. Particularly, private education expenses can also be deducted, so it’s important to keep track of this part. If I hadn't properly accounted for this, it would have been a significant loss.

3. Charitable Contribution Tax Deduction

You Can Get Tax Deductions for Donations Too

Did you know that you can receive tax deductions for donations? I don’t donate often, but every time I do, I make sure to keep the receipts and report them, resulting in a pretty decent tax refund.

What Donations Are Included

Donations to registered charitable organizations recognized by the National Tax Service are eligible for tax deductions. Receiving tax deductions through donations makes me feel good, and it’s a win-win situation as I can help someone in need. It’s worth keeping track of these donation items!

4. Insurance Premium Tax Deduction

Insurance Premiums Are Also Deductible

Lastly, don’t forget about the insurance premium tax deduction! Although insurance premiums are paid monthly, reporting them as tax deductions can lead to a larger refund than you might expect.

What Insurance Is Included

Primarily, life insurance, health insurance, and pension insurance are eligible for tax deductions. From my experience of receiving tax deductions each year, I’ve realized that keeping track of insurance premiums can be helpful later on. It’s quite rewarding!

Conclusion

I’ve summarized the tax deductions that are easy to overlook when filing taxes. In fact, keeping track of these can make the tax refund process enjoyable! If you have any questions, feel free to leave them in the comments. It would be great to check these items off your list while filing your taxes this time!
