The only 'Nano Material Safety Evaluation Center' in Korea opens in Miryang, Gyeongnam

Recently, the 'Nano Material and Product Safety Evaluation Support Center', the only institution for nano material safety evaluation in Korea, has opened in Miryang, Gyeongnam. This center is located within the Nano Convergence National Industrial Complex and is equipped with state-of-the-art testing equipment to scientifically verify the harmfulness of nano materials and produce reliable safety evaluation data. As a result, companies are expected to conduct safety evaluations of nano products more quickly and at lower costs.

The opening of this center is expected to have a significant impact on the growth of the nano industry and entry into international markets. Gyeongnam Province aims to solidify its position as a 'mecca of the nano industry' through this initiative. Given that safety evaluations and international certifications of nano materials are essential for industrial growth, the establishment of this center is highly significant.

Key Summary

  • Opening of the nano material safety evaluation center in Miryang, Gyeongnam.
  • The center is equipped with 62 types of state-of-the-art testing equipment to verify the harmfulness of nano materials.
  • Expectations for reduced testing and evaluation time and costs for nano products.
  • Gyeongnam Province plans to grow as a 'mecca of the nano industry' through this initiative.
  • The center aims to be designated as an internationally accredited testing institution by 2026.

Key Highlights

  • Only in Korea: An institution specializing in nano material safety evaluation, established outside the metropolitan area.
  • Cost Reduction: While using existing foreign institutions costs between 700 million to 1.3 billion won, costs will be reduced to 500 million to 1 billion won when using the center.
  • Rapid Evaluation: The safety evaluation period is shortened from one year to about six months.
  • Industry Connection: Synergy effects are expected through collaboration with the Miryang Nano Convergence National Industrial Complex.
  • International Certification: Efforts to obtain designation from the Korea Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (KOLAS) and Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) institutions.

Why Background Context is Important

Nano materials are crucial resources that can be utilized across various industrial sectors. However, with insufficient verification of their safety, companies have had to rely on overseas testing institutions, leading to burdens in time and costs. To address these issues, Gyeongnam Province established the Nano Material Safety Evaluation Center. This initiative aims to invigorate the local economy and enhance the competitiveness of the nano industry.

Why It Matters

The opening of this center could be a pivotal turning point for the development of the nano material industry. Since safety evaluations and international certifications are essential for industrial growth, an environment is being created that allows companies to enter the market more easily. This will ultimately have a positive impact not only on the local economy but also on the national economy.
