Gyeonggi Province Party of the People Power Party, Single Recommendation for Candidates for Mayors of Gwangmyeong, Dongducheon, and Osan

Ahead of the 2026 6·3 local elections, the Gyeonggi Province Party of the People Power Party has made a single recommendation for candidates for local government heads in Gwangmyeong, Dongducheon, and Osan. The recommended candidates are Kim Jeong-ho, former representative member of the Gyeonggi Provincial Council from Gwangmyeong, Park Hyeong-deok, the current mayor of Dongducheon, and Lee Gwon-jae, the current mayor of Osan. They are expected to be officially confirmed as candidates after the final resolution of the Central Party's Supreme Committee.

This nomination is interpreted as a strategy to strengthen the position of the People Power Party in Gyeonggi Province and to achieve victory in the local elections. In particular, each candidate has already accumulated political experience in their respective regions, and their nomination is expected to promote the party's stability and continuity.
