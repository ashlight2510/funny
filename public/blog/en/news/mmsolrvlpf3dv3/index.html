Gyeongbuk Gumi, Home of Kyochon Chicken's First Store, Challenges to Create 'K-Chicken Belt'

Gyeongbuk Gumi City is embarking on the creation of a 'K-Chicken Belt' centered around Songjeong-dong, where Kyochon Chicken's first store is located. This project, supported by the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs, aims to establish a gourmet street specializing in chicken dishes. Gumi City is pushing this initiative based on the significant increase in sales and tourist numbers achieved through the already established 'Kyochon 1991 Cultural Street' in collaboration with Kyochon Chicken. The city plans to develop a gourmet tourism model linked to local tourism resources.

This news goes beyond the local success of a chicken brand, showcasing the potential for mutual growth between the local economy and the tourism industry. The success story of Kyochon Chicken serves as an important example of how a regional brand can gain national recognition, and through this, Gumi aims to create new tourism content.

Key Points

  • Creation of K-Chicken Belt: Establishing a gourmet street specializing in chicken dishes with support from the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs.
  • Kyochon 1991 Cultural Street: A cultural street established around Kyochon Chicken's first store, resulting in a 40% increase in sales and a 100% increase in visitors.
  • Linking Tourism Resources: Developing a gourmet tourism model in connection with the Geumo Mountain tourist area and the Gumi Ramen Festival.
  • Revitalizing the Local Economy: Expecting mutual growth of the local economy and tourism industry based on the success of Kyochon Chicken.
  • Cultural Content Development: Attracting tourists with limited menus and historical experiential content.

Background and Context

Kyochon Chicken is a brand that started in 1991 in Gyeongbuk Gumi and has now grown into a widely recognized chicken franchise across the country. This success can be attributed not only to delicious chicken but also to collaboration with the local community and the integration of cultural elements. Gumi City has been working to enhance the value and identity of local brands through collaboration with Kyochon Chicken, aiming to revitalize the local economy.

The 'K-Chicken Belt' is an extension of this trend, attempting to create a regionally specialized gourmet street with support from the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs. Gumi City aims to develop new tourism content based on the success of the already established cultural street.

Why This Issue is Important

This issue illustrates the interaction between the local economy and the tourism industry, going beyond just the success story of Kyochon Chicken. Through the 'K-Chicken Belt', Gumi City has the opportunity to strengthen local brands and attract tourists, thereby revitalizing the local economy. This could serve as a benchmark for other regions and contribute to enhancing the self-sufficiency and sustainability of local economies.
