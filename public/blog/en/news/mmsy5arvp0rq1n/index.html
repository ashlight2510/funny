Trump Calls for Participation of South Korea and Others in Policing the Hormuz Strait

Recently, former US President Donald Trump requested that several countries, including South Korea, send warships to patrol the Hormuz Strait. He addressed this issue through social media, emphasizing the need for an international joint response to Iran's attempts to block the strait. Trump stated, "Countries that import oil through the Hormuz Strait must protect that route, and we will provide a lot of help." This is interpreted as a call for multinational cooperation rather than a unilateral responsibility of the United States.

Trump's remarks are not merely a request but encompass a complex context intertwined with military tensions and economic interests in international affairs. The Hormuz Strait is a crucial route for global oil transportation and serves as an example of how Iran's military threats can manifest.

Key Points

  • Trump requested the deployment of warships from South Korea, Japan, China, France, and the UK for the security of the Hormuz Strait.
  • He identified Iran's attempts to block the strait as an international collective responsibility.
  • The US claims it will strengthen military operations to eliminate Iran's military threats.
  • South Korea has previous experience conducting independent operations in the Hormuz Strait.
  • Trump's remarks highlight the necessity of military cooperation in the international community.

Why Background Matters

This issue arises amid escalating tensions between Iran and the United States. Notably, during the Trump administration in 2020, South Korea conducted independent operations to escort its vessels in the Hormuz Strait. This was in response to a US request but was also a strategic choice to avoid conflict with Iran. With Iran's military threats resurfacing, Trump is using this opportunity to call for international military cooperation.

Why It Matters

This news goes beyond a mere military request and illustrates the complexities of international politics. The Hormuz Strait is a key corridor for global oil transportation, and Iran's military threats can have direct impacts on the global economy. Additionally, if allies like South Korea respond to the US request, it could lead to significant changes in South Korea's diplomatic and military policies.

Points to Watch

In my view, the most important aspect of this issue is what stance South Korea will take. Based on past experiences, South Korea needs to establish a balanced diplomatic strategy that maintains its alliance with the US while avoiding conflict with Iran. If South Korea decides to send warships, it could be an opportunity to further highlight its military role internationally.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While Trump's remarks emphasize the need for military cooperation in the international community, Iran's response and the positions of other countries are also crucial. Iran may assert its sovereignty and could respond militarily, which could directly impact the military operations of South Korea and the US. Therefore, it is essential to closely monitor Iran's reactions.

Current Points to Check

Attention should be paid to what official position the South Korean government will take on this issue. Additionally, an analysis of how military tensions between Iran and the US will unfold, and the direction of South Korea's foreign policy, is necessary.
