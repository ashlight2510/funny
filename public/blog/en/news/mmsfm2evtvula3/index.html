5 Ways to Save Money with the Best Card Benefits

Have you ever felt unsure about what benefits you get when using a card? There are many cards available, but it can be overwhelming to decide which one to choose. So, I’ve gathered the card benefits that I’ve personally tried and experienced. After reading this article, you’ll discover some really smart ways to save money!

1. Utilize Points Accumulation Cards

The Power of Points Cards

These days, almost every card offers points accumulation. For example, when I buy coffee at my favorite café using a points card, I can enjoy another cup for free next time. By using cards affiliated with frequently visited stores, you can enjoy even more benefits.

My Experience

Personally, I really liked the Starbucks card. Every time I bought a drink, points accumulated, and during specific events, I received additional points. So even when I went often with friends, it felt less burdensome. My recommendation is to look for a card that accumulates points well at the places you frequently visit.

2. Choose Cards with Many Discount Benefits

Practical Discount Cards

Choosing cards with many discount benefits can significantly save you money in your daily life. It’s especially useful to use cards that offer discounts at large supermarkets or online shopping malls.

My Experience

I use a Hana Card, which offers a 5% discount at large supermarkets. So, I was able to save a little every time I shopped. When I calculated at the end of the year, I realized I saved more than I expected. I think it would be good for you to look for cards with discount benefits at the supermarkets you frequently visit.

3. Prepare for Overseas Travel with Cards that Offer Travel Benefits

The Appeal of Travel Cards

If you’re planning an overseas trip, it’s important to use cards that offer travel benefits. There are various perks like flight discounts and hotel booking discounts. Using such cards can help you save a lot on travel expenses.

My Experience

I changed my credit card before a trip in the past, and thanks to the hotel discount benefits provided by the card company, I was able to book a nice place at a low price. I highly recommend switching to a travel-specific card when you’re going on a trip!

4. Compare Accumulation Cards and Cashback Cards

Accumulation vs. Cashback

Accumulation cards and cashback cards each have their pros and cons. Accumulation cards allow you to gather points to use later, while cashback cards provide immediate refunds, which feels more rewarding. It’s important to choose a card that fits your spending habits.

My Experience

I primarily use cashback cards, which allow me to receive a certain percentage of the amount I spend each month back in cash. For example, since I pay my rent with a card, I accumulate cashback little by little every month. I hope you think about which method is more advantageous for you.

Conclusion

Today, we explored 5 ways to save money with card benefits. There were various methods like points accumulation, discount benefits, travel perks, and comparing accumulation and cashback cards. If you utilize these tips well, you can save a significant amount of money. If you have any questions, leave them in the comments! I think starting with this would be a great idea.
