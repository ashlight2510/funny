Former Minister Won Hee-ryong Banned from Leaving the Country, New Phase in Yangpyeong Expressway Suspicion

Recently, it has been reported that former Minister of Land, Infrastructure and Transport Won Hee-ryong has been banned from leaving the country in relation to the preferential treatment allegations regarding the change of the endpoint of the Seoul-Yangpyeong Expressway. This incident arose after the launch of the Yoon Suk-yeol government, as the endpoint of the expressway was changed to connect with the land owned by the family of First Lady Kim Kun-hee, leading to allegations of abuse of power against former Minister Won. This ban on leaving the country has sparked interest in whether the full details of the case can be clarified.

This incident is not just a personal issue but a complex matter intertwined with government policy-making processes and political interests. Former Minister Won has previously stated that he would stake his political life on the claim that he was not involved, but the recent actions of the special investigation team present a situation that contradicts that statement.

Why Background Matters

This suspicion began in May 2023 when the endpoint of the Seoul-Yangpyeong Expressway was changed from Yangseo-myeon, Yangpyeong-gun, to Gang Sang-myeon. This change is closely related to the land owned by First Lady Kim Kun-hee's family, raising allegations of preferential treatment. Former Minister Won has denied these allegations at the center of the case, but the special investigation team has once again made him a target of investigation, complicating the situation. Notably, his previous statement about "staking his political life" enhances the scrutiny of his credibility.

Therefore, this case has the potential to expand beyond political ramifications to broader societal trust issues.
