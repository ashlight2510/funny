What is the age of the 360 oreums across Jeju?···Research on formation period underway

Research to determine the formation periods of oreums in Jeju has officially begun. The Jeju World Heritage Center has announced plans to confirm the formation periods of up to 200 oreums out of the 360 by 2028. So far, the eruption dates of about 90 oreums have been confirmed, and this research will play a crucial role in understanding volcanic activity in Jeju and predicting future volcanic activity.

This research is expected to enhance the geological understanding of Jeju by scientifically determining the formation periods of the oreums and strengthening their value as tourist resources. In particular, it is anticipated to have a positive impact on the protection of Jeju's unique volcanic terrain and ecosystem.

Key Highlights

  • Research is underway to determine the formation periods of over 360 oreums across Jeju.
  • The dating of up to 200 oreums is planned to be completed by 2028.
  • The eruption dates of about 90 oreums have been confirmed so far.
  • Provides foundational data for understanding and predicting volcanic activity.
  • Plans to expand collaboration with related research institutions for the construction of Jeju's geological map.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This research is part of a long-term project aimed at determining the formation periods of Jeju oreums, which has been revitalized since the 1980s with advancements in techniques. Initially, research was limited due to technical constraints and cost issues, but since the 2000s, precise techniques such as argon dating and carbon dating have been introduced, allowing for active research. This is essential for understanding the unique geological structure of Jeju and the historical context of its volcanic activity.

Why is it Important?

This research provides important foundational data not only for knowing the age of the oreums but also for understanding the volcanic activity and geological characteristics of Jeju. Additionally, as Jeju has high value as a tourist destination, the results of this research will significantly influence tourism resource development and conservation policies. Furthermore, it can contribute to the sustainable development and ecosystem protection of Jeju.

Practical Impact of Jeju Oreum Research

From the perspective of general readers or practitioners, this research can directly impact Jeju's tourism industry. Knowing the formation periods of oreums can be a crucial factor in developing tourist routes, thereby providing visitors to Jeju with a richer experience. Additionally, the research findings may be reflected in Jeju's ecosystem protection policies, positively affecting local residents and the ecosystem.

Points to Note

In my view, the most noteworthy aspect of this research is the advancement of scientific approaches. Compared to past techniques, current precise methods provide much more reliable data. However, challenges remain regarding costs and technical limitations during the research process. For instance, it is uncertain whether dating can be conducted for all oreums, and there is a possibility that oreums in specific regions may be excluded from the study.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While this research provides important foundational data for understanding volcanic activity in Jeju, there may be a lack of comparative studies with other volcanic regions outside of Jeju. Therefore, discussions on how the research results of Jeju oreums can be applied to volcanic studies in other regions are also necessary.
