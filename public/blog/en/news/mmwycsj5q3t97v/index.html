Gimhae-Incheon transfer increased to 4 times a week… Expansion of 'local connections' for summer flights

According to the summer flight schedule recently announced by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, the transfer-exclusive passenger flights connecting Gimhae and Incheon will increase to 4 times a week. Along with this, a new route from Jeju to Incheon is planned to be established, which is expected to further strengthen the connection of flights from regional airports. These changes will take effect from the summer season of 2026 and will be accompanied by enhanced safety management of domestic and international flight routes.

This announcement carries significance beyond merely increasing the number of flights. By strengthening the connection between regional airports and major cities, the convenience of travel for local residents is expected to improve significantly. Airlines are establishing flight plans that consider safety more thoroughly, which will contribute to enhancing the reliability of air travel.

Key Highlights

  • The Gimhae-Incheon transfer flights will increase from the existing 35 times a week to 39 times.
  • A new Jeju-Incheon route is planned, with a total of 20 domestic routes operating 1,806 times a week during the summer period.
  • International flights will operate a total of 4,820 times across 245 routes, representing an increase of about 0.8% in flight frequency.
  • Strengthening safety management standards for airlines, with a comprehensive safety review of the overall flight scale.
  • Low-cost carriers (LCCs) are expected to start operations related to the merger of Korean Air and Asiana Airlines.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This increase in flight frequency can be seen as part of the government's policy to revitalize regional airports. Following the significant impact of COVID-19 on the aviation industry, the government has been making various efforts to enhance the competitiveness of regional airports. In particular, improving accessibility for local residents to major cities is a primary goal. This trend is expected to become more visible with the onset of the summer season, as airlines seek to build trust through flight plans that enhance safety.

Why It Matters

The increase in flights between regional airports and major cities is expected to have a positive impact on regional economic revitalization and the tourism industry, beyond merely increasing transportation options. In particular, the establishment of the route connecting Jeju and Incheon will greatly aid in attracting tourists. Additionally, the strengthening of safety management standards will enhance trust in air travel, encouraging more people to utilize flights.

Personal Perspective

A notable aspect of this change is the increase in travel convenience that local residents can actually feel. For example, if residents of the Gimhae area find it easier to access Incheon, it will facilitate business opportunities and visits to tourist attractions. This could have a positive impact on the local economy. However, it remains to be seen how effectively these changes will be communicated to local residents.

Hidden Variables to Watch

While the increase in flights and strengthening of safety standards are taking place, these may only yield short-term effects. In reality, there is a possibility that airlines may compromise service quality in their pursuit of profits. Furthermore, if demand for regional airports is not sufficiently supported, the sustainability of these routes may be in question.

Points to Check Now

  • Changes in airline services and the actual implementation of strengthened safety management standards following the increase in the Gimhae-Incheon route.
  • The start date of operations for the Jeju-Incheon route and initial reactions.
  • Changes and satisfaction levels experienced by local residents due to the increased flight frequency.

FAQ

A1: The increase will take effect starting from 2026 when the summer flight schedule is implemented.

Q2: When will the Jeju-Incheon route start operating?
A2: It is expected to start operations as early as May.

Q3: How will the safety management standards be strengthened for this increase in flights?
A3: They will be strengthened through a comprehensive review of the overall flight scale and safety of airlines.

Q4: What impact will this change have on the local economy?
A4: It is expected to have a positive impact on the local economy and tourism industry as the convenience of travel for local residents increases.

In conclusion, the increase in flights between Gimhae and Incheon is not just about the rise in the number of operations; it represents a significant opportunity to enhance the quality of life for local residents and revitalize the local economy. The key point to focus on now is how these changes will be applied in practice and what effects they will bring to the residents.
