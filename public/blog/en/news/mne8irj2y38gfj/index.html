How to Utilize National Pension for Parents and Its Benefits

March 31, 2026

How Parents Can Utilize the National Pension and Its Benefits

The National Pension is an essential element in preparing for retirement. Many people overlook the importance of the National Pension, but it serves as a crucial financial foundation for a stable retirement life. Especially for the generation of parents, it is necessary to understand how to utilize the National Pension to build a secure retirement. In this article, we will explore how parents can utilize the National Pension and the benefits it offers in detail.

We will provide tailored information on how parents can make the most of the National Pension, helping them prepare for a more stable retirement.

Basic Understanding of the National Pension: Eligibility and Conditions for Receiving Benefits

The National Pension is a social insurance system operated by the state, allowing all citizens who pay premiums for a certain period to receive pensions in retirement. It plays an important role in supporting the living expenses needed in old age.

Eligibility and Contribution Period

Anyone over the age of 18 can enroll in the National Pension. The minimum contribution period is at least 10 years, and once this period is met, pensions can be received starting at age 62.

Checkpoint: Check if your parents are enrolled in the National Pension and verify their contribution period to ensure they qualify for pension benefits.

Major Benefits of the National Pension: A Stable Source of Income and Additional Support

The National Pension provides various benefits necessary for retirement life. One of the biggest advantages is a stable source of income. By receiving a fixed amount monthly through the National Pension, retirees can support their living expenses.

Medical Expense Support and Survivor's Pension

The National Pension also includes medical expense support, which can alleviate the financial burden of medical costs that may arise in old age. Additionally, in the event of a parent's death, a survivor's pension is paid to support the living of the remaining family members. This is a crucial factor in ensuring the economic stability of the family.

Action Tip: Check in advance the conditions for medical expense support and survivor's pension that your parents can receive through the National Pension.

How to Utilize the National Pension: Methods of Receiving Benefits and Additional Perks

There are several ways to utilize the National Pension. Here are some important points your parents should know about receiving their pension.

Methods of Receiving Pension

Parents can start receiving their pension at age 62, and they can choose between a lump sum or monthly payments. For instance, if they opt for monthly payments, a fixed amount will be paid each month, allowing for a stable living.

Example: If your parents start receiving their pension at age 65, it is advisable to calculate the expected amount. The amount received will vary based on the contribution period and the premiums paid.

Utilizing Additional Benefits

The National Pension and the Basic Pension are different systems. The Basic Pension is an additional pension paid to low-income seniors, which can be received alongside the National Pension. Furthermore, tax deductions can be claimed when paying National Pension contributions.

Checkpoint: If your parents are contributing to the National Pension, it is beneficial to actively utilize the tax deduction benefits to reduce their tax burden.

National Pension Application and Management Checklist

To apply for the National Pension, several documents are required. Here is a checklist to review before applying.

  • Required Documents: Resident registration card, National Pension enrollment certificate, income certificate, etc.
  • Application Methods: Applications can be made online (through the National Pension Service website) or offline (by visiting a nearby National Pension Service branch).

Pension Management Tip: Regularly check the pension details and monitor any changes in the contribution period or amount received.

Points to Note When Utilizing the National Pension: Information Verification and Income Reporting

There are also precautions to take when utilizing the National Pension. Be careful not to fall for incorrect information, and it is important to adjust the timing of pension receipt. Opting for early receipt may reduce the amount received, so careful consideration is necessary.

Income Reporting After Receiving Pension

Even after receiving the pension, income reporting is required. By reporting income, potential tax issues can be prevented in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How do I enroll in the National Pension?

    • Anyone over the age of 18 can enroll in the National Pension, either by visiting a nearby National Pension Service branch or online.

  2. How is the pension amount calculated?

    • The amount received varies based on the contribution period and premiums paid, and tools to estimate the expected amount are available on the National Pension Service website.

  3. What other retirement preparations are necessary besides the National Pension?

    • In addition to the National Pension, various retirement preparation methods such as personal pensions, savings, and investments should be considered.

Understanding how to properly utilize the National Pension for your parents is important. Start by checking your parents' enrollment status and the possibility of receiving benefits. This will be the first step toward a stable retirement.
