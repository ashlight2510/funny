BTS comeback concert, foreign media say “A grand return of Korean soft power”

On the 21st, BTS's comeback concert was held in Gwanghwamun, Seoul, after about four years. This concert was live-streamed globally through Netflix, reaffirming BTS's global influence. Foreign media regarded this concert as a symbolic return of Korean soft power, forecasting that the upcoming world tour could generate over $2 billion in revenue. Through this comeback, BTS plans to emphasize traditional Korean culture and deepen communication with fans.

This concert has established itself as an important case that goes beyond a simple music event, showcasing the re-examination of Korean culture and the solidarity of the global fandom. BTS's influence extends beyond music, impacting culture, economy, and society as a whole.

Key Summary

  • BTS's comeback concert was held in Gwanghwamun, Seoul, after four years and was live-streamed globally through Netflix.
  • Foreign media evaluated this concert as a symbolic return of Korean soft power.
  • The upcoming world tour, consisting of 82 shows, is expected to generate over $2 billion in revenue.
  • The concert emphasizes traditional Korean culture and values communication with fans.
  • BTS's global fandom has a significant impact on culture and the economy.

Key Scenes

  • Global Live Broadcast: The concert was live-streamed globally through Netflix, reaffirming BTS's global influence.
  • Economic Ripple Effect: The upcoming world tour is expected to generate over $2 billion in revenue, which will positively impact the Korean economy.
  • Cultural Symbolism: The album title ‘Arirang’ is derived from a traditional Korean folk song, highlighting the re-examination of Korean culture.
  • Communication with Fans: BTS emphasizes deep communication with fans, reaffirming their presence as central to the concert.
  • Safe Event Management: Thorough management by the police was implemented to control the large crowd.

Reasons to Consider the Background

BTS had a hiatus due to military service and personal activities since 2022. During this time, fans eagerly awaited their return, and this concert was an event that met those expectations. Gwanghwamun, where the concert was held, is a historical symbol of Korea and a cultural center, making it an important location to announce Korea's cultural identity to the world alongside BTS's return.

Why It Matters

BTS's comeback is more than just the resumption of a group's activities; it serves as an opportunity to elevate the global status of Korean culture. This concert is an event that proves Korean soft power, strengthening ties with the global fandom and contributing to the recognition of Korean cultural content worldwide. This is expected to have a positive impact on the Korean economy as well.

Points of Attention

BTS's concert shows that they are not just a simple idol group but have established themselves as cultural icons. They have formed a global fandom and play a role in spreading Korean music and culture. Notably, this concert impressively modernizes and reinterprets traditional Korean culture for the world.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The successful hosting of this concert was backed by thorough safety management. The police and city authorities took measures such as closing nearby roads and controlling public transportation. Without such safety management, controlling the large crowd would have been challenging.

Current Points to Check
