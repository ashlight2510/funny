Seoul's Nowon District Publishes Tourism Guidebook 'New Let’s Nowon'

Seoul's Nowon District has recently published a tourism guidebook titled 'New Let’s Nowon,' which gathers natural and cultural attractions in one place. This guidebook introduces various tourist resources, including urban natural recreation forests like Surak Rest Area, healing towns of Bulaemsan and Suraksan, and the Hwarangdae Railroad Park. The Nowon District Office expects this guidebook to contribute to the promotion of local tourism resources and leisure spaces.

This publication is seen as an opportunity to effectively utilize Nowon’s natural and cultural resources and contribute to revitalizing the local economy. Notably, Nowon District includes spaces that have won awards for five consecutive years in the National Land Competition of South Korea, highlighting their value even more.

Why Background Matters

Nowon District is located in the northeastern part of Seoul, where the development of natural and leisure spaces has been actively pursued in recent years. Particularly, new concepts like urban natural recreation forests have drawn attention, highlighting the area's tourism potential. In this context, the Nowon District Office has published the guidebook to widely promote the region's attractions.

Why It Matters

The publication of 'New Let’s Nowon' carries significance beyond being a simple tourism guide. It provides both local residents and tourists with opportunities to discover and experience the charm of Nowon, contributing to the revitalization of the local economy. Additionally, it is expected to have a positive impact on protecting the natural environment and enhancing cultural values through sustainable tourism development.
