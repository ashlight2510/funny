10 Tips for Saving on Air Conditioner Electricity Bills

Is there always something to worry about in the summer? It's the electricity bill from the air conditioner. It feels like I might faint from the heat, but seeing the electricity bill makes me sigh. So, I've organized some tips. Let's explore ways to save on electricity while using the air conditioner!

1. Set the Temperature

Maintain an Appropriate Temperature

Setting the air conditioner temperature too low can significantly increase your electricity bill. Personally, I found that keeping it between 24°C and 26°C is the most comfortable. At first, it might feel warm, but once you adjust, it actually feels cooler. This way, your body also gets less tired!

Use with a Fan

Using the air conditioner along with a fan is more effective. The air conditioner creates cool air, and the fan helps distribute that cool air, making it feel even cooler. By doing this, I was able to reduce my electricity bill while feeling cooler.

2. Manage the Filter

Clean the Filter

If the air conditioner's filter is dirty, air circulation is hindered, which leads to higher electricity consumption. Therefore, cleaning is essential. It's good to clean it once a month. After I cleaned it, the coolness was noticeably different.

Get a Professional Check-Up

Sometimes, it's good to have a professional check it out. If the air conditioner is old, its performance can decline, and if you keep using it without knowing, your electricity bill can increase. After getting it checked by a professional, my air conditioner became much cooler!

3. Air Conditioner Usage Habits

Adjust Usage Time

You should also consider when to use the air conditioner. From 1 PM to 4 PM is the hottest time, so it's best to use the air conditioner intensively during that period and replace it with a fan during the other times. By doing this, I was able to avoid the heat and save on electricity.

Reduce Unnecessary Usage

I used to have the habit of keeping the air conditioner on all the time. However, keeping it on unnecessarily significantly increases the electricity bill. So, I made it a habit to turn it off when I go out and diligently switch it off after use. This has greatly helped in saving on electricity.

4. Utilize Windows and Curtains

Block Sunlight with Curtains

When the sunlight is strong, it's good to close the curtains. This helps maintain a lower indoor temperature and reduces the need for the air conditioner, saving on electricity. I utilized curtains well throughout the summer, and it definitely helped me stay cool.

Open Windows

At night, opening the windows to let in natural breezes is great. When the air cools down, you can reduce air conditioner usage. I found that natural breezes are really refreshing, which helped a lot in reducing air conditioner use.

Conclusion

I've organized ways to save on electricity while using the air conditioner. Small changes in habits can make a big difference, so I recommend trying them out! If you have any questions, please leave a comment. I think starting with these tips would be great! Let's have a cool summer!
