Jeong Yong-jin, Site Inspection of 'Starfield Cheongna' "Pride and Responsibility for a National Landmark"

Jeong Yong-jin, chairman of Shinsegae Group, recently expressed his expectations that the 'Starfield Cheongna' construction project, currently underway in Incheon Cheongna International City, will become a landmark representing Korea. Starfield Cheongna is a complex facility that combines a 23,000-seat multi-stadium, a hotel, and a shopping mall, and is set to establish itself as the world's first ultra-large leisure and entertainment space. Chairman Jeong emphasized the pride and responsibility associated with this project, stating that safety and quality must be prioritized.

This article is not just about construction news. Starfield Cheongna deserves attention due to its significant impact on Korea's culture and tourism industry. Chairman Jeong hinted that the completion of the multi-stadium would lead to an increase in foreign tourists. This suggests that it is not just a single building, but a project that could influence Korea's global image and economy.
