‘Pulrim’ partners with Kolon Sport... Han Soo-jung releases 8 types of goods featuring plant microstructures

Recently, the Korea National Arboretum announced that it will launch 8 new products featuring plant microstructures in collaboration with the outdoor brand Kolon Sport. This brand ‘Pulrim’ is a garden shop brand designed to extend the value of nature and plants into everyday life, with the new products emphasizing the importance of biodiversity by incorporating plant images captured with a scanning electron microscope into their designs. The launch date is set for the 4th, which is Arbor Day, and a commemorative event will be held at the National Sejong Arboretum.

This news carries significance beyond just a product launch. It reflects an effort to convey the importance of environmental protection and biodiversity to consumers, thereby raising awareness about nature.

Key Summary

  • Collaboration between the Korea National Arboretum and Kolon Sport to launch 8 types of goods featuring plant microstructures.
  • Product designs utilize plant images captured with a scanning electron microscope.
  • Launch date is the 4th, coinciding with Arbor Day commemorative events.
  • Products consist of various items that combine practicality and symbolism.
  • Focus on conveying a message of nature conservation to consumers.

Key Scenes

  • Purpose of Collaboration: Efforts to raise awareness of forest biodiversity conservation.
  • Design Elements: Unique visuals utilizing scanning electron microscopy.
  • Launch Products: Practical and symbolic items, including clothing and gardening kits.
  • Event Activities: Encouraging participation through giveaways of fir trees at the commemorative event.
  • Social Message: Conveying the importance of biodiversity to consumers.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This collaboration was planned in response to the growing social interest in environmental issues and the demand from consumers who feel the need for nature conservation. In particular, the launch of products that allow people to experience the value of plants and nature in their daily lives is receiving more attention. While various brands have previously released environmentally themed products, these new items uniquely integrate biological elements into their designs, giving them special significance.

Why It Matters

This collaboration can be seen as an important attempt to raise social awareness about environmental protection and biodiversity, rather than just a simple product launch. It provides consumers with an opportunity to develop an interest in nature through these products and to expand their thoughts on environmental issues in their daily lives. Moreover, by demonstrating corporate responsibility toward environmental issues, it is expected to positively impact the brand image.

Points to Focus On

In my view, the most important aspect of this collaboration is communication with consumers. It is impressive that the effort goes beyond merely selling products to convey a message about nature and the environment, encouraging consumer participation. For example, giving away fir trees at the event helps consumers feel a connection to nature and provides an opportunity for them to engage in environmental protection.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While this product emphasizes conveying a message of environmental conservation, how well consumers actually purchase these products and accept the message is another matter. With the heightened interest in environmental issues, additional efforts will be needed to guide consumers toward truly sustainable consumption.

Points to Check Now

  • Launch Event: Whether to participate in the commemorative event at the National Sejong Arboretum on the 4th.
  • Product Purchase: Checking the types and prices of products sold at each garden shop.
  • Nature Conservation Activities: Information and participation methods related to nature conservation provided during the event.

FAQ

Q: Where can I purchase the products?
A: They can be purchased at the National Baekdudaegan Arboretum, National Sejong Arboretum, National Korean Native Plant Center, and National Garden Culture Center.

Q: Are there any special benefits during the event period?
A: The first 500 attendees will receive a fir tree, and various activities will be conducted to spread the message of nature conservation.

Q: What is the price range for the products?
A: Price information can be checked at each garden shop, and products will be available at various price points.
