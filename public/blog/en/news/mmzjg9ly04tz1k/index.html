Allegations of Twitter Stock Manipulation, Musk's Liability Risk

A judgment has been made that Elon Musk intentionally lowered the stock price during the acquisition process of Twitter. The jury in the U.S. Northern District Court of California found that Musk caused harm to investors by suspending the acquisition contract due to issues with spam and fake accounts, holding him liable for damages. The compensation is expected to be between $3 to $8 per share, with the total amount likely reaching billions of dollars. This case centers around the significant drop in stock price following Musk's agreement to acquire Twitter in April 2022.

This case is not merely a personal issue for Musk; it is a significant matter affecting both large-scale investors and individual shareholders. The court's ruling carries important implications, as it determined that Musk's suspension of the acquisition contract undermined investor confidence and directly impacted the stock price. Notably, the court's finding that Musk's actions were not intentional could become a legal issue in the future.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Musk signed a contract to acquire Twitter in April 2022 but later announced a temporary halt to the transaction due to spam and fake account issues. This caused a sharp decline in stock prices, resulting in significant losses for investors. Ultimately, Twitter filed a lawsuit against Musk, leading to the jury's ruling. While Musk completed the acquisition contract, the issue of investor protection during the process remains contentious.

Why It Matters

This case is not just about a drop in stock prices. It illustrates how the statements of a CEO like Musk can significantly affect stock prices. This could have profound implications for the trust relationship between corporate executives and investors. With both large-scale and individual investors being identified as victims, it is crucial to observe how legal standards for similar incidents will be established in the future.
