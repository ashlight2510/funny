Government to phase out low-yield retirement pension products

Recently, the government has decided to phase out low-yield products from the retirement pension market. The Ministry of Employment and Labor announced during a meeting of the Pension Reform Special Committee that it would evaluate the yields of default option products and take measures such as suspending enrollment and expelling products that do not meet the standards. This action aims to increase the yields of retirement pensions and help workers prepare for a more stable retirement.

These changes are being implemented alongside the mandatory retirement pension system, with the government planning to gradually introduce retirement pensions in all workplaces and require retirement benefits to be accumulated in external financial institutions. Detailed institutional plans, including a fund-type model for retirement pensions, are expected to be prepared in the future.

Key Summary

  • Government announces plan to phase out low-yield default option products
  • Average yield of 3.7% deemed insufficient
  • Plans for mandatory retirement pensions and introduction of fund-type models
  • Review of AI-based retirement pension management pilot project
  • Plans to alleviate liquidity burdens for small businesses

Key Scenes

  • Phasing out default option products: Suspension of enrollment and expulsion for low-yield products
  • Mandatory retirement pensions: Gradual enforcement of retirement pension introduction in all workplaces
  • AI management pilot project: Review of asset management methods using artificial intelligence
  • Introduction of fund-type models: Proposals for various types of fund-based retirement pension models
  • Support for small businesses: Measures to alleviate liquidity burdens from external accumulation of retirement benefits

Why Background Matters

This measure arises from the recognition that low yields on retirement pensions are making it difficult for workers to prepare for retirement. As of last year, the average yield of default option products was only 3.7%, a figure that raises doubts about the effectiveness of retirement pensions. Therefore, the government has decided to conduct yield evaluations for retirement pension products and phase out those with inadequate performance from the market.

Why It Matters

Retirement pensions are an important system that helps workers live stably in retirement. By phasing out low-yield products, the government allows workers to expect better yields, which can have a positive impact on the economy as a whole. Additionally, the mandatory retirement pension system will provide a foundation for equitable opportunities for all workers.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the government's actions are not just about phasing out products but represent a first step toward overall improvement of the retirement pension system. In particular, the use of AI for asset management could contribute to increasing the profitability of retirement pensions in the long term. However, it remains to be seen how effectively the proposed measures will be implemented.

Hidden Variables to Watch

One limitation of this policy is that simply phasing out low-yield products will not solve all problems. To increase the yields of retirement pensions, it is essential to strengthen market competitiveness and provide workers with information to choose appropriate products. Additionally, how support for small businesses will be implemented is also an important variable.

Points to Check Now

  • Yield of default option products: Check the yield of the products you are currently enrolled in and be prepared to respond to any changes.
  • Timeline for mandatory retirement pensions: It is important to check how future changes will progress and what impact they will have on your workplace.
  • AI investment methods: Pay close attention to whether AI-based asset management will be implemented and its effects.

FAQ

Q: What is a default option product?
A: A default option is a system that manages accumulated funds according to pre-established methods without requiring separate management instructions from the participant.

Q: How can I check if a product has a low yield?
A: You can check the yields of each product through official announcements from the Financial Supervisory Service or the Ministry of Employment and Labor.

Q: When will mandatory retirement pensions be implemented?
A: The government plans to announce a specific implementation schedule within the year.

Q: How will AI-based asset management be conducted?
A: AI analyzes various investment data to automatically establish and execute optimal investment strategies.

To ensure these changes positively impact workers, we must closely monitor the progress of these developments.
