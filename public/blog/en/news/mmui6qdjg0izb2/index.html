Air Purifier Selection Tips and Recommended Models

March 17, 2026

Points to Consider When Choosing an Air Purifier and Recommended Models

In modern society, the increasing levels of fine dust and harmful substances have heightened the need for air purifiers. Keeping indoor air clean is essential for health, and with various models and features available, it is important for consumers to choose products that meet their needs. In this article, we will explore the basic functions of air purifiers and important considerations when selecting a model, along with recommended options.

Understanding the Basic Functions of Air Purifiers

Air purifiers play a role in purifying indoor air to protect health. They use various filters to remove fine dust, allergens, and harmful gases.

Types of Filters and Their Operation

  • HEPA Filter: A high-performance filter that can remove over 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3μm.
  • Activated Carbon Filter: Effective in adsorbing odors and harmful gases.

Many products combine these two types of filters, and it is important to understand the advantages and disadvantages of each. For example, if you are an allergy sufferer, a HEPA filter is essential.

Importance of Maintenance

Filters need to be replaced regularly to maintain optimal performance. Generally, the replacement cycle for filters is suitable between 6 months to 1 year. It is important to frequently check the filter's condition and be mindful not to miss the replacement cycle. Checkpoint: Choosing a model with a filter replacement reminder feature can be convenient.

Factors to Consider When Choosing an Air Purifier

When selecting an air purifier, several factors should be considered.

Type of Filter

A combination of HEPA and activated carbon filters is the most effective. HEPA filters excel at removing fine dust, while activated carbon filters are effective at removing odors. It is important to be well-informed about the characteristics of each filter when making a selection.

Area of Use

You should choose an air purifier that matches the area of the space you will be using. For example, in a room with an area of 30㎡, it is advisable to select an air purifier that can cover more than 30㎡. Execution Tip: Measure the area of the room and search for air purifiers that fit that area.

Noise Level

Choosing a model with low noise is important. Especially if you plan to use it at night, a noise level below 20dB is suitable. Example: If you want a quiet environment, choose a model with a noise level below 20dB.

Additional Features

Smart features or air quality monitoring functions provide extra convenience. It is advisable to consider whether you need these features when making your choice. Checkpoint: List out the features you frequently use.

Precautions When Using an Air Purifier

To use an air purifier effectively, there are several precautions to take.

Regular Filter Replacement

You should check the replacement cycle based on the filter's condition. If the filter becomes dirty, the performance of the air purifier will decrease. Therefore, it is important to regularly inspect the filter and replace it when necessary.

Cleaning and Maintenance

The main body of the air purifier should also be cleaned regularly. If dust accumulates, air circulation will not be smooth. It is advisable to clean it at least once a week. Execution Tip: Setting a cleaning schedule in advance can help you remember to manage it.

Proper Placement

The optimal location for placing the air purifier is where air can circulate well. It should be positioned a certain distance from walls and not interfere with furniture. Checkpoint: Check the air flow after installation.

Air Purifier Purchase Checklist

When purchasing an air purifier, consider using the following checklist:

  1. Measure the area of the space to be used
  2. Check the type of filter and replacement cycle
  3. Check the noise level
  4. Consider the necessity of additional features
  5. Set a budget

By organizing the necessary information in advance using this checklist, you can more easily select a suitable air purifier.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. What is the most important factor when choosing an air purifier?

    • The most important factors are the type of filter and the area of use. The performance of the filter determines the efficiency of the air purifier, and it is essential to select a product that matches the area of use.

  2. How should I determine the filter replacement cycle?

    • The filter replacement cycle varies depending on usage frequency and environment, but generally, it is advisable to replace it every 6 months to 1 year. Regularly checking the condition of the filter is important.

  3. What should I be cautious about while using an air purifier?

    • Regular filter replacement and cleaning, as well as proper placement, are important. Be mindful not to forget filter replacements and maintain the air purifier's performance through cleaning.

In conclusion, when choosing an air purifier, it is important to clearly understand your living environment and needs. Start measuring the area of your space today, check the type of filter and noise level, and find the air purifier that is most suitable for you.
