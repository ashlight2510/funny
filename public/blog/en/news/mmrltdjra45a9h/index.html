7 Tips to Reduce Air Conditioning Electricity Bills in Summer

As summer approaches, it's hard to get through a day without an air conditioner. The problem is that turning on the air conditioner significantly increases the electricity bill. I used to sigh every summer when I saw my electricity bill. So this time, I’ve compiled some methods to reduce air conditioning electricity costs. Let’s take a look together!

1. Clean the Air Conditioner Filter

Keep the Filter Clean

When the air conditioner filter gets dirty, it blocks airflow and reduces efficiency. That's why it's important to clean it regularly. From my own experience, keeping the filter clean makes the air conditioner work better. It’s good to clean it at least once a month.

2. Adjust the Temperature Setting

Is 26 Degrees the Optimal Temperature?

Are you setting your air conditioner to a temperature that’s too low? Generally, 26 degrees Celsius is considered the optimal temperature. I used to set it to 22 degrees and sweat while feeling cool, but later I found out that my electricity bill was astronomical. If you adapt to a little heat at 26 degrees, it can actually be good for your health and save on electricity costs.

3. Use a Fan Alongside

A Fan to Help Circulate Air

Sometimes it feels stuffy when only the air conditioner is on. In such cases, using a fan is a good idea. The fan circulates the air, increasing the efficiency of the air conditioner. I tried this, and it felt like the coolness was doubled. I highly recommend the combination of air conditioner and fan!

4. Utilize Curtains and Blinds

Blocking Sunlight is Essential

On sunny summer days, it’s important to keep curtains or blinds closed. This reduces the burden on the air conditioner. I have regretted leaving the curtains open all summer long. Even a short time of sunlight coming in makes the air conditioner work continuously. So, let’s close the curtains before turning on the air conditioner!

5. Check Energy Efficiency Ratings

Choose an Efficient Air Conditioner

It’s also important to check the energy efficiency rating of the air conditioner when purchasing. Models with good energy efficiency may be expensive initially, but in the long run, they can save a lot on electricity bills. I remember buying a cheap model at first, but ultimately my electricity bill was much higher. Investing in an efficient product will be a big help later on.

6. Adjust Usage Times

Avoid Peak Hours

Avoiding peak hours when electricity rates are high is also a good strategy. Usually, the most expensive time is between 2 PM and 4 PM, so try to minimize air conditioner use or go out during this time. I found that doing this significantly reduced my electricity bill.

7. Utilize Smart Plugs

Transition to Smart Home

Finally, how about using a smart plug? With a smart plug, you can automatically turn the air conditioner on and off at desired times. I found this really convenient. Setting it up in advance can greatly help in reducing electricity costs.

By using these various methods, you can reduce your air conditioning electricity bills. With just a little attention in summer, you can save on electricity costs, so I hope you give these tips a try! If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment!
