Family of Professor Park Tae-young Donates 200 Million Won to Wonkwang University

The family of Professor Park Tae-young, an honorary professor at Wonkwang University, has donated 200 million won to the university's development fund to honor his wishes. Professor Park dedicated his life to education and expressed his desire to contribute to the university's development multiple times during his lifetime. This donation also symbolizes a wish for the university's leap forward, coinciding with its 80th anniversary and various changes.

This news is not just a simple donation announcement; it exemplifies how the values of education and sharing can continue to thrive. The family of Professor Park decided to make this donation to carry on his legacy, which sends a positive message to future generations.
