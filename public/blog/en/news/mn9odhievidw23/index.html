Is it a direct path for Choo Mi-ae or a dramatic turnaround... How to watch the Democratic Party's Gyeonggi Province governor primary

The Democratic Party's Gyeonggi Province governor primary is heating up. While Representative Choo Mi-ae is in a favorable position as a candidate, the rapid rise in support for Representative Han Jun-ho raises the possibility of a shift in the dynamics. This primary is a crucial contest that will determine the candidate for the Gyeonggi Province governor, reflecting the conflicts among various factions within the Democratic Party and changes in the support base.

This primary has become an important issue that goes beyond merely selecting a candidate, as it will determine the future direction of the Democratic Party. In particular, the changing ratio of party member votes to public opinion polls makes it difficult to predict the outcome of the primary. This article will examine the key summaries and background of this primary, as well as why it is important.

Key Summary

  • Competition between Representatives Choo Mi-ae and Han Jun-ho: While Representative Choo holds a favorable position, Representative Han's rising momentum is noteworthy.
  • Influence of the 'New Lee Jae-myung' Support Base: Representative Han is gaining support from the 'New Lee Jae-myung' faction, leading to a rise in his approval ratings.
  • Changes in Primary Rules: With party member votes and public opinion polls each accounting for 50% in the final round, there is a possibility of a runoff if no candidate achieves a majority.
  • Future Direction of the Democratic Party: The outcome of this primary is expected to significantly impact the Democratic Party's chances of winning in the 6·3 local elections.
  • Response from the People Power Party: There are discussions about the possibility of strategic nominations to counter the Democratic Party's candidate.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This primary reflects the internal conflicts within the Democratic Party. With the emergence of a new support base called 'New Lee Jae-myung', criticism of Representative Choo Mi-ae, who was once part of the traditional support base, is growing. In particular, political positions related to judicial reform are changing, leading to a reevaluation of Representative Choo's past actions. These changes are shaking up the primary landscape and contributing to the rapid rise in support for Representative Han Jun-ho.

Why It Matters

This primary is not just about selecting a candidate for the Gyeonggi Province governor. It stands at a crucial crossroads that will determine the identity and direction of the Democratic Party. Changes in the support base could significantly influence the party's policy direction and future presidential strategies. If Representative Han Jun-ho wins, it would signify the rise of a new faction within the Democratic Party, while a victory for Representative Choo Mi-ae would convey that the existing pro-Moon faction remains strong.

My Perspective

In my view, the rise in support for Representative Han Jun-ho in this primary can be interpreted as an important signal that could change the overall political landscape of the Democratic Party, rather than just a shift in the support base. Particularly, if Representative Choo Mi-ae maintains her past hardline stance while the 'New Lee Jae-myung' faction is gaining strong support, it is likely to lead to a loss of her support base. Therefore, understanding the psychology of the support base is as important as evaluating the individual capabilities of the candidates.

Hidden Variables to Watch

A key point to note in this primary is the impact of changes in the primary rules on the candidates. Since party member votes and public opinion polls will each account for 50% in the final round, if no candidate surpasses the majority, it could lead to a runoff. It is difficult to predict which candidate will gain more support in this process. Particularly, if Representative Han Jun-ho's momentum continues, he could become a variable in the contest against Representative Choo.

Current Points to Check

It is important to closely monitor the current progress of the Democratic Party's Gyeonggi Province governor primary and the changes in support rates for each candidate. In particular, tracking how public opinion poll results and party members' reactions change will have a significant impact on the future outcome of the primary.

FAQ

Q: Can Representative Choo Mi-ae recover her approval ratings?
A: There is a possibility, but if the current changes in the support base continue, it may be difficult. Especially if Representative Han Jun-ho's momentum persists, it will be even harder.

Q: Is there a high likelihood of a runoff?
A: Since it could lead to a runoff if no candidate surpasses the majority in the final round, fluctuations in candidates' approval ratings are crucial.

Q: What impact will this primary have on the Democratic Party?
A: The outcome of the primary is expected to have a significant influence on the Democratic Party's policy direction and future presidential strategies.

Q: How is the People Power Party planning to respond?
A: Although a specific candidate has not yet been determined, the possibility of strategic nominations is being discussed.
