Tax Season: Essential Tax Benefits You Might Overlook

March 15, 2026

Tax Filing Season: A Summary of Easy-to-Miss Tax Benefits

As the tax filing season approaches, many people find themselves in deep thought. "How can I save on taxes?" I'm sure many of you are pondering this question. Especially since tax filing can feel overwhelming due to complicated paperwork and regulations. So, I've compiled a list of easy-to-miss tax benefits. I hope this helps you a bit!

1. Earned Income Tax Deduction

Various Deductions for a Single Income!

For those with earned income, earned income tax is generally imposed, but there are many items you can deduct. For example, you can receive deductions for medical expenses, education expenses, donations, housing funds, and more. When I filed my taxes recently, I brought my medical expense receipts, and I was surprised by the amount that was deducted.

Receipts You Must Keep

Medical expenses are one of the items where you often spend a lot, so it's important to keep your receipts organized. From my experience, gathering receipts in advance makes the year-end organization much easier. Let's not overlook those receipts that we might think, "I wish I had known this!"

2. Difference Between Income Deduction and Tax Credit

Income Deduction vs. Tax Credit

It's important to distinguish between income deductions and tax credits. Income deductions are used to lower the taxable income, while tax credits are directly deducted from the tax amount. For example, retirement savings or personal pension insurance premiums are tax credit items, so utilizing them can directly reduce the amount.

My Experience

From my experience with retirement savings, receiving tax credits significantly reduced my burden. Saving a little each year turns out to be a big help later on. I hope you don't miss out on these aspects!

3. Credit Card Usage Records

Saving on Taxes with Card Usage!

If you use credit cards a lot, you can receive income deductions. A certain percentage of your annual spending is deductible, so it's important to keep track of your card usage records. I usually use an app that makes it easy to check my card usage records. It's really convenient.

Tip!

When organizing your credit card usage records, it's also good to distinguish between necessary and unnecessary expenses. By reducing unnecessary spending, you can enjoy tax benefits, making it a win-win situation!

4. Housing Fund Deduction

Don't Forget Housing Fund Deductions

If you purchase a house or take out a loan for a leasehold, you can receive a housing fund deduction. This deduction is directly subtracted from your tax amount, so you shouldn't miss it. After I took out a loan for my house, I kept track of this aspect and saved a considerable amount on taxes.

Check Before Taking Out a Loan

It's important to research the deduction items before taking out a loan. There are many reviews and information available, so it would be good to refer to various sources. This is also an easy-to-miss benefit, so be sure to remember it!

Conclusion

I hope you remember the tax benefits summarized here! Tax filing can feel complicated and burdensome every time it comes around, but by effectively utilizing these benefits, you can reduce your burden a bit. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! It would be great to start with this!
