Judo Heavyweight Lee Hyun-ji Confirms First Asian Games Participation

Promising heavyweight Lee Hyun-ji has secured her first Asian Games participation. On the 16th, at the 2026 President's Cup National Judo Championships held at the Boryeong Comprehensive Gymnasium in Chungcheongnam-do, she defeated World No. 3 Kim Ha-yoon to earn a spot at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. With 2 minutes and 10 seconds left in the match, Lee scored a valid point with an inside leg technique and added another half point with a counter throw to claim victory. Lee began to gain attention when she won the International Judo Federation World Youth Judo Championships during her time at Namnyeong High School, and she has continued to stand out in various competitions since her senior debut last year.

This news carries significance beyond a simple victory. Lee's participation could serve as a beacon for the future of Korean judo, and it is particularly important that she has been given the opportunity to showcase her skills on the stage of the Asian Games.
