Busan Gijang-gun SUV Accident, One Family Member Dead

An accident occurred in Busan's Gijang-gun where an SUV carrying a family fell into the sea, resulting in one death and one serious injury. On the evening of the 24th, a report was received that a vehicle fell into the sea while reversing in front of a restaurant in Jukseong-ri, and fire authorities quickly responded, rescuing two individuals, but one was confirmed dead. The survivor is currently receiving treatment in the hospital and is in a recovering state. This incident serves as a reminder of the need for safety and caution while driving, especially concerning family outings, and provides important implications from various perspectives.
