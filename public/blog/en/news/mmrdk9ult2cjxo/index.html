All About Kimchi Storage: Proper Temperature and Humidity Management

When storing kimchi, it's crucial to maintain the right temperature and humidity. If kimchi is stored improperly, its taste can change, and oh no, it might even get moldy! So today, I've gathered some information about kimchi storage. Here are some tips to help you enjoy your precious kimchi deliciously for a long time!

What is the Proper Temperature?

Optimal Temperature for Kimchi Storage

One of the most important factors when storing kimchi is the temperature. Generally, it's best to store kimchi at a temperature between 0°C and 4°C. Since the average temperature of a refrigerator is around this range, most homes can store kimchi in the fridge without any issues.

Personally, I find that taking kimchi out just before eating and letting it sit at room temperature for a bit enhances its flavor. A slight temperature change seems to enrich the taste. However, if left out too long, it can spoil, so it's better to consume it in moderation.

What Happens if the Temperature is Too Low or High?

If the temperature is too low, kimchi can freeze. This can ruin the texture of the kimchi, so be careful. Conversely, if the temperature is too high, fermentation can speed up, altering the taste of the kimchi.

Therefore, it's a good idea to check the refrigerator's temperature frequently, and if possible, using a dedicated kimchi refrigerator is recommended. The kimchi fridge I used automatically adjusts the temperature and humidity, making it really convenient.

Managing Proper Humidity

Humidity for Kimchi Storage

Humidity is also an essential factor in kimchi storage. Kimchi is typically well-stored at humidity levels above 80%, but achieving this can be challenging. One method I've tried is placing a small bowl of water in the container where the kimchi is stored. This helps maintain a certain level of humidity.

What Happens if the Humidity is Too Low or High?

If the humidity is too low, kimchi can dry out, and if it's too high, mold can develop. Therefore, it's important to check the humidity regularly. Especially during the summer, when humidity levels are high, mold can form easily, so frequent checks are necessary. Many of you might have experienced this.

Choosing the Right Container for Kimchi Storage

What Kind of Container is Good?

The container used for storing kimchi is important as well. Glass containers or dedicated kimchi containers are said to be the best. Personally, I liked using glass containers because they are transparent, making it easy to check the condition of the kimchi. Plus, they don't retain odors, which is really useful.

Points to Note When Storing

When storing kimchi, it's crucial to seal it tightly to prevent air from getting in. If air enters, fermentation speeds up, and the taste can change. Therefore, when packing kimchi, try to remove as much air as possible.

Conclusion

Today, we learned about the proper temperature and humidity for kimchi storage. Just by following these tips, you can enjoy much tastier and fresher kimchi. If you have any questions, please leave them in the comments! And I hope you try these methods. Enjoy delicious kimchi for a long time!
