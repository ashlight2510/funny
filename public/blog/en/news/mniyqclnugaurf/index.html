Japanese shipping company vessel passes through the Strait of Hormuz for the first time since Iran's blockade

The liquefied natural gas vessel ‘Sohar’ from the Japanese shipping company Mitsui O.S.K. Lines has passed through the Strait of Hormuz for the first time since Iran's blockade. This incident occurred amid escalating tensions between the U.S. and Iran and is likely to serve as a significant milestone for Japan's shipping industry. The Japanese government has stated that it went through consultations with Iran to ensure the safe passage of the vessel, but specific details regarding the passage time and destination have been kept unofficial.
