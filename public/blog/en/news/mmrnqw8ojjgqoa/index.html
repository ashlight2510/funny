Top 5 Southeast Asia Resorts for Great Value

March 15, 2026

Southeast Asia's Best Resorts: 5 Places with High Value for Money

Choosing accommodation while planning a trip can be quite a dilemma, right? Especially in Southeast Asia, where there are so many diverse resorts, it can be overwhelming to figure out which ones are truly worth it. So, I’ve compiled a list of Southeast Asian resorts that I personally visited and found to offer great value for money. These places allow you to save on your budget while also creating wonderful memories.

1. Bali, The Ritz-Carlton Bali

Fantastic Views and Service

I chose this resort in Bali, and it was truly amazing. The infinity pool overlooking the sea and the friendly service from the staff were unforgettable. The interior of the resort was so beautiful that it was perfect for taking photos. The breakfast here was especially delicious! There was a variety of options to kickstart the day energetically.

Personal Tip

You must watch the sparkling sunset by the beach here. That view will be unforgettable!

2. Thailand, Centara Grand Beach Resort

Perfect for Family Travel

I chose this resort while traveling in Thailand with my family, and it was great for the kids to play. There’s a water park, so they could play all day without getting tired. Plus, the beach is right in front of the resort, making it easy to jump into the sea.

My Experience

I cherish the memories of spending time with my kids here. I still vividly remember having dinner together after playing in the water and sharing our travel stories while laughing.

3. Vietnam, InterContinental Danang

A Blend of Luxury and Comfort

This resort in Danang was truly excellent for the price. The luxurious atmosphere and comfortable beds made me feel relaxed all day long. The spa service was especially fantastic! If you want to unwind during your travels, I recommend this place.

What I Liked

I really enjoyed taking walks on the beach in the morning. It was a great way to escape from daily life and spend some leisurely time.

4. Philippines, Shangri-La Mactan Resort

Harmony with Nature

This resort in the Philippines felt like a little paradise on an island. The color of the sea was stunning, and the restaurants within the resort were excellent. It’s perfect for those who want an active vacation with various water sports available.

Tip

It’s a good idea to book water sports early in the morning here. Popular programs fill up quickly, so planning ahead is advisable!

Conclusion

I’ve introduced a variety of resorts that offer high value for money. Since each has its unique features, it would be great to choose according to your travel style. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! Booking one of these resorts could lead to truly wonderful memories!
