“You can taste freshly cooked rice without heating it in the microwave”… Seven-Eleven to launch upgraded triangular gimbap next month

Seven-Eleven has announced the launch of triangular gimbap that allows consumers to enjoy fresh rice flavor without the need for a microwave. This new product is the result of a year of research and development, aimed at solving the existing problem of hard rice grains and applying moisture preservation technology, garnering consumer expectations. The new triangular gimbap will consist of a total of 10 varieties, including existing best-selling products, and a half-price trial event will also be held to celebrate the launch.

This news is not just about a product launch; it is an important case that reflects the improvement in the quality of convenience store food and the enhancement of consumer experience. The technological advancements achieved by Seven-Eleven have the potential to change the competitive landscape of the ready-to-eat meal market in the future.

Key Summary

  • Seven-Eleven is launching triangular gimbap that allows you to taste freshly cooked rice without a microwave.
  • Developed through the 'Rice Project,' new technology improves the moisture and texture of the rice.
  • A total of 10 new varieties of triangular gimbap will be released, along with a half-price trial event.
  • Addresses the existing issues of hard rice grains and soggy seaweed in triangular gimbap.
  • Plans to apply the technology to other ready-to-eat meals like kimbap in the future.

Key Scenes

  • Technological Innovation: Development of technology to prevent aging of refrigerated rice and preserve moisture.
  • Consumer Experience: Providing an option to enjoy delicious rice without a microwave.
  • Market Response: The impact of improved quality of convenience store triangular gimbap on consumer preferences.
  • Event Utilization: Strategy to maximize consumer influx through a half-price trial event.
  • Expansion Potential: Possibility of expanding to other ready-to-eat meals after successful technology application.

Why Background Matters

Seven-Eleven has established itself as a leader in the convenience store triangular gimbap market. However, existing products had the drawback of varying taste and quality depending on whether a microwave was used. To address this issue, research and development through the 'Rice Project' led to the birth of new technology. This will resolve consumer complaints and elevate the quality of convenience store ready-to-eat meals.

Why It Matters

This launch news is not just a simple product change. The technological advancements of Seven-Eleven become a crucial factor in increasing consumer loyalty in the fiercely competitive convenience store market. In particular, the ability to provide delicious food without a microwave will be a significant attraction for busy modern individuals. This is expected to have a positive impact not only on convenience store sales but also on the overall ready-to-eat meal market.

FAQ

A: It will be launched on the 7th of next month.

Q: How can I participate in the half-price trial event?
A: You can purchase it at half price through the Seven-Eleven official app when you buy for same-day pickup.
