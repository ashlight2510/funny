Recently, Luxembourg's Foreign Minister strongly rebutted U.S. President Donald Trump's demand for participation in military operations in the Strait of Hormuz, stating "Don’t threaten us." Foreign Minister Xavier Bettel mentioned the issue of rising oil prices due to the Iran war and criticized the U.S. sanctions relief measures. At the EU Foreign Ministers' meeting, member countries collectively decided not to participate in Trump's troop deployment request, with Luxembourg taking a firm stance and emphasizing the importance of international law.
