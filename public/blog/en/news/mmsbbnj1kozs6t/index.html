Prompt Engineering: How to Get the Answers You Want from ChatGPT

"I’m not sure what to ask AI, how can I get the answers I want?" I think many of you might have this concern. In fact, I also struggled at first with what questions to ask when talking to ChatGPT. So, I’ve organized my thoughts on this. Let’s learn how to get the answers you want from ChatGPT, that is, about prompt engineering!

Understanding the Importance of Prompts

What is a Prompt?

A prompt is simply a question or instruction given to AI. Writing it well can greatly help you get the answers you want. For example, asking "Can you recommend a good book?" is less effective than "Is there a fantasy novel you would recommend that I recently read?" The latter will yield much more specific answers. I also had experiences where I didn’t get the answers I wanted because my questions were too vague at first.

I Found That Specificity is Important

Recently, when I asked ChatGPT for travel destination recommendations, I initially asked, "Tell me a good travel destination," and received only general responses. So, I specifically asked, "What overseas travel destinations would you recommend for a woman in her 20s who loves beaches and good food?" and I was able to gather really useful information. Therefore, it’s better to make your questions a bit more specific.

Utilizing Various Question Formats

Open-ended Questions vs. Closed Questions

When crafting prompts, it’s important to effectively use open-ended and closed questions. Open-ended questions start with "What..." or "Why..." while closed questions can be answered with "Yes/No." For example, "What are the advantages of AI?" is an open-ended question, while "Can AI process information faster than humans?" is a closed question.

Once, I asked a closed question, "Can AI accurately convey information?" and received a simple yes/no answer. On the other hand, when I asked an open-ended question, "What could be the advantages of AI?" I received a wide variety of responses. Thus, it’s important to choose the question format that fits the situation.

Utilizing Feedback

Requesting Answer Modifications

If you didn’t receive the answer you wanted from AI, it’s a good idea to ask additional questions based on that answer. For example, you can say, "I’m not satisfied with this answer. Can you explain it in more detail?" By providing feedback like this, AI can give better answers.

I found that by modifying my questions in this way several times, the responses gradually aligned more with what I wanted. Initially, I felt a bit anxious, but I started to enjoy the conversation with AI more and more.

Conclusion

Prompt engineering is truly a crucial element in getting the answers you want from ChatGPT. Asking specific questions, effectively utilizing question formats, and providing feedback are key. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! Starting with this will make your conversations with ChatGPT much more enjoyable!
