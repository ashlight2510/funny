10 Tips to Save on Electricity Bills While Using Air Conditioning in Summer

10 Tips for Reducing Electricity Bills from Air Conditioning in Summer

When summer comes, it's hard to imagine a house without air conditioning, right? But turning on the air conditioner makes you worry about the electricity bill. Every summer, when the electricity bill arrives, my heart sinks. So this time, I've compiled tips to help reduce electricity costs from using air conditioning in the summer.

1. Set the Right Temperature

Setting the air conditioner temperature too low can significantly increase your electricity bill. It's generally recommended to set it between 24°C and 26°C. From my experience, this temperature is the most comfortable while also being less burdensome on the electricity bill. So, I keep it at this temperature during the summer.

2. Use a Fan Together

Using a fan along with the air conditioner allows you to raise the air conditioner's temperature a bit while still maintaining a cool environment. When I turn on the air conditioner and the fan together, the cool breeze spreads more effectively. This method has been really useful!

3. Utilize Shades

In the hot summer, it's good to use shades or blinds on the windows. When sunlight comes in, the indoor temperature rises, but shades can block that. This small tip is really effective for keeping your home cool.

4. Regular Filter Cleaning

If the air conditioner filter is dirty, air circulation is hindered, reducing efficiency. So, I clean the filter about once a month. Just cleaning the filter allows for better airflow and significantly helps in reducing electricity bills.

5. Check Energy Efficiency Rating

When purchasing an air conditioner, it's essential to check the energy efficiency rating. Choosing a high-efficiency product may require a higher initial investment, but it helps save on electricity bills in the long run. Personally, I've definitely felt the difference since using a high-energy-efficient product.

6. Use Automatic Mode

Utilizing the air conditioner's automatic mode is also a tip. When set to automatic mode, it operates according to the indoor temperature, which greatly helps in saving electricity. From my experience, this feature is really convenient.

7. Close Doors and Windows

When using the air conditioner, you must keep doors and windows closed. If outside heat enters, the air conditioner has to work harder. I used to forget this at first, but after sticking to it, I noticed a significant drop in my electricity bill.

8. Turn It Off When Not in Use

It's really a waste to leave the air conditioner on when going out. So, I always make sure to turn it off before leaving. This small habit greatly helps in reducing electricity bills. I recommend not forgetting to turn it off like I used to!

9. Set a Smart Timer

If your air conditioner has a smart timer function, I highly recommend using it. When I used this feature, the air conditioner was already on by the time I returned home, which was incredibly convenient. It saves electricity, and returning to a cool home is truly great!

10. Get Professional Inspections

Lastly, it's important to have a professional check to ensure the air conditioner's performance doesn't decline. Getting it checked annually keeps the air conditioner in optimal condition and helps reduce electricity bills. I usually get it checked before summer each year, and it keeps me cool all the time.

I've summarized ways to reduce electricity bills through air conditioning in the summer. Some of these tips are really simple yet effective, so I hope you give them a try. If you have any questions, please leave a comment!
