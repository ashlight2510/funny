Coupang Raises 'Free Shipping' Criteria for Regular Members... "Is This How You Compensate for Personal Data Breach Loss?"

Coupang has decided to change the criteria for free rocket delivery for regular members. To receive free shipping on rocket delivery items, the final payment amount must now exceed 19,800 won. This change is from the previous standard based on the amount before applying coupons and immediate discounts, which increases the burden on consumers. Coupang explained that this is a measure to prevent price distortion by some sellers, but the industry interprets it as a strategy for improving profitability. Additionally, in the wake of the personal data breach incident, customer attrition has intensified, leading to negative consumer reactions.

This change goes beyond just shipping costs; it could significantly impact Coupang's profitability and customer trust. Especially in a situation where distrust due to personal data breaches has increased, this policy is likely to provoke further consumer backlash.

Key Summary

  • Coupang changes the free rocket delivery criteria for regular members, requiring a final payment amount of at least 19,800 won for free shipping.
  • The reason for the change is interpreted as a measure to prevent price distortion by some sellers and improve profitability.
  • Following the personal data breach incident, customer attrition has increased, and consumer reactions are negative.

Key Scenes

  • Change in free shipping criteria: A final payment amount of at least 19,800 won is required.
  • Coupang's operating profit margin remains in the 1% range, indicating a need for profitability improvement.
  • Customer trust has declined following the personal data breach incident, leading to intensified consumer backlash.
  • Official stance claims it is a measure to prevent price distortion by some sellers.
  • Paid membership 'Wow' members continue to enjoy free shipping benefits as before.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Coupang's policy change is not just about shipping costs. Recently, Coupang has been in urgent need of profitability improvement due to low operating profit margins. Particularly, following last year's personal data breach incident, customer attrition has surged, making the recovery of customer trust a pressing issue. In this context, Coupang has changed its policy under the pretext of consumer protection, but the prevailing analysis is that it is a measure aimed at improving profitability.

Why It Matters

This change could have significant implications for Coupang's business model and customer relationships. As the burden on consumers increases, trust in Coupang may further decline. Especially as the differentiation between paid memberships becomes clearer, the imbalance between regular consumers and paid members may deepen. This could negatively impact customer loyalty and brand image in the long term.

Particularly in a situation where trust has already been lost due to the personal data breach incident, a policy that adds further burden may accelerate customer attrition. Ultimately, I hope Coupang does not overlook the importance of regaining customer trust.

Hidden Variables to Consider

Consumers should note that this policy change is not limited to shipping costs but is directly related to Coupang's overall business strategy. Furthermore, despite Coupang's claim that it is a measure to prevent price distortion by some sellers, it should not be overlooked that it may lead to unfavorable outcomes for consumers.
