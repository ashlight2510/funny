Minister Hwang Jong-woo Emphasizes Fisheries Modernization in Inaugural Speech: Key Points to Watch Now

March 25, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article Overview Title: Minister of Oceans and Fisheries Hwang Jong-woo emphasizes 'modernization of fisheries' in his inaugural speech... "We must perform our role 120% to prevent damage from the Middle East situation." Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun On the 25th, Minister of Oceans and Fisheries Hwang Jong-woo highlighted the importance of 'modernization of fisheries' in his inaugural speech. This is interpreted as a declaration of his commitment to focus on fisheries modernization projects during his term. At the inauguration ceremony held at the Ministry's annex in Dong-gu, Busan, Minister Hwang stated, "We must enhance the competitiveness of the fishing industry and fishing villages," noting that the number of fishing households has decreased by over 70% in the past 30 years and that the challenges posed by climate change are significant.

Inaugural Speech of Minister Hwang Jong-woo: The Need for Modernization of Fisheries and Response to the Middle East Situation

The 'modernization of fisheries' emphasized by Minister of Oceans and Fisheries Hwang Jong-woo in his inaugural speech presents an important direction to address various challenges currently faced by the Korean fishing industry. He pledged to prioritize the safety of crew members and vessels amid the unstable situation in the Middle East, stating that he would perform his role 120% to minimize damage to shipping companies and fishermen. These remarks reflect a commitment to establishing the value of the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries.

This inaugural speech serves not only as a starting point for an individual but also as a significant milestone that can guide the future direction of the Korean fishing industry. In particular, Minister Hwang's emotional response, shedding tears, illustrates how sincerely he is approaching this issue.

Key Summary

  • Minister Hwang emphasized the necessity of 'modernization of fisheries' in his inaugural speech and pledged to enhance the competitiveness of the fishing industry.
  • He mentioned that the safety of crew members and vessels would be prioritized in response to the Middle East situation.
  • He highlighted the importance of the shipping and port industry and mentioned the need for investment in AI and decarbonization.
  • Specific directions for fisheries modernization include restructuring production stages, modernizing distribution and processing, and creating brands.
  • He expressed a desire for open communication with employees during the emotionally charged inauguration ceremony.

Key Scenes

  • Modernization of Fisheries: The need for innovative changes to strengthen the competitiveness of the fishing industry.
  • Safety Management: Prioritizing the safety of crew members and vessels due to the Middle East situation.
  • AI and Decarbonization: Emphasizing the need for technological investment for the future of the shipping and port industry.
  • Support for Fishing Villages: Policy directions for the sustainable development of fishing villages.
  • Importance of Communication: Building trust through open communication between the minister and employees.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Minister Hwang's inaugural speech was made in the context of seeking practical solutions to various problems faced by the Korean fishing industry. With the number of fishing households decreasing by 70% over the past 30 years and the increasing impact of climate change on the fishing industry, modernization is seen as a necessity rather than an option. Additionally, the unstable situation in the Middle East directly affects the shipping industry, necessitating proactive responses.

Why It Matters

This inaugural speech goes beyond the personal beliefs of the minister, presenting a policy direction that could influence the future of the Korean fishing industry. The fishing industry plays a crucial role in the Korean economy, and without modernization, a decline in competitiveness and inevitable economic losses will occur. Furthermore, the Middle East situation directly impacts shipping companies and fishermen, so the minister's remarks can provide psychological reassurance to them.

Important Points in My Opinion

In my view, the 'modernization of fisheries' mentioned by Minister Hwang should not only involve technological changes but also aim to improve the quality of life for those engaged in the fishing industry. Achieving this requires collaboration not only from government policies but also with the private sector. In fact, the modernization of the fishing industry could revitalize the economy of fishing villages and encourage the influx of young people.

Hidden Variables to Consider

A disappointing aspect of this inaugural speech is that specific execution plans or timelines for the emphasized 'modernization of fisheries' were not mentioned. Additionally, there is a lack of concrete plans regarding how the response to the Middle East situation can be realistically implemented. For the government's policies to be effective, more detailed strategies are necessary.

Points to Check Now

  • We should await government announcements related to specific execution plans for the modernization of fisheries.
