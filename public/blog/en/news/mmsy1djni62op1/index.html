Ministry of Labor, Investigation into the Overwork Death Suspicion of a 30s Accountant at Samjeong KPMG

Recently, the Ministry of Employment and Labor has begun an investigation into Samjeong KPMG, bringing renewed attention to the death of accountant Mr. A, who was suspected of overwork. Mr. A was found dead on the 6th, and long working hours are cited as the cause of death. Notably, he reportedly worked over 80 hours a week, while the company recorded his working hours as 40 hours, raising various suspicions. The ministry plans to check the appropriateness of the flexible working system and take strong measures if any legal violations are found.

This incident raises serious issues regarding the working environment and labor practices for young professionals, going beyond a simple personal tragedy. It serves as a case that demonstrates the potential consequences of an unchanged labor environment, despite similar incidents occurring in the past, highlighting the need for social discussion.

Why Background Matters

This incident reveals that long working hour practices in the accounting industry are still prevalent. Specifically, while Samjeong KPMG has introduced a flexible working system, the health and safety of workers are being overlooked in practice. Despite similar incidents occurring in the past, the lack of fundamental change makes this issue even more serious. In this context, attention is drawn to what results the ministry's supervision will yield.

Why It Matters

This incident raises serious questions about the labor culture and working environment in Korean society, going beyond the tragedy of one individual. Given that such issues are occurring in many sectors with a large youth workforce, the significance of the issue is heightened. The results of the ministry's supervision could play a crucial role in preventing similar incidents in the future and creating improved working conditions.
