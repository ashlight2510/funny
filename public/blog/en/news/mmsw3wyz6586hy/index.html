Board of Audit and Inspection Reveals Seoul City Proceeded with Han River Bus Despite Knowing It Did Not Meet Speed Standards

The Han River Bus project, led by Mayor Oh Se-hoon, was found by the Board of Audit and Inspection to have been pushed forward despite knowing that it did not meet the required speed standards. The Board pointed out procedural issues in the project implementation, such as errors in the total project cost estimation and the omission of the necessary investment review by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety, raising concerns that the project may fail to achieve its goal of improving citizens' commuting convenience. While Seoul City accepted the audit results and promised to implement corrective measures, this issue could significantly damage the credibility of the city's transportation policies.

Key Summary

  • The Board of Audit and Inspection found that Seoul City proceeded with the Han River Bus project despite knowing it did not meet speed standards.
  • Seoul City announced a cruising speed of 17 knots, but the actual speed was only 14.5 to 15.6 knots.
  • Errors in total project cost estimation led to the omission of the investment review by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety.
  • The Board requested Seoul City to devise measures to adjust the cruising time.
  • Seoul City expressed its intention to accept the audit results and restore public trust.

Key Scenes

  • Failure to Meet Speed Standards: Seoul City proceeded with the project knowing that the expected speed and actual cruising speed were different.
  • Procedural Issues: Essential investment reviews were omitted due to errors in total project cost estimation.
  • Inconvenience to Citizens' Commuting: There is a risk of failing to achieve the project's objectives.
  • Seoul City's Response: The city announced it would accept the audit results and implement corrective measures.
  • Political Ramifications: Potential negative impact on the credibility of Mayor Oh Se-hoon's policies.

Why Background Matters

The Han River Bus project is an initiative by Seoul City to promote water-based public transportation and is one of Mayor Oh Se-hoon's key projects. However, there have been various controversies since the project's inception, and the recent audit results indicate that Seoul City intentionally distorted the speed standards of the project. This could significantly undermine the transparency and credibility of the project.

Why It Matters

The audit results are not limited to the Han River Bus project but could serve as a catalyst for questioning the overall credibility of Seoul City's transportation policies. Citizens expect safety and convenience in public transportation, but such scandals can lead to increased distrust in policies. This could ultimately have negative repercussions not only for Seoul City's transportation policies but also for citizens' daily lives.

The intention to enhance citizens' commuting convenience could ironically lead to the opposite effect, making the situation even more serious.
