Ganghwa County stated, “The facility director, A (62), has been arrested and indicted on charges of sexual intercourse with a disabled person under the Sexual Violence Punishment Act and assault under the Welfare of Persons with Disabilities Act, and the results of two in-depth investigations into the facility confirmed violations of human rights...” Read the original article

The Shock of the Disability Sexual Violence Incident and the Decision to Close Sae Dong Won

Sae Dong Won, a facility for severely disabled individuals in Ganghwa County, Incheon, will be closed due to allegations of sexual violence and abuse against disabled individuals. The facility director, A, has been arrested and indicted for violations of the Sexual Violence Punishment Act and the Welfare of Persons with Disabilities Act, and subsequent in-depth investigations revealed violations of human rights. Consequently, Ganghwa County has determined that normal operations are impossible and has ordered the closure by the end of the year. However, a grace period of up to 9 months will be provided for the safe relocation of residents.

This incident signifies more than just the closure of a facility. The issues of violence and abuse against disabled individuals reveal a dark side of society and highlight the urgent need for institutional efforts to address these problems. Through this incident, we can reaffirm the necessity of protecting the human rights of disabled individuals and the need for policy changes to achieve this.

The Sae Dong Won incident highlights that the issues of violence and abuse against disabled individuals are still serious social problems, going beyond mere operational concerns of a facility. In recent years, there have been numerous cases of human rights violations in facilities for disabled individuals, raising social awareness. This incident is expected to further urge such awareness.

Disabled individuals have rights that must be protected as members of our society, and when these rights are violated, society as a whole must not turn a blind eye. Therefore, this incident will serve as a reminder of the social responsibility to protect the human rights of disabled individuals, extending beyond just one facility's issues.

In fact, this incident should prompt various stakeholders to collaborate towards strengthening the human rights of disabled individuals.
