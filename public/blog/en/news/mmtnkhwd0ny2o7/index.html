GTC 2026 Opening... Samsung HBM4E First Reveal, SK's Chey Tae-won Attends

At 'GTC 2026' held in San Jose, California, Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix unveiled next-generation High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) technology. Samsung Electronics showcased the physical HBM4E chip and core die wafer for the first time, while SK Hynix emphasized the competitiveness of its AI memory technology through collaboration with NVIDIA. Notably, Chey Tae-won, Chairman of SK Group, attended the event to express his commitment to strengthening partnerships.

This GTC 2026 serves as a platform to observe the latest trends in AI technology and industry, and the announcements from Samsung and SK are expected to make the competitive landscape in the AI memory market even more interesting.

Key Highlights

  • Samsung Electronics emphasizes technological leadership by unveiling the HBM4E physical chip for the first time.
  • SK Hynix strengthens partnerships by showcasing HBM4 integrated into the NVIDIA AI platform.
  • Chey Tae-won attends the event to discuss global cooperation strategies and strengthen relationships.
  • HBM4E shows improved performance over HBM4 in data processing speed and bandwidth.
  • As AI technology advances, memory is becoming a core element of AI infrastructure.

Key Scenes

  • Next-Generation Technology Reveal: Samsung's HBM4E, SK Hynix's HBM4.
  • AI Platform Collaboration: Strengthening technological competitiveness through partnership with NVIDIA.
  • Attendance of Chairman Chey Tae-won: Discussion of global cooperation strategies and relationship strengthening.
  • Performance Improvement: HBM4E's data processing speed is 16Gbps per pin, with a bandwidth of 4TB/s.
  • Importance of AI Memory: Highlighting the core role of memory technology across data centers and on-device applications.
