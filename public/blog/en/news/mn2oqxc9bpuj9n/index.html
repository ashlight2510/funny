Gyeongpo Moonlight Lake Garden Created in Gangneung Chodang-dong

Recently, Gangwon Province's Gangneung City has newly established the 'Gyeongpo Moonlight Lake Garden' within the Chodang-dong Gyeongpo Ecological Wetland. This space is part of a project aimed at expanding tourism resources around Gyeongpo Lake and promoting stay-type tourism, and it is expected to provide a healing space harmonized with nature. Gangneung City has invested approximately 870 million won in this project to install viewing decks and a deck bridge connecting the ecological wetland area, while also enhancing the surrounding environment to create a space where citizens and tourists can enjoy nature.

This news is expected to significantly impact the revitalization of tourism in the Gangneung area and improve the quality of life for residents. In particular, the creation of a nature-friendly space is anticipated to provide positive experiences for both local residents and tourists.
