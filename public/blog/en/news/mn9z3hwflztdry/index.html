‘Yoon Again’ Lee Hyuk-jae, at the National Power Youth Council Audition, “Let’s win the local elections centered around Jang Dong-hyuk”

Recently, Lee Hyuk-jae participated as a judge in the National Power’s youth proportional council member open audition, emphasizing the need to win the local elections centered around Representative Jang Dong-hyuk. Contrary to media reports suggesting that the National Power is at a disadvantage, he argued that victory is possible through the strength of the youth. In this process, Lee compared the protests and struggles of the youth to assets and stressed the importance of the audition in discovering young talent for the party. Alongside this, Representative Jang Dong-hyuk promised to give the youth a central role in politics, outlining the party's direction.

This event has emerged as a significant issue that goes beyond a simple audition, highlighting the changing political landscape and the importance of youth in politics.

Lee Hyuk-jae’s remarks and the National Power’s youth audition are part of a broader trend emphasizing youth participation in the current political environment.
