Seoul City Launches Middle-aged Employment Academy: A New Beginning of Opportunities

Seoul City has launched the 'Middle-aged Employment Academy' for the middle-aged population. This program provides comprehensive support including job counseling, training, and job matching for citizens aged 40 to 60, operating across five campuses in Seoul. It offers opportunities to build new job competencies through various training courses in fields such as AI, digital technology, electrical work, and caregiving. This program is designed specifically to respond to the changing industrial environment and participation is free of charge.

This news represents an important opportunity for the middle-aged population to explore career transitions and new possibilities, going beyond a simple employment support program. In particular, the emphasis on practical training that can adapt to technological changes is expected to play a significant role in enhancing competitiveness in the job market.

Key Summary

  • The Middle-aged Employment Academy has opened across five campuses in Seoul.
  • Training is provided in various fields including AI, digital technology, electrical work, and caregiving.
  • Citizens aged 40 to 64 can participate for free, with 800 spots available.
  • The program is divided into regular classes, intensive classes, and exploratory classes.
  • It supports career transitions and the enhancement of new job competencies for the middle-aged population.

Why It Matters

This program is significant in addressing the employment instability faced by the middle-aged population and providing opportunities for career transitions. Expanding employment opportunities for the middle-aged in an aging society contributes to social stability. In recent years, Seoul has seen a serious social issue with declining employment rates among the middle-aged population alongside aging problems. Consequently, there has been a growing need for tailored employment support programs for this demographic, and the Middle-aged Employment Academy has been designed to meet this demand. The inclusion of education in AI and digital fields can be seen as a strategy to cultivate a workforce that can adapt to technological changes.
