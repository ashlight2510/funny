Minister Cho Hyun, on whether to request US troop deployment: “It could be a request, or it might not be”

Minister of Foreign Affairs Cho Hyun recently took an ambiguous stance regarding questions about the US request for troop deployment in the Strait of Hormuz during a National Assembly session. He mentioned that there may or may not have been a request during the consultation process with the US, avoiding a definitive answer regarding the troop deployment request. Such statements can be interpreted as a signal that South Korea is carefully adjusting its diplomatic position in relation to the US. Minister Cho also reported that he emphasized the importance of safety in the Strait of Hormuz and the stability of international oil prices during a call with US Secretary of State Rubio.

This situation illustrates the complex position of South Korean diplomacy. While maintaining relations with the US, South Korea must prioritize its national interests and the safety of its citizens. Minister Cho's remarks provide an opportunity to glimpse the South Korean government's diplomatic strategy.
