How to Avoid Electric Bill Shock from Air Conditioner Use in Summer

When summer comes, the weather becomes unbearable without an air conditioner. However, the concern when turning on the air conditioner is the electric bill. Many people sigh while looking at their monthly electric bill, wondering, "How much will my electric bill be this month?" So, I’ve compiled some ways to avoid electric bill shock from air conditioner use during the summer.

1. Adjust the Air Conditioner Temperature

One of the easiest ways to reduce air conditioner usage is by adjusting the temperature. Setting it between 26 to 28 degrees Celsius can help save on electricity costs. From my experience, setting it to 26 degrees and using a fan makes it feel much cooler. The fan circulates the air, increasing the efficiency of the air conditioner.

Also, using the air conditioner only during the hot afternoons is a good strategy. In the morning or evening, you can open windows for natural ventilation, which helps you feel less heat. I’ve noticed a significant reduction in my electric bill by doing this!

2. Clean the Air Conditioner Filter

When the air conditioner filter gets dirty, air circulation is hindered, causing the air conditioner to work harder. This inevitably leads to a higher electric bill. Therefore, I clean the filter every summer before it gets too hot. It’s quite simple; just take out the filter, wash it with water, and let it dry.

Personally, I find that regularly cleaning the filter not only provides cool air but also helps lower my electric bill, making it a win-win situation. It’s good to clean the filter about once every 1-2 weeks.

3. Adjust Air Conditioner Usage Time

Adjusting the time you use the air conditioner is also a great way to reduce your electric bill. Especially during the day, keeping the air conditioner on for long periods can lead to a hefty bill. Therefore, I try to limit air conditioner use during the day and mainly use it in the evening or at night.

During these times, the evening breeze is naturally cooler, which reduces the need for the air conditioner. I’ve noticed a significant decrease in my electric bill by doing this.

4. Precautions When Using the Air Conditioner

Lastly, there are some precautions to take when using the air conditioner. If you set it too high when you first turn it on, the electric bill will spike. It’s better to start with a low setting and gradually increase it. Also, make sure to close the windows for the air conditioner to function properly.

This part is really important. I remember once I left the window open while using the air conditioner, and my electric bill was shockingly high. Since then, I always make sure to close the windows when using it.

Conclusion

I’ve shared a few ways to avoid electric bill shock from air conditioner use in the summer. From adjusting the air conditioner temperature, cleaning the filter, managing usage time, to precautions while using it. By utilizing these tips, you can save a bit on your electric bill. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! Let’s get through the summer safely!
