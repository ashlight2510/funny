President Yoon's Directive on Constitutional Review and the Ministry of Justice's Initiation of Legal Review

Recently, President Lee Jae-myung directed a review of the constitution, and Justice Minister Jeong Seong-ho announced that he would begin a legal review regarding this. Minister Jeong highlighted the historical significance of the 1987 constitutional amendment and emphasized the necessity of a new constitution that aligns with the current era of AI technological revolution. He also expressed his commitment to reflecting issues that resonate with the majority of the public in the new constitution. President Lee proposed a gradual constitutional amendment that prioritizes resolving issues agreed upon by the public during the Cabinet meeting.

This constitutional review is not merely a legal procedure but could serve as an important opportunity to advance democracy and strengthen the rights of the people. Therefore, it is necessary to examine this matter deeply within its political and social context.

Key Highlights

  • President Lee Jae-myung directed a phased constitutional review.
  • Justice Minister Jeong Seong-ho announced the initiation of legal review.
  • Emphasis on the historical significance of the 1987 constitutional amendment and the need for a constitution suitable for the AI era.
  • Proposal to prioritize resolving issues agreed upon by the public.
  • The Ministry of Justice promises support to reflect diverse public opinions.

Background and Context of the Issue

This constitutional review can be seen as a response to the various changes and challenges faced by Korean society. In particular, with the advancement of AI technology, there are arguments that the structure of society is changing, and the constitution should also evolve accordingly. Given that the 1987 constitutional amendment was a historical event that ended authoritarianism and established democracy, the current amendment must also move in a direction that aligns with the new era.

Why It Matters

This constitutional review offers an important opportunity not only for legal changes but also for further developing democracy in Korean society and ensuring the rights of the people. Additionally, preparing a constitution that fits the AI technological revolution will play a crucial role in indicating the direction of future society. This could be a significant factor in determining what Korean society will look like in the next 10 or 20 years.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the most significant meaning of this constitutional review is that it provides an opportunity to directly reflect the voices of the people. The goal mentioned by Minister Jeong to incorporate issues that resonate with the majority of the public into the constitution could positively change the direction of political discussions. In particular, issues like regional decentralization or including the spirit of May 18 in the preamble of the constitution are topics that could garner significant public support.

Hidden Variables to Consider

For this constitutional amendment to proceed successfully, public participation and the collection of opinions are essential. However, it is also important to be mindful of the possibility that some opinions may be marginalized in a government-led process. Therefore, it is crucial to reflect the voices of various sectors of society in a balanced manner.

Points to Check Now

  • How will public opinion be collected during the constitutional review process?
  • What specific issues will be addressed in the legal review?
  • If the amendment occurs, what changes are expected in practice?

FAQ

Q: What changes are expected if the constitutional amendment takes place?
A: If the amendment occurs, it may include strengthening regional decentralization, enhancing democratic values, and establishing legal standards for AI technology.

Q: How will public opinions be reflected in the amendment process?
A: The government plans to collect public opinions through various channels and incorporate them into the legal review and constitutional proposal.

Q: How long will it take for the amendment to be implemented?
