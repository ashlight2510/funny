UAE's Willingness to Join Trump's Proposal for 'Hormuz Escort' Amid Alliance Neglect

Recently, the United Arab Emirates (UAE) has expressed its willingness to join U.S. President Donald Trump's request for international security cooperation in the Strait of Hormuz. Anwar Gargash, the UAE's diplomatic advisor, stated that the UAE could cooperate in international efforts led by the U.S. to ensure safety and security in the Strait of Hormuz. This is interpreted as the UAE's intention to strengthen its security through cooperation with the U.S. after suffering serious damage from Iranian attacks.

This article highlights the background and significance of the UAE's positive response to the U.S. request. In particular, it shows the UAE's intention to strengthen its alliance with the U.S. in the context of the significant damage it has suffered from Iranian attacks. This situation could have important implications for the political tensions in the Middle East and the military response of the international community.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This issue has arisen as tensions with Iran have intensified. The UAE has faced numerous missile and drone attacks from Iran and feels the need to strengthen its security amid military tensions. President Trump has received negative responses from NATO allies regarding participation in military operations, increasing reliance on Middle Eastern countries like the UAE. In this context, the UAE's decision is interpreted as a strategy to solidify its relationship with the U.S.

This could further escalate military tensions with Iran and exacerbate political instability in the region. Additionally, if U.S. military intervention expands, it could change the military response strategies of the international community.
