Lisa Su, CEO of AMD, makes a bold move during her visit to Korea... Samsung to prioritize HBM4 supply to AMD

AMD's CEO Lisa Su is visiting Korea to explore collaboration with Samsung Electronics and Naver for the expansion of the AI ecosystem. Samsung Electronics has decided to prioritize the supply of HBM4 memory for AMD's next-generation AI accelerators and is also in discussions regarding foundry cooperation. This presents an opportunity to further strengthen Samsung's dominance in the memory market alongside advancements in AI technology.

This collaboration opens up possibilities to enhance the performance of AI data centers and cloud services by combining AMD's AI technology with Samsung's memory technology. The partnership with Naver also highlights efforts to secure diversity in the AI ecosystem.

Key Summary

  • Lisa Su, CEO of AMD, discusses AI ecosystem collaboration with Samsung Electronics and Naver.
  • Samsung Electronics is selected as the priority supplier of AMD's HBM4 memory.
  • Ongoing discussions about foundry cooperation are expected to strengthen Samsung's competitiveness.
  • Collaboration with Naver aims to enhance AI infrastructure technology.
  • Cooperation with AMD secures technological diversity for Naver.

Key Highlights

  • AI Ecosystem Expansion: Collaboration among AMD, Samsung, and Naver contributes to building AI infrastructure.
  • Priority Supply Agreement: Samsung Electronics is designated as the priority supplier of AMD's HBM4 memory.
  • Foundry Cooperation: Potential for contract manufacturing of AMD's next-generation chips.
  • Strengthened Competitiveness: Samsung can leverage memory supply to both NVIDIA and AMD for market dominance.
  • Technological Diversity: Naver aims to reduce dependency on specific AI infrastructure through collaboration with AMD.

Why Background Matters

AMD is strengthening its position in the AI semiconductor market as a rival to NVIDIA, and CEO Lisa Su's visit can be seen as part of this strategy. Samsung Electronics is striving to gain an edge in the AI accelerator market based on its strengths in the memory market, which will differentiate it from competitors like SK Hynix and Micron.

In particular, Samsung is already collaborating with major global tech companies and is seeking new opportunities in the foundry business through its partnership with AMD. This could be a significant factor in the competition with Taiwan's TSMC.

Why It Matters

This collaboration provides Samsung Electronics with an opportunity to solidify its leadership in the AI memory market, while AMD can secure diversity in AI technology through its partnership with Naver. As AI technology becomes increasingly integrated into various industries, this can have a direct impact on corporate survival and competitiveness.

Points to Note

In my view, Samsung's priority supply agreement for HBM4 will not just be about memory supply; it will significantly influence the pace of AI technology development. Given the increasing importance of AI data processing speed, the performance of HBM4 is likely to be central to the next-generation AI infrastructure.

Hidden Variables to Consider

It is also important to consider that CEO Lisa Su's visit and the signing of the cooperation agreement may yield only short-term results. The AI market is rapidly changing, and technological advantages may not always be sustained. Therefore, it remains to be seen how much actual success this collaboration will achieve.
