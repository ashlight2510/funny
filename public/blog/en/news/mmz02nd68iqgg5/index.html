'Courtroom Commotion' Kim Yong-hyun's Lawyer's Arrest Warrant Denied···“Insufficient Grounds for Arrest”

Recently, the arrest warrant for Kim Yong-hyun's lawyer, who caused a disturbance in the courtroom, has been denied. The Seoul Central District Court determined that there was insufficient necessity for arrest and did not accept the warrant related to lawyer Kwon's charges of contempt of court and disturbance. Lawyer Kwon left the courtroom without answering questions and this incident is related to a courtroom disturbance that began during a trial last November.

This case goes beyond a mere courtroom disturbance and may lead to significant discussions regarding the authority of the judiciary and the role of lawyers. The decision to deny the arrest warrant will serve as an important milestone in assessing how legal responsibilities concerning lawyer Kwon will be handled.
