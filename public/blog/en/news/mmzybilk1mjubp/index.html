“Even Buddha loves BTS”···Gwanghwamun area turns ‘purple’

The Gwanghwamun area in Seoul was dyed purple in celebration of the comeback performance of BTS. The symbolic color of BTS, purple, was prominently displayed throughout the city, creating a festive atmosphere enjoyed by fans and citizens alike. Various merchandise and banners commemorating the performance appeared, and notably, at Jogyesa Temple, a painting of Buddha waving a BTS light stick drew attention. This performance will be live-streamed worldwide on Netflix, providing a special experience for fans eagerly awaiting new music from BTS.

This phenomenon can be seen as a demonstration of BTS's influence on Korean culture and society, transcending a mere fan event. The festival, participated in by fans and citizens alike, proves that BTS has established itself as a social phenomenon, not just a music group.
