Seoul's Official Housing Prices Rise 18.7%... Number of Properties Subject to Comprehensive Real Estate Tax Increases by 170,000

Recently, the average official housing price for multi-family housing in Seoul has increased by 18.67%, resulting in an increase of about 170,000 properties subject to the comprehensive real estate tax. This surge is primarily attributed to the rise in transaction prices of high-end housing last year, particularly in areas densely populated with high-end apartments in Seoul. This announcement of official prices is expected to increase the tax burden on the real estate market and lead multi-property owners and high-end property holders to consider selling to minimize taxes.

This issue is not merely a change in numbers but a significant matter that has a practical impact on tax burdens and trends in the real estate market. In particular, the rise in official prices directly translates to increased taxes for high-end property owners, necessitating close attention to related policy changes and market reactions.

Key Summary

  • Average official housing price for multi-family housing in Seoul rises by 18.67%, with an increase of 170,000 properties subject to the comprehensive real estate tax.
  • The rise is particularly high in high-end housing, with areas like Gangnam, Seocho, and Songpa seeing a 24.7% increase.
  • The national average official price increase rate is 9.16% compared to last year, while some areas, including Jeju and Gwangju, saw declines.
  • Due to increased holding taxes, multi-property owners and high-end property holders may increase their selling volume.
  • The period for reviewing official prices is from the 18th to the 6th of the following month.

Key Highlights

  • Official Price Increase Rate: Seoul 18.67%, National Average 9.16%.
  • Increase in Properties Subject to Comprehensive Real Estate Tax: 487,362 properties, an increase of 169,364 from last year.
  • Increased Tax Burden: Significant increases in holding taxes for high-end property owners are expected.
  • Market Reaction: Potential increase in selling volume.
  • Review Period: Official prices can be reviewed from the 18th, with decisions made after collecting opinions for 30 days.

Why Background Context Matters

The recent rise in official prices can be seen as a result of the sharp increase in high-end housing transactions in Seoul last year. In particular, the rise in areas densely populated with high-end housing, such as Gangnam, significantly lifted the average. The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport emphasized that the method of calculating official prices differs, explaining the impact of high-end housing price increases on overall official prices. This trend is directly linked to tax policies in the real estate market and could significantly influence future policy directions.

Why It Matters

The change in official prices goes beyond mere tax issues, having a substantial impact on the overall trends in the real estate market. In particular, the increased tax burden on high-end property owners may lead them to reconsider their selling decisions. This could enhance liquidity in the real estate market and exert price adjustment pressure, making it crucial for market participants to closely observe these changes.

FAQ

Q: How much will taxes increase if official prices rise?
A: An increase in official prices leads to a rise in holding taxes, with the increase being larger for high-end properties.

Q: How can I check the official prices?
A: Official prices can be reviewed on the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport's website from the 18th to the 6th of the following month.

Q: How are properties subject to comprehensive real estate tax determined?
A: Properties with official prices exceeding 1.2 billion KRW are subject to the comprehensive real estate tax.

Q: What impact will this rise in official prices have on the market going forward?
A: An increase in selling volume may lead to greater price adjustment pressure in the market, which will affect the liquidity of the real estate market.
