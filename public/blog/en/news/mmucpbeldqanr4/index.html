The Significance of Oh Se-hoon's Candidacy Registration and Future Prospects

On the 17th, Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon officially announced his candidacy registration for the Seoul mayoral election. He had previously refused to apply for candidacy twice, demanding personnel renewal and the early launch of an innovative election campaign committee, but ultimately decided to register due to pressure from the party. In a press conference, Mayor Oh mentioned his sense of responsibility towards the citizens of Seoul and indicated his intention to conduct an independent election campaign at the Seoul city level. This decision reflects his determination to continue on an independent path despite conflicts with the party leadership.

The importance of this article lies in the fact that Mayor Oh's actions could further complicate the political landscape of Seoul and the internal conflicts within the People Power Party. While he has shown a willingness to contribute to the party's change through the formation of an innovative campaign committee, this also carries the potential to deepen conflicts with the party leadership.

Why Background Matters

Mayor Oh's candidacy registration is closely related to the internal conflicts within the party over the past few weeks. He refused to apply for candidacy while demanding innovative changes, but as pressure from the party increased, he ultimately decided to register. During this process, conflicts with party leader Jang Dong-hyuk intensified, which is expected to have a significant impact on future election strategies. Additionally, there are analyses suggesting that the declaration of candidacy by Representative Park Soo-min also influenced Mayor Oh's decision.

Why It Matters

Mayor Oh's actions could have a significant impact on the political landscape of Seoul. If he emphasizes internal conflicts through an independent election campaign and forms an innovative campaign committee, it could accelerate the reorganization of power within the People Power Party. This is an important issue that could directly affect the citizens of Seoul.
