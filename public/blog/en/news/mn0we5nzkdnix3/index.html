RM's Grit and J-Hope's Confession... BTS 'A Night of Thrill' Viewed by 300 Million

BTS's comeback live 'ARIRANG' held on the 21st at Gwanghwamun was recorded as a symbolic moment that opens a new chapter in K-pop, beyond just a simple performance. About 40,000 fans gathered at the venue, and the concert was broadcast live to 300 million subscribers worldwide via Netflix, marking BTS's first performance as a complete group after military service. Despite suffering an injury during rehearsal, RM participated in the performance and continued to communicate with fans, while J-Hope candidly shared his anxiety and longing for fans during the album preparation. This concert became an important milestone announcing their new beginning, featuring new songs that embody BTS's identity.

This performance was not just about musical achievement; it was a moment of deep emotional sharing between BTS and their fans. Despite RM's injury, his presence on stage alongside fans showcased their strong bond.
