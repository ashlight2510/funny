5 Hidden Settings to Save iPhone Battery Life

As smartphone usage increases, battery drain has become a significant issue for many users. Especially for iPhone users, utilizing hidden settings can help extend battery life, allowing for longer smartphone use in daily life. In this article, we will introduce five hidden settings that can help save your iPhone battery.

1. Activate Low Power Mode to Extend Battery Life

Low Power Mode reduces battery consumption and is useful in emergencies. Activating this mode slightly limits the performance of your iPhone but can drastically extend battery life.

How to Apply

  1. Open the Settings app and go to the "Battery" menu.
  2. Activate the "Low Power Mode" option.

Checkpoint

  • When Low Power Mode is activated, email push notifications may be disabled, and background app refresh may stop. It's best to use it only when necessary.

2. Optimize Location Services to Reduce Unnecessary Drain

Location services consume a lot of battery, so optimization is necessary. Apps with GPS functionality can quickly drain the battery.

How to Apply

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy > Location Services.
  2. Set the location access permission for each app to "While Using the App."

Checkpoint

  • Apps that always need location services, like navigation apps, should be exceptions. Optimize only for apps that are used less frequently.

3. Manage Background App Refresh to Reduce Drain

If apps continue to run in the background, they will consume battery. It's important to prevent unnecessary apps from running in the background.

How to Apply

  1. Go to Settings > General > Background App Refresh.
  2. Disable apps that you don't need.

Checkpoint

  • Disabled apps may not receive the latest information immediately. It's advisable to keep frequently used apps enabled.

4. Adjust Screen Brightness and Auto-Lock Settings

Screen brightness is one of the main causes of battery drain. Therefore, adjusting brightness can help save battery life.

How to Apply

  1. Go to Settings > Display & Brightness.
  2. Enable "Auto-Brightness" adjustment.
  3. Set the "Auto-Lock" time to a shorter duration.

Checkpoint

  • Auto-brightness adjustment may be inconvenient, so if you prefer to manually adjust brightness, consider turning off this feature and adjusting it manually.

5. Adjust Push Notifications and Sync Settings

Push notifications can impact battery consumption. Reducing unnecessary notifications can help extend battery life.

How to Apply

  1. Go to Settings > Notifications.
  2. Disable unnecessary push notifications.

Checkpoint

  • You may miss important notifications, so it's best to keep notifications for necessary apps. Only disable notifications for apps that are used less frequently.

Checklist: iPhone Battery Saving Settings

  • Activate Low Power Mode
  • Optimize Location Services
  • Manage Background App Refresh
  • Adjust Screen Brightness and Auto-Lock Settings
  • Adjust Push Notifications and Sync Settings

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How does the iPhone battery saving mode work?

    • Low Power Mode adjusts the system's performance to reduce battery consumption. When this mode is activated, background app refresh, automatic downloads, and more are limited.

  2. What features are restricted after optimizing location services?

    • After optimizing location services, the real-time location tracking feature of certain apps may be limited. For example, navigation apps always need location access.

  3. What is the difference between Low Power Mode and normal mode?

    • Low Power Mode minimizes battery consumption by limiting system performance, while normal mode allows all functions to operate normally. Low Power Mode is suitable for use in emergencies.

By applying these settings to save your iPhone battery, you can use your smartphone more efficiently in daily life. Open the Settings app now and activate Low Power Mode!
