Significance of Lisa Su's Visit to Samsung Electronics' Pyeongtaek Campus

Lisa Su, CEO of AMD, is scheduled to visit Samsung Electronics' Pyeongtaek campus to discuss cooperation strategies with Vice Chairman Jeon Young-hyun and key executives. This meeting aims to explore potential collaborations in the memory semiconductor and foundry sectors, which could open up important new opportunities for collaboration beyond Samsung supplying memory semiconductors to AMD. The partnership between AMD and Samsung Electronics has the potential to evolve into a strategic alliance for the growth of both companies, transcending a simple supply relationship.

This visit comes amid intensifying global competition in the semiconductor industry, drawing attention to how the relationship between the two companies will evolve. Particularly, as Samsung's foundry division receives contract manufacturing orders from major companies like NVIDIA and Tesla, collaboration with AMD could positively impact Samsung's performance.
