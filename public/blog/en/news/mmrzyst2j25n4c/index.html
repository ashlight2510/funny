Midjourney vs DALL-E: A Comparison of Image Generation AIs

March 16, 2026

Midjourney vs DALL-E: A Comparative Analysis of Image Generation AIs

These days, the images created by AI are truly amazing, right? However, many people might feel overwhelmed about whether to use Midjourney or DALL-E. I’ve summarized my thoughts based on my experiments.

Features of Midjourney

Unique Style and Emotion

Midjourney is particularly strong in artistic expression. I’ve used it a few times, and it generated many images that sparked my imagination. For example, when I input the prompt “a cat dancing in space,” it produced an image with stunning colors and a unique composition that left me in awe.

This aspect is important because Midjourney effectively conveys the emotions or atmosphere that users desire. Therefore, it seems suitable for artistic projects or poster designs. If you’re looking for emotional images, I recommend Midjourney!

Features of DALL-E

Realism and Flexibility

DALL-E truly excels in generating realistic images. After several attempts, when I requested “a whale playing piano on the beach,” it produced an incredibly lifelike image. In this regard, DALL-E is great for commercial use.

DALL-E supports various styles, allowing you to adjust the images according to your needs. For instance, if you request a specific artist's style, it will create images accordingly, making it useful for professional applications.

Usability Comparison

Interface and Accessibility

Midjourney requires image generation through Discord, which presents a slight technical barrier. I was a bit confused at first, but once I learned how to use it, I found it enjoyable. On the other hand, DALL-E is web-based and very intuitive, making it easily accessible to anyone. For beginners, DALL-E might be more convenient.

Considering this, those who prioritize user-friendliness may prefer DALL-E. However, if you want more creative images, trying out Midjourney isn’t a bad idea.

Pricing and Licensing

Cost Considerations

Both AIs offer free trials, but after generating a certain number of images, they switch to paid plans. Midjourney’s pricing varies based on usage, while DALL-E uses a credit system. Personally, I find DALL-E to be more economical for occasional use.

Cost issues can vary depending on the user's purpose or frequency of use, so it’s important to consider a price range that suits you.

Conclusion

In conclusion, both Midjourney and DALL-E have their respective strengths. If you want emotional images, go for Midjourney; if you need realistic and commercial images, I recommend DALL-E. Feel free to leave any questions in the comments! It would be great to start using one of these tools.
