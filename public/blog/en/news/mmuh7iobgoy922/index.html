The stock market has calmed down after the ‘Roller KOSPI’, and retail investors are feeling fatigued

Recently, after the KOSPI experienced drastic fluctuations, trading volume and margin trading funds have sharply decreased. The difference in trading volume between the first and second weeks of March is significant, which is interpreted as a reflection of the fatigue felt by individual investors. This week, the KOSPI has continued to hover around the 5500-5600 range, further highlighting the psychological contraction among investors.

This situation is not just a simple market change; it is also affecting the psychological state and investment patterns of individual investors. In particular, the decrease in trading volume of exchange-traded funds (ETFs) and the balance of margin loans can be interpreted as signals of overall market contraction.

Key Highlights

  • KOSPI trading volume decreased from 48 trillion won in the first week of March to 27 trillion won in the second week.
  • The trading volume of ETFs, primarily invested by individual investors, also decreased from 35 trillion won to 19 trillion won.
  • Margin trading funds have sharply decreased, with Samsung Electronics' margin loan balance dropping by 170.8 billion won.
  • Investor fatigue is increasing, leading to heightened volatility in the stock market.
  • Ahead of earnings announcements from semiconductor companies like NVIDIA, expectations are beginning to revive.

Reasons to Consider the Background

These changes stem from the drastic fluctuations observed in recent weeks. The KOSPI has experienced daily fluctuations exceeding 7%, causing investors to experience significant volatility. As a result, many investors are feeling anxious, leading to a decrease in trading volume and margin trading funds. In particular, individual investors are feeling fatigued in the face of the rapidly changing market conditions, which is reflected in a change in their investment patterns.

Why It Matters

This phenomenon is not just a change in numbers; it significantly impacts the psychology and investment strategies of individual investors. As market volatility increases, investors tend to become more cautious, which can lead to a decrease in trading volume. Therefore, this phenomenon is likely to have a significant impact on future market trends.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the changes in the stock market this week are not merely a temporary phenomenon. The fatigue felt by individual investors is directly linked to market confidence and will play a crucial role in future investment decisions. Particularly, the anticipation surrounding earnings announcements from large companies like NVIDIA can be a positive signal, but it is essential not to overlook the remaining uncertainties.

Hidden Variables to Watch

It is noteworthy that individual investors are turning their attention to safe assets like ETFs. This could signal increasing market uncertainty, and if this trend continues, the recovery of the market may proceed slowly. Additionally, the decrease in margin trading funds indicates a contraction in short-term investment sentiment, but it may also create a more stable investment environment in the long run.

Points to Check Now

  • Considering the recent volatility in the stock market, it will become increasingly important for individual investors to shift towards safe assets.
  • It is necessary to closely monitor the earnings announcements of NVIDIA and Micron. Predicting their performance's impact on the market is crucial.

FAQ

  1. What is the biggest mistake individual investors are making in the current stock market?

    • Making emotional decisions in a rapidly changing market situation is a significant mistake. A cautious approach is necessary.

  2. What impact will the decrease in margin trading funds have on the stock market going forward?

    • A reduction in margin trading funds may lead to decreased short-term volatility, but it can also result in a decline in investor confidence.

  3. How should investment strategies regarding ETFs change?

    • Given the increasing market uncertainty, investing in stable ETFs will become even more important.

At this point, it is essential to carefully observe the flow of the stock market. In particular, news related to NVIDIA will be very helpful in determining future investment strategies.
