Essential Japan Travel Itinerary: 10 Hidden Gem Attractions

March 15, 2026

Essential Japan Travel Course: 10 Hidden Gem Attractions

Many of you might be wondering, "Where should I go?" while planning your trip to Japan. Since famous tourist spots are already well-known, it can be a dilemma for those seeking a more unique experience. So, I’ve compiled a list of hidden gem attractions that I personally visited!

1. Amanohashidate

Japan's 'Heavenly Bridge'

Amanohashidate is a coastline located in Kyoto, Japan, and is nicknamed the 'Heavenly Bridge.' The scenery from this place is truly fantastic. I visited during sunset, and the view of the sunset coloring the sea and sky is something I will never forget. It's especially nice to explore by bike, so renting a bicycle would be a great idea!

2. Uji

The Land of Tea, Uji

Uji is famous for its high-quality green tea. When you visit, you can taste various teas, and personally, I found the Uji matcha ice cream to be incredibly delicious! Learning about tea culture while enjoying a cup of tea is also a great experience. A stroll along the Uji River while savoring a cup of tea would be delightful.

3. Nagano

Hidden Natural Treasure

Nagano is one of the regions in Japan with the most beautiful natural scenery. In particular, the hot springs in the 'Shinshu' area are famous, and soaking in the hot springs completely relieves fatigue. When I visited, I found a quiet hot spring and spent some truly peaceful time. If you want to heal in nature, I highly recommend this place!

4. Shimane

Mysterious Ancient Ruins

Shimane is known for its ancient ruins and mysterious shrines, and I especially recommend visiting the 'Izumo Taisha' shrine. This place allows you to feel the beginnings of Japanese mythology. I felt a sense of peace when I visited the shrine. Also, don't forget to enjoy fresh seafood from the sea in the Shimane area!

Conclusion

I’ve introduced some hidden gem attractions in Japan. If you want to have a special trip that’s different from famous tourist spots, make sure to add these places to your list! If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment. I think it would be great to start your next trip with these attractions!
