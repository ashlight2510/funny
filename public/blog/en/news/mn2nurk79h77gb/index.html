Wearing Life Jackets on Deck is No Longer a Choice but a Necessity

From July 1, wearing life jackets will be mandatory in Gangwon Province, and to prepare for this, the province plans to distribute life jackets to fishermen. This measure is part of the amendment to the Fishing Vessel Safety Operations Act, which requires all crew members on deck to wear life jackets regardless of weather conditions or the number of people on board. To alleviate the economic burden on fishermen, Gangwon Province has secured a supplementary budget last year to promote the distribution of inflatable life jackets.

Now is the time for fishermen to take responsibility for their own safety. The mandatory wearing of life jackets is not just a legal requirement but has become an essential measure to protect the lives of fishermen.

Reasons to Consider the Background

The mandatory wearing of life jackets is part of the amendment to the Fishing Vessel Safety Operations Act, which previously required wearing only during weather warnings. However, as accidents in the fishing industry have become more frequent, there has been a growing recognition of the need for more comprehensive safety measures. Consequently, Gangwon Province has proactively prepared necessary measures for the safety of fishermen.

Why is it Important?

This legal amendment is not merely about strengthening regulations; it is a crucial measure to protect the lives of fishermen. Particularly, since the working environment at sea can be dangerous, wearing life jackets is a matter of life and death. Therefore, this mandatory requirement can be seen as a necessary change for the safety of fishermen.
