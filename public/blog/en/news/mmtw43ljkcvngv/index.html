Seoul Eunpyeong Hospital Resumes 24-Hour Psychiatric Emergency Care

Seoul Eunpyeong Hospital has announced the resumption of its 24-hour psychiatric emergency room, which had been suspended due to COVID-19. The hospital is currently operating 13 beds and plans to add more public beds for psychiatric emergency patients in the future. Director Park Yu-mi stated that this initiative will strengthen active responses to psychiatric emergencies in Seoul and enhance community connections.

This decision was made due to multiple complex factors, including the collective resignation of residents and the hospital modernization project. Eunpyeong Hospital has now set a goal to establish a 24-hour psychiatric emergency response system, aiming to eliminate medical blind spots.

Key Summary

  • Seoul Eunpyeong Hospital resumes 24-hour psychiatric emergency room.
  • Currently operating 13 beds, with plans for additional public beds.
  • Re-establishment of the treatment system through the hiring of psychiatric specialists.
  • Focus on treatment of emergency patients and community collaboration.
  • Hospital modernization project expected to conclude in the first half of the year.

Key Highlights

  • Restoration of 24-Hour Treatment System: Immediate response capability for psychiatric emergency patients.
  • Expert Recruitment: Resumption of treatment with the hiring of three additional psychiatric specialists.
  • Additional Public Beds: Plans to operate 26 beds in the future, expanding patient capacity.
  • Community Collaboration: Strengthening the treatment system for psychiatric emergency patients through cooperation with various organizations.
  • Elimination of Medical Blind Spots: Providing a better treatment environment for psychiatric emergency patients.

Why Background Matters

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Eunpyeong Hospital operated as a dedicated hospital, leading to the suspension of psychiatric emergency care. Additionally, the collective resignation of residents severely exacerbated the shortage of medical personnel. However, with the recent hiring of psychiatric specialists and the ongoing hospital modernization project, the situation has begun to improve. The city of Seoul is showing its commitment to enhancing the quality of mental health services through these changes.

Why It Matters

Mental health issues are a highly significant social concern. Especially in the wake of COVID-19, where mental stress and depression have increased, the resumption of a 24-hour psychiatric emergency care system is essential. This initiative will not only provide treatment to patients but also contribute to raising the overall mental health level of the community.
