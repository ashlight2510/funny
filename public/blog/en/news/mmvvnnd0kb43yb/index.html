“Preparing for Drone Terrorism”… Crowd Control and Traffic Management with 31 Gates

Ahead of the scheduled BTS concert at Gwanghwamun Square in Seoul, the government and police have initiated large-scale crowd management. In preparation for the 'BTS Comeback Live: Arirang' on the 21st, the concert area will be controlled through 31 metal detector gates, and the terror alert level has been raised. The police expect up to 100,000 people to gather in addition to the 22,000 ticket holders, and plan to deploy over 7,000 police officers and more than 3,400 firefighters for safety management. These measures appear to be essential responses for ensuring the safety and smooth execution of the concert.

Such large-scale crowd management and security measures highlight public safety issues in downtown Seoul beyond just the concert's safety. Particularly, the need for vigilance against new forms of threats like drone terrorism gives this event significance beyond a mere performance.

Key Summary

  • Large-scale crowd management and traffic control implemented in downtown Seoul in preparation for the BTS concert.
  • Terror alert level raised, with a significant deployment of police and firefighting personnel.
  • Crowd control and risk item checks conducted through 31 metal detector gates.
  • Major subway stations and roads expected to be closed due to traffic control.
  • A total of over 10,000 personnel, including police and firefighters, deployed for concert safety.

This incident occurs ahead of a large-scale event, the BTS concert. The concert venue in downtown Seoul is typically a place where many people gather. However, with the recent increase in new forms of crime such as drone terrorism, safety management at such large events has become even more critical.
