The Wave of 'Purple Marketing' Following BTS's Comeback

As BTS's comeback concert approaches, the retail industry has fully engaged in 'purple marketing.' Purple is BTS's symbolic color, and various events and products are being prepared to capture the hearts of the fan club 'ARMY.' In particular, the demand for purple ice cream, beverages, and light sticks has surged in the Gwanghwamun area, highlighting BTS's economic influence once again. This concert is particularly anticipated as it marks the first stage where all BTS members return as a complete group after completing their military service.

This marketing goes beyond mere consumer promotion; it serves as a reaffirmation of BTS's influence on the Korean economy and the concept of 'BTSnomics.' Retailers are launching various purple-themed products to commemorate BTS's return and are strengthening their communication with fans.

Why Background Matters

BTS has gained immense popularity worldwide and has played a pivotal role in promoting K-POP globally. Their comeback is generating positive impacts on the Korean economy that go beyond mere musical activities. Especially since this is the first concert after BTS members complete their military service, both fans and businesses are filled with great expectations. In this context, the retail industry is leveraging BTS's symbolic color, purple, to further attract fans' attention.

Why It Matters

BTS's comeback is not just a festival for their fandom. It is also the moment when the influence on the Korean economy, known as 'BTSnomics,' becomes a reality. Everything fans spend for the BTS concert positively impacts the Korean economy, and an influx of tourists can also be anticipated. Therefore, this concert will serve as an important case showing how BTS, fans, and the economy are interconnected.

Points of Interest

In reality, the economic effects brought by BTS's comeback extend beyond concert revenues. Fans flocking to Seoul for the concert will engage in spending across various sectors, including accommodation, dining, and shopping. Thus, it is essential to consider the ripple effect of BTS's influence beyond just music, impacting the overall economy.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While the marketing related to BTS's comeback has many positive aspects, potential side effects from excessive commercialization should also be considered. Fans may feel burdened by purchasing light sticks at excessively high prices or by marketing strategies that encourage excessive spending. In this regard, retailers need to understand fans' psychology and maintain appropriate prices and quality.
