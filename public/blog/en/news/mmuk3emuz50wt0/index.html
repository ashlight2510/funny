BTS Concert Three Days Away... Excited and Nervous Gwanghwamun

As BTS's comeback concert approaches in three days, Gwanghwamun Square in Seoul is filled with both excitement and tension. While event staff are busy with stage installation, fans and tourists from various countries are flocking to the area. However, concerns about safety management and traffic control are growing as large crowds are expected. The police are deploying approximately 6,500 personnel to ensure safety measures, and nearby shops are contemplating their business operations.

This situation reflects not only the BTS concert but also the impact of large-scale events on the local community and economy. It is time to consider both the positive effects the concert will bring and the various issues that may arise as a result.
