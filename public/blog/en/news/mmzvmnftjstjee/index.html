Prime Minister Kim Inspects the BTS Concert Site in Gwanghwamun... Emphasizes Responsibility on HYBE

On the 21st, Prime Minister Kim Min-seok inspected the site of the BTS (Bangtan Sonyeondan) comeback concert in Gwanghwamun, Seoul, emphasizing the importance of safety management and HYBE's responsibility. He mentioned that since this concert is an event of national interest, HYBE should understand the public's inconveniences and take responsibility. On the day of the concert, 15,000 personnel will be deployed on-site to ensure safety, and roads near the venue will be controlled.

This issue is noteworthy as it goes beyond a simple concert inspection; it demands accountability from BTS and HYBE as they engage in an event supported by the nation and the community. In particular, this concert carries significance beyond being just a cultural event.

Key Summary

  • Prime Minister Kim emphasized that HYBE should recognize the public's inconveniences while inspecting the BTS concert site.
  • He reviewed safety management measures and plans, reiterating HYBE's responsibility.
  • 15,000 personnel will be deployed on-site for safety management, and nearby roads will be controlled.

Key Scenes

  • Importance of Safety Management: A large-scale personnel deployment and management system are necessary for concert safety.
  • HYBE's Responsibility: The company must recognize its responsibility for cultural events and consider the public's inconveniences.
  • National Support: Recognizing that the BTS concert is an event of national interest, it should be acknowledged as one supported by the nation and the community.
  • Significance of the Concert: The importance of the BTS concert as a cultural event in a national and historical context.
  • Crowd Management: Effective measures need to be established for the safety management of standing and seated spectators.

Reasons to Consider the Background

BTS is a group that enjoys immense popularity both domestically and internationally, and this concert is a significant event announcing their comeback. However, concerns regarding safety management have grown due to the anticipated large crowd. Accordingly, the government is working with HYBE to establish thorough safety measures, with Prime Minister Kim emphasizing HYBE's responsibility in the process. This can be interpreted as an effort to elevate awareness of cultural events at a national level.

Why It Matters

This concert is not only a significant event announcing BTS's comeback but is also taking place at a time when Korean cultural content is gaining global attention. Therefore, recognizing safety management and HYBE's responsibility could set an important precedent for similar incidents in the future. Additionally, minimizing public inconveniences and ensuring safety are directly linked to the success of the concert, making this inspection very important.
