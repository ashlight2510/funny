‘Suspension of Disciplinary Action’ Pro-Han Group Kim Jong-hyuk “Jang Dong-hyuk, take responsibility for ruining the party”

Recently, conflicts within the People Power Party have resurfaced. After the court suspended the disciplinary action against him, former Supreme Council member Kim Jong-hyuk of the Pro-Han faction held Representative Jang Dong-hyuk accountable for ruining the party. He argued that both Representative Jang and the Supreme Council should apologize to the public and party members, criticizing the party's Ethics Committee and Audit Committee as having devolved into tools for political purges. Such statements are further deepening the internal conflicts within the party, raising attention to how the court's decision will affect the future direction of the People Power Party.

This incident goes beyond a mere individual disciplinary issue, revealing the power struggles and political conflicts within the People Power Party. Through the court's acceptance of his injunction application, Kim emphasized that the disciplinary actions against him and lawmaker Bae Hyun-jin were unjust, claiming that the current behavior of the party leadership is unconstitutional. This raises serious questions about the party's autonomy and democratic processes.
