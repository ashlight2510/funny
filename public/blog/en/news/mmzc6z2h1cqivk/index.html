The Reason President Lee Jae-myung Demanded an Apology from 'That’s What I Want to Know'

Recently, President Lee Jae-myung expressed strong opposition and demanded an apology from SBS's current affairs program 'That’s What I Want to Know'. President Lee claimed that the program asserted his connection with organized crime, damaging his political image. This allegation began in 2018 and became a major factor that tormented him during the 2022 presidential election, which he referred to as part of the political terror inflicted upon him. Why Background Matters

The gangster connection theory regarding President Lee Jae-myung was first raised in the SBS program 'That’s What I Want to Know' in 2018. Particularly during the 2022 presidential election, this allegation resurfaced and significantly impacted his election defeat. President Lee criticizes this situation as 'manipulated broadcasting' and emphasizes the media's responsibility.
