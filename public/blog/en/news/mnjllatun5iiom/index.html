Rogers Coupang interim CEO, another large stock compensation... worth 6.1 billion won

Harold Rogers, the interim CEO of Coupang, has received another large stock compensation after about two months. This compensation consists of 213,884 shares, which would increase his total stock holdings to 930,041 shares if fully received. Coupang's parent company, Coupang Inc., stated that this stock is valued at approximately 6.14 billion won. However, these shares can only be received upon meeting certain conditions, and Rogers is currently under police investigation for perjury in the South Korean National Assembly.

Additionally, Coupang has granted stock compensation to other senior executives, and such large compensations are determined based on the company's management strategy and the performance of the executives.
