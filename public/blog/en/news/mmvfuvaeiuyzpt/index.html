As BTS's comeback approaches, hotels and resorts are seizing the opportunity to showcase various promotions. This movement is expected to have a positive ripple effect across the tourism industry, suggesting that fans may engage in experiential tourism beyond simply attending the concert.

Key Scenes

  • BTS Themed Packages: Launch of hotel packages that include BTS-related goods and menus.

Reasons to Consider the Background

BTS has formed a global fandom that extends beyond Korea, and their activities have always garnered significant attention. This comeback is expected to have a substantial impact not only on music but also on the tourism industry, as fans are anticipated to visit Korea to see the concert. In this flow, the hotel industry is moving toward providing special packages and experiences to attract fan visits.

Why It Matters

This BTS comeback holds significance beyond mere musical activities. Fans will feel a deeper connection through various experiences related to them, in addition to watching the concert. This could establish a new business model for the Korean tourism industry while providing fans with special experiences and injecting new vitality into the industry.

Points of Attention

In my view, the most important aspect of this promotion is the quality of the experiences offered to fans. It’s not just about purchasing products; various experiences that allow fans to feel connected to BTS will hold great meaning for them. For instance, tasting dishes themed around BTS's music in their rooms or participating in events that allow interaction with other fans can provide the special experiences they desire.

Hidden Variables to Consider

It remains uncertain whether these changes in the tourism industry will lead to sustainable development. A strategy that maintains ongoing communication with fans is necessary, rather than relying solely on a one-time event. Additionally, it will be important to continuously develop diverse content and experiences rather than simply depending on BTS's popularity.
