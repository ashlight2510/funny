US Treasury "If Trump wants to command war in the US, the US-China talks may be postponed"

Recently, US Treasury Secretary Scott Vesant mentioned the schedule of the US-China summit in relation to President Donald Trump's request for escorting commercial vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. He suggested that if President Trump needs to remain in Washington to lead operations against Iran, there is a possibility that the US-China summit could be postponed. However, Secretary Vesant emphasized that Trump's demand for cooperation to reopen the Strait of Hormuz would not lead to a postponement of the summit.

This statement reveals the complex context of US-China relations and appears to be a very important issue considering its impact on international affairs. In particular, it helps to understand how President Trump's diplomatic decisions could affect the global economy and security.
