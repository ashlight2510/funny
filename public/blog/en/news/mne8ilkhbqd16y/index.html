The stark difference in ticket prices and fan policies between Euro 2028 and FIFA World Cup

It has been reported that ticket prices for the 2028 UEFA European Championship (Euro 2028) are likely to be frozen at a level similar to Euro 2024. In contrast, ticket prices for the FIFA World Cup, which will be held in the same summer, are set at a very high level, drawing fierce criticism from fans. This highlights the stark contrast between UEFA and FIFA's fan policies, resulting in significant differences in fan experiences and accessibility to sports events.

This article emphasizes that Euro 2028's ticket policy is set with a fan-first approach and includes considerations for disabled fans. On the other hand, the FIFA World Cup is facing disappointment from many fans due to high ticket prices and abnormal resale fees, showcasing how the fan policies of the two tournaments are shaped differently.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This issue arises from the fact that the ticket policies of Euro 2028 and the FIFA World Cup are moving in different directions. While UEFA is freezing prices to make it easier for fans to attend matches and strengthening policies for disabled fans, FIFA is facing complaints from fans due to high pricing and expensive resale fees. These policy differences reflect the operational philosophies of the two organizations and their approaches to fans.

Why It Matters

This news goes beyond simple price comparisons and highlights the importance of fan experiences in sports. Policies that expand opportunities for fans to attend matches can enrich the essence of sports, which is the 'fan experience.' Conversely, high prices and unreasonable fees can lead many to give up on attending sports events. This can ultimately have a negative impact on the popularity and accessibility of sports.

Personal Interpretation

In my view, Euro 2028's fan-first policy can be interpreted as UEFA's effort to solidify its relationship with fans. It seems to recognize that attending matches is not just a consumption activity, but a way for fans to express their passion. In contrast, FIFA appears to prioritize commercial interests, which could lead to a loss of trust among fans in the long run. Sports should be centered around fans, but FIFA's current policies seem to go against this flow.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The dynamic pricing of the FIFA World Cup poses a significant problem as fans are exposed to unpredictable price fluctuations. This makes it difficult for fans to budget for attending matches, ultimately preventing many from going to the stadium. Additionally, FIFA's lack of support for disabled fans reveals significant limitations in terms of inclusivity.

Points to Check Now

It is essential to pay attention to how fans will respond to Euro 2028's ticket policies and how FIFA will address these criticisms. In particular, there is a need to be interested in what policies FIFA will implement in future World Cups.
