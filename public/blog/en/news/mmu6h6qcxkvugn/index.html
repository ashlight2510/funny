Maximum 2 Million Won Self-Care Subsidy for Family Caregiving Children and Youth: Key Issues

March 17, 2026 · 한겨레 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Maximum 2 million won self-care allowance for family caregivers of children and youth Source: Hankyoreh Children and youth who have given up their studies or employment to care for sick family members will be able to receive a self-care allowance of up to 2 million won. The Ministry of Health and Welfare announced on the 17th that the 'Enforcement Decree of the Act on Support for Crisis Children and Youth, including Family Care' was approved at the Cabinet meeting. Read the original article

Maximum 2 million won payment to family caregivers of children and youth: What does it mean?

According to a recent announcement by the Ministry of Health and Welfare, a self-care allowance of up to 2 million won will be provided to children and youth who have given up their studies or employment to care for sick family members. This measure is part of the enforcement decree of the 'Act on Support for Crisis Children and Youth,' aimed at preventing social isolation and supporting the independence of crisis children and youth. The enforcement decree is set to take effect on the 26th, and children and youth wishing to receive support must apply to their local district office.

This announcement includes the establishment of a dedicated organization for crisis children and youth, as well as the development of case management plans tailored to the circumstances of the beneficiaries. This will serve as a foundation to help them reintegrate into society beyond mere financial support.

Key Points

  • Target Group: Children and youth aged 13 to 34 caring for sick family members.
  • Support Amount: A one-time self-care allowance of up to 2 million won.
  • Application Method: Applications must be submitted to the mayor, county head, or district head, followed by a consultation and selection process.
  • Support Method: Provided in the form of vouchers that can be used through credit or debit cards for self-development, health care, etc.
  • Establishment of Dedicated Organization: A dedicated organization for crisis children and youth will be formed to manage cases and operate programs.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This issue has emerged as part of efforts to establish detailed implementation standards for the Crisis Children and Youth Act enacted in February last year. Children and youth caring for sick family members often find themselves in situations where they must give up their studies or employment. This exacerbates their social isolation and economic difficulties, leading to a growing recognition of the need for institutional measures to address these issues.

Why It Matters

This support goes beyond mere financial assistance, providing a crucial opportunity for crisis children and youth to reintegrate into society. Helping them grow into healthy adults is directly linked to the sustainability of our entire society, which emphasizes its importance even further.

Perspectives of Practitioners and General Readers

The policy supporting family caregivers of children and youth will present new opportunities and challenges for practitioners working in the welfare sector. They will need to consider how to select beneficiaries and what programs to implement. General readers should also understand the necessity and effectiveness of this policy and check if there are individuals in their surroundings who need such support.

Points to Note

In my view, the effectiveness of this policy will depend on how it is implemented. Simply providing financial support does not resolve the issues; continuous attention and management are required. For example, monitoring how the children or youth receiving support actually use the funds is necessary.

Hidden Variables to Consider

This policy may not encompass all crisis children and youth. For instance, there are children and youth isolated for various reasons beyond family caregiving. If they do not receive the benefits of this policy, new inequalities may arise.

Points to Check Now

  • Eligibility for Application: Check if your situation qualifies for support.
  • Share Information: Inform children and youth in need around you about this policy.
  • Monitor Policy Implementation: Pay attention to how the policy operates after implementation and provide feedback if necessary.

FAQ

Q: How is the self-care allowance provided?
A: The self-care allowance is provided through credit or debit cards in the form of vouchers that can be used for self-development or health care.

Q: What should I do to receive support?
A: Those wishing to receive support must apply to the mayor, county head, or district head, and go through a consultation and selection process.

Q: Who is eligible for support?
A: Children and youth aged 13 to 34 who care for sick family members are eligible for support.

Q: When will this policy take effect?
A: The enforcement decree is scheduled to take effect on the 26th.

It is important to closely observe how much practical help this policy can provide to children and youth, as well as any potential issues that may arise during the process.
