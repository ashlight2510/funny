Middle East Crisis and Supply Overcapacity: Emergency Response of Daesan Petrochemical Complex

The Daesan Petrochemical Complex in Seosan, South Chungcheong Province, is facing a severe crisis due to the Middle East crisis and global supply overcapacity. In response, the South Chungcheong Province government has decided to support a total of 464.4 billion won for five projects, including wage support for workers and an artificial intelligence transformation project. This support targets workers and related companies facing employment crises, aiming for short-term wage support and long-term industrial transformation.

In this situation, the crisis response of the Daesan Petrochemical Complex is becoming a significant test not only for economic support but also for the sustainability of the regional industry and future competitiveness.

Key Summary

  • The Daesan Petrochemical Complex in South Chungcheong Province is struggling due to the Middle East crisis and supply overcapacity.
  • The province announced a support plan worth 464.4 billion won, promoting wage support for workers and AI transformation projects.
  • The support targets construction and plant day laborers and cargo transportation businesses in the Seosan area.
  • Long-term measures also include transitioning to carbon-neutral new businesses and reducing electricity costs.
  • The government announced a support package worth 2.1 trillion won for the restructuring of the Daesan petrochemical industry.

Key Highlights

  • Emergency Support: Supporting workers and businesses through a total of 464.4 billion won in aid.
  • AI Transformation Project: Strengthening competitiveness through the artificial intelligence transformation of the petrochemical industry.
  • Electricity Cost Reduction: Expected annual savings of 15 to 17 billion won through the commercial operation of LNG combined heat and power plants.
  • Carbon Neutral Demonstration Center: Support for developing sustainable aviation fuel production technology.
  • Employment Crisis Response: Direct wage support for approximately 5,000 workers.

Why Background Matters

This crisis has been exacerbated by the political instability in the Middle East and the imbalance in global supply chains. The Daesan Petrochemical Complex is a crucial hub for the domestic petrochemical industry, and its crisis could significantly impact not just the regional economy but also the national economy. The South Chungcheong Province government is exploring various ways to overcome this crisis, and the government has also announced a support package to promote industrial restructuring.

FAQ

Q: Who will receive the support funds?
A: They will be provided to construction and plant day laborers and cargo transportation businesses in the Seosan area.

Q: When will the AI transformation project start?
A: A specific schedule has not yet been announced, but it is planned to be reflected in government research and development (R&D) projects.

Q: How will the electricity cost reduction be achieved?
A: Electricity generated from the LNG combined heat and power plant will be supplied to 14 companies, with expected annual savings of 15 to 17 billion won.

Q: What does the carbon-neutral new business entail?
A: It aims to develop integrated production technology for sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) throughout its lifecycle.
