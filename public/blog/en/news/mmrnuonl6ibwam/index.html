Effective Cleaning Methods to Remove Washing Machine Odor

Effective Cleaning Methods for Removing Washing Machine Odor

It's common to experience odors when using a washing machine, right? Especially when there's an unpleasant smell even after washing, it can be really frustrating. I faced this problem at first too, and I was at a loss about what to do. So, I've compiled the methods I've personally tried for removing washing machine odors. Let's explore them together!

1. Clean the Inside of the Washing Machine

The First Thing to Check

Cleaning the inside of the washing machine is really basic, but it's something you can't skip. Residual detergent or stains left in the drum are the main culprits of odors. The method I tried is simple.

  1. Pour 1 cup of vinegar into the washing machine drum.
  2. Run an empty wash cycle on the hottest setting.

After trying this, I noticed a significant reduction in the washing machine odor! It seems that the acidic properties of vinegar help eliminate bacteria. It was really effective.

2. Check the Filter and Hose

Cleaning the Filter is Essential!

Most people forget to clean the filter, but this can also be a source of odors. If dust or foreign substances accumulate in the filter, it can lead to poor drainage and odors.

A tip I've tried is to check the filter regularly. It's good to take it out and rinse it with water once a month. Also, be sure to check the hose! If it's blocked, water may not drain properly from the washing machine, causing odors.

3. Maintain the Exterior of the Washing Machine

Don't Neglect the Exterior

You also need to clean the exterior of the washing machine. Especially the rubber gasket around the door, which often gets wet, is an optimal breeding ground for bacteria. The method I tried is to wipe it down with a cloth, and it's good to regularly clean it with wet wipes.

Additionally, don't forget to leave the door open after using the washing machine! This helps to eliminate moisture inside, preventing odors. Thanks to this method, I've significantly reduced the washing machine odor.

4. Choose the Right Detergent

Detergent Affects Odor

Lastly, the detergent you use is also related to odors. Personally, I recommend using detergents with natural ingredients. Since they contain fewer chemicals, there's less odor after washing. Also, using too much detergent can leave residues that cause odors, so use only the appropriate amount!

From my experience of switching detergents, I've definitely noticed an improvement in odor. I feel good that there’s no strong smell after washing.

Conclusion

To remove washing machine odors, it's important to clean the inside, check the filter and hose, maintain the exterior, and choose the right detergent. I've been able to significantly reduce washing machine odors using these methods. If you have any questions, please leave them in the comments! I think starting with these steps would be great.
