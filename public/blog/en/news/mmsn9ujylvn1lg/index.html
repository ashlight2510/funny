As a result, the government has decided to conduct a special audit of the association and has opted to request an investigation into three serious misconduct cases. Additionally, the payment of 300 million won in national subsidies for this year has been completely suspended. These measures reveal issues within the association's operations and are likely to severely damage the credibility of an organization that should represent the rights of people with disabilities.
