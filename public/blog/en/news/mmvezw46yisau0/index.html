Gwanghwamun BTS Concert, Seoul City Takes Action with Comprehensive Traffic Measures

On the 21st, BTS's comeback concert is scheduled to take place at Gwanghwamun. In preparation for the expected large crowds, the Seoul city government has established comprehensive traffic measures. On the day of the event, road controls and subway station closures will be implemented, along with the operation of temporary trains and bus detours. These measures aim to minimize traffic congestion and ensure the safety of attendees.

This concert is expected to attract many domestic and international fans due to BTS's popularity, and the city has developed detailed traffic management plans in anticipation. In particular, by coordinating traffic control and temporary alternative transportation in line with the event schedule, the city aims to facilitate smoother participation for attendees.

Key Summary

  • Announcement of traffic measures around Gwanghwamun due to the BTS concert.
  • Road closures, non-stop subway operations, and station closures on the day of the event.
  • Deployment of 12 temporary trains for returning home and bus detours for city buses.
  • Suspension of personal mobility device (PM) rentals for pedestrian safety.
  • Multilingual announcements and information provided for foreign tourists.

Key Scenes

  • Road Closures: Major roads such as Sejong-daero, Sajik-ro, and Saemunan-ro will be closed.
  • Subway Operations: Non-stop passage and station closures at Gwanghwamun Station, City Hall Station, and Gyeongbokgung Station.
  • Temporary Train Operations: 12 temporary trains will be deployed for mass transportation after the concert ends.
  • Bus Detours: 51 city bus routes will temporarily detour.
  • Safety Measures: Enforcement against illegal parking and suspension of personal mobility device rentals.

Reasons to Consider the Background

BTS has a massive fan base both domestically and internationally, and their concerts always draw large crowds. The Seoul city government is proactively preparing to reduce traffic congestion by anticipating these situations. Notably, Gwanghwamun, being a central area in Seoul, experiences high traffic volumes, making congestion inevitable during large events. Therefore, this plan is not just about traffic management but also a comprehensive approach considering the safety and convenience of attendees.

Why It Matters

This BTS concert is not just a music event; it is a significant opportunity to test Seoul's traffic management capabilities and response to large-scale events. The success of the traffic measures will influence the trust in future similar events, affecting not only the many fans attending but also local residents.

Personal Interpretation

In my view, this plan signifies more than just traffic control. The Seoul city government has the chance to practically validate the complexities of managing large events through the BTS concert, which will positively impact preparations for other cultural events or large-scale gatherings in the future. Additionally, the multilingual announcement system for foreign tourists will help enhance Seoul's status as an international city.

Hidden Variables to Watch

One aspect that might be overlooked in this plan is how efficiently the traffic control and temporary train operations will be executed. Unexpected variables may arise in situations with large crowds, potentially leading to traffic congestion. Furthermore, if information about the turnaround stations for the temporary trains is not adequately communicated, attendees may face inconveniences.

Points to Check Now

  • It is important to check real-time traffic conditions through Seoul's traffic information website or app on the day of the event.
  • Before heading home after the concert, confirm the operation information for the temporary trains and clarify your destination.

FAQ

Q: What is the best way to avoid traffic congestion on the day of the concert?
A: It is best to check traffic information in advance and use public transportation. Be sure to check the information on temporary trains and detours.

Q: When will the non-stop subway operations begin?
A: All entrances will be closed between 2 PM and 3 PM on the day of the concert, after which non-stop operations will commence.

Q: How do I use the temporary trains after the concert?
A: Temporary trains will operate from 9 PM after the concert ends. Check the station you need to return to and use the trains that service that station.
