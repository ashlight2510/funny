The Secret of Diet and Supplements to Lower Cholesterol Levels

Recently, as interest in health has increased, many people are worried about their cholesterol levels. I also had a time when I was concerned after my blood test showed high cholesterol levels. So, I decided to organize this information. I want to talk about diets and supplements that can lower cholesterol!

The Power of Diet: Choose Healthy Fats

Benefits of Omega-3 Fatty Acids

To lower cholesterol levels, it's really important to choose healthy fats in your diet. From my experience, frequently consuming foods rich in omega-3 fatty acids has been very helpful. Eating fish like salmon and mackerel, or nuts like flaxseeds and walnuts is beneficial.

I often made grilled mackerel, and it tasted really good. I could feel the benefits of omega-3 in fish while enjoying delicious meals. These healthy fats help regulate cholesterol levels and also support cardiovascular health, so it's definitely worth incorporating them into your diet.

Make Sure to Eat Plenty of Vegetables and Fruits

Additionally, eating a lot of vegetables and fruits cannot be overlooked. In particular, consuming foods rich in fiber greatly helps lower cholesterol levels. I make it a habit to eat a salad every morning, and adding a variety of colorful vegetables makes it visually pleasing and healthy.

Doing this has improved my digestion and made me feel light, which has been a great help in starting my day. Among fruits, apples and pears are especially good, so it's nice to have them as snacks.

Choosing Supplements: What’s Best?

Fish Oil and Dietary Fiber Supplements

Sometimes, diet alone may not be enough, right? In such cases, considering supplements can be a good option. I recommend fish oil. It contains omega-3, which can help lower cholesterol levels.

Additionally, taking dietary fiber supplements is also beneficial. I tried it once, and I experienced improved gut health and relief from constipation. I think taking these two together would be even more effective.

Consult with Experts

However, when choosing supplements, consulting with an expert is the safest approach. I was initially confused about which supplements to take, but through consultation, I was able to find products that suited me. So, rather than buying and taking them blindly, I recommend discussing it with a professional.

Conclusion

In conclusion, to lower cholesterol levels, a healthy diet and appropriate supplements should go hand in hand. Make sure to consume plenty of vegetables, fruits, and healthy fats, and utilize supplements when necessary. Doing this will greatly help in maintaining your health.
