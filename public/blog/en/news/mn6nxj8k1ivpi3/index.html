Adore vs. Daniel and Min Hee-jin, 4.3 Billion Won Lawsuit Begins

Today, the 4.3 billion won damages lawsuit filed by Adore, the original agency of girl group NewJeans, against member Daniel and former CEO Min Hee-jin begins. Adore announced the termination of its exclusive contract with Daniel last December and stated that it would hold legal responsibility for various disputes arising from this. This case is expected to have a significant impact on NewJeans' future activities and the complex interests between the agency and the members.

This lawsuit will be an important first step in addressing the conflict between Adore, Daniel, and former CEO Min in court. In particular, since Adore is claiming damages amounting to 4.3 billion won against Daniel, his family, and former CEO Min, the outcome of this case is likely to have a major ripple effect across the industry.

Key Summary

  • Adore has filed a 4.3 billion won damages lawsuit against Daniel and former CEO Min Hee-jin.
  • The lawsuit has been assigned to the 31st Civil Division of the Seoul Central District Court, with the first preparatory hearing taking place today.
  • Adore claims legal responsibility related to the termination of Daniel's exclusive contract.
  • Members of NewJeans filed a lawsuit asserting the validity of their contract with Adore, but the court ruled in favor of Adore.

Key Scenes

  • Conflict between Adore and Daniel: Legal disputes arising from the termination of the exclusive contract.
  • Responsibility of Daniel's family: Adore claims that Daniel's family also bears legal responsibility.
  • Role of former CEO Min Hee-jin: Former CEO Min emerges as a key figure in this case.
  • Court's decision: The court's previous rulings in favor of Adore may influence future lawsuits.
  • Industry impact: The outcome of this case could affect the K-POP industry as a whole.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This lawsuit arises from the complex contractual relationship between Adore and Daniel. In November of last year, the members of NewJeans filed a lawsuit claiming a breach of contract by Adore, but the court dismissed their claims and ruled in favor of Adore. Such court decisions are likely to work against Daniel and former CEO Min. Notably, former CEO Min is currently playing a significant role in a stock dispute with HYBE, making the developments in this case particularly noteworthy.

Why It Matters

This case is important as it goes beyond a simple lawsuit and could set standards regarding contracts and legal responsibilities within the K-POP industry. It may serve as a case study on how the relationships between K-POP artists and their agencies are legally handled. Additionally, the outcome of this case could prompt a reevaluation of future artist contracts.
