Jung Won-oh “The only winning card to face Oh Se-hoon” Park Joo-min “A unique card”

In the midst of fierce competition among the candidates for the Seoul mayoral election, former Seongdong-gu mayor Jung Won-oh claimed that he is the only winning card against current mayor Oh Se-hoon. During this preliminary election joint debate, the candidates emphasized their connections to the Lee Jae-myung administration while comparing each other's policies and visions. Notably, candidate Jung is confident in his competitiveness in the upcoming election, given his high support in the polls.

The debate centered around discussions related to Seoul's real estate policies, with candidates attacking each other's weaknesses while presenting their own pledges. Candidate Jung emphasized his intention to gain support from the centrist voters in his confrontation with Mayor Oh, but other candidates expressed critical views on his policies. Attention is drawn to how the outcome of the Seoul mayoral election will be determined amidst these discussions.

Why Background Matters

This preliminary candidate debate was the first joint debate ahead of the Seoul mayoral election, clearly showcasing the ruling party candidates' efforts to rally support by emphasizing their connections to the Lee Jae-myung administration. Candidate Jung, enjoying high support in the polls, faced concentrated attacks from other candidates. The issue of real estate is one of the biggest concerns for Seoul citizens, making discussions on how candidates plan to address this issue crucial.

Why It Matters

The Seoul mayoral election is not just about electing a mayor for a city. It is closely related to the future policy direction of the Lee Jae-myung administration, and the resolution of Seoul's real estate issues has significant implications for the entire nation. Additionally, Jung Won-oh's claim of being the winning card could become an important variable in future election strategies.
