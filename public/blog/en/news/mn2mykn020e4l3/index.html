Yeongdeungpo-gu Demands Complete Re-examination of Susaek-Gwangmyeong High-Speed Railway

Recently, Yeongdeungpo-gu submitted a petition demanding a complete re-examination of the Susaek-Gwangmyeong High-Speed Railway construction project. This petition, containing the signatures of over 10,000 residents, was delivered to the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, emphasizing growing concerns about the safety and living environment of residents, as the high-speed railway line passes through elementary and middle schools and densely populated apartment complexes in Yeongdeungpo-gu. In particular, worries have been raised about the noise and vibrations that would occur if the line operates at a speed of 230 km/h, as well as damage caused by the installation of large ventilation shafts.

The voices of the residents go beyond a simple request for a route change, calling for a re-examination of the overall policy, with the Yeongdeungpo district mayor emphasizing that the safety of residents must be the top priority. Additionally, an alternative route suggested, ‘Sihung-daero to Yeouibang-daero,’ is also gaining attention.

Key Highlights

  • Over 10,000 residents of Yeongdeungpo-gu submitted a petition demanding a re-examination of the Susaek-Gwangmyeong High-Speed Railway.
  • The high-speed railway line passes through elementary and middle schools and densely populated residential areas, raising safety concerns.
  • Residents are worried about environmental damage due to noise, vibrations, and dust.
  • The Yeongdeungpo district mayor promised to adequately reflect residents' opinions.
  • The alternative route ‘Sihung-daero to Yeouibang-daero’ also needs to be considered.

Background and Context of the Issue

The Susaek-Gwangmyeong High-Speed Railway construction project is planned as an important transportation network connecting Seoul and the metropolitan area. However, as this line passes through densely populated residential areas and near schools in Yeongdeungpo-gu, concerns about residents' safety and living environment are increasing. In particular, recent years have seen significant stress among residents due to accidents or noise issues related to high-speed rail, prompting them to raise their voices collectively.

Why It Matters

This incident is an important case that illustrates the conflict between urban development and resident safety, going beyond just a local transportation issue. Large infrastructure projects like high-speed rail have direct impacts on local residents, and ignoring their opinions can lead to social conflicts. Therefore, this issue suggests that similar situations could arise in other areas as well.

Points of Interest

In my view, the key aspect of this incident is the voice of the residents. The fact that residents are actively expressing their opinions on safety and environmental issues is positive. However, it remains uncertain how these demands will actually be reflected in policies. There is a need for discussions on whether the government and local authorities can accommodate residents' demands and how realistic the alternatives are.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While the article emphasizes the residents' demands and concerns, discussions on the economic benefits or transportation convenience that the high-speed railway construction may bring are relatively lacking. In other words, there is a need for discussions on how to balance residents' safety and convenience. Additionally, the actual feasibility of the alternative route is also an important variable.

Current Points to Check

It is important to pay attention to how discussions between Yeongdeungpo-gu and the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport are progressing, and the likelihood of residents' opinions being reflected in policies. Furthermore, since similar cases may arise in other regions, it is necessary to listen to related policy changes and residents' reactions.
