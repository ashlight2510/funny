Oh Se-hoon Registers as a Candidate for Seoul Mayor… What’s the Reason for His Entry?

Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon has registered as the People Power Party's candidate for the June 3 local elections. He emphasized his sense of responsibility towards the citizens of Seoul by stating, “I enter the battlefield with the heart of a frontline commander.” However, his registration has led to criticism of party leader Jang Dong-hyuk and demands for innovation within the party, indicating that debates over party direction are likely to continue. It is analyzed that Mayor Oh decided to register as a candidate based on the judgment that it would be difficult to bear the backlash if he did not run.

This decision not only resolves the internal vacancy crisis within the People Power Party but also signals Mayor Oh's intention to lead the party's innovation. However, concerns about potential internal conflicts and Mayor Oh's future political prospects also exist concurrently.
