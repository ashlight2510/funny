Recently, Gyeonggi Governor Kim Dong-yeon has begun preparations for support for people's livelihood and energy in line with the supplementary budget (supplementary budget) that the government will prepare due to the impact of the Middle East war. Governor Kim emphasized cooperation with the government and directed that Gyeonggi Province actively establish a supplementary budget to support these areas. In particular, he instructed to focus on people's livelihood and energy sectors, exploring ways to expand tax revenue through the collection of high-value delinquents and uncovering hidden tax sources.

This measure is in accordance with the government's policy to alleviate the economic shock caused by the Middle East war and the surge in oil prices and to support vulnerable groups.
