Police, Drug Driving Repeats in Banpo Bridge Fall and Rolls-Royce Accident: What Questions Remain?

March 18, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article Overview Title: Police begin research on repeated drug driving incidents such as 'Banpo Bridge fall' and 'Rolls-Royce accident' Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun Recently, as concerns about drug driving have increased due to accidents such as falling from the Banpo Bridge while under the influence of drugs, the police have decided to review the establishment of a crackdown system that prohibits driving by introducing blood concentration standards for major drugs. The National Police Agency has started a study on "the introduction of blood concentration standards and the review of driving prohibition standards" in cooperation with related organizations such as the National Forensic Service, the Korea Road Traffic Authority, and the Korean Medical Association. Read the original article

Reasons Behind the Police's Move to Establish Drug Driving Crackdown Standards

Recently, serious accidents caused by driving under the influence of drugs have prompted the police to initiate research to establish drug driving crackdown standards. In particular, incidents like the fall from the Banpo Bridge and the Rolls-Royce accident have raised awareness. The National Police Agency has announced plans to strengthen the crackdown system on drug driving by introducing blood concentration standards. This comes in response to criticism that, while penalties for drug driving have been strengthened, there are no specific crackdown standards, leading to confusion.

This move is an important measure to reduce the risks of traffic accidents and protect public safety. The National Police Agency has begun research in collaboration with the National Forensic Service, the Korea Road Traffic Authority, and the Korean Medical Association, and plans to establish more specific standards in line with the revised Road Traffic Act that will take effect on April 2.

Key Summary

  • The police have initiated research to establish drug driving crackdown standards.
  • Social concerns about drug driving have increased due to recent accidents.
  • Penalties for drug driving will be strengthened under the revised Road Traffic Act effective April 2.
  • Plans to establish clearer crackdown systems through blood concentration standards.

Key Scenes

  • Increase in Drug Driving Accidents: In recent years, accidents caused by drug driving have surged, becoming a social issue.
  • Lack of Legal Standards: While penalties for drug driving have been strengthened, the absence of specific blood concentration standards makes enforcement difficult.
  • Collaboration Among Agencies: The National Police Agency is conducting research in cooperation with various organizations to seek more effective enforcement measures.
  • Public Safety First: The National Police Agency emphasizes that drug driving is a serious crime that threatens the lives and safety of the public.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This action has been significantly influenced by several recent accidents, particularly the fall from the Banpo Bridge and the Rolls-Royce accident. These incidents vividly demonstrate the dangers of drug driving and have heightened awareness of the issue across society. The number of license cancellations due to drug driving has also surged, which is one of the urgent reasons for the police to establish crackdown standards.

Why It Matters

This issue goes beyond simply establishing legal standards; it is essential for ensuring safety across society. Drug driving, like drunk driving, is a serious crime that can result in innocent victims. Therefore, the police's research and the establishment of a crackdown system will be an important step toward protecting the safety of all drivers.

Points to Note

In my view, the most noteworthy aspect of this research is the establishment of blood concentration standards. This will enhance the effectiveness of enforcement and provide a foundation for clarifying driver responsibility. Additionally, once these standards are established, it is expected to significantly help reduce the risks associated with drug driving.

Hidden Variables to Watch

For this research to proceed successfully, cooperation among related agencies is essential. However, differing interests and priorities among these agencies may affect how the enforcement system is ultimately established. It is also important to consider that blood concentration levels can vary depending on the type of drug and individual constitution.

Points to Check Now

  • Trends in Drug Driving Law Amendments: It is important to familiarize yourself with the contents of the revised Road Traffic Act that will take effect on April 2.
  • Accident Prevention Education: Participating in education or campaigns that raise awareness about the dangers of drug driving is also necessary to enhance social consciousness.

FAQ

Q: How much will penalties for drug driving be strengthened?
A: Under the revised Road Traffic Act, those caught drug driving may face imprisonment of up to 5 years or fines of up to 20 million won.

Q: When will the blood concentration standards be established?
A: The National Police Agency is currently conducting research, and specific blood concentration standards are expected to be announced soon.

Q: How is drug driving enforcement conducted?
A: Currently, the determination of drug driving is made through behavior assessments and simple reagent tests following alcohol measurements.

These issues are not just legal matters; they are directly related to the safety of our society. It is necessary to reflect on the dangers of drug driving immediately and to enhance understanding of related laws and standards.
