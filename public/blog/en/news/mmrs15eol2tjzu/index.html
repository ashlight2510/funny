8 Must-Visit Local Eateries in Southeast Asia Travel

March 15, 2026

8 Local Eateries You Must Not Miss on Your Southeast Asia Trip

One of the most anticipated aspects of traveling is the local food. Southeast Asia, in particular, is filled with delicious street food and hidden gems, making it hard to decide what to eat. So, I've compiled a list of 8 local eateries you must visit in Southeast Asia!

1. Som Tam in Bangkok

When you visit Bangkok, Thailand, you must try Som Tam. This is a papaya salad that has a spicy and tangy flavor that is simply exquisite. When I was in Bangkok, I saw locals lining up to get it, and I followed suit—it was truly delicious. Som Tam can be quite spicy, so those who are sensitive to heat should be cautious. However, personally, I found the spiciness just right!

2. Phở in Hanoi

You can't miss Phở in Hanoi, Vietnam. It's a noodle dish that is great for breakfast, lunch, or dinner, and the deep flavor of the broth is really appealing. When I was in Hanoi, I had Phở at a place known only to locals, and the broth was absolutely amazing. Enjoying Phở while soaking in the atmosphere of Hanoi was truly delightful.

3. Nasi Goreng in Jakarta

When you go to Jakarta, Indonesia, Nasi Goreng is a must-try. It's fried rice packed with meat and flavored with soy sauce. When I entered a local restaurant in Jakarta, I was really satisfied with the affordable price and generous portion. Eating it alongside other Indonesian dishes allows you to truly experience the flavors of the country.

4. Laksa in Kuala Lumpur

Laksa is famous in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. It's a dish where noodles are served in a broth rich with coconut milk and spices, offering a truly unique taste. Especially for those who love spicy food, it's the best. When I first tried it, I was captivated by the flavor of the broth. Eating it with locals made it taste even better.

Conclusion

When traveling in Southeast Asia, experiencing the charm of local food at various eateries is truly important. It's a great opportunity to experience the culture and flavors of each region. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment. And be sure to check out these eateries!
