Retail Investors Exhausted by KOSPI's Sideways Movement... Trading Volume and Margin Trading 'Plummet'

Recently, after KOSPI experienced significant fluctuations, the fatigue among retail investors has increased, leading to a substantial decrease in trading volume and margin trading funds. According to statistics from the Korea Exchange, the average daily trading volume of KOSPI in the second week of March was about 27 trillion won, nearly halving from 48 trillion won in the first week, which experienced dramatic ups and downs. In particular, the trading volume of exchange-traded funds (ETFs), which are primarily traded by individual investors, has also plummeted. This is interpreted as a result of growing concerns about market volatility, which has dampened investor sentiment.

This situation is not just a numerical change but is directly linked to the psychological pressure felt by retail investors. Experts explain that this fatigue is a natural phenomenon and also mention expectations for future market trends. Notably, with NVIDIA's annual developer conference and Micron's earnings announcement approaching, the possibility of a market rebound is being speculated.

Key Summary

  • KOSPI's average daily trading volume decreased from 48 trillion won in the first week to 27 trillion won in the second week.
  • ETF investments also plummeted, dropping from 35 trillion won in the first week to 19 trillion won in the second week.
  • Retail investor fatigue is increasing, leading to a significant decrease in margin trading funds.
  • There are also expectations for a market rebound.

Key Scenes

  • Decrease in Trading Volume: KOSPI's trading volume has nearly halved, reducing market vitality.
  • Contraction in ETF Investment: Retail investors are moving to safer assets, leading to a decline in ETF trading.
  • Decrease in Margin Trading: The contraction in investor sentiment has led to a reduction in stock investments through borrowing.
  • Causes of Fatigue: Increased psychological burden from dramatic fluctuations has caused market participants to become more cautious.
  • Future Outlook: The announcements from NVIDIA and Micron are seen as potential catalysts for a rebound.

Why Background Matters

This issue began in early March when KOSPI experienced repeated dramatic fluctuations. In particular, during the first week, there were extreme volatility levels exceeding 7% in a single day, prompting retail investors to invest heavily in stocks. However, as the market entered a sideways trend in the second week, these investors began to feel anxious and reduced their trading activity. Especially as KOSPI remained around the 5500-5600 range, with Samsung Electronics stabilizing around 180,000-190,000 won, investor sentiment appeared to have further weakened.

Why It Matters

This phenomenon is not merely a decrease in trading volume; it is directly linked to the overall investment sentiment in the market. The fatigue of retail investors can significantly impact the future direction of the market, which can also affect corporate performance and overall economic confidence. Therefore, market participants should not overlook these psychological factors.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the current market situation holds significance beyond mere fatigue. The sentiment felt by investors is connected to the overall confidence in the market, and if this sentiment persists, market recovery could be challenging. Particularly, if the announcements from NVIDIA and Micron yield positive results, it could serve as a trigger for a rebound; however, the opposite could lead to a more serious situation.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While the article emphasizes the current decrease in trading volume and fatigue, it is essential to note that if market volatility continues, a reevaluation of long-term investment strategies may be necessary. For instance, if the earnings announcements of specific companies are negative, the overall impact on the market could be more significant. Therefore, investors should focus on the market from a long-term perspective rather than just short-term trends.

Points to Check Now

  • Earnings Announcements from NVIDIA and Micron: It is crucial to closely monitor the impact and reactions to these announcements on the market.
  • Changes in Investor Sentiment: Continuous monitoring of how retail investors' sentiment evolves is necessary.
  • Trends in Margin Trading: Analyzing how changes in margin trading funds affect the market is essential.
