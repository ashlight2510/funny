The Iran War: Trump's Complex Calculations

The Iran war, which began with the recent preemptive strikes by the United States and Israel, has reached the one-month mark. President Donald Trump has declared that he will end the war quickly, but the reality is not that simple. The possibility of military clashes between Iran and Gulf countries still exists, and Israel is poised to continue its attacks even after the United States declares an end to hostilities. This article will explore the complex aspects of this war and its global implications.

A month has passed since the war began, yet President Trump continues to show determination to end it within 2 to 3 weeks. However, the situation is becoming more complicated due to Iran's retaliatory attacks, the backlash from Gulf countries, and Israel's ongoing assaults. Iran holds control over the Strait of Hormuz, which significantly impacts international oil prices.

Key Points

  • President Trump has declared he will quickly conclude the war, but the actual situation is unfolding in a complex manner.
  • The possibility of military clashes between Iran and Gulf countries still exists, raising concerns about continued Iranian attacks and retaliation.
  • Israel is prepared to continue its attacks even after the U.S. declares an end to hostilities, increasing tensions further.
  • Iran, controlling the Strait of Hormuz, is likely to pursue economic benefits through its influence on international oil prices.
  • President Trump's political situation significantly affects the war's duration.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This war can be seen as a result of President Trump's hardline stance on Iran during his election campaign. However, since the war began, public opinion in the U.S. has worsened, and rising oil prices have increased economic burdens. Iran is attempting to navigate the situation by attacking neighboring Gulf countries instead of engaging in a full-scale war with the U.S., complicating American military intervention further.

Why It Matters

The Iran war extends beyond a simple regional conflict, having significant implications for international politics and economics. The Strait of Hormuz is a major route for global energy transport, and Iran's strengthening control could pose a serious threat to global supply chains. Additionally, it has a considerable impact on President Trump's political standing, drawing attention to changes in international affairs linked to the political situation in the U.S.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the reason President Trump initiated the war was primarily to make a political gamble, which could turn out to be a political miscalculation. As the war drags on, public opinion in the U.S. is likely to worsen, negatively affecting his political standing. Iran is strengthening its international position through the war, which could further weaken the U.S.'s diplomatic standing.

Hidden Variables to Watch

While the article mentions President Trump's political pressures and Iran's military strategies, the long-term economic impacts of the war cannot be overlooked. The pain experienced by ordinary citizens due to high oil prices and inflation could deepen, leading to political instability.

Current Points to Check

  • Attention should be paid to how Iran's control strategy over the Strait of Hormuz unfolds.
  • Observing how President Trump's political standing influences the progression of the war is crucial.
  • It is important to examine how Israel's military actions might change the course of the war.

FAQ

  1. Why does President Trump want to end the war quickly?

    • The main reason is the decline in his approval ratings and worsening public opinion. The longer the war lasts, the more unfavorable his political standing may become.

  2. What strategy is Iran pursuing during the war?

    • Iran is attempting to avoid a full-scale war with the U.S. by attacking neighboring Gulf countries and is seeking to strengthen its control over the Strait of Hormuz.

  3. What is Israel's response?

    • Israel is prepared to continue its attacks even after the U.S. declares an end to hostilities, aiming to completely neutralize Iran.

  4. What impact will this war have on the international economy?

    • Rising oil prices and inflation could negatively affect the global economy, placing a significant burden on ordinary citizens.

The Iran war is not just a military clash but has complex implications for international politics and economics. Now is the time to closely monitor Iran's military strategies and the situation in the Strait of Hormuz.
