4 Essential Factors to Check When Buying an Air Purifier

In modern society, air purifiers have become a necessity rather than a choice. To protect health in an environment filled with fine dust and harmful substances, it is important to choose the right air purifier. This article introduces four essential factors to check when purchasing an air purifier.

1. Filter Type: Which Filter is Suitable?

The performance of an air purifier greatly depends on the type of filter. Since the air purification effect varies by filter, it is essential to check which filter is installed.

Functions of Filter Types

  • HEPA Filter: A high-performance filter that removes 99.97% of fine dust and allergy-causing substances. It is the most commonly used filter.
  • Activated Carbon Filter: Effective in removing odors and gases, making it useful in kitchens or homes with pets.

Checkpoints

  • Filter Replacement Cycle: Filters need to be replaced regularly, typically between 6 months to 1 year. This may vary depending on the usage environment, so check the replacement cycle in advance.
  • Replacement Cost: It is advisable to consider the cost of filter replacements when budgeting.

2. Air Purifier Performance: Check the CADR Value

An important indicator of an air purifier's performance is the CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate). The higher the CADR value, the more clean air it can deliver.

CADR Value Standards

Before purchasing, check the CADR value and select an appropriate performance level based on it. The general standards are as follows:

  • Less than 20 pyeong: CADR 200 or more
  • 20-30 pyeong: CADR 300 or more
  • More than 30 pyeong: CADR 400 or more

Checkpoints

  • Select Performance Suitable for the Area: If the performance is excessively high or low, efficiency may decrease. Choose a product that fits your living space.

3. Noise Level: Consider Its Impact on Daily Life

Noise level is a factor to consider regarding its impact on daily life when using an air purifier. High noise can interfere with sleep or concentration.

Noise Level Standards

Noise is measured in dB, and products below 30dB are generally considered quiet. Refer to the following standards:

  • 20dB: Whispering sound
  • 30dB: Quiet library
  • 40dB: Normal conversation sound

Checkpoints

  • Select Noise Level Suitable for the Environment: If used mainly at night, products below 30dB are recommended.

4. Additional Features: Smart Functions for Convenience

Smart features or air quality monitoring functions enhance the convenience of air purifiers. These additional features greatly improve user experience.

Explanation of Additional Features

  • Wi-Fi Connectivity: Allows for remote control via smartphone, making it convenient.
  • Auto Mode: Automatically adjusts the operating mode based on indoor air quality.

Checkpoints

  • Avoid Overspending on Unnecessary Features: Clearly identify the features you need and choose a product that fits those requirements.

Air Purifier Purchase Checklist

Before purchasing an air purifier, use the following checklist:

  1. Check filter type and performance
  2. Check noise level
  3. List necessary additional features
  4. Set a budget and compare prices

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. What is the most important factor when choosing an air purifier?

    • The type and performance of the filter are the most important. It is advisable to choose a product with a HEPA filter.

  2. How often should filters be replaced?

    • Generally, filters should be replaced every 6 months to 1 year, depending on the usage environment.

  3. What impact does a noisy air purifier have?

    • High noise can interfere with sleep or concentration, so caution is needed.

Conclusion: Choosing the Optimal Air Purifier

When purchasing an air purifier, it is important to consider essential factors to select the optimal product. Use the checklist now to find an air purifier that meets your needs! As a first step, it is recommended to analyze your living space and environment to determine the necessary filter type and performance.
