President Yoon's Constitutional Amendment Proposal: Meaning and Impact of Gradual Amendments

President Lee Jae-myung has directed an official review of a constitutional amendment to include the spirit of the May 18 democratic movement in the preamble of the constitution. This proposal aims to promote gradual amendments through consultations with the opposition, coinciding with the national referendum on constitutional amendments suggested by National Assembly Speaker Woo Won-sik. President Lee has also suggested including the Busan-Masan uprising alongside the spirit of May 18, emphasizing that the amendment should start with easy agendas that can gain public consent. However, pushing for amendments without the cooperation of the opposition poses significant political burdens, and it remains uncertain whether the amendments will actually take place.

  • Proposal for gradual amendments through consultations with the opposition.

  • The deadline for proposing the amendment is approaching, and cooperation from the opposition is necessary, but there are political burdens involved.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This constitutional amendment proposal appears to be an extension of President Lee Jae-myung's commitments made during the last presidential election. The May 18 democratic movement and the Busan-Masan uprising are significant events in modern Korean history, and including them in the constitution carries the meaning of reinforcing democratic values and historical identity. However, given the escalating opposition and political conflicts, it remains uncertain whether the amendments can actually be realized.

Why It Matters

This amendment proposal aims to solidify collective memory and identity by explicitly stating historical events in the constitution. It can also be interpreted as an attempt to foster democratic consensus through cooperation with the opposition in the current political climate. If the amendment is successfully enacted, it could mark a new turning point in Korean politics.

However, in a context of deepening political conflict, there is a risk that the amendment could devolve into a mere political tool. This point should not be overlooked.
