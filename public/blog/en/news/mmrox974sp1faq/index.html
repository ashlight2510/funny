Best Resorts for a Relaxing Getaway in Bali, Southeast Asia's Hidden Paradise

March 15, 2026

The Hidden Paradise of Southeast Asia: The Best Resorts for Relaxation in Bali

When planning a trip, there's always a dilemma: "Where should I stay?" This is especially true in a captivating place like Bali. So, I've put together a list of must-visit resorts in Bali. You can enjoy a wonderful retreat at these places.

1. Amalfi Resort in Ubud

Harmony of Nature and Art

One place I highly recommend is the Amalfi Resort in Ubud. This place harmonizes nature and art, making it perfect for a quiet retreat. I spent leisurely time by the pool, listening to the sounds of nature, and I felt my mind becoming at ease.

Special Experience

What I loved the most here was the daily morning yoga classes. It was truly healing. After yoga, a delicious breakfast awaited, allowing me to start the day on a positive note. I really appreciated these little touches.

2. Four Seasons Resort in Jimbaran

Luxurious Retreat

Next on the list is the Four Seasons Resort in Jimbaran. If you're looking for a truly luxurious experience, this is a must-visit. Watching the sunrise from a sea-view room was an unforgettable memory. Enjoying a cup of coffee while taking in such beautiful scenery makes you feel like the world is yours!

Various Activities

This place also offers a variety of activities. You can enjoy water sports like snorkeling or surfing, making it perfect for activity lovers. Personally, I took a surfing lesson; it was a bit challenging at first, but I felt a sense of accomplishment afterward.

3. The Legian Bali in Seminyak

Comfortable Atmosphere

I also recommend The Legian Bali located in Seminyak. Here, you can experience luxury and comfort at the same time. The sunset at the beach was truly magical. The feeling of enjoying a cocktail while watching the sunset is something else!

Variety of Restaurants

Additionally, this resort has several excellent restaurants, so you don't have to worry about meals. I particularly remember the restaurant where I could taste traditional Indonesian cuisine. Experiencing various flavors made the trip even more enjoyable.

Conclusion

A retreat in Bali is truly a special experience. The Amalfi Resort in Ubud, the Four Seasons Resort in Jimbaran, and The Legian Bali in Seminyak each have their unique charms, so you can choose based on your travel style. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment! I hope you enjoy a wonderful retreat at one of these resorts!
