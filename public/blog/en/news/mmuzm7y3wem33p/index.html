Samsung Electronics Accelerates Semiconductor Innovation through Collaboration with NVIDIA

Samsung Electronics is accelerating innovation in the fields of artificial intelligence (AI) agents and digital twins through its collaboration with NVIDIA. Song Yong-ho, head of Samsung Electronics' AI Center, announced semiconductor engineering strategies at NVIDIA's developer conference GTC 2026 held in San Jose, California, emphasizing that AI-based optimization is being applied throughout the entire process from design to manufacturing. Notably, the use of digital twin technology to achieve high-quality chip production at Samsung's Pyeongtaek Plant 1 has garnered attention.

This announcement serves as a significant milestone that forecasts the future of the semiconductor industry, highlighting Samsung Electronics' efforts to enhance manufacturing process efficiency and strengthen quality management through AI and digital twins.

Key Highlights

  • Samsung Electronics is promoting semiconductor engineering innovation using AI agents and digital twin technology through its collaboration with NVIDIA.
  • AI is applied throughout the entire design and manufacturing process to maximize efficiency and quality.
  • The case of high-quality chip production at Samsung's Pyeongtaek Plant using digital twin technology was introduced.
  • The announcement took place with the attendance of key figures from the semiconductor industry, drawing significant attention.
  • Samsung Electronics plans to continuously support the implementation of next-generation AI systems for its clients.

Why Background Matters

Samsung's announcement comes at a time when competition in the semiconductor industry is intensifying, and the importance of AI and digital twin technologies is becoming more prominent. AI technology has become essential for enhancing the complexity and competitiveness of semiconductor manufacturing processes, and maintaining productivity and quality is now a survival strategy for companies. The collaboration with NVIDIA reflects Samsung's commitment to leading technological innovation in this trend.
