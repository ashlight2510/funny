Iran Confirms Death of Security Chief and Militia Commander... "Harsh Revenge"

The Iranian leadership is warning of a strong retaliation against the U.S. and Israel, and this incident is expected to have significant implications for Iran's security and foreign policy.

This death is not merely the loss of individuals but could serve as a crucial turning point that might alter Iran's political landscape. Iran has shown strong resistance against the U.S. and Israel over decades of tension, and this incident is likely to escalate that tension further.
