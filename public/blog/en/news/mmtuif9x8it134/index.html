Firmly promote prosecutorial reform, no excess” Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun President Lee Jae-myung stated on the 17th regarding the government's re-legislation proposal for the establishment of a Serious Crime Investigation Agency and Public Prosecution Service, “I have instructed the government to delete the provisions regarding the oversight of special judicial police (special law enforcement) and the involvement of prosecutors during investigations.” President Lee posted on X that “the party-government agreement made through party-government consultations should be within the necessary scope to exclude prosecutorial investigations.” Read the original article

President Lee Jae-myung Expresses Strong Will for Prosecutorial Reform

President Lee Jae-myung announced that he would delete the oversight provisions for special judicial police and the provisions regarding prosecutors' involvement in investigations related to the government's re-legislation proposal for the establishment of a Serious Crime Investigation Agency and Public Prosecution Service. This reflects a commitment to firmly promote the exclusion of prosecutorial investigations and the separation of investigation and prosecution, while also mentioning concerns about excessive reform. President Lee emphasized the need for a cautious approach to ensure that prosecutorial reform does not deviate from its essence.

These remarks reveal a strong commitment to prosecutorial reform while attempting to strike a balance between political interests and the essence of reform. President Lee warned that reforms must yield tangible results and that unnecessary excess should be avoided.

Key Summary

  • President Lee Jae-myung instructed the deletion of the oversight provisions for special judicial police in the bill related to the establishment of the Serious Crime Investigation Agency and Public Prosecution Service.
  • Firmly promote the exclusion of prosecutorial investigations and the separation of investigation and prosecution as a national agenda.
  • Emphasized caution against excessive measures during the reform process and the need to remain faithful to its essence.

Key Scenes

  • Deletion of Special Judicial Police Oversight Provisions: An intention to minimize prosecutorial intervention in investigations.
  • Separation of Investigation and Prosecution: A core goal of prosecutorial reform aimed at establishing an independent investigation system.
  • Caution Against Excessive Reform: A need for a careful approach to ensure that reforms do not deviate from their essence.
  • Political Interests: The potential for political backlash during the reform process.
  • Emphasis on Tangible Results: A strategy to enhance the effectiveness of reforms.

Reasons to Consider the Background

President Lee's remarks come at a time when discussions on prosecutorial reform have become a hot issue. Past political events have raised concerns about the abuse of prosecutorial power, leading to calls for reform. In particular, various measures are being discussed to ensure the independence and fairness of prosecutorial investigations, and this action can be seen as a reflection of that trend.

Why Is It Important?

This statement serves as a reminder that prosecutorial reform is not merely a political investigation but an essential process for the rule of law and a fair society. President Lee's will could become a significant milestone in determining the direction of future prosecutorial reform. Particularly, if social consensus on prosecutorial reform is not achieved, the effectiveness of the reform could be significantly diminished.

Points of Attention

In my view, a particularly noteworthy aspect of President Lee's remarks is the caution against 'excess.' This can be interpreted as an intention to minimize potential political backlash during the reform process. In other words, it acknowledges that if conflicts between supporting and opposing forces intensify, the reform could actually regress.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One aspect missing from this announcement is the specific implementation plan for the reform. Simply deleting oversight provisions does not mean that prosecutorial powers will be completely reduced. In reality, the details of the bill and how they are applied will determine the success or failure of the reform. Therefore, how this aspect is addressed in future discussions will be crucial.

Current Checkpoints

  • Attention should be paid to how President Lee's reform intentions will be reflected in the actual legislation.
  • It is necessary to observe the political backlash in future discussions on prosecutorial reform and how to respond to it.
  • Continuous monitoring of the specific implementation plans for prosecutorial reform and the resulting social reactions will be essential.
