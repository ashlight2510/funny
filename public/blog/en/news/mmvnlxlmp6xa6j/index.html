Cherry blossoms in full bloom at Seoul's Hadong Plum Blossom Street, and 'Spring' rain

Recently, cherry blossoms signaling the arrival of spring have fully bloomed on Hadong Plum Blossom Street in Seongdong-gu, Seoul. On the 18th, just two days before the vernal equinox, citizens enjoyed a stroll under umbrellas, admiring the beauty of the cherry blossoms. While cherry blossoms are a familiar sight every spring, this year, it was particularly striking to see so many people taking photos and appreciating the flowers. This is seen not just as a scenic appreciation but as an important moment marking the start of a new season.

The full bloom of these cherry blossoms is not only a signal of spring's arrival but also an opportunity that brings small joys to the daily lives of Seoul citizens. In particular, during this time, Hadong Plum Blossom Street has established itself as a beloved destination for many.

  • Citizens have been spotted enjoying the cherry blossoms and taking photos.
  • At this point, just before the vernal equinox, the cherry blossoms symbolize the start of a new season.

  • Citizen participation: Many people were seen enjoying the cherry blossoms, taking photos, and experiencing small joys in their daily lives.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Hadong Plum Blossom Street is known for its cherry blossoms that fully bloom every spring. This area is home to many cherry trees, and as spring arrives, numerous visitors come to enjoy the flowers. Especially this cherry blossom season, more people are gathering as the vernal equinox approaches. This provides an opportunity to experience not just flower viewing but also the beauty of nature and the change of seasons.

FAQ

Q: When does the cherry blossom season end?
A: Cherry blossoms typically bloom from mid-March to early April. There may be some variation depending on the weather.

Q: How do I get to Hadong Plum Blossom Street?
A: You can take Seoul Subway Line 2 and get off at Seongsu Station, then walk about 10 minutes.

Q: Where else can I enjoy cherry blossoms?
A: In Seoul, you can also enjoy cherry blossoms at Yeouido and Seoul Forest. The timing of the full bloom may vary by region, so it's good to check in advance.
