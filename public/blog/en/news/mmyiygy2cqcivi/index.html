Organizing Photos and Videos on Galaxy: Simple Methods

March 20, 2026

Organizing Photos and Videos on Your Galaxy: A Simple Method

With the increase in smartphone usage, the amount of photos and videos has also surged. Many people capture precious moments with their smartphones, but disorganized media files take up storage space and can be hard to find when needed. Therefore, efficient organization is necessary. In this article, we will explore how to effectively organize photos and videos using a Galaxy smartphone. Each step will provide the necessary information and useful tips, so try it out right away.

The Importance of Organizing Photos and Videos on Your Galaxy Smartphone

Why is organizing photos and videos important?

Organizing photos and videos on a Galaxy smartphone is important for two main reasons. First, it helps free up storage space. By deleting unnecessary files, you can save storage on your smartphone. Second, it allows for efficient searching. With organized files, you can easily find the photos or videos you want, ensuring you don't miss important moments.

What are the methods for organization?

You can use the default Gallery app on your Galaxy to organize photos and videos. This app automatically organizes photos and helps users create albums by theme. Additionally, it can be linked with cloud services for automatic backup and organization.

What should you be careful about?

Be careful not to accidentally delete important files while organizing. It's a good idea to back up photos or videos that capture significant moments separately. Also, it's necessary to develop a habit of organizing regularly. It's advisable to set aside time for organization at least once a month.

How to Use the Galaxy Gallery App

Introduction to Basic Features

The Galaxy Gallery app offers features that automatically organize photos and videos. Through this app, you can easily manage the files you have already captured.

How to Create and Manage Albums

Try creating themed albums. For example, if you divide albums into categories like travel, family, and friends, it will be easier to find them later. Moving or deleting files within an album is also simple. Just press and hold the file and select the desired action.

Utilizing Convenient Search Features

The search function in the Gallery app is very helpful for finding photos by date, location, or person. For example, if you want to find photos from a specific trip, you can enter the date or search for the name of the destination to easily locate related photos. Adding tags is also a way to enhance searchability.

Backing Up Photos and Videos with Cloud Services

Advantages of Cloud Services

Cloud services allow for safe data storage. Even if you lose your device or it malfunctions, you can recover your data. Additionally, you can access files from multiple devices, allowing you to check them anytime, anywhere.

How to Utilize Cloud Services

Services like Google Photos or Samsung Cloud are recommended. These services offer automatic backup features that automatically save photos and videos taken on your smartphone to the cloud. The setup process is simple; you can find the cloud backup option in the settings menu of the Gallery app.

Points to Note

Cloud services may have data capacity limits. If you exceed the free capacity, you may need to switch to a paid service. Also, it's important to check security settings when storing data in the cloud for privacy protection.

Photo and Video Organization Checklist

Pre-Organization Checklist

  • Check for Gallery app updates
  • Complete cloud backup settings
  • Create a list of unnecessary files

Post-Organization Checklist

  • Complete creation of new albums
  • Complete deletion of duplicate files
  • Establish a regular organization plan

Using the checklists before and after organization can help you organize systematically.

Tips and Tricks for Organizing Photos and Videos

Efficient Organization Methods

It's important to develop a habit of organizing regularly. Setting aside time each week or month for organization and deleting unused apps to free up storage space is also a good method.

Example

Try creating albums organized by specific themes or events. This way, you can easily compare how the Gallery screen changes after organization. Refer to the table below.

Before Organization After Organization
Unnecessary photos and videos mixed together Albums organized by theme
Time-consuming to search Quick search possible

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. How can I easily organize photos and videos on my Galaxy?

    • You can use the Galaxy Gallery app to create albums by theme and delete unnecessary files for organization.

  2. How do I recover files stored in cloud services?

    • You can log into the app or website of the cloud service and select the files you want to recover.

  3. What is an easy way to find duplicate files in the Galaxy Gallery app?

    • You can use the feature in the Gallery app to check for and delete duplicate files, or use specific search filters to locate duplicates.

Through this article, you have learned how to organize photos and videos on your Galaxy smartphone. Start with the first step by opening the Gallery app and deleting unnecessary files. Once you develop the habit of organizing, using your smartphone will become much more convenient.
