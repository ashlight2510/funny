Daejeon Fire Factory CEO Faces Demands for Compensation While Apologizing to Bereaved Families

A major incident occurred at an automobile parts manufacturing factory in Daedeok-gu, Daejeon, resulting in the deaths of 11 people and injuries to 59 others. In connection with this, Son Joo-hwan, CEO of Safe Industrial, personally visited the bereaved families to express his apologies; however, the families demanded not just an apology but substantial compensation and responsible action. The bereaved families showed strong resistance, demanding the submission of specific documents regarding compensation and support plans, as well as the direct involvement of a decision-making representative.

This incident highlights not just a mere accident but also raises issues regarding corporate safety management and accountability. It is essential to observe how the families' demands will be addressed and what impact this incident will have on corporate safety management and sense of responsibility in the future.
