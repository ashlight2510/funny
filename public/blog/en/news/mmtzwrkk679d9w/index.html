“Until the Whole World Cries”…Gwanghwamun Billboard, BTS and a Message of Hope

Recently, Kyobo Life and BTS collaborated to present a gigantic wrap on the Gwanghwamun Billboard containing a message of hope. This project aims to encourage citizens to realize their potential and move towards a larger world through a message connected to BTS's new album. Kyobo Life emphasized that this collaboration effectively conveys the values of hope and encouragement shared by these two cultural icons.

This project focuses not only on visual elements but also on spreading positive messages to a wider audience through the culturally symbolic Gwanghwamun Billboard. It is essential to observe what kind of resonance this attempt will create and how people will actually receive it.

Key Summary

  • Kyobo Life and BTS collaborated to install a gigantic wrap on the Gwanghwamun Billboard with a message of hope.
  • The phrase “The story that started with me will resonate with the whole world” conveys an encouraging message to citizens.
  • The design is inspired by the trigrams of the Korean flag and incorporates design elements from BTS's new album.
  • This collaboration aims to spread positive social messages through the combination of cultural icons.

Key Scenes

  • Collaboration of Cultural Icons: The meeting of Kyobo Life and BTS is significant as it represents two powerful cultural icons delivering a message of hope together.
  • Encouraging Citizen Participation: Through BTS's message, citizens are encouraged to recognize and realize their potential.
  • Symbolism of Design: The design, inspired by the trigrams of the Korean flag, emphasizes Korea's cultural identity.
  • Social Resonance: It is necessary to observe how many people this project will actually impact and what their reactions will be.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Kyobo Life is a long-established company, and the Gwanghwamun Billboard has become an iconic cultural space in Seoul. BTS is a globally popular K-pop group, and their messages extend beyond mere music to exert social influence. The combination of these two elements holds great significance in cultural and social contexts. Particularly, in recent years, many have faced difficult times due to COVID-19, making messages of hope even more urgently needed.

Why It Matters

This project can be seen as an attempt that combines cultural symbolism with social messaging rather than just an advertisement. The combination of BTS's global fandom and Kyobo Life's traditional values has the potential to positively impact a larger audience. It is important to observe whether such attempts can bring about positive changes in society and how many people resonate with this message.
