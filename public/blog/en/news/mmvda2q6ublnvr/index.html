Oh Se-hoon expresses intention to sue special prosecutor Min Joong-ki for the crime of distorting the law

Recently, Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon announced his intention to sue special prosecutor Min Joong-ki for the crime of distorting the law. This is based on his claim that the special prosecutor, who investigated him for alleged violations of the Political Fund Act, reversed the roles of victim and perpetrator. Mayor Oh criticized the fact that two criminals who testified in court were not indicted and claimed that the special prosecutor adjusted his indictment to coincide with the election. This case is a significant issue that could have political repercussions and influence public opinion beyond a simple legal dispute.

Mayor Oh Se-hoon's remarks can be interpreted not just as a counterattack, but as a strategy for political survival. This incident reveals the complex interests related to the upcoming Seoul mayoral election, along with the legal challenges he faces.
