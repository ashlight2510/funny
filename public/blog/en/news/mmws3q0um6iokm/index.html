Seoul Spring Fires: 7079 Cases in 5 Years... Half Due to 'This'

Cigarette butts have been identified as a major cause of spring fires in Seoul. According to the Seoul Fire and Disaster Headquarters, approximately 49% of the 7,079 spring fires in the past five years were caused by cigarette butts, and 62% of all fires were attributed to negligence. In response, the city of Seoul plans to strengthen its 'Spring Fire Safety Measures' and promote prevention activities, focusing on residential facilities and construction sites.

This article not only lists the occurrences of fires but also raises important awareness regarding citizen safety. It illustrates how a seemingly minor act of negligence, such as discarding a cigarette butt, can lead to significant fires, emphasizing the need for prevention.

Key Summary

  • A total of 7,079 spring fires occurred in Seoul over the past five years.
  • Fires caused by cigarette butts account for about 49%.
  • Negligence is the leading cause, making up 62% of all fires.
  • The city of Seoul plans to enhance safety measures for fire prevention.
  • The risk of spring fires is heightened by dry weather and strong winds.
