Daejeon Safety Industry Fire Disaster: The Loss of 14 Lives and Its Aftermath

On March 20, 2023, a massive fire at the Safety Industry in Daejeon’s Daedeok District claimed 14 lives and injured 60 people. At the time of the incident, out of 170 employees working at the factory, over 90 managed to evacuate safely, but more than 50 were injured while jumping from windows, resulting in severe casualties. Particularly heartbreaking is the fact that one of the victims left a message for his girlfriend saying, "Tell them I love them" in his final moments.

This fire marks one of the deadliest industrial accidents in recent years, highlighting the urgent need for safety management. This incident is not just an accident but a warning of how vulnerable our society's safety systems are.
