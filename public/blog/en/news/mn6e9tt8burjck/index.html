Lee Se-woong, North Pyongan Governor, Rises to 1st in Wealth Due to Samsung Electronics Stock Surge

According to the '2026 Regular Property Change Report' recently released by the Ministry of Personnel Management, Lee Se-woong, the Governor of North Pyongan Province, has gained attention by ranking first in total wealth. His wealth amounts to 158.7 billion won, primarily due to the increase in the value of Samsung Electronics stock. Last year alone, he recorded an increase of over 54.0 billion won in wealth, thanks to the surge in the Samsung Electronics stocks he holds.

This news highlights not only the wealth change of an individual but also the broader impact of Samsung Electronics' stock price on society. It serves as an important case illustrating how the volatility of the stock market can affect individual wealth as well as the economy as a whole.
