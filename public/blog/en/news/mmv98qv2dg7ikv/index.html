Government Raises Terror Alert to 'Caution' in Jongno and Jung-gu for BTS Concert

Ahead of BTS's comeback concert on the 21st, the government has raised the terror alert level for the Jongno and Jung-gu areas in Seoul from 'Attention' to 'Caution'. This measure is in response to the Prime Minister's Office's directive to enhance safety measures, considering the potential terror threat at events with large crowds. The raised terror alert will be maintained from the 19th to the 21st, and relevant agencies plan to strengthen vigilance and establish immediate response systems for major event venues and public facilities.

This news goes beyond just safety measures for the BTS concert; it could significantly impact the national safety management system for large-scale events and public safety awareness. Safety issues at events with many fans are always sensitive matters, and this action carries considerable importance.
