How to Save 30% More Every Year by Comparing Insurance Products

Many people are suffering as insurance premiums rise every year, right? I used to think, "I can just renew it," but I found out that I could save a significant amount of money. So, I’ve organized a way to save 30% more each year through comparing insurance products.

The Need for Insurance Product Comparison

Insurance can be a great help in unexpected situations, but it also comes with a hefty price tag. That's why I realized how important it is to compare insurance products. From my experience, even with the same coverage, the prices vary greatly among insurance companies.

For example, a few years ago, I had health insurance with Company A, but when I compared it with Company B, I found that it was about 30% more expensive for similar conditions. The reason for these differences is that each insurance company has different operational policies and risk assessments. So, rather than just signing up, it's best to compare.

Utilizing Insurance Comparison Sites

So, how should you start comparing? Nowadays, there are many insurance comparison sites that are very useful. You can compare multiple insurance companies at a glance and filter according to your desired conditions.

Among the sites I've used, ‘Insurance Damoa’ was the best. By going there, I could select the coverage I needed and input my expected premium, and it would show me the products offered by various insurance companies. At first, it felt overwhelming, but once I tried it, I found it really easy to compare. I’ve saved several thousand won each year through this.

Carefully Examine Coverage Details

When comparing insurance products, simply looking at the price can be a bit risky. You also need to carefully examine the coverage details. Just because a product is cheap doesn’t mean it’s necessarily good.

In one case I saw, Company A offered a cheap insurance policy, but the coverage was so limited that I ended up unable to cover significant hospital bills. So, I checked whether the coverage met my desired level first, and then compared prices. This turned out to be a really important part.

Considering Expert Consultation

Finally, getting advice from an expert when comparing insurance products is also a good approach. Initially, I struggled alone, but after consulting with an expert, I was able to cover aspects I hadn’t thought of.

Consultations with insurance planners are often free, so you can discuss without any pressure. I was able to find better conditions for products after a few consultations, and during this process, I adjusted to reduce unnecessary coverage and add necessary coverage.

Conclusion

Insurance is an essential part of our lives, but there are definitely ways to save money. Don’t forget that you can save 30% more each year through insurance product comparison, and I highly recommend you give it a try. If you have any questions, please leave them in the comments! This is a good place to start.
