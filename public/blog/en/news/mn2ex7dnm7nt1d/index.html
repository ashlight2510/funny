Why Are Gunsan, Busan, and the Capital Region Important for Park Ji-won's and Cho Kuk's Candidacies?

March 23, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Park Ji-won, “Cho Kuk, Busan and the Capital Region are Possible Areas for Candidacy... Gunsan is Not Acceptable to Voters” Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun On the 23rd, Park Ji-won, a member of the Democratic Party, stated that if Cho Kuk, the leader of the Cho Kuk Innovation Party, runs in the by-election for the National Assembly, “Busan and the capital region are possible areas for candidacy.” In an SBS radio interview on <Kim Tae-hyun's Political Show>, Park said, “Members of the National Assembly run in their home regions.” He added, “If he says he will run in Honam, it should be at least in symbolic Gwangju or Jeonju, which symbolizes North Jeolla Province...” Read the original article

Park Ji-won's Opinion on Cho Kuk's Candidacy Area

Park Ji-won, a member of the Democratic Party, expressed his opinion on the area where Cho Kuk, the leader of the Cho Kuk Innovation Party, might run for the National Assembly by-election. He mentioned that Busan and the capital region are viable areas for candidacy, emphasizing that Gunsan would not be acceptable to voters. Park also showed a negative stance on the possibility of the Democratic Party not fielding a candidate if Cho runs in Gunsan. This statement provides important insights into Cho Kuk's political position and future electoral strategy.

This article addresses the potential for Cho Kuk's political resurgence and the reactions of local voters to it. Park's remarks can be interpreted as a strategic analysis that goes beyond mere opinion, considering the importance of political home regions and voter sentiment.
