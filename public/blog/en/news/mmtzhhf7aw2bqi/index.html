YouTuber Insults and Mocks Police Officers, Sentenced to Prison at Buchon Station

Recently, a YouTuber who insulted and mocked police officers responding to noise complaints at Buchon Station was sentenced to prison in an appeal. During a live broadcast, the YouTuber made insulting remarks towards the police officers and streamed it on his channel, leading the court to determine that his actions could lead to a disregard for public authority. This incident is noteworthy as it is not just an individual’s transgression but has significant implications for society as a whole.

This ruling will serve as a wake-up call not only for the punishment of one YouTuber but also for the respect of public authority and social responsibility online. It conveys a message that insulting behavior towards public officials, such as police officers, will no longer be tolerated.
