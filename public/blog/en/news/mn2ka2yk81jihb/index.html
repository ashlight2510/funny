Mayor Park Hyung-jun's Head Shaving and Call for the Busan Global Hub City Special Law

On the 23rd, Busan Mayor Park Hyung-jun shaved his head to demand the prompt passage of the Busan Global Hub City Special Law. He emphasized that this bill is crucial for developing Busan into a global city like Singapore or Dubai and argued that practical changes must be pursued beyond political strife. With little time left in his term, Mayor Park warned that if this opportunity is missed, he would not be able to walk around Busan with his head held high. This incident symbolizes the conflict over Busan's future and political standing, rather than being merely a head shaving.

Key Summary

  • Mayor Park Hyung-jun shaves his head urging the swift passage of the Busan Global Hub City Special Law.
  • The bill includes regulatory and tax benefits aimed at developing Busan as a center for logistics, finance, and advanced industries.
  • Mayor Park criticized the delay in the National Assembly's Administrative and Interior Committee despite the absence of political strife and completion of government consultations.

Key Scenes

  • The Busan Global Hub City Special Law is seen as an important bill to enhance Busan's international status, expected to have a significant impact on the local economy.
  • The bill has been pending in the National Assembly for over two years since its proposal in May 2024, with calls for it to be processed regardless of political strife.
  • Local Busan lawmakers attended the head shaving ceremony, demonstrating political solidarity.

Why Background Matters

The Busan Global Hub City Special Law aims to develop Busan into an international logistics and financial hub, and Mayor Park Hyung-jun seeks to revitalize Busan's economy through it. However, the bill has been stalled in the National Assembly for nearly two years and has not passed due to political conflicts. Mayor Park criticizes this situation, emphasizing the need for it to be passed within his term. This can be viewed as a necessary measure for both Busan's development and his political survival.

Why It Matters

This incident reveals the complex interests surrounding Busan's future and political power. Mayor Park Hyung-jun has chosen an extreme method to strengthen his political standing, which could significantly impact the citizens of Busan. If the bill passes, Busan will have the opportunity to leap forward as an international city, but if it fails, citizens may experience increased disappointment. Therefore, this incident has become a crucial issue encompassing both Busan's politics and economy.
