President Yoon and First Lady Kim take first flight on new Air Force Two... to be operated as South Korea's dedicated aircraft for five years

Recently, President Lee Jae-myung and First Lady Kim Hye-kyung took their first flight on the new Air Force Two, marking the beginning of operations for the new dedicated aircraft. This flight represents the first journey of the new Air Force Two, which will be used as South Korea's dedicated aircraft for five years, replacing the existing Air Force Two that has been in service for 41 years. The new Air Force Two is notable for its enhanced performance, allowing for direct flights to major countries such as Indonesia and Uzbekistan.

This issue holds significance beyond the mere introduction of a new aircraft. We will examine how the new Air Force Two, as a symbol of the nation's diplomacy and security, will be utilized, along with the expectations and concerns that accompany it.
