The Cultural Vision of ‘Teacher Kim Gu’ and BTS

Recently, the cultural vision of Kim Gu has been highlighted again during performances by BTS (Bangtan Sonyeondan). Baekbeom Kim Gu emphasized the power of culture beyond mere national strength in his 1947 work ‘My Wish,’ which resonates with BTS's presentation of Korean culture on the global stage today. BTS is elevating the status of Korean culture through their music, showcasing how Kim Gu's thoughts and the cultural pride he envisioned are being reinterpreted in modern times.

This article will explore how BTS's recent performances connect with Kim Gu's vision, summarizing their significance and key points we should pay attention to.

Key Summary

  • BTS has mentioned Kim Gu's vision of a cultural powerhouse in their recent performances, promoting Korean culture to the world.
  • In ‘My Wish,’ Kim Gu emphasized the power of culture, proposing a cultural powerhouse instead of just a rich and strong nation.
  • BTS's music embodies the will to make Korean sentiment a global standard.
  • These performances symbolically show that Kim Gu's dream is approaching reality.

Key Scenes

  • The Power of Culture: Kim Gu emphasized that culture brings happiness and argued the importance of promoting Korea's cultural assets to the world.
  • BTS's Influence: BTS plays a significant role in globally disseminating Korean culture, and their music embodies Korean identity.
  • Geopolitical Context: It reminds us of the need for autonomy and identity establishment through the power of culture in a situation where Korea is surrounded by great powers.
  • 150th Anniversary: This year marks the 150th anniversary of Baekbeom Kim Gu's birth, providing an opportunity to reflect on his thoughts.

Why Background Matters

The re-examination of Kim Gu's vision during BTS's performances is not merely coincidental. Korea has made significant efforts to find its cultural identity after experiencing colonial rule and war, and within this historical context, Kim Gu dreamed of a cultural powerhouse. BTS is modernly realizing that dream, and their music and messages contribute to the globalization of Korean culture.

Why It Matters

This issue extends beyond just BTS's performances. Kim Gu's vision offers direction for Korean society's future and reminds us of the importance of culture having power. BTS's success contributes to the global recognition of Korean culture, which instills a sense of pride in all Koreans.

My Perspective

In my view, the phenomenon of Kim Gu's vision manifesting through BTS's music is not a mere coincidence but an inevitability. The connection between the two elements reveals Korea's identity and cultural strength, and we should seize this opportunity to further refine the future of Korean culture. The message conveyed by BTS transcends mere music, demonstrating how the power of culture can transform our lives.

Hidden Variables to Consider

While BTS's music is globally recognized, there are also cultural misunderstandings and limitations of acceptance that arise in the process. As Korean culture expands globally, we must be cautious not to lose our cultural identity. Additionally, BTS's success does not equate to the success of all Korean culture, and we must remember that diverse cultural expressions should also be recognized.
