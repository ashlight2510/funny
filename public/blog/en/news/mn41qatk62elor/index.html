Meloni's Judicial Reform Proposal Defeated in Referendum... Political Blow Inevitable

The judicial reform proposal pushed by the Italian government was recently defeated in a referendum. As a result, Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni is expected to face a political blow. The reform included complex contents such as the separation of job selection for judges and prosecutors and the personnel committee, with opposition reaching 54% and support only at 46%. While acknowledging the defeat, Prime Minister Meloni stated that she has no intention of resigning. Given that this voting result occurred despite high support for the Meloni government, it could lead to significant changes in the political landscape going forward.

Key Summary

  • Italy's judicial reform proposal was defeated in a referendum.
  • Prime Minister Meloni acknowledged the defeat but declared she would not resign.
  • The contents of the reform are complex and technical, highlighting its political significance.
  • Public dissatisfaction emerged despite the Meloni government's high approval ratings.
  • Difficulties in pushing government policies are expected ahead of next year's general elections.

Key Scenes

  • Although Prime Minister Meloni has no intention of resigning, her political standing may weaken.
  • Questions are raised about the performance of the right-wing coalition government in next year's general elections.

Why Background Matters

This judicial reform proposal is one of several policies pursued by the Meloni government, requiring constitutional amendments and containing technically complex content. In Italy, a referendum is perceived as a significant political blow to the proposing faction, with similar past instances. Prime Minister Meloni has built an 'invincible' image based on high approval ratings, but this defeat could fracture that image. The loss is particularly shocking given that the ruling party, 'Brothers of Italy,' has support exceeding 30%.

Why It Matters

The defeat of the judicial reform proposal is not merely a failure of a single policy. It is an event that significantly impacts the Meloni government's policy drive and political credibility, serving as an important indicator of the future direction of Italian politics. Additionally, it is likely to play a crucial role in predicting how voter reactions will change ahead of next year's general elections.

Points to Note

Prime Minister Meloni's statement that she will not resign indicates a determination to overcome the political crisis, but it may negatively affect other policies she has pursued. Additional efforts will be necessary to regain public trust.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The defeat of this judicial reform proposal is not limited to issues related to the judiciary. It is the result of accumulated political dissatisfaction and distrust within Italy, which could also affect other policies of the Meloni government. Furthermore, public reactions possess a complex social context that cannot be judged solely by simple opinion poll figures.

Current Checkpoints

It will be noteworthy to see what measures the Meloni government will implement moving forward and how voters will respond in next year's general elections. In particular, it is important to observe public reactions to other policies pursued by Meloni and the government's responses.
