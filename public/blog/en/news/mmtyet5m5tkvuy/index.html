Ulsan University Hospital Expects to Alleviate Medical Gap with Introduction of Robotic Surgery Equipment

Ulsan University Hospital has been selected for the 2026 Regional Cancer Center Equipment Cost Support Project and plans to introduce the robotic surgery equipment 'Da Vinci 5'. This selection was made in a competition involving 13 regional cancer centers across the country, and Ulsan University Hospital plans to invest a total of 3 billion won to introduce this equipment and establish a fund to support vulnerable cancer patients. This is expected to improve the quality of cancer treatment in the Ulsan area and contribute to alleviating the medical gap with the metropolitan area.

This project will play an important role in providing better medical services to cancer patients in the region, not just limited to equipment introduction. In particular, Ulsan University Hospital plans to recruit specialized personnel and utilize profits to create a fund for integrated cancer care, thereby contributing to the health promotion of the local community.

Key Summary

  • Ulsan University Hospital selected for the 2026 Regional Cancer Center Equipment Cost Support Project.
  • Introduction of robotic surgery equipment 'Da Vinci 5' planned, with a total investment of 3 billion won.
  • Plans to establish a fund to support vulnerable cancer patients.
  • Expectations to alleviate the medical gap with the metropolitan area.

Key Highlights

  • Equipment Introduction: 'Da Vinci 5' is a robotic surgery device with improved features compared to existing models.
  • Financial Support: A total budget of 3 billion won, including 1.2 billion won from the national government, 300 million won from the city, and 1.5 billion won from self-funding.
  • Recruitment of Specialized Personnel: Plans to secure additional specialized personnel for equipment operation.
  • Contribution to the Local Community: Establishing an integrated cancer care fund to support vulnerable groups and for research utilization.

Why It's Important to Consider the Background

This project selection can be seen as part of efforts to strengthen the cancer treatment infrastructure in the Ulsan region. Ulsan University Hospital has continuously worked to enhance its role as a regional cancer center and improve the quality of medical services. In particular, the issue of medical gaps with the metropolitan area has a significant impact on local residents, and various measures were needed to address this. The introduction of this equipment holds significant meaning in this context.

Why It Matters

This selection provides an opportunity to offer better medical services to the residents of Ulsan. The introduction of robotic surgery equipment will not only enhance the accuracy and safety of surgeries but also improve the efficiency of treatments. This is expected to have a positive impact on patients, especially for cancer patients, making it even more significant. Additionally, the establishment of a fund for vulnerable groups demonstrates the hospital's commitment to social responsibility.
