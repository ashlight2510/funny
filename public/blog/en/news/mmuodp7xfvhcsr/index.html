What is the significance of the Seoul Mayor's nomination application press conference?

On the 17th, Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon attended a press conference held in the Seoul City Hall briefing room to announce details related to his nomination application. During this event, he emphasized the importance of the nomination along with his policy vision for the future of Seoul, delivering a direct message to the citizens of Seoul. This press conference is expected to serve as an opportunity to re-examine the political environment of Seoul and Mayor Oh's leadership, going beyond a simple announcement of the nomination application.

This press conference marks a significant turning point for the political landscape of Seoul and Mayor Oh Se-hoon's future political endeavors. It is a matter that can directly affect the citizens of Seoul, drawing considerable political interest.
