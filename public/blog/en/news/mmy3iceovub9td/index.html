Trump’s Mention of Pearl Harbor Signals Potential Fallout in U.S.-Japan Relations

Recently, U.S. President Donald Trump caused embarrassment for Japan during a meeting with the Japanese Prime Minister by mentioning the Pearl Harbor attack. He explained that the reason for not notifying allies about the attack on Iran in advance was that he wanted a "surprise." He then made a pointed joke, asking, "Who knows surprises better than Japan?" This remark recalled painful memories from the past in the diplomatic relationship with Japan, leading to a reaction of discomfort from the Japanese side.

Trump's comments are a highly sensitive topic considering the long-standing alliance between Japan and the United States. There is a need to discuss the implications of bringing up past wounds on bilateral relations and the intent behind such remarks.
