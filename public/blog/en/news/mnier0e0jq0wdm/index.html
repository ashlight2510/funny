Shocking Sexual Exploitation Crime by Current Police Officer Revealed on SNS

Recently, a current police officer who engaged in sexual conversations with a minor and demanded sexual exploitation materials was arrested through an undercover investigation by the police. Officer A from the Incheon Michuhol Police Station had several inappropriate conversations with a minor, B, via SNS and designated a location for a real meeting, but was caught by the police. This incident violates the Act on the Protection of Children and Youth from Sexual Abuse, and the police have seized Officer A's mobile phone and are conducting a digital forensic investigation.
