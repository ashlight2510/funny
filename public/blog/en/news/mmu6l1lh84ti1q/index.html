Enjoy Forest Experiences and Making Terrariums and Habariums at Democracy Park

On November 4, Busan Democracy Park will host a program for forest experiences and making terrariums and habariums as part of the '2026 Democracy Park Cultural and Artistic Education Project'. This program is open to everyone, and particularly those aged 5 and above will have the opportunity to participate for free. Participants will be able to take home the terrarium or habarium they create, enhancing their understanding of plants through a creative experience.

This event is organized by the Busan Democracy Struggle Memorial Association, aiming to raise citizens' awareness of democracy based on the historical background of Democracy Park. Registration for participation is available on the Democracy Park website and will close on a first-come, first-served basis.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Busan Democracy Park is a space established to honor historical democratization movements such as the April 19 Revolution, the October 16 Busan-Masan Democratic Uprising, and the June 10 Democratic Uprising, where citizens can learn and experience the values of democracy. This program is designed to further highlight the historical significance of Democracy Park and provide citizens with opportunities to enhance their understanding of democracy through experiences in nature.

Why It Matters

This program offers more than just plant-related experiences; it provides citizens with a profound opportunity to understand the history and values of democracy. Particularly, it is meaningful for children and teenagers to feel a sense of democracy through nature, fostering a sense of social responsibility.
