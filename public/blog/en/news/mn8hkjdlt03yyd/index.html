The Significance of the Documentary Film <Ran 123> on Illegal Martial Law

The film <Ran 123> is set to be released on April 22. This work addresses the illegal martial law incident of December 3rd under the Yoon Suk-yeol administration, directed by Lee Myung-se. Unlike traditional documentaries, it excludes narration and interviews, aiming to provide a powerful experience to the audience through various visuals and sounds. The director aims to cinematically convey the strength of the Korean people's resistance and solidarity, structured in a way that allows the audience to intuitively understand the events.

The background and context of this film go beyond merely re-examining past events. It captures the voices of citizens affected by the ongoing political situation, raising awareness about the illegal exercise of power.

Key Summary

  • Film Overview: Directed by Lee Myung-se, <Ran 123> is a documentary about Yoon Suk-yeol's illegal martial law incident, composed of visuals and sounds without narration.
  • Goal: To allow the audience to intuitively understand the events and convey a cinematic tension that emphasizes the people's resistance.
  • Production Background: Based on materials provided by over 150 citizens, highlighting the importance of citizen participation.
  • Music: Music director Jo Sung-woo contributes emotional melodies to deepen the film's impact.
  • Crowdfunding: A total of 1 billion won was raised through the participation of over 15,000 citizens.

Why It Matters

This film transcends being a simple documentary; it is an important work that reflects the current political situation. By visually representing citizens' resistance to illegal power, it confronts the audience with the issues facing society today. Furthermore, the crowdfunding effort, with voluntary participation from citizens, emphasizes the importance of social solidarity and illustrates how deeply engaged citizens are with political issues.

Interpretation of the Film

In my view, the greatest appeal of <Ran 123> lies in its structure. The ability to convey a powerful message without narration or interviews is highly innovative. This approach allows the audience to feel the events more intuitively and accept the facts as they are. In fact, this method provides an opportunity for the audience to think more deeply and connect emotionally.

Hidden Variables to Consider

It is easy to overlook that the political context and citizens' resistance depicted in this film are not merely historical but remain relevant today. The events covered in the film are still valid in the current political environment, and if the audience fails to recognize this, they may miss the film's true message.
