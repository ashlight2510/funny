This incident involves a woman in her 30s, A, who was arrested by the police for causing the death of her 3-year-old daughter and disposing of her body. A is accused of abusing her daughter, C, who was 3 years old in February 2020, leading to her death, and then conspiring with her partner B to dispose of the body in a mountain area. The case came to light after the school reported C's unusual absence before she was set to start elementary school. A is believed to have concealed C's death for six years, and the police are investigating the specific methods and motives behind her crimes.
