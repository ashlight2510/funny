You at the Edge, a Watchman or an Explorer

This article explores how we should understand and utilize writing in the AI era. Through the AI usage experiences of various readers, it reminds us that writing is not just a technical act but an important process that embodies each person's identity and emotions. In particular, the effort to maintain one's voice and sensibility while receiving help from AI is emphasized. In this context, 'the edge of writing' presents the possibility of opening new avenues of expression beyond existing boundaries.

While the advancement of AI lowers the threshold for writing, we cannot overlook the ethical dilemmas and identity crises that arise as a result. Ultimately, we must ponder what 'writing' means in our respective positions and how we can safeguard its power.

Reasons to Consider the Background

In recent years, AI technology has rapidly advanced, leading to a variety of tools related to writing. In particular, generative AI provides convenience in writing for many people but can also act as a threat to personal identity. This article sheds light on the meaning of writing in the AI era and the conflicts we experience in that process, prompting us to consider how we can preserve our voices.

Why It Matters

While AI lowering the threshold for writing is positive, we cannot ignore the ethical issues and identity crises that arise as a result. It is important to protect one's unique voice and sensibility, which is also a process of reflecting on human existence and the meaning of life beyond mere technical approaches. Such considerations are essential for future writing education and social discourse.

Personal Comment

In my view, the advancement of AI is certainly changing the writing environment, but we must note that this change is not always positive. Many people risk losing the essence of writing in the process of refining sentences and receiving feedback through AI. In reality, if writing that captures personal experiences and emotions disappears, we will ultimately consume only dry sentences produced by machines. Therefore, we must continuously question and contemplate how to utilize AI as a tool while preserving our voices and sensibilities.

Hidden Variables to Consider Together

In the process of AI lowering the threshold for writing, the ethical aspects of writing and the preservation of personal identity may be neglected. Additionally, the loss of emotional elements due to AI's involvement could lead to a breakdown of social empathy and communication, which should not be overlooked.

Points to Check Now

  • Consider how you can maintain your voice and sensibility when using AI tools.
  • Review the parts of your recent writing where you received help from AI and how that changed your work.
  • Reflect once again on the importance of writing that captures personal experiences and emotions.
