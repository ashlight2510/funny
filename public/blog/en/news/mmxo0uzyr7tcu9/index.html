This achievement is expected to be an important turning point for Sonoh as they head towards 'spring basketball'.

Sonoh's growth is not just about the winning streak, but is the result of the overall improvement in team performance and the harmonious cooperation among players. Head coach Son Chang-hwan emphasized that the players are fulfilling their respective roles well and mentioned the pressure they will face moving forward. As a result, Sonoh is narrowing the gap with the 4th place team, increasing their chances of making the playoffs.
