Perfect Comparison of Hotels by Region in Japan: Where to Stay?

One of the biggest concerns when preparing for a trip is choosing accommodation. There are often moments when you feel lost about where to stay, right? Japan, with its attractive hotels in regions like Tokyo, Osaka, and Kyoto, makes it hard to choose. So, I’ve compiled a list of recommended hotels by region. I hope this article helps you make a great choice just like I did!

Tokyo: Modern Sensibility and Convenience

Hotels in the Shinjuku Area

Shinjuku is the heart of Tokyo and a paradise for shopping and dining. The 'Hyatt Regency Tokyo' where I stayed had a great location and excellent service. The night view from the hotel was absolutely stunning. However, the price can be quite high, so you need to consider your budget.

The Vibe of Shibuya

Shibuya is a youthful and vibrant area. The 'Shibuya Grand Hotel' is located right near the Shibuya Scramble Crossing with modern interiors. It’s great for walking around at night, and there are many restaurants and cafes that make you want to visit often.

Osaka: The City of Taste and Fun

The Charm of Namba

Namba in Osaka is the perfect place for a food tour. The 'Hotel Granvia Osaka' is conveniently connected to the train station. The breakfast buffet is especially delicious! I loved being able to enjoy a variety of traditional Japanese dishes all at once.

Comfort in Umeda

The 'Hilton Osaka' located in Umeda is also suitable for business travelers. The rooms are spacious and quiet, allowing for a comfortable rest. There are many shopping malls nearby, making it a nice place for a leisurely stroll sometimes.

Kyoto: A Place to Experience Traditional Charm

Traditional Hotels in Gion

If you want a traditional atmosphere in Kyoto, choosing a 'ryokan' is a great option. 'Ryokan Inari' is a traditional Japanese inn where you can enjoy hot springs in your room. I stayed there and felt like a samurai from Japan. However, reservations can be difficult, so planning ahead is important!

Nature in Arashiyama

The 'Arashiyama Ryokan' near Arashiyama is a place where you can relax in nature. The quiet and peaceful atmosphere was wonderful. Waking up in the morning and taking a walk felt refreshing.

Conclusion: The Beginning of Choosing the Perfect Accommodation

I’ve compiled a list of recommended hotels by region in Japan. I hope you get to experience the modern sensibility of Tokyo, the delicious food of Osaka, and the traditional charm of Kyoto. I hope my experiences help you! If you have any questions, please leave a comment. This is a great place to start!
