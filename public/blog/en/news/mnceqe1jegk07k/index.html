A bowl of 'blessed' soup made from a carefully boiled pig's head

Recently, a story related to pig's head is introduced in EBS1's program with the theme of 'Special Cuts Road'. In Andong, Gyeongbuk, pork soup made from pig's head has established itself as a traditional local dish, carrying cultural significance beyond mere cooking, as it is associated with wishing for blessings. Ms. Jeong Young-sook has been running a pork soup restaurant in the area for 45 years, continuing the local food culture by making pig's head slices on festive days.

This broadcast highlights the traditional practice of wishing for blessings through pork soup made from boiled pig's head, exploring how food connects to the joy of the community. In particular, in the Andong region, influenced by Confucian thought, pig's head is used as an important offering, demonstrating how food functions as a cultural symbol beyond mere nutritional supply.
