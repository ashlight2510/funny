Ukraine Sends Drone Experts to the Middle East: The Military Strategy Behind It

Ukraine has decided to send drone defense experts to the Middle East. This move is a response to the threat of Iranian drone attacks and appears to be a strategy to secure military support from allied nations by utilizing Ukraine's drone technology. President Volodymyr Zelensky announced that experts to support defense capabilities against Iranian drones would be dispatched to several countries, including the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia. Alongside this, Ukraine is also planning to expand its drone supply by establishing a manufacturing partnership with the UK.

This news reflects a complex strategy not just for military cooperation but also to strengthen Ukraine's international standing and gain support from allies. Particularly, amid military tensions in the Middle East, Ukraine seems to be aiming to build new alliances through its drone technology.
