As a result, Suwon FC Women will face North Korea's My Home Sports Team in the semifinals, which is scheduled for May 20. This is Suwon FC Women’s first participation in the tournament, and they made a miraculous advancement to the quarterfinals by finishing third in the group stage.

This achievement comes as a result of Suwon FC Women’s qualification as the WK League champions last season, marking an important milestone that showcases their growth and potential.
