BTS Gwanghwamun Concert D-4···Nervousness, Excitement, and Complaints Intersecting at Gwanghwamun Square

As BTS's comeback concert approaches in four days, Gwanghwamun Square in Seoul is becoming a site of mixed emotions. While fans and tourists from around the world are excitedly waiting for BTS's return, concerns about accidents due to the crowd and complaints about traffic control are also being raised. In particular, with hundreds of thousands expected to gather on the day of the concert, worries about safety and convenience are increasing.

This situation is not just about BTS's concert; it serves as an important case showing social reactions and preparations for large-scale events. It is a complex scenario involving people wanting to enjoy the concert, citizens experiencing inconveniences, and officials responsible for safety.

Particularly, safety incidents and traffic control in places where fans gather can have direct effects on citizens.
