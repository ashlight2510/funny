Myongji University awards honorary doctorate to Chairman Wei Wei

Myongji University has awarded an honorary Doctor of Public Administration degree to Chairman Wei Wei of the Eastern Cambridge Education Group. The event took place at the Bangmok Academic Information Center on the Humanities Campus, and Chairman Wei is known for his dedication to the education sector for over 20 years, having established an integrated education system that encompasses early childhood education to higher education. He also serves as an honorary director and special professor at Peking University, contributing to the practice of educational publicness and academic development.

This honorary doctorate award goes beyond mere symbolism; it serves as an opportunity to emphasize the importance of global leadership and collaboration in the education sector. During the ceremony, Chairman Wei conveyed a message that "investment in human growth" is the most valuable thing, reminding everyone of the essence of education.
