The Seriousness of Youth Unemployment and the Need for Startup Support

Recently, the youth unemployment rate has reached a serious level. The economic shock caused by COVID-19 continues, and the job market is in an increasingly difficult situation. According to the recently released employment trends, the number of employed youth decreased by 146,000, and the youth unemployment rate hit 7.7%, the highest since 2021. In this situation, the government is trying to address the unemployment issue by discovering young entrepreneurs and fostering small and medium-sized enterprises, but it remains uncertain whether these efforts will yield immediate results.

This issue is not just a statistical figure; it brings frustration and anxiety to young people and economic burdens to families. Youth unemployment is also linked to the low birth rate issue, and the government must prepare extraordinary measures to address this problem.

Key Points

  • Youth unemployment rate at 7.7%, the highest since 2021.
  • Employment among older individuals has increased, while youth employment has decreased.
  • Over 100,000 jobs in professional, scientific, and technical services have been lost.
  • Despite an increase in hiring of new employees by large corporations, they make up only 10% of total employment.
  • The government announced plans to create jobs through youth entrepreneurship and the nurturing of small and medium-sized enterprises.

Why We Need to Look at the Background

The current youth unemployment issue has arisen amid a slow economic recovery following the COVID-19 pandemic. External factors such as U.S. tariffs and rising oil prices from the Middle East are negatively impacting the job market. While the number of older employed individuals is increasing, the employment of young people is declining, leading to a polarization phenomenon. This trend is particularly pronounced in manufacturing and information communication sectors, with advancements in AI technology negatively affecting new hiring.

Why It Matters

The youth unemployment issue goes beyond mere economic concerns; it is associated with social instability and low birth rates. If young people cannot find stable jobs, they will delay marriage and childbirth, which will negatively impact the overall demographic structure of society. Therefore, addressing the youth unemployment issue is an urgent matter for the future of the nation.

Points to Note

The startup support and nurturing of small and medium-sized enterprises emphasized in this article are presented as important alternatives to alleviate youth unemployment. However, it remains questionable how effectively the government's plans will be implemented. Particularly, in a reality where large corporations primarily hire experienced workers, the key issue is how many jobs can be created that young people can easily access.

Personal Perspective

In my view, the youth unemployment issue is not merely a lack of jobs but stems from a gap between the jobs young people want and those that are actually available. The government's proposed startup support policies are positive, but there is a need to create an ecosystem that enables young people to take on entrepreneurial challenges. Additionally, unless the hiring practices of large corporations change, the youth unemployment issue will not be easily resolved.

Hidden Variables to Consider

As mentioned in the article, the advancement of AI and automation technologies is significantly impacting the reduction of jobs for young people. This is not just about technological advancement but is also related to changes in industrial structure. Therefore, policies that support young people in acquiring new skills and adapting to the changing market must be implemented concurrently.

Current Points to Check

  • Specific details and execution plans of the government's youth startup support policies.
  • The impact of AI and automation technologies on youth employment and strategies to respond to this.
  • The current status of programs for vocational education and skill acquisition for young people.

FAQ

Q: What are the reasons for the high youth unemployment rate?
A: Economic factors, changes in industrial structure, and the advancement of AI technology are all contributing factors.

Q: What does the government's startup support policy entail?
A: The government plans to discover 1,000 young entrepreneurs and nurture over 6,000 promising small and venture businesses.

Q: What support do young people need to start a business?
A: Various supports such as funding, mentoring, and educational programs are necessary.

Q: What role should companies play in solving the youth unemployment issue?
A: Companies should increase hiring of new employees and provide opportunities for young people to gain practical experience.

Finally, it is crucial to examine the specific execution plans of the youth startup support policies right now. This will help determine what tangible benefits young people can receive.
