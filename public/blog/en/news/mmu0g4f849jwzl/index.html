Crisis, climax, and conclusion contained in ‘1 minute’... Can ‘short dramas’ that even Chungmuro directors have jumped into take root in Korea?

Recently, short dramas have been rapidly growing in the Korean content market. These short dramas, containing crisis, climax, and conclusion within a minute or so, possess a different charm from existing OTT platforms and are particularly attracting the interest of the younger generation. Now, famous directors and actors are also entering this market, and short dramas are establishing themselves as a new trend beyond mere B-grade content.

This article provides an in-depth analysis of the background of the growth of short dramas, the resulting changes in the industry, and their future potential. Short dramas are not just short content; they are significantly influencing content consumption patterns through efficient production methods and profitability.

Key Summary

  • Short dramas are short formats of around 1 minute, attracting the interest of the younger generation with their rapid development and provocative themes.
  • The domestic short drama market is expected to grow to approximately 650 billion won by 2024.
  • The participation of famous directors and actors is enhancing the quality of content.
  • Cost reductions through the use of AI technology are further increasing the profitability of short dramas.
  • However, there are concerns about sustainable growth, and uncertainties in the market also exist.

Key Scenes

  • Attraction of Short Format: Short dramas of around 1 minute capture consumer interest through rapid development and urgency.
  • Growing Market: The domestic short drama market is rapidly growing and is expected to reach about 650 billion won by 2024.
  • Participation of Famous Personalities: The involvement of famous directors and actors is increasing the quality and credibility of short dramas.
  • Introduction of AI Technology: Cost reductions through AI are enhancing the profitability of short dramas, allowing for the production of more content.
  • Concerns about Sustainability: The growth of the short drama market may be temporary, and a sustainable model is needed.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Short dramas have emerged in line with changes in content consumption patterns over the past few years. In particular, as short-form content has gained popularity on platforms like YouTube and Instagram, users have become accustomed to short and provocative content. In this flow, short dramas are breaking away from the traditional drama framework and creating new consumption patterns.

Additionally, a survey by the Korea Creative Content Agency shows that 58.6% of respondents use short-form content, proving sufficient demand. It is also noteworthy that domestic production companies are seeking to enter overseas platforms.

Why It Matters

The emergence of short dramas is changing the paradigm of the content market. Short dramas, which can deliver intense messages in a short time, are likely to become optimized content for busy modern individuals. Furthermore, as they require less production time and cost, there are opportunities for producing more content. This creates an environment that can benefit both content creators and consumers.

Points to Note

More importantly, the sustainability of the short drama market is crucial. While the participation of famous directors and actors is positive, it remains uncertain whether they will continue to engage in short dramas. In situations where fees and production costs are insufficient, it may be challenging for them to continue participating.

Hidden Variables to Consider

The success of short dramas may be temporary. Consumer interest can change depending on market conditions, and it is uncertain how long the short drama format will last. Additionally, while the introduction of AI technology brings positive results, there is also a risk that the quality of content may decline.

Current Points to Check

It is advisable to pay attention to the growth trend of the current short drama market and to experience the content offered on related platforms firsthand. Furthermore, it is important to continuously monitor how short dramas will develop in the future.

FAQ

  • Q: Where can I watch short dramas?
    A: Various platforms such as Lezhin Snack and Beagle offer short dramas.

  • Q: What is the average production cost of a short drama?
    A: The production cost of a short drama ranges from about 150 million to 200 million won.

  • Q: How is AI technology utilized in short dramas?
    A: AI technology is used for special effects and background compositing, significantly lowering production costs.

  • Q: Will the popularity of short dramas continue?
    A: Currently, there are positive signals, but sustainability is uncertain due to changes in consumer preferences.

Short dramas are influencing content consumption patterns beyond being a mere trend. Why not experience short dramas now and join in on this wave of change?
