5 Super Easy Air Fryer Side Dishes Made with Simple Ingredients

Introducing air fryer side dishes that can be easily made even in a busy daily life. The air fryer is an optimal tool for making healthy home-cooked meals, as it minimizes cooking time and oil usage. Now, let's create delicious side dishes with simple ingredients.

Advantages and Uses of the Air Fryer

The air fryer cooks food by circulating hot air, providing a crispy texture while using less oil. It is loved by many households for its ability to quickly prepare meals in a busy lifestyle.

Key Advantages of the Air Fryer

  • Reduced Oil Usage: You can maintain a crispy texture while using minimal oil.
  • Shortened Cooking Time: Compared to traditional cooking methods, it has a shorter cooking time, making it suitable for busy lives.

Checkpoint: The temperature and time settings of air fryers may vary by device, so it's important to adjust the appropriate temperature and time according to the recipe.

5 Super Easy Air Fryer Side Dishes

1. Crispy Air Fryer French Fries

Make crispy French fries with simple ingredients. Potatoes are easily accessible and can become a delicious side dish with just a little preparation.

  • Cooking Method: Peel the potatoes and cut them to your desired thickness. Toss them with olive oil and salt, then place them in the air fryer and cook at 180 degrees for 15 minutes.
  • Caution: Cooking time may vary depending on the thickness of the potatoes, so if cut thick, you may need to increase the time slightly.

2. Nutrient-Packed Air Fryer Broccoli

Easily cook broccoli, which is rich in vitamins and minerals.

  • Cooking Method: Wash the broccoli and cut it into appropriate sizes, then season with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Cook at 200 degrees for 10 minutes.
  • Caution: Be careful not to overcook, as nutrients can be destroyed. Flip it once during cooking for more even cooking.

3. High-Protein Air Fryer Tofu Steak

Make a nutritious side dish easily with tofu, which is great for protein supplementation.

  • Cooking Method: Cut the tofu into thick slices and season with soy sauce, garlic, and pepper. Cook at 180 degrees for 15 minutes.
  • Caution: It is essential to remove moisture from the tofu. Pat it dry with a kitchen towel before seasoning for better flavor.

4. Easy Air Fryer Squid

Create a delicious seafood side dish without special ingredients.

  • Cooking Method: Clean the squid thoroughly, then season with salt, pepper, and olive oil. Cook at 200 degrees for 10 minutes.
  • Caution: Be careful not to exceed 10 minutes of cooking time, as it can become tough.

5. Low-Calorie Air Fryer Cabbage Steak

Easily prepare cabbage, which is great for a diet-friendly side dish.

  • Cooking Method: Cut the cabbage into thick slices and season with olive oil and salt. Cook at 200 degrees for 12 minutes.
  • Caution: Cooking time may vary depending on the thickness of the cabbage, so it's important to cut it to an appropriate thickness.

Air Fryer Side Dish Preparation Checklist

  • Required Ingredients:

    • Potatoes, broccoli, tofu, squid, cabbage
    • Olive oil, salt, pepper, soy sauce, garlic

  • Cooking Tools:

    • Air fryer
    • Kitchen towel
    • Mixing bowl

  • Summary of Cooking Time and Temperature for Each Side Dish:

Side Dish Temperature (°C) Cooking Time (minutes)
French Fries 180 15
Broccoli 200 10
Tofu Steak 180 15
Squid 200 10
Cabbage Steak 200 12

Tips for Making Air Fryer Side Dishes

Preparing all ingredients and seasonings in advance will make the cooking process much smoother. Store any leftover ingredients in the refrigerator for use in the next meal. Additionally, it is essential to clean the air fryer after cooking to maintain hygiene.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. What ingredients can I cook in an air fryer?

    • You can cook a variety of ingredients in an air fryer, including potatoes, vegetables, tofu, and seafood.

  2. How do I set the temperature on the air fryer?

    • You should set the appropriate temperature according to each recipe, generally between 180 and 200 degrees.

  3. Can I prepare air fryer side dishes in advance and store them?

    • Yes, you can store them in the refrigerator after cooking, and they will stay fresh for 2-3 days. Reheating them in the air fryer will help maintain their crispy texture.
