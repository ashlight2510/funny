Police Investigating 'Private Revenge' Crimes and Jongmyo Tea Meeting

Recently, the police have arrested 40 individuals nationwide for so-called 'private revenge' crimes that exploit delivery applications. Simultaneously, the investigation into the 'Jongmyo Tea Meeting' incident involving First Lady Kim Geon-hee is ongoing, with former secretary Shin Soo-jin being investigated as a suspect. This case addresses significant issues regarding personal information protection and crime investigation, and it is expected to have a substantial impact across society.

This incident goes beyond a simple criminal investigation, raising serious awareness about public safety and the protection of personal information. According to police findings, criminals disguised themselves as customer service representatives of a delivery app to extract personal information and used it for their crimes. This method highlights how easily personal information can be misused in modern society.

Key Points

  • Police arrest 40 for private revenge crimes, with 53 cases reported.
  • Criminals disguised as delivery app customer service representatives to steal personal information.
  • Investigation ongoing into First Lady Kim Geon-hee's 'Jongmyo Tea Meeting,' with former secretary Shin Soo-jin being questioned.
  • Cases involving intermediaries are being investigated together, while other cases are being handled by respective city and provincial police agencies.
  • Emphasis on the importance of personal information protection and crime prevention.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This private revenge crime reflects the increasing interpersonal conflicts and dissatisfaction in society that are leading to criminal acts. Particularly, with the proliferation of delivery applications, an environment has been created where individuals' addresses and personal information can be easily exposed, providing criminals with opportunities to exploit this information. The police's proactive investigation can be seen as a first step toward addressing these issues.

Additionally, the 'Jongmyo Tea Meeting' incident illustrates how unofficial meetings among public officials can spark social controversy. The revelation that First Lady Kim Geon-hee held informal tea meetings with external figures has led to growing suspicions and controversies surrounding the matter. This could lead to issues of political trust and foster public distrust.

This incident is emerging as a crucial issue not only in terms of criminal investigation but also in building personal information protection and social trust.
