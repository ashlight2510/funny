Israeli Army: "Missiles from Yemen are flying in"···Will the Houthi rebels join the fight?

Recently, the Israeli army announced that the Houthi rebels, an Iran-aligned armed group in Yemen, had launched missiles towards Israel. Since the outbreak of the Iran war, Hezbollah in Lebanon and Shiite militias in Iraq have participated on Iran's side, but the Houthi rebels had not intervened until now. However, if the Houthi rebels join the fight in earnest, it is expected that military tensions in the Middle East will escalate further as their alliance with Iran strengthens.

This incident occurred amid Iran's blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, and the possibility of Houthi rebel participation could lead to a blockade of the Red Sea shipping routes. This situation could severely impact international shipping and energy transport. Notably, the leader of the Houthi rebels, Abdul Malik al-Houthi, has hinted at military responses, making future developments even more noteworthy.

Why Background Matters

As tensions between Israel and Iran escalate, the participation of the Houthi rebels could deepen military instability in the Middle East. The Houthi rebels have received support from Iran but have avoided direct military involvement until now. However, in strategic situations like Iran's blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, the Houthi rebels' participation could introduce new variables. Particularly, if the Red Sea shipping routes controlled by the Houthi rebels are blocked, it could severely impact international shipping and energy supply chains.

Why It Matters

This incident could have significant implications not just for military clashes between Israel and the Houthi rebels, but also for security and economic conditions in the Middle East. Instability in maritime logistics could have ripple effects on the global economy, making the international community's response crucial. Especially if the alliance with Iran strengthens, military tensions in the Middle East are expected to rise further.

Points to Watch

In my view, the participation of the Houthi rebels carries implications beyond mere military conflict. Strengthening military alliances with Iran could change the power dynamics in the Middle East. Particularly, military activities in the regions controlled by the Houthi rebels could provide opportunities for Iran to expand its influence. Iran has already strengthened its military position in the Middle East through its influence in Lebanon and Iraq, and the participation of the Houthi rebels could accelerate this process.

Hidden Variables to Consider

A key point to watch in this incident is how the military responses from the Houthi rebels could affect the future developments of the Iran war. If the Houthi rebels strengthen their military strategies through their experiences in the Yemeni civil war and their alliance with Iran, military tensions in the Middle East will likely intensify. Additionally, there is a possibility that the actions of the Houthi rebels could be adjusted according to Iran's strategic goals.
