Seogyo Public Union Urges Suspension of Oh Se-hoon's "Last-Minute Audit Appointments"

The first union of the Seoul Transportation Corporation, the KCTU Seoul Transportation Corporation Union, has strongly opposed the appointment of a permanent auditor being pushed by Mayor Oh Se-hoon and demanded its suspension. At a press conference held in front of Seoul City Hall on the 17th, the union declared, "We will mobilize all means to prevent the attempt at last-minute appointments until it is stopped," criticizing Mayor Oh for pushing through the auditor appointments ahead of the elections. They argue that this appointment is intended to cover up management failures and emphasize the need for transparent management and safety restructuring.

Key Summary

  • The Seoul Transportation Corporation Union held a press conference opposing Mayor Oh Se-hoon's push for the appointment of a permanent auditor.
  • The union considers the attempt to expedite the auditor appointment at the temporary board meeting as "last-minute appointments."
  • They demand management transparency alongside criticism of Mayor Oh's past appointment methods.

Key Scenes

  • Criticism that the auditor appointment being pushed by Mayor Oh Se-hoon is a "last-minute appointment" considering the upcoming elections.
  • The union's strong expression of intent to resist, stating they will mobilize all means.
  • The auditor's term is three years, and the appointment is linked to the upcoming elections.
  • Claims that allegations related to the corporation's management failures have influenced this appointment.
  • The second union, the Korean Federation of Trade Unions (KFTU) Seoul Transportation Corporation Unified Union, is also expected to join the resistance.

Why the Background Matters

This situation is noteworthy because Mayor Oh Se-hoon's attempt to appoint a permanent auditor at the Seoul Transportation Corporation comes at a time just before the elections. After the previous auditor resigned for political reasons, the corporation is quickly trying to appoint a new auditor, which is perceived by the union as a "last-minute" appointment. The union warns that such appointments could further exacerbate the corporation's management crisis, which could significantly impact the relationship between the Seoul City and the corporation.

Why It Matters

This issue goes beyond a simple appointment matter; it is a significant test of the management transparency and fairness of the Seoul Transportation Corporation. Mayor Oh's appointment methods could influence other institutions within the Seoul City in the future, directly affecting the credibility of public institutions. Furthermore, making such appointments ahead of the elections is likely to provoke political controversy, garnering significant public attention.
