Thorough Inspection for BTS Gwanghwamun Concert Venue Preparedness Against Terrorism

Recently, ahead of BTS's comeback performance, the Acting Police Chief inspected the Gwanghwamun concert site. This inspection was conducted to ensure thorough preparedness for large crowds and terrorism threats, with the police deploying 6,700 personnel and implementing various safety management measures. In particular, considering the unstable situation in the Middle East, they emphasized the strengthening of counter-terrorism measures and the establishment of triple barricades around the concert venue to prioritize safety.

These preparations are not just about the safety of the concert; they are crucial issues that can affect South Korea's image and international credibility. Since the concert will be broadcast to 190 countries worldwide, even a minor incident could have significant repercussions.
