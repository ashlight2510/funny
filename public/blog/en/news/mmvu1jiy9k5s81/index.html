Introduction of KOSDAQ League System and Shortening of T+1 Settlement System: An Opportunity for Reform Amid Crisis

Recently, during a meeting chaired by President Yoon Suk-yeol on stabilizing and normalizing the capital market, an announcement was made regarding the introduction of a league system for the KOSDAQ market and the shortening of the stock payment settlement system to T+1. This is interpreted as a series of reform measures aimed at enhancing the competitiveness of the KOSDAQ market and strengthening investor protection. In particular, President Yoon emphasized the necessity of reform in crisis situations and demonstrated a strong commitment to restoring trust in the financial market.

This reform includes the separation of the KOSDAQ market into 1st and 2nd leagues, prohibition of dual listings, and the introduction of the T+1 settlement system. Such changes are expected to enhance market dynamism and strengthen investor protection by providing opportunities for companies to move between leagues according to their growth stages.

Key Summary

  • Introduction of KOSDAQ League System: The KOSDAQ market will be divided into 1st and 2nd leagues, allowing companies to move between leagues based on their growth stages.
  • T+1 Settlement System: The stock payment settlement period will be shortened to one day after the transaction, increasing convenience for investors.
  • Prohibition of Dual Listings: A general prohibition on dual listings to protect ordinary shareholders.
  • Restoration of Trust in Financial Markets: Emphasis on the need for reform in crisis situations and a declaration of intent to eradicate stock price manipulation.
  • Market Reaction: Following the announcement at the meeting, the KOSPI index surged, reflecting positive reactions from investors.

Why Background Matters

This announcement can be seen as part of efforts to address structural issues in the Korean capital market. The Korean capital market has long been viewed through the lens of the 'Korea Discount,' leading to negative perceptions among foreign investors. Therefore, the introduction of the league system and the shortening of the settlement system are interpreted as strategies to resolve these issues and restore trust in the Korean market.

Additionally, as the trend of adopting T+1 systems spreads in global financial markets, Korea has shown a strong intention to align itself with this trend. The United States has already implemented the T+1 system, and Europe plans to do so starting in 2027.

Why It Matters

This reform is not merely about changing market rules; it will play a crucial role in enhancing the credibility of the Korean capital market and improving the investment environment. In particular, it is expected to have a positive impact on all market participants as it considers both the protection of ordinary investors' rights and the growth potential of companies.

Personal Perspective

If this reform is successfully implemented, the Korean capital market is likely to transform into a more transparent and fair environment. In particular, the prohibition of dual listings will clarify corporate values and contribute to the protection of ordinary shareholders. However, it remains to be seen how these changes will be realized in practice. Continuous monitoring and policy implementation by the government are necessary to ensure that the reforms do not remain mere declarations.

Hidden Variables to Consider

One aspect that may be overlooked in this announcement is the actual impact of the league system and the T+1 settlement system on the market. In particular, discussions are needed on how companies can grow through league movement and the potential issues that may arise from the T+1 system. Furthermore, a long-term vision is necessary to ensure that the effects of the reform do not result in only short-term stock price increases.

Points to Check Now

  • T+1 System Implementation Schedule: The specific roadmap and preparations for the T+1 system, which is expected to be implemented from 2027.
  • Specific Criteria for League System Introduction: Details on the criteria for the 1st and 2nd leagues and how companies can move between them.
  • Measures to Eradicate Stock Price Manipulation: The government's specific plans and enforcement strategies regarding stock price manipulation.

FAQ

A: With the introduction of the T+1 system, payments for stock transactions will be settled in one day, increasing the turnover rate of investors' funds and improving liquidity.

Q: How will companies change with the introduction of the league system?
A: Companies will be able to move between the 1st and 2nd leagues based on their growth stages, enhancing their competitiveness and providing more options for investors.

Q: What impact will the prohibition of dual listings have on ordinary investors?
A: The prohibition of dual listings will clarify corporate values and create an environment where ordinary shareholders can protect their rights.
