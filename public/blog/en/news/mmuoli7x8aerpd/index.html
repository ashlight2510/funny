Trump's Request to Postpone Visit to China... Possibility of 'Diplomatic Advantage' Amid Increased 'Uncertainty' for the U.S.

U.S. President Donald Trump's request to postpone his visit to China has amplified uncertainty in the summit between the U.S. and China. The postponement, cited due to the war with Iran, significantly impacts the diplomatic relations between the two countries, increasing the likelihood that China may seize an opportunity to gain a diplomatic advantage. Although the Chinese Foreign Ministry has not issued an official response to the request for postponement, attention is focused on how China will respond amid increasing U.S. pressure.

This issue goes beyond a simple diplomatic postponement, having significant implications for the complex relationship between the U.S. and China and the global situation. In particular, as tensions with Iran escalate, China appears to be gaining an opportunity to strengthen its negotiating power while avoiding U.S. pressure.
