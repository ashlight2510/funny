Propofol Theft Incident in Hospital: The Nurse Aide's Crime

Recently, a nurse's aide in Gwangju was involved in an incident where she stole and administered propofol at the hospital where she worked. Propofol is classified as a psychotropic drug used as a sedative, and it poses a significant risk of abuse, potentially leading to social controversy. The nurse's aide, identified as A in her 40s, was caught by the police for stealing propofol from the hospital refrigerator and is currently under investigation. She has admitted to the crime, and the police are conducting further investigations to ascertain the frequency and circumstances of the drug administration.

This incident reveals serious issues beyond a simple crime, highlighting concerns about the reliability of the medical field and the drug management system. It is crucial to examine how such an event could occur and who may be affected by it.
