5 Smart Ways to Save on Air Conditioning Electricity Bills in Summer

When summer arrives, air conditioning becomes essential. However, seeing the electricity bill that arrives every month can be quite painful. Have you ever wondered, "How can I save on my electricity bill?" I’ve compiled some smart ways to save on air conditioning electricity bills during the summer. These methods have helped me a lot!

1. Set the Air Conditioner Temperature

Maintain an Appropriate Temperature

Setting the air conditioner temperature too low can significantly increase your electricity bill. It is generally said that 24-26 degrees Celsius is appropriate, and from my experience, setting it at 26 degrees keeps it cool while saving a lot on electricity. When it’s really hot, using a fan along with the air conditioner is also a good idea.

Save on Electricity by Adjusting the Temperature

It is said that raising the temperature by just 1 degree can save about 7% on your electricity bill, so it’s important not to set it unnecessarily low but to adjust it appropriately.

2. Clean the Air Conditioner Filter

Increase Efficiency with Filter Maintenance

If the air conditioner filter is dirty, air circulation is hindered, leading to higher power consumption. Therefore, it’s good to clean it regularly. From my experience, keeping the filter clean makes the air feel fresher and cooler.

Cleaning Method

You can simply wash the filter with water, and it’s good to check it about once a week. Improving air conditioner efficiency through filter cleaning is fundamental!

3. Block Direct Sunlight

Control Indoor Temperature

Before turning on the air conditioner, closing windows or curtains can help lower the indoor temperature. Especially during the afternoon when strong sunlight comes in, keeping the curtains drawn can reduce the burden on the air conditioner. Since I started doing this, I’ve definitely felt that the indoor space stays cooler.

Utilize Installation Items

Using window tinting or thermal-blocking curtains is also a good method. This way, the air conditioner doesn’t have to work as hard, and you can save on electricity bills.

4. Use a Smart Timer

Add Convenience with Automatic Adjustment

If your air conditioner has a timer function, be sure to use it. By setting the timer before going out, you can ensure it’s cool by the time you return. This way, you don’t have to keep it running unnecessarily.

Utilize Reservation Features

Many air conditioners can be controlled via smartphones, allowing you to adjust them conveniently through an app at any time. Thanks to these features, I’ve been able to save significantly on my electricity bills.

5. Check Energy Efficiency Ratings

Consider When Choosing Products

It’s important to check the energy efficiency rating when purchasing an air conditioner. The air conditioner I recently bought has a 1st-grade rating, and it has definitely reduced my electricity bills.

Long-Term Investment

Even though the initial investment may be a bit higher, the long-term savings are substantial, so be sure to consider efficiency when replacing your air conditioner.

There are many ways to save on electricity bills by using your air conditioner wisely in the summer. I hope you find the methods I introduced helpful. If you have any questions, please leave a comment!
