Top 5 Regional Dishes to Try in Japan

March 15, 2026

Top 5 Regional Foods You Must Try While Traveling in Japan

If you're planning a trip to Japan and feeling overwhelmed about what to eat, you're not alone. Japan is filled with unique foods that vary by region. So, I've compiled a list of regional dishes that I truly enjoyed during my travels in Japan. I hope you get to taste them at least once!

1. Takoyaki from Osaka

Takoyaki, a Bite of Happiness

One of the must-try foods in Osaka is definitely takoyaki. The crispy exterior and soft interior create a fantastic harmony. When I first tried takoyaki, the flavor was absolutely amazing. Especially, the combination of the sauce and mayonnaise is exquisite.

Enjoying it on the Streets

You can taste it at various shops, and watching it being grilled on the street is also fun. The shop I visited had a long line, but it was worth the wait. When the takoyaki came out hot, the thrill of taking that first bite is something I still can't forget.

2. Sushi from Tokyo

Freshness You Can Feel in Sushi

When you go to Tokyo, sushi is a must! Sushi made with fresh ingredients is truly an art. The sushi place I went to was an omakase style, and enjoying the sushi recommended by the chef was really special.

Challenge Yourself with Various Types

From fresh tuna to salmon and eel! At first, I was unsure about what to eat, but after trying everything the chef recommended, I was amazed at how delicious it was. Personally, the eel sushi left the biggest impression on me. The soft texture and sweet sauce paired perfectly.

3. Yuba from Kyoto

Yuba, a Taste of Special Texture

You must try yuba when you visit Kyoto! Yuba is a thin film that comes from the process of making tofu, and its nutty yet soft flavor is truly captivating. I was surprised by its unique texture when I first tasted it.

Enjoy it in Various Dishes

You can taste various dishes made with yuba, and personally, yuba rice bowl was the best. Its mild flavor makes it really delicious when eaten with rice. Eating yuba in a quiet atmosphere during my trip was truly healing.

4. Ramen from Fukuoka

Rich Broth is the Charm of Ramen

You can't miss tonkotsu ramen when you go to Fukuoka! The rich and savory broth is exceptional. The shop I visited had a long line, but it was worth the wait.

The Harmony of Soft Noodles

The noodles are chewy, and the broth is rich, making you feel happy with each bite. Personally, adding garlic made it even more delicious. I was able to fully experience the charm of Fukuoka with just one bowl of ramen.

Conclusion

I've compiled a list of regional foods you must try on your trip to Japan. There are many dishes that allow you to experience the uniqueness of each region, so it would be great to make a list before you go. If you have any questions, please leave a comment! Tasting these foods first will make your trip to Japan much more special.
