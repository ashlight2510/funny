Significance of Netflix's BTS comeback video achieving 18.4 million views

Recently, Netflix recorded 18.4 million viewers for the live broadcast of BTS's comeback performance video 'BTS Comeback Live: Arirang'. This video features BTS's first official stage since their full group return after 3 years and 9 months, and it was broadcast live from a free concert held in Gwanghwamun, Seoul on the 21st. This achievement secured the overwhelming #1 spot among non-English shows and ranked #1 for the week in 24 countries, including South Korea. Netflix stated that this performance was the first live music event broadcast from Korea to the world.

These records go beyond mere numbers, holding significant implications for BTS's global influence and Netflix's content strategy. As BTS has returned after a long military service, their comeback holds special meaning for fans, which is reflected in the viewership ratings.
