Traffic control in Gwanghwamun on Saturday, April 4... Easter Parade to be held

On Saturday, April 4, traffic control will be implemented in the Gwanghwamun area of Seoul due to the '2026 Easter Parade'. The Seoul city government will gradually control the roads from midnight to midnight on the day of the event, and various programs and concerts for children and family audiences are also being prepared. In particular, as a large number of people are expected to participate, safety management and traffic guidance will be strengthened.

This parade, hosted by the Korean Council of Churches, has established itself as a large-scale event with about 8,000 participants. The Seoul city government urges citizens to check the traffic situation in the Gwanghwamun Square area, which is expected to be congested, and provides alternative routes. These controls and safety measures appear to be essential actions for the safety of citizens.

Key Summary

  • Traffic control will be implemented in the Gwanghwamun area on April 4 for the '2026 Easter Parade'.
  • Various programs and open concerts for children and families will be held on the day of the event.
  • Measures for safety management and traffic guidance will be strengthened.
  • Congestion is expected due to large-scale participation.

Key Scenes

  • Traffic Control: Traffic will be controlled from midnight to midnight on the day of the event, with certain bus routes being diverted.
  • Safety Measures: Installation of pedestrian safety fences and deployment of safety management personnel.
  • Program Diversity: Experience programs and concerts for children and families will be held.
  • Citizen Participation: A large-scale event with participation from over 8,000 citizens.

Reasons to Consider the Background

This Easter Parade is an event hosted by the Korean Council of Churches, held annually to commemorate Easter. Notably, this year marks the first large-scale event since the easing of social distancing measures due to COVID-19, and many citizens are expected to participate. This change is likely to have a positive impact on the recovery of citizens' daily lives and the resumption of cultural activities.

Why It Matters

The Easter Parade is not just a festival but an important event that strengthens community bonds and social solidarity. In particular, programs for children and family audiences will contribute to promoting harmony and communication within the local community. While traffic control may cause inconvenience, it is necessary to understand it as a measure prioritizing safety.

Points to Note

In reality, many citizens may experience inconvenience due to traffic control. Therefore, it is important to check alternative routes in advance and consider using public transportation. Additionally, this event is likely to revitalize the local economy, presenting opportunities for merchants.

Hidden Variables to Consider

If prior announcements about this event are insufficient, unexpected congestion or inconveniences may arise. Particularly, if there is a lack of information about the traffic control areas, citizens may experience confusion, so it is essential to pay attention to the guidance from the Seoul city government.

Points to Check Now

  • Check Alternative Routes: It is necessary to check traffic control information in the Gwanghwamun area on the day of the event in advance and plan to use public transportation.
  • Follow Safety Guidelines: If participating in the event, it is important to adhere to safety guidelines and exercise caution in crowded areas.

The Easter Parade is not just a festival but an opportunity for the citizens of Seoul to come together and reaffirm the values of culture and community. Therefore, it is hoped that this event will further strengthen communication and solidarity among each other.
