Netanyahu "The removal of Larijani provides an opportunity for a popular uprising by shaking the regime"

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu stated that the removal of Ali Larijani, the Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council of Iran, is a strategic goal aimed at destabilizing the Iranian regime and providing the Iranian people with an opportunity to oust the regime. Netanyahu emphasized the instability of the Iranian regime and expressed hope that the Iranian people would have the chance to determine their own destiny. He suggested that pressure would be applied to the Iranian regime through close cooperation with the United States amid ongoing Iranian attacks.

This statement reveals both the political tension within Iran and Israel's military intentions, implying a desire to incite resistance among the Iranian people. Given the possibility that the removal of key figures in the Iranian regime could lead to internal chaos, the impact on the situation in the Middle East is noteworthy.
