Review of National Assembly Law Amendment to Limit Chairman's Authority: What Questions Remain?

March 19, 2026 · 경향신문 · View source

Original Article at a Glance Title: Han Byeong-do “Reviewing the amendment of the National Assembly Act to ‘restrict the powers’ of non-working chairpersons”… Targeting the ruling party's standing committee chairpersons Source: Kyunghyang Shinmun On the 19th, Han Byeong-do, the floor leader of the Democratic Party, stated regarding the operation of the National Assembly's standing committees, “We will mobilize all means, including promoting single meetings centered on the secretaries and amending the National Assembly Act to restrict the powers of non-working chairpersons.” He also hinted again at the possibility of the ruling party monopolizing the 17 standing committee chairpersons ahead of the formation negotiations in May. During a party policy coordination meeting held at the National Assembly, the floor leader pointed out that the handling of livelihood bills is slow under the chairpersons of the People Power Party... The Significance of Han Byeong-do's Statement on Restricting Chairperson Powers

Recently, Han Byeong-do, the floor leader of the Democratic Party, announced that he would consider "amending the National Assembly Act to restrict the powers of non-working chairpersons" regarding the operation of the National Assembly's standing committees. This is a response to criticism that the handling of livelihood bills is slow in committees chaired by the People Power Party, demonstrating the Democratic Party's commitment to fulfilling its responsibilities as the majority party. Notably, there is also a warning regarding the ruling party's potential monopoly on standing committee chairpersons ahead of the formation negotiations in May.

This statement transcends mere political sparring and could lead to practical measures aimed at resolving livelihood issues. The floor leader emphasized that the behavior of delaying bill processing in specific committees is causing suffering to the public and that he would seek legal means to address this issue.

Key Summary

  • Han Byeong-do, floor leader, considers amending the National Assembly Act to restrict powers of non-working chairpersons.
  • Criticism of delayed handling of livelihood bills under the People Power Party chairpersons.
  • Emphasis on responsible actions as the majority party.
  • Mention of the possibility of a comprehensive review of the distribution of standing committee chairpersons between parties.
  • Concerns about public suffering due to delays in processing economic bills.

Key Scenes

  • Restriction of Chairperson Powers: The Democratic Party is considering measures to restrict the powers of chairpersons who delay bill processing.
  • Handling of Livelihood Bills: There are delays in processing livelihood-related bills in committees chaired by the People Power Party.
  • Political Responsibility: The Democratic Party emphasizes its responsibility as the majority party and will take measures to minimize public harm.
  • Review of Chairperson Distribution: A warning that the distribution of chairpersons may be re-evaluated if deemed unfair.
  • Economic Impact: Delays in processing economic bills are directly affecting the lives of citizens.

Reasons to Consider the Background

Currently, the National Assembly is struggling to process livelihood bills due to conflicts between the ruling and opposition parties. In particular, there has been criticism that bill processing is sluggish in committees chaired by the People Power Party, which is becoming more pronounced in the context of an economic crisis. Han Byeong-do's remarks can be seen as a response from the Democratic Party to this situation, indicating a move towards practical problem-solving beyond political confrontation.

Why It Matters

This statement could serve as an important turning point in addressing livelihood issues that directly impact citizens' lives, rather than merely being a political skirmish. If the Democratic Party legally pursues restrictions on chairperson powers, it could lead to significant changes in the operation of the National Assembly and likely have a positive impact on citizens' lives.

My Perspective

In my view, Han Byeong-do's remarks demonstrate a strong will to take necessary actions in the current political situation, transcending mere political intent. Particularly in a situation where the processing of economic bills is delayed, such statements can be interpreted as a commitment to fulfilling political responsibilities. However, whether this legal amendment will actually take place will depend on political consensus and cooperation between the parties.

Hidden Variables to Consider

An important point in this statement is the Democratic Party's intention to re-evaluate the distribution of standing committee chairpersons from scratch. This could significantly impact future political negotiations and may serve as a signal for intensified conflicts between the parties. Additionally, we must not overlook the seriousness of the suffering citizens are experiencing due to delays in bill processing.
