BTS Comeback Performance, Tension at Gwanghwamun

On the 21st, the day of BTS's comeback performance, an unexpectedly large crowd gathered at Gwanghwamun in Seoul. At 3 PM, five hours before the performance began, about 28,000 people were present in Gwanghwamun Square, and the police set up metal detectors to conduct inspections for safety. During this process, an incident occurred where a chef was caught carrying a kitchen knife, and the police emphasized that they were taking all necessary precautions for the performance.

This incident serves as a reminder of how crucial safety management is at events where large crowds gather, going beyond simple safety checks. The impact of this incident was significant for both BTS fans and the general public.
