Traffic Control and Non-stop Subway Operations Around Gwanghwamun Due to BTS Concert

On the 21st, the day of BTS's comeback concert, large-scale traffic control will be implemented in the Gwanghwamun area of Seoul. The city will close major subway stations such as Gwanghwamun Station, City Hall Station, and Gyeongbokgung Station, and will take measures to pass through these sections without stopping. Road control will begin at 9 PM the day before the concert and will continue after the concert ends, with temporary trains and detour routes operating to minimize congestion in public transportation. The reason for this large-scale traffic control is to ensure the safety and convenience of BTS fans and attendees, and the city is preparing to provide information in various languages to ensure that foreign tourists do not face inconveniences.

This event holds significance beyond just a concert. BTS has a massive fan base both domestically and internationally, and their performances can have a positive impact on the local economy and tourism. However, there is also the possibility that ordinary citizens and local residents may experience inconveniences due to the traffic control.
