Essential Checklist for Terminating Employment Contracts for Workers

March 17, 2026

Essential Checklist for Termination of Employment Contracts

Termination of contracts is an important issue that frequently arises in professional life. There are various reasons why employees terminate their contracts, and it is essential to understand the legal effects and procedures involved. This understanding can help prevent unnecessary legal disputes and facilitate a smooth resignation process. In this article, we will summarize the key points that employees must check when terminating a contract.

Basic Concept of Contract Termination: Voluntary vs Involuntary Termination

Contract termination refers to the act of ending the effectiveness of a contract by mutual agreement between the parties. Termination can be broadly categorized into voluntary termination and involuntary termination.

  • Voluntary Termination: When an employee chooses to end the contract themselves, they must clearly communicate their intention.
  • Involuntary Termination: When the employer dismisses the employee, valid reasons are required.

Checkpoints

  • The notice of termination must be given according to the specified deadline and method; failure to comply may lead to legal issues. For example, if the notice period specified in the contract is exceeded, the termination may not be recognized.

Legal Requirements to Check When Terminating a Contract

When terminating a contract, it is crucial to verify the legal requirements. Legal requirements refer to the standards that must be adhered to during the termination process, and failure to comply can lead to legal problems.

  • Record of Termination Reasons: In the case of voluntary termination, specific reasons should be stated instead of vague expressions like 'personal circumstances.' For example, it is better to write clearly, "Due to family health issues."
  • Notice Method and Deadline: Generally, it is advisable to notify the termination in writing and within the timeframe specified in the contract.

Execution Tips

  • Before terminating the contract, prepare all relevant documents and ensure that legal requirements are met.

Important Considerations After Termination: Checking Severance Pay and Compensation

The first thing to check after termination is severance pay and compensation. Severance pay is calculated based on the length of service and is typically paid to employees who have worked for more than one year.

  • Conditions for Compensation Payment: The amount of compensation may vary depending on the reason for termination, and specific conditions must be met for payment. For instance, compensation may be claimed in cases of wrongful termination by the employer.

Tax Issues and Payment Delays

Taxes may apply when receiving severance pay or compensation, and if there are delays in payment, legal action may need to be taken.

Follow-Up Actions and Procedures After Termination

After termination, follow-up actions and subsequent procedures are essential.

  • Follow-Up Actions: Measures to prevent potential issues after termination, such as checking health insurance enrollment after resignation.
  • Subsequent Procedures: Issuance of a certificate of employment, return of company property, etc. Failing to complete these procedures may lead to difficulties in future re-employment.

Execution Tips

  • Request essential documents like the certificate of employment in advance and coordinate the schedule for returning company property.

Contract Termination Checklist: Essential Items to Confirm

Utilize the following checklist when preparing for contract termination:

  • Review the contract
  • Organize the reasons for termination
  • Draft the termination notice
  • Check severance pay and compensation
  • Plan subsequent procedures

This checklist will help ensure that all necessary items are thoroughly checked during the contract termination process.

Contract Termination Related FAQ

  1. What documents are needed for contract termination?

    • A termination notice, documents related to severance pay, and documents related to compensation are required.

  2. What happens if I miss the termination notice deadline?

    • Missing the termination notice deadline may result in the termination not being recognized, potentially leading to legal disputes.

  3. Will contract termination affect re-employment?

    • The reason for contract termination may impact re-employment opportunities. Particularly, termination due to wrongful dismissal may positively influence future job prospects.

Comparison of Practical Cases of Contract Termination

Case Reason for Termination Outcome
Case A Dissatisfaction with work environment No legal disputes
Case B Lack of valid termination reasons Legal disputes arise
Case C Employer's wrongful termination Compensation paid

Contract termination should be approached with caution, and it is important to thoroughly understand and prepare for the legal requirements and procedures involved. Employees should refer to the checklist above to ensure that all necessary items are addressed during the contract termination process.

First Step to Take Right Now

If you are considering terminating your contract, first carefully review the contract and organize your reasons for termination. This can help prevent potential legal issues in the future.
