Green Remodeling Interest Support Resumes After 3 Years… Free Consulting Available

Recently, the government announced the resumption of the Green Remodeling interest support program after three years. This program supports remodeling to improve the energy performance of existing buildings, including insulation performance improvements, window replacements, and the replacement of aging facilities. The resumption includes various improvements such as increasing the interest support rate, expanding the support limit, and adding free consulting services. These changes are interpreted as measures to enhance energy efficiency and reduce cost burdens.

This policy appears to provide citizens with the opportunity to contribute to energy savings through green remodeling while also addressing the issues of cost and lack of information that discourage building owners from remodeling.

Key Points

  • Interest Support Resumed: The Green Remodeling interest support program resumes after three years, with the support rate expanded to a maximum of 5.5%.
  • Free Consulting Service: Provides tailored solutions from experts to help building owners make informed decisions.
  • Improved Support Conditions: The support limit for non-residential large buildings has been expanded from 5 billion KRW to 20 billion KRW.
  • Simplified Application Process: The complex document submission process has been simplified, increasing accessibility.
  • Emphasis on Energy Performance Improvement: Additional support is provided when the energy performance improvement ratio is high.

Background and Context

The Green Remodeling program, which started in 2014, recorded about 80,000 support cases but was suspended in 2024. The government decided to resume this program to enhance energy performance and respond to climate change. In light of the recent energy crisis and the growing policy need to achieve carbon neutrality, green remodeling is expected to play a crucial role.

Why This Issue is Important

This issue reflects a significant policy trend aimed at not just remodeling buildings but also enhancing energy efficiency and responding to climate change. Especially at a time when energy performance improvements are socially demanded, government support can provide substantial help to building owners. Additionally, this is expected to positively impact the construction industry and related sectors.

However, it remains to be seen how many people will actually benefit from this support.

Often Overlooked Aspects

While the article emphasizes improvements in support conditions and procedures, it is uncertain how many building owners will receive actual benefits from this support. Additionally, whether the effects of green remodeling will contribute to long-term energy efficiency rather than just short-term cost savings is also an important variable.

What to Check Now

  • Application Method: You can check the application method for the interest support program and information about consulting support services on the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport's website.
  • Consulting Service: It is important to understand the eligibility requirements and procedures for applying for free consulting in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Can anyone apply for the Green Remodeling interest support program?
A1: Anyone who meets certain conditions as a private building owner can apply. Detailed conditions can be found on the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport's website.

Q2: What does the free consulting service include?
A2: Experts provide services such as energy performance diagnosis of the building, analysis of estimated construction costs, and suggestions for optimal improvement plans.

Q3: What is the support limit?
A3: For non-residential large buildings, support can be received up to a maximum of 20 billion KRW.

The Green Remodeling interest support program and free consulting service present an important opportunity to enhance energy efficiency. It is advisable to check relevant information now and explore ways to benefit from it.
