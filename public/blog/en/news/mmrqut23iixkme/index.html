How to Save Money with Card Points and Cashback

March 15, 2026

How to Reduce Spending Smartly with Card Points and Cashback

In this era, utilizing card points and cashback with every purchase has become essential. However, many people are unsure how to effectively use them, right? Until recently, I was just using my card without much thought. So, I've organized some ways to reduce spending smartly with card points and cashback.

How to Accumulate Card Points?

Basic Methods for Accumulating Card Points

Accumulating card points is really simple. Generally, you just need to take advantage of the benefits offered by the card issuer. For example, you can earn more points by making purchases at specific merchants during certain months.

From my experience, using affiliated cards from cafes or supermarkets I frequently visit has been really effective. I used to just use any card, but I often regretted it when I realized I wasn't accumulating points. So nowadays, I utilize the cards from places I go often. This way, I can visibly see the points accumulating.

Be Careful of Point Expiration!

Points have an expiration date, so it's essential to check them regularly. I've forgotten about this a few times and lost points. Therefore, setting reminders or planning how to use points is a good idea. Developing the habit of frequently checking, "When can I use this card?" will be helpful.

Utilizing Cashback

Choosing a Cashback Card

Cashback is a system where you receive a certain percentage back every time you spend. From my experience, using cards with high cashback rates has been really beneficial. Especially, there are many ways to earn cashback on everyday purchases.

For instance, by using a card with high cashback at my frequently visited supermarket, I felt like I was getting back several tens of thousands of won each month. As it accumulated, I was surprised when it turned into a significant amount later. So, I felt it would be good to settle on one card with great cashback.

Tailoring Cashback to Spending Patterns

It's important to choose a card that matches your spending patterns for cashback. For example, I often dine out or order delivery, so it's better for me to choose a card specialized for those types of spending.

My friend shops a lot, and they use a card with high cashback for shopping. Since everyone's spending patterns are different, selecting a card that fits your own pattern is a smart way to go.

Combining Card Points and Cashback

Using Both Together

Utilizing both card points and cashback together is even better. For example, you can make a payment with a card that accumulates points and then make an additional payment with a card that offers high cashback. This way, you can accumulate points and receive cashback simultaneously, which is a win-win.

From my experience, seeing points and cashback accumulate at the same time feels rewarding. I think this method might be the most efficient way to spend.

Conclusion

Ultimately, effectively utilizing card points and cashback can greatly help reduce spending. I hope you can refer to the methods I've experienced and develop your own strategy. If you have any questions, feel free to leave a comment, and let's discuss! I think starting with this would be a good idea.
