Hana Financial Investment Selected as the Management Company for Chungnam Corporate Growth Venture Fund

Hana Financial Investment has been selected as the management company for the 'Chungnam Corporate Growth Venture Fund' promoted by Korea Venture Investment Corp. This fund aims to invest in the growth stages of companies in the Chungnam region and will be established with a total size of 19.1 billion won. It is scheduled to launch within the second quarter and will include investments in promising companies in Chungnam's strategic industries such as display materials and equipment, eco-friendly automotive parts, and bio. Hana Financial Investment also plans to expand investments in innovative companies in the Chungcheong region.

This fund is expected to be a significant catalyst for revitalizing the economy in the Chungnam region and supporting the growth of innovative companies. In particular, the launch of a venture fund that supports the growth of local companies is expected to have a positive impact on the industrial ecosystem in the region.

Why Background Matters

The launch of this fund can be seen as part of efforts to overcome the economic challenges faced by the Chungnam region and to diversify its industrial ecosystem. Over the past few years, local companies have struggled with capital shortages and limitations on growth, and the establishment of a venture fund can provide these companies with new growth momentum. Hana Financial Investment had already formed a regional innovation investment partnership worth 50.5 billion won last year, so it is expected to continue its active support for the region through the operation of this fund.

Companies in the Chungnam region need both financial support and technological innovation. Furthermore, Hana Financial Investment's ongoing investment strategy can instill confidence in other investors, increasing the likelihood of more capital flowing into the region.
